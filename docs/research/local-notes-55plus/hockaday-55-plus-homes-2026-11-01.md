## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. In Hockaday this law has almost nothing to attach to: the sub-area's stock is freehold detached houses, which cannot carry a strata age bylaw at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. For Hockaday's freehold houses the point is simpler: a house's suite is governed by City suite rules and tenancy law, not strata bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996** is Coquitlam's zoning bylaw. The area's small-scale residential zones are R-1, R-2 and R-3 (subdivision minimums 650 m² / 18.5 m width for R-1 and R-3; 465 m² / 12.5 m for R-2); the zone of a specific Hockaday lot: UNCONFIRMED until checked parcel by parcel. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) in Coquitlam:** the City's adopted rules allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the rules — in a detached-house sub-area like this one, SSMUH is the entire "seniors housing" conversation: a suite or carriage house for family or a caregiver, created by the owner. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Watercourse setbacks:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam; Zoning Bylaw s.519 sets flood protection setbacks from watercourses and s.523 covers riparian areas. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Property tax dates that apply citywide:** Coquitlam property tax payment was due July 2, 2026 for the Jan 1–Dec 31, 2026 year, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after September 15, 2026, including unclaimed Home Owner Grants. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Hockaday?** None that any source verifies — and there is no strata complex in the sub-area to buy into at all on the research: September 2026 recorded 4 detached listings and a single condos & townhomes listing, and no named complex inside Hockaday was confirmed on a developer or strata source. A senior set on this pocket is buying a freehold house (with or without a suite), not an age-restricted community. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.)
- **Q: Is there a seniors building near Pinetree Secondary?** No. Pinetree Secondary (3000 Pinewood Ave) is the sub-area's anchor, and no retirement residence, assisted-living registration, subsidized seniors building or 55+ strata with an address in this sub-area appears in any source checked, including BC Housing's Zone 4 seniors directory. https://www.sd43.bc.ca/school/pinetree/pages/newsitem.aspx?ItemID=135&ListID=d738f4fa-8b6d-458e-a506-4154261d2016&TemplateID=Announcement_Item and https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: Can a Hockaday house work for ageing in place with family or a caregiver?** That is the realistic route here: Coquitlam's SSMUH rules allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes, where the lot is connected to municipal water and sewer — and at least one Hockaday Street house listing already shows the pattern, with an above-ground self-contained 2-bedroom suite with a separate entry. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId= and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3110833-1422-hockaday-street-coquitlam-british-columbia-v3e2x9-1699000/brochure
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. If we buy into a 55+ strata elsewhere in Coquitlam, can we both live there?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Where do older adults here actually go for programming?** The City's 50+ hubs are its two pavilions; the one for this side of the city is Glen Pine Pavilion, 1200 Glen Pine Court, by City Hall on Pinetree Way's corridor — the City describes the pavilions as adult-oriented centres focused on programming for older adults, with activity groups run with the Glen Pine 50 Plus Society. https://coquitlam.ca/455/Glen-Pine-Pavilion and https://www.coquitlam.ca/1159/50-Plus-Services
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Eagle Ridge Hospital, 475 Guildford Way, Port Moody — the Fraser Health hospital on the Port Moody / Coquitlam border, west across the city from Hockaday. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** The Evergreen Line stations Lincoln and Lafarge Lake-Douglas are on Pinetree Way at the sub-area's edge, which is the transit the Town Centre side markets itself on; bus routes on Hockaday's interior streets are UNVERIFIED, so a car-free buyer should check the exact address's bus service, not the station distance alone. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01). https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/station/coquitlam-central/schedule
- **Q: Are the September 2026 numbers for Hockaday meaningful?** Only as a snapshot of a very small market: 1 detached sale from 4 listings produces the headline 25% ratio, and the single strata listing did not sell. Any price case here has to be built on Central Coquitlam, Canyon Springs and Eagle Ridge solds plus the Coquitlam community medians, with the raw counts shown beside any percentage. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.)
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in Hockaday?** Not in a seniors building — there is none in the sub-area. The in-city ladder is: a house with a suite or caregiver arrangement here, 55+ strata in the Town Centre pocket, then retirement and care elsewhere in Coquitlam and the Tri-Cities through the health-authority process for care beds. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
