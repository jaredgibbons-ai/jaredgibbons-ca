## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017** is Delta's zoning bylaw; seniors housing in Delta is typically delivered through site-specific comprehensive development zones — South Pointe sits in CD98A. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3075824-5371-regatta-way-delta-bc-v4k-4r9
- **"Seniors congregate housing" is a named use in Delta's newer apartment zones.** The Apartment Residential 6 (RA6) zone added to Bylaw No. 7600 lists apartment building and seniors congregate housing as principal uses, for six-storey residential development along arterial and collector streets under the OCP's Neighbourhood Centres and Corridors designation. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future")** is Delta's current OCP — a single consolidated plan; the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, and its area-plan schedules are superseded. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing:** Delta's SSMUH rules allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide, subject to the bylaw and to any hazard or streamside permit area on the lot — relevant to a 55+ buyer in this band adding a suite for a caregiver or family member. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Property taxes:** Delta's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Tax relief that matters at 55+:** the Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Will this under-$1.8M feed show me Ladner's 55+ homes?** Mostly no. The band is detached freehold houses; Ladner's verified 55+ ownership is strata — Delta Green (4686 51 St, built 2021) and South Pointe (bare land strata ranchers in Neilsen Grove) — which surface on condo / bare-land-strata searches, not on a detached band feed. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent, or as an investment?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. Confirm the current position in the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or a buyer who purchases without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I add a suite to a Ladner detached house for a caregiver or family member?** Delta's SSMUH rules allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide, subject to the zoning bylaw and to any hazard or streamside permit area on the lot. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, in Ladner — Fraser Health's community hospital for Delta with a 24/7 emergency department, beside the Ladner Exchange bus loop. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- **Q: Is Ladner in a floodplain?** Flood protection in Delta is a city-wide system: the City is the diking authority and operates and maintains over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure — lot-level flood construction requirements still have to be checked parcel by parcel. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: Are pets allowed in Ladner's 55+ buildings?** Building by building: Delta Green's listing data records cats and dogs OK, limited to one; South Pointe listings record pets allowed. Confirm in the strata bylaws for the specific purchase. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/4686-51-street/106 and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3075824-5371-regatta-way-delta-bc-v4k-4r9
- **Q: What if I can't find a 55+ purchase — what are Ladner's seniors rental options?** Subsidized: Evergreen Court (130 homes), Evergreen Plaza (138 units) and Marina Park (50 units), all 55+ through BC Housing's Housing Registry. Care/assisted: Augustine House on Arthur Drive (Fraser Health assisted living program at 55+, via a case manager). All rental — nothing to buy. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
