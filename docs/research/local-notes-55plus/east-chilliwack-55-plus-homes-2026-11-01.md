# East Chilliwack, Chilliwack — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **The strata age-bylaw rules, for the record — with no local strata to apply them to.** Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; age bylaws below 55 invalid since Nov 24, 2022, under Bill 44); the bylaw restricts residency, not ownership; Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19; and residential rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, while short-term restrictions remain allowed. A rural buyer gets none of this machinery, because the purchase is freehold land, not a strata lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **ALR housing caps are the rules that actually bind a 55+ buyer here.** Land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **The second-dwelling rule is the ageing-in-place rule on rural land.** The ALC permits one additional residence without an ALC application where its conditions are met — detached, 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and Chilliwack's bylaws must also permit it. Anything larger needs an ALC application. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Chilliwack adds a Farm Home Plate permit area.** The Chilliwack 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 5500, Schedule D includes D-4 Farm Home Plate among its development permit areas — the City's siting control over where the farm residence sits on the parcel — alongside D-1 Drinking Water Supply, D-2 Hillside Environment, D-3 Riparian Area and D-5 Hillside Development. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000** — Section 6 sets the agricultural zones (AL Agricultural Lowland, AU Agricultural Upland, AS Agricultural Small Lot, AR Agricultural Residential, AC Agricultural Commercial, AFP Agricultural Food Processing). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Manufactured-home parks run on tenancy law, not strata law** (relevant to the Rosedale park in Section 2): pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, and the assignment rules expressly recognise a park where every pad is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55, per s. 10(2)(b)(i) of the Human Rights Code — Mt Cheam's listing states families are allowed, so it is not such a park. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/consol20/consol20/02077_pit
- **Farm classification is a continuing obligation.** BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income requirements at farm gate prices are $2,500 (0.8–4 ha), $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha (over 4 ha), and $10,000 (under 0.8 ha). A downsizing buyer who stops producing can lose the classification. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property taxes (2026, as published by the City):** due the first working day after July 1; a 5% penalty applies for taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026. The Province's regular property tax deferment program is for people 55 and older. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ housing in East Chilliwack itself?** No — none was verified in any source. The sub-area is rural ALR ground; Chilliwack's verified 55+ complexes and its one 55+ park are all in the urban core and Sardis side. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: The Rosedale park looks like a seniors park. Is it 55+?** No. Mt Cheam Mobile Home Park's own listing describes it as mostly senior-occupied but states families are allowed — there is no age restriction in its rules as listed. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing/r3089923-52604-yale-road-10-rosedale-bc-v0x-1x1/brochure
- **Q: I'm 55+ and buying a hobby farm here. What limits the house I can build?** On ALR land, a principal residence built without an ALC application is capped at 500 m² total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; the OCP's Farm Home Plate permit area (D-4) also controls siting. Confirm the parcel's ALR status on the ALC maps first. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://chilliwack.com/ocp and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can a caregiver or my adult child live in a second dwelling on the parcel?** The ALC permits one additional residence without an application where its size rules are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels, detached — and City bylaws must also permit it. There is no strata-style caregiver exemption because there is no strata. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: If I stop farming the land, do I keep farm-class taxes?** Not automatically — BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income thresholds ($2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha) must keep being met, by you or by a lessee who produces. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Chilliwack General Hospital, 45600 Menholm Rd — a Fraser Health community hospital with emergency and acute care, west of this sub-area in the urban core. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes at 55+?** The Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
- **Q: Is this area flood-safe?** The sub-area runs from valley-floor farm ground up toward the Cheam / Fraser River side; the OCP's Schedule D includes riparian (D-3) and drinking-water (D-1) permit areas, and flood status is parcel-specific — check the specific parcel with the City rather than assuming from the district name. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
