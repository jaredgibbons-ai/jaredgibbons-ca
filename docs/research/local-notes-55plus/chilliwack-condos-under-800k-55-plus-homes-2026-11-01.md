## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **First-time buyer PTT ceiling sits just above this band.** The provincial first time home buyers' program requires a fair market value of $835,000 or less for the full exemption (effective April 1, 2024), exempts the property transfer tax on the first $500,000 of the purchase price, and applies a partial exemption above $835,000 and below $860,000; the property must be 0.5 hectares or smaller, used only as the buyer's principal residence and contain only residential improvements. A qualifying buyer anywhere in this under-$800K band sits under the value ceiling — the other program conditions still decide. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000** is Chilliwack's zoning bylaw. Its Section 2 defines a secondary suite as an accessory dwelling unit of not more than 90 m² and less than 40% of the habitable floor space of the building — the cap is the lesser of 90 m² and 40%. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Accessory dwelling units:** Section 4.06(1) of the same bylaw permits only 1 ADU per lot where the zone permits an ADU; the listed types are secondary suite, coach house and garden suite, and garden suites are capped at a maximum gross floor area of 100 m² by Section 4.06(1)(g). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **SSMUH:** Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395 is the City's small-scale multi-unit housing amendment to Bylaw No. 5000, allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing; Bylaw 5395 was adopted June 18, 2024. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **City plan: Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500** is the current city-wide plan. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Property taxes:** City of Chilliwack property tax notices are mailed annually at the end of May and are due the first working day after July 1 of every calendar year; a 5% late payment penalty is applied for taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% for taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many 55+ buildings can I actually BUY in Chilliwack?** Five verified age-restricted strata ownership complexes city-wide: Ivy Green and Spadina Regency on Spadina Avenue (board label Chilliwack Downtown), Mountainview Estates on Yale Road (Chilliwack Proper East), Knight Lodge on Knight Road (Sardis West Vedder) and Cottage Grove on south Young Road. A sixth name, Mountview Apartments, is a flagged conflict only — see Section 2. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is there a 55+ option that is not a condo?** Yes, two forms: Cottage Grove's gated ranchers (strata ownership, 8500 Young Rd) and Cottonwood Village (7610 Evans Rd), a 55+ manufactured-home park where the buyer owns the home and rents the pad on First Nations leased land, with a listing-stated lease expiry in 2076 plus an option to extend. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Does the $800,000 cap matter for the first-time home buyers' PTT exemption?** Yes — in the buyer's favour. The full exemption runs to a fair market value of $835,000, exempting the tax on the first $500,000 of the purchase price, provided the buyer and property meet all the program's other conditions — so a qualifying first-time buyer anywhere in this band sits under the full-exemption value ceiling. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent, or to move into later?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Building by building, and the Spadina corridor proves it: Ivy Green records pets not allowed, Spadina Regency's listings state pets are permitted, and Chilliwack Gardens — which is not 55+ at all — records no pets. Confirm the specific building's bylaws on the Form B. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Why are there no market numbers for condos under $800K in Chilliwack?** Chilliwack is a CADREB market that SnapStats does not cover, CADREB publishes statistics board-wide only with no sub-area tables, and no source used here publishes a price-band or condo-only cut — so any band statistic is UNVERIFIED rather than borrowed from a neighbouring board. https://www.cadreb.com/market/
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in Chilliwack?** The in-city ladder is: independent strata (the five complexes in Section 2) → subsidized independent rental for low-income seniors 55+ (Jubilee Place, 45440 Hodgins Ave, 36 units) → independent/assisted retirement rental in the hospital precinct (the three Chartwell residences and Waverly Seniors Village) → registered assisted living (Birchwood and The Cascades, both class SN in the Assisted Living Registry) — centred on Chilliwack General Hospital at 45600 Menholm Rd. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: When are Chilliwack property taxes due, and what is the penalty?** Notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1 each year; for 2026, a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522

- **The 40+ and 45+ parks are not a back door into 55+ stock.** Country Park Village (45+) and Rainbow Estates (40+) carry park age rules, not strata bylaws — and since Nov 24, 2022 a strata age bylaw below 55 is invalid in BC in any case, so a "40+" or "45+" label never means the same legal product as a 55+ strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **One operator runs the whole hospital-corner care cluster.** Chartwell operates all three retirement residences at the Landing / hospital corner — Birchwood (45650 Patten Ave), Hampton House (45555 Hodgins Ave) and Lynnwood (9168 Corbould St) — and its own copy for each notes it is on the same corner as two other Chartwell residences, so a senior comparing the three is comparing suite types and service packages inside one operator, not three competitors. https://chartwell.com/bc/chilliwack/hampton-house and https://chartwell.com/bc/chilliwack/lynnwood and https://chartwell.com/bc/chilliwack/birchwood
