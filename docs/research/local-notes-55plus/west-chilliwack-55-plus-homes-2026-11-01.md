# West Chilliwack — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "West Chilliwack" is a Paragon (board) sub-area name; the City of Chilliwack publishes no street-by-street boundary for it. The ground it covers is the western rural and agricultural part of the city, which the City plans through its Agricultural Area Plan, a schedule of the Official Community Plan; the plan states the farm sector there is dominated by dairy and poultry operations, nurseries and greenhouses. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SB1%5FAgricultural%5FArea%5FPlan%5F1%2Epdf
- The named corridor inside the area is Evans Road: Evans Elementary (K–5) is at 7600 Evans Road, and BC Transit route 52 Evans runs between Cottonwood Mall and Downtown Chilliwack with timing points at Cottonwood Mall, Evans Rd at Yale Rd, Hodgins Ave at Ashwell Rd and Downtown Chilliwack. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school and https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=52
- SD33 catchments are address-based: the locator result for the sample address 7600 Evans Road is Evans Elementary → Vedder Middle → Sardis Secondary (locator note: all students attending Vedder Middle must attend Sardis Secondary). Catchments for other addresses: run the specific address in the locator. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- Highway 1 serves the north side of Chilliwack; interchange detail for this area is UNVERIFIED on the sources read. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
- The nearest City recreation centres are outside the area — Chilliwack Landing Leisure Centre, Unit 1, 9145 Corbould Street, and Cheam Leisure Centre, 45501 Market Way — and the nearest library is Chilliwack Library, 45860 First Avenue. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2246 and https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations
- One health service IS inside the corridor: the Chilliwack Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at #104 - 7955 Evans Road, co-located at the Chilliwack and Fraser Health Rural Primary Care Centre. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/Chilliwack-Urgent-and-Primary-Care-Centre
- Where 55+ housing actually sits in Chilliwack: the Evans Road corridor holds the area's one verified 55+ product (a manufactured-home park, Section 2); the city's 55+ strata condos and townhomes sit in Downtown Chilliwack, Sardis and Vedder Crossing, and its care residences sit on the east/downtown side — see the "Checked and NOT in this area" list in Section 2. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

The only strata building verified on Evans Road, Creekside Estates, is explicitly NOT age-restricted (see "Checked and NOT 55+").

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home parks run on a different statute:** pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA), not the Strata Property Act. A home owner must provide a purchaser with the written tenancy agreement and the written park rules, and a sale is an assignment of the pad tenancy that requires the park owner's consent; the assignment rules expressly recognise a park where every pad is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 (or 2+ tenants, at least one of whom has reached 55), as set out in s. 10(2)(b)(i) of the Human Rights Code — the park owner can refuse an assignment where no purchaser meets that age requirement. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/consol20/consol20/02077_pit
- **First Nations leased land adds a second layer at Cottonwood Village:** the park is recorded as First Nations leased land, so the head lease (listing-stated expiry 2076 with an option to extend) sits above the pad tenancy — a buyer is reviewing the lease term and the park rules, not a strata Form B. https://bentruscottrealestate.com/manufactured-homes.html?utm_medium=paid&utm_source=fb&utm_id=52609658579346&utm_content=52609658626346&utm_term=52609658585146&utm_campaign=52609658579346 and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/listing/r3069101-50-7610-evans-road-chilliwack-bc-v2r-2y8
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000** — in the AL (Agricultural Lowland) zone that frames the rural west, the minimum lot area is 7.5 ha for land excluded or exempted from the ALR or approved for subdivision within the ALR, 1 ha for a boundary adjustment, and 0.2 ha for a homesite severance or boundary adjustment approved by the Agricultural Land Commission; minimum lot width is 25 m. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **City plan: Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500** (adopted December 16, 2025) is the city-wide plan; the Agricultural Area Plan is its Schedule B1 and covers this area, and the OCP's housing policies support forming partnerships with senior levels of government and local sectors to deliver more affordable housing. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387 and http://engagechilliwack.com/chwk2050 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3553
- **Flood rules:** the City's Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 sets minimum building setbacks of 60 m from the Fraser River, 75 m from the Vedder River and 30 m from the Chilliwack River and Vedder Canal; the City maintains a diking network for the Fraser and Vedder River systems, and Chilliwack levies additional taxes for drainage and dyking on top of the municipal rates. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ strata condo or townhome complex in West Chilliwack?** No. No age-restricted strata or freehold ownership complex with a West Chilliwack address was verified in any source checked. The one strata building on Evans Road, Creekside Estates (7694 Evans Rd), is recorded as having "no age restrictions." https://rennie.com/listings/evans-7694-road-creekside-303-chilliwack
- **Q: What CAN a 55+ buyer actually buy on the Evans Road corridor?** A manufactured home in Cottonwood Village (7610 Evans Rd) — a 55+ park of 143 units with a clubhouse, where you own the home, rent the pad, and the land is First Nations leased land with a listing-stated lease expiry of 2076 plus an option to extend. That is leasehold park tenure under the MHPTA, not strata or freehold ownership. https://bentruscottrealestate.com/manufactured-homes.html?utm_medium=paid&utm_source=fb&utm_id=52609658579346&utm_content=52609658626346&utm_term=52609658585146&utm_campaign=52609658579346 and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/listing/r3069101-50-7610-evans-road-chilliwack-bc-v2r-2y8
- **Q: Can the park refuse to let me buy because of age?** In a 55+ park, yes, in effect: assignment of a pad tenancy needs the park owner's consent, and the provincial assignment rules recognise refusal where no purchaser meets the park's 55+ age requirement (Human Rights Code s. 10(2)(b)(i) framework). Get the park rules in writing before writing an offer — the seller must provide them. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Cottonwood Village?** The park specialist's list records 1 pet allowed at Cottonwood Village; an individual listing records 2 smaller pets permitted — the park rules govern, so confirm the current rule with the park before relying on either figure. https://bentruscottrealestate.com/manufactured-homes.html?utm_medium=paid&utm_source=fb&utm_id=52609658579346&utm_content=52609658626346&utm_term=52609658585146&utm_campaign=52609658579346 and https://sutton.com/ca/real-estate/22-7610-evans-road-sardis-west-vedder-chilliwack-british-columbia-canada-v2r2t4-29089692
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. What are the rules?** In a 55+ STRATA, a younger spouse/partner is exempt by provincial regulation. In a manufactured-home PARK, the test in the assignment rules is that at least one of 2+ tenants has reached 55 — read the park's own rules, because park rules, not strata exemptions, govern a pad tenancy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- **Q: Where are Chilliwack's 55+ strata buildings, then?** Downtown and the central city: e.g. Ivy Green (45775 Spadina Ave, downtown — 55+, 24 condos, built 1994, LMS1336, no pets) and Knight Lodge (45660 Knight Rd — 55+ per listing restriction data). https://www.admiraal.ca/buildings/view/27410 and https://www.estateblock.com/chilliwack/203-45660-knight-rd-49464
- **Q: Where is the hospital and urgent care?** Chilliwack General Hospital is at 45600 Menholm Rd; the Chilliwack Urgent and Primary Care Centre is inside this corridor at #104 - 7955 Evans Road. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/Chilliwack-Urgent-and-Primary-Care-Centre
- **Q: Is there a bus on Evans Road?** Yes — route 52 Evans runs between Cottonwood Mall and Downtown Chilliwack via Evans Rd and Yale Rd, with timing points at Evans Rd at Yale Rd and Hodgins Ave at Ashwell Rd. Service levels/frequency: check the current BC Transit schedule before relying on it. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=52
- **Q: What flood checks matter on a rural west-side lot?** Measure the bylaw setbacks — 60 m from the Fraser River, 75 m from the Vedder River, 30 m from the Chilliwack River and Vedder Canal — and budget for the drainage and dyking levies the City adds on top of municipal tax rates. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- **Q: If I need subsidized seniors housing or care, where do I look?** Not in this area: the BC Housing Zone 7 directory's Chilliwack seniors addresses are downtown/central (e.g. the Paramount, 46187 Yale Rd — 65+, income-tested, rent at 30% of income), and the registered assisted-living residences (Waverly Seniors Village, 8445 Young Rd; The Cascades, 45586 McIntosh Dr) are on the east/downtown side. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-7-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf and https://www.comserv.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Subsidized-Units-Seniors-Paramount-Application-Fillable-NEW-1.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The two Evans Road addresses side by side are opposite products:** 7610 Evans Rd (Cottonwood Village) is 55+ leasehold park housing, and 7694 Evans Rd (Creekside Estates), a few doors from Evans Elementary, is a general-market strata with "no age restrictions" stated in its listings — search results that blur the corridor blur that distinction. https://bentruscottrealestate.com/manufactured-homes.html?utm_medium=paid&utm_source=fb&utm_id=52609658579346&utm_content=52609658626346&utm_term=52609658585146&utm_campaign=52609658579346 and https://rennie.com/listings/evans-7694-road-creekside-estates-314-chilliwack
- **The lease clock is the real due-diligence item at Cottonwood:** listings state a 2076 lease expiry with an option to extend on First Nations leased land — what a lender, an insurer and a future buyer will all ask about is the remaining term and the extension mechanics, so get the head-lease summary and park rules before subject removal. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/listing/r3069101-50-7610-evans-road-chilliwack-bc-v2r-2y8
- **"45+" and "40+" parks nearby are a different legal animal than they look:** Country Park Village (45918 Knight Rd) is listed as 45+ and Rainbow Estates (9055 Ashwell Rd) as 40+ — in a strata those age numbers have been void since Nov 24, 2022, and in a park they survive only as park rules under the Human Rights Code 55+ framework's assignment rules, which are written around age 55. Read the actual park rules rather than the marketing number. https://bentruscottrealestate.com/manufactured-homes.html?utm_medium=paid&utm_source=fb&utm_id=52609658579346&utm_content=52609658626346&utm_term=52609658585146&utm_campaign=52609658579346 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The Sardis Neighbourhood Plan (Schedule B5 of the 2050 OCP) expects the fastest growth in the seniors cohort city-wide** — residents aged 75+ growing at 2.4% to 4.5% per year between 2016 and 2041 — which is the City's own context for why the Evans/Cottonwood corridor's seniors product is a park, not new strata construction. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- **Chilliwack taxes have two penalty steps and a dyking add-on:** property tax notices are due the first working day after July 1 each year, with a 5% penalty on taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% on taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026, plus separate drainage and dyking levies on top of the municipal rates. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- **The City does not run any of the housing in its own inventory:** the Chilliwack Housing Inventory 2026 states the City does not own, operate or manage any listed housing option and that inclusion does not imply endorsement or guaranteed availability — eligibility and availability always come from the operator. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8558
