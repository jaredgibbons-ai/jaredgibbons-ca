## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. This is the test for Highgate Terrace above: a record that says "age restrictions" without a number cannot be rounded up to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. Parkway Estates and Parkway II still carry "rentals not allowed" in their building records; for a long-term rental that line cannot be relied on. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Langley City short-term rentals: UNVERIFIED.** STR status was not verified on any City page in the program's rules-gaps pass — no Langley City STR rule is stated in this file, which matters for investor math in this band and is flagged rather than filled. Source: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, cited in the research file.
- **Two municipalities, two zoning bylaws — kept separate per complex:** Langley City Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 governs the City complexes (Parkway Estates, Parkway II, Glenmont Park, Huntsfield Green); Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs the Township complexes (Waterfront at Bedford Landing and the Township townhouses and parks). Neither bylaw creates a 55+ status — the age rule comes from the strata bylaw, park rule or co-op rule. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/schedule-zoning-bylaw-2025-no-3300pdf
- **Suites and small-scale multi-unit housing, by municipality:** Langley City — SSMUH Bylaw 3284, adopted June 17, 2024, permits RS1/RS2 lots "4; 6 if within 400 m of frequent bus stop"; a City secondary suite may not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area, and the registered owner must reside on the lot. Township — SSMUH Bylaw 6020, adopted November 18, 2024: 3 units on lots ≤280 m², 4 on lots to <4,050 m², larger lots ineligible, no 6-unit tier; Township suite cap 120 m². Sources: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, cited in the research file.
- **Manufactured-home parks run on park rules, not strata law:** the age rules at Cedar Creek Estates, Fernridge Park and Pineridge are park rules attached to a leasehold pad, and Pioneer Park's are co-op rules; pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and its Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003), which recognises a park reserved for tenants who have reached 55 and allows a landlord to refuse an assignment where the purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement. Pad rent increases are capped at 2.3% for 2026 plus a proportional levy/utility amount, once per 12 months on 3 full months' notice. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **First-time buyer tax programs in this band:** on the Province's published figures, general property transfer tax is 1% up to and including $200,000, 2% on the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% above that; the first time home buyers' program full exemption requires a fair market value of $835,000 or less (effective April 1, 2024) and exempts tax on the first $500,000, with a partial exemption only above $835,000 and below $860,000, subject to the program's other conditions. FHSA and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits: UNVERIFIED — not read off a live CRA page in this pass. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Property taxes — two calendars, no single Langley date:** the community spans two municipalities with separate tax notices. Township taxes and utility fees were due Thursday, July 2, 2026 (5% penalty at midnight July 2, 2026, further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026); the City also sets a July 2 due date, with the City's penalty rate UNCONFIRMED on the page cited in the roster file. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx and https://www.langleycity.ca/PayPropertyTaxes

- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ condo?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building for a parent or as an investment?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC strata — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, whatever an older building summary still shows for Parkway Estates or Parkway II. Two catches remain: the tenant must still satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. Langley City's own STR status is UNVERIFIED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the Langley City 55+ condos?** Building by building, and the two Parkway buildings are the strict end: Parkway Estates and Parkway II building sources record pets not allowed. Glenmont Park listings do not state a pet rule in the sources cited here — UNCONFIRMED for that building; Waterfront at Bedford Landing records pets allowed with restrictions (cats and dogs OK, limit two). Confirm the specific building's bylaw before writing. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/parkway-estates-5360-205-street and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/parkway-ii-5363-206-street and https://www.cotala.com/tours/85164/FullPublic.pdf?i=603115479
- **Q: Does "Langley" on this page mean Langley City or the Township?** Both. The SnapStats community combines the Township and the City across eleven sub-areas, and the feed's first page carries Township clusters (Aldergrove, Willoughby Heights, Murrayville) and City addresses side by side — the two municipalities have separate bylaws and tax notices, which matters on suites, STRs and closing adjustments. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-condos-under-499k.html
- **Q: Why would a 55+ unit be larger than a new unit at the same price?** In the feed as read, the in-band first page ran from a 465 sq ft junior 1-bedroom to a 1,225 sq ft 2-bedroom in the 55+ Langley City building — at this ceiling, age of building trades directly against floor area. That trade-off is built into every BC 55+ strata since the 2022 law change confined age bylaws to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
