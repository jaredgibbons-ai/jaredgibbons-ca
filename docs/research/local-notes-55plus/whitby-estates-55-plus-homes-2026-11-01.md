# Whitby Estates (West Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Headline finding:** Whitby Estates has **no 55+ housing of any tenure** — no age-restricted strata, no seniors rental, no retirement residence, no care home and no subsidized seniors building inside the pocket. It is a detached-house subdivision (plus two small general-market strata pockets) on the British Properties slope. West Vancouver's verified seniors stock is all in Ambleside, down the hill, and is carried in Section 2 as clearly labelled out-of-area context only.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Framing, stated honestly:** the strata age-bylaw rules below are general BC law. They do not attach to anything in Whitby Estates today — the pocket has no 55+ strata, and its two strata complexes (Garden Court, Highgrove) have no verified age bylaw. They matter to a Whitby Estates buyer in two ways: they govern the Ambleside 55+ buildings in Section 2 if the buyer redirects there, and they are the rules any BC strata the buyer considers will live under.
- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. The Province's page cites the same provisions: Strata Property Act ss. 123.1, 123.2 and Strata Property Regulation 7.01. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This applies to Garden Court and Highgrove exactly as it does to any Ambleside strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010** is the District of West Vancouver's zoning bylaw (District bylaw page, number 4662). Houses sit in the RS single-family dwelling zones in Section 200, which set minimum site areas from 488 m² in RS5 to 8,094 m² in RS1 and list secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among the permitted uses in the single-family zones — the bylaw basis for the large-lot pattern of the British Properties and for adding a suite to a Whitby Estates house. CONFLICT FLAGGED: the numbers "Zoning Bylaw 2024 No. 2506" and "OCP Bylaw 2017 No. 2220" supplied with this page's brief do not match any District of West Vancouver bylaw found on the District's site — the District's are 4662 (zoning) and 4985 (OCP), below. Do not publish 2506 / 2220 for West Vancouver. https://westvancouver.ca/government-administration/bylaws-licensing/find-bylaw/zoning-bylaw-no-4662-2010 and https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018.** The OCP treats the British Properties as an established existing neighbourhood and directs growth to the town and village centres, not into the Properties — which is why no seniors-density product is planned for this hillside under the current plan. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- **Building scheme on top of District rules:** British Properties subdivisions carry restrictive covenants registered at the Land Title Office — under them, an owner must file an application for approval of plans with British Pacific Properties Limited before building a new home, extending or altering an existing building, or erecting a fence or any structure; the covenants differ by subdivision, bind every subsequent owner, and the District's zoning and building bylaws apply in addition. Confirm the Whitby Estates subdivision's specific covenant set per property, on title. https://illustrator.uservoice.com/assets/243142901/Plan-Approval-Instructions-11.22.18.pdf
- **Development permits on this slope:** a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit applies to work within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface, and an Environmental Development Permit applies within 15 metres of the top of a watercourse bank — both live issues on Whitby Estates lots backing onto forest or creeks. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Property taxes:** District of West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on September 2, 2026; roughly half the bill is collected for other taxing authorities, and water/sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes and https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees
- **Additional school tax:** the Province's additional school tax applies at Whitby Estates price points — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **Care is not a strata product:** the Ambleside residences in Section 2 are registered with BC's Assisted Living Registry, and publicly funded long-term care (Inglewood's 230 beds) is allocated through Vancouver Coastal Health after an assessment — neither is bought, and neither uses a strata age bylaw. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=may5&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=41&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc&f%5B0%5D=city%3AWest%20Vancouver

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ housing in Whitby Estates itself?** No — none of any tenure. The Assisted Living Registry's West Vancouver list has four residences, all in Ambleside or by Park Royal; the North Shore guide to 55+ bylaw buildings lists three, all in Ambleside; and the pocket's own two strata complexes show no age restriction in any source checked. A "Whitby Estates 55+" search is really a search for detached houses and two general-market strata pockets. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=may5&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=41&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc&f%5B0%5D=city%3AWest%20Vancouver and https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/on-the-market/55-plus-buildings-west-vancouver/
- **Q: So what does a 55+ buyer actually buy in Whitby Estates?** An ordinary home with no age rules attached: a detached freehold estate house on a large lot, or a unit in one of the two small strata pockets — Aerie at Garden Court (8 residences, built 2010) or Highgrove (18 townhomes, built 2013). Downsizing into the strata pockets trades the house-maintenance load for strata living, but the homes themselves are large, multi-level view homes, not single-level seniors product. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3108529-102-2575-garden-court-west-vancouver-british-columbia-v7s0a2-2770000/brochure and https://storage.ubertor.com/wendytianying.ourubertor.com/content/featuresheet_35_document/94170.pdf
- **Q: Where is the nearest 55+ home I can buy?** Ambleside, down the hill: the verified age-restricted strata set for the whole District is Westview Terrace (1390 Duchess Ave), Tudor Gardens (843 22nd St) and Lincoln Gardens (2110 Marine Dr) — all on or near the flat Marine Drive corridor, near the Community Centre, library and Seniors' Activity Centre. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Where are the nearest retirement and care options?** Also Ambleside: Amica West Vancouver (659 Clyde Ave — the closest, at the Taylor Way end), Hollyburn House (2095 Marine Dr), Kiwanis Manor (959 21st St) and Westerleigh PARC (725 22nd St) for rental retirement living, Amica Lions Gate (701 Keith Rd) on the Park Royal side, and Inglewood Care Centre (725 Inglewood Ave) for publicly funded long-term care. All are rental or care — none is in Whitby Estates and none is strata ownership. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes, under provincial law — this is the question that applies if you redirect to an Ambleside 55+ building. Provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. Nothing in Whitby Estates itself has an age bylaw to be exempt from. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. Again, this is the Ambleside scenario — in Whitby Estates there is no age bylaw on any property. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a home I buy here?** For a strata unit (Garden Court or Highgrove), long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022; a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. For a detached house there is no strata at all. If the unit were in an Ambleside 55+ building instead, the tenant would additionally have to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I add a suite for a caregiver or family member?** The zoning basis exists: the RS single-family zones in Section 200 of Zoning Bylaw No. 4662 list secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses. Two Whitby Estates overlays still apply on top of zoning — the British Pacific Properties building-scheme plan approval for any new structure, and the District's wildfire/watercourse development permits where they apply. Whether a specific lot can take a detached suite is UNCONFIRMED until the lot's zone, covenants and permit areas are checked. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf and https://illustrator.uservoice.com/assets/243142901/Plan-Approval-Instructions-11.22.18.pdf
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** Plan on a car. No frequent transit runs into the Estates; the nearest major service is Park Royal Exchange via Taylor Way (R2 RapidBus one-seat to Lonsdale Quay, Phibbs Exchange and Metrotown since September 7, 2026, plus Blue Bus routes), and route-level service on the slope is UNVERIFIED. The District's Seniors' Activity Centre in Ambleside runs its own shuttle bus service for seniors — a centre service, not a Whitby Estates bus route. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/ and https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-programs-services/seniors-services
- **Q: Where is the hospital, and if I need care later can I stay in West Vancouver?** The hospital is Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver, serving the whole North Shore. The in-District care ladder is entirely in Ambleside: independent 55+ strata and Kiwanis/PARC rental, retirement residences with assisted living (Amica West Vancouver, Hollyburn House, Amica Lions Gate), then Inglewood Care Centre's 230 publicly funded long-term care beds, allocated through Vancouver Coastal Health. Staying in West Vancouver for care means moving down to Ambleside — there is no care option on the British Properties hillside. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital and https://www.seniorsadvocatebc.ca/quickfacts/location/75989/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The same house can be listed under two sub-area names.** Some Whitby Road homes are marketed under Chartwell and others under Whitby Estates, so a comp set or a saved search built on one name only will miss real Whitby Estates sales. Search both names, plus street searches on Whitby Road, Folkestone Way and Chartwell Drive. (Area research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/whitby-estates.md)
- **There is a second approval office nobody expects.** On top of District permits, any new build, extension, alteration, fence or structure in a British Properties subdivision needs plan approval from British Pacific Properties Limited under the restrictive covenants registered on title — and the covenant set differs by subdivision. A downsizer planning a main-floor addition or a caregiver suite should budget time for that step, not just the District's. https://illustrator.uservoice.com/assets/243142901/Plan-Approval-Instructions-11.22.18.pdf
- **All of the District's seniors infrastructure sits on one flat corridor.** The four Assisted Living Registry entries, the long-term care home, all three verified 55+ strata buildings, the Community Centre and Aquatic Centre (2121 Marine Drive), the Memorial Library (1950 Marine Drive) and the Seniors' Activity Centre (695 21st Street) are all within the Ambleside flat or its edges.
