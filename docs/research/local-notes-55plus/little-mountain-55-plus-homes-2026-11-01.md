# Little Mountain, Chilliwack — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**DISTINCTION, stated as required: Little Mountain is NOT Chilliwack Mountain. Chilliwack Mountain is a separate hillside community on the named mountain on the south side of the Fraser River, west of the downtown core; Little Mountain is the smaller hill immediately east of downtown, anchored by Little Mountain Elementary at 9900 Carleton Street. Listings, schools and hazard rules for the two areas are not interchangeable.**

**Plain finding up front: there is no verified 55+ housing of any kind on Little Mountain. The sub-area's confirmed stock is custom detached hillside houses on view lots. Chilliwack's verified 55+ stock is on the valley floor downtown and in Chilliwack Proper, minutes west — listed in Section 2 for contrast.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. On Little Mountain there is no strata stock for this to apply to — the rule matters to a buyer here only for the valley-floor complexes in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. The bylaw restricts residency, not ownership. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** as of Nov 24, 2022; stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Hillside rules are the rules that actually bind a Little Mountain buyer.** The Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 5500, Schedule D includes D-2 Hillside Environment and D-5 Hillside Development permit areas — hillside lots carry development-permit and geotechnical diligence that valley-floor lots do not. Confirm the specific lot's permit areas with the City before purchase. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **City plan: Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 5500** presents the long-term vision and growth strategy for the whole city, including long-term growth strategies for residential (urban, rural and hillside), commercial, industrial and institutional uses. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000** sets Chilliwack's urban residential zones, including the R1-A (Urban Residential) Zone with a minimum lot area of 500 m2 on community water and sewer and a minimum lot width of 15 m on the same services, and the R1-C (Urban Infill) Zone with a minimum lot area of 300 m2 on an interior lot and 360 m2 on a corner lot. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **SSMUH:** Chilliwack's small-scale multi-unit housing amendments are in Bylaw No. 5395, adopted June 18, 2024. The Province later granted Chilliwack an extension for part of the Proper neighbourhood and surrounding Hazel Park area, with a new compliance date of Dec. 31, 2030 for that area — SSMUH timing is not uniform across the city. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0178-001534
- **Property taxes (2026, as published by the City):** notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; a 5% penalty applies for taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% for taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. The Province's regular property tax deferment program is for people 55 and older. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ housing on Little Mountain itself?** No — none was verified in any source. The hill's confirmed stock is detached hillside houses; Chilliwack's verified 55+ complexes are on the valley floor downtown and in Chilliwack Proper. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is "Mountainview Estates" on Little Mountain?** No. Mountainview Estates (46966 Yale Road) is a genuine 55+ complex, but it sits on Yale Road in Chilliwack Proper East — a valley-floor address. The name refers to the view, not the sub-area. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/mountain-view-estates-46966-yale-road-chilliwack-east
- **Q: What extra diligence does a hillside lot need?** The OCP's hillside permit areas — D-2 Hillside Environment and D-5 Hillside Development (Schedule D of Bylaw No. 5500) — put development-permit and slope/geotechnical review into hillside purchases. Confirm the lot's permit areas and any registered geotechnical reports with the City and on title before removing subjects. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Q: Can I add a suite to a Little Mountain house for a caregiver or family member?** Chilliwack's SSMUH amendments (Bylaw No. 5395, adopted June 18, 2024) brought small-scale multi-unit housing into the city's single-residential zones — subject to the lot's zone and any hillside permit area on it. Confirm the specific lot with the City. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0178-001534
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata downtown?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption), with further defined exemptions for children and live-in caregivers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Chilliwack General Hospital, 45600 Menholm Rd, at the foot of the hill's west side — a Fraser Health community hospital with emergency and acute care. The Chilliwack Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at #104 - 7955 Evans Road. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/Chilliwack-Urgent-and-Primary-Care-Centre
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool (for the valley-floor buildings)?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or a buyer who purchases without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes at 55+ on a hillside house?** The Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Two different hills share almost the same name, and the listings cross them.** Little Mountain is the small hill east of downtown (Little Mountain Elementary, 9900 Carleton St); Chilliwack Mountain is the separate community on the south side of the Fraser River, west of downtown. A "mountain" 55+ claim has to be pinned to a civic address before it means anything — Mountainview Estates is on neither hill. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/little-mountain.md and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/mountain-view-estates-46966-yale-road-chilliwack-east
- **The hill sits inside the OCP's hillside permit-area framework.** Schedule D of the Chilliwack 2050 OCP (Bylaw No. 5500) carries both a Hillside Environment (D-2) and a Hillside Development (D-5) permit area — the paperwork layer behind the view lots. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **A subdivision name on the hill could not be verified.** "Shannon Heights" appears in a live listing for the hill, but no official or developer source confirmed it in the research pass — UNVERIFIED, and not used as a place name in these notes. https://jaredgibbons.ca/little-mountain.html
- **Chartwell Birchwood's own location copy names the view.** The downtown retirement residence (45650 Patten Avenue) describes views of Mt. Cheam, the Eastern Hillsides and Little Mountain from the valley floor — the care anchor for the area sits below the hill, on Patten Avenue near the Landing Leisure Centre and Chilliwack General Hospital. https://chartwell.com/bc/chilliwack/birchwood/senior-townhouses and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f[0]=city:Castlegar&f[1]=city:Chilliwack&f[2]=city:Nanaimo&f[3]=residence_type:Seniors and Persons with Disabilities&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=desc
