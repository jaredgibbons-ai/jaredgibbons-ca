## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **No age bylaw can attach to farmland itself.** An age restriction in BC is created by a strata corporation's bylaws or by a manufactured-home park's rules. A Langley farm parcel is freehold land: nothing in its title, zoning or ALR status makes it "55+." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **ALR housing caps are the rules that actually bind a 55+ farm buyer.** Land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence permitted without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only when it is located entirely within the principal residence; local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **The second-dwelling rule is the ageing-in-place rule on a farm.** The ALC permits one additional detached residence without an ALC application where its conditions are met — a maximum total floor area of 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less, and 186 m² on properties more than 40 ha — and Township bylaws must also permit it. That is the legal route to a caregiver's or family member's dwelling on a Langley farm; anything larger needs an ALC application. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata in the ALR runs through ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6** — the governing reference for the ALR strata frame (settled position in this program); the bulletin's detailed strata conditions were not re-read for this file, so no strata condition beyond that reference is stated — detail UNVERIFIED. Source: batch-15 research file for this topic.
- **Township zoning frame:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 applies across the Township; s.102 defines a coach house as an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage on a lot. A general minimum parcel size for the rural (RU) zones from direct bylaw zone text, and a farm home plate rule, are UNVERIFIED in this file. Source: batch-15 research file for this topic.
- **Farm classification is a continuing obligation, not a one-time label.** BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income requirements at farm gate prices are $2,500 if the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha, and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha, with farm classification applications due by October 31. A downsizing buyer who stops producing can lose the classification and its tax treatment. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Water and septic are diligence items with legal teeth:** under the Water Sustainability Act, domestic groundwater use does not need a licence, but non-domestic use including irrigation requires a water licence, and well records are searched in the provincial GWELLS registry; a home not connected to a community sewer must use an onsite sewage disposal system that complies with the Sewerage System Regulation, and only an Authorized Person can design, install, repair, maintain or inspect a sewerage system. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Flood and dykes:** the Township's documented dyke system is on the Fraser River at the Salmon River mouth, and the Township directs applicants to check a property's designations and watercourses in GeoSource; Township-wide floodplain mapping and parcel-level floodplain status are UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/salmon-river-dike-upgrades.aspx
- **The strata age-bylaw rules, for the record — with no farm-belt strata to apply them to.** Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; age bylaws below 55 invalid since Nov 24, 2022, under Bill 44); the bylaw restricts residency, not ownership; Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19; and residential rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, while short-term restrictions remain allowed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Property transfer tax (general tiers):** 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% on the portion above $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% above $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. How PTT and GST apportion on a specific Langley farm purchase is UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

Do not state quota facts from this file. Source: batch-15 research file for this topic.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ farm community or seniors farm complex in Langley?** No — none was verified on any source. Farm parcels are overwhelmingly freehold, strata farm product is not a Langley pattern, and ALR residences cannot be stratified without ALC permission. The 15 verified 55+ ownership complexes in Langley are urban strata, manufactured-home parks and one co-op in Aldergrove, Brookswood, Willoughby, Walnut Grove, Fort Langley and Langley City. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: How much of Langley is in the Agricultural Land Reserve?** The Township of Langley states 235 square kilometres of the Township is within the Agricultural Land Reserve; check a specific parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission maps by PID or civic address. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: I'm 55+ and buying a farm to downsize onto. What limits the house I can build?** On ALR land, a principal residence built without an ALC application is capped at 500 m² total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. Confirm the parcel's ALR status on the ALC maps before designing anything. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can my adult child or a caregiver live in a second dwelling on the farm?** The ALC permits one additional residence without an application where its size rules are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels, detached — and Township bylaws must also permit it. There is no strata-style caregiver exemption here because there is no strata; the land rules are the whole answer. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: If I stop farming, do I keep farm-class taxes?** Not automatically: BC Assessment requires a producing farm to keep meeting minimum gross farm income thresholds that scale with the operation's size ($2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that; $10,000 under 0.8 ha), with applications due by October 31. Plan the production, or lease the land to someone who produces, before assuming the classification survives. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Is the big manufactured-home community on Fraser Highway a seniors park?** The large one, Langley Grove Estates in Otter District, is explicitly not: its listings state no age restrictions and all ages welcome. The Aldergrove park on Fraser Highway states the same. A rural highway address does not make a park a 55+ park. https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/24330-fraser-highway/52
- **Q: Can I buy a farm parcel and later strata-title a seniors rancher development on it?** No source supports that route: ALR residences cannot be stratified without ALC permission (Information Bulletin 09, s.6), and a non-ALR rural parcel would still need Township zoning and subdivision approval — no such 55+ farm development exists or was proposed in any source checked. Treat any pitch of that kind as UNCONFIRMED until the ALC and Township positions are in writing. Source: batch-15 research file for this topic.
- **Q: Do I need a water licence for the farm?** Domestic groundwater use is exempt; non-domestic use, including irrigation, requires a licence under the Water Sustainability Act. Search GWELLS for the parcel's well record — older wells may not appear in the registry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- **Q: Is there a median price for Langley farms?** No. SnapStats publishes no farm or agricultural-land category, so no farm median, price-per-acre or farm sales count exists in the sources used; the Langley residential figures are community context only, and farm-specific numbers are UNVERIFIED in this file. The feed's 211 count is a reading-day snapshot, not a statistic. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-farms-for-sale-acreages-agricultural-land-mls.html
- **Q: How do the rural sub-areas actually trade?** In the September 2026 residential rows: Salmon River recorded 22 detached listings / 5 solds (a 23% sales ratio, the second-highest in the Langley detached sub-area set), while Campbell Valley (16 / 0), County Line Glen Valley (0 / 1) and Otter District (2 / 0) recorded 0 or 1 detached solds — residential rows for rural districts, not farm figures, and too thin in three of the four to support a trend claim. (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, as recorded in the batch-15 research file.)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **235 km² is the context for everything on this page.** That is the area of the Township the Township itself states is in the Agricultural Land Reserve — most Langley farm parcels are reserve land with ALC housing and use rules layered over Township zoning, which is why "55+ farm" product does not exist here in strata form. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx
- **Read this feed critically: it is 211 listings wide.** The first 12 cards alone included residential acreages in four rural sub-areas, a Brookswood acreage and an urban Willoughby Heights court-ordered land listing — the filter admits far more than working farms, so each candidate's zoning, ALR status and permitted uses have to be confirmed independently rather than inferred from its presence on a farms page. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-farms-for-sale-acreages-agricultural-land-mls.html
- **The suite rule on ALR land is narrower than in town.** In the Township's urban areas a suite or accessory unit is a zoning question; on ALR land a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence, and a detached second dwelling is capped at 90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size. Buyers transferring an urban "coach house" assumption onto a farm parcel will over-read their options. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **The farm belt's one big manufactured-home community markets itself on the opposite promise.** Langley Grove Estates sells "all ages welcome" on a rural Fraser Highway site in Otter District — the belt's manufactured-home supply is aimed at families and downsizers alike, with no age gate. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3111178-150-3665-244-street-langley-british-columbia-v2z1n1-210000/brochure
- **A Langley farm listing can carry a manufactured home without being a "mobile home" purchase.** Parcels pairing a manufactured home with barns and acreage are priced on land value — the dwelling is incidental, the ALR land is the asset, and none of the park-tenancy or strata-age analysis applies. Source: batch-15 research file for this topic.
- **The Township's own seniors-housing tools never touch the belt.** Langley's verified seniors product — its 55+ strata, its Brookswood parks, its subsidized Langley Lions campus in Langley City — is entirely urban, and no seniors-housing project on farm land was verified under any Township tool in any source checked. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Salmon River is the rural exception in the sales data.** Its 23% detached sales ratio in September 2026 was the second-highest in the full Langley detached sub-area set (Walnut Grove highest at 30%) — while the other three rural sub-areas recorded 0 or 1 detached solds. A farm buyer comparing "rural Langley" as one market is averaging together districts that did not trade alike that month. (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, as recorded in the batch-15 research file.)
