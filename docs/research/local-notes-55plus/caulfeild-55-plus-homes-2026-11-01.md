## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**No verified 55+ strata, 55+ rental, retirement residence, care home or subsidized seniors building exists inside Caulfeild in any source checked.** Structurally it cannot be a surprise: in the CD86 (Caulfeild Zone) Residential Area A — the former Land Use Contract lands — stratification of buildings or land is prohibited, so that part of Caulfeild is legally detached and freehold in form. The one strata complex verified in the area, Caulfeild Cove, is NOT 55+ (see the Checked list). Nearest District stock is listed below, labelled as outside the sub-area.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why the "rentals not allowed" lines recorded for Caulfeild Cove and Tudor Gardens in building data must be re-read against current law and each building's current bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Caulfeild zoning: CD86 (Caulfeild Zone) in Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010,** set by Amendment Bylaw No. 5321, 2024, replacing the former Caulfeild Land Use Contract. CD86 has Residential Area A, Residential Area B, a Commercial Area and a Parks and Greenspaces Area; the minimum permitted site area for a residential parcel is the site area lawfully registered on the parcel; Residential Area A density is a maximum of three dwelling units per lot, and **stratification of buildings or land is prohibited in Residential Area A** — the zoning reason no 55+ strata can exist in that part of Caulfeild. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Appendix%20B%20-%205321%20Zoning%20Bylaw%20No%204662%202010%20Amendment%20Bylaw%20No%205321%202024.pdf
- **Nearby RS zones (Section 200 of Bylaw No. 4662)** state minimum site areas of 8,094 m² in RS1, 1,625 m² in RS2, 975 m² in RS3, 731 m² in RS4 and 488 m² in RS5, with secondary suites and detached secondary suites among permitted uses; the bylaw's ground-oriented rules allow 4 dwellings per lot, or 6 within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop — outside CD86 Area A, subject to each lot's zone. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf and https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf
- **Heritage:** Lower Caulfeild is a designated Heritage Conservation Area — Francis Caulfeild planned the subdivision from 1899 — and owners within the area need a Heritage Alteration Permit for any work. That permit layer applies to the suite/coach-house "stay in place" route in Lower Caulfeild. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/heritage-conservation/lower-caulfeild-heritage-conservation-area
- **Hazard permits:** an Environmental Development Permit applies within 15 metres of the top of a watercourse bank, a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface, and a Foreshore Development Permit on waterfront/coastal-flood-risk lots — all three can apply in Caulfeild, the foreshore one on Lower Caulfeild waterfront lots. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **District and provincial policy context:** the OCP, Bylaw No. 4985, 2018, names the Caulfeild Village Centre in Policy 2.3.4, and on December 1, 2025 Council adopted OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 5406, 2025, aligning the OCP with the 2024 Interim Housing Needs Report. The District's own reporting states seniors rose from about one in five residents in 2001 to nearly one in three in 2021 (median age 51 in 2021), with the OCP aiming for more seniors and rental housing options — none of it directed at Caulfeild specifically in the sources checked. https://westvancouver.ca/business-development/housing/official-community-plan-housing-policy-update and https://www.ubcm.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/West%20Vancouver.pdf
- **Taxes:** District of West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026. At Caulfeild price points the Province's additional school tax can apply — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. Water and sewer are metered and billed quarterly. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Caulfeild?** None inside the area — no verified 55+ strata exists in Caulfeild, and in the CD86 Residential Area A lands stratification is prohibited outright. The nearest verified 55+ ownership buildings are east in Dundarave and Ambleside: Tudor Gardens, 843 22nd St, and Westview Terrace, 1390 Duchess Ave. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Appendix%20B%20-%205321%20Zoning%20Bylaw%20No%204662%202010%20Amendment%20Bylaw%20No%205321%202024.pdf and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/tudor-gardens-843-22-street
- **Q: Is Caulfeild Cove a seniors complex? It looks like downsizer product.** No age restriction appears in any source checked for Caulfeild Cove (4957 Marine Dr, 30 townhouse units, built 1974): building sources record pets allowed and rentals not allowed, and nothing about age. It is a general-market strata — any age can live there. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/caulfeild-cove-4957-marine-drive
- **Q: Could a Caulfeild strata vote in a 55+ bylaw later?** In BC a strata can adopt a 55+ age bylaw under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1–123.2 — that is the only age bylaw now permitted — but residents already living in a unit when such a bylaw passes are protected as legacy residents under Strata Property Regulation 7.01. Whether Caulfeild Cove has ever considered one is UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata like Tudor Gardens?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: If I stay in my Caulfeild house, can I add a suite or coach house instead of moving?** It depends which side of the zoning line the lot sits on: CD86 Residential Area A allows a maximum of three dwelling units per lot but prohibits stratification (so added units cannot be sold separately); RS-zone lots nearby list secondary suites and detached secondary suites as permitted uses. In Lower Caulfeild, any work also needs a Heritage Alteration Permit. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Appendix%20B%20-%205321%20Zoning%20Bylaw%20No%204662%202010%20Amendment%20Bylaw%20No%205321%202024.pdf and https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/heritage-conservation/lower-caulfeild-heritage-conservation-area
- **Q: Is there a 55+ rental building near Caulfeild?** Yes, in Ambleside: the Kiwanis North Shore Housing Society's West Vancouver buildings (Kiwanis Manor, 959 21st St; The Court, 2151 Gordon Ave; The Cypress, 999 21st St; The Terrace, 975 21st St) are independent-living rentals for 55+, below-market, with income/asset eligibility, preference for North Shore residents, and no pets. Rental only. https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/kiwanis-manor/
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the nearest 55+ options?** Tudor Gardens is recorded as no pets, and Kiwanis seniors communities do not permit pets; Caulfeild Cove (not 55+) records pets allowed. Westview Terrace's pet position is UNCONFIRMED from the sources checked. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/tudor-gardens-843-22-street and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/caulfeild-cove-4957-marine-drive
- **Q: Where is the hospital, and what if I need care later?** Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver, serves the North Shore including West Vancouver. The in-District care ladder is east in Ambleside: registered assisted living at Amica West Vancouver (659 Clyde Ave), Amica Lions Gate (Keith Rd) and Hollyburn House (2095 Marine Dr), then long-term care at Inglewood Care Centre (725 Inglewood Ave, 230 publicly funded beds). None of it is in Caulfeild. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital and https://www.seniorsadvocatebc.ca/quickfacts/location/75989/
- **Q: What transit can a Caulfeild senior actually use?** Route 253 links Caulfeild Village to Park Royal with select trips through to downtown Vancouver over the Lions Gate Bridge; route 262 links the village to Horseshoe Bay and Lions Bay; route 250 runs on Marine Drive. It is bus-served but car-oriented for most daily trips. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/192/tt253.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Caulfeild's zoning literally bans the product this page is about:** since Amendment Bylaw No. 5321, 2024 created CD86, stratification is prohibited in Residential Area A of the old Land Use Contract lands — so no condo, townhouse strata or 55+ complex can be created there, whatever demand does. The area's one strata, Caulfeild Cove on Marine Drive, predates that structure (built 1974) and carries no age bylaw in any source checked. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Appendix%20B%20-%205321%20Zoning%20Bylaw%20No%204662%202010%20Amendment%20Bylaw%20No%205321%202024.pdf and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/caulfeild-cove-4957-marine-drive
- **The Land Use Contract era just ended here:** CD86 replaced the Caulfeild Land Use Contract in 2024 — the area's most recent District-significant land-use change — and Residential Area A's minimum site area is simply whatever was lawfully registered on each parcel, so lot-by-lot history matters more than a zone table. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/development-regulations/contracts
- **Lower Caulfeild's heritage rules are a second permit system:** the designated Heritage Conservation Area (subdivision planned by Francis Caulfeild from 1899) requires a Heritage Alteration Permit for any work — a cost and timeline factor for any senior planning to adapt a Lower Caulfeild house for aging in place. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/heritage-conservation/lower-caulfeild-heritage-conservation-area
- **The whole District has only three buildings a North Shore guide lists as 55+-bylaw strata** — Westview Terrace (1390 Duchess Ave), Tudor Gardens (843 22nd St) and Lincoln Gardens (address not stated on the guide page — UNCONFIRMED here) — all in Ambleside/Dundarave. A Caulfeild downsizer's realistic choice is: one of those, a Kiwanis rental on 21st Street, an Amica/Hollyburn residence, or staying put with a suite and services. https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/on-the-market/55-plus-buildings-west-vancouver/
- **The District's big seniors build is the Inglewood redevelopment, east of here:** on September 21, 2026, Council approved the two-phase redevelopment of Inglewood Care Centre (725 Inglewood Ave) — Phase 1, a 364-bed publicly funded long-term care facility; Phase 2, 200 independent-living units and 161 seniors rental housing units. The District does not handle tenant placement; Baptist Housing does. https://westvancouver.ca/business-development/development-applications/inglewood-campus-care
- **Kiwanis Manor carries two descriptions in two sources** — independent 55+ rental on the society's site, registered assisted living in BC's Assisted Living Registry under a partnership name. Confirm which product it actually offers before describing it to a client. https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/kiwanis-manor/ and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3AArmstrong&f%5B1%5D=city%3AMasset&f%5B2%5D=city%3AWest%20Vancouver&f%5B3%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=asc
- **Both of Caulfeild's schools are inside the area, which keeps family demand — and family pricing — under senior sellers' houses:** Caulfeild Elementary (4685 Keith Rd) and Rockridge Secondary (5350 Headland Dr) are a matched K–12 pair in one pocket, rare in West Vancouver (Altamont and Chelsea Park have no school inside the pocket at all). https://westvancouverschools.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/WVS-District-Directory-Sept-2024.pdf
