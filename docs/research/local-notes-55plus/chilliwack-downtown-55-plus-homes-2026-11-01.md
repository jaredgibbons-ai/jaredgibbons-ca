## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000** is Chilliwack's zoning bylaw. Its Section 2 defines a secondary suite as an accessory dwelling unit of not more than 90 m² and less than 40% of the habitable floor space of the building, located within a building containing only 1 principal dwelling unit — the cap is the lesser of 90 m² and 40%. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Accessory dwelling units:** Section 4.06(1) of the same bylaw permits only 1 ADU per lot where the zone permits an ADU; the listed types are secondary suite, coach house and garden suite, garden suites are capped at a maximum gross floor area of 100 m² by Section 4.06(1)(g), and ADU parking is set at s.5.04 (1 space for a 1-bedroom ADU or any secondary suite; 2 spaces for a 2-or-more-bedroom coach or garden suite). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Suite legality:** no secondary suite is legal unless built with a building permit meeting the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **SSMUH:** Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395 is the City's small-scale multi-unit housing amendment to Bylaw No. 5000, allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing; Bylaw 5395 was adopted June 18, 2024. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **City plan: Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500** is the current city-wide plan; its Schedule B3 is the Downtown Neighbourhood Plan and its Schedule D development permit areas run D-1 Drinking Water Supply through D-10 Industrial, with D-6 Downtown applying in the core — which areas apply to a given downtown parcel beyond D-6 is a per-parcel check: UNVERIFIED for any specific parcel. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Short-term rentals:** the City regulates short-term rental operations under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf
- **Property taxes:** City of Chilliwack property tax notices are mailed annually at the end of May and are due the first working day after July 1 of every calendar year; a 5% late payment penalty is applied for taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% for taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually BUY in Chilliwack Downtown?** Two verified age-restricted strata buildings, both on Spadina Avenue: Ivy Green (45775 Spadina Ave — 24 condos, built 1994, LMS1336, no pets) and Spadina Regency (45765 Spadina Ave — concrete, built 1989, rentals and pets permitted per listings). City-wide the verified roster is 5 strata plus Cottonwood Village park, but the other three strata (Mountainview Estates, Knight Lodge, Cottage Grove) and the park are outside downtown. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Three buildings share Spadina Avenue — are they all 55+?** No. Ivy Green (45775) is 55+ with no pets; Spadina Regency (45765) is 55+ with rentals and pets permitted; Chilliwack Gardens (45749) states "no age restrictions" in its own listing. Check the civic number and the bylaws, not the street. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live at Ivy Green or Spadina Regency?** Yes in a strata — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent, or to move into later?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Spadina Regency says rentals are permitted — in a 55+ building?** That is what its listings state, and it is consistent with current provincial law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022. The age bylaw still governs who may RESIDE there — a tenant must meet the 55+ bylaw or an exemption — and pet rules remain a valid bylaw matter, building by building. Confirm both against the registered bylaws and Form B. https://www.robbiejohal.com/listing/r3101299-45765-spadina-avenue-501-chilliwack-bc-v2p-1t5/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are The Willows or Magnolia Manor 55+ buildings?** UNCONFIRMED. Both names appear in the downtown feed's listing text only, and neither was confirmed as 55+ on a developer, strata or manager source in this research — do not treat either as a 55+ purchase without the strata documents. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-downtown.html
- **Q: Can a downtown house take a suite or a coach house for a caregiver or family?** One accessory dwelling unit per lot where the zone permits it — a secondary suite (capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space), a coach house, or a garden suite (capped at 100 m²) — under Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, ss. 2 and 4.06; a suite must be built with a permit meeting the BC Building and Fire Code and flood protection requirements, and new suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. Downtown parcels also sit inside the Schedule B3 Downtown Neighbourhood Plan and the D-6 Downtown permit area — confirm the parcel's designation with the City before valuing infill potential. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay downtown?** The in-area ladder runs: independent strata (Ivy Green, Spadina Regency) → subsidized independent rental (Jubilee Place, 45440 Hodgins Ave, 36 units, 55+ low-income with veterans emphasis; The Paramount, 46187 Yale Rd, 65+ income-tested) → independent/assisted retirement rental in the hospital precinct (Chartwell Birchwood on Patten Ave, Chartwell Hampton House, 45555 Hodgins Ave, Chartwell Lynnwood, 9168 Corbould St) → registered assisted living (Birchwood and The Cascades, 45586 McIntosh Dr, both class SN) — all within a few blocks of Chilliwack General Hospital at 45600 Menholm Rd. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Why are there no market numbers for this area?** Chilliwack is a CADREB market that SnapStats does not cover, and CADREB publishes statistics board-wide only with no sub-area tables — so any Chilliwack Downtown statistic is UNVERIFIED here rather than borrowed from a neighbouring board. The live page's FAQ claim that its listings update from the Fraser Valley Real Estate Board feed is also incorrect for a CADREB market and is flagged in the research file as a rebuild fix; which feed actually supplies the page is UNVERIFIED. https://www.cadreb.com/market/ and https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-downtown.html
