## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents lawfully living in the unit when the bylaw passed; live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; stratas may still restrict SHORT-TERM rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Where the listings data comes from, in the page's own words:** the footer states the data comes in part from the MLS® Reciprocity program of the Greater Vancouver REALTORS® (GVR), the Fraser Valley Real Estate Board (FVREB) or the Chilliwack and District Real Estate Board (CADREB); listings held by participating firms are marked with the MLS® logo; the representation is based on board-generated data the boards do not warrant for accuracy; and the materials may not be reproduced without the boards' express written consent. https://jaredgibbons.ca/our-listings.html
- **Who may use the MLS® system:** the Canadian Real Estate Association (CREA) owns the MLS® and REALTOR® trademarks; agents and brokers must become members of a local real estate board and CREA to access the MLS® system — described by the Competition Bureau of Canada as a comprehensive database of current and historical listing information — and must adhere to CREA's rules and their local boards' rules. The boards' own display rules are not public documents: UNVERIFIED in the research file beyond this. https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/how-we-foster-competition/promotion-and-advocacy/regulatory-adviceinterventions-competition-bureau/submission-ftc-and-doj-residential-real-estate-brokerage
- **The BC regulator's disclosure rules apply to every enquiry from this page:** BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA) states a licensed BC real estate professional must give consumers information about commissions and fees, must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party; disclosure must happen at the outset of dealings, and dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers

- **Q: If a card doesn't say 55+, can the building still be age-restricted?** Yes, possibly — a card is marketing copy, not the registered bylaws. The check that settles it is the strata's Form B and registered bylaw set for the specific complex, read against the verified list in Section 2. Conversely, a card that says "adult-oriented" without a stated 55+ bylaw is not verification either: sub-55 age bylaws have been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, and only a registered 55+ bylaw restricts residency. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we buy and live in a 55+ unit from this page?** Yes, if the unit is in a verified 55+ strata — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). The same exemption set covers a live-in caregiver, a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: If I enquire on one of these listings without my own agent, who represents me?** No one, until a client relationship is formed. BCFSA requires the listing professional to disclose at the outset whether they can represent you, the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and the risks of dealing unrepresented with the seller's professional; a BC licensee must work for either the buyer or the seller, and dual agency is prohibited except in the rarest of circumstances. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: How current is what I'm seeing?** Check the card's Status line and the feed's own timestamp — the footer read "Data was last updated November 1, 2026 at 03:35 PM (UTC)" at the crawl moment, and the Reciprocity notice says the boards do not warrant the data's accuracy. The introduction's "new listings added daily" is the page's own claim, not a verified update cadence. https://jaredgibbons.ca/our-listings.html
- **Q: Why is a Penticton property on a South Surrey / Langley REALTOR®'s listings page?** The card itself shows how: it is Jared's listing carried under a different board's MLS® numbering (10395824) and listed by ROYAL LEPAGE NORTHSTAR (W.R.). It sits outside the page's own stated scope ("South Surrey, White Rock, Langley & the Fraser Valley"); whether that is deliberate is a question the research file leaves for Jared. https://jaredgibbons.ca/our-listings.html
- **Q: Can I rent out a 55+ unit I buy from this page?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
