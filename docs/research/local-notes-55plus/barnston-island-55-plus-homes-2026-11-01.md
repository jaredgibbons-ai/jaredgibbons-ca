# Barnston Island — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**GEOGRAPHY FLAG — read first:** Barnston Island is NOT in the City of Surrey, despite "Surrey" mailing addresses. Most of the island is unincorporated Metro Vancouver Electoral Area A; the southeast portion is Barnston Island Indian Reserve No. 3 (Katzie First Nation). Planning, permits and taxes run through Metro Vancouver / the Province, not Surrey City Hall. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Barnston Island is a Fraser River island between Surrey (Port Kells) on the south bank and Pitt Meadows across the main channel; there is no bridge — the only access is the free Barnston Island Ferry across Parson's Channel from 104 Ave in Port Kells. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry
- The island's main road is its perimeter dike road — the dike rings the island and doubles as the transportation route; Dyke Road addresses run around it (the SD36 locator test range was 2–328 Dyke Rd). https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
- The ferry is a barge-and-tug provincial inland ferry, free, capacity 5 vehicles and 50 passengers, running on demand roughly 6:15 a.m.–11:55 p.m. (to 12:55 a.m. weekends). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry
- Metro Vancouver's Barnston Island Regional Park provides riverfront rest areas on the island; there are no shops, services or recreation facilities on the island itself — all services are in Surrey (Port Kells/Fleetwood) or Pitt Meadows, via the ferry. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
- Population is roughly 150 residents, in farmhouses and rural single-detached homes on farm parcels; the island has been farmed since the mid-1800s. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
- School District 36 (Surrey) serves the island; the SD36 locator for the island test address 52 Dyke Road (range 2–328 Dyke Rd) returned Bothwell Elementary and École Fraser Heights — island children cross by ferry to school. Port Kells Elementary serves landing-side Port Kells addresses, not island addresses. (SD36 School Locator, mybaragar.com, run 2026-11-01, recorded in the area research file.)
- Where 55+ housing concentrates: **nowhere — no source found places any 55+ complex, seniors building, retirement residence, care facility or manufactured-home park on Barnston Island.** The housing stock is freehold farm parcels plus Katzie IR 3 reserve lands, which are federal reserve land and not fee-simple market housing. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. Note this is largely academic on Barnston Island, where no strata product was identified. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Planning authority: Metro Vancouver, not a municipality.** The Electoral Area A Official Community Plan (adopted by the Metro Vancouver Board in July 2018) applies to Barnston Island and expressly does NOT apply to First Nations Reserves; its vision for the island is a thriving agricultural community. Metro Vancouver also holds the zoning for Electoral Area A, including a Barnston Island zoning map (Map 12). https://metrovancouver.org/services/electoral-area-a/official-community-plan and https://metrovancouver.org/services/electoral-area-a/zoning
- **ALR rules dominate this island.** The island is predominantly farmland — Metro Vancouver's 2019 Barnston Island Agricultural Viability Study was commissioned because agriculture is the island's land base and economy — so ALR status controls subdivision, non-farm use and building on most parcels; pull the ALC map for a specific parcel before writing an offer. Exact ALR percentage of the island: UNCONFIRMED. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
- **Suite rules:** no source states a Barnston-specific secondary-suite regime separate from the Electoral Area A OCP/zoning framework and ALR rules above; on ALR land a suite's permissibility turns on the parcel's ALR status — parcel-specific answer UNCONFIRMED. https://metrovancouver.org/services/electoral-area-a/zoning
- **Rural property taxes (Province):** as unincorporated Electoral Area A land, taxes are collected under the provincial rural system (Surveyor of Taxes), with Metro Vancouver and school levies on top. The Province's published rural dates: notice mailed May 31, deadline July 2, a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance July 3, and an additional 5% penalty on November 1. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/important-dates and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area
- **Flood construction:** the whole island is low Fraser River floodplain behind a perimeter dike, and Metro Vancouver publishes a Barnston Island Flood Construction Level Study — flood construction levels for a specific parcel are UNCONFIRMED until checked against that study. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf and https://metrovancouver.org/services/electoral-area-a/resources

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ housing on Barnston Island I can buy?** No. No source found places any 55+ strata, age-restricted complex, seniors rental, retirement residence or manufactured-home park on the island; the market stock is freehold farm parcels, plus Katzie IR 3 reserve land that is not market housing. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
- **Q: Is Barnston Island part of Surrey?** No. It uses Surrey mailing addresses, but the island is unincorporated Metro Vancouver Electoral Area A, plus the Katzie First Nation's Barnston Island IR No. 3 — services, taxes, building permits and planning run through Metro Vancouver and the Province, not Surrey City Hall. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
- **Q: How would I get to medical care or a hospital?** There are no medical or care facilities on the island in any source checked; every trip starts with the free 5-minute ferry to Port Kells (on demand within operating hours, roughly 6:15 a.m.–11:55 p.m., to 12:55 a.m. weekends), then by road. Overnight, outside ferry hours, there is no vehicle access — a fact a senior buyer must weigh directly. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry
- **Q: If BC only allows 55+ strata bylaws now, could an island property still be "45+"?** A 45+ strata age bylaw has been invalid province-wide since Nov 24, 2022 — only 55+ bylaws survive, with the Regulation 7.01 exemptions. In any case no strata exists on the island in the sources checked, so the question does not arise here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I buy a small lot and build a retirement home?** Most of the island is agricultural land under an OCP vision of a farming community; building is tied to farm parcels and ALR rules, so a parcel-by-parcel check with Metro Vancouver and the Agricultural Land Commission is essential before assuming a building lot exists. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
- **Q: Who collects my property tax?** The Province, under the rural system — rural owners receive their notice from the Surveyor of Taxes and pay the Province, with a July 2 deadline, 5% penalty July 3 and a further 5% on November 1; Metro Vancouver and school levies ride on top. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/important-dates
- **Q: Is the island in a floodplain?** Yes — it is a low Fraser River island protected by a perimeter dike that also serves as its main road, and Metro Vancouver maintains a flood construction level study for the island; confirm dike condition, flood construction level and insurance availability for the specific parcel early. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
- **Q: Where is the nearest 55+ housing, then?** Off-island, in Surrey or Pitt Meadows — no source in this research names a specific nearest complex, so any named "nearest" building is UNCONFIRMED here; what is verified is that reaching it means a ferry crossing first, every time. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry
- **Q: Does the ferry cost anything or run all night?** It is free, and it does not run all night: on demand roughly 6:15 a.m.–11:55 p.m., extended to 12:55 a.m. on weekends, with capacity of 5 vehicles and 50 passengers, and crossings can be interrupted by river traffic. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The "(Surrey)" label is a postal convention only (verified 2026-11-01 research).** No part of the island is inside the City of Surrey, it does not appear as a City of Surrey neighbourhood, and no Fraser Valley board sub-area named "Barnston Island" was found in SnapStats FVR reporting — a page that calls it a Surrey neighbourhood would be wrong. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
- **The dike is the road (Metro Vancouver agriculture study, 2019).** The perimeter dike doubles as the island's main transportation route, so flood infrastructure and daily driving are literally the same asset. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
- **The island's school answer was corrected by an actual locator run (Nov 1, 2026).** An earlier document-based reading pointed to Port Kells Elementary; the SD36 locator run for 52 Dyke Road returned Bothwell Elementary and École Fraser Heights for island addresses — Port Kells Elementary serves the landing side, not the island. (SD36 School Locator, run 2026-11-01, recorded in the area research file.)
- **Fraser Valley Regional Library serves island residents under a Metro Vancouver arrangement** — Metro Vancouver publishes a library-information sheet specifically for Barnston Island residents, a small sign of how services here are stitched together across jurisdictions rather than delivered by a city. https://metrovancouver.org/services/electoral-area-a/resources
- **There is no bus service on the island and no bridge** — the ferry is the single connection, which is why the complete absence of on-island seniors services (Section 2) matters more here than in any mainland area: a resident who stops driving has no local fallback. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry
