# False Creek (Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "False Creek" is the Paragon sub-area name. It is NOT one of the City of Vancouver's 22 official local areas: the City treats its parts separately — False Creek North, on the north shore inside the Downtown local area; False Creek South, on the south shore inside the Fairview local area; and the False Creek Flats, at the east end, listed within the Strathcona local area and planned under the False Creek Flats Plan. A board search on "False Creek" crosses three City local areas (Downtown, Fairview, Strathcona), so City documents must always be read by part — North, South or Flats. A single boundary for the Paragon name: UNCONFIRMED. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- Boundaries of the parts, as the City states them: False Creek South is between the Cambie and Burrard Street bridges on the south shore, excluding Granville Island and Sen̓áḵw. The False Creek Flats Plan study area runs from Main Street to the west, Prior and Venables Streets to the north, Clark Drive to the east, and Great Northern Way to the south. The City's Coastal Adaptation Plan states False Creek North includes portions of Northeast False Creek, Yaletown and Chinatown, and False Creek South passes through Fairview, Southeast False Creek, Olympic Village and the False Creek Flats along the south shore. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/false-creek-south.aspx
- Main streets: Pacific Boulevard and Expo Boulevard serve the north shore; West 2nd Avenue and West 6th Avenue serve the south shore; Terminal Avenue and Great Northern Way serve the east end; the Burrard Street, Granville Street and Cambie Street bridges cross the water. The Northeast False Creek area is bisected by the Georgia and Dunsmuir viaducts. https://vancouver.ca/images/web/northeast-false-creek-refined-area-plan.pdf
- Schools: Vancouver School Board (SD39) catchments are address-based and differ by shore — never apply a north-shore result to a south-shore address. Located around the water: Crosstown šxʷwəq̓ʷəθət Elementary, 55 Expo Boulevard, and Elsie Roy Elementary, 150 Drake Street, on the north shore (listed by VSB under Downtown), and False Creek Elementary, 900 School Green, on the south shore in False Creek South. A public secondary school located on either shoreline: UNCONFIRMED. SD39 has no middle-school tier (K–7 / 8–12). https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods and https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
- Parks and waterfront: Charleson Park, 999 Charleson Street, sits on the south shore seawall, and the False Creek seawall runs on both shores — the City's Coastal Adaptation Plan describes the area around False Creek as parks used for living, working, recreation and marine activities. A Vancouver Public Library branch located on the False Creek shoreline: UNCONFIRMED. https://covapp.vancouver.ca/parkfinder/parkdetail.aspx?inparkid=43
- Health: Vancouver General Hospital, 899 West 12th Avenue, sits south of the south shore in Fairview, and St. Paul's Hospital, 1081 Burrard Street, sits north of the north shore in Downtown; the new St. Paul's Hospital campus is planned at Station Street in the False Creek Flats at the east end. The one care home on the shoreline itself is Yaletown House on the north shore (Section 2). https://www.vch.ca/en/location/vancouver-general-hospital and https://www.providencehealthcare.org/en/future-sites-projects/new-st-pauls-hospital
- Transit: TransLink route 50 (Waterfront Station / False Creek South) runs from Waterfront Station via Chinatown, Granville Island and the False Creek south shore to Olympic Village Station on the Canada Line, with buses continuing as route 15. Granville Island, on the south shore at the west end, is named by the City on its Fairview page as one of Vancouver's attractions inside Fairview. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt050.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/fairview.aspx
- Where the seniors housing actually is: thin, and split by shore. On the north shore, one long-term care home in Yaletown (False Creek North). On the south shore, the City's own lease inventory counts non-market seniors rental as a category in False Creek South (four buildings / 319 units across the non-market rental and non-market seniors rental classes combined — see Section 2), and BC Housing's directory lists several Fairview-section buildings at Olympic Village and Spyglass / Sitka addresses. No 55+ ownership building was verified on either shore (Section 2).

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**No verified 55+ strata (ownership) stock exists in False Creek.** No age-restricted ownership building appears in any source checked on either shore. The trap in this sub-area is tenure, not age: a large share of the south-shore stock a downsizer will be shown is leasehold (strata leasehold, co-op or non-market rental on City-held land), which is a different purchase from freehold strata and still carries no 55+ bylaw in any source checked.

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- None verified in False Creek. If a listing calls a False Creek building "55+," treat the age claim as UNCONFIRMED until the registered strata bylaws say so — and check the tenure (freehold vs strata leasehold) before the age claim, because on the south shore the tenure is the fact that changes the deal.

### Verified 55+ rental (not ownership)

- No stand-alone market-rental 55+ apartment building was verified on either shore — the rental-form seniors stock is the subsidized and non-market entries below.

Admission is through the long-term care system — you cannot buy or rent a suite here. https://travelclinic.vch.ca/en/location/yaletown-house and https://www.yaletown.org/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **How the City creates new 55+ rental:** Vancouver's Seniors Housing Rezoning Policy (approved July 23, 2024; last amended May 19, 2026) treats "Social Housing (where occupancy is restricted to older adults 55+)" as social housing secured by a housing agreement restricting occupancy to residents aged 55 or older, owned and operated by a non-profit or government, and secures seniors supportive or independent living housing as rental — no stratification, no separate sales covenants — for 60 years or the life of the building, whichever is greater. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-rezoning-seniors-housing.pdf
- **False Creek South land tenure — the defining rule file on the south shore:** the City holds title to 80 acres of land in False Creek South (its leases page states the City, through its Property Endowment Fund, owns 80% of the land there); when the neighbourhood was built in the 1970s and 1980s, much of the City land was leased via 60-year ground leases, with most expiring between 2036 and 2046, and no new housing has been built in False Creek South since the 1980s. In March 2024 Council directed staff to report back with an updated and enhanced False Creek South development framework, and in summer 2025 the City conducted technical investigations in the neighbourhood. The lease terms and end date for a specific building: UNCONFIRMED — they must be read off that building's own lease documents. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/false-creek-south.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/false-creek-south-leases-on-city-land.aspx
- **Zoning:** there is no single district schedule for False Creek — the Southeast False Creek / Olympic Village lands and the False Creek North lands sit under site-specific CD-1 Comprehensive Development districts and Official Development Plans, including the False Creek North Official Development Plan; FM-1 applies on the Fairview Slopes above the south shore, permitting single detached houses, duplexes and multiple dwellings with a maximum floor space ratio of 0.60. The zone for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/northeast-false-creek-play-and-work.aspx
- **Plans:** the Northeast False Creek Plan proposes a mixed-use neighbourhood with new and renewed parks, childcare, social housing, artist space, plazas and a cultural centre, and replacement of the viaducts with an at-grade street network; Southeast False Creek is being developed by the City as a sustainable community including the Olympic Village; the False Creek Flats Plan covers the east-end employment lands from Main Street to Clark Drive. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/false-creek-south.aspx
- **Tax calendar:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after each due date, plus the annual Empty Homes Tax property status declaration (2025 payment due April 16, 2026). On leasehold buildings, how City property tax is billed follows the building's lease — confirm for the specific building: UNCONFIRMED. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in False Creek?** None that any source verifies. No age-restricted strata building was found on either shore — a False Creek buyer who wants an age-restricted community buys in another neighbourhood, and a buyer set on the seawall buys a general-market strata unit (checking tenure first on the south shore) whose bylaws to check are its short-term rental and pet bylaws, not an age bylaw. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is a False Creek South home freehold?** Often not. The City owns 80% of the land in False Creek South; homes there sit in strata leasehold buildings (12 buildings / 669 units), co-ops (seven buildings / 517 units) and non-market rental on 60-year ground leases, most expiring between 2036 and 2046. Financing, pricing and remaining lease term all turn on the specific building's lease — read it before subjects, not after. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/false-creek-south-leases-on-city-land.aspx
- **Q: What seniors housing does exist around False Creek?** One care home and a non-market band: Yaletown House (1099 Cambie Street, False Creek North, 127 subsidized long-term care beds, built 1985); the City-counted non-market seniors rental class in False Creek South (within four buildings / 319 units combined with non-market rental, unnamed in the City source); the BC Housing directory's Olympic Village and False Creek South shore buildings (Olympic Village Parcels 5 and 9, Strathearn Court, Van Coevorden Court); and, in construction, the mixed affordable building at 95 Nelson Street including homes for seniors. All rental or care — nothing to buy. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/false-creek-south-leases-on-city-land.aspx and https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-6-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a BC 55+ strata elsewhere?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in the defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. Under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a 55+ parent can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a False Creek unit, or run it as a short-term rental?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Short-term rentals are the exception that survives: a strata can still restrict them by bylaw. On a strata leasehold unit, the ground lease itself is the prior document to check before any rental plan. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is flooding a real factor for False Creek homes?** Yes — the lands around False Creek are inside the City's designated floodplain system; the City's False Creek Coastal Adaptation Plan covers the floodplain area encompassing False Creek North, False Creek South, Granville Island and the Flats, and parcels in the floodplain carry Vancouver Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements. A specific parcel's status and a specific building's flood construction level: UNCONFIRMED — check both in due diligence, including the strata's insurance position. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Q: Which shore should an older buyer choose?** On the sourced facts they are different products: the north shore is Downtown (Crosstown and Elsie Roy elementaries, CD-1 / False Creek North ODP, Yaletown House care home); the south shore is Fairview (False Creek Elementary, leasehold tenure in False Creek South, the non-market seniors rental class); the east end is the Flats (employment lands, the planned new St. Paul's campus at Station Street). Catchments, tenure and plans all differ by shore — never quote one shore's facts for the other. https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods and https://www.providencehealthcare.org/en/future-sites-projects/new-st-pauls-hospital
- **Q: What transit serves the south shore and Granville Island?** TransLink route 50 runs from Waterfront Station via Chinatown, Granville Island and the False Creek south shore to Olympic Village Station on the Canada Line, with buses continuing as route 15 — the south shore's one-seat ride to the downtown hub. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt050.pdf
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay around False Creek?** At the long-term care step, yes on the north shore — Yaletown House is on the water in False Creek North, with admission through the Vancouver Coastal Health assessment process. The False Creek South care facilities described in aggregate by the neighbourhood association (an assisted-living facility and supportive care homes) are UNCONFIRMED name-by-name in this pass, so no specific south-shore care step is promised here. https://travelclinic.vch.ca/en/location/yaletown-house and http://www.falsecreeksouth.org/replan/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **False Creek South is a 1970s social experiment whose leases are now the story:** the City states the neighbourhood was built by transforming industrial land into a waterfront neighbourhood known for its open space and intentional design, with roughly a third each of co-op, strata and non-profit rental housing on leased City land — and no new housing built there since the 1980s. The lease expiry window (most between 2036 and 2046) and the City's updated development framework (Council direction March 2024; technical investigations summer 2025) will decide what this community is next. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/false-creek-south.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/false-creek-south-leases-on-city-land.aspx
- **"False Creek" is three City geographies wearing one board name:** North inside Downtown, South inside Fairview, Flats inside Strathcona. Every City document — school, plan, flood, tenure — must be read by part, and a listing that just says "False Creek" has not yet said anything a buyer can rely on. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- **Yaletown House is older than most of the condos around it:** VCH records the care home was built in 1985 in False Creek North — a four-storey crescent around a secure courtyard garden with 127 subsidized beds — predating the Expo-lands residential build-out around it. https://travelclinic.vch.ca/en/location/yaletown-house
- **The Northeast False Creek Plan requires flood protection as a condition of the new neighbourhood:** new developments there must be built to protect homes and critical infrastructure from flooding and rising sea levels, with a continuous line of flood protection across the site — plan-area buildings and older shoreline buildings are not carrying the same flood file. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **The next seniors-relevant supply on the creek is non-market and mixed, not 55+ strata:** the 95 Nelson Street building (283 non-market homes for seniors, families, individuals and people with disabilities, Brightside-operated, City-anticipated completion 2029) and its 450 Pacific and 1010 Expo companion sites are the False Creek North affordable program — rental homes that include seniors, secured as non-market, with nothing stratified for sale. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/affordable-homes-childcare-false-creek-dec-2025.aspx
