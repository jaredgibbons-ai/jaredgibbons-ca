## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Walnut Grove is a planned community and FVREB sub-area in the Township of Langley (not the City of Langley), north of Highway 1 across from Willoughby Heights, planned since 1979 under its own Township community plan — the Walnut Grove Community Plan, Bylaw 1978 No. 1836, adopted November 26, 1979, now Schedule 6 to the 2025 OCP Update (Bylaw No. 6200) per the guide page. The street-level board boundary is UNVERIFIED — no public street-level boundary was opened in the research pass. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/resources/get-involved/ocp-update/ATT-F---Walnut-Grove-CP---Redlined.pdf and https://jaredgibbons.ca/walnut-grove-guide.html
- The guide page describes an established, self-contained community with shops, movie theatres and the Walnut Grove Community Centre, with the Golden Ears Bridge connecting it north across the Fraser River. https://jaredgibbons.ca/walnut-grove-guide.html
- Where the 55+ stock actually sits: a townhouse belt along the 88 Avenue / 208 Street / 212 Street corridor in the community's core — Churchill Park (9208 208 St), Spencer Green (21138 88 Ave), Chartwell Green (8737 212 St) and Chelsea Green (20391 96 Ave) — plus the Forest Green Estates manufactured-home park at 9080 198 Street at the community's west edge, by Highway 1 (flagged in Section 2: listing-verified as 55+, absent from the settled Langley roster). Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Board tempo: Walnut Grove's own SnapStats row for September 2026 is Detached 43 active / 13 sold / 30% — the highest published detached ratio in the Langley community that month — and Condos & Townhomes 55 / 10 / 18%. No Walnut Grove-specific median is published; the Langley community medians ($1,345,000 detached; $611,000 attached) are whole-Langley figures (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report).
- Recreation core: Walnut Grove Community Centre is at 8889 Walnut Grove Drive, a Township community / recreation centre; the guide page describes a 50 m pool with waterslide, diving boards and dive tower, a leisure pool, a fitness centre, gymnasium, preschool and the Walnut Grove library branch at the same address. https://www.tol.ca/en/parks-recreation/walnut-grove-community-centre.aspx and https://jaredgibbons.ca/walnut-grove-guide.html
- Schools confirmed against the BC Ministry K-12 file, by name and street address in Walnut Grove (all SD35): James Kennedy Elementary, 9060 212 St; West Langley Elementary, 9403 212 St; Alex Hope Elementary, 21150 85 Ave; Dorothy Peacock Elementary, 20292 91a Ave; Gordon Greenwood Elementary, 9175 206 St; Topham Elementary, 21555 91 Ave; Walnut Grove Secondary, 8919 Walnut Grove Dr. Catchments are address-based and UNVERIFIED for any specific Walnut Grove address — no SD35 locator run for a Walnut Grove address is on record in this program.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. Read listing wordings like Chelsea Green's "only one occupant to be 55+" against these exemptions: a listing summary is not the bylaw, and the bylaw cannot remove the regulation's exemptions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **A park's 55+ rule is a park rule, not a strata bylaw.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003) recognises a park where every site is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 and allows a park landlord to refuse an assignment where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement — the frame that would govern Forest Green Estates, whose own park rules were not obtained in this pass. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Community plan: the Walnut Grove Community Plan is Bylaw 1978 No. 1836**, adopted November 26, 1979, carried as Schedule 6 to the 2025 OCP Update (Bylaw No. 6200) per the guide page; the Township's OCP update is revising community plans including this one. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/resources/get-involved/ocp-update/ATT-F---Walnut-Grove-CP---Redlined.pdf and https://jaredgibbons.ca/walnut-grove-guide.html
- **SSMUH (Township): Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 Amendment (SSMUH) Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024** — eligible lots (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, on both municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area) may have 3 units if 280 m² or smaller, or 4 units if between 280 m² and 4,050 m²; lots larger than 4,050 m² are not eligible, and there is no 6-unit transit tier in the Township. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **GST on a new home, where a buyer is looking at new product instead:** the CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 records that Bill C-4 received royal assent on March 13, 2026, introducing the first-time home buyers' GST/HST rebate — 100% of the GST on a new or substantially renovated home valued up to $1 million, gradually reduced between $1 million and $1.5 million, and none at $1.5 million or more — alongside the long-standing GST/HST new housing rebate (36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phased out between $350,000 and $450,000). Walnut Grove's 55+ stock is resale product, so GST will rarely arise inside it; the figures are stated here for the new-home comparison only. HBP and FHSA figures: UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Township taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies at midnight July 2, 2026, and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Walnut Grove?** Four verified strata townhouse complexes: Churchill Park (9208 208 St, 147 units), Spencer Green (21138 88 Ave, 97 units), Chartwell Green (8737 212 St) and Chelsea Green (20391 96 Ave). Beyond those, Forest Green Estates (9080 198 St) is a manufactured-home park described as 55+ across multiple listings but is absent from this program's settled Langley roster, so it is flagged rather than counted, and Twin Lakes and Garden Terrace rest on single listings and stay UNCONFIRMED. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: How is Forest Green Estates different from the four strata complexes?** Tenure. At Churchill Park, Spencer Green, Chartwell Green and Chelsea Green you buy a freehold strata lot. At Forest Green you buy the manufactured home and hold the pad under park rules and the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, with park approval required — listings state pad rents of $930–$990/month for individual homes, figures that vary by home and date and must be confirmed with the park. https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/9080-198-street/34 and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3061958-9080-198-street-46-langley-bc-v1m-3a8/brochure
- **Q: Do both of us have to be 55?** At Chelsea Green a listing states the form as "only one occupant to be 55+ years old," and provincial strata rules in any case exempt a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement. Get the registered bylaw wording for the specific complex before writing — the listing form and the bylaw form are not the same document. https://vancouvernashdom.com/langley-walnut-grove-townhouses/SearchResults.form/?_pg=4 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a Walnut Grove 55+ complex for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted in a strata — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. In a park, the purchase is an assignment of the pad tenancy and the park can refuse an assignment where no purchaser meets the age requirement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ complex?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC strata — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. Park homes are different: renting and assignment run through the park rules. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Complex by complex: Chartwell Green listings state 2 pets allowed with dogs no higher than 16 inches at the shoulder; a Forest Green Estates listing states one small pet (dog up to 18 inches) with park approval required. The registered bylaw or park rule for the specific complex governs — never the listing that happens to be showing. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Chartwell Langley Gardens something I can buy in Walnut Grove?** No. It is a continuum-of-care rental residence at 8888 202 St (independent living, assisted living, memory living and long-term care), and it is a different property from the Chartwell Green strata townhouses at 8737 212 St that you can buy. https://chartwell.com/en/bc/langley/langley-gardens
- **Q: How did Walnut Grove sell in September 2026?** Its own SnapStats row shows Detached 43 listings / 13 sold / 30% — the highest published detached ratio in Langley that month — and Condos & Townhomes 55 / 10 / 18%. No Walnut Grove-specific median is published; the $1,345,000 / $611,000 medians are whole-Langley community figures (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report).
- **Q: Where is the hospital, and what recreation is in the community?** Langley Memorial Hospital is at 22051 Fraser Highway, south across Highway 1, with 24/7 emergency service. In the community itself, Walnut Grove Community Centre (8889 Walnut Grove Drive) is the Township recreation centre beside Walnut Grove Secondary — the amenity the guide page's townhouse cards point buyers at when they check pet or age rules. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Langley/langley-memorial-hospital and https://www.tol.ca/en/parks-recreation/walnut-grove-community-centre.aspx
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. Walnut Grove softens that trade-off more than most Langley areas because it holds four verified complexes, so a seller is rarely the only 55+ listing a buyer can compare. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: When are Township property taxes due?** Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Walnut Grove is one of only two real 55+ clusters in Langley.** Downtown Langley City holds four verified strata complexes and Walnut Grove holds four more; everywhere else in Langley the verified count is one complex per sub-area or zero. A buyer who wants choice inside a single neighbourhood is choosing between those two clusters. Sources: Section 2 above and ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/langley-homes-under-1-8m-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md, Section 2.
- **The guide page's only feed card is a park home, not a townhouse.** The one card surfaced in the research re-read of the walnut-grove-guide page is a manufactured home in Forest Green Estates — the flagged park in Section 2 — not a unit in any of the four verified strata complexes the guide's own townhouse cards discuss. Feed cards and verified stock are different things on this page. https://jaredgibbons.ca/walnut-grove-guide.html
- **Two Chartwells, one neighbourhood.** Chartwell Green (strata townhouses you can buy, 8737 212 St) and Chartwell Langley Gardens (a rental continuum-of-care residence, 8888 202 St) sit about a kilometre apart in Walnut Grove with near-identical search names — every "Chartwell Walnut Grove" enquiry needs the address checked before anyone discusses price or availability. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Forest Green Estates is the roster's open question in this community.** Its 55+ description is consistent across multiple listings from different brokerages and homes, yet the park appears nowhere in the settled Langley roster (11 strata + 3 parks + 1 co-op) that the rest of this set is built on. Until a park or manager source is read, this file carries it flagged — neither counted nor dismissed. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **The unverified pair sits beside the verified belt.** Twin Lakes (20655 88 Ave) and Garden Terrace (8889 212 St) are both described as 55+ in exactly one listing each, on the same corridor as the verified complexes — the pattern that produces false 55+ pages when a single listing is treated as a building fact. Both stay UNCONFIRMED here until a second source states the rule. Sources: Section 2, Checked.
- **Walnut Grove's detached tempo led Langley while its 55+ stock is all attached.** The sub-area's 30% detached sales ratio in September 2026 was the community's highest, but none of that detached stock carries an age bylaw in any source checked — the entire verified 55+ supply here is townhouse strata plus one flagged park (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report).
