## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**There is ZERO verified 55+ ownership stock as acreage product in Langley — no 55+ acreage community, no age-restricted estate strata on rural land, no seniors acreage park.** An acreage purchase is a freehold land purchase: there is no strata corporation to hold an age bylaw, and on ALR land a residence cannot be stratified without ALC permission. A senior who buys an acreage is buying a regular house on land with no age bylaw at all — which also means no age restriction on resale. Langley's verified 55+ ownership roster (11 strata complexes, 3 manufactured-home parks and 1 co-operative) is all urban product and is listed at the end of this section for contrast — it is **not** acreage stock.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **The strata age-bylaw rules, for the record — with no acreage strata to apply them to.** Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; age bylaws below 55 invalid since Nov 24, 2022, under Bill 44); the bylaw restricts residency, not ownership; Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19; and residential rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, while short-term restrictions remain allowed. An acreage buyer gets none of this machinery, because an acreage purchase is freehold land, not a strata lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **ALR housing caps are the rules that actually bind a 55+ acreage buyer.** Land in the ALR may generally have one principal residence of 500 m² or less total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill; a secondary suite is permitted only within the principal residence; and one additional residence may be permitted at up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. Building more, or larger, requires ALC approval, and local government bylaws may be more restrictive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **The additional-residence rule is the ageing-in-place rule on an acreage.** That 90 m² / 186 m² additional residence is the legal route to a caregiver's or family member's dwelling on an ALR acreage; an attached garage below a second-storey additional residence is excluded from its floor area only on the ALC's stated conditions (one-storey garage for the principal residence's vehicles, no larger than the residence's maximum, no internal connection, clustered close to the principal residence). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **ALR strata point:** depositing a strata plan on ALR land is a subdivision requiring ALC permission under ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6 — carried as UNVERIFIED in the research file because the bulletin itself was not opened in that pass. Either way, no 55+ acreage strata exists in Langley in any source checked. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/langley-acreages-for-sale.md
- **Township zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 2500** governs rural Langley acreages; the feed's own cards cite RU-2 and RU-3 zoning. The zone for a specific parcel must be confirmed with the Township — a card's zoning line is listing text, not a Township confirmation. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-acreages-for-sale.html and https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
- **Township SSMUH does not rescue a large rural lot:** eligible lots must be 4,050 m² or smaller, inside the Urban Containment Boundary and on municipal water and sewer — most acreages fail on size and servicing alone (Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024). https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Farm classification is a continuing obligation, not a one-time label.** BC Assessment's thresholds are $2,500 in farm-gate sales for a farm operation area of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for larger operations; $10,000 if the total area is under 0.8 ha; applications must be delivered by October 31. A downsizing buyer who stops producing can lose the classification and its tax treatment. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo89/loo89/411_95_pit and https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Well and septic are diligence items with legal teeth:** the live page advises testing water flow, water quality and the septic system before removing subjects on 1–5 acre properties, and the feed's own cards repeatedly cite wells, septic sizing and drilled wells — per-property well/septic status is UNVERIFIED in this file and must be tested parcel by parcel. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-acreages-for-sale.html
- **Property taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies at midnight July 2, 2026, and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. Farm classification, above, is separate from the municipal tax notice. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- **Tax relief at 55+:** the Province's regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ acreage community or seniors estate development in Langley?** No — none was verified on any source. Acreage parcels are freehold land, there is no strata to hold an age bylaw, and ALR residences cannot be stratified without ALC permission. The 55+ complexes that exist in Langley are urban strata, manufactured-home parks and one co-operative in Langley City, Walnut Grove, Willoughby, Aldergrove, Fort Langley and Brookswood. Sources: Section 2 above and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Langley, then?** Fifteen verified ownership entries, all urban: 11 strata complexes (Huntsfield Green, Parkway Estates, Parkway II and Glenmont Park in the City; Willow Park Estates, Churchill Park, Spencer Green, Chartwell Green, Chelsea Green, Creekside Villa and Waterfront at Bedford Landing in the Township), manufactured homes on pads at Cedar Creek Estates, Fernridge Park and Pineridge, and a home-plus-shares purchase at Pioneer Park co-operative. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: I'm 55+ and buying an acreage to downsize onto. What limits the house I can build?** On ALR land, a principal residence built without an ALC application is capped at 500 m² total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. Confirm the parcel's ALR status before designing anything, and confirm the zone with the Township. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can my adult child or a caregiver live in a second dwelling on the acreage?** The ALC permits one additional residence without an application where its size rules are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — and Township bylaws must also permit it. There is no strata-style caregiver exemption here because there is no strata; the land rules are the whole answer. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me put 3–4 units on a Langley acreage for family?** No for most acreages: Township SSMUH (Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024) requires a lot of 4,050 m² or smaller, inside the Urban Containment Boundary and connected to municipal water and sewer. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Q: If I stop farming or producing, do I keep farm-class taxes?** Not automatically: BC Assessment requires a producing operation to keep meeting minimum gross farm income thresholds that scale with size ($2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that; $10,000 under 0.8 ha), with applications due by October 31. Plan the production, or lease the land to someone who produces, before assuming the classification survives. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Is the big manufactured-home community on Fraser Highway a seniors park?** The large one, Langley Grove Estates, is explicitly not: its listings state no age restrictions and all ages welcome. The Aldergrove park on Fraser Highway states the same. A rural highway address does not make a park a 55+ park. https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/24330-fraser-highway/52
- **Q: What about Cedar Lane Park and Deer Creek Estates — directories call them 55+?** Both stay UNCONFIRMED in this program and are not counted: for Cedar Lane, listing evidence states a 55+ form ("one owner must be 55+") but no park-operator source was obtained; for Deer Creek, the listing evidence found states only "adult oriented," and its tenure is unresolved. Confirm both with the park, in writing, before relying on either. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3097877-2305-200th-street-46-langley-bc-v2z-1z2/brochure and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3105942-33-2315-198-street-langley-british-columbia-v2z1z1-259900/brochure
- **Q: What pre-subject checks matter most on an acreage?** The page's own list, adopted here: test the well (flow and quality) and the septic system, confirm permits for outbuildings, suites and shops, and ask the Township what the zone allows for a second home, a shop or a suite. Per-property well and septic status is UNVERIFIED until tested. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-acreages-for-sale.html
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes at 55+ on an acreage?** The Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities). Farm classification is a separate BC Assessment question, above. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Read the feed, not the label.** This page defines an acreage as a home on 1 to 10 acres, yet its feed at reading carried a 39.5-acre estate and a bare-land Land listing — a downsizer using this page should verify lot size and property type card by card, and a seller should not assume a listing's presence here proves it meets the page's own definition. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-acreages-for-sale.html
- **SnapStats publishes no acreage category.** The research file is explicit that the Langley and rural sub-area figures are whole-community residential figures used as context only — there is no acreage median, acreage inventory count or acreage days-on-market in that source, and any such number on an acreage page is UNVERIFIED. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/langley-acreages-for-sale.md
- **The suite rule on ALR land is narrower than in town.** In the Township's urban areas a suite is a zoning question; on ALR land a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence, and a detached second dwelling is capped at 90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size. Buyers transferring an urban "coach house" assumption onto an acreage parcel will over-read their options. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **The acreage belt's big manufactured-home community markets itself on the opposite promise.** Langley Grove Estates sells "all ages welcome" and family-friendly living on a rural Fraser Highway site — the belt's manufactured-home supply is aimed at families and downsizers alike, with no age gate. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3111178-150-3665-244-street-langley-british-columbia-v2z1n1-210000/brochure
- **An acreage listing can carry a manufactured home without being a "mobile home" purchase.** Parcels like the ALR property at 24183 Fraser Hwy pair a manufactured home with barns and acreage and are priced on land value — the dwelling is incidental, the ALR land is the asset, and none of the park-tenancy or strata-age analysis applies. https://www.zillow.com/homedetails/24183-Fraser-Hwy-Langley-BC-V2Z-2L3/2071787377_zpid/
- **Shops are the dominant first-page product, not seniors product.** The feed snapshot in the research file is houses with shops — a 61' × 35' shop in Salmon River, a heated shop and 9-stall barn in Campbell Valley, twin shops in Otter District — alongside estate houses. The buyer this feed serves is pricing workspace and land, not age-restricted community amenities. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-acreages-for-sale.html
- **The resale freedom cuts both ways.** Because an acreage has no age bylaw, a 55+ buyer here never faces the shrunken-buyer-pool trade-off of a 55+ strata — and never gets its protections either: no age gate on who buys next door, no strata maintaining common property, and well, septic, drainage and farm-class obligations that run with the land. That trade is the real decision on this page type. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-acreages-for-sale.html
