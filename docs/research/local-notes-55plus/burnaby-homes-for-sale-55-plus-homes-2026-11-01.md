## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. No Burnaby strata checked has a verified 55+ bylaw — the absence is the city-wide fact. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://choa.bc.ca/resources/strata-property-act/legislative-updates/
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Zoning: Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026** was adopted June 9, 2026, replaced the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965, and took effect July 1, 2026. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/bylaws/zoning-bylaw
- **R1 SSMUH zoning:** Burnaby consolidated its single- and two-family districts into the R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing district in June 2024 — 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; minimum lot width 10 m and minimum lot area 281 m²; secondary suites count toward the unit total and cannot be stratified. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP: the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan** was adopted December 9, 2025 and replaces the 1998 OCP. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **The Dania campus 55+ test is a housing agreement, not a strata bylaw:** the City's housing agreement for the Dania site defines "Senior" as a person 55 or older (Housing Agreement Bylaw 14477, for 4279 Norland Ave). https://bylaws.burnaby.ca/media/14000/14477.pdf
- **Property taxes:** Burnaby's 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date; the detached single-family water utility charge is $652.33, with a 5% discount if paid by the due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **FHSA / Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits:** UNVERIFIED — no live CRA page was read for this file, so no limit figure is stated.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Burnaby?** None that any source verifies as an age-restricted strata, in any of the 20 sub-areas checked. Burnaby's seniors housing is entirely rental, care and subsidized stock (Section 2). A 55+ buyer in Burnaby buys an ordinary house or strata unit with no age bylaw, or rents in a seniors building. (Sub-area 55+ notes, Nov 1, 2026.)
- **Q: I keep seeing Burnaby buildings marketed to seniors. Why are none of them for sale?** Because the verified stock is tenure-specific: Derby Manor (122 units, 55+ non-profit rental), the New Vista buildings (348 independent seniors, mostly rent-geared-to-income), Dania Gardens (155 affordable rental homes) and the assisted-living and care homes are all applied-for housing, not strata title. https://derbymanor.ca/ and https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/affordable-homes-open-for-seniors-in-burnaby
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes, where a 55+ strata exists (none verified in Burnaby) — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is Hillside Place Co-op in Forest Grove a 55+ building?** Not proven. BC Housing's Zone 5 directory lists the co-op but states no age restriction on its row, while printing ages on other rows. Treat any "55+" claim as UNCONFIRMED until the co-op confirms it; and it is membership housing with a share purchase, not a strata purchase. https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** It is building-by-building and, in Burnaby's case, operator-by-operator, because there is no 55+ strata to generalize from. Confirm the pet rule in the specific building's bylaws or occupancy agreement before relying on it. (No city-wide pet source states a Burnaby 55+ rule — UNCONFIRMED as a general claim.)
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St — the one Fraser Health hospital in the city, in central Burnaby. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- **Q: What transit can a senior actually use day to day?** Two SkyTrain lines cross the city (Millennium and Expo), and the seniors clusters sit unevenly against them: the Edmonds cluster is near Edmonds Station, the Brentwood building (Rideau) is near Brentwood Town Centre Station, while the Norland Ave (Dania) and Sussex Ave (Fair Haven) campuses are bus-served Canada Way / Kingsway corridor locations. Check the exact address's bus service — station proximity is not uniform. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in Burnaby?** The in-city ladder is the fullest in this batch: independent subsidized rental (New Vista, Fair Haven Apartments, Dania Gardens, Kingsway Court), independent 55+ rental (Derby Manor), assisted living (Rideau, Swedish, Dania Manor, Courtyard Terrace, Nikkei Home), then long-term care (George Derby Centre, 300 beds; New Vista Care Home, 240 residents; Normanna, 100 beds; Fair Haven Lodge, 100 beds; Dania Home, 102 beds). Care access is through Fraser Health assessment, not purchase. Sources: Section 2 above.

It is counted once in this city file, under East Burnaby, with the flag carried — not double-counted. https://derbymanor.ca/ and https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/the-crest-real-estate/
- **CONFLICT — the Dania campus count.** BC Housing's 155-unit figure is for Dania Gardens alone (109 subsidized + 46 market-rate); the Dania Society's "over 200 suites" wording covers Carl Mortensen Manor and Dania Gardens together. Adding the two figures together double-counts the campus. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/affordable-homes-open-for-seniors-in-burnaby and https://www.dania.bc.ca/residences/independent-living/
- **CONFLICT — Canada Way campus operator.** The retirement-living side at 7197 Canada Way now operates as Marigold Gardens by Optima Living; the care centre next door at 7195 Canada Way was recorded as AgeCare Harmony Court (80 publicly funded beds, opened 1976) in a care directory, and Fraser Health's current list names Marigold Gardens at 7197. Whether the 7195 care centre has also changed operator or name is UNCONFIRMED — confirm before repeating the AgeCare name. https://goodcaring.ca/agecare-harmony-court-care-centre-burnaby/ and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Services/ltc-al/long-term-care
- **Boundary-strict files lose real buildings.** Chartwell Carlton, AgeCare Carlton and Cascade Gardens (Norfolk St / Kalyk Ave) and Willingdon Care Centre / Chaffey Lane (Grange St) are real Burnaby seniors buildings whose MLS sub-area placement stayed UNCONFIRMED across two or three sub-area files each. A city-wide page is the only level at which they can be listed honestly — with the placement flag attached, as in Section 2. (Burnaby Hospital, Central BN, Central Park BS and Forest Glen BS 55+ notes, Nov 1, 2026.)
- **Burnaby replaced its entire zoning bylaw in 2026.** The Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (adopted June 9, 2026, in force July 1, 2026) repealed and replaced the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 — strata documents, depreciation reports or summaries that still cite the 1965 bylaw are out of date. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **The name on the building is not the tenure.** "Manor," "Court," "Gardens," "Villa" and "Residences" in Burnaby seniors names denote non-profit rental, co-op membership, assisted living and care in different cases — Derby Manor is a monthly occupancy agreement with no lease, Hillside Place is a co-op share, and Rideau is a HousingHub-preserved rental. Read the tenure line, not the name, before shortlisting. https://derbymanor.ca/
