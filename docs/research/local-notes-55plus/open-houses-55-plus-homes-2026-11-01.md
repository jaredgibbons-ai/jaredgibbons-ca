## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT THIS HOME TYPE

- **The time problem on this page is different from its sibling feeds — read this before planning a run.** On the sibling city open-house feeds checked in this program, every labelled card's top time block was exactly one hour later than the "Open House on..." line beneath it, and which figure is authoritative could not be established from the page. This page's cards are a different layout with **no time block at all**: a red "OPEN" badge shows no time, and the only open-house time on a card is inside its description text, which is visually truncated on the card (worked example from page 1: a Langley card whose description ends "OPEN HOUSE: SUNDAY, NOV 1, 2-4 PM" — the only time stated for that open house). The one-hour offset therefore cannot occur here; the weakness here is a time you may not see at all unless you open the full description. Either way: confirm the time with the listing brokerage before you drive (browser-verified, live visit 2026-11-01). https://jaredgibbons.ca/open-houses.html and https://jaredgibbons.ca/mission-open-houses.html
- **Who the host works for:** BCFSA states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party — an unrepresented buyer at an open house is exactly that consumer. The host at an open house is the seller's licensee unless you have your own client relationship with a licensee. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **The open-house disclosure exception:** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report, provides that a disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about their motivation, financial qualifications or needs. Use the open house for facts; save your story for your own agent. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Sign-in and identification:** an identification or sign-in requirement for open-house visitors is not stated in any BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source found — UNVERIFIED as a rule; an individual host may still ask on the seller's instructions. How open house dates and times are set and entered for publication on the brokerage/board side is likewise UNVERIFIED — no BC Gov or BCFSA source states it. Source: the slug's research file, Section 4.
- **Visiting a 55+ home is not residing in it.** The BC strata age framework restricts who may LIVE in a unit under a 55+ bylaw — a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, and people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it. Nothing in that framework bars an under-55 buyer from attending an open house, viewing the home, or buying it for a 55+ parent; the exemptions (Regulation 7.01 — younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents, and the May 1, 2023 expansions) attach to residents, not visitors. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022 — relevant when an open-house host or a directory describes a 55+ complex as "rentals not allowed": that line does not survive the 2022 law, though the tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **No open-house statistics exist.** SnapStats publishes no open-house category for any community, and no single SnapStats community describes this site-wide page — open-house counts, traffic and conversion figures are UNVERIFIED, and no market figures are stated for this page. Judge a home against its own city file's community figures in this program (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report, via the slug's research file).

- **Q: I'm under 55 — can I go to an open house at a 55+ complex, and can I buy there?** Yes to both, with the residence caveat: the 55+ bylaw governs who may LIVE in the unit, not who may view or own it. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a 55+ parent can be the resident, and a younger spouse/partner of a qualifying resident is exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Who does the agent hosting the open house represent?** The seller, unless you have your own client relationship with a licensee: BCFSA states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: Does the hosting agent have to give me a disclosure of representation?** Not for the hosting itself: Rule 5-10(3) excuses disclosure while a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or giving factual answers to general questions — unless the licensee solicits or receives information from you about your motivation, financial qualifications or needs. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Q: Do I have to sign in or show ID at an open house?** No official source found states such a requirement — UNVERIFIED as a rule. An individual host may still ask on the seller's instructions; that is a house rule for that showing, not a provincial rule. Source: the slug's research file, Section 4.
- **Q: How many open houses are on this page, and how often does it update?** The page's own display statements, browser-verified 2026-11-01: 2,747 listings found, of which 1,500 are available for public display, shown 12 to a page with pagination to page 125. How often the feed updates is UNVERIFIED — no update interval is stated, and SnapStats publishes no open-house category. https://jaredgibbons.ca/open-houses.html
- **Q: For a 55+ search, is this page or a city page the better tool?** The city page. This page is the regional overview its title describes, with no per-city 55+ filtering stated; the city open-house files in this program carry the per-city feeds, their own time checks, and the verified complex rosters (Surrey's corridor cluster and Chilliwack's downtown / Evans Road stock in Section 2 are the two deepest). Plan a route by city, not by this page alone. https://jaredgibbons.ca/open-houses.html
- **Q: Can I rent out a 55+ unit I buy after seeing it at an open house?** In a strata, long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. In a manufactured-home park, the equivalent answer comes from the park rules and the pad-tenancy assignment consent, not strata law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
