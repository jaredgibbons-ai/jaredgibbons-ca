## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. A farm parcel is not a strata, so this law does not create any age rule on ALR land — it applies only to the two strata complexes in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **ALR housing rules — the rules that actually govern a Cloverdale farm home:** one principal residence per parcel is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m², with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence, never in a detached building; and one additional detached residence may be permitted at a maximum of 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha, without needing to be for farm help, provided local bylaws are met. Local governments may further regulate or restrict ALR residences, and anything beyond the permitted number or size requires ALC approval. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **ALR short-term accommodation:** whole-house short-term rental of a principal residence is not permitted outright in the ALR and requires ALC approval; bed-and-breakfast use inside a lived-in principal residence is permitted within the ALC's four-bedroom / 30-day limits. Surrey's own short-term rental rules sit on top: owner-host only, primary residence, business licence at $358 per year. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Farm classification (BC Assessment Class 9):** the farm operation must meet a minimum income threshold — $2,500 if the operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 if the operation is under 0.8 ha — and farm-class applications are due by the legislated deadline of October 31 for the next assessment and tax year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Urban rules do not transfer onto a farm parcel:** Surrey's suite rule (one suite per dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit, never inside a coach house) and its coach-house / garden-suite permissions (35–75 m², up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots) are urban-lot rules; on ALR land the ALC frame above is the stricter rule — the suite must be inside the principal residence. Whether a coach house can serve as the ALR additional residence on a given parcel is a parcel-level ALC + City check: UNVERIFIED as a general rule (Cloverdale farms area research file, Section 4). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Zoning caution:** the live farms page states Surrey A-1 / A-2 zoning applies to Cloverdale ALR parcels, but no City of Surrey zoning page opened in this program confirms those zone names for these parcels — UNVERIFIED on an official City source; confirm a parcel's zone in the City's mapping / Zoning Bylaw 12000 before relying on it (Cloverdale farms area research file, Section 4). City zoning generally: Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **OCP: "Surrey 2050" Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2026, No. 21900** is the current OCP — finally adopted June 15, 2026, replacing the 2013 OCP Bylaw No. 18020. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Property taxes (city-wide):** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2 — farm-class parcels are taxed on their classification, so confirm the parcel's own position rather than assuming the urban rate treatment. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a 55+ farm in Cloverdale?** No such product was found in any source checked. Age restrictions in BC attach to strata bylaws (or park / membership rules), and a Cloverdale farm parcel is freehold ALR land governed by the ALC residence frame — one principal residence (≤500 m²) plus at most one small additional residence. A 55+ buyer can buy a farm at any age; the age rule only appears if they instead buy into Carriage House or Manor Ridge in town. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What 55+ homes can I buy in Cloverdale if I give up the farm idea?** Two strata complexes: Carriage House (17695 58 Ave, 1987 condo building, no pets) and The Estates at Manor Ridge (6140 192 St, 1989 townhouses, 1 occupant must be 55+, 1 pet with restrictions). Both are urban and off the ALR. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/17695-58-avenue/102 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8
- **Q: How big can the farmhouse be, and can I add a suite for family?** The principal residence is capped at 500 m² of total floor area (1,000 m² fill limit for its construction) without an ALC application, and a suite is permitted only inside that principal residence — not in a barn or a detached building. A detached second home is the separate "additional residence," capped at 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can my adult child or a farm worker live in the second dwelling?** The ALC additional residence does not need to be intended for farm help, provided its size cap and local bylaws are met — but it is one small dwelling, not a full second house, and anything beyond the permitted number or size needs ALC approval. Family-occupancy plans should be sized against the 90 m² / 186 m² caps before pricing the parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I rent out the farmhouse short-term when I'm not using it?** Not by right: whole-house short-term rental of a principal residence in the ALR requires ALC approval, and Surrey separately requires an owner-host living at the property as their primary residence plus a $358-per-year business licence. Bed-and-breakfast use inside a lived-in principal residence is the permitted route, within the ALC's four-bedroom / 30-day limits. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Q: Do both of us have to be 55 to live at Manor Ridge or Carriage House?** Manor Ridge listings state 1 occupant must be 55+. Provincially, a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident is exempt in any case, as are live-in caregivers and legacy residents — the exemption set is in Strata Property Regulation 7.01. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What keeps a Cloverdale farm's taxes at farm rates?** BC Assessment farm classification: the operation must meet the income thresholds in Section 3 ($2,500 / $2,500-plus-5% / $10,000 by operation size) and the application deadline is October 31 for the next tax year. Check the parcel's own farm income against the thresholds before pricing it on farm-class treatment. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Are the Cloverdale market numbers farm prices?** No. SnapStats publishes no farm category; the residential context for the parent community in September 2026 was a detached median of $1,212,500 and a Condos & Townhomes median of $740,000, and Serpentine's residential rows were 0 / 0 — farm value is per-parcel, on production, water, buildings and ALR status (SnapStats FVR September 2026, via the Cloverdale farms research file).
- **Q: Is flood a factor on the Serpentine lowland parcels?** The live page states lowland parcels sit within City floodplain mapping and rely on municipal ditches and pump stations, but no City floodplain page was opened in this program, so that statement is UNVERIFIED on an official source here — flood construction level is a per-parcel check before valuing any lowland parcel (Cloverdale farms area research file, Section 4).

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Cloverdale farms" as a feed is really "Serpentine farms."** Both visible listings at reading were Serpentine agri-business listings on 176 Street — a buyer browsing this topic page is looking at one corridor's farm market, not the whole Cloverdale community's acreages (Cloverdale farms area research file, Section 1).
- **Serpentine's empty residential rows do not mean an empty farm market.** SnapStats recorded 0 / 0 for Serpentine in both residential splits in September 2026 while the farm feed carried active agri-business listings there — the residential table and the farm channel count different things (SnapStats FVR September 2026, via the Cloverdale farms research file).
- **The downsizer's real choice in Cloverdale is farm-freehold versus town-strata, with nothing in between.** There is no 55+ park, no 55+ bare-land strata and no age-restricted acreage product in any source checked — a senior who wants land keeps full freehold obligations under ALC rules; a senior who wants the age-restricted community gives up the land for Carriage House or Manor Ridge. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **A suite in the barn is the classic Cloverdale farm mistake.** Urban Surrey allows a suite in a house and a separate coach house; ALR land allows the suite only inside the principal residence. Buyers pricing a parcel on "suite in the shop" income are pricing a use the ALC frame does not permit. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **The farm-named townhouse complex is neither a farm nor 55+.** Harvest at Bose Farm (16488 64 Ave, 222 townhomes, 2016–2017) carries the Bose Farm name into a general-market subdivision — it appears in Cloverdale farm searches on the name alone. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/listing/r3178832-111-16488-64-avenue-surrey-bc-v3s-6x6
- **Cloverdale's seniors housing cluster and its farm belt are at opposite ends of the community.** Carriage House, Bethshan Gardens and Manor Ridge are all in the built-up town-centre / 192 Street side; the Serpentine farm corridor is along 176 Street in the lowlands. A buyer cannot combine "walk to the seniors centre" with "on the farm" in one Cloverdale property in any source checked. Sources: Sections 1 and 2 above.
