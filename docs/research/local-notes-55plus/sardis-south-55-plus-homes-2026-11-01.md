## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. That is why a "45+" label, like Promontory Lake Estates', cannot be a valid strata age bylaw in BC today — at leasehold or mixed-tenure communities the restriction, if any, has to live in the lease or community rules instead, and its exact legal form at Promontory Lake Estates is UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://choa.bc.ca/resources/strata-property-act/legislative-updates/
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City short-term rentals are separately regulated** under the City of Chilliwack's Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf
- **Manufactured-home parks are not strata.** Selomas Park homes sit on rented pads, so the SPA age-bylaw frame above does not govern the park the way it governs a 55+ strata; the park's age rule, pad rent and rules live in the park's own tenancy documents, which must be read directly. One listing states a $550/month pad fee (listing-stated only). https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3114824-37-6035-vedder-roadsardis-south-chilliwack-british-columbia-v2r1e5-299990/brochure
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000** is Chilliwack's zoning bylaw. Its small-scale multi-unit (SSMUH) amendment, Bylaw 2024, No. 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024), allows 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Suites and accessory units:** under Bylaw 5000 only 1 accessory dwelling unit may sit on a lot; a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the habitable floor space of the building, and a garden suite is capped at 100 m². https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Property taxes:** City of Chilliwack notices are mailed at the end of May, taxes are due the first working day after July 1, a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, and a further 5% to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ strata building I can buy in Sardis South?** No — none was verified in this pass. The verified Sardis-side 55+ strata buildings are west of Vedder Road: Knight Lodge (45660 Knight Rd), Cotton Ridge (45645 Knight Rd), The Sapphire (7685 Amber Dr) and 7651 Amber Dr. See the Sardis West Vedder file in this series.
- **Q: What 55+ product does Sardis South actually have?** Two things, neither of them strata ownership: Selomas Park (6035 Vedder Rd), a 55+ manufactured-home park where you buy the home and rent the pad, and Chilliwack Lifestyles (6980 Vedder Rd), an independent-living retirement residence where the studio suites are rented, not sold. https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/listing/r3085025-6035-vedder-road-108-chilliwack-sardis-south-homes-for-sale-249900/brochure and https://chilliwacklifestyles.ca/about
- **Q: How does buying in Selomas Park work?** You buy the manufactured home itself and take on the pad tenancy — one listing states a pad fee of $550 per month, and listings describe double-wide homes close to bus routes and shopping. There is no strata corporation and no Form B; the documents that matter are the park's rules, the pad tenancy agreement and the home's own records. Pad fee is listing-stated — confirm the current amount with the park. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3114824-37-6035-vedder-roadsardis-south-chilliwack-british-columbia-v2r1e5-299990/brochure
- **Q: Promontory Lake Estates says "senior community" — is it 55+?** No. Board data records "Senior Community: Yes," but the community's own marketing in every source opened is 45+, and its listing text shows both a maintenance fee and a lease fee — treat it as a 45+ leasehold-side community, read the tenure documents, and note board data files it under this sub-area even though Promontory Road is on the east hillside. https://www.zolo.ca/chilliwack-real-estate/6001-promontory-road/91
- **Q: If a complex says 45+, can it legally keep out a younger buyer?** In a BC strata, no: since Nov 24, 2022 the only valid age bylaw is 55+, so a strata "45+" bylaw is invalid. Promontory Lake Estates and the Englewood communities are not ordinary freehold stratas on the sources opened, so any age rule there would sit in lease or community documents instead — that form is UNCONFIRMED, and a buyer under 55 should get the actual documents before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. If we buy in a true 55+ strata nearby, can we both live there?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals (and the City regulates short-term rentals under Bylaw 5141). Manufactured-home parks like Selomas set rental terms in the park's own documents, not under the strata rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** At Chilliwack Lifestyles the operator's suites page says you can bring your small pet to live with you. For Selomas Park, one listing states one small dog allowed and no cats (listing-stated). Strata buildings elsewhere in Sardis vary from no pets (The Sapphire) to pets with restrictions (Knight Lodge) — there is no area-wide rule. https://chilliwacklifestyles.ca/place/your-home and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3114824-37-6035-vedder-roadsardis-south-chilliwack-british-columbia-v2r1e5-299990/brochure
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Chilliwack General Hospital, 45600 Menholm Road — a Fraser Health community hospital providing primary, core secondary and some specialty services, on the Chilliwack Proper side north of Highway 1. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay on the Sardis side?** The in-area step verified here is independent living only: Chilliwack Lifestyles (6980 Vedder Rd, rented studio suites with household and personal support services per the operator). Chilliwack's registered assisted-living residences in the sources opened — for example the Waverly Seniors Village, The Cascades and Chartwell Birchwood — are all on the Chilliwack Proper / downtown side, so assisted living currently means leaving Sardis. https://chilliwacklifestyles.ca/about and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f[0]=city:Castlegar&f[1]=city:Chilliwack&f[2]=city:Nanaimo&f[3]=residence_type:Seniors and Persons with Disabilities&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=desc

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **This sub-area's 55+ stock is a park and a rental residence, not a single condo building.** Sardis South has family detached, townhome and new condo stock in quantity, but its only verified 55+ addresses are Selomas Park (manufactured homes on rented pads) and Chilliwack Lifestyles (rented studios) — both on Vedder Road, the sub-area's edge, not in the Tyson / Watson core. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **The board files a Promontory hillside community under this flatland sub-area.** Promontory Lake Estates (6001 Promontory Rd) is labelled "Sardis South" in board data while sitting on the east hillside; a buyer searching only Sardis South will be shown hillside 45+ ranchers, and a buyer searching the Promontory side will miss them. Verify geography by street address, never by sub-area label alone. https://www.zolo.ca/chilliwack-real-estate/6001-promontory-road/91
- **The adult communities just south of here are 45+ and in a different sub-area.** Englewood Village and Englewood Courtyard on Keith Wilson Road are board-labelled Vedder Crossing and market 45+ — they are the closest large adult-oriented stock to Sardis South, and they belong on neither a 55+ page nor this sub-area's page. https://activeadultliving.com/BC/british-columbia/age-restricted-communities/chilliwack/V2R3M7/englewood-village
- **The live guide page's plan label is stale.** Pages for this area still state the Sardis Neighbourhood Plan was adopted January 5, 2021 "as OCP Schedule D, through Bylaw 2020, No. 5024." In the current OCP the Sardis plan is Schedule B5 of Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500, and Schedule D is the Development Permit Areas schedule. Do not copy the old label into an offer or a listing. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Flood checks matter on the Vedder-side lots, and they are lot-specific.** Southern Sardis sits toward the Vedder River side of the city; the floodplain bylaw specifics and any lot's Flood Construction Level were not opened in this program and are UNVERIFIED here — confirm the parcel's own flood construction level and floodplain position with the City before removing subjects.
