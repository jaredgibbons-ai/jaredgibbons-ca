# Popkum (FVRD Electoral Area D) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

> **⚠️ GEOGRAPHY FLAG — READ FIRST: Popkum is NOT in the City of Chilliwack.** It is a small unincorporated community in Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) Electoral Area D (Popkum–Bridal Falls), east of Chilliwack near Rosedale and Bridal Falls. The FVRD — not the City — is its local government for planning and zoning, so City of Chilliwack zoning, Chilliwack tax penalties and the Chilliwack 55+ roster do **not** apply here. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **There is no age bylaw on a freehold rural home, and none was found on any product here.** Strata age law only bites inside a strata corporation with a registered 55+ bylaw; no source checked states such a bylaw for any property in Popkum. A senior buying in Popkum faces FVRD zoning, servicing and — where it applies — Agricultural Land Reserve rules, not a 55+ residency bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **If the same buyer instead buys 55+ strata elsewhere in the region, the provincial frame is this:** under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it — people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01), wherever a 55+ bylaw does apply:** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide in strata.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Local government: the FVRD, not the City.** Popkum is in FVRD Electoral Area D (Popkum–Bridal Falls); the FVRD is the local government for its electoral areas, providing planning and development approvals, building inspection and bylaw enforcement among its electoral-area services, while member municipalities govern themselves — City of Chilliwack zoning does not apply in Popkum. https://jaredgibbons.ca/popkum-guide.html
- **Community plan:** the guide page states the Popkum-Bridal Falls (Area D) Official Community Plan, FVRD Bylaw No. 1501, was adopted by the FVRD Board in January 2021, after a public hearing on December 15, 2020. The bylaw text itself was not opened on an FVRD source in the research pass, so the bylaw number and adoption details are UNVERIFIED against the FVRD record — confirm with the FVRD before relying on them. https://jaredgibbons.ca/popkum-guide.html
- **Zoning:** the FVRD sets zoning for Electoral Area D, but the specific FVRD zoning bylaw and the zone of any Popkum parcel were not confirmed on an FVRD source in the research pass: UNVERIFIED — confirm the parcel's zone with the FVRD before relying on any use or building claim. https://jaredgibbons.ca/popkum-guide.html
- **ALR housing frame, for any parcel in the Agricultural Land Reserve:** the principal residence maximum is a total floor area of 500 m²; a secondary suite is permitted only when it is entirely contained within the principal residence and local bylaws permit it; and one additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application at up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, subject to local bylaws. Parcel-level ALR status for any specific Popkum property: UNVERIFIED — confirm on the ALC's ALR maps; the guide page likewise states no separate ALR share or boundary for Popkum is published. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Servicing is parcel-specific.** The research file's own checklist directs buyers to confirm how the property is served (water and sewer) before counting on a suite or addition — servicing in an electoral-area community is not the city system a Chilliwack buyer may assume. https://jaredgibbons.ca/popkum-guide.html
- **Flood and hazard:** the research file states the published records it read do not list an FVRD or BC document stating a flood or debris-flow hazard for the Popkum area. Parcel-level flood or hazard mapping for any Popkum property: UNVERIFIED — check site-specific hazard mapping with the FVRD before building or buying. https://jaredgibbons.ca/popkum-guide.html
- **Property taxes — the RURAL regime applies here, not any city's.** Because Popkum is unincorporated FVRD ground, its taxes are levied under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s. 11: taxes are due July 2, a 5% penalty is added to taxes unpaid on July 2, and a further 5% penalty is added to taxes still unpaid on October 31. Do not apply the City of Chilliwack penalty dates across the regional-district line — they do not govern here. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01

Popkum is unincorporated Electoral Area D ground; the FVRD handles its planning, zoning, building inspection and bylaw enforcement, and City of Chilliwack bylaws do not apply. On ALR land: a suite only inside the principal residence (which is capped at 500 m²) where local bylaws permit it, plus at most one additional detached residence of 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). The parcel's ALR status and its FVRD zone are both UNVERIFIED until checked — on the ALC maps and with the FVRD respectively — and servicing has to be confirmed parcel by parcel before counting on either. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://jaredgibbons.ca/popkum-guide.html
- **Q: Can I get a bus without driving into Chilliwack?** The guide page describes BC Transit route 71 (Agassiz–Harrison) running along Yale Road through the Popkum corridor, but its timetable document was not opened in the research pass and the routing detail is UNVERIFIED in this file — and the one routing point confirmed on BC Transit's own route-overview page is that on-request trips can route to Bridal Falls and/or the Agassiz recreation centre. - **Q: Where is the hospital, and what if I need care later?** Chilliwack General Hospital, 45600 Menholm Road in Chilliwack, provides 24/7 emergency and acute care for this area. There is no retirement residence, assisted living residence or long-term care home in Popkum in any source checked — the care ladder is a move into Chilliwack, where the registered residences (The Cascades and the Chartwell residences) sit. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3AChilliwack&f%5B1%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The Popkum-named "park" is a racetrack, not a neighbourhood amenity for residents.** Popkum Motor Park's camping is unserviced, unmarked and first-come in its parking lots, tied to riding days, with a daily waiver and wristband — a buyer picturing a quiet RV community beside a lake is picturing a different product entirely. https://popkummotorpark.com/services-camping/
- **The guide page's own framing does not match its feed.** The guide's "What to look at in a Popkum home" cards divide the stock into rural homes on larger parcels and a "Townhomes and condos — strata living" card, but the one feed card read is bare land, not a strata home — a 55+ buyer landing on the guide expecting condo or townhome stock will not find it here. https://jaredgibbons.ca/popkum-guide.html
- **The nearest regional park bans pets outright.** Cheam Lake Wetlands Regional Park, the 107-hectare FVRD park in Electoral Area D with 184 recorded bird species, does not permit pets or other domestic animals at all, under FVRD Bylaw No. 1500, 2018 — a real adjustment for a dog-owning buyer who pictures daily walks there. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/cheam-lake-wetlands.html?media=standard
- **Popkum and Bridal Falls share one plan, one electoral area and one zero-stock answer.** Both communities sit under the Popkum-Bridal Falls (Area D) frame in the research program, and both files in this series verify zero 55+ housing — the age-restricted supply for the whole Highway 1 corridor east of Chilliwack is on the city side of the municipal line. Sources: Section 2 above and ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/bridal-falls-guide-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
