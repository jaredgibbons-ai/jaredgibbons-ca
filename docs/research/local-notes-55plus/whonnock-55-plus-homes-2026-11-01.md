## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. There is no verified strata stock in Whonnock for this law to bite on — it matters here when a buyer compares against East Central's Fraserview Village buildings. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is strata law; a Whonnock freehold acreage is not in a strata at all, so its rental and suite position is set by City zoning and, on ALR land, provincial rules — see below. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home parks, for contrast:** pad tenancies elsewhere in the city (Alouette, Val Maria) are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, with a park's 55+ rule operating through park rules and assignment consent referencing s. 10(2)(b)(i) of the Human Rights Code — not strata law. No park of any kind was verified in Whonnock, so this frame is comparison only. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit and ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/maple-ridge-mobile-homes-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019** (not 7600-2020). Its schedule minimum lot areas for this area's zones include A-1 2.0 ha; A-2 4.0 ha; A-3 8.0 ha; RS-2 0.4 ha; RS-3 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha depending on the zone text — the rural zones that make attached, age-restricted product structurally unlikely here. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **City plan: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 7060-2014** is Maple Ridge's OCP. No OCP designation creating a seniors-housing site in Whonnock was identified in any source checked: UNCONFIRMED as a planned 55+ site. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/01ocp-chapter-1201402201612431396
- **Suites and added units — the rural substitute for a 55+ complex:** one secondary suite per lot is permitted in listed zones including A-1, A-2 and A-3, contained within the single detached building, to a maximum of 40% of gross floor area, and not in the Floodplain Area. On ALR parcels, the provincial ALR Use Regulation also governs: it permits one secondary suite in the principal residence (s.31) and an additional residence only if the s.32 conditions are met. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo105/loo105/30_2019
- **ALR, wells and septic — the rural due-diligence trio:** ALR status is per-parcel — check the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder. Wells are recorded in the provincial GWELLS database, and onsite sewerage must comply with the Sewerage System Regulation, which sets setbacks including 30 m from a well unless professional advice is obtained. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo104/loo104/22_326_2004
- **Flood and hazards:** the City's floodplain framework addresses flood risks from the Fraser River, the Alouette River system, steep creek watercourses and groundwater-related flooding; low land along the Fraser River at the area's south edge is the area to check on City flood maps, property by property, and secondary suites are prohibited in the Floodplain Area under the City's suite rules. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-advances-climate-action-flood-resilience-environmental-protection-and-mckenney-creek and https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Property taxes / deferment — the 55+ rule that does reach Whonnock:** Maple Ridge property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on any outstanding balance on July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% on September 2, 2026. Separately, the Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities) — a homeowner-age benefit that applies to a Whonnock acreage exactly as it does in town. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** for this area (Zone 4 directory, May 2025) — but every Maple Ridge building in that directory is in the urban core, so a Whonnock senior using it is applying for a move west, not a unit in this area. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
