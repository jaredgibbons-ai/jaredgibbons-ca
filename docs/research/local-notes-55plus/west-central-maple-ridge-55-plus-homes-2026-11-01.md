## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. This is the rule that disposes of the Canuck Plaza "25+" listing wording in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://choa.bc.ca/resources/strata-property-act/legislative-updates/
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured home parks run on different law.** Pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and its Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003), not the Strata Property Act; the Regulation expressly contemplates parks where every site is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55, and a park landlord may refuse an assignment where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement in such a park. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Co-op and life-lease age rules are contract/membership rules, not strata bylaws.** St. George's Village's 55-year membership qualification and Haney Pioneer Village's 60+ threshold are set by those co-ops' own documents; the provincial strata exemptions (spouse, caregiver, legacy) do not automatically apply to them — read the membership or occupancy documents themselves. https://www.stgeorgesvillage.ca/_files/ugd/070889_39221c746a9b483980e20a3596cdb580.pdf and https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019.** Houses sit under RS-1 (s. 605 — minimum lot area 668.0 m², width 18.0 m, depth 27.0 m, maximum coverage 40%). http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **City plan: the Official Community Plan and the Town Centre Area Plan — OCP CITATION CONTESTED, flagged not resolved.** Program sources cite the Maple Ridge OCP bylaw two ways: as Bylaw No. 7060-2014 (the citation in the earlier batch files) and as Bylaw No. 7998-2024. Both numbers appear in program sources; this file does not resolve which is current — confirm with the City before publishing the number. Settled on substance: Council adopted updates to the OCP and the Town Centre Area Plan on November 25, 2025; the Town Centre Area Plan was first adopted in November 2008 and covers the downtown core at this sub-area's east edge; and the Transit-Oriented Area Designation Bylaw No. 8001-2024 covers the Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station TOAs. One guide claim is recorded as an error in the batch-18 research file and is NOT repeated as fact here: the guide page states the Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan was adopted on January 27, 2026 — City sources in this program show only initial bylaw readings at the end of 2025. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- **Suites and added units:** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws — the route for a West Central 55+ homeowner to add housing for a caregiver or family member on a detached lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Floodplain (south edge only):** along River Road / Port Haney the flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), no habitable floor space may be located below the flood construction level on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. Floodplain status is per-lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** for this area (Zone 4 directory, May 2025), with some buildings apply-direct to the society — the directory gives the route per building. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata (in the East Central buildings)?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption), with further defined exemptions for children and live-in caregivers. The co-ops (55+ at St. George's Village, 60+ at Haney Pioneer Village) and the Legion life leases east of here set their own occupancy terms — the strata exemptions do not automatically apply to them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC strata — every rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. In a manufactured-home park, assignment and renting run through the park rules and the MHPTA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Q: Can I add a suite to a West Central house for a caregiver or my parents?** Maple Ridge allows one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building (maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area), and eligible lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. Note the floodplain exception: s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation on floodplain properties at the south edge. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street — a Fraser Health community hospital with 24/7 emergency care serving Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows. The Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is in Haney Place Mall, 11900 Haney Place, open 7 days a week, 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/F/C/urgent-primary-care-centre---ridge-meadows
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or a buyer who purchases without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
