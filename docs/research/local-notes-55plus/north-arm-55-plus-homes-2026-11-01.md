## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. In North Arm this is a filter rule only: no strata stock was verified in the sub-area for such a bylaw to attach to. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: New Westminster Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001** governs the slope above the foreshore; the RS-1 district states a site of not less than 557.40 sq. m. (6,000 sq. ft.) and a floor space ratio not over 0.5. Foreshore and water-lot rules are provincial and are not confirmed for a specific lot. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Plan: Our City 2041, the Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 7925, 2017),** governs the area; its Green Zone list includes the Esplanade (North Arm) and the Annacis Channel shoreline. No North Arm area plan was confirmed on a City source. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
- **Flood:** the City floodplain study covers Fraser River land at the west end of the city in Floodplain Zone 2, and the North Arm is Fraser River foreshore — but the floodplain status of a specific North Arm lot is UNVERIFIED at lot level and must be confirmed parcel by parcel. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
- **Property taxes:** City of New Westminster 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current taxes remaining unpaid on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on any unpaid balance on September 3, 2026. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in North Arm?** None that any source verifies. The sub-area recorded zero listings and zero sales in September 2026, no named strata complex was confirmed in it, and it is not a City residential neighbourhood — a "North Arm" search almost always resolves in practice to homes in West End, Connaught Heights, Downtown, the Quay or Queensborough, each a separate sub-area with its own stock. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.)
- **Q: Is North Arm in Richmond?** No — not on the sources checked. The research file for this name places it in the City of New Westminster, on the North Arm of the Fraser River; the City of Richmond lies across and along the same river system (Sea Island and Lulu Island), which is how the name gets mis-filed. If a listing says "North Arm, Richmond," treat the municipality as UNCONFIRMED until the address itself is checked. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
- **Q: Is the North Arm Housing Co-op a North Arm building I can apply to?** It is a real seniors co-op — 58 homes for low- and moderate-income seniors at 710 East 19th Avenue — but that address is in Vancouver, and co-op membership is not a purchase. It has nothing to do with the New Westminster sub-area beyond sharing the river's name. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/nearly-60-affordable-homes-for-seniors-open-in-vancouver
- **Q: Where would a North Arm-area senior actually live if they need assisted living?** In upland New Westminster: the city's two Assisted Living Registry residences are Victoria Heights (230 Ross Drive) and Thornebridge Gardens (649 8th Avenue) — both rental/care, both outside this sub-area. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANew%20Westminster&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=asc
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. If we buy into a 55+ strata elsewhere, can we both live there?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is a foreshore property a flood risk?** It needs lot-level checking, not a blanket answer: the City floodplain study covers west-end Fraser River land in Floodplain Zone 2 and this is Fraser River foreshore, but no source states the floodplain zone, foreshore/water-lot tenure or insurance position of any specific North Arm parcel — confirm all three before subject removal. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
- **Q: What transit and hospital serve the area?** No station sits in the sub-area — 22nd Street and New Westminster stations (Expo Line) are nearest and route 100 serves the Marine Way corridor; the city's hospital is Royal Columbian Hospital, 330 East Columbia Street. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2024-schedules/september/burnaby-new-west-p155.pdf and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/New-Westminster/royal-columbian-hospital

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The board name and the City name are two different geographies with the same words.** The board lists "North Arm" as a sub-area; the City uses "North Arm North / North Arm South" for industrial statistical areas on the Queensborough/river side, and does not list North Arm among its OCP residential neighbourhoods at all. A search under one system does not retrieve the other's documents. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/OUR_CITY_2041_Official_Community_Plan_New_Westminsters_Residential_Neighbourhoods_Map.pdf
- **A zero month is the normal state here, not a crash.** Zero inventory and zero solds in September 2026 means no sales ratio, median or days-on-market figure exists for this sub-area — any North Arm "median price" quoted anywhere is either a New Westminster community figure or invented. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.)
- **The trail is the amenity.** The Waterfront Esplanade (North Arm) is named in the City's OCP Green Zone list — the foreshore's documented public use is the trail, not housing. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/rte/2_4 Parks and Open Space.pdf
- **New Westminster runs its own electric utility,** alongside City water/sewer services — a utility setup unlike Richmond's or Burnaby's, and a genuine running-cost difference for a buyer comparing across the river. A 2026 utility dollar figure for a North Arm property: UNVERIFIED. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
- **The Queensborough Bridge is the sub-area's hinge:** it carries Highway 91A over the North Arm between the mainland and Queensborough, which is why Queensborough addresses surface in "North Arm" searches despite being a separate sub-area across the water. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
