## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Hazelmere is the rural, acreage board sub-area in the south-east corner of South Surrey, in the City of Surrey — one of nine sub-areas of the Fraser Valley board community "S Surrey White Rock," and inside the City's South Surrey community (one of the City's seven communities). ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. In Hazelmere this rule is mostly academic today: the sub-area's stock is freehold acreage with no strata to hold a bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **ALR housing frame — the rule set that actually governs Hazelmere parcels in the Agricultural Land Reserve:** land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel; a principal residence may be up to 500 m² total floor area without ALC application; one additional detached residence may be permitted of up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence and only where local bylaws allow it; local governments may further restrict residences through zoning. Whether any specific Hazelmere parcel — or what share of the sub-area — is inside the ALR is UNVERIFIED in this file: ALR status is parcel-specific on ALC mapping. The guide page's statement that a large part of the Little Campbell River watershed is in the ALR is site copy consistent with that parcel-specific position, but no ALC map result for a Hazelmere parcel was opened in this pass. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere-guide.html
- **Zoning:** the sibling page states A-1 General Agriculture zoning for Hazelmere Valley acreages, Part 10 (Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000) minimum lot areas of 4 hectares inside the ALR and 2 hectares outside it for new A-1 lots, one single-family dwelling which may contain one secondary suite under A-1, and RA Acreage Residential zoning on some nearby land with a 4,050 m² minimum lot size. The RA figure is consistent with the City's SSMUH zone table, which carries RA lots at over 4,050 m²; the A-1 / Part 10 figures themselves were not re-read in Bylaw 12000 in the research pass — UNVERIFIED on the bylaw text; confirm in the current consolidated bylaw before quoting them for a listing. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere.html and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Plans:** the current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — never cite the replaced Bylaw No. 18020 as current. No City land use plan is named for Hazelmere (the guide page states this expressly); the neighbouring plan frame is the South Campbell Heights Local Area Plan, immediately north. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere-guide.html and https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/SouthCampbellHeightsLocalAreaPlan.pdf
- **SSMUH caution for this ground:** Surrey's small-scale multi-unit allowances apply to eligible residential-zoned properties under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including lands not connected to community water and sewer — most Hazelmere acreage ground is outside that shape, so the standard 3–4 unit framing must not be applied to Hazelmere lots without a parcel check. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **A separate coach house on an A-1 or ALR acreage:** the sibling page states this is not confirmed on a City source, and the ALC frame permits an additional residence only within its size limits (90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size) — a Hazelmere coach-house claim is UNVERIFIED parcel-by-parcel, even though guide feed listings here include coach-house product. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere-guide.html and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Watershed / flood note from the guide page:** the guide states there are no dykes on the Little Campbell River and the floodplain follows the river channel closely, with streamside considerations on Jacobsen Creek as well in the sibling account — guide-page copy compiled from the City per the page; a specific parcel's floodplain and setback position is UNVERIFIED until checked parcel by parcel. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere-guide.html
- **Property taxes (city-wide, not area-specific):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. Whether a specific Hazelmere property carries farm-class taxation is a BC Assessment / parcel question: UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

Well/septic and setback detail is UNVERIFIED on a Province source in this file; confirm parcel-by-parcel. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere-guide.html and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Does that matter if we buy a Hazelmere acreage?** Not under strata law — a freehold acreage has no strata age bylaw, so the provincial 55+ exemptions are not engaged at all. The spouse/partner exemption (and the child and caregiver exemptions in Regulation 7.01) matter only if you instead buy into a 55+ strata in a neighbouring sub-area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I add a suite or a coach house for a caregiver on a Hazelmere lot?** Parcel-by-parcel, and the answer differs by regime: on ALR land, a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence where local bylaws allow it, and one additional detached residence is capped at 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha); a separate coach house on A-1 land is UNCONFIRMED on a City source even in the live pages' own account, despite coach houses appearing in the guide feed. Get the parcel's ALR status, zone and servicing confirmed before designing around a caregiver unit. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere-guide.html
- **Q: Can I rent out a Hazelmere home, or run a short-term rental?** There is no strata here, so the province-wide voiding of strata rental-restriction bylaws is not the governing question; tenancy law, City rules and — on ALR parcels — the ALC residence frame are. - **Hazelmere is the only South Surrey sub-area in this series with literally nothing on the seniors ladder.** No ownership complex, no park, no rental residence, no care campus, no subsidized building — every other South Surrey sub-area checked in this series has at least one verified seniors product. - **The guide page's own FAQ is split in two.** In the guide page's main FAQ section, two questions appear with NO answer — "What heritage sites are in Hazelmere?" and "Which schools serve Hazelmere?" — with both answers appearing only in a second, separate FAQ block lower on the page. A buyer who stops at the first block leaves thinking the guide does not cover its two most local topics. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere-guide.html
- **The sub-area's name changed once already, and the old name still searches.** Hall's Prairie was renamed Hazelmere after the rail station named for Henry Thrift's homestead, per the City's South Campbell Heights plan document — acreage listings and local references still use both names for the same ground. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/SouthCampbellHeightsLocalAreaPlan.pdf
- **A century of rural character is the plan document's own finding, not a slogan.** The City's plan document records that Hazelmere largely maintained its rural, agricultural character over the past century — which is the sourced reason there is no strata stock here for a 55+ bylaw to attach to. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/SouthCampbellHeightsLocalAreaPlan.pdf
- **The river here has no dykes.** The guide page states the Little Campbell River floodplain follows the channel closely with no dykes — unlike the diked Fraser-side ground many Fraser Valley buyers are used to, flood diligence in Hazelmere is a channel-and-setback question on each parcel, not a dike-system question. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere-guide.html
- **The urban Surrey rules most downsizers read about do not reach this ground.** Surrey's SSMUH 3–4 unit allowances apply to eligible lots under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary on community services, with exemptions including lands not connected to community water and sewer — Hazelmere acreages generally sit outside that shape, so a Hazelmere lot's "development potential" claim needs a parcel-level City check, not the standard framing. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **The highest sales ratio in South Surrey in September 2026 was two sales.** Hazelmere's 29% detached ratio — the set's highest — is 2 solds on 7 listings, the set's smallest pool. Quoting that ratio without its base makes the thinnest market in the set look like its hottest. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.)
- **Even the local schools' catchments are not settled on paper in this program.** Xw'epiteng Elementary (18035 8 Ave) and East Kensington Elementary (2795 184 St) are confirmed by name and address, but no SD36 locator run for a Hazelmere address is on record and the sibling page itself flags a catchment mismatch for the 17–20 Avenue strip — a grandchild-care plan built on a Hazelmere school claim needs the exact address run first. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere-guide.html
