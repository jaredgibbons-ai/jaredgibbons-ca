# Mount Pleasant VE — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Mount Pleasant VE is the eastern half of the City of Vancouver's Mount Pleasant local area, east of Main Street: roughly Main Street to the Clark Drive/Nanaimo side, between East 2nd Avenue/Great Northern Way and East 16th Avenue/Kingsway. "VE" is the GVR board's Vancouver East suffix; the part west of Main is Mount Pleasant VW. (Area research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/mount-pleasant-ve.md, Section 1.)
- The Mount Pleasant Community Centre and the Vancouver Public Library's Mount Pleasant Branch are at 1 Kingsway, where Kingsway meets Main Street — the civic anchor at the sub-area's southwest corner. https://mountpleasantcc.ca/facilities-rentals/ and http://www.vpl.ca/location/mount-pleasant-branch
- The Broadway Plan covers this area: Council approved it June 22, 2022 as a 30-year framework (Clark Drive to Vine Street, 1st to 16th Avenues) built around the Broadway Subway, with buildings up to 20–30 storeys planned near the future Mount Pleasant Station at Main Street and Broadway. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx and https://syc.vancouver.ca/projects/broadway-plan/broadway-plan-summary-highlight-document.pdf
- The Province states the Broadway Subway "is on schedule to open in fall 2027," and that all four Broadway lanes between Main and Quebec Streets reopened by July 2026 after station construction. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026TT0046-000500.pdf
- Public schools named in the research file for the area include Mount Pleasant Elementary, Florence Nightingale Elementary and Queen Alexandra Elementary, with Vancouver Technical Secondary as the public secondary school (SD39); the one catchment actually run was 2600 East Broadway → Chief Maquinna Elementary / Vancouver Technical Secondary. Catchments for other addresses: UNCONFIRMED. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/039
- Flood exposure is concentrated at the north edge: the Great Northern Way lowland is former False Creek shoreline inside the City's designated floodplain, so parcels there are subject to the Building By-law's flood construction levels; the residential grid south of 2nd Avenue sits on the Mount Pleasant slope. Confirm per parcel. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates: it does not form a cluster. The two seniors buildings found are both in the blocks south of Broadway — Cavell Gardens on Sophia Street and The Aster / the former Mount Pleasant Lions Manor site on East 6th Avenue, west of Main's VE edge by a block in the Lions Manor case (325/349 East 6th Avenue sit just east of Main Street). No 55+ building was found on Kingsway, on East Broadway east of Main, or in the Great Northern Way/Flats lands. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

### Verified 55+ rental (not ownership)

- **Cavell Gardens — 2835 Sophia St (Mount Pleasant VE, south of East Broadway).** A rental retirement / supportive-housing residence. The operator describes it as a seniors community in Mount Pleasant's "Soma" district providing a lifestyle option for active seniors in a secure, affordable home-like residence, with studio, one-bedroom and one-bedroom deluxe (2-bath) suites, meals prepared by its chefs, amenities and activities, and respite stays available. A supportive-housing description of the same residence states the rent includes weekly housekeeping and limited laundry, meals in a common dining room, social/recreational/fitness programming and a 24-hour emergency response system. Rental only — nothing to buy. ### Subsidized seniors rental (non-profit, income-based — not ownership)

- **The Aster — 349 East 6th Ave.** An 82-unit affordable rental building (studio to three-bedroom, 12 storeys) owned and operated by the non-profit Brightside Community Homes Foundation, in partnership with BC Housing and Wall Financial Corporation; it opened in 2024. It is a MIXED building, not a seniors-only building: BC Housing describes the homes as for families, seniors and people living with disabilities, with 42 units at rent geared to income (30% of income), 19 units for people with very low incomes (including people on a basic seniors' pension) and 21 units at low-end market rates. The City of Vancouver's Non-Market Housing Inventory breaks the same 82 units down as 60 seniors units and 22 other units. SOURCE DIFFERENCE, flagged: the City inventory's 60/22 split and BC Housing's family/seniors/disabilities description are two different ways of counting the same building — neither source states a 55+ age bylaw or a seniors-only tenancy rule, so The Aster is listed here as subsidized mixed rental with a large seniors component, not as a verified 55+ building. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/new-affordable-accessible-homes-open-in-vancouver and https://app.vancouver.ca/NonMarketHousing_Net/ProjectSearch.aspx
- **Mount Pleasant Lions Manor — 325 East 6th Ave (former building, replaced).** Brightside's former 36-unit seniors housing building at this address, built without accessibility features, was replaced by The Aster next door; BC Housing states its tenants moved to The Aster continuing to pay the same income-based rents, and that the 325 East 6th Ave building will be sold to Wall Financial Corporation for redevelopment into market and social housing. Do not list 325 East 6th Ave as current seniors housing. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/new-affordable-accessible-homes-open-in-vancouver

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning is the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575.** For detached-house lots in this area, By-law No. 13817 replaced the RS districts with the R1-1 district, allowing up to 6 strata units (or 8 secured rental units) on former single-family lots. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf
- **City of Vancouver Seniors Housing Strategy (approved July 23, 2024):** the City's first seniors housing strategy, with a Seniors Housing Rezoning Policy (last amended May 19, 2026) to enable more seniors housing across all neighbourhoods and a Resident Relocation Policy adding protections for seniors in community care and assisted living residences in the event of a redevelopment. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/seniors-housing-strategy-address-needs-of-older-adults-july-2024.aspx and https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-rezoning-seniors-housing.pdf
- **The Broadway Plan governs this area's future form** (approved June 22, 2022; updated December 2024): buildings up to 20–30 storeys are planned near Mount Pleasant Station, and the plan's renter protections and tenant relocation requirements apply to the older Kingsway/Broadway rental buildings that are this area's affordability stock. The plan is policy, not zoning — most higher-density forms still require rezoning. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx
- **Finding subsidized seniors housing in Vancouver runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry and the Seniors Services Society's Seniors Housing Directory**, which the City points seniors to; City staff do not manage these facilities or their rental processes. BC Housing's Zone 6 (Vancouver) seniors directory is the Zone 6 listing used for the non-market buildings above. https://vancouver.ca/people-programs/find-supportive-housing-for-seniors.aspx and https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-6-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Tax calendar:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid balances; the City's Empty Homes Tax charges 3% of assessed value on homes declared or deemed empty, with the annual declaration due February 3, 2026 and payment due April 16, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Mount Pleasant VE?** None that any source verifies. There is no age-restricted strata building in the sub-area in any source checked; the seniors buildings here (Cavell Gardens, The Aster) are rental. A 55+ buyer in Mount Pleasant VE is buying a regular condo, townhouse or house with no age bylaw. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Cavell Gardens something I can buy into?** No. It is a rental retirement/supportive-housing residence at 2835 Sophia Street — you rent a suite with services (meals, weekly housekeeping, 24-hour emergency response) included in the arrangement described by its sources; there is no strata lot to purchase. https://cavellgardens.com/ and https://healthchoicesfirst.com/business/cavell-gardens
- **Q: Can I just apply to rent at The Aster on East 6th?** It is non-market housing operated by Brightside Community Homes Foundation: 42 of its 82 units are rent-geared-to-income, 19 are for very-low-income households and 21 are low-end market. It is a mixed family/seniors/disabilities building, not a seniors-only 55+ building, and tenancies run through the non-market housing system, not the open rental market. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/new-affordable-accessible-homes-open-in-vancouver
- **Q: I keep seeing Mount Pleasant Lions Manor listed as seniors housing. Is it still open?** No — as a separate building it has been replaced. Its 36 tenants moved next door into The Aster (349 East 6th Ave) at the same income-based rents, and BC Housing states the old 325 East 6th Ave building will be sold for redevelopment. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/new-affordable-accessible-homes-open-in-vancouver
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Vancouver?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent, or as an investment?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit I buy here?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022 — and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. If the building had a 55+ bylaw, the tenant would still have to satisfy it or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Where is the hospital for this area?** Cavell Gardens' operator states Mount St. Joseph Hospital is within a two-block radius of its Sophia Street site; Vancouver General Hospital is the city's large referral hospital a few kilometres west. Treat the two-block figure as operator-stated. https://cavellgardens.com/
- **Q: When does the SkyTrain actually reach Mount Pleasant?** On the Province's schedule, fall 2027: the Broadway Subway's Mount Pleasant Station is at Main Street and Broadway, and Broadway's four lanes at the station block reopened in July 2026. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026TT0046-000500.pdf
- **Q: If I hold a home here vacant, what does it cost me?** Budget for the City's Empty Homes Tax: an annual declaration is required, and homes declared or deemed vacant are taxed at 3% of assessed taxable value, on top of the ordinary tax calendar (advance taxes February 3, 2026; main taxes July 3, 2026, 5% penalty after each due date). https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The seniors housing on East 6th Avenue changed buildings, not just names.** The 36-unit Mount Pleasant Lions Manor (325 East 6th Ave, built without accessibility features) was replaced by the 82-unit Aster at 349 East 6th Ave, and its tenants carried their income-based rents across the lane with them. Any directory still showing Lions Manor as a standalone current building is out of date. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/new-affordable-accessible-homes-open-in-vancouver
- **The City counts The Aster's seniors component differently than BC Housing describes the building.** The City's Non-Market Housing Inventory records 60 seniors units and 22 other units; BC Housing's release describes one mixed building for families, seniors and people with disabilities with an income mix (42 RGI / 19 very-low-income / 21 low-end market). Both counts are cited in Section 2 rather than merged. https://app.vancouver.ca/NonMarketHousing_Net/ProjectSearch.aspx and https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/new-affordable-accessible-homes-open-in-vancouver
- **There is a seniors-serving neighbourhood house, but it is not housing.** Mount Pleasant Neighbourhood House at 800 East Broadway runs dedicated activities for older adults (55+) and a caregiver program; the research file also records that the Broadway Plan commits to renewal/expansion of the Neighbourhood House among its public benefits. It has no homes attached. https://vancouverseniors.ca/resources/category/immigration and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx
- **The sub-area's only retirement residence markets its hospital distance, not an age bylaw.** Cavell Gardens sells a services-included rental (meals, housekeeping, emergency response) on a side street a block off Main Street, and states Mount St. Joseph Hospital is within two blocks. It is not on the Assisted Living Registry results checked — a buyer comparing it with registered assisted living elsewhere in Vancouver is comparing two different regulatory products. https://cavellgardens.com/ and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3AVancouver
- **The Broadway Plan's renter protections were written for this area's exact stock.** The Kingsway walk-up rental buildings are the buildings the plan's tenant relocation requirements apply to, which changes redevelopment economics for those sites compared with strata or house sites — relevant to any 55+ buyer considering an older walk-up condo conversion here. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx
- **Citywide land-use direction changed in 2026.** The Vancouver Official Development Plan replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026 — area-plan references in older Mount Pleasant material now sit under the ODP framework. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf
