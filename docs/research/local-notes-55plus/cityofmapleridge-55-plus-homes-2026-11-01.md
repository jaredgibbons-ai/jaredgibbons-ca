# City of Maple Ridge — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Scope note: this is the CITY-WIDE file for the City of Maple Ridge — the slug behind it is the city's homes-for-sale hub page, not a single sub-area. It consolidates the verified city set carried in this program: 4 Fraserview Village strata in East Central, St. George's Village co-op (55+), the Alouette and Val Maria parks, and Legion Gardens life lease. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This overrides the "rentals not allowed" wording still displayed for Rosewood Court in building-data sources. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home parks run on different law.** Pad tenancies at Alouette and Val Maria are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and its Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003), not the Strata Property Act; the Regulation contemplates parks where sites are reserved for tenants who have reached 55, and a park landlord may refuse an assignment where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement in such a park. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Co-op and life-lease age rules are membership/contract rules, not strata bylaws.** St. George's Village's 55-year membership qualification, Haney Pioneer Village's 60+ threshold and the Legion life leases' terms are set by those organizations' own documents; the provincial strata exemptions do not automatically apply to them. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019.** Houses sit under zones including RS-1 (s. 605 — minimum lot area 668.0 m², width 18.0 m, depth 27.0 m, maximum coverage 40%); Town Centre parcels carry Town Centre commercial, multi-family and comprehensive development zones under the same bylaw. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **OCP CITATION — CONTESTED, flagged and NOT resolved here.** Earlier research in this program cites the Maple Ridge Official Community Plan two ways: OCP Bylaw No. 7060-2014 and OCP Bylaw No. 7998-2024, with OCP and area-plan updates adopted in November 2025 (OCP, Hammond, Silver Valley, Town Centre) and Albion Area Plan updates adopted in October 2025 also recorded in the batch-15 research file. Do not publish either number as the sole current citation until the City confirms it. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/official-community-plan and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/cityofmapleridge.md
- **Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan adoption date — UNVERIFIED.** The live page states "adopted January 27, 2026"; City sources read for the research file show initial bylaw readings at the end of 2025 with pre-applications being accepted, and no adoption on that date. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/cityofmapleridge.md
- **Suites and added units:** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-family and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **ALR housing frame (rural east side):** any Maple Ridge parcel in the Agricultural Land Reserve follows the ALC frame of one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). Whether a specific parcel is in the ALR: UNVERIFIED at parcel level. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property taxes:** Maple Ridge property taxes are due on the first business day in July — July 2, 2026 for 2026 — with a 5% penalty from July 3, 2026 on outstanding balances up until September 1, 2026, and an additional 5% on any balance outstanding on September 2, 2026; an unclaimed home owner grant counts as outstanding. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/property-taxes/understanding-property-taxes
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** for this area (Zone 4 directory, May 2025), with some buildings apply-direct to the operating society. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Tax relief at 55+:** the Province's regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Maple Ridge?** Four strata buildings, all in one community: Rosewood Court, Emerald Manor, The Willows and Castlemount at Fraserview Village in East Central. Beyond strata, the buyable 55+ forms are shares in St. George's Village co-operative (55+ membership), a manufactured home on a rented pad at Alouette or Val Maria, and a Legion life lease. Every other sub-area verifies zero buyable 55+ stock. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Haney Pioneer Village a 55+ option?** It is a seniors co-operative, but its bar is 60+, not 55+ — every source states 60+. A 55-to-59-year-old does not qualify on the sources checked. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a Fraserview Village strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases, and live-in caregivers are exempt. In the co-op, the parks and the life leases, the organization's own documents govern instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, including the "rentals not allowed" wording still shown for Fraserview Village buildings in building-data sources. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: How is buying in Alouette or Val Maria different from buying a strata?** You own the manufactured home and rent the pad under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act — there is no strata and no freehold land. Listing-stated terms differ sharply by park (Alouette: no dogs, indoor cat only, "no financing permitted in this community"; Val Maria: 2 small pets allowed) — confirm the current tenancy agreement and park rules before writing. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Q: Where is the hospital, and can I stay in Maple Ridge if I need care later?** Ridge Meadows Hospital (11666 Laity St) is in the city. The in-city ladder runs from the Fraserview Village strata and the Legion life leases, through the 224 Street retirement residences (Chartwell Willow, Sunwood, Greystone Manor on Ritchie Ave), to assisted living at Royal Crescent Gardens and the independent/assisted/complex-care campus at Maple Ridge Seniors Village. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital and https://www.retirementconcepts.com/locations/maple-ridge-seniors-village/
- **Q: Is the West Coast Express a real commute option from the 55+ cluster?** It is a commuter service only: five trains in each direction on weekdays (mornings toward Vancouver, evenings toward Mission), with no weekend or holiday service, per TransLink's timetable effective Jan 5, 2026. Port Haney Station is the station nearest the Fraserview Village pocket, per the live page's station naming. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. With only four strata buildings in the city, the pool is also geographically thin. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I add a suite for a caregiver or family member?** In a house, the City's route is one secondary suite inside the building (maximum 40% of gross floor area), with up to three, four or six units on eligible lots under the SSMUH bylaws — but not on floodplain properties, where Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits suites. In a 55+ strata, a live-in caregiver is exempt from the age bylaw under Regulation 7.01. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes at 55+?** The Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
