## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. For a bare land strata like South Pointe, the same Act and the same age-bylaw rules apply as for a condo strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Open-house conduct is a representation question, not a viewing formality.** In BC, licensed real estate professionals must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party; disclosure — whether the professional can represent the consumer, the duties owed, and how to file a complaint with BCFSA — must happen at the outset of dealings, and dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances. An identification or sign-in requirement for open house visitors is NOT stated in the BCFSA consumer source — UNVERIFIED as a rule; a host may still ask, on the seller's instructions. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **How Delta Green's 55+ status was secured at approval:** the City's 2018 public hearing material for the 51 Street site discusses the project's "55+ age category" and a restrictive covenant on "adaptable-ready" design under the BC Building Code, so residents can age in place with future unit modifications. A strata age bylaw and a covenant are different instruments — confirm which one(s) are registered on title and in the bylaws via the Form B and strata documents. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/179807/
- **Bare land strata specifics that matter at South Pointe:** in a bare land strata the owner typically insures and maintains the house itself while the strata maintains common property (gates, internal roads, landscaping). Listings for South Pointe record bare land fees of $113/mo and $173/mo at their respective listing dates — treat both as dated listing figures; the current fee, and what it covers, comes from the Form B and the strata's financial statements. https://plsrealestate.com/british_columbia/house/delta/R2435695-5359_jibset_bay and https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/5379-regatta-way
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017** assigns zoning parcel by parcel; South Pointe's listing data records zoning CD98A — a site-specific comprehensive development zone — and the Apartment Residential 6 (RA6) zone added in 2024 lists "seniors congregate housing" as a permitted principal use for six-storey apartment development under the OCP's Neighbourhood Centres and Corridors designation. https://www.zolo.ca/ladner-real-estate/5371-regatta-way and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future")**, adopted July 8, 2024, is Delta's consolidated OCP covering Ladner. It has no lettered area-plan schedules — older references to a Ladner or East Ladner area plan as a schedule of the current OCP are citing the previous plan. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/ and http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Suites and SSMUH:** the RS1 zone lists a secondary suite as an accessory use to a single detached dwelling, and Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing amendments add coach house and garden suite options where the zoning applies — relevant to a 55+ buyer at a Ladner open house weighing a house with a suite for a caregiver or family member. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Property tax deferment at 55+:** BC's regular deferment program lets a homeowner 55 or older during the current year defer annual property taxes on a principal residence as a provincial loan; Delta's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026 (5% penalty after, a further 5% after September 2, 2026), and the City tax page links the deferment and Home Owner Grant programs. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **SnapStats does not cover South Delta / Ladner** — its Delta coverage is North Delta only, and it publishes no open-house category — so no market figures and no open-house counts are stated in this file: UNVERIFIED. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-open-houses.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Ladner, and will they be on the open-houses page?** Two verified complexes for the whole community: Delta Green (4686 51 St — 24-unit strata condo, built 2021, EPS8064) and South Pointe (Regatta Way / Jibset Bay / Tidewater Bay off Ferry Road — gated bare land strata ranchers, built 1989, NWS2528). They appear on this topic page only in a week when a unit in one of them is both listed and holding an open house — with 24 units at Delta Green and an UNCONFIRMED unit count at South Pointe, that is an occasional event, not a standing feature of the feed. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3091870-107-4686-51-street-delta-bc-v4k-2v7 and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3075824-5371-regatta-way-delta-bc-v4k-4r9
- **Q: Does every listing on the Ladner open-houses page hold an open house?** Not on the evidence of the cards read for the area research: five of the six fully read cards carried an open-house time block and one (a Ladner Rural farm listing) carried no open-house label or time at all, so treat an unlabelled card as a listing to confirm privately, not as an open house. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-open-houses.html
- **Q: How do I know the open house time on the page is right?** Check it carefully and confirm it: on every labelled card read for the research, the card's top time block is exactly one hour later than the "Open House on..." line directly under it, and the listing text on the two cards whose text states a time matches the top block. Which of the stated times is authoritative is UNVERIFIED — confirm the actual time with the listing brokerage before setting out. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-open-houses.html
- **Q: Do I need my own agent to attend a Ladner open house, and can the host act for me too?** No official source opened for this research requires you to bring an agent. BCFSA states a licensee must work for either the buyer or the seller, must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who represents another party, and cannot represent two clients whose interests conflict except in the rarest of circumstances (dual agency, prohibited in BC in 2018) — so the host at the door cannot simply "also represent" a buyer who walks in. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live at Delta Green or South Pointe?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a Ladner 55+ complex for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. Delta Green's own rental position must be confirmed in its bylaws — no building source in this research states it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is River West 55+? It keeps coming up in Ladner searches.** Not verified. One aggregator page shows an age-restriction field with no value in it; current listings for the building record pet and rental bylaws only, with no age bylaw. Treat River West as a general-market riverfront building unless the strata's registered bylaws, produced on a purchase, say otherwise. https://www.estateblock.com/ladner/202-4743-w-river-rd-52494
- **Q: What is Marina Park, and can I buy there?** No — Marina Park (4843 48 Ave) is subsidized non-market rental: 50 one-bedroom units at 30% of monthly gross income, age requirement 55+, applied for through BC Housing's Housing Registry. It will never appear as a purchase on an open-houses page; it is a rental option for income-qualifying seniors. https://www.seniorsservicessociety.ca/location/marina-park/
- **Q: Where is the hospital, and what if I need care later?** Delta Hospital (5800 Mountain View Blvd) is Fraser Health's Delta community hospital with 24/7 emergency care, beside Ladner Exchange. For care housing in Ladner itself, Augustine House (3820 Arthur Dr) offers independent and assisted living plus care, including a 20-unit Fraser Health assisted living program at 55+ accessed through a case manager. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital and http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
