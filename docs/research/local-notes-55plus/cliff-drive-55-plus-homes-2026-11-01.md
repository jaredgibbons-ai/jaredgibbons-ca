# Cliff Drive (Tsawwassen), Delta — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Plain finding up front: there is no verified 55+ housing of any kind inside the Cliff Drive pocket. The pocket is detached houses around Cliff Drive Elementary. Delta's verified 55+ ownership stock sits in Tsawwassen Central (The Lexington, Tsawwassen Greene) and in Ladner (South Pointe) — see Section 2.**

- Nearby sub-areas: English Bluff to the west, Tsawwassen Central to the east, Pebble Hill / Tsawwassen East to the south-east, Beach Grove to the south (working geography from City and SD37 sources in the research file). ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/cliff-drive.md

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017** is Delta's zoning bylaw; seniors housing in Delta is typically delivered through site-specific comprehensive development zones (The Waterford's site is CD Zone No. 276, permitting seniors' congregate housing and a seniors' congregate care facility). https://delta.civicweb.net/document/71542/
- **"Seniors congregate housing" is a named use in Delta's newer apartment zones.** The Apartment Residential 6 (RA6) zone added to Bylaw No. 7600 lists apartment building and seniors congregate housing as principal uses, for six-storey residential development along arterial and collector streets under the OCP's Neighbourhood Centres and Corridors designation. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future")** is Delta's current OCP — a single consolidated plan; there is no Tsawwassen area-plan schedule in it (the old area plans were schedules of the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, which 8400 superseded). https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing:** Delta's SSMUH rules allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide, subject to the bylaw and to any hazard or streamside permit area on the lot — the legal route for a 55+ Cliff Drive owner to add a suite or coach house for a caregiver or family member. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Property taxes:** Delta's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Tax relief that matters at 55+:** the Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
- **Subsidized seniors rental runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** (Zone 3 — Richmond and West Delta directory, May 2025), which is how the KinVillage subsidized units are filled. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ building right in Cliff Drive?** No — none was verified in any source. The pocket is detached housing; the nearest verified 55+ ownership is in Tsawwassen Central (The Lexington, 1150 54A St; Tsawwassen Greene, 1140 55 St). Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: I want to stay in a detached house but in a 55+ setting. What is the closest match in Delta?** South Pointe in Ladner's Neilsen Grove — a gated 55+ bare land strata of detached single-level ranchers on their own strata lots (plan NWS2528, built 1989). Buyers own the strata lot and the house; the strata carries the gates, roads and common landscaping. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/5379-regatta-way
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. Older bylaw summaries (like The Lexington's "rentals not permitted") cannot be relied on — read the current Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I add a suite or a coach house to a Cliff Drive house for a caregiver or my parents?** Delta's SSMUH rules allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide, subject to the zoning bylaw and to any hazard or streamside permit area on the lot. Confirm the specific lot's permit areas with the City before designing. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or a buyer who purchases without living in the unit. That trade-off is built into every BC 55+ strata since the 2022 law change confined age bylaws to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, in Ladner — Fraser Health's community hospital for Delta with a 24/7 emergency department. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- **Q: Is Tsawwassen in a floodplain?** Flood protection in Delta is a city-wide system: the City is the diking authority and operates and maintains over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure — lot-level flood construction requirements still have to be checked parcel by parcel. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: What help exists if I want subsidized or below-market seniors rental in Tsawwassen instead of buying?** KinVillage in Tsawwassen Central: The Tides (152 homes — 106 subsidized, 46 market rental) and the independent-living courts, with eligibility including age 55 or older and subsidized rent at 30% of household gross income through BC Housing's Housing Registry. https://www.kinvillage.org/independent-living and https://canada.constructconnect.com/joc/news/projects/2025/11/seniors-find-new-homes-as-152-units-open-in-delta
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the Tsawwassen 55+ buildings?** Building by building: The Lexington's sources record one pet allowed but no dogs; Tsawwassen Greene's listing records one dog or cat to 12 inches at the shoulder. KinVillage's rental buildings do not allow pets. Confirm in the strata bylaws / tenancy documents for the specific building. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/the-lexington-1150-54a-street and https://www.kinvillage.org/independent-living

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Cliff Drive" has no official boundary — it is an MLS sub-area name.** The City of Delta does not publish a neighbourhood boundary for it, so two listing feeds can draw the pocket differently; the anchor on the ground is Cliff Drive Elementary at 5025 12 Ave. https://cd.deltasd.bc.ca/ and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/cliff-drive.md
- **The 55+ cluster one pocket over is mid-rebuild.** In Tsawwassen Central, KinVillage's The Tides (152 homes) replaced the 86-home North Court building, and Phase 2 at 1050 54A Street (143 more affordable seniors rental units plus a replacement community centre, City File LU009921) is in the rezoning process — the seniors campus south-east of Cliff Drive will be a construction zone in phases. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/new-affordable-seniors-housing-breaks-ground-in-delta and https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009921
- **Delta's 2024 OCP quietly removed the old area plans.** Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) is a single consolidated plan with no Tsawwassen area-plan schedule — the schedules buyers and agents still cite belonged to the superseded OCP Bylaw No. 3950. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Seniors congregate housing is now a named principal use in Delta's RA6 apartment zone** — the zoning route for future purpose-built seniors buildings along Delta's arterial and collector corridors, added to Zoning Bylaw No. 7600. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **School catchments do not follow the sub-area names.** SD37 catchments are address-based; a Tsawwassen North sample address returned Ladner Elementary and Delta Secondary in the district locator during this program's research — run the exact address rather than assuming. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
