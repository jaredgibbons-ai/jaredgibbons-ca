# Terra Nova (Richmond) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Terra Nova is the north-west Richmond pocket on Lulu Island, west of No. 1 Road at the end of Westminster Highway, beside the Middle Arm / Sturgeon Bank foreshore. The City Heritage Inventory describes the Terra Nova area as from No. 1 Road westward, with Westminster Highway to the south, the Fraser River to the north and west, and No. 1 Road in the east, including the foreshore outside the dyke to the north and west. https://richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/inventory/Details.aspx?ID=160 and https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?id=80
- The board sub-area name is "Terra Nova"; the City plans the same ground as a sub-area in the Thompson planning area — the City lists the plan as Schedule 2.2B Thompson (Terra Nova) in the Richmond Official Community Plan, and the City Heritage Inventory lists its planning area name as Thompson. https://Richmond.Ca/city-hall/strategies/sale.htm?PageMode=HTML
- Nearby areas: Thompson / Granville to the east across No. 1 Road, Seafair to the south across Westminster Highway, Sea Island across the Middle Arm to the north, and the Sturgeon Bank Wildlife Management Area on the foreshore to the west. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?id=80
- Roads: Westminster Highway, No. 1 Road and River Road are the main roads; Terra Nova Rural Park is at the west end of Westminster Highway west of No. 1 Road. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?id=80
- Schools: Spul'u'kwuks Elementary, 5999 Blanshard Drive, is physically inside the pocket; J.N. Burnett Secondary, 5011 Granville Avenue, is in the wider Thompson area east of No. 1 Road. School District 38 catchments are address-based with no middle-school tier; for the sample address 5999 Blanshard Drive the SD38 locator gives Spul'u'kwuks Elementary and Burnett Secondary. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
- Parks and trails: Terra Nova Rural Park, 2631 Westminster Highway, is a 63-acre site with trails, an Adventure Play Environment and the Sharing Farm, along the Pacific Flyway and adjacent to the Sturgeon Bank Wildlife Management Area — and it is a NO DOGS ALLOWED area under Animal Control Bylaw 8612. The West Dyke Trail runs along the dyke from Terra Nova Rural Park to Garry Point Park. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?id=80
- Recreation, library, health: Thompson Community Centre is at 5151 Granville Avenue in the wider Thompson area; there is no library branch in the pocket (nearest full branch named on an official source is Brighouse (Main), 100-7700 Minoru Gate); the nearest hospital is Richmond Hospital, 7000 Westminster Highway, and Richmond City Centre Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at #110, 4671 No. 3 Road. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/centres/thompson.htm and https://rpl.yourlibrary.ca/about/hours-locations and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/richmond-city-centre-urgent-and-primary-care-centre/plan-your-visit
- Transit: bus service on No. 1 Road and Westminster Highway connects the pocket to Richmond–Brighouse Station on the Canada Line; no Canada Line station is inside the sub-area. Route-level detail beyond that connection: UNVERIFIED. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt410.pdf
- Where the seniors housing actually is: in exactly one place — **Quilchena Green, 5531 Cornwall Drive**, the gated 55+ townhouse complex on Cornwall Drive in the pocket's residential streets west of No. 1 Road. It is the sub-area's only verified age-restricted housing of any tenure; there is no 55+ rental building, retirement residence, assisted-living residence or subsidized seniors building verified inside Terra Nova. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. This is directly live at Quilchena Green, where a 2016 MLS sheet records a 45+ restriction and current listings record 55+ (Section 2, conflict note). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, whatever a building database still records (see the Quilchena Green rental conflict in Section 2). Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Zoning — two regimes meet in this pocket.** Quilchena Green carries RTL 1 zoning on its MLS record. For farm land beside the pocket, Agriculture (AG1) in Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 Section 14.1 provides for farming and compatible uses consistent with the Agricultural Land Reserve, one principal dwelling unit per lot, and a secondary suite as a secondary use; subdivision in the ALR is not permitted unless approved by the Agricultural Land Commission (2.0 ha minimum lot area where that approval is not required), and single detached housing is not to be built on a lot under 828.0 m² in that zone. https://faithwilson.com/listings/75-5531-cornwall-drive/ and https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=53061
- **Suites on non-ALR lots:** the City states Richmond allows at least one secondary suite and/or an accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots — but AG1 farm lots beside the pocket follow the ALR framework instead (Agricultural Land Commission guidance: one principal residence and, if the regulation conditions are met, one additional residence per parcel). https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Plan: Richmond Official Community Plan Schedule 2.2B Thompson (Terra Nova)** is the sub-area plan; the city-wide plan is the City of Richmond 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000. https://Richmond.Ca/city-hall/strategies/sale.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **Flood — the pocket's west and north edges are the dike itself:** Richmond is protected by a perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations (49 km of dikes, 39 pump stations city-wide), designed for a 1:500 flood event, with dike raising from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise under way; the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets setbacks from a dike right-of-way and bars building below the flood plain construction level in its Schedule B. Quilchena Green listings themselves market the complex as "just steps from the Richmond Dyke" — the amenity and the flood file are the same feature. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm and https://vancouvernashdom.com/richmond-terra-nova-townhouses/SearchResults.form/?_pg=2
- **Property taxes and utilities:** City of Richmond property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. Richmond's 2026 typical single-family metered utility total is $2,251 per year, including a $342 flood protection line. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm and https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/finance/utility-fees/utilityrates.htm

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Terra Nova?** One complex: Quilchena Green, 5531 Cornwall Drive — 91 freehold strata townhouses, built 1992, gated, with the current MLS record stating "Age Restricted 55+." Legally it cannot be 45+ today — sub-55 age bylaws have been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 — but the registered bylaw's exact wording is what governs, so read it in the strata documents before relying on either listing. Confirm the complex's own bylaw wording on the Form B. https://faithwilson.com/listings/75-5531-cornwall-drive/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy at Quilchena Green for a parent, or as an investment?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted under provincial law — a BC age bylaw governs who may reside, and the Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a 55+ parent can be the resident. (One listing's "owner on title" wording conflicts with that — another reason the registered bylaw has to be read; see Section 2.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a Quilchena Green unit?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, which is why the sources disagree (one database still says "rentals not allowed"; current MLS data says rentals allowed with restrictions). Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/quilchena-green-5531-cornwall-drive
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Quilchena Green?** Building and listing sources record pets allowed with restrictions. The specific limits (number, size) are UNCONFIRMED from public sources — check the bylaws. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/quilchena-green-5531-cornwall-drive and https://faithwilson.com/listings/75-5531-cornwall-drive/
- **Q: What does the complex offer day to day?** Listings and building sources describe a gated complex with a live-in caretaker, a clubhouse, a swirlpool/hot tub and landscaped grounds, steps from the dyke trails — and Terra Nova Rural Park (63 acres, trails, Sharing Farm) and the West Dyke Trail are the pocket's outdoor amenity, with the park's no-dogs rule under Bylaw 8612 worth knowing before a dog owner buys. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/quilchena-green-5531-cornwall-drive and https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?id=80
- **Q: Is Terra Nova in a floodplain?** All of Richmond sits behind the perimeter dike system; Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and a flood plain construction level, and this pocket's west and north edges are the foreshore outside the dyke — get the parcel-level dike and flood-construction position early on any purchase here. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in Terra Nova?** No retirement residence, assisted-living residence or care home with a Terra Nova address was verified — the care step means leaving the pocket for Richmond's City Centre / Steveston / Hamilton residences or long-term care elsewhere in the city. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B1%5D=city%3ARichmond&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc
- **Q: Does the farm land beside the pocket change what I can do with a house lot?** For ALR/AG1 parcels, yes: Agricultural Land Commission rules allow one principal residence plus, if conditions are met, one additional residence per parcel, and ALC approval gates subdivision (2.0 ha minimum where approval is not required) — a completely different rulebook from the non-ALR house streets, so establish which side of the line a parcel sits on before pricing suite or rebuild potential. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=53061

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The complex's name points at the wrong neighbourhood.** "Quilchena Green" is named for Quilchena, but the board, listing sources and building sources all place 5531 Cornwall Drive in Terra Nova — and the separate Quilchena pocket (around Quilchena Elementary on Moresby Drive, south-east of here) has no 55+ housing at all. A buyer searching "Quilchena 55+" and a buyer searching "Terra Nova 55+" are looking for this same one complex. https://www.estateblock.com/richmond/72-5531-cornwall-dr-55264 and https://www.zolo.ca/richmond-real-estate/5531-cornwall-drive/72
- **The pocket's Indigenous and settler names are both in daily use:** Indigenous Peoples used Terra Nova for seasonal fishing camps during salmon and sturgeon runs, and the area was named Spul'u'kwuks — now the elementary school's name — before Newfoundland fishermen renamed it Terra Nova, meaning "new land"; the Terra Nova Cannery was built here and operated until the middle of the 20th century. https://www.richmondmuseum.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Terra-Nova_Self-Guided-Historical-Tour-Brochure.pdf
- **The big park is a no-dogs park.** Terra Nova Rural Park is a NO DOGS ALLOWED area under Animal Control Bylaw 8612 — dog owners in Quilchena Green (where pets are allowed with restrictions inside the complex) walk the dyke trail, not the 63-acre park across the road. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?id=80
- **Shopping in the pocket is officially unconfirmed.** Terra Nova Village at 3671–3679 Westminster Highway and any grocery anchor there: UNVERIFIED on any official source in this pass — listings name a "Terra Nova Shopping Centre" as an amenity, but no City or operator source checked confirms its tenant mix. https://www.zolo.ca/richmond-real-estate/3555-westminster-highway/29
- **Old MLS data for this complex is actively misleading twice over:** the 2016 sheet's 45+ age figure is a now-invalid bylaw class, and the building database's "rentals not allowed" line describes a bylaw class the Province voided in 2022. Terra Nova's one 55+ complex is exactly where stale restriction data does the most damage — date every restriction claim. https://coreyzach.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/86-5531-Cornwall-Drive-Richmond.pdf and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/quilchena-green-5531-cornwall-drive
- **The dike is part of the utility bill:** Richmond's 2026 typical single-family metered utility total of $2,251 per year includes a $342 flood protection line — in Terra Nova that charge buys the dike at the end of the street. https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/finance/utility-fees/utilityrates.htm
