# South Cambie — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- South Cambie is the City of Vancouver's Cambie Street corridor between roughly West 16th Avenue and the Fraser River (Marine Drive), including Cambie Village and the area around the King Edward and Oakridge–41st stations' northern edges. The GVR board sub-area name is "South Cambie." Nearby areas: Oakridge, Riley Park, Shaughnessy, Douglas Park, Marpole and Langara. (Area research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-cambie.md, Section 1.)
- The corridor is planned under the Cambie Corridor Plan: Council approved it May 9, 2011 to guide transit-oriented growth along Cambie Street from 16th Avenue to the Fraser River, centred on the Canada Line stations at Marine Drive, Langara–49th Avenue, Oakridge–41st Avenue and King Edward; Phase 3 (2018) extended policy to the neighbourhood streets within walking distance of the stations, adding townhouse and low-rise apartment forms off the arterial. https://www.cip-icu.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Cambie-Corridor-Plan-FINAL-Aug-2011_WEB.pdf and https://council.vancouver.ca/20180417/documents/rr2_presentation.pdf
- The corridor's institutional west side: BC Children's Hospital and BC Women's Hospital & Health Centre share the Provincial Health Services Authority campus at 4500 Oak Street (Oak Street at West 28th Avenue) — one of the largest health-care employment and patient-trip generators on the west side. http://www.bcchildrens.ca/
- Queen Elizabeth Park (52 hectares, 125 metres above sea level, the highest point in the City of Vancouver) anchors the corridor's east side, with Douglas Park, Tisdall Park and Oak Meadows Park also inventoried in the City's corridor park planning; Douglas Park Community Centre is at 801 West 22nd Avenue. https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/queen-elizabeth-park.aspx and http://vancouver.ca/files/cov/oakridge-park-open-house-boards-december-2017.pdf
- The Heather Lands, on Heather Street between West 33rd and West 37th Avenues, is the corridor's biggest redevelopment: on March 31, 2026 Council approved a replacement rezoning for 16 buildings of 4 to 46 storeys with roughly 4,200 homes, including about 2,940 discounted leasehold strata (provincial Attainable Housing Initiative) units, about 700 market leasehold strata units and about 610 social housing units, on MST Nations / Canada Lands Company land. That product is leasehold strata, not freehold — and no source describes any of it as age-restricted. https://www.shapeyourcity.ca/4911-5255-heather-637-657-w-37-ave-and-620-689-w-35-ave
- Schools run by SD39: the sample address run in the research file (500 West 20th Ave, Edith Cavell Elementary's own address) returns Edith Cavell Elementary / Eric Hamber Secondary — not Sir Winston Churchill Secondary, even though Churchill (7055 Heather St) is the secondary school physically inside the corridor. Location is not catchment. Catchments for other corridor addresses: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.vsb.bc.ca/edith-cavell and https://www.vsb.bc.ca/schools/sir-winston-churchill
- Flood rules for the corridor's low south end: the streets running down to Marine Drive sit at the edge of the Fraser River floodplain environment, and the Vancouver Building By-law's Flood Plain Standards set a minimum flood construction level of 4.6 m (GVRD datum) for new construction in designated flood plains. Lot-by-lot designation must be confirmed with the City. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/guidelines-flood-plain-standards-requirements.pdf
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates: in one block and on one street. The corridor's only verified 55+ ownership building, OPAL by Element, occupies the south side of West King Edward Avenue between Yukon and Cambie Streets; the corridor's care buildings — Honoria Conway assisted living and Youville Residence long-term care — are both on Heather Street, on the Queen Elizabeth Park side. No 55+ building was found on Cambie Street itself south of King Edward, or anywhere in the corridor's Marine Drive end. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- **OPAL by Element — 438 West King Edward Ave (Cambie Village, at Yukon/Cambie).** The one verified age-restricted strata ownership building in this corridor — and a mixed-tenure complex, so read the tenure split carefully. The development comprises three connected structures (one six-storey and two four-storey buildings) spanning the block on the south side of King Edward Avenue between Yukon and Cambie Streets, completed/opened in 2019. The original project breakdown was 130 units in total: 44 condos for sale, 56 for rental, and 30 complex-care suites, with one- and two-bedroom condo options. Listings for resale units record Title to Land: Freehold Strata, sub-type Strata, and the MLS field "Senior Community: Yes," and state the rule as "One resident must be 55+." Amenities recorded in listings include roughly 30,000 sq ft of indoor and outdoor amenity space, 24-hour concierge, theatre, rooftop patio/garden, exercise facilities and dining. The operator frames the model as a continuum: residents can live independently, access assisted living, and have priority access to the complex-care units with 24-hour registered nursing care in the same complex. Confirm against the strata documents and the current service agreement which parts of a purchase are strata fees and which are mandatory service charges — the sources checked describe both ownership and care services at this address, and the current fee structure for a specific unit is UNCONFIRMED from those sources. http://elementlifestyleretirement.com/blog/2017/01/20/opal-by-element-our-revolutionary-model-is-catching-the-attention-of-editors-at-the-vancouver-sun/ and https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3089486-438-w-king-edward-avenue-104-vancouver-bc-v5y-0m5/brochure and https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/438-west-king-edward-avenue/671 and http://opal.elementlifestyleretirement.com/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. At OPAL the listing-stated form of the rule is "one resident must be 55+" — confirm the exact bylaw wording in the strata documents, because the provincial framework attaches the requirement and the exemptions to a "specified resident." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3089486-438-w-king-edward-avenue-104-vancouver-bc-v5y-0m5/brochure
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning is the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575.** For detached-house lots away from the corridor arterials, By-law No. 13817 replaced the RS districts with the R1-1 district, allowing up to 6 strata units (or 8 secured rental units) on former single-family lots. On or near Cambie Street, the Cambie Corridor Plan designation — not just the base zoning — drives what a lot can become, and most corridor densities still require a rezoning application. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf and https://council.vancouver.ca/20180417/documents/rr2_presentation.pdf
- **City of Vancouver Seniors Housing Strategy (approved July 23, 2024):** the City's first seniors housing strategy, with a Seniors Housing Rezoning Policy (last amended May 19, 2026) to enable more seniors housing across all neighbourhoods and a Resident Relocation Policy adding protections for seniors in community care and assisted living residences in the event of a redevelopment — the policy that would govern any future redevelopment of the Heather Street care campus. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/seniors-housing-strategy-address-needs-of-older-adults-july-2024.aspx and https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-rezoning-seniors-housing.pdf
- **Tenure warning specific to this corridor:** the research file records four different ownership structures within one sub-area — freehold side-street houses, strata corridor apartments/townhouses, purpose-built rental buildings, and the Heather Lands' predominantly leasehold strata on MST/CLC land. OPAL's condos are recorded in listings as Freehold Strata; the future Heather Lands strata product is leasehold. Do not carry OPAL's tenure across to Heather Lands product, or vice versa. (Area research file, Section 5; Heather Lands: https://www.shapeyourcity.ca/4911-5255-heather-637-657-w-37-ave-and-620-689-w-35-ave)
- **Tax calendar:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid balances; the City's Empty Homes Tax charges 3% of assessed value on homes declared or deemed empty, with the annual declaration due February 3, 2026 and payment due April 16, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in South Cambie?** One building: OPAL by Element at 438 West King Edward Avenue, where resale condos are recorded as Freehold Strata with "Senior Community: Yes" and a "one resident must be 55+" rule. It is a mixed complex — the original breakdown was 44 condos for sale alongside 56 rental units and 30 complex-care suites — so a buyer is purchasing a strata condo inside a retirement community with on-site care, not a unit in a conventional condo tower. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3089486-438-w-king-edward-avenue-104-vancouver-bc-v5y-0m5/brochure and http://elementlifestyleretirement.com/blog/2017/01/20/opal-by-element-our-revolutionary-model-is-catching-the-attention-of-editors-at-the-vancouver-sun/
- **Q: Besides strata fees, what else will I pay at OPAL?** The sources checked establish that OPAL combines strata ownership with an operating retirement community (concierge, dining, wellness amenities, assisted living and complex-care access), but they do not publish a current mandatory service-charge schedule for a specific resale unit. Treat the service agreement as a required document: the amount and what it covers are UNCONFIRMED from public sources and must be confirmed against the strata documents and the operator's current agreement before an offer. http://opal.elementlifestyleretirement.com/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live at OPAL?** The listing-stated rule is that one resident must be 55+, and provincial rules separately require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). The operator's own framing is intergenerational — it describes residents ranging from children to grandparents in its dining/community program — but the legal test is the strata bylaw plus the provincial exemptions, so confirm the bylaw wording in the strata documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://opal.elementlifestyleretirement.com/farm-to-table-dining/
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy an OPAL condo for a parent, or as an investment?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out an OPAL unit?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022 — and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. Two OPAL-specific catches: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ rule or an exemption, and 56 units in the same complex were built as operator rentals, so confirm how the strata and the operator treat owner rentals in this building. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in the corridor?** The corridor's ladder is unusually complete: independent strata living with services available (OPAL), assisted living (Honoria Conway-Heather, 4875 Heather St; OPAL's own assisted living), then long-term care (Youville Residence, 4950 Heather St, 42 residents; OPAL's 30 complex-care suites with 24-hour registered nursing care). The care homes are health-system/care admissions, not purchases. https://www.providencehealthcare.org/en/our-facilities-locations/our-long-term-care-facilities and https://travelclinic.vch.ca/en/location-service/long-term-care-youville-residence
- **Q: Is Park Langara, down by the golf course, a seniors building? It feels like one.** No. Its MLS data records "Senior Community: No." It is a general-market 2025 strata building at 6859 Cambie Street. https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/6859-cambie-street
- **Q: Will the Heather Lands give me a cheaper 55+ option nearby?** The approved Heather Lands product (roughly 4,200 homes from March 31, 2026) is predominantly discounted and market LEASEHOLD strata on MST Nations/Canada Lands land, plus social housing — leasehold, not freehold, with no age restriction or seniors designation in any source, and no published build-out timing. Price and finance any future unit there as leasehold from the first conversation. https://www.shapeyourcity.ca/4911-5255-heather-637-657-w-37-ave-and-620-689-w-35-ave
- **Q: Which SkyTrain stations serve the corridor?** The Canada Line stations at King Edward Avenue and Oakridge–41st Avenue, with Langara–49th Avenue at the corridor's south end — King Edward Station is the station OPAL's listings cite at its doorstep. https://www.cip-icu.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Cambie-Corridor-Plan-FINAL-Aug-2011_WEB.pdf
- **Q: Is the south end of the corridor in a floodplain?** The streets down to Marine Drive sit at the edge of the Fraser River floodplain environment, and new construction in designated flood plains must meet a minimum flood construction level of 4.6 m (GVRD datum). The King Edward / Queen Elizabeth Park end sits on the park's high ground (the park is the city's highest point at 125 m). Confirm designation lot by lot with the City. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/guidelines-flood-plain-standards-requirements.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/queen-elizabeth-park.aspx

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **OPAL is three tenures wearing one name.** The same address (438 West King Edward Ave) contains strata condos you can buy, operator rental suites you cannot, and complex-care suites you are admitted to. A listing, a rental advertisement and a care brochure for "OPAL" are three different products; the original split was 44 for-sale condos, 56 rentals and 30 complex-care suites out of 130 units. http://elementlifestyleretirement.com/blog/2017/01/20/opal-by-element-our-revolutionary-model-is-catching-the-attention-of-editors-at-the-vancouver-sun/
- **The "one resident must be 55+" wording is the listing form of the rule, not the bylaw.** Listings state the rule that way; the provincial framework instead attaches the age requirement and its exemptions (spouse/partner, caregiver, legacy resident, defined children) to a "specified resident." A buyer whose household does not fit the listing sentence neatly should read the registered bylaw, not the listing. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3089486-438-w-king-edward-avenue-104-vancouver-bc-v5y-0m5/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Heather Street holds the corridor's whole care ladder within a few blocks.** Honoria Conway-Heather (assisted living, 4875 Heather St) and Youville Residence (long-term care, 4950 Heather St) are both Providence Health Care buildings on the Queen Elizabeth Park edge of the Heather Lands — a fact that matters more, not less, because the surrounding Heather Lands site was rezoned in March 2026 for roughly 4,200 homes around them. https://www.providencehealthcare.org/en/our-facilities-locations/our-long-term-care-facilities and https://www.shapeyourcity.ca/4911-5255-heather-637-657-w-37-ave-and-620-689-w-35-ave
- **Even the care homes' neighbourhood label is contested.** Providence describes Youville Residence as being in the "Riley Park—Little Mountain area," while a facility-review source places the same 4950 Heather Street address in "the South Cambie neighborhood." The building does not move; the label depends on which boundary set the source uses. This file flags it in Section 2 rather than smoothing it. https://www.providencehealthcare.org/en/our-facilities-locations/our-long-term-care-facilities and https://goodcaring.ca/youville-residence-vancouver/
- **The corridor's school catchment contradicts its map.** Sir Winston Churchill Secondary stands inside the corridor at 7055 Heather Street, yet the one address actually run through the SD39 locator (500 West 20th Ave) returns Eric Hamber Secondary as the regular catchment. A family buying near Churchill on the assumption of a Churchill catchment is making exactly the mistake the research file warns against — run the locator for the specific address. (Area research file, Section 4.)
- **The newest building at the corridor's south end is explicitly not a seniors building.** Park Langara (6859 Cambie St, completed 2025) records "Senior Community: No" in its MLS data — a useful negative data point, because its golf-course-fronting, low-rise format is precisely the format buyers tend to assume is 55+. https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/6859-cambie-street
