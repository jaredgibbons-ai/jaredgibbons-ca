# Sumas Mountain (Abbotsford) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Sumas Mountain sits between Abbotsford and Chilliwack, above Sumas Prairie to the south and the Fraser River lowland to the north; the built part is inside the City of Abbotsford and the upper and eastern rural part is unincorporated Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) land. http://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/sumas-mountain.html
- The City uses "Sumas Mountain" for the mountainside neighbourhoods on the west and south slopes — Auguston, McKee and the Eagle Mountain side; Clayburn Village sits at the northwest foot. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- Main roads are Sumas Mountain Road (becoming Upper Sumas Mountain Road toward the regional park), McKee Road, Dawson Road and Whatcom Road, connecting down to Highway 1 at the Whatcom Road interchange at the south foot. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/new-park-dianne-brook-ave
- Schools on the slopes: Auguston Traditional Elementary, 36367 Stephen Leacock Drive; Mountain Elementary, 2299 Mountain Drive; Irene Kelleher Totí:ltawtxw Elementary, 2540 Eagle Mountain Drive; Clayburn Middle, 35139 Laburnum Avenue, is at the northwest foot. No secondary school is on the mountain — Robert Bateman Secondary (35045 Exbury Ave) and Yale Secondary (34620 Old Yale Rd) are the east-side secondary schools at the foot, and catchments split by slope (see Section 5). https://mountain.abbyschools.ca/schoolplan/ and https://clayburn.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/codeofconduct/clayburn
- Transit: BC Transit Central Fraser Valley route 7 (Sumas Mountain) runs downtown Abbotsford to Sumas Mountain, route 9 (McKee) runs to McKee, and route 15 (Auguston) serves Auguston at the southwest foot; no route to the upper mountain trailheads was confirmed. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=7 and https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=9 and https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=15
- Sumas Mountain Regional Park (FVRD) is at 39100 Sumas Mountain Forest Service Road; park hours are 7 a.m. to sunset, and hiking, trail running and mountain biking are listed activities. http://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/sumas-mountain.html
- No recreation centre or library branch is on the mountain; the nearest City recreation centre named on the City facilities page is Abbotsford Recreation Centre, 2499 McMillan Road, at the west foot. https://www.abbotsford.ca/parks-recreation-culture/facilities-hours
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates in Abbotsford: NOT here. Every verified 55+ ownership complex found for the city sits in Abbotsford West / Central Abbotsford, with a small cluster on the Abbotsford East side (Bourquin Crescent East / Fraser Street side) — see Section 2. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Two governments, two rule sets on one mountain:** Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 applies on the City side (including Country Residential minimum lot 2.0 ha, Suburban Residential 0.4 ha and Rural Residential 4.0 ha), while the rural part is under the FVRD Sumas Mountain Official Community Plan, Consolidated OCP Bylaw No. 584, with no public sewer or water at rural density — homes use wells and onsite (septic) services. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014
- **City plan:** the McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023) is Schedule E of the City Official Community Plan and guides the west slope; the city-wide plan is Abbotsforward 2050 (Bylaw No. 2600-2016). Ground-oriented homes (townhome/multiplex forms) are planned in the McKee plan under its Urban 2 – Ground Oriented designation, but no age-restricted or seniors project in the plan area was verified on any source checked. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea and https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- **Slope hazards:** the City Official Community Plan includes a Steep Slope Development Permit Area for slope land, and the McKee plan protects unstable geotechnical areas and slopes over 40% as open space — the governing constraint on adding a suite, coach house or second unit on a slope lot. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- **Flood:** the mountain itself is above the floodplain; the floodplain land is at its foot in Sumas Prairie, the low-lying area south of the mountain named in City flood notices and evacuation alerts. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/evacuation-orders-modified-additional-properties-sumas-prairie
- **Rural-side taxes are provincial, not City:** a property not in a city, town, district or village pays property taxes to the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office; under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11, rural taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty on any portion unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on any portion unpaid on October 31. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ complex on Sumas Mountain itself?** No. No age-restricted strata, 55+ park, seniors rental or care residence with an Auguston, McKee, Eagle Mountain or Clayburn address was verified in any source checked, and the sub-area recorded zero attached listings in September 2026. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: The Straiton strata on Sumas Mountain Road — is that 55+?** No source says so. It is a 26-lot bare-land-style strata of detached houses (Strata Plan EPS1299) verified in a City report; no age restriction for it appears in any source checked. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/45965/
- **Q: Where is the nearest actual 55+ ownership stock?** Down the mountain in Abbotsford West / Central Abbotsford — e.g. Evergreen Village (31955 Old Yale Rd, 55+, built 1995, 205 units) and Carriage Lane (32691 Garibaldi Dr, 55+, strata plan NWS2539). https://www.rodfriesen.com/abbotsford-condos/31955-old-yale-rd-evergreen-village-condos and https://www.estateblock.com/abbotsford/220-32691-garibaldi-dr-34506
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata (in the city complexes)?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I age in place in an Auguston or McKee house and add a suite or coach house later?** The constraint is slope, not age: the OCP's Steep Slope Development Permit Area applies on slope land and the McKee plan puts unstable geotechnical areas and slopes over 40% into protected open space; on the rural FVRD side there is no public water or sewer, so any added unit runs on well and septic. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy and https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014
- **Q: Is there bus service if I stop driving?** On the built slopes, yes: routes 7 (Sumas Mountain), 9 (McKee) and 15 (Auguston) serve the foot and the built neighbourhoods; no route to the upper mountain was confirmed. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=7
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Abbotsford Regional Hospital and Cancer Centre, 32900 Marshall Rd, and the urgent and primary care centre at 2692 Clearbrook Rd #100 — both Fraser Health services west of the mountain. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Abbotsford/abbotsford-regional-hospital-and-cancer-centre
- **Q: If I need care later, is there anything on the mountain?** No care or assisted-living residence on the mountain appears in BC's Assisted Living Registry; the registered Abbotsford residences (Bevan Village, Hallmark on the Park, Menno, Tabor Court) are all at central/east Abbotsford addresses, down at the foot and across the city. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?address=&f%5B0%5D=city%3AAbbotsford&f%5B1%5D=city%3APort%20Alberni&f%5B2%5D=city%3AQuesnel&f%5B3%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&name=&order=alr_title&sort=asc

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The mountain splits into two school worlds, and neither has a secondary school on it:** the SD34 locator run for Auguston Traditional Elementary's address (36367 Stephen Leacock Dr) returns Auguston → Clayburn Middle → Robert Bateman Secondary, while the run for Irene Kelleher Totí:ltawtxw Elementary's address (2540 Eagle Mountain Dr) returns that school → W.A. Fraser Middle → Yale Secondary. Run the exact address — there is no single Sumas Mountain catchment. https://auguston.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/registration/auguston and https://engage.abbyschools.ca/eagle-mountain-catchment-review?tool=qanda
- **There are five working quarries on the mountain:** Semá:th (Sumas) First Nation states gravel-hauling vehicles travel to and from five quarries on Sumas Mountain using an access road through the Lightning Rock site, with an alternate-route study agreed by the Province, Semá:th First Nation and the City; a City council report separately covers gravel extraction to the 170 m elevation at Jamieson Quarry. https://sumasfirstnation.com/press-releases/sacred-land-returned-to-semath/ and https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/40562/
- **Lightning Rock is a culturally significant Semá:th site on the mountain,** and ownership of the site was transferred to Semá:th First Nation under an agreement with the Province. https://sumasfirstnation.com/press-releases/sacred-land-returned-to-semath/
- **Which tax bill you get depends on which side of the City boundary the lot is on:** City-side lots pay City of Abbotsford taxes (due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty July 3 and a second 5% September 16, 2026), while rural FVRD lots pay the Province through the Surveyor of Taxes (due July 2; 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after October 31). https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **The next housing on the mountain is planned family product, not seniors product:** the City's July 10, 2025 council-consideration notice for the McKee Road lands (35922–36160 McKee Rd area) describes approximately 108 single detached lots, 192 townhouses and four rural residential lots — no age-restricted or seniors component is described in the notice. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2025-07/2025-07-10%20City%20Page.pdf
- **Market depth on the mountain is thin for any buyer:** the SnapStats report shows only 3 detached listings and 0 sales for the sub-area in September 2026, so month-to-month figures swing on very small numbers. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
