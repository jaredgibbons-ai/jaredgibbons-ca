# Nordel — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Nordel is in North Delta, the part of the City of Delta north of Burns Bog and south of the Fraser River. It is both a Paragon (FVREB MLS) sub-area name and a City of Delta neighbourhood name — neighbourhood 5 of the 14 named neighbourhoods in the former North Delta Area Plan (Schedule C.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950). The City publishes no street-by-street written boundary for Nordel. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf
- The core is the ND12 Nordel Social Heart Development Permit Area in the 2024 OCP, centred on 84 Avenue and 112 Street: George Mackie Library at 8440 112th Street, North Delta Recreation Centre at 11415 84 Avenue, North Delta Community Park at 11311 84 Avenue and the North Delta Public Health Unit at 11245 84 Avenue all sit in that core. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations and https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/north-delta-recreation-centre
- Nordel Way runs east–west across the area toward the Highway 91 interchange and the Alex Fraser Bridge; 112 Street and 116 Street run north–south; 84 Avenue is the central east–west street. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt312.pdf
- The south side of the sub-area is Sunstone (Delsom Crescent / Delsom Way), the master-planned townhome community described by its developer, Polygon; the 55+ rental in Section 2 sits in Sunstone Village at its entrance. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Delta Nature Reserve access is at 10388 Nordel Court — the publicly accessible part of Burns Bog, at Nordel's west edge. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/delta-nature-reserve
- School District 37 catchments are address-based. For the sample address 11415 84 Avenue, the SD37 School Locator result is Richardson Elementary (K–7), 11339 83 Ave., and North Delta Secondary (8–12), 11447 82 Ave. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- Transit: route 312 runs Scottsdale Exchange to Scott Road Station via 72 Avenue, 112 Street, 92A Avenue, 114 Street, 96 Avenue and Scott Road, with stops at Nordel Way; route 391 runs the same endpoints via 116 Street; both connect to the Expo Line at Scott Road Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt312.pdf
- The nearest hospital is Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, in Ladner. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates in Nordel: there is no verified 55+ ownership stock here (see Section 2). The one verified 55+ product is a rental building — Sunstone 55+ Apartments in Sunstone Village on Delsom Crescent, at the sub-area's south side.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **A rental building's 55+ rule is a tenancy rule, not a strata bylaw.** Sunstone's requirement — at least one resident over 55, proven by ID on file — is set by the rental operator, not by the Strata Property Act provisions above; a renter's protection runs through the tenancy agreement and the Residential Tenancy Act, not strata documents. https://www.comfortlife.ca/retirement-homes/sunstone-apartments/1370
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017** applies parcel by parcel; no City source names a predominant zone for Nordel. The residential ladder runs from RS1 at a 4,000 m² subdivision minimum through RS4 at 610 m², RS5 at 550 m² or 460 m², RS6 at 390 m² and RS7 at 335 m², with duplex zones RD1 at 836 m² and RD4 at 504 m²; there is no RD5 zone. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Seniors congregate housing is a named use in Delta's apartment zones.** The City's Apartment Residential 6 (RA6) zone, added to Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 in 2024 for six-storey apartment development under the OCP's Neighbourhood Centres and Corridors designation, lists "seniors congregate housing" as a permitted principal use alongside apartment buildings. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future")** is Delta's adopted OCP, adopted July 8, 2024; it consolidated the former area plans into a single Land Use Plan and carries the ND12 Nordel Social Heart Development Permit Area in Appendix B, whose stated objective is to revitalize Nordel as the community focus for North Delta. Two mixed-use/apartment applications (LU009689 at 112 Street and 82 Avenue; LU009611 at 112 Street and 84 Avenue) are active inside that permit area. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Secondary suites and small-scale multi-unit housing:** small-scale multi-unit housing is permitted on all single-detached and duplex lots citywide, and Delta's SSMUH amendments allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/235301/ and http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Property tax deferment at 55+:** BC's Property Tax Deferment Program (regular program) lets a homeowner who is 55 or older during the current year defer annual property taxes on a principal residence as a provincial loan; the City of Delta's tax page links the program and the Home Owner Grant. Delta's 2026 tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility and http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ condo I can buy in Nordel?** No verified one. The 84 Avenue condos and the Sunstone townhomes have no public source stating a 55+ bylaw. Nordel's verified 55+ product is a rental — Sunstone 55+ Apartments — so a buyer set on owning 55+ in North Delta has no verified option in this sub-area. https://www.comfortlife.ca/retirement-homes/sunstone-apartments/1370
- **Q: What is Sunstone 55+ Apartments, exactly?** A 70-unit, 4-storey market rental building at 10775 Delsom Crescent in Sunstone Village, run by Delsom Estates Ltd. At least one resident per unit must be over 55, proven by ID kept on file. Units are 1- and 2-bedroom, many with dens. It is independent living — the operator states it is not for anyone seeking regular care or daily assistance. https://www.comfortlife.ca/retirement-homes/sunstone-apartments/1370
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). At Sunstone's rental building the test is different and simpler on its face: at least one resident must be over 55. Children can also be exempt in strata in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.comfortlife.ca/retirement-homes/sunstone-apartments/1370
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is there subsidized seniors housing in Nordel?** No building in Nordel appears in the City of Delta Affordable Housing Guide (April 2026) — its North Delta non-market seniors buildings are all in Scottsdale. Applications for those run through BC Housing's Housing Registry. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
- **Q: Is there a seniors centre in the sub-area?** Nordel's seniors centre is Kennedy Seniors Recreation Centre at 11760 88 Avenue, at the sub-area's north-east side in the Kennedy cluster — the City bills it as recreation for 50+, run with the Kennedy Seniors Society. North Delta Recreation Centre (11415 84 Ave) and George Mackie Library (8440 112 St) are the in-core civic facilities. http://www.delta.ca/services/seniors/seniors-recreation-centres/lists/seniors-recreation-centres/kennedy-seniors-recreation-centre
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day, and is there a seniors bus?** Routes 312 (112 Street) and 391 (116 Street) run between Scottsdale Exchange and Scott Road Station (Expo Line), with stops at Nordel Way. Separately, the City runs a free Delta Seniors Bus for Delta residents aged 65+ — note the 65+ threshold, not 55+ — with curbside pick-up/drop-off inside Delta, medical appointments given priority, and booking up to 7 days ahead. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt312.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/seniors-services/seniors-bus
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in Nordel?** Not in a care building here — the City guide lists Delta's subsidized assisted living only in Ladner (Augustine House) and Tsawwassen (KinVillage), both through Fraser Health. Sunstone is independent living only. The in-sub-area health facility is the North Delta Public Health Unit at 11245 84 Avenue; the hospital is Delta Hospital in Ladner. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes here at 55+?** Yes, if the home is your principal residence — BC's regular deferment program is open at 55 or older during the current year (also to surviving spouses and persons with disabilities). Delta's tax page links the program; the City's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility and http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Two buildings share the Sunstone Village name and only one is 55+.** The village pairs the 70-unit apartments for those aged 55+ with a separate 54-unit Residences building that carries no 55+ requirement in the sources. A renter searching "Sunstone" has to confirm the exact building — 10775 Delsom Crescent — not the village name. https://www.loopnet.ca/Listing/10727-Delsom-Cres-Delta-BC/33040875/
- **Sunstone's own sources disagree about when it opened and how many floor plans it has.** The operator profile says opened 2022 in its narrative and 2020 in its details field, and seven floor plans where its 2019 launch advertisement said five. Treat both figures as UNCONFIRMED until the operator confirms them; the 55+, 70-unit and 4-storey facts are consistent across both sources. https://www.comfortlife.ca/retirement-homes/sunstone-apartments/1370 and https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/NDR/190801/NDRN190801_A16.pdf
- **The 55+ rental's pharmacy and doctor's office are in the building.** The operator profile states Sunstone 55+ Apartments sit in the Sunstone Village Commercial with an in-building pharmacy and doctors office — a tenancy detail, not a care service. https://www.comfortlife.ca/retirement-homes/sunstone-apartments/1370
- **Nordel's civic core is also its development front.** The ND12 Social Heart permit area centred on 84 Avenue and 112 Street — library, recreation centre, community park, public health unit — is the same area carrying two active mixed-use/apartment applications (LU009689, LU009611). Lots inside the permit area carry different development expectations than the interior streets. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **The west edge is a protected bog, not future lots.** Delta Nature Reserve (access 10388 Nordel Court) is the publicly accessible part of Burns Bog, protected as the Burns Bog Ecological Conservancy Area — west-edge lots border a conservation area, with the development-permit and setback checks that come with it. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/delta-nature-reserve
- **The seniors centre's age is 50+ and the seniors bus's age is 65+.** Kennedy Seniors Recreation Centre is billed as recreation for 50+; the Delta Seniors Bus is for residents 65+. Neither threshold is the 55+ figure in this file's title — check the threshold for the specific service. http://www.delta.ca/services/seniors/seniors-recreation-centres/lists/seniors-recreation-centres/kennedy-seniors-recreation-centre and http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/seniors-services/seniors-bus
