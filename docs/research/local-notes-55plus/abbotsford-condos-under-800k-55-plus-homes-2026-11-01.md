## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw, and any tenant in a 55+ building must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **The $800K band and first-time buyer PTT:** the Province's general PTT is 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value and 2% on the portion above $200,000 and up to $2,000,000; under the first time home buyers' program the full exemption requires a fair market value of $835,000 or less (effective April 1, 2024) and exempts the tax on the first $500,000 of the purchase price, with a partial exemption only for properties above $835,000 and below $860,000, subject to the program's other conditions — so a qualifying first-time buyer anywhere in this page's under-$800K band sits under the full-exemption value ceiling. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **FHSA and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits: UNVERIFIED** — no live CRA page stating those limits was read for this file, so no FHSA or HBP figure is stated here.
- **Manufactured-home pads follow tenancy law, not strata law.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act covers most manufactured home tenancies in BC — the buyer owns the home and rents the site (as at Paradise Park, Georgian Park and Maplewood Estates in Section 2). Pad rent can be increased only once every 12 months, with at least three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for increases taking effect in 2026) plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Suites in a house a senior already owns:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014, s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings, and the bylaw's s.140 small-scale multi-unit provision allows 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on lots over 280 m² in the stated conditions (SSMUH amendments adopted as Bylaw No. 3560-2024 on June 25, 2024). https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **The City's zoning vocabulary for seniors projects:** the Zoning Bylaw framework defines "Congregate Housing" as an independent living setting with common dining and amenity areas (added by Bylaw 3249-2022) — the use category under which supportive seniors projects are processed. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-abbotsford-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/7217/8514/3396/d9117e5011287c79b090adc71e32c234_Appendix_B_-_Draft_ZB_Amendments.pdf
- **Property taxes:** Abbotsford taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many Abbotsford condos under $800,000 are 55+ right now?** That count cannot be stated from the sources used here. SnapStats publishes no price-band cut and no condo-only cut — its Abbotsford attached figures are community-level condos and townhomes combined (572 listings in September 2026) — and no source states which listings in the under-$800K feed are in 55+ buildings. The live page's feed is the only place the current band supply can be seen. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-condos-under-800k.html (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report)
- **Q: Does the $800K cap cover the typical Abbotsford condo?** The cap sits above the middle of the combined attached market: the Abbotsford Condos & Townhomes median sale price was $497,500 in September 2026 — $302,500 below the cap — though that median mixes townhomes with condos, so treat it as context for where the cap sits, not as the price of a typical condo (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report).
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in one of these buildings?** Yes, in law: Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption), and some buildings state the rule themselves — Royal Court's listing data says only one owner must be at least 55, Regency Park's says "One owner must be 55+", and The Wedgewood's says only one resident needs to be over 55. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.zolo.ca/abbotsford-real-estate/31930-old-yale-road/215
- **Q: I'm under 55 — can I buy a unit for my parent, or as an investment?** Ownership is not age-restricted; the bylaw restricts residing. The Province states under-55s can own in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in the unit, so a 55+ parent can be the resident of a unit you own. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: A listing says "no rentals allowed." Can I actually rent my unit out?** Yes for long-term tenancies: every strata rental-restriction bylaw in BC has been void since Nov 24, 2022, whatever the listing sheet or an old bylaw printout says. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be banned by bylaw. The manufactured-home parks are different — Georgian Park's listings state rentals of the homes are not allowed under park rules. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I keep a pet?** It is building-by-building, and Abbotsford's 55+ buildings lean strict: Evergreen Village, Elmwood Manor, Heather Ridge, Silverwood Manor, Fernwood Manor, Centennial Court and Fircrest Estates all record no pets in listing/directory sources; Cloud Croft Manor allows one lap-sized dog or two cats; Crown Point records cats/dogs allowed, limit one, with restrictions; Parkside Place allows one cat and no dogs; Trethewey Tower's current MLS data allows one cat. Summerset Place is unresolved — listing sources conflict — so treat pets there as UNCONFIRMED until the registered bylaws are read. Sources: the building entries in Section 2.
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer use the PTT exemption under $800K in Abbotsford?** In principle, yes — the first time home buyers' program's full exemption runs to a fair market value of $835,000 (exempting the first $500,000 of price), with a partial exemption to $860,000, provided the buyer meets all the program's personal and property conditions; the exemption is claimed through the buyer's legal professional on the PTT return. FHSA and Home Buyers' Plan limits are UNVERIFIED in this file (no live CRA page was read). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: What's the difference between Garden Park Tower and a condo at Evergreen Village?** Tenure. Evergreen Village is freehold strata — you own the unit and can sell it on the open market. Garden Park Tower is life lease: an up-front capital payment (typically the BC Assessment value, per the Society) buys lifetime occupancy plus monthly maintenance charges, and on departure the Society redeems the lease under its terms — there is no strata lot to list. https://gardenparktower.ca/life-lease and http://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/evergreen-village-31955-old-yale-road
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale market?** Mechanically yes: the next buyer must be able to live there — 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case — or buy without residing. That is the built-in trade-off of every BC 55+ strata since the 2022 changes confined age bylaws to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: If I need care later, what's in Abbotsford?** The ladder exists city-wide: independent supportive living and assisted living at the Menno Place campus and Tabor Village, independent living rental at The Mayfair and the Hallmark residences, and long-term care at MSA Manor, Maplewood House, Tabor Home and Menno Home/Hospital — with admissions to the publicly funded beds coordinated through Fraser Health. Sources: Section 2 above.

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Almost all of Abbotsford's 55+ strata stock is in one west-side corridor.** Fifteen verified age-restricted strata complexes sit in Abbotsford West, most of them built 1979–1996 along Old Yale Road, Tims Avenue and Gladwin Road — the comparable counts are nine strata complexes in Central Abbotsford, two in Poplar, and none verified in Abbotsford East, Matsqui, Aberdeen, Bradner or Sumas Mountain. A buyer who insists on another part of Abbotsford is mostly choosing between detached houses, life lease, or rentals. Sources: the sibling files in ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/
- **The under-$800K page's own claims are not verified building facts.** The live page states strata insurance-deductible ranges, floor-plan size ranges, rental-bylaw treatment and 55+ building claims as fact; none of those figures was confirmed on an official source in the underlying research, so none is repeated here — building bylaws, fees and deductibles must be checked per building in the strata documents. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-condos-under-800k.html
- **Old directories still call Trethewey Tower 45+.** A building directory in circulation describes 32440 Simon Ave as 45+; sub-55 age bylaws have been unenforceable since Nov 24, 2022, and current MLS data for the tower records "Restricted Age: 55+". Always qualify against the current registered bylaws, not a directory page. https://geraldlucas.ca/mylistings.html/listing.r3052595-1602-32440-simon-avenue-abbotsford-v2t-5r3.106964680 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Building data for the biggest 55+ building conflicts between sources.** Evergreen Village is recorded by the building directory as 205 units, 10 levels, built 1985 (plan NWS1737); an agent page for the same address says built 1995 with 12 levels. The directory figures are used in Section 2, but a buyer should reconcile against the strata plan and Form B before writing. http://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/evergreen-village-31955-old-yale-road
- **Cloud Croft Manor's build year disagrees with itself across sources.** Directories say 1976; MLS listings say 1975. It is a small 18-unit building from the mid-1970s either way — the oldest verified 55+ strata in Central Abbotsford — but a depreciation report and Form B, not a listing sheet, settle which year the strata uses. https://www.rew.ca/buildings/areas/abbotsford-bc/page/3
- **Alameda Court has two civic addresses in listings — 2277 and 2279 McCallum Rd.** Buyers searching only one number will miss listings filed under the other. https://www.rodfriesen.com/55-plus-condos-in-abbotsford
- **Three "Maplewoods," three tenures, two sub-areas.** Maplewood Manor (32040 Tims Ave, Abbotsford West) is a buyable 55+ strata; Maplewood House (1919 Jackson St, Poplar) is a Fraser Health-funded care home with 78 beds; Maplewood Estates (2035 Martens St, Poplar) is a 55+ manufactured-home park where you rent the pad. Search results blend all three — check the street before booking a viewing. Sources: the building entries in Section 2.
- **The subsidized care beds beside the hospital are scheduled to move.** Fraser Health's new long-term care community at 1919 Jackson Street (Poplar) will replace the 109 beds at Cottage and Worthington Pavilion and add 91 more; construction was expected to begin in 2025 with completion in 2027. https://thebeat.fraserhealth.ca/news/2023/Jun/new-long-term-care-community-coming-to-abbotsford
- **Abbotsford's tax penalty is a two-step.** Taxes were due July 2, 2026, with 5% added July 3 and a second 5% on September 16, 2026 — a buyer closing in late summer inherits whichever step has already landed on an unpaid balance. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
