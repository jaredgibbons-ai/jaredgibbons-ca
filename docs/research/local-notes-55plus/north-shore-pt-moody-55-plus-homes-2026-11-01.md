# North Shore Pt Moody — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "North Shore" here is the board (MLS/GVR) sub-area name used in the SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, not an official City of Port Moody neighbourhood name. The official OCP areas along the north side of the Port Moody Arm of Burrard Inlet are Ioco, Pleasantside and April Road, plus the Heritage Mountain grouping (Heritage Mountain, Twin Creeks, Noons Creek, Mountain Meadows, Heritage Woods). https://www.portmoody.ca/en/city-hall/resources/Documents/OCP-neighbourhood-plan-areas_-_edited_for_EngagePM_website.pdf
- The Port Moody Official Community Plan is Bylaw 2026, No. 3522 ("Port Moody 2050"); Chapter 14 covers the north shore in Section 14.2 (Pleasantside and April Road), Section 14.3 (Heritage Mountain grouping) and Section 14.4 (Ioco Lands). https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733
- Ioco Road is the primary traffic corridor for the north shore neighbourhoods, connecting south to St. Johns Street, Barnet Highway and the Moody Centre and Inlet Centre stations. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733
- The OCP describes Pleasantside and April Road as primarily single-detached homes, and the Heritage Mountain grouping as single detached homes and townhouses with a small number of apartments. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733
- Old Orchard Park, on the north shore, has Port Moody's only swimming beach (salt-water) and is a starting point for the Shoreline Trail; Old Orchard Hall is at 646 Bentley Road and the Port Moody Recreation Complex is at 300 Ioco Road. https://engage.portmoody.ca/rocky-point-and-old-orchard-parks-master-plans/news_feed/background-rocky-point-today and https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/recreation/recreation-facilities-and-locations/
- School District 43 serves the area; SD43 schools on the north shore include Pleasantside, Aspenwood, Heritage Mountain and Mountain Meadows elementary schools, Eagle Mountain Middle (in Anmore) and Heritage Woods Secondary; catchments are address-based. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- Where 55+ housing concentrates: it does not concentrate anywhere on the north shore. No verified 55+ strata, 55+ rental, retirement residence, assisted-living residence or subsidized seniors building was found inside the Ioco / Pleasantside / April Road / Heritage Mountain area in the sources checked for Section 2. The City of Port Moody's seniors housing and care buildings that do exist sit south of the inlet in Moody Centre / Inlet Centre (see the Port Moody Centre notes file). Sources: Section 2 below.
- The Ioco townsite, on the north shore, is a heritage conservation area where a heritage alteration permit can be required for changes; the townsite was built for Imperial Oil refinery workers. https://www.portmoody.ca/arts-culture-and-heritage/heritage-and-history/al-sholund-s-historical-insights/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw 2026, No. 3522** (Port Moody 2050) covers this area in Chapter 14, ss. 14.2–14.4, and its housing chapter states a policy to "encourage the creation of seniors focused housing opportunities in transit-oriented areas that are adaptable and affordable" — a policy direction, not an existing north shore building. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/tk1n0rrw/chapter-7-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670726
- **Zoning: Port Moody Zoning Bylaw No. 2937** puts single detached homes in RS zones and multi-residential homes in RT and RM zones; small-scale multi-unit housing is permitted in RS and RT zones outside the transit-oriented areas. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/
- **Small lots:** small lot subdivision from RS1 to RS1-S (minimum 9 m width and 325 m² lot area) is available only in Moody Centre, Seaview and Pleasantside where the OCP designates Single Family–Low Density — Pleasantside is the one north shore area on that list. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/subdivision/
- **Heritage:** the Ioco townsite is a heritage conservation area; a heritage alteration permit can be required for changes to included properties. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** (Zone 4: Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, Maple Ridge, Pitt Meadows directory, May 2025). https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ condo or townhouse I can buy on Port Moody's north shore?** No verified one. No age-restricted strata ownership building was found in Ioco, Pleasantside, April Road or the Heritage Mountain grouping in any source checked; the OCP describes those areas as single-detached homes and townhouses with a small number of apartments, with no 55+ complex named. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in the Tri-Cities?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Where is the nearest hospital?** Eagle Ridge Hospital, 475 Guildford Way in Port Moody (Fraser Health) — south of the inlet, in the Port Moody Centre area; it is a community hospital with a 24/7 emergency department. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- **Q: How do north shore residents get to transit?** TransLink buses run along Ioco Road to Moody Centre Station (Millennium Line Evergreen Extension and West Coast Express); Ioco Road is the only through-corridor, so bus, school and car trips all share it. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733
- **Q: If I downsize from a north shore house, where do Port Moody seniors actually move?** On the sources checked, the in-city options are all south of the inlet: the non-profit seniors rental at Legion Kinsmen Manor (2909 Hope St), the Housing Registry buildings at 101 Noons Creek Dr and 2330 St. Johns St, and long-term care at Eagle Ridge Manor — plus the verified 55+ strata ownership option in Port Coquitlam (Mayfair Terrace). Details are in the Port Moody Centre and Port Coquitlam notes files. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: Can I add a suite or extra units to a north shore house instead of moving?** Port Moody permits small-scale multi-unit housing in RS and RT zones outside the transit-oriented areas under Zoning Bylaw No. 2937, and Pleasantside RS1 lots in OCP Single Family–Low Density areas can be considered for RS1-S small lots (minimum 9 m width, 325 m²). Confirm the specific lot with the City. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/ and https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/subdivision/
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink the resale buyer pool in a 55+ building?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. That trade-off applies to every BC 55+ strata since the 2022 law change confined age bylaws to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"North Shore" is a listings label, not a place the City plans by.** A buyer searching City documents for "North Shore" will find nothing — the OCP plans this shore as Ioco, Pleasantside/April Road and the Heritage Mountain grouping, so heritage, small-lot and development rules have to be looked up under those names. https://www.portmoody.ca/en/city-hall/resources/Documents/OCP-neighbourhood-plan-areas_-_edited_for_EngagePM_website.pdf
- **The City itself records a seniors-housing gap in Port Moody.** In its Canada Community-Building Fund questionnaire the City states there is a growing number of seniors needing accessible, affordable housing, that senior households on the BC Housing registry rose from 21 in 2016 to 62 in 2024, and that Port Moody has 75 long-term care and assisted living beds offered through Fraser Health, with all other seniors housing in the community for independent living. https://wWW.ubcm.ca/sites/default/files/2026-01/Port%20Moody%20Questionnaire.pdf
- **One road in, one road out.** Ioco Road is the primary corridor for every north shore neighbourhood; there is no second through-route, which is why the research file's first buying tip is to ask about Ioco Road before anything else. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733
- **The north shore detached market can print a zero month.** The sub-area recorded 14 detached listings and zero detached sales in September 2026 (SnapStats GVR), so a single month's north shore numbers say almost nothing — price off the Port Moody community figures and a longer window. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.
- **The Ioco lands are an open planning file, not a project.** OCP Section 14.4 addresses future planning for the former Imperial Oil (Ioco) lands; no specific active presale project on the north shore was verified on a developer or City page — UNCONFIRMED. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733
- **Port Moody taxes and utilities are billed separately.** Property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after September 15, 2026; the 2026 utility bill was due February 27, 2026. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/property-taxes-and-utility-fees/property-taxes/
