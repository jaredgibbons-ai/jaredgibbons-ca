## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1/123.2; any bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** Under-55s can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; younger spouse/partner; children under 19 in the resident's care; adult children cared for before 19 (expanded May 1, 2023). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rental bylaws are still allowed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600; OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future"** — Delta's current consolidated OCP covering Ladner, including the Ladner Village development permit areas. "Seniors congregate housing" is a permitted principal use in the RA6 apartment zone added in 2024. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **Delta Green's 55+ status at approval:** the City's 2018 hearing material pairs the project's "55+ age category" with a restrictive covenant on "adaptable-ready" design under the BC Building Code. A strata age bylaw and a title covenant are different instruments — confirm which are registered via the Form B and strata documents. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/179807/
- **Secondary suites:** Delta legalized secondary suites in single-family dwellings in 2010; Bylaw No. 8242 (2023) amended Bylaw No. 7600 so a suite's floor area is less than 50% of the gross floor area of the single detached dwelling (excluding an attached garage). Relevant to the house alternative a condo buyer is comparing against, not to a condo unit itself. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/218413/
- **Property transfer tax (general, Province):** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000; 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000; 3% above $2,000,000 — a purchase under $800,000 sits entirely in the 1% and 2% tiers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First time home buyers' program:** the full exemption requires a fair market value of $835,000 or less (effective April 1, 2024), applies to the first $500,000 of price, with a partial exemption above $835,000 and below $860,000, plus all buyer and property conditions — so a qualifying first-time buyer can in principle use it anywhere in this band. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **FHSA and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits: UNVERIFIED** in this file — no live CRA page stating the limits was read for this research, so no FHSA or HBP figure is stated.
- **Property taxes and deferment:** Delta's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026 (5% penalty after, a further 5% after September 2, 2026); BC's regular deferment program opens at 55 or older on a principal residence. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which Ladner condo buildings are actually 55+?** One verified building: Delta Green (4686 51 St, built 2021, 24 units, listing data "Restricted Age: 55+," City approval record confirming the 55+ category). Every other central-Ladner condo building checked is general-market or unverified. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3091870-107-4686-51-street-delta-bc-v4k-2v7 and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/179807/
- **Q: Is Delta Green under $800K?** UNCONFIRMED. No source states the building's price band, and this file makes no price claim for any complex — check the live feed for any current Delta Green listing and its price at the time. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-condos-under-800k.html
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live at Delta Green?** Yes — a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident is exempt; caregivers, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a 55+ condo for a parent or as an investment?** Ownership is not age-restricted; the bylaw governs residence. A 55+ parent can be the resident of a unit owned by a younger buyer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022; the tenant must still meet the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. Delta Green's own rental wording must be confirmed in its bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer use the PTT exemption under $800K in Ladner?** In principle, yes — the full exemption runs to a fair market value of $835,000 (on the first $500,000 of price) with a partial exemption to $860,000, if all personal and property conditions are met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Delta Green?** A current unit's listing data records cats and dogs OK, limited to one. Confirm size/breed rules in the registered bylaws. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/4686-51-street/106
- **Q: How many Ladner condos under $800K are for sale right now?** That figure cannot be stated from the sources used here — SnapStats publishes no South Delta figures and no price-band cut, and no official source publishes a Ladner condo count. The live page's feed is the only place the current band supply can be seen. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-condos-under-800k.html
- **Q: What flood questions should I ask on a Ladner condo?** Whether the building is within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch (the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area), and how the depreciation report and insurance certificate treat Ladner's setting behind over 63 km of dikes and seawalls and 31 pump stations. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: Where are the hospital and seniors centre?** Delta Hospital (5800 Mountain View Blvd, 24/7 emergency) beside Ladner Exchange; McKee Seniors Recreation Centre (5155 47 Ave) runs programs for adults 50+, and the free Delta Seniors Bus is home-based there. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2021-06/SocialServicesInventory%20CS.pdf

- **Ladner has exactly one verified 55+ condo building, and it has 24 units.** Delta Green's small count is why 55+ condo supply in Ladner is episodic — a buyer waiting for a second 55+ condo building to choose between is waiting for a building that does not exist in any source checked. https://en.youlive.ca/delta-building/13200-delta-green

Do not present Hampton Cove as 55+ stock. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/84874/
- **No Ladner market statistics exist to anchor this band.** SnapStats' Delta coverage is North Delta only, with no South Delta section and no price-band or condo-only cut — any "typical Ladner condo price / fee / size" figure for this band is UNVERIFIED.
