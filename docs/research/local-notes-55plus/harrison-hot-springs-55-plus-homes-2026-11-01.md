## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The Village of Harrison Hot Springs is an incorporated resort village at the south end of Harrison Lake, in the Fraser Valley Regional District, surrounded by — but separate from — the District of Kent. Esplanade Avenue is the waterfront street facing the lake; Hot Springs Road is the main road in and out, running south to Agassiz. https://www.kentbc.ca/media/0enenmdy/doc_636900753607636141.pdf and https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/Official-Community-Plan-Bylaw-No.-864-Consolidated.pdf
- The village is small: 2021 Census population 1,905 (up from 1,468 in 2016), with 885 occupied private dwellings — and seniors are a large share of it: people 65+ were 32% of the 2021 population. https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/sites/default/files/2025-02/2025.02.19---VHHS-Housing-Needs-Report.pdf and https://getintoitharrison.ca/seniors-housing-project
- The hot springs are the village's reason for being: two spring types — Potash at 40°C and Sulphur at 62°C — are pumped and cooled to supply the public mineral pool and the resort pools. The public indoor pool, the Harrison Mineral Baths at Hot Springs Road and Esplanade Avenue, is owned and operated by the Harrison Hot Springs Resort; there is no natural outdoor hot spring open to the public. https://tourismharrison.com/explore/harrison-river-valley/our-region/our-hot-springs/
- Everyday services are not in the village: grocery and everyday shopping, the arena/recreation complex and the secondary school are all in Agassiz (District of Kent), roughly a 10-minute drive south. The village school is Harrison Hot Springs Elementary (K–6) at 501 Hot Springs Road (~100 students); grades 7–12 go to Agassiz Elementary-Secondary School. https://sd78.bc.ca/harrison/ and https://www.new.agassizsecondary.com/school-information/principal-s-message
- Transit: BC Transit Route 71 (Agassiz–Harrison) runs from downtown Chilliwack through Agassiz to Harrison Hot Springs, terminus at St. Alice St/Esplanade Ave, administered by the FVRD. Service is limited — check schedules per trip. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/transit.html
- Recreation anchors: the lakeshore promenade and beach, Village Memorial Hall at 290 Esplanade Avenue, Rendall Park on the east side, and the 1.6 km Lagoon Trail loop past the Resort and the springs source building. https://tourismharrison.com/listing/lagoon-trail/ and https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/Official-Community-Plan-Bylaw-No.-864-Consolidated.pdf
- Where 55+ housing concentrates: it doesn't — yet. No source places any operating 55+ strata, 55+ rental building, retirement residence or care home inside the village. The one seniors project in play is a **proposal** (Section 2 and Section 5), not a building. The Esplanade condo strip is resort/second-home stock, not age-restricted stock.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Village zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 1115, 2017.** "Tourist Accommodation" is a commercial-zone use under this bylaw — village zoning, not strata bylaws alone, decides whether a condo can run as nightly rental. The Village began an OCP and Zoning Bylaw update process in 2025, so confirm the current text with the Village before relying on a zone. https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/village-office/public-notices/june-16-2025-regular-council-meeting-highlights (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/harrison-hot-springs.md, Section 4)
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 1184, 2022** (consolidated to November 3, 2025) is the current plan of record; it repealed OCP Bylaw No. 864, 2007. Definitions in the OCP take the Zoning Bylaw No. 1115, 2017 definitions where not otherwise defined. https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/Bylaw-1184---Official-Community-Plan-%28Consolidated-to-Nov-3%2C-2025%29.pdf
- **Short-term rentals — the Village is exempt from the provincial principal-residence rule** as one of BC's designated resort regions, so nightly rental runs on Village zoning and licensing instead. Since Dec 15, 2025, Business Licence Bylaw No. 1128 (amended by Bylaw No. 1231, 2025) requires at s. 2.1(h) a separate business licence for each dwelling unit operated as tourist accommodation. Accommodation in the designated area (Village + District of Kent + FVRD Electoral Area C) also carries the 3% MRDT (Municipal and Regional District Tax), whose current cycle runs to a repeal/renewal date of January 1, 2027. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2023hous0060-001598 and https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/sites/default/files/2026-01/Bylaw-1128---Business-Licence-and-Regulation-%28Consolidated-to-December-15%2C-2025%29.pdf and https://tourismharrison.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2026-Tourism-Harrison-One-Year-Tactical-Plan-FINAL.pdf
- **Flood construction level: 14.55 m** (Bylaw No. 1179, 2022); the 1:200-year lake flood elevation is 13.95 m. Lakeshore and Miami River properties have to be checked against the FCL lot by lot. https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/sites/default/files/2026-09/Harrison-Hot-Springs-WTP-Master-Plan.pdf
- **ALR:** a small ALR fringe exists on the village's east side; exact share UNVERIFIED. ALC housing rules apply on any ALR parcel on top of Village zoning. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Taxes:** Village municipal taxes plus regional levies; 2026 rates are set by Tax Rate Bylaw No. 1238, 2026 (adopted May 4, 2026) and 2026 taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026 at 4:30 p.m., with the Home Owner Grant claimed yearly. The numeric 2026 residential rate per $1,000 sits in a scanned schedule and is UNVERIFIED in these notes — do not quote a rate. https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/Bylaw-1238---Tax-Rate-%282026%29.pdf and https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/village-office/public-notices/council-meeting-highlights-june-15-2026

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ condo I can buy in Harrison Hot Springs?** No verified one. The Esplanade buildings (Aqua Shores, Oasis, Laguna Beach, Echo Beach, The Cascades, Harrison On The Lake) are general-market resort condos — a current Aqua Shores listing records no age restriction — and no source states a 55+ bylaw for any village strata. Treat any "55+" claim as UNCONFIRMED until the strata's registered bylaws are in hand. https://www.zolo.ca/harrison-hot-springs-real-estate/120-esplanade-avenue/608
- **Q: Is there any retirement or assisted-living residence in the village?** No. Nothing in the village appears in BC's Assisted Living Registry in the sources checked, and there is no retirement residence operating in the village. The care ladder — assisted living, long-term care, subsidized seniors rental — is in Agassiz, about 10 minutes south. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3AAgassiz&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc
- **Q: What about the seniors housing project I heard about?** It is a proposal, not a building: Agassiz-Harrison Community Services proposed a 60-unit, four-storey affordable seniors housing project on the Village's public-works-yard / overflow-parking lands beside Village Hall. Council voted 4-1 in July 2025 to write a joint letter of intent and support the funding application — but on Feb. 17, 2026 BC Housing told applicants the 2025 Community Housing Fund round would not proceed, and the project has not advanced. There is nothing to buy or rent there. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/AHO/250801/AHON250801_A04.pdf and https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/AHO/260227/AHON260227_A02.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. If a building here did have a 55+ bylaw, could we both live there?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a Harrison condo — including nightly?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC strata law (invalid since Nov 24, 2022), though a strata can still restrict short-term rentals by bylaw. Nightly rental in the village additionally runs on Village rules: Tourist Accommodation is a commercial-zone use under Zoning Bylaw No. 1115, 2017, each unit needs its own business licence since Dec 15, 2025, the village is exempt from the provincial principal-residence rule as a resort region, and the 3% MRDT applies (renewal due Jan 1, 2027). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/sites/default/files/2026-01/Bylaw-1128---Business-Licence-and-Regulation-%28Consolidated-to-December-15%2C-2025%29.pdf
- **Q: Are there manufactured homes in the village?** Yes — as rental pads, not ownership product. The FVRD's 2020 Kent–Harrison housing inventory lists the Glencoe Motel and Trailer Park at 259 Hot Springs Rd (11 units; 27 pads/mobile homes) and Harrison Holiday Park at 973 Hot Springs Rd (12 pads/mobile homes), the latter operated as a registered not-for-profit society. Neither source states any age restriction — do not describe either park as 55+. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/Homelessness/2020%20Kent-HHS%20Housing%20Inventory.pdf
- **Q: How old is this community, really?** Old for its size: seniors 65+ were 32% of the 2021 population, and the Village's 2025 Interim Housing Needs Report puts total need at 185 units over 5 years and 637 over 20 years, driven mainly by anticipated growth. That demand is exactly why the stalled seniors project matters here. https://getintoitharrison.ca/seniors-housing-project and https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/sites/default/files/2025-02/2025.02.19---VHHS-Housing-Needs-Report.pdf
- **Q: Is the village in a flood zone?** Yes, on the lakeshore and Miami River side. Council minutes (April 22, 2025) record the existing dike crest at 13.9 m — below the 1894 flood of record at 14.1 m and the 15.1 m crest provincial standards require — with a flood construction level of 14.55 m. Check a home's floor elevation against the FCL before writing. https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/sites/default/files/2025-05/2025.04.22---Regular-Council-Meeting-Minutes.pdf
- **Q: Where do we shop and see a doctor day to day?** In Agassiz. The village has a small resort strip on Esplanade Avenue and Hot Springs Road (cafés, shops, hotels); grocery, everyday services, the Fraser Health community-health cluster and the secondary school are in Agassiz. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/your-community/agassiz/services-in-agassiz

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The seniors housing project stalled on provincial budget cuts (Feb 2026).** The proposed 60-unit, four-storey affordable seniors project (Agassiz-Harrison Community Services, with Terra Social Purpose Real Estate and The Nerdy Architect) had letters of support from Harrison's Age Friendly Committee, the Sts'ailes First Nation, the Agassiz-Harrison Senior Citizens Housing Society and AHCS's Seniors Services Department, and a July 2025 council letter of intent — but the Feb. 27, 2026 Agassiz-Harrison Observer reports BC Housing's Feb. 17, 2026 notice that the 2025 Community Housing Fund RFP would not proceed, so applications under that call will not be advanced. The Village's own project page had staged design/permits for summer/fall 2026 **only if funding were awarded**. Status: unfunded proposal, no timeline. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/AHO/260227/AHON260227_A02.pdf and https://fraservalleynewsnetwork.com/2025/08/26/reaction-resignation-of-board-member-over-the-harrison-seniors-housing-project/
- **The project was contentious before it was stalled.** Two Agassiz-Harrison Community Services board members resigned over the project in August 2025, and the Aug. 11, 2025 council agenda carries resident letters both for and against "55+ social housing on overflow parking" — the site is the village's public works yard / tourist overflow parking beside Village Hall, which is the core of the local argument. https://fraservalleynewsnetwork.com/2025/08/26/reaction-resignation-of-board-member-over-the-harrison-seniors-housing-project/ and https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/sites/default/files/2025-08/RC-Mtg-2025.08.11.pdf
- **The dike project is mid-design.** In September 2025 Council directed detailed design for a deployable waterfront dike system for Zones 3–6 (pump station to Rendall Park), after resolving in April 2025 to proceed with a combined fixed + deployable project; the village floods from the south only if Kent's Dikes A–C fail. Lakeshore buyers should ask about timing and any cost-sharing before writing. https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/village-office/public-notices/council-meeting-highlights-september-8-2025 and https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/sites/default/files/2025-05/2025.04.22---Regular-Council-Meeting-Minutes.pdf
- **A new elementary school is being explored — and the first MOU already failed.** The current Harrison Hot Springs Elementary building is 75+ years old and over capacity. The Village and SD78 are exploring a shared "Civic Campus," but the June 15, 2026 council motion to forward a revised MOU to SD78 failed; as of Aug 10, 2026 the stage is a new exploratory MOU — conceptual and unfunded, no timeline. Present it as a possibility, never a promise. https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/village-office/public-notices/press-release-civic-campus and https://getintoitharrison.ca/civic-campus-building-tomorrow-together
- **The OCP on the Village's website changed in 2025.** OCP Bylaw No. 1184, 2022 (consolidated to November 3, 2025) is the current plan and repealed the 2007 Bylaw 864 plan that older pages and directories still cite; the Village also began an OCP/Zoning update process in June 2025. Any page copy citing "OCP Bylaw 864" is out of date. https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/Bylaw-1184---Official-Community-Plan-%28Consolidated-to-Nov-3%2C-2025%29.pdf and https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/village-office/public-notices/june-16-2025-regular-council-meeting-highlights
- **The village runs on events.** The Harrison Festival of the Arts held its 47th edition July 10–19, 2026, and Sasquatch Days ran June 13–14, 2026, co-hosted by the Village and the Sts'ailes Nation — peak-season crowds are part of owning on or near Esplanade. https://harrisonfestival.com/world-music-arts-festival/a-festival-to-remember/ and https://tourismharrison.com/event-directory/sasquatch-days/
- **There are no board statistics for Harrison.** No Harrison Hot Springs row exists in SnapStats' Fraser Valley report, and CADREB publishes board-wide statistics only — pricing here rests on comparable sales work, not a stats sheet.
