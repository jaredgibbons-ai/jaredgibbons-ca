Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://choa.bc.ca/resources/strata-property-act/legislative-updates/
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home parks are not stratas.** The strata age-bylaw rules above do not govern Hideaway Park: a park home is owned on a rented pad, and the park's 55+ rule, pad rent and pet / rental rules live in the park's own rules and the tenancy paperwork — get them from the park in writing before purchase. https://www.boldcalgaryrealestate.ca/listing-crea/r3069442-52-4200-dewdney-trunk-road-coquitlam-british-columbia-v3k2x8/brochure
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996** is Coquitlam's zoning bylaw, and the Citywide Official Community Plan is Bylaw No. 3479, 2001. The specific zone of any one 55+ building in Section 2: UNCONFIRMED from the sources checked. https://www.coquitlam.ca/323/How-to-Plan-for-Growth
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) in Coquitlam:** up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the rules. Small-scale residential zones are R-1, R-2 and R-3 under Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Watercourse setbacks:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 30 metres of the top of bank of any watercourse in Coquitlam — 50 metres in Northeast Coquitlam (Burke Mountain); Zoning Bylaw s.519 sets flood protection setbacks and s.523 covers riparian areas. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Property tax dates that apply citywide:** Coquitlam property tax payment was due July 2, 2026 for the Jan 1–Dec 31, 2026 year, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after September 15, 2026, including unclaimed Home Owner Grants. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes
- **FHSA / Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits:** UNVERIFIED — no live CRA page was read for this file, so no limit figure is stated.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Coquitlam?** Three strata buildings, all in the Town Centre pocket — Marlborough House (3098 Guildford Way), Princess Gate (2985 / 2995 Princess Crescent) and Burlington Estates (2963 / 2973 Burlington Drive) — plus manufactured homes on rented pads at Hideaway Park (4200 Dewdney Trunk Rd, Ranch Park). No verified 55+ strata exists anywhere else in the city. https://www.zolo.ca/coquitlam-real-estate/3098-guildford-way/118 and https://www.zolo.ca/coquitlam-real-estate/2973-burlington-drive/103
- **Q: Are Marlborough House and Princess Gate the same kind of building?** They are marketed as a pair — both 55+, both with an indoor pool, fitness centre, workshop, library / social rooms and 3 guest suites, and both state that fees include heat and hot water — but they are separate strata corporations on different streets with separate bylaws, finances and documents. Read each building's own Form B and bylaws. https://www.zolo.ca/coquitlam-real-estate/3098-guildford-way/118 and https://www.zolo.ca/coquitlam-real-estate/2995-princess-crescent/418
- **Q: Is Burlington Estates the same as The Burlington?** No. Burlington Estates (2963 / 2973 Burlington Dr, built 1987) is the verified 55+ gated community with its own detached clubhouse. The Burlington (2968 / 2978 Burlington Dr, plan LMS923, 128 units, built 1993) has no age restriction in any source checked. Same street, near-identical name, opposite age answer — verify the civic address. https://www.zolo.ca/coquitlam-real-estate/2973-burlington-drive/103 and https://www.cotala.com/tours/85548/FullPublic.pdf?i=512363045
- **Q: Is Wildwood Park a 55+ park?** No. Wildwood Park at 201 Cayer St is marketed in its own listings as a family-oriented park with a playground, pool, tennis court and clubhouse, on leasehold non-strata pads. The one verified 55+ park in the city is Hideaway Park. https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/listing/r3091187-201-cayer-street-212-coquitlam-maillardville-homes-for-sale-288880/brochure
- **Q: What does a Hideaway Park purchase actually include?** The manufactured home only — the pad is rented from the park, and listings state the pad rent separately for each home (figures conflict: $625, about $750 and $800 per month all appear), so confirm the current pad rent, what it includes, and the park's 55+, pet and rental rules with the park in writing. The site has also been marketed as a transit-oriented redevelopment opportunity (68 pads, 5.76 acres), which is a tenure risk to price in. http://www.database.land/Land/download-attachment/166
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in one of these buildings as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. At Hideaway Park the position is the park's own rules — listings record rentals allowed with restrictions — not strata law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the Town Centre 55+ buildings?** Listing data records cats OK at Marlborough House, cats OK at one Princess Gate address and "one dog up to 16 inches at the shoulder" in another Princess Gate listing, and pets with restrictions at Burlington Estates — three buildings, three different listing statements. The registered bylaws for the specific building govern; confirm there, not from a listing. https://www.zolo.ca/coquitlam-real-estate/3098-guildford-way/118 and https://www.zolo.ca/coquitlam-real-estate/2995-princess-crescent/418
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Eagle Ridge Hospital, 475 Guildford Way, Port Moody — the Fraser Health hospital on the Port Moody / Coquitlam border, and the same Guildford Way corridor the Town Centre 55+ buildings sit on, at its west end. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in Coquitlam?** The in-city ladder is: independent 55+ strata living in the Town Centre buildings; retirement-residence rental at Venvi Parkwood Manor (1142 Dufferin St) and assisted living at Residences at Belvedere (750 Delestre Ave, 60 funded suites by referral); then long-term care at Dufferin Care Centre (1131 Dufferin St), Foyer Maillard (1010 Alderson Ave, 125 beds) and Cartier House (1419 Cartier Ave) through the health-authority process. Two further 55+ affordable rental buildings (S.U.C.C.E.S.S., 158 homes total) are under construction. Sources: Section 2 above.

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The board-name trap is the whole story of this city's 55+ stock:** the three verified 55+ strata buildings are marketed and indexed as North Coquitlam / Canyon Springs / Town Centre, while "Central Coquitlam" as a board name means the Austin Heights / Harbour Chines / Chineside ground to the south-west, where no 55+ strata was verified. A search filtered to the board sub-area "Central Coquitlam" alone will miss every verified 55+ strata building in this file. https://www.merylhamdillah.com/neighbourhoods/coquitlam-neighbourhood-guide/ and https://bridgewellgroup.ca/coquitlam-neighbourhood-guide/
- **Two Burlington buildings, one street, opposite answers:** Burlington Estates (2963 / 2973, 1987, 55+, gated, detached clubhouse) faces The Burlington (2968 / 2978, LMS923, 1993, 128 units, no age restriction found). Showing reports, strata documents and even the parking entrance differ by civic address — a buyer who books "the Burlington building" without the number can tour the wrong legal building. https://www.zolo.ca/coquitlam-real-estate/2973-burlington-drive/103 and https://www.cotala.com/tours/85548/FullPublic.pdf?i=512363045
- **Princess Gate is two buildings, not one:** the 55+ complex runs under two civic addresses — 2985 and 2995 Princess Crescent — with listing years of 1994 and 1993 respectively.
