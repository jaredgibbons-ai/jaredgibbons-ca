# Boulevard — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Boulevard is a corridor, not a single-municipality neighbourhood: it STRADDLES the municipal line. South of Highway 1 it is in the City of North Vancouver; north of Highway 1 it is in the District of North Vancouver, running as Grand Boulevard / Grand Boulevard East up to Boulevard Crescent and Lynn Valley Road. Any rule, tax or permit question has to be answered for the correct side of the highway. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boulevard.md)
- The spine is Grand Boulevard between Keith Road and Highway 1 on the City side — a wide, tree-lined garden boulevard. A neighbourhood profile describes Boulevard as "one of North Vancouver's most distinctive detached neighbourhoods," centred on Grand Boulevard, with the east streets running from Keith Road through East 22nd Street. http://wallacegreen.ca/boulevard
- Grand Boulevard began as a 1906 garden subdivision: the boulevard itself is 105 m wide with a 1.6 km central garden, and the Grand Boulevard Park / Green Necklace area is treated as a heritage character area. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boulevard.md; register entry: https://www.historicplaces.ca/fr/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=2425)
- Schools beside the corridor: Queensbury Elementary, 2020 Moody Ave; Ridgeway Elementary, 420 E 8th St; Sutherland Secondary, 1860 Sutherland Ave. The SD44 school locator was not run for corridor addresses, so catchment assignments are UNVERIFIED; SD44's new Cloverley elementary school (opening 2026/27) may change Ridgeway and Queensbury catchments. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boulevard.md)
- Transit: TransLink route 240 runs on the corridor to downtown Vancouver via the Lions Gate Bridge. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boulevard.md)
- Where the housing stock sits: predominantly freehold detached houses. No named condo or townhome complex on the corridor was confirmed on any official or developer source checked. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boulevard.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- Provincial law (applies everywhere in BC, and to any strata a Boulevard buyer purchases nearby): under the Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, only 55+ age-restriction bylaws are valid in BC; all lower age bylaws (19+/40+/45+) have been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- Exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01: legacy residents who lived in the unit when the bylaw passed, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse/partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the resident cared for before age 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- All residential rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022; stratas may still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- City of North Vancouver side (south of Highway 1): the City's GO Zone rules, adopted Dec 8, 2025, allow up to 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, and up to 4 units on larger lots in Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 or up to 6 units in Low Rise Neighbourhood 2 / Residential Level 4 areas. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boulevard.md)
- District of North Vancouver side (north of Highway 1): District zoning (Bylaw 3210) sets R1 minimums of 4,000 m² lot area / 30 m width / 34 m depth and R2 minimums of 660 m² / 18 m / 34 m, with modifiers R2(S1) 335 m², R2(S2) 1,100 m² and R2(S3) 4,000 m²; since June 2024 a principal house plus a secondary suite plus a coach house (3 units) is permitted on single-family lots inside the Urban Containment Boundary, and small-scale multi-unit housing of up to 6 units applies on lots over 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boulevard.md)
- Taxes differ by side of the highway. City of North Vancouver 2026: total residential rate 3.34028 plus storm drainage 0.10697 per $1,000 of assessed value, due Jul 2, 2026. District of North Vancouver 2026: total residential rate 3.28195 per $1,000. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boulevard.md)
- Heritage: the Grand Boulevard Park / Green Necklace area is a heritage character area, which is the layer a buyer should check before planning exterior changes to a corridor house. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boulevard.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

**Q: Is there any 55+ condo or townhome I can buy in Boulevard?**
A: No — none verified. No building on the corridor appears on the North Shore 55+ buildings list, and no named strata complex on the corridor was confirmed on any official or developer source checked. https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/on-the-market/55-buildings-north-vancouver/ and (area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boulevard.md)

**Q: Where is the nearest verified 55+ ownership stock?**
A: West in Central Lonsdale: Ridge Park Gardens, 2059 Chesterfield Ave, and Sundale, 1420 Chesterfield Ave — both verified 55+ strata. See the Central Lonsdale 55+ local notes for their sourced details.

**Q: Can a detached house on Grand Boulevard be "55+"?**
A: Age-restriction bylaws are a strata tool under the Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2; Boulevard's houses are freehold detached, so no 55+ designation attaches to them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

**Q: If I buy in a 55+ strata nearby, do I have to be 55 to buy?**
A: No. The bylaw restricts who may live in the unit, not who may own it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

**Q: Can my younger spouse or partner live with me in a 55+ strata?**
A: Yes — since the May 1, 2023 expansion of Strata Property Regulation 7.01, a younger spouse/partner of the specified 55+ resident is exempt, as are children under 19 in that resident's care and adult children the resident cared for before age 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

**Q: Which municipality's taxes and permits apply to a Boulevard house?**
A: It depends on which side of Highway 1 the lot sits: City of North Vancouver south of the highway (2026 total residential 3.34028 + 0.10697 storm drainage per $1,000, due Jul 2, 2026), District of North Vancouver north of it (2026 total 3.28195 per $1,000). (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boulevard.md)

**Q: Can I add a suite or extra units to a Boulevard house?**
A: On the City side, the GO Zone rules adopted Dec 8, 2025 allow 3 units on lots up to 280 m² and 4–6 units on larger lots depending on the designation; on the District side, a principal house plus secondary suite plus coach house (3 units) has been permitted since June 2024 inside the Urban Containment Boundary. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boulevard.md)

**Q: If I buy into a 55+ strata, can I rent the unit out?**
A: Yes — all residential rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022, though a strata may still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation

**Q: What transit serves the corridor?**
A: TransLink route 240 runs on the corridor to downtown Vancouver via the Lions Gate Bridge. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boulevard.md)

**Q: Which schools serve the corridor?**
A: Queensbury Elementary (2020 Moody Ave), Ridgeway Elementary (420 E 8th St) and Sutherland Secondary (1860 Sutherland Ave) sit beside the corridor, but catchment assignments for a specific address are UNVERIFIED — the SD44 locator was not run — and SD44's new Cloverley elementary (2026/27) may change Ridgeway and Queensbury catchments.
