## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**Fraserview NW has ZERO verified 55+ ownership stock — no 55+ strata among its Jamieson Court towers, its Ginger Drive and Richmond Street low-rises, or its slope houses.** The sub-area is strata-heavy (53 of its 55 September 2026 listings were condos & townhomes) and none of it is age-restricted in any source checked. Its only seniors facility is a care home.

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- None. Every named Fraserview strata checked — Palace Quay on Jamieson Court, Fraser Mews on Ginger Drive, and the Richmond Street low-rises — shows pet and/or rental rules in its sources but no age restriction anywhere (see Checked list).

### Verified 55+ rental (not ownership)

- None verified inside this sub-area. No purpose-built 55+ rental building with a Fraserview address appears in any source checked. https://newwestcity.ca/database/rte/Housing%20web%20update%20-%20Non-Market%20details.pdf

### Retirement residences and care (rental/care — NOT strata ownership)

- **AgeCare Royal City — 77 Jamieson Court.** The city's long-term care home, on the same Jamieson Court cul-de-sac as the Palace Quay strata towers: 165 licensed beds, of which 120 are publicly funded and 45 private pay, per the operator, overlooking the Fraser River, with 24-hour nursing care. Admissions to the publicly funded beds begin with a Fraser Health assessment. Care only — you cannot buy in, and it is not a retirement residence you rent by choice in the ordinary way. Note a conflict in other Muse files: AgeCare Royal City has also been described as a "Sapperton" facility — Jamieson Court is not in Sapperton; Fraserview NW listings place the Jamieson Court addresses in this sub-area, and this file follows that geography. https://www.agecare.ca/communities/royal-city/ and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/palace-quay-71-jamieson-court
- No registered seniors assisted-living residence has a Fraserview address: BC's Assisted Living Registry lists exactly two seniors (class SN) residences in New Westminster — Thornebridge Gardens, 649 8th Avenue (Moody Park / Uptown edge), and Victoria Heights, 230 Ross Drive (Victoria Hill) — and neither is in this sub-area. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANew%20Westminster&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=asc

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. No Fraserview strata checked has used this power — the absence of a 55+ bylaw is the verified fact here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why Palace Quay's database line "rentals allowed w/restrictions" has to be read against the current registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/palace-quay-71-jamieson-court
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001** governs the area. Houses sit in the Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series, s.310) — RS-1 sets a minimum site area of 557.40 m² (6,000 sq. ft.) and a floor space ratio of 0.5; the RS districts allow secondary suites, and laneway / carriage houses are regulated under the same bylaw. The bylaw's townhouse districts are RT-2 through RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B (the RT-3 series is in Queensborough), and the 836.10 m² (9,000 sq. ft.) figure in the bylaw is a density threshold in the RM multiple-dwelling districts, not a townhouse minimum — an earlier draft of this area's research stated RT-A/RT-B districts that do not exist in this bylaw; that error was corrected in the research file and must not be repeated. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Plan: Our City 2041, the Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 7925, 2017**, governs the area, including its Development Permit Areas (Schedule B) for the mainland. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- **Flood — the slope's defining rule set:** the City states that both Queensborough and sections of the mainland are included within the provincial floodplain, and that five of the city's twelve square kilometres fall within the Ministry of Environment flood boundaries. Lot-level flood status for a specific Fraserview property: UNVERIFIED — check with the City, and for waterfront-edge strata read the building's flood and insurance position in the strata documents. http://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management
- **City taxes and utilities:** New Westminster 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 3, 2026; the City operates its own electrical utility and City water / sewer services. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/whats-happening/index/main/175/site_post/page10.php

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Fraserview NW?** None that any source verifies. The sub-area's market is strata — 53 condos & townhomes listed in September 2026 with 10 sold (19%) against just 2 detached listings — and every named complex checked is general-market. A buyer who wants a legal 55+ purchase is not finding one in New Westminster's Fraserview; do not confuse the answer with Vancouver's Fraserview, a different sub-area in a different city. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file; building checks Nov 1, 2026.)
- **Q: Is Palace Quay a seniors building? It has a pool, a caretaker and a quiet cul-de-sac.** No. Palace Quay (69/71 Jamieson Court) is a general-market Bosa strata from the early 1990s; its recorded restrictions are pets and rentals, with no age restriction, and its listings market it to families and professionals as well as downsizers. Buy the building for its amenities and river views — not for an age-restricted community it does not legally have. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/palace-quay-71-jamieson-court
- **Q: There is a care home on the same street — does that make Jamieson Court a seniors enclave?** No. AgeCare Royal City (77 Jamieson Court) is a 165-bed long-term care home whose publicly funded beds are allocated through a Fraser Health assessment; it shares a cul-de-sac with two general-market stratas. Living beside it gives a household no priority, no services and no age-restricted neighbours. https://www.agecare.ca/communities/royal-city/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a Jamieson Court or Ginger Drive condo?** Long-term rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022, whatever a building database still prints; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Confirm the building's current registered bylaws and the City's short-term rental rules before counting on income. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the waterfront buildings?** Building by building: Palace Quay's record shows pets allowed with restrictions, and a current Palace Quay listing records 2 pets OK with a storage locker and secure parking; Fraser Mews is described in listings as pet-friendly. The Form B and registered bylaws for the specific building govern — not the listing summary. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/palace-quay-71-jamieson-court and https://www.zolo.ca/new-westminster-real-estate/69-jamieson-court/1603
- **Q: Is this slope in a floodplain?** Sections of the mainland are within the provincial floodplain and five of the city's twelve square kilometres fall within the Ministry flood boundaries — so the answer is lot-by-lot and building-by-building, not uniform. For a waterfront-edge strata, the working questions are the unit's floor level, the parkade's position, and what the strata's insurance summary and depreciation report say. http://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management
- **Q: How do I get to SkyTrain without a car?** Route 103 runs the Quayside–Fraserview waterfront to New Westminster Station; the nearest Expo Line stations are New Westminster Station and 22nd Street Station, neither inside the sub-area. A car-free senior should test the walk from the specific building to the route 103 stop, on a slope, before buying. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2024-schedules/september/burnaby-new-west-p155.pdf
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in Fraserview?** The only in-area rung is long-term care at AgeCare Royal City, reached through the Fraser Health assessment process — there is no independent or assisted seniors rental in the sub-area to step into first. The city's registered assisted-living residences are Thornebridge Gardens (649 8th Ave) and Victoria Heights (230 Ross Dr), both outside Fraserview. https://www.agecare.ca/communities/royal-city/ and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANew%20Westminster&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=asc

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The name collision is the file's biggest practical risk (flagged in the area research, Nov 1, 2026):** "Fraserview" without the "NW" pulls Vancouver's Fraserview — different city, different school district (this one is SD40), different price structure. Every ad, comp pull and showing note for this sub-area needs "Fraserview NW" or "Fraserview New Westminster" written out, or the evidence base quietly becomes another city's. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/rte/2_4 Parks and Open Space.pdf
- **One cul-de-sac holds the whole seniors story of this sub-area (checked Nov 1, 2026):** Jamieson Court contains a general-market high-rise strata (Palace Quay, 69/71), a low-rise strata (Jamieson Place, 72) and a 165-bed long-term care home (AgeCare Royal City, 77) side by side. From the street it looks like a planned seniors enclave; legally it is two unrestricted stratas beside a Fraser Health-allocated care facility. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/palace-quay-71-jamieson-court and https://www.agecare.ca/communities/royal-city/
- **The zoning error that must not travel (corrected in the area research, Nov 1, 2026):** an earlier pass at this area's research invented "RT-A / RT-B" townhouse districts for New Westminster. The real bylaw (No. 6680, 2001) has RT-2, RT-2A, RT-2B and RT-2C for townhouses, with RT-3/3A/3B in Queensborough, and the 836.10 m² figure sometimes quoted is an RM-district density threshold, not a townhouse lot minimum. Any Fraserview copy built on RT-A/RT-B is built on nothing. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **The sub-area's own name is on its buildings — and means nothing about age (checked Nov 1, 2026):** the low-rises at 25 Richmond Street ("Fraserview") and 58 Richmond Street (Fraser View Park) carry the sub-area's name, and neither shows an age restriction in any source checked. In this sub-area "Fraserview" branding is about the river view over the North Arm, full stop. (Research statement: sources checked Nov 1, 2026.)
- **The detached market here is a rounding error (September 2026):** 2 detached listings and 0 detached solds against 53 strata listings and 10 strata solds (19%) — a senior house-hunting specifically in Fraserview NW is shopping a two-listing market, while the strata stock that dominates has no age-restricted option inside it (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file).
- **Ginger Drive is mainland, whatever the name suggests (checked Nov 1, 2026):** the Ginger Drive buildings (Fraser Mews, the two co-ops) carry V3L postal codes and Fraserview NW board labels — they sit on the mainland slope near Queen's Park, not in Queensborough (V3M) across the river. Co-op and strata documents filed under a Queensborough search will miss them, and vice versa. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3158816-415-365-ginger-drive-new-westminster-bc-v3l-5l5
