## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. This matters in White Rock in particular, where older listings and older news coverage describe a mix of age restrictions — only the 55+ form can still be enforceable. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents — the exact situation the 2019 White Rock news report describes); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. Listing lines in this file's stock that say "rentals not allowed" or "No rentals permitted" (Semiahmoo Estates directory, The Capistrano listing) predate or ignore this — confirm the current position on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Which municipality's rules apply is decided by the civic address, not the mailing flavour.** Surrey: Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026; 18020 superseded) and Zoning By-law No. 12000. White Rock: Zoning Bylaw 2024, No. 2506 and OCP Bylaw 2017, No. 2220. The 16 Avenue buildings in Section 2 sit on both sides of that line within a few blocks of each other. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Surrey suites and short-term rentals (Surrey buildings only):** one secondary suite per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, capped at 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area and never inside a coach house or garden suite — there is no current 90 m² suite cap in Bylaw No. 12000. A short-term rental (under 90 consecutive days) may be hosted only by an owner who lives at the property as their primary residence, with a $358-per-year business licence. White Rock's own tax due date, penalties and short-term rental rules are UNVERIFIED in this series — they were not read on a City of White Rock page and are not stated here. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Strata documents are the purchase:** for a condo in this band the live page's own checklist is the Form B (fees, levies, contingency reserve), the Form F, the bylaws and rules (pets, parking, rentals — and the age bylaw itself) and the depreciation report, which stratas with five or more lots must have under provincial rules. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-condos-under-800k.html
- **Sea Ridge Bays is not a pad tenancy:** it is a bare-land strata, so the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act pad rules do not apply to it. Where a true BC park pad tenancy applies, the 2026 increase limit is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees, once every 12 months on three full months' notice. No verified 55+ pad-rental park exists in South Surrey / White Rock in this series in any case. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. White Rock's own calendar is UNVERIFIED here (above). https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

- **Q: I want a 55+ condo — not a townhouse — in South Surrey. What actually exists?** Condo-form, verified: Semiahmoo Estates (2239 152 St) and the building at 13965 16 Ave, both in Surrey's Sunnyside Park. - **Q: Why do so many White Rock listings mention an age restriction?** Because White Rock's older condo stock genuinely carries them — a 2019 Peace Arch News report described age-restriction bylaws as common in White Rock condos. But "common" is not "this building": The Tiffany's current listing says "All ages welcome" in terms. Read the specific building's registered bylaws and Form B. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/PAN/190807/PANN190807_A03.pdf and https://www.zolo.ca/white-rock-real-estate/15080-prospect-avenue/206
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ condo here?** Yes — provincial rules exempt a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); Peninsula Village's listings put the local form as "One person 55+." Caregivers, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a 55+ condo for a parent, or to hold?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside, not who may own. A 55+ parent can be the resident of a unit owned by a younger buyer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: The listing says "no rentals permitted." Can I still rent my unit out long-term?** A residential rental-restriction bylaw has been unenforceable anywhere in BC since Nov 24, 2022 — the listing line does not change that. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted (and, in Surrey, need the owner-occupier licence in Section 3). Confirm the strata's current bylaws on the Form B rather than arguing from the listing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** The verified stock splits: Semiahmoo Estates' directory records pets not allowed, and The Capistrano's listing states no pets permitted; Peninsula Village and Crescent Gardens (care) record pet-friendly positions in their sources. There is no area-wide answer — the specific building's bylaws decide, and a listing's pet line is listing-stated until the bylaws confirm it. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Does "under $800K" describe this market?** Carefully: SnapStats publishes no price-band data and no condo-only cut. Its S Surrey White Rock Condos & Townhomes median was $700,000 in September 2026 (593 active listings, 90 sold, 99% sale-to-list, 33 days) — a combined condo-and-townhome, two-municipality figure that sits $100,000 below this page's cap. It is context for where the cap sits, not a count or a price for condos under $800K, and no band-specific figure is stated anywhere in this file. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report.)
- **Q: Where does the attached stock actually sit?** By September 2026 inventory, White Rock held 193 of the community's 593 Condos & Townhomes listings, Grandview 146 and King George Corridor 131; the highest attached sales ratio was Sunnyside Park at 32% (9 sales on 28 listings). Combined condo-and-townhome counts — not counts of condos under the cap, and not counts of 55+ units. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report.)
- **Q: Where is the hospital, and can I get to Vancouver without a car?** Peace Arch Hospital (15521 Russell Ave, White Rock) is the local hospital, 24 hours, with emergency, surgery and maternity care. Route 351 runs from White Rock Centre via the South Surrey Park & Ride and Highway 99 to Bridgeport Station on the Canada Line. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/White-Rock/peace-arch-hospital and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt351.pdf
- **Q: If I widen from a condo to any 55+ ownership form in South Surrey, what opens up?** The gated rancher townhouse complexes — Peninsula Village (95 townhomes), Bishop's Green, St. Martin's Lane, The Crescent (57 homes) — plus The Greens at Douglas (83 townhomes, built 2012–2013, the newest of the set, carried as a flagged roster addition) and Sea Ridge Bays' bare-land strata lots.
