# Chelsea Park (West Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

Shopping or grocery inside Chelsea Park itself: UNVERIFIED (none confirmed). https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf and https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/facilities/seniors-activity-centre

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why the "rentals not allowed" line in older building data for Tudor Gardens must be re-read against current law and the building's current bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **District zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010.** Chelsea Park houses sit in the RS single-family zones (RS2, RS3, RS5 and RS7 appear in District material here); Section 200 states minimum site areas of 8,094 m² in RS1, 1,625 m² in RS2, 975 m² in RS3, 731 m² in RS4 and 488 m² in RS5, with secondary suites and detached secondary suites (coach houses) among the permitted uses addressed in District building-permit guidance. The bylaw's ground-oriented rules allow 4 dwellings per lot, or 6 if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop — the stay-in-place route (suite for a caregiver or family) instead of a 55+ move. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf and https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf
- **Community plan:** the District of West Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 4985, 2018, covers Chelsea Park; no Chelsea Park-specific local area plan is confirmed. On December 1, 2025, Council adopted OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 5406, 2025, aligning the OCP with the 2024 Interim Housing Needs Report. https://westvancouver.ca/government-administration/major-projects/official-community-plan-review and https://westvancouver.ca/business-development/housing/official-community-plan-housing-policy-update
- **District seniors-housing policy context:** the District's own Canada Community-Building Fund reporting states seniors were about one in five West Vancouver residents in 2001 and nearly one in three in 2021, with a median age of 51 in 2021, and that the 2018 OCP's policies include expanding housing choice in existing neighbourhoods; the Ambleside planning work expressly contemplates "new non-market rental, market rental, rent-to-own, co-op, seniors, strata apartment, and strata 'missing-middle' housing" — in Ambleside, where the existing seniors stock already sits, not in pockets like Chelsea Park. https://www.ubcm.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/West%20Vancouver.pdf
- **British Properties context:** the District's history page states a British group led by the Guinness family purchased most of the Upper Lands and began to build the British Properties, and built the Lions Gate Bridge — Chelsea Park sits at the south edge of those lands, which is why its comparables and marketing often sit under British Properties labels. Whether any British Properties building scheme or covenant step applies to a specific Chelsea street is UNCONFIRMED — check title per lot. https://westvancouver.ca/arts-culture/heritage/west-vancouver-yesterday-and-today
- **Hazard permits:** an Environmental Development Permit is required for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank, and a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit for work within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface; a creek, slope or foreshore fact specific to Chelsea Park is UNVERIFIED. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Taxes:** District of West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026. At Chelsea Park price points the Province's additional school tax can apply — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. Water and sewer are metered and billed quarterly; the District's water report lists a pump house at 2600 Chelsea Court, confirming District water service on the street. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Chelsea Park?** None inside the pocket — there is no verified strata of any kind in Chelsea Park, let alone a 55+ one; the housing is freehold detached houses on Chelsea Court, Chelsea Place, Chelsea Close, Chairlift Road and Skilift Place. The nearest verified 55+ ownership buildings are downhill: Tudor Gardens, 843 22nd St (Dundarave), and Westview Terrace, 1390 Duchess Ave (Ambleside). https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/DWVMunicipalMap202306.pdf and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/tudor-gardens-843-22-street
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata like Tudor Gardens?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, whatever older building data says. The tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is there a 55+ rental building near Chelsea Park?** Yes, in Ambleside: the Kiwanis North Shore Housing Society's West Vancouver buildings (Kiwanis Manor, 959 21st St; The Court, 2151 Gordon Ave; The Cypress, 999 21st St; The Terrace, 975 21st St) are independent-living rentals for 55+, below-market, with income/asset eligibility, preference for North Shore residents, and no pets. Rental only — nothing to buy. https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/kiwanis-manor/
- **Q: If I stay in my Chelsea Park house, can I add a suite or coach house?** That is the route the zoning contemplates: secondary suite and detached secondary suite (coach house) applications are addressed in District building-permit guidance for the RS zones, and the ground-oriented rules allow up to 4 dwellings per lot (6 within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop), subject to the lot's confirmed zone on the District Zoning Map and the wildfire/watercourse permit checks. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf and https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Q: Which school catchment is a Chelsea Park house in?** UNVERIFIED — do not take it from the street name or from proximity. The SD45 locator returned no result for the sample address 2600 Chelsea Court because it falls outside the locator's listed ranges for the street (even 2620–2748, odd 2611–2757); run the exact listing address. Westcot Elementary at the foot of the British Properties is nearby, but proximity is not catchment. https://westvancouverschools.ca/admissions/student-admissions-guidelines/
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the nearest 55+ options?** Tudor Gardens is recorded as no pets, and Kiwanis seniors communities do not permit pets; Westview Terrace's pet position is UNCONFIRMED from the sources checked — confirm in the specific building's bylaws/Form B. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/tudor-gardens-843-22-street and https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/kiwanis-manor/
- **Q: Where is the hospital, and what if I need care later?** Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver, serves the North Shore including West Vancouver. The in-District ladder is downhill in Ambleside: registered assisted living at Amica West Vancouver (659 Clyde Ave), Amica Lions Gate (Keith Rd) and Hollyburn House (2095 Marine Dr), then long-term care at Inglewood Care Centre (725 Inglewood Ave, 230 publicly funded beds). https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital and https://www.seniorsadvocatebc.ca/quickfacts/location/75989/
- **Q: What transit can a Chelsea Park senior actually use?** The pocket's own service is the 256 community shuttle via Skilift Road to Park Royal, with the 251 on Queens Avenue below — useful for a Park Royal trip, but most Chelsea Park households drive for daily trips. The Seniors' Activity Centre in Ambleside also runs a seniors' shuttle bus. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/210/tt256.pdf and https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-programs-services/seniors-services

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Chelsea Park" is not a District neighbourhood name at all** — the District's own material shows only the street cluster (Chelsea Court/Place/Close, Chairlift Road, Skilift Place, Bramwell Road) and a pump house at 2600 Chelsea Court. Comparable sales and even school-locator ranges may sit under British Properties or Chartwell labels, so a comp set built only on the "Chelsea Park" name will be thin and possibly wrong. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/DWVMunicipalMap202306.pdf
- **The school locator literally has a hole on Chelsea Court:** its listed ranges start at 2620 (even) and 2611 (odd), so an address like the District's own pump house at 2600 returns no catchment result. Any listing that states a Chelsea Park catchment without an address-level locator run is asserting something the tool itself did not confirm. https://westvancouverschools.ca/admissions/student-admissions-guidelines/
- **The whole District has only three buildings a North Shore guide lists as 55+-bylaw strata** — Westview Terrace (1390 Duchess Ave), Tudor Gardens (843 22nd St) and Lincoln Gardens (address not stated on the guide page — UNCONFIRMED here) — all down the hill in Ambleside/Dundarave. For a pocket this small, "55+ in Chelsea Park" as a page promise would be empty; the honest product is the house-plus-suite route or the downhill buildings. https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/on-the-market/55-plus-buildings-west-vancouver/
- **The one residence that name-checks this end of the District:** Amica Lions Gate's own page says many of its residents come from the West Vancouver communities of Ambleside, Dundarave and British Properties — Chelsea Park sits at the British Properties' south edge, so that residence is where neighbours from these streets have actually gone, per the operator. https://www.amica.ca/lions-gate
- **The District's big seniors build is the Inglewood redevelopment, in Ambleside:** on September 21, 2026, Council approved the two-phase redevelopment of Inglewood Care Centre (725 Inglewood Ave) — Phase 1, a 364-bed publicly funded long-term care facility; Phase 2, 200 independent-living units and 161 seniors rental housing units. The District does not handle tenant placement; Baptist Housing does. https://westvancouver.ca/business-development/development-applications/inglewood-campus-care
- **Kiwanis Manor carries two descriptions in two sources** — independent 55+ rental on the society's site, registered assisted living in BC's Assisted Living Registry under a partnership name. Confirm which product it actually offers before describing it to a client. https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/kiwanis-manor/ and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3AArmstrong&f%5B1%5D=city%3AMasset&f%5B2%5D=city%3AWest%20Vancouver&f%5B3%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=asc
- **The District's seniors services are concentrated exactly where the seniors housing is:** the Seniors' Activity Centre at 695 21st Street — 55+ programs, Garden Side Bistro, SACS on 21st shop, rehabilitation programs and a shuttle bus — sits across from Hollyburn House and a block from the Kiwanis 21st Street cluster. A Chelsea Park senior who moves downhill joins that cluster; one who stays uses it by car or shuttle. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-programs-services/seniors-services
