# Oakdale (Burnaby) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**SOURCE CONFLICT FLAGGED AT THE TOP:** this file was assigned as a City of Vancouver (east side) area, but the area research file for Oakdale (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/oakdale.md) frames Oakdale as a **City of Burnaby** sub-area — the GVR board sub-area along the Lougheed Highway corridor east of Lougheed Town Centre, in School District 41 — and warns against confusing it with Oakdale in Coquitlam (Burquitlam, SD43). There is no Vancouver neighbourhood called Oakdale in the sources read. This file follows the research file: Oakdale is treated as Burnaby throughout, with Burnaby bylaws, Burnaby taxes and Fraser Health services.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. (This provincial law is the same in Burnaby as in Vancouver — it is reproduced here because a buyer comparing Oakdale with Vancouver stock is under the same strata statute.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Zoning bylaw — note the name change.** The new **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026** was adopted June 9, 2026, replaced the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965, and took effect July 1, 2026. Use the 2026 bylaw. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **R1 SSMUH zoning:** Burnaby consolidated its single- and two-family districts into the R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing district — 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; SSMUH lots require a minimum lot width of 10 m and minimum lot area of 281 m², permitted forms include single-family homes, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. On Oakdale's detached streets, SSMUH infill is the only mechanism adding smaller ground-oriented homes; none of it is age-restricted. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP: the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan** was adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP, and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict; Lougheed is one of Burnaby's town centres under that framework. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Property taxes and utilities:** Burnaby's 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date; Council approved a 2.9% tax increase (about $61 on the average home) in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan. The detached single-family water utility charge is $652.33, with a 5% discount if paid by the due date, and for single-family homes sewer is billed as a sewer parcel tax on the property tax notice. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **Hazards:** no floodplain, ALR or hazard designation for this sub-area was confirmed in the sources read; parcel-level status is a property-specific check (ALR status is checked in the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder): UNVERIFIED for individual lots. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Oakdale?** None that any source verifies — and the reason is broader than age bylaws: Oakdale (Burnaby) has effectively no strata stock at all in the current market (5 detached listings, 0 condos & townhomes listed in September 2026). A 55+ buyer here is buying a regular detached house with no age bylaw, or looking at a different sub-area for a 55+ building. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Oakdale in Vancouver?** No — and this is the first thing to settle in any Oakdale conversation. This Oakdale is a City of Burnaby board sub-area on the Lougheed corridor (Burnaby bylaws, Burnaby taxes, School District 41). A second Oakdale in Burquitlam, across North Road, is in the City of Coquitlam (SD43). Neither is in Vancouver. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres and https://burnabyschools.ca/about-sd41-overview/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Burnaby?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — a BC age bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I add homes to an Oakdale house lot for a parent or caregiver?** That is the realistic ageing-in-place route here: under Burnaby's R1 SSMUH district most lots can hold 3–4 homes (duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites are permitted forms), with up to 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and multi-unit lots may be stratified. Distance to the Lougheed corridor stops is therefore a priced fact, not a slogan. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day?** Lougheed Town Centre Station (Millennium Line / Expo Line Lougheed branch) west of the sub-area is the rapid-transit anchor; there is no station inside Oakdale, and specific bus routes for its streets were UNVERIFIED in the sources read. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** The city's hospital is Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid Street (Fraser Health), in central Burnaby south-west of Oakdale. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- **Q: If I need seniors housing or care later, can I stay in Oakdale?** Not inside the sub-area — there is no assisted-living residence, long-term care home, retirement residence or subsidized seniors building verified with an Oakdale address. The care and rental steps mean moving to another Burnaby sub-area (the Registry's Burnaby list is in Section 2) or another city. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ABurnaby&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc
- **Q: How do I price a house here when almost nothing sells?** Carefully, and from outside the sub-area: Oakdale recorded zero sales in September 2026 (0 solds on 5 detached listings, no attached listings), so both sales ratios are not applicable and pricing has to be built from Lougheed-side, Sullivan Heights and Forest Hills comparables over a longer window. Burnaby-wide, the median detached sale price that month was $1,825,000 (all figures as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file).

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **There are three Oakdales in Canadian housing searches and only one is this file's.** Oakdale, Burnaby (this sub-area); Oakdale in Burquitlam, Coquitlam — where the presale towers using the name are actually being marketed; and the "Senior Residences of Oakdale," a real 55+ project in Charleswood, Winnipeg. A name search alone will hand a buyer all three as if they were one place. https://www.oakdaleresidences.com/faq-mojave and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- **The school-district line is the cleanest way to separate the two local Oakdales.** Burnaby's SD41 has no middle-school tier (K–7 elementary, 8–12 secondary); Coquitlam's SD43 across North Road does. If a listing's school pitch mentions a middle school, it is not this Oakdale. https://burnabyschools.ca/about-sd41-overview/
- **A zero-sale month is normal here, not a red flag by itself.** With 5 detached listings and 0 sales in September 2026, Oakdale's market reads through its neighbours — the sub-area is a handful of house streets, and thin data is its permanent condition. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.)
- **Burnaby replaced its entire zoning bylaw in 2026.** The Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (adopted June 9, 2026, in force July 1, 2026) repealed and replaced the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 — documents or summaries that still cite the 1965 bylaw, or the old single-family district names, are out of date. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **The density story is west of here, not in here.** Tower and apartment development concentrates at Lougheed Town Centre under the town-centre framework, while Oakdale itself stays detached houses under R1 SSMUH — and the Burnaby 2050 OCP (adopted December 9, 2025) now governs over the older community-plan maps where expectations conflict. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Burnaby taxes have two penalty steps and a discounted water bill.** 2026 property taxes were due July 3, 2026 (5% penalty after July, a further 5% after September), and the detached-house water charge of $652.33 carries a 5% discount if paid by the due date — small numbers that belong in a first-year budget for a senior downsizing into a house here. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
