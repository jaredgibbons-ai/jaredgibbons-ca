## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Manufactured-home parks run on tenancy law, not strata law.** Pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and its Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003), not the Strata Property Act — so the strata age-bylaw machinery (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, Regulation 7.01 exemptions) does not apply inside a park. A home owner must provide a purchaser with the written tenancy agreement and the written park rules, and a sale is an assignment of the pad tenancy that requires the park owner's consent. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- **A park's 55+ rule has its own legal footing.** The MHPTA assignment rules expressly recognise a park where every pad is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 (or 2+ tenants, at least one of whom has reached 55), as set out in s. 10(2)(b)(i) of the Human Rights Code — the park owner can refuse an assignment where no purchaser meets that age requirement. That is how the three Newton / Fleetwood parks' 55+ rules operate; South Surrey has no verified pad-rent park for the rule to attach to. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/consol20/consol20/02077_pit
- **Assignment consent has a clock.** Under MHPTA s. 28 a tenant may assign only with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the regulations), an arbitrator's order, or where the tenancy agreement authorizes it; the park owner must respond within 10 days of the written request (RTB Form 10), silence counts as consent given, and no fee can be charged for consent. Before asking, the seller must give the proposed buyer written copies of the park rules and the relevant parts of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Pad rent increases are capped.** A park landlord may increase rent only with 3 full months' notice, only once every 12 months, and only up to the yearly limit — 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026, and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027; the proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park; further increases require a Residential Tenancy Branch application. On assignment, the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, as does the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Park closure / conversion (MHPTA s. 42, as the Province states it):** where a landlord intends to convert all or a significant part of a park to another use, the landlord must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; the tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, may dispute within 15 days, and may in defined circumstances apply for additional compensation of the home's assessed value minus the $20,000. Surrey's Tenant Protection Bylaw, 2025, No. 21788 does NOT apply to property governed by the MHPTA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_reg_21788.pdf
- **Registration is what makes a sale effective, and the CSA label is a sale gate.** In BC, the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered with the Manufactured Home Registry, through a lawyer or notary filing the Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership forms. A home may not be sold unless it has the original legible CSA decal attached (Z-240 for mobile homes, A-277 for modular); if the label is lost, Technical Safety BC has a licensed electrical contractor inspect the wiring, and the electrical approval label prepared can be used in place of a CSA label. A permit to move a home is issued by the Registry and expires 30 days after issue. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Taxes on a home in a park:** the Province states a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, but the assessed value will not include the value of the land if the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **For contrast — the strata rule that applies at Sea Ridge Bays, not in any park:** only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; sub-55 bylaws invalid since Nov 24, 2022), the bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit rather than who may own it, Regulation 7.01 exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19; and strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning:** Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000 carries a dedicated zone for this housing form — Part 19, Zone RM-M, Manufactured Home Residential Zone — and the City's regulatory-bylaw list separately carries Bylaw No. 6142, "Mobile Homes & Trailer." The live page's claim applying RM-M to the specific South Surrey parks, and its claim that White Rock's Zoning Bylaw 2506 has no manufactured-home-park zone, were not read in the bylaw texts in this pass: UNVERIFIED as applied. A specific park's zone is confirmed parcel by parcel in COSMOS. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/regulatory-bylaws
- **OCP: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2026, No. 21900** is the current OCP — adopted June 15, 2026, replacing Bylaw No. 18020. White Rock addresses, if any appear, fall under White Rock's own OCP Bylaw 2017, No. 2220 instead. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan and https://www.whiterockcity.ca/514/Resources-Documents

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which South Surrey manufactured-home communities are actually 55+?** One verifies: Sea Ridge Bays (15875 20 Ave), a bare-land strata where you own the lot — listings state "Sea Ridge Bays is 55+" and record title as Freehold Strata. No pad-rent park in South Surrey verifies as 55+; Surrey's three verified 55+ parks are in Fleetwood and Newton. The Cranley Drive community's 55+ claim is listing text only — UNVERIFIED, not counted. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: What do I own when I buy in a park, versus at Sea Ridge Bays?** In a park, the manufactured home only — you rent the pad under a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement, and your purchase is an assignment of that tenancy requiring the park owner's prior written consent under MHPTA s. 28, effective once registered with the Manufactured Home Registry. At Sea Ridge Bays you own the strata lot as well, pay a strata fee instead of pad rent, and close on strata documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live at Sea Ridge Bays?** Yes, under the strata framework: provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). In a pad-rent park, by contrast, a younger spouse is governed by the park's own rules and the Human Rights Code framing (a pad reserved for a tenant who has reached 55, or 2+ tenants at least one of whom has reached 55) — the strata exemption does not apply inside a park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- **Q: Can a park raise my pad rent when I buy, or charge me for approving me?** No to both mechanics: on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same and the scheduled date of a subsequent increase does not change; increases otherwise need 3 full months' notice, at most once every 12 months, capped at the yearly limit (2.3% plus the proportional amount for 2026). Consent itself cannot be charged for, and the park owner must respond within 10 days of a written request — silence counts as consent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: The live page names parks, an operator and a 50+ covenant on Cranley Drive. Can I rely on those?** Not yet. The research pass could not confirm any of them on a City, developer, strata or park-operator source — the park names and operator attribution for 1840 160 Street, the Cranley Drive covenant claim, the fee figures and the two zoning claims are all UNVERIFIED in this file. Pull the park rules, the tenancy agreement and the title documents for the specific home before writing. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-mobile-homes.html and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/south-surrey-mobile-homes.md

For the 160 Street and Cranley Drive communities, pet rules are UNVERIFIED — read the park rules or community documents. Sources: Section 2 above and ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/surrey-mobile-homes-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: What if the home has lost its CSA label?** A manufactured home may not be sold unless it has the original and legible CSA decal attached (Z-240 for a mobile home, A-277 for a modular). If the label is removed or lost, the home must be re-certified: Technical Safety BC arranges a licensed electrical contractor's inspection, and the electrical approval label prepared can be used in place of a CSA label to list the home for sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
