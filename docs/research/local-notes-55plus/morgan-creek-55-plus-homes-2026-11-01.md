## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why the "rentals not allowed" lines in the Carrington-area directory records cannot be read at face value. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **OCP — STANDING CORRECTION:** Surrey's current plan is the **Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2026, No. 21900**, finally adopted June 15, 2026. The previous PlanSurrey / OCP Bylaw, 2013, No. 18020 is superseded and is never cited as current. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Local plans for this ground:** the Rosemary Heights Central and West Neighbourhood Concept Plans and the Rosemary Heights Business Park Plan are the City's approved South Surrey land use plans covering the Rosemary Heights side of this sub-area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000**, as amended for small-scale multi-unit housing: a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; the City adopted the SSMUH zoning changes on July 8, 2024. This affects the detached lots, not the strata lots inside the Carrington community. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites:** a secondary suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area under Surrey Zoning Bylaw 12000; only one secondary suite is permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, and a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite. A "90 m²" figure is NOT the current Surrey cap and is not used here. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Property taxes (city-wide, not area-specific):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Property tax deferment and the home owner grant** are provincial and age-based: the regular deferment loan program covers homeowners 55 and older, and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) covers seniors 65+, both for a principal residence. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
- **For strata purchases,** the Form B (Information Certificate), the bylaws and rules, minutes, budget and depreciation report are where a complex's age bylaw is actually proved — for Carrington, that Form B check is the step that converts the listing-stated 55+ rule into a verified bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Morgan Creek?** One community: the Carrington townhouses on Rosemary Heights Crescent (The Carrington at 15500, with Braemore at Carrington at 15425 in the same community), verified 55+ in this pass from a current listing stating the rule in terms and a directory recording age restrictions for Braemore. It sits OUTSIDE Surrey's consolidated roster of 15 — carry it as a flagged addition pending re-consolidation, and prove the bylaw on the Form B. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is the complex actually called "Morgan Creek" the 55+ one?** No. The townhouse complex named Morgan Creek (15715 34 Ave, built 2000) records only pet and rental restrictions in its building directory — no age restriction. The 55+ community in this sub-area is Carrington, on Rosemary Heights Crescent. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/morgan-creek-15715-34-ave-wedgewood
- **Q: Edgewater looks like a seniors resort — is it 55+?** No source says so, and its building directory records only "pets allowed with restrictions, rentals allowed." It is a gated general-market condo community (built 2013) with a large clubhouse — amenities are not an age bylaw. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/edgewater-15195-36-avenue
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in Carrington?** Under the provincial framework, yes: a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption) is exempt, as are live-in caregivers, legacy residents, children under 19 in the resident's care and certain adult children. Confirm Carrington's registered bylaw wording on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in Carrington as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a Carrington townhome?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC strata — rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, even where a directory still records "rentals not allowed" for Braemore at Carrington. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Do I have to join or pay the golf club to live here?** No — the live page states Morgan Creek Golf Course describes itself as a public golf course, so living in the sub-area is not tied to golf; a buyer who never plays is buying the setting. Confirm any course question with the course directly. https://jaredgibbons.ca/morgan-creek.html
- **Q: If my care needs grow, can I stay in the sub-area?** There is an in-area ladder: independent strata living at Carrington, then Rosemary Heights Seniors Village (15240 34 Ave) with independent living, assisted living (42 Fraser Health suites) and long-term care (90 beds, 85 publicly subsidized) on one campus. The care campus is rental/care — placement runs through Fraser Health for the funded suites. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/9/4/assisted-living-at-rosemary-heights-seniors-village
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Peace Arch Hospital, 15521 Russell Avenue, White Rock — a Fraser Health community hospital with 24-hour emergency, surgery and maternity care, serving South Surrey from just south of the Surrey–White Rock line. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/White-Rock/peace-arch-hospital
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
