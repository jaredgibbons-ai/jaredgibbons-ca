## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT THIS HOME TYPE

- **ALR housing frame (ALC):** land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless the parcel qualifies for an additional residence, and local or First Nation governments may further regulate or restrict residences through zoning. A principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m², with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Suite rule (ALC):** a secondary suite is permitted in the ALR only when located within the principal residence — entirely contained within it, not in a separate or detached dwelling — and only where local bylaws permit suites; a local government may further restrict or prohibit suites or limit their size. In Langley that local layer is the Township's Zoning Bylaw 2500. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.tol.ca/en/services/parking-regulations.aspx
- **Additional residence (ALC):** at most one additional residence, of up to 90 m² on parcels 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. More, or larger, requires ALC approval. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Subdivision on ALR land (ALC):** strata subdivision is prohibited on ALR land under the frame addressed in ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6, and subdivision of ALR land generally requires ALC approval — the live page's FAQ statement to that effect is consistent with this frame, while its "rarely granted" characterization is the page's own wording. In the Township, subdivision also needs the Approving Officer's approval under the Land Title Act. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx and https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-poultry-farms-for-sale.html
- **Chicken quota — verified from the Board in this pass:** Section 4.01(c.1) of the BC Chicken Marketing Scheme, 1961 gives the BC Chicken Marketing Board the power to establish, issue, permit transfer, revoke or reduce quotas on terms the Board sets; quotas confer no property interest and remain at all times within the exclusive control of the Board. There is no centralized platform (or "quota exchange") for transferring chicken quota in BC — transfers are facilitated privately through hatchery/processor and grower connections, advertising or word-of-mouth, and the Board's only role currently is to approve quota transactions. https://bcchicken.ca/quota-use-and-access-consultation/ and https://bcchicken.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/QUAC-Discussion-Paper-FINAL.Oct_.2024.pdf
- **New entrant program — Board status:** the Board's January 2024 discussion paper records the New Entrant Grower program closed to applications since 2016, a February–April 2024 consultation on a renewed program, and a June 18, 2024 decision putting the final program decision in abeyance until the quota-access consultation completed; the Board's September 2025 Policy Directions record staff directed to develop a new merit-based program. Treat the program as under redesign, not an open route in, and confirm its live status with the Board. https://bcchicken.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/New-Entrant-Program-Discussion-Paper-January-2024.pdf and https://bcchicken.ca/quota-use-and-access-consultation/
- **Egg and turkey quota — UNVERIFIED in this file:** the live page's egg and turkey accounts (layer quota trading on a BC Egg exchange in the mid-to-high $300s per unit; turkey allocation measured in kilograms and leasable for at most two consecutive quota years; all three sectors supply managed in the terms the page states) were NOT verified on any BC Egg, BC Turkey or hatching-egg board page opened for this file. The chicken Board's verified "no quota exchange" position is a direct caution against carrying an exchange story across sectors without a board source. Treat every egg/turkey quota statement, and every quota price, as site copy only. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-poultry-farms-for-sale.html and https://bcchicken.ca/quota-use-and-access-consultation/
- **BC Assessment farm classification:** farm classification is a voluntary program; the gross-income thresholds are $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha, and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha; applications must be submitted by October 31 for the next assessment and tax year, and a buyer who plans to continue farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property transfer tax — general tiers only:** 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% on the portion above $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% on the portion above $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion of value above $3,000,000. How PTT and GST are apportioned between land, buildings, residence and quota on a specific poultry-farm purchase is UNVERIFIED in this file — have an agricultural lawyer and accountant model it before an offer is written. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Township SSMUH does not convert a farm parcel:** Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024; tiers 3 units ≤280 m², 4 units to <4,050 m², >4,050 m² ineligible; no 6-unit tier) is settled in this program as the Township's SSMUH instrument. No source read for this file states that SSMUH unit counts override the ALR residence frame on a poultry parcel — the ALC caps in this section are the operative housing limits on ALR land. Position as settled in this program; a parcel-specific Township confirmation is UNVERIFIED here. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo110/loo110/262_2023
- **Strata age law — why it does not reach a farm parcel:** the BC strata age regime (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ bylaws valid since Nov 24, 2022; residency restricted, not ownership; rental-restriction bylaws void since the same date) applies to strata corporations, and strata subdivision is prohibited on ALR land, so no 55+ strata product can be created out of a Langley poultry parcel under the frames in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Still UNVERIFIED in this file, recorded plainly:** barn construction, ventilation, manure-storage, biosecurity and agricultural environmental management requirements as stated rules (the live page's eight-item barn checklist is site copy only); any avian-influenza effect on a sale; the page's statements that most Langley/Fraser Valley poultry sales are off-market and that a turn-key operation sits well above raw farmland value, as verified market facts; and the farm-class position of Langley poultry farms as a group (classification is property-by-property with BC Assessment). https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-poultry-farms-for-sale.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is this page a Langley poultry listings page?** Not as read: the page text contains no listing cards or result count — it is an editorial guide to Langley poultry sectors, barn diligence, financing structure and quota questions, ending in an ALR address-check tool and a phone call to action. - **Q: Is quota included when I buy a Langley poultry farm, and what does quota cost?** For chicken, the verified frame is: quota remains within the exclusive control of the Board, there is no quota exchange, transfers happen privately and the Board approves the transaction — so inclusion is a contract question and cost is a private-negotiation question, not a published price. The live page's egg-layer figure (mid-to-high $300s per unit on BC Egg exchanges) is UNVERIFIED in this file and is not adopted here; verify any quota position and any price with the applicable marketing board before subjects are removed. https://bcchicken.ca/quota-use-and-access-consultation/ and https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-poultry-farms-for-sale.html
- **Q: What residence can a Langley poultry-farm parcel in the ALR have?** Under the ALC frame: one principal residence of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, with Township of Langley rules possibly more restrictive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What income must a farm operation show to keep farm classification?** BC Assessment's thresholds are $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha; applications are due by October 31 for the next tax year, and a buyer continuing to farm must apply by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Can I use the Township's new small-scale multi-unit rules to add homes on the farm?** Not on anything read for this file: Bylaw 6020's tiers (3 units ≤280 m²; 4 units to <4,050 m²; >4,050 m² ineligible; no 6-unit tier) are the Township's urban SSMUH instrument as settled in this program, while housing on ALR land is capped by the ALC frame — one principal residence, a suite inside it, one small additional residence. A multiplex plan on a poultry parcel has no source in this file. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo110/loo110/262_2023 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I split off a homesite, or retire on part of the farm and sell the rest?** Strata subdivision is prohibited on ALR land (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6, as settled in this program), subdivision generally requires ALC approval, and Township subdivision needs the Approving Officer. A retirement plan that depends on carving a residential lot out of the parcel is not supported by the frames in this file — the in-frame retirement housing on a farm is the additional residence (90 m² / 186 m²) beside the principal residence. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is there a 55+ community on, or attached to, any Langley poultry farm?** None verified: the verified Langley 55+ roster in this program (11 strata, the Cedar Creek Estates / Fernridge Park / Pineridge parks, and the Pioneer Park co-op) sits on residential parcels, none of it on poultry-farm land, and the six held names in Section 2 remain UNCONFIRMED and unadded. A buyer who wants both a poultry operation and a 55+ home is making two separate purchases in two separate regimes. https://murphyrealestategroup.ca/recip.html/1272399.search/SearchResults.form?_pg=3
- **Q: What did Langley homes sell for in September 2026 — does that price a poultry farm?** No. As residential board context only — NOT poultry-farm figures: the Langley community detached median sale price was $1,345,000 on 449 active listings and 64 sales, and the Condos & Townhomes median was $611,000 on 699 listings and 128 sales (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via this topic's research file). SnapStats has no farm or poultry category, and any Langley poultry-farm price figure is UNVERIFIED in this file — a farm valuation stands on the specific land, barns, residence and any quota included, checked part by part.
- **Q: Can a new entrant get chicken quota without buying an existing Langley farm?** Not as an open application route on the Board's own record: the New Entrant Grower program has been closed to applications since 2016, its renewed-program decision was put in abeyance in June 2024, and the Board's September 2025 directions describe a new merit-based program still being developed.
