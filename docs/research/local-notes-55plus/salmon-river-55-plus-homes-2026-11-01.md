# Salmon River, Township of Langley — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Plain finding up front: no 55+ complex of any kind — strata, manufactured-home park, rental, subsidized or care — was verified inside the Salmon River district in this pass. The 55+ angle for this area is thin to the point of absence, and the notes below document what was checked rather than dressing that up.**

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Salmon River is a rural district in the Township of Langley, along the Salmon River in the township's northwest, north of the Otter area and south of Fort Langley. The district follows the Salmon River valley between 232 Street and 264 Street, from the Fraser River side south toward 40 Avenue, with Rawlison Crescent and 56 Avenue on the upland side; no Township-published boundary for the board sub-area was confirmed. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- "Salmon River" is the name the board's data uses for this sub-area in the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, where the stock was effectively all detached: 22 detached listings against 4 attached listings in September 2026 (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, cited in the Salmon River research file, ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/salmon-river.md).
- The housing form is farm and acreage houses on large rural lots along the valley and on the 56 Avenue upland, under Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 and the Township's Rural Plan; the research file confirms no condo or townhome project inside the district was named on the Township's development-applications route. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- No SD35 school physically inside the Salmon River valley itself was confirmed; for the sample address 4452 256 Street, the SD35 School Locator returns North Otter Elementary and D.W. Poppy Secondary, with no middle school shown, and Coghlan Fundamental Elementary, a district choice school, stands at 4452 256 Street on the upland east side. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
- No recreation centre or library branch inside the district was confirmed; the nearest Township facilities are in Fort Langley and Walnut Grove, and the nearest store named with its address on a Township page is Lee's Market, 23320 Mavis Avenue, Fort Langley. https://www.tol.ca/en/parks-recreation/community-and-recreation-centres.aspx
- Transit: TransLink route 562 runs on Glover Road via 96 Avenue, 88 Avenue and 202 Street to Carvolth Exchange, serving the Fort Langley edge of the district; no TransLink route was confirmed on 56 Avenue, Rawlison Crescent or the valley roads inside the district. There is no SkyTrain or West Coast Express station in the district. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt562.pdf
- Health: no hospital or clinic inside the district was confirmed. Langley Memorial Hospital, 22051 Fraser Highway, is the Fraser Health community hospital with 24/7 emergency serving the area, southwest of the district. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Langley/langley-memorial-hospital
- Flood is a defining local fact: the valley is Fraser River floodplain land, and the Township states the Salmon River Pump Station was built to mitigate flooding of the surrounding agricultural lands during freshet and provides flood protection when Fraser River levels rise, with flood box gates that close as river levels rise. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **The strata age-bylaw rules have almost nothing to attach to here.** Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now: under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older, and any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). Salmon River's stock is non-strata freehold acreage, so there is no local strata for such a bylaw to govern — a buyer who wants a legally age-restricted community is not buying one in this district. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **For completeness, the standard BC rules:** the bylaw restricts who may LIVE in a unit, not who may own it; exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19; and residential rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022, while short-term rental restrictions remain allowed. All three points would matter only if a strata existed here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **What actually governs a downsizer purchase here is ALR and rural zoning, not age law.** Farm land in the valley is in the Agricultural Land Reserve in places; on ALR land the ALC permits one additional residence without an ALC application where its size rules are met — up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha — subject to Township bylaws. That additional-residence rule, not a "granny flat" assumption, is the legal route to housing a parent or caregiver on a Salmon River acreage. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Township small-scale multi-unit wording:** the Township states its zoning bylaw was updated for the provincial SSMUH rules, and that on lots larger than 4,050 m² the rules allow one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit in addition to the primary unit depending on zoning — again subject to the ALR rules above where the parcel is in the reserve. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Rural services are the real "rules" of daily life here:** properties may use wells (search the provincial GWELLS registry; well reports are due within 90 days of constructing, altering or decommissioning a well), and a home not on community sewer uses an onsite sewage system for which Fraser Health requires an Authorized Person to file a record of sewerage system before work and a letter of certification after. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/understanding-aquifers and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/resources-for-onsite-sewerage-systems-property-owners-and-authorized-persons
- **Manufactured-home parks, where they exist, run on park rules and the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not strata law** — which is why the age position of Westfield Courts above can only be settled by the park's own rules, not by the Strata Property Act exemptions. No park rule document for any park in this district was obtained in this pass — UNCONFIRMED.
- **Property taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes and utility fees were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to current-year taxes unpaid at midnight July 2, 2026, with a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026, and utility fees are now billed separately. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Salmon River?** None that any source verifies. There is no age-restricted strata, manufactured-home park, or seniors complex inside the district in any source checked. A 55+ buyer who wants this valley is buying an ordinary freehold acreage or farmhouse with no age restriction on resale — or buying into a verified 55+ complex in a neighbouring area instead. Sources: Sections 1 and 2 above.
- **Q: Is there a seniors manufactured-home park in the district?** Not verified. Westfield Courts at 4426 232 St is a manufactured-home park at the district's west edge, but no source checked states an age restriction for it. The nearest manufactured-home community whose listings actually discuss age, Langley Grove Estates on Fraser Highway, states it has no age restrictions and is in the Otter District, not Salmon River. https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/24330-fraser-highway/52
- **Q: Can I put a second home on an acreage for my parent or a caregiver?** Possibly, under land rules rather than seniors-housing rules: on ALR land, one additional residence is permitted without an ALC application at up to 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha), subject to Township bylaws, and the Township's SSMUH wording allows a suite or accessory unit on lots over 4,050 m² depending on zoning. Confirm the specific parcel's ALR status on the ALC maps first. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in the district?** No care option inside the district was verified — no long-term care home, assisted-living residence or retirement residence with a Salmon River address appears in the sources checked, including the Assisted Living Registry's Langley list. The care ladder for this area is reached by leaving the district, toward Fort Langley, Aldergrove or the urban Langley core. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?address=&f%5B0%5D=city%3ALangley&f%5B1%5D=city%3APort%20Hardy&f%5B2%5D=city%3ASooke&f%5B3%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&name=&order=alr_title&sort=asc
- **Q: Is the valley floor in a floodplain?** Yes, as a district fact: the Township states the Salmon River Pump Station provides flood protection when Fraser River levels rise and was built to mitigate flooding of the surrounding agricultural lands during freshet, and upgrades to it are in detailed design and tendering. Parcel-level status still has to be checked for the specific lot. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **Q: What transit can I use without driving?** Very little inside the district: route 562 serves Glover Road at the Fort Langley edge, and no route was confirmed on 56 Avenue, Rawlison Crescent or the valley roads. A car is the working assumption for a Salmon River purchase. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt562.pdf
- **Q: Are there rancher-style homes for one-level living?** Rancher-form houses exist as individual acreage listings — for example a current listing describes a rancher on a 1-acre Salmon River parcel — but they are ordinary freehold houses with no age restriction, no strata maintenance and no seniors services attached. One-level living here means finding the right house, not buying into a 55+ product. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3109660-23566-52-avenue-langley-british-columbia-v2z2p5-1899000/brochure
- **Q: What should I check about water and septic before buying?** The well record in GWELLS and the septic paper trail: well reports are due within 90 days of work on a well, and Fraser Health's Authorized Person filings (record of sewerage system before work, letter of certification after) are the compliance documents for an onsite system. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/understanding-aquifers and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/resources-for-onsite-sewerage-systems-property-owners-and-authorized-persons
- **Q: Which schools serve a Salmon River acreage?** Catchments are address-based in SD35: the sample upland address returned North Otter Elementary and D.W. Poppy Secondary, with no middle school shown — run the exact address in the School Locator, and note no school physically inside the valley was confirmed. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The "55+" search results for this district are borrowed from its neighbours.** Because the district has no age-restricted stock, portal searches for "Salmon River 55+" return product from Aldergrove, Brookswood and Langley City — the Zolo neighbourhood page for Salmon River itself lists Langley City, Willoughby Heights and Campbell Valley as its nearby neighbourhoods, which is how out-of-district complexes get attached to this name. https://www.zolo.ca/sitemap/langley/salmon-river
- **The Fraser Highway frontage at the district's south end misleads park searches twice over.** Langley Grove Estates fronts Fraser Highway near the district, is repeatedly described in listings as having no age restrictions, and is filed by the board under Otter District — a buyer told there is a "seniors park near Salmon River on Fraser Highway" is being pointed at an all-ages park in a different sub-area. https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/24330-fraser-highway/52
- **A manufactured home on a farm parcel is not a park home.** The district does produce manufactured-home listings — the 24183 Fraser Hwy offering is a manufactured home on 6.05 ALR acres with barns, sold on land value — with no park, no pad tenure and no age rule of any kind. https://www.zillow.com/homedetails/24183-Fraser-Hwy-Langley-BC-V2Z-2L3/2071787377_zpid/
- **Flood protection here is mechanical, and it is mid-upgrade.** The Salmon River Pump Station's flood box gates close as Fraser River levels rise, and the Township has authorized detailed design and tendering for upgrades to the station — the flood question on a valley parcel is partly a question about that station's works, not just elevation. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **The upland and the valley floor are different purchases.** The research file places Rawlison Crescent and 56 Avenue on the upland side and the farm parcels on the valley floor inside the dyke system — same sub-area name, different flood, well, septic and access realities, and neither side contains any seniors housing. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **The district's seniors infrastructure is a park name, not housing.** The Salmon River Natural Area is a Township park listed among the Fort Langley-area parks in the Township Annual Report — it is the only "Salmon River" institution aimed at recreation in the sources checked; there is no seniors centre, hall or housing office in the district in any source. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/plans-reports-strategies/Annual-Reports/Document-Feed/2019-Annual-Report.pdf
