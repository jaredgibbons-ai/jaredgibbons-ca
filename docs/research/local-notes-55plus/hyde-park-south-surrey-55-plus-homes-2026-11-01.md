## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. Neither Hyde Park complex has an age bylaw in any source checked — this rule matters here only because a buyer searching "Hyde Park 55+" needs to know both complexes were checked and carry none. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. The live page states the same frame for Hyde Park — review the current bylaws and minutes of the correct strata and confirm with its manager. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://jaredgibbons.ca/hyde-park-south-surrey.html
- **Two stratas, two rulebooks:** Hyde Park and Hyde Park Corner are separate complexes with separate strata plans per the live page, so the fees, bylaws, depreciation report and minutes differ between them — a bylaw answer for one complex is not an answer for the other. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hyde-park-south-surrey.html
- **Plans and zoning:** the current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900 (finally adopted June 15, 2026) — never cite the replaced Bylaw No. 18020 as current. Surrey's zoning bylaw is Zoning By-law No. 12000. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Secondary suites in Surrey:** the City defines a secondary suite as an accessory dwelling unit whose habitable floor area does not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit, within a single family, semi-detached or duplex building, and not a separate strata lot; only one suite is permitted per dwelling unit. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Suites in townhouses:** Surrey Council approved bylaw changes to permit secondary (lock-off) suites in townhouses in the RM-15, RM-23 and RM-30 zones for new developments in transit-oriented areas and frequent bus stop areas, with a suite minimum of 30 sq. m and less than 40% of the habitable floor area of the larger principal unit — a new-development permission, not a right inside an existing complex. https://www.Surrey.ca/news-events/news/surrey-creating-more-housing-options-secondary-suites-townhouses
- **Property taxes (city-wide):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Hyde Park a 55+ complex?** No — and neither is Hyde Park Corner. No source checked states an age restriction at either complex; listings describe family townhomes, and Hyde Park Corner's own listing copy calls its homes "ideal for families." Both are general-market strata townhome complexes. https://www.tonycikes.com/listing/r3083053-2828-156-street-12-surrey-bc-v3z-0c7/brochure
- **Q: Are Hyde Park and Hyde Park Corner the same complex?** No. The live page states Hyde Park Corner is a separate 16-townhome phase at 2828 156 Street, completed in 2021, with its own strata plan, and the developer's brochure lists the two separately — check which strata a listing belongs to before comparing fees or documents. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hyde-park-south-surrey.html
- **Q: Where is the nearest actual 55+ ownership stock?** In the neighbouring South Surrey sub-areas: Semiahmoo Estates (2239 152 St), 13965 16 Ave, Bishop's Green (2533 152 St) and St. Martin's Lane (15020 27A Ave) in Sunnyside Park; Peninsula Village (2500 152 St) and Sea Ridge Bays (15875 20 Ave) in King George Corridor; and The Crescent (3500 144 St) in Elgin Chantrell. Sources: the closing list in Section 2.
- **Q: Can I rent out a Hyde Park townhome?** For long-term rentals, yes — every BC strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. A strata can still restrict short-term rentals, so confirm the current bylaws of the correct complex (Hyde Park or Hyde Park Corner) with its strata manager. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Does any age rule affect us at Hyde Park?** No — neither complex carries a verified age bylaw, so the provincial 55+ exemptions are not engaged here. They would matter only in a verified 55+ strata, where a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident is expressly exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What should I check before buying at Hyde Park?** First, which strata the unit belongs to — Hyde Park or Hyde Park Corner — then that complex's package: the Form B information certificate, the strata bylaws and rules, the last two years of minutes and the depreciation report. The live page also warns that floor plans differ materially inside the main complex (tandem-garage, double-garage and main-floor primary bedroom layouts), so compare plans, not just prices. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hyde-park-south-surrey.html
- **Q: How many homes are in Hyde Park?** The developer's own brochure lists Hyde Park at 156 townhomes; the live page's header states the same count. Hyde Park Corner's 16 homes and 2021 completion rest on the live page only and are UNVERIFIED on a second source. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hyde-park-south-surrey.html
- **Q: How did Hyde Park's ground sell in September 2026?** Neither complex has a board row of its own. Their ground — the Grandview sub-area — recorded Detached 66 listings / 9 solds / 14% and Condos & Townhomes 146 listings / 20 solds / 14% in September 2026; medians are published at community level only (S Surrey White Rock attached median $700,000), which is context, not a Hyde Park price (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via the area research file).
- **Q: If I need support later, what is near Grandview?** The rental ladder nearby is Aspira Pacifica Retirement Living at 2525 King George Boulevard (independent living, assisted living and respite, rental only), and the hospital is Peace Arch Hospital at 15521 Russell Avenue, White Rock. There is no care residence at either Hyde Park complex. https://www.aspiralife.ca/our-locations/british-columbia/surrey/aspira-pacifica-retirement-living/ and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/White-Rock/peace-arch-hospital
