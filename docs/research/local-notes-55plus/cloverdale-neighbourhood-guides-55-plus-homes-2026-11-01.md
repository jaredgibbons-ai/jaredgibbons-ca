## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000** governs all three areas, including the SSMUH zones: up to 3 units on lots under 280 sq m, up to 4 units on lots over 280 sq m, up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (SSMUH changes of July 8, 2024). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites:** one secondary suite is permitted per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, not inside a coach house or garden suite, capped at 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Short-term rentals in Surrey:** under 90 consecutive days per patron counts as short-term; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at $358 per year is required before operating. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **OCP: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2026, No. 21900** is the current OCP — adopted June 15, 2026, replacing the 2013 OCP Bylaw No. 18020. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **ALR frame (Serpentine guide's ground):** on Agricultural Land Reserve parcels the ALC housing frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha), with any secondary suite inside the principal residence, and subdivision requires Agricultural Land Commission permission outside limited exceptions. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property taxes (city-wide):** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **First-home programs:** the First Home Savings Account and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits are UNVERIFIED in this file — no live CRA page stating the current limits was read in this pass, so no figure is published here.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which of the three guide areas actually has 55+ homes to buy?** Only the Cloverdale BC area, on this research: Carriage House (17695 58 Ave, condo building, listings state "55+, No pets permitted") and The Estates at Manor Ridge (6140 192 St, townhouses, listings state "1 occupant must be 55+"). Clayton and Serpentine have zero verified 55+ complexes — a buyer set on Clayton's newer stock or a Serpentine acreage is buying general-market product with no age rule. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/17695-58-avenue/102 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a Cloverdale 55+ complex?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in Carriage House as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, including any older "rentals not allowed" record for Manor Ridge. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** It splits the hub's only two complexes: Carriage House listings state no pets permitted; Manor Ridge listings allow 1 pet with restrictions. A pet owner reading only the hub page would not know the downtown option is closed to them. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/17695-58-avenue/102 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8
- **Q: Is Bethshan Gardens something I can buy?** No — Bethshan Gardens (17528 59 Ave) is an independent-living rental residence owned by the Cloverdale Seniors Citizen Housing Society; a rental directory records 71 units, built 2008, and its numeric age eligibility is UNCONFIRMED from sources checked. It is the rental rung in Cloverdale, not an ownership option. https://www.apartments.com/profile/8z5mld1/
- **Q: Which school will a Cloverdale home feed to?** The schools exist — twelve SD36 schools across the three areas are confirmed by name and address in the Ministry K-12 file (Section 1) — but catchments are address-based and can be split, the hub's own FAQ says so, and no locator result for a Cloverdale address is on record in this program: run the exact address in the Surrey Schools School Locator before relying on any school. https://jaredgibbons.ca/cloverdale-neighbourhood-guides.html
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** In a strata, mechanically yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: If I buy a Clayton house instead, can I add a suite for a parent?** Surrey permits one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex unit, capped at 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area, and SSMUH allows up to 3–4 units on most lots by size band (up to 6 near frequent bus stops) — the suite route, not an age bylaw, is how most Clayton multi-generational living happens. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
