# Dunbar (Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The City's local area is "Dunbar-Southlands" — one of the City's 22 distinct areas — pairing the Dunbar house streets in Vancouver's southwest with the Southlands country lots and the Musqueam First Nation land at its south edge, north of the Fraser River and east of UBC and Pacific Spirit Regional Park. "Dunbar" is the board / site name for the residential core. The City publishes no street-by-street written boundary in the sources read, so exact boundary roads are UNVERIFIED. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/dunbar-southlands.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- Dunbar Street is the north–south spine and its shopping strip is Dunbar Village; West 16th Avenue, West King Edward Avenue, West 33rd Avenue and West 41st Avenue cross it east–west, and SW Marine Drive runs along the south edge by the Fraser River. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/dunbar-southlands.aspx

Catchments are address-based; SD39 has no middle-school tier (K–7 elementary, 8–12 secondary). https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods and https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries

The one seniors-specific project in the area — a large seniors' community care facility approved for West 41st Avenue at Blenheim Street — is a rezoning approval, not an open residence (see Section 2, Checked). ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Dunbar's own plan already contemplated seniors housing — at low-rise scale.** The Dunbar Community Vision's Direction 9.1 states seniors should have a variety of housing choices that let them stay in the community as they age (congregate housing, intermediate and extended care), and Direction 9.2 states low-rise buildings (up to four storeys) committed to seniors should be permitted near local shopping and transit, implemented through site-specific rezonings. The approved West 41st Avenue project was considered under that Vision — and its height above the Vision's low-rise direction was the centre of the neighbourhood dispute the Residents' Association documents. https://dunbar-vancouver.org/rezoning-application-for-seniors-community-care-facility/
- **City of Vancouver Seniors Housing Strategy (approved July 23, 2024)** and its Seniors Housing Rezoning Policy now provide the citywide route for new seniors housing — including in house-only areas like Dunbar — plus a Resident Relocation Policy for seniors in care and assisted-living residences facing redevelopment. https://vancouver.ca/people-programs/seniors-housing-strategy.aspx
- **Arterial rental is the other new supply channel here.** The Dunbar Residents' Association notes most current Dunbar building activity is under the City's Secured Rental Policy, which encourages five- and six-storey rental apartment buildings on arterials in low-density areas close to amenities, and four-storey buildings on adjacent side streets. Secured rental is general-market rental, not age-restricted — but it is the form downsizers in Dunbar are actually getting. http://dunbar-vancouver.org/author/cathy/
- **Zoning splits in two inside this one area.** The Dunbar house streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 (secondary suite and laneway house with a house; multiplex up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units; minimum site areas 306 m² to 557 m² by form), while the Southlands country lots at the south edge sit under their own agricultural / limited-agriculture districts — the exact schedule for a given Southlands parcel is UNVERIFIED, and parcel-level Agricultural Land Reserve status must be checked lot by lot. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Tax calendar:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; owners must also file the annual Empty Homes Tax declaration — 3% of assessed taxable value for homes declared or deemed vacant for the 2025 reference year. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Dunbar?** None that any source verifies. There is no age-restricted strata building in Dunbar and no operating seniors residence. A 55+ buyer here is buying a regular house, townhome or condo with no age bylaw, or downsizing into the new general-market secured-rental stock on the arterials. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: What about the big new Amica seniors building on West 41st Avenue?** It exists as an approval, not as a residence. The Residents' Association reported Council approved the 232-unit seniors' residence at 3329–3429 West 41st Avenue / 5649–5683 Blenheim Street unanimously after an April 9 public hearing, at 85 feet with upper-floor stepbacks. No operator page or opening date was found — treat any "now open" claim as UNCONFIRMED until Amica publishes one. http://dunbar-vancouver.org/author/cathy/
- **Q: Is "Fifty-Five 55 Dunbar" at 5555 Dunbar Street a 55+ building?** No source says so. The name repeats the building's street number, 5555 Dunbar Street; listings for the 2015 building record ordinary market condos with pets allowed and no age restriction. Do not buy it expecting a 55+ bylaw — confirm any bylaw claim against the strata's Form B and filed bylaws. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3160098-207-5555-dunbar-street-vancouver-bc-v6n-1w5
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Vancouver?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — a BC age bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I add a suite or a laneway house so a parent or caregiver can live with me?** On the Dunbar house streets, that is the realistic ageing-in-place route: under the R1-1 schedule a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house. Southlands lots are different — they sit under agricultural / limited-agriculture districts, so do not assume the same permissions at the south edge. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day?** Buses only — there is no SkyTrain in Dunbar. Route 7 runs the Dunbar Street corridor between Nanaimo Station and the Dunbar loop, with east–west routes (including the 25, 33 and, at the south edge, the 49) connecting to UBC and downtown. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/regional-maps/2026/january/vancouver.pdf
- **Q: Is Dunbar in a floodplain?** Only at the south edge. The City's designated floodplain covers the Fraser River coastal floodplain at the south end of the city, and land in it carries Vancouver Building By-law floodplain setback and flood construction level requirements; whether a specific Dunbar or Southlands parcel is in it is UNVERIFIED until checked in the City dataset. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in Dunbar?** Not on any source verified today: there is no assisted-living residence or long-term care home operating inside Dunbar-Southlands in the registry and operator sources checked. The care step currently means leaving the area — the nearest verified west-side options sit in the adjoining areas covered by their own local-notes files — until the approved West 41st Avenue project opens, which is UNCONFIRMED. Sources: Section 2 above.

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Dunbar has wanted seniors housing on paper since its Community Vision — and fought over its height, not its existence.** Vision Directions 9.1 and 9.2 endorse seniors housing choices and low-rise (up to four storeys) seniors buildings near shopping and transit, delivered by site-specific rezoning. The West 41st Avenue application was linked to that Vision, but at a proposed 94 feet — later reduced to 85 feet — it departed from the Vision's low-rise scale, which is what the Residents' Association's July 5, 2023 letter to the City is about. https://dunbar-vancouver.org/rezoning-application-for-seniors-community-care-facility/
- **The cranes at Dunbar and 41st are mostly not seniors housing.** The Residents' Association reports the current building boom around 41st Avenue and Dunbar is driven mainly by the City's Secured Rental Policy — five- and six-storey general rental buildings on arterials — with the seniors' community care facility as the one seniors-specific approval among them. http://dunbar-vancouver.org/author/cathy/
- **"Dunbar" and "Southlands" are two different products filed under one City area name.** The R1-1 house streets price off house and multiplex land value; the Southlands country lots at the south edge price off their own agricultural districts and possible ALR status, beside the Musqueam land and the Fraser River floodplain. A Dunbar-Southlands address alone tells a buyer nothing about which regime a lot is under. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/dunbar-southlands.aspx and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **The area's history runs deep at the south edge:** the City records archaeological evidence of Indigenous habitation in the Southlands area as early as 400 B.C., the Musqueam Reserve formally dedicated in 1879, and Southlands joining the District of South Vancouver in 1892. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/dunbar-southlands.aspx
- **There is no hospital inside Dunbar** in the sources read, and no health-authority page confirmed a nearest hospital for this sub-area in the underlying research — UNCONFIRMED. Plan on leaving the area for both hospital care and, for now, residential care. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/dunbar-southlands.aspx
