## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why the Semiahmoo Estates directory line "rentals not allowed" cannot be read at face value. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000** provides the small-scale multi-unit housing zones RA to R6 for the detached streets around these complexes: up to 3 units on lots under 280 sq m, up to 4 units on lots over 280 sq m, and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. This affects the house streets, not the strata lots inside the 55+ complexes. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Plans: the Semiahmoo Town Centre Plan borders this sub-area.** The City's plan page records the plan was approved and later updated to align with the King George Boulevard Bus Rapid Transit and the Surrey 2050 OCP designations; the plan area, 16 Avenue to 24 Avenue from Martin Drive to Earl Marriott Secondary School, borders Sunnyside Park — land-use pressure on the 152 Street corridor's older low-rise stock comes from this plan, so a 55+ building's redevelopment context should be read there, not assumed. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/semiahmoo-town-centre-plan
- **Property taxes (city-wide, not area-specific):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **For strata purchases,** the Province's buyer paperwork path is Form B and Form F, strata bylaws and rules, fees and special levies, with filings confirmed at the Land Title Office — the Form B is where the age bylaw for any complex in Section 2 is actually proved. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Sunnyside Park?** Four complexes verified in this research, all on or just off 152 Street: Semiahmoo Estates (2239 152 St, condo), the building at 13965 16 Ave (condo), Bishop's Green (2533 152 St, gated townhouses) and St. Martin's Lane (15020 27A Ave, gated townhouses). No 55+ rental building or subsidized complex was verified inside the sub-area. https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/listing/r3061986-2239-152-street-211-surrey-sunnyside-park-surrey-homes-for-sale-365000/brochure and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/2533-152-street/98
- **Q: Is Peninsula Village part of Sunnyside Park?** Its homes are at 2500 152 Street and it is a genuine 55+ gated complex, but MLS files Peninsula Village under King George Corridor, not Sunnyside Park — expect to find its listings on the King George Corridor / Surrey-wide 55+ notes, not this page, and do not let a search that merges the two corridors double-count it. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3098715-2500-152-street-67-surrey-bc-v4p-1m8/brochure
- **Q: Do both of us have to be 55?** Provincially, a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption) is exempt, as are live-in caregivers and legacy residents. Peninsula Village listings (in the neighbouring sub-area) state the common local form expressly — "One person 55+" — but each Sunnyside complex's own bylaw wording must be confirmed in its Form B; no listing checked for the four in-sub-area complexes stated the one-occupant detail in terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3065456-2500-152-street-66-surrey-bc-v4p-1m8/brochure
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ complex as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are these single-level homes, or will I have stairs?** Mixed by complex. The two condo buildings are apartment form (Semiahmoo Estates listings describe 1-bed units with in-suite laundry; the 13965 16 Ave listing describes a ground-level single-storey layout). Bishop's Green and St. Martin's Lane are rancher townhouses **with walkout basements** — main-floor living, but not stair-free homes; a buyer who needs true single-level living should verify the floor plan unit by unit. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/2533-152-street/98 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/15020-27a-avenue/25
- **Q: Is Amica White Rock in White Rock?** No — despite the name, its address is 15333 16th Avenue, Surrey, on the Surrey side of 16 Avenue in this sub-area, three blocks from Peace Arch Hospital in the operator's description. It is a rental care residence, not a strata you can buy. https://www.amica.ca/white-rock
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Building by building, and the sources conflict in tone: a building directory records Semiahmoo Estates as pets not allowed, while Peninsula Village listings next door (King George Corridor) state pets ok with a 2-pet limit in one listing. Read the individual complex's bylaws — do not carry one corridor building's pet rule into another. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/semiahmoo-estates-2239-152-street
- **Q: Where is the closest hospital?** Peace Arch Hospital, 15521 Russell Avenue, White Rock — in the next municipality, a short drive south on 152 Street from every complex in Section 2. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/white-rock/peace-arch-hospital
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Sunnyside Park's 55+ stock and South Surrey's best-known 55+ complex are in different MLS sub-areas.** Peninsula Village (2500 152 St, 95 townhomes) is filed under King George Corridor while Bishop's Green (2533 152 St) — a few blocks north on the same street — is filed under Sunnyside Park. A buyer searching only one sub-area's 55+ page misses half the 152 Street corridor. https://storage.ubertor.com/cl2464/listing/Listing_780_extra/128267.pdf and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/2533-152-street/98
- **The sub-area's boundary is an MLS filing, not a City line.** The City describes Sunnyside as lying between 16 Avenue and 24 Avenue, yet MLS files Bishop's Green (2533 152 St) and St. Martin's Lane (15020 27A Ave) — both south/north of that band's edges as addressed — under Sunnyside Park Surrey. Price comparisons "in Sunnyside" inherit that filing choice. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bakerview-park
- **"No age restrictions" is stated in terms for the sub-area's best-known townhouse complex.** Highgrove (15055 20 Ave) is marketed as one of the most desirable complexes in South Surrey, and its own listing states it has no age restrictions — a Sunnyside 55+ search that includes Highgrove is wrong, however senior its buyer pool looks. https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/listing/r3097496-15055-20th-avenue-8-surrey-sunnyside-park-surrey-homes-for-sale-1199000/brochure
- **A building actually named "Sunnyside" is not a 55+ building.** The Sunnyside building at 15290 18 Ave carries no age restriction in the directory record checked and is filed in King George Corridor; the name is a building name, not an age bylaw. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/sunnyside-15290-18-ave-stratford-by-the-park
- **The care option inside the sub-area carries the neighbouring city's name.** Amica White Rock is in Surrey (15333 16 Ave), opened as a Surrey residence in 2019 by its operator's account, and contains a medical clinic (Abby Lane Health Centre) in the building — the "stay in Sunnyside if care needs grow" option exists, but it is a rental residence, and its name will send a careless search to White Rock listings instead. https://www.amica.ca/white-rock
- **The Morgan Crossing / Grandview growth story is not this sub-area's 55+ story.** No new age-restricted strata project inside Sunnyside Park appears in any source checked — the verified 55+ supply is the early-1990s corridor stock (Semiahmoo Estates, Bishop's Green, St. Martin's Lane) plus the 1980s 16 Avenue building, while the Semiahmoo Town Centre Plan's updated densities apply to the town-centre lands bordering the sub-area, not to a new seniors project inside it. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/semiahmoo-town-centre-plan
