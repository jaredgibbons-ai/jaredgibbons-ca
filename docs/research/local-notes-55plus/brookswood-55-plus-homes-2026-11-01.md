## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **A park's 55+ rule is a park rule, not a strata bylaw.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003) expressly recognises a park where every site is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 (per Human Rights Code s. 10(2)(b)(i)) and allows a park landlord to refuse an assignment where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement. The Strata Property Act exemptions do not answer a park occupancy question — get the park's rules in writing. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **The governing law for a rented pad is provincial.** Manufactured home site tenancies are governed by BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA) wherever the park sits; the tenant owns the manufactured home and rents the site under a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement, and landlords and tenants must have a written tenancy agreement for the site. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- **The 2026 pad-rent increase limit: 2.3% plus a proportional amount.** For manufactured home site tenancies the 2026 limit is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees; the 2027 limit is 2.2% plus the proportional amount. The proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park. Increases require 3 full months' written notice and can occur only once every 12 months; the landlord may not round up and may not carry forward an unused portion of a prior year's limit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Buying a park home does not reset the pad rent (MHPTA s. 28 assignment).** Assignment permanently transfers the site tenancy to the buyer; a tenant may assign only with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the regulations), an order of the director, or where the tenancy agreement authorizes it; the landlord cannot charge a fee for consent, and on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, as does the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Park closure / conversion (MHPTA s. 42):** where a landlord intends to convert all or a significant part of a park to another use, the landlord must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date, with defined additional-compensation routes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Registration and the CSA label gate every park purchase.** In BC the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered with the Manufactured Home Registry, through a lawyer or notary filing the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership; a home may not be sold without its original legible CSA decal (Z-240 for mobile homes, A-277 for modular), and a lost label requires Technical Safety BC re-certification. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Tax on a park home excludes the land.** The Province states a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, but the assessed value will not include the value of the land if the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **For comparison — the strata rules, which do NOT govern Brookswood's parks:** under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, only a 55+ age bylaw is valid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44); the bylaw restricts residency, not ownership; Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and since May 1, 2023 also a younger spouse/partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19; and strata rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Township zoning: Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500** governs Brookswood. Its Section 110.1 zone ladder, as read out in Township council reports, includes SR-1 (minimum lot area 3,716 m², frontage 40 m), SR-2 (8,094 m², 45 m) and SR-3 (1,765 m², 27.45 m); the Brookswood-Fernridge plan designates Cedar Creek's site as Manufactured Home Park. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf and https://www.tol.ca/en/news/brookswoodfernridge-community-plan-adopted.aspx
- **The Township's bylaw has its own "seniors' housing" definition:** a Township council report varying Zoning Bylaw 2500 for a seniors' housing project records the bylaw definition's maximum seniors' housing dwelling-unit size as 70 m² (753 ft²) — a zoning term, separate from any park's or strata's 55+ age rule. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=866EA2E9-916E-485E-9075-F59C90961D19&folderId=47498C260508154601958591
- **Property taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes and utility fees were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to current-year taxes unpaid at midnight July 2, 2026, with a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026, and utility fees are now billed separately. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Brookswood?** Manufactured homes in three verified 55+ parks: Cedar Creek Estates (3031 200 St), Fernridge Park (20071 24 Ave) and Pineridge (2270 196 St). In each case you buy the home and rent the pad under a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement — there is no verified 55+ strata complex in Brookswood. https://www.estateblock.com/langley/5-3031-200-st-1968212 and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3109616-33-20071-24-avenue-langley-british-columbia-v2z2a1-99000/brochure and https://realestatecoalharbour.com/fraser-valley-real-estate/listing.r3165233-23-2270-196-street-langley-v2z-1n6.109859015/
- **Q: How much is pad rent at these parks?** UNCONFIRMED — no official source publishes a Langley pad-rent figure, average or range, and no park-operator source stating a current pad rent for any of the three parks was obtained. The figure that matters is the specific park's current rent in its written site tenancy agreement, confirmed with the park before an offer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up each year?** Up to the Residential Tenancy Branch limit for manufactured home site tenancies: 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026, and 2.2% plus the proportional amount for 2027. The proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of sites in the park. Increases require 3 full months' notice and can occur only once every 12 months. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does the park raise my pad rent when I buy the home?** No. On assignment under s. 28 of the MHPTA, the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same and the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase does not change; the landlord's prior written consent is required (or deemed given) and the landlord cannot charge a fee for consent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a Brookswood park home?** Not automatically. A park's 55+ rule is set in tenancy regulation and the park's own rules, and a landlord may refuse an assignment where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement; the strata exemptions for younger spouses do not govern a pad tenancy. Ask the specific park for its occupancy rule in writing before writing. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Park by park, per the listing sources: Cedar Creek Estates records pets allowed with restrictions; Fernridge Park listings state no pets; Pineridge's pet rule is UNCONFIRMED in the sources checked. Confirm the current rule with the park before writing. https://www.estateblock.com/langley/5-3031-200-st-1968212 and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3109616-33-20071-24-avenue-langley-british-columbia-v2z2a1-99000/brochure
- **Q: Can I rent out a park home I buy?** Cedar Creek Estates and Fernridge Park listings both record rentals not allowed, and Fernridge requires park manager approval of a buyer. Park rental and approval rules are park rules under the tenancy frame, not strata bylaws — confirm the current rule with the park. https://www.estateblock.com/langley/5-3031-200-st-1968212 and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3109616-33-20071-24-avenue-langley-british-columbia-v2z2a1-99000/brochure
- **Q: What happens if the park is converted to another use?** Under MHPTA s. 42 the landlord must first have all necessary permits and approvals, then give 12 months' notice and pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date, with defined additional-compensation routes where the home cannot be moved or no site is available within a reasonable distance. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Do I pay property tax on a park home?** Yes, on the home: the Province states a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, but the assessed value does not include the value of the land where the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land. Township taxes were due July 2, 2026, with 5% penalties at midnight July 2 and again at midnight September 3, 2026. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Langley Memorial Hospital, 22051 Fraser Highway — a Fraser Health community hospital with 24/7 emergency. Brookswood has no hospital of its own. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Langley/langley-memorial-hospital

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Every verified 55+ home in Brookswood is a manufactured home on a rented pad.** Brookswood holds all three of Langley's verified 55+ pad-rental parks — Cedar Creek Estates, Fernridge Park and Pineridge — and no 55+ strata at all. A buyer who wants a Brookswood address and a 55+ rule is shopping park tenure, with MHPTA rules, not strata rules. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **The three parks sit on three different corridors.** Cedar Creek Estates is on 200 Street (the area's main north–south road, and the address the Brookswood-Fernridge plan itself designates), Fernridge Park is on 24 Avenue in the Fernridge neighbourhood, and Pineridge is on 196 Street at the area's west edge — three different pockets of Brookswood, not one cluster. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **A park's age rule can lawfully stop a sale.** Under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Regulation, a landlord in a 55+ park may refuse an assignment where the proposed purchaser does not meet the age requirement — a refusal power with no equivalent in a strata sale, where the bylaw restricts residency but not who may own. Build the park's written consent into the timeline. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **The proportional amount makes two parks' increases differ in the same year.** It is the park's own levy plus regulated-utility change divided by that park's own site count — so the same 2.3% headline limit for 2026 produces a different dollar increase at Cedar Creek Estates than at Fernridge Park or Pineridge. Ask the specific park for its last proportional-amount calculation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **The CSA label is a hard gate, not a detail.** A manufactured home without its original legible CSA decal (Z-240 mobile, A-277 modular) cannot be sold until Technical Safety BC re-certification — label status is a price and timeline fact on every Brookswood park purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Brookswood's plan protects trees and watercourses while it grows.** The Brookswood-Fernridge Community Plan allows previously undeveloped areas to grow while protecting trees and watercourses, with Booth, Fernridge and Rinn Neighbourhood Plans under it — the plan frame, not an age rule, is what shapes what gets built around the parks. https://www.tol.ca/en/news/brookswoodfernridge-community-plan-adopted.aspx
