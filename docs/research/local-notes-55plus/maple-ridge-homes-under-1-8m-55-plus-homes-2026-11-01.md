## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://choa.bc.ca/resources/strata-property-act/legislative-updates/
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **SOURCE CONFLICT — FLAG, do not smooth over:** building sources for Rosewood Court (Fraserview Village) still record its bylaws as including "rentals not allowed," and Carriage Lane's building source records the same. Under the provincial change above, a residential rental-restriction bylaw has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 regardless of what an older bylaw set or a building-data site still displays. Have the strata's current bylaws and a Form B reviewed rather than rely on either the building-data summary or a listing. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/rosewood-court-22611-116-ave
- **Manufactured home parks run on different law.** Pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and its Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003), not the Strata Property Act. The Regulation expressly contemplates parks where every site is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 (or two or more tenants, at least one of whom has reached 55), per the Human Rights Code s. 10(2)(b)(i) permitted age requirements — and a park landlord may refuse an assignment or sublease where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement in such a park. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Co-op and life-lease age rules are contract/membership rules, not strata bylaws.** St. George's Village's 55-year membership qualification (with a dependent aged 19+ allowed), Haney Pioneer Village's 60+ threshold and the Legion life leases' terms are set by those organizations' own documents; the provincial strata exemptions (spouse, caregiver, legacy) do not automatically apply to them — read the membership application, occupancy agreement or life lease itself. https://www.stgeorgesvillage.ca/_files/ugd/070889_39221c746a9b483980e20a3596cdb580.pdf and https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019; OCP Bylaw No. 7060-2014.** Houses sit under RS-1 (s. 605 — minimum lot area 668.0 m², width 18.0 m, depth 27.0 m, maximum coverage 40%); the bylaw also includes a dedicated manufactured-home park zone, s. 626, Zone RMH (Manufactured Home Park Residential); Town Centre parcels carry Town Centre zones under the same bylaw, and Transit-Oriented Area Designation Bylaw No. 8001-2024 covers the Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station TOAs. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading and https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-01/Zoning%20bylaw%20report.pdf and https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- **Suites and added units:** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-family and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws — the route for a 55+ homeowner in this band to add housing for a caregiver or family member on a detached lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Floodplain (south edge only):** along River Road / Port Haney the flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), a covenant on title identifies construction in the floodplain, no habitable floor space may be located below the flood construction level on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. Floodplain status is per-lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Property transfer tax and the first-time buyers' program, on the Province's published figures:** general PTT is 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion greater than $200,000 and up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% of the portion greater than $2,000,000 — purchases in this band are taxed in the 1% and 2% tiers only. Under the first time home buyers' program, the full exemption requires a fair market value of $835,000 or less (effective April 1, 2024), exempting the first $500,000 of price, with a partial exemption only above $835,000 and below $860,000, subject to the program's other conditions — so the program bears only on the lowest-priced end of this band. FHSA and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits: UNVERIFIED in this file — not read off a live CRA page in this pass. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Property taxes:** Maple Ridge property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to any outstanding balance on July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% on September 2, 2026. The Province's regular property tax deferment program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities). https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

Both are share-purchase co-ops, not strata: different age bars, different legal form, different resale rules. https://www.stgeorgesvillage.ca/_files/ugd/070889_39221c746a9b483980e20a3596cdb580.pdf and https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Q: What is a life lease at Legion Gardens?** A purchased leasehold interest for life under the Legion's own terms — you buy the right to live in the suite (Legion Gardens: 56 suites from 1994, per the Legion case study), not a strata lot or freehold title. Resale and buy-back terms are in the Legion's documents and are UNCONFIRMED from public sources — read them before valuing a unit. https://dev.ibabchyperarticles.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Seniors-Housing-Development-2010.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption), with further defined exemptions for children and live-in caregivers. Note this is the STRATA rule; St. George's Village's form allows a dependent aged 19+ to live with a member (whether a younger spouse qualifies as that "dependent" is not spelled out in the form — UNCONFIRMED, ask the co-op), and Haney and the Legion buildings set their own occupancy terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.stgeorgesvillage.ca/_files/ugd/070889_39221c746a9b483980e20a3596cdb580.pdf
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC strata — every rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, whatever an older building summary still shows (Rosewood Court's included). The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. In the parks, renting and assignment run through the park rules and the MHPTA instead; in the co-ops and life leases, the organization's own documents govern — UNCONFIRMED from public sources for those forms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Q: What do the parks actually cost to carry?** Listing-stated figures: at Alouette, pad rental fee $713.31, city taxes $457.37, metered water billed quarterly — plus the park's stated rules (no dogs, indoor cat only, 2 residents per unit, "no financing permitted in this community"); at Val Maria, pad rent $850/month and 2 small pets allowed. All listing-stated: confirm against the tenancy agreement and park rules for the specific pad. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3067037-12868-229th-street-18-maple-ridge-bc-v2x-6r1/brochure and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3089789-18-21163-lougheed-highway-maple-ridge-british-columbia-v2x2r4-259000/brochure
- **Q: Can I add a suite to a Maple Ridge house in this band for a caregiver or my parents?** One secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building (maximum 40% of gross floor area, not on a floodplain property — s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation on floodplain properties), and eligible lots may also add up to three, four or six SSMUH units under the City's updated bylaws. Legality is a permit-level check on the specific lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street — a Fraser Health community hospital with 24/7 emergency care serving Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows. The Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is in Haney Place Mall, 11900 Haney Place, open 7 days a week, 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/F/C/urgent-primary-care-centre---ridge-meadows
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes, for any age-restricted product: the next buyer must also qualify — in a strata, be 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or a buyer who purchases without living in the unit; in a park, meet the park's age requirement on assignment; at St. George's, meet the co-op's 55-year membership qualification, with its stated $3,500 transfer fee on the share transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.stgeorgesvillage.ca/_files/ugd/070889_39221c746a9b483980e20a3596cdb580.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Four strata buildings, one amenity building.** Fraserview Village's 55+ buildings share a single amenity building (pool, hot tub, clubhouse, workshop, guest suite) at the village address 22610 116 Ave in the Seniors Resource Directory — a buyer comparing fees across the four buildings is partly paying for the same shared facility four different ways. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/MRN/180831/MRNN180831_Z38.pdf
- **The city's 55+ supply is almost entirely attached, in a band page framed as detached.** The live page's copy frames the under-$1.8M feed as detached houses, but every verified 55+ ownership complex in the city is a strata apartment, a townhouse co-op or a manufactured home on a rented pad — a 55+ buyer who filters this band to detached only will filter out the entire verified age-restricted supply. https://jaredgibbons.ca/maple-ridge-homes-under-1-8m.html
- **The Legion runs a small housing system, not one building.** Branch 88 owns and operates two rental facilities (Earl Haig and Legion House) and two life-lease facilities (Legion Gardens and Legion Manor) — and Legion Manor's street address does not appear in any source checked, so it cannot be mapped from public sources alone. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/MRN/190830/MRNN190830_Z31.pdf
- **A directory ghost sits on the retirement row.** "Panorama by ECRA," 12148 224 St, appears in the 2018 Seniors Resource Directory and nowhere current — a buyer handed an old directory will ask for a building that no 2026 source confirms exists. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/MRN/180831/MRNN180831_Z38.pdf
