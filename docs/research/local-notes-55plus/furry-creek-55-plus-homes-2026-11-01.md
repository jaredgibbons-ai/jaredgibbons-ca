# Furry Creek — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Furry Creek is an unincorporated, master-planned community in Electoral Area D of the Squamish-Lillooet Regional District (SLRD), on the east shore of Howe Sound along Highway 99 (the Sea-to-Sky Highway), between Lions Bay and Squamish; the SLRD's Electoral Area D Official Community Plan lists it as one of eight settlement areas in Area D. https://slrd.civicweb.net/document/149848/
- It forms part of the Howe Sound East sub-area with Britannia Beach (to the south) and the future community of Porteau Cove (to the north), under Schedule C of the Electoral Area D OCP. https://slrd.civicweb.net/document/149848/
- The community's anchor is Furry Creek Golf & Country Club, 150 Country Club Road — an 18-hole, par-72 course of about 6,060 yards opened in 1993, with a roughly 30,000 sq ft clubhouse and the Sea to Sky Restaurant overlooking the 18th green (club's own site). http://www.furrycreekgolf.com/
- The two named residential complexes are at the waterfront/south side: Oliver's Landing (Beach Drive / Whitecap Court / Point Terrace street names in listings) and Ocean's Crest at the south entrance. https://getinvolved.slrd.bc.ca/sites/default/files/pdfs/planning/Zoning/Waterworks%20Master%20Plan%20REV%204%20-%20October%202021.pdf and https://www.zolo.ca/furry-creek-real-estate/25-whitecap-court
- Country Club Road serves the golf course and hillside homes above Howe Sound; Highway 99 is the only road in or out — no public transit service to Furry Creek was confirmed on an official source, and daily life is car-based. https://www.slrd.bc.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw-electoral-area-d2pdf
- Schools: School District 48 (Sea to Sky) serves the corridor "from Furry Creek to D'Arcy"; there is no school in Furry Creek itself — SD48's Policy 501 catchment table puts Furry Creek in the Squamish Elementary (K–6) catchment in Squamish, with Don Ross Middle School (7–9) and Howe Sound Secondary (10–12) as the Squamish-area middle/secondary schools. https://www.sd48seatosky.org/ and https://22.files.edl.io/0f17/07/30/26/170123-5484041d-9258-492f-a2ad-1e6938a06351.pdf
- Where 55+ housing concentrates: **nowhere — no source found places any 55+ complex, seniors building, retirement residence or care facility in Furry Creek.** The nearest registered seniors residence found is in Squamish (Section 2). https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B1%5D=city%3ASquamish&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=desc

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Local planning: SLRD Electoral Area D Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 1135-2013** (adopted October 28, 2013), with the Howe Sound East Sub-area Plan as Schedule C covering Furry Creek. https://slrd.civicweb.net/document/149848/
- **Zoning: SLRD Electoral Area D Zoning Bylaw No. 1350-2016.** Furry Creek zones on the bylaw schedules include CD3 (Furry Creek Comprehensive Development 3), FCR residential zones, FCC1 (Furry Creek – Commercial 1) and FCC2 (Furry Creek Golf Course Commercial 2). https://www.slrd.bc.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw-electoral-area-d2pdf
- **No SSMUH density windfall here:** the Province has determined the SLRD's master-planned communities of Britannia Beach, Furry Creek and Porteau Cove are NOT subject to the additional 3–6 unit small-scale multi-unit housing densities, because they are not within an urban containment boundary for SSMUH purposes; SLRD Amendment Bylaw No. 1887-2025 removed the extra densities briefly added to the Furry Creek FCR3 and CD3 zones. Suite rights are whatever the base zoning and the building scheme allow. https://www.slrd.bc.ca/media/file/250129-ssmuh-area-d-amendment-bb-extension-update-rfd
- **Building schemes sit on top of zoning:** the SLRD records that the Furry Creek neighbourhood has existing statutory building schemes and covenants, which constrain what owners can build or alter — read the scheme before planning changes, additions or a suite. https://www.slrd.bc.ca/media/file/250129-ssmuh-area-d-amendment-bb-extension-update-rfd
- **Hazard development permits:** expanded Development Permit Areas for Howe Sound East were adopted through OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 1717-2021 (adopted May 25, 2022), covering landslide hazard areas, excavated rock and foundation stability, seismic liquefaction and fan stability, coastal hazards and creek hazards; creek-fan guidelines address dike design and a flood construction level achieved by landfill. https://getinvolved.slrd.bc.ca/sites/default/files/pdfs/planning/staff-reports/Area%20D%20DPA%20Housekeeping%204th%20Reading%20Report-FULL.pdf
- **Taxes and levies:** as an SLRD electoral-area community, properties pay provincial rural property tax plus SLRD service levies; the SLRD provides Furry Creek-specific water, wastewater treatment, street lighting, solid waste disposal, open spaces maintenance, fire protection and dyking/drainage services under local service bylaws (e.g., Furry Creek Dyking Local Service Establishment Bylaw No. 481; Furry Creek Sewer Local Service Bylaw No. 1619). Per-lot levy amounts: UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://slrd.civicweb.net/document/149848/
- **Rural tax penalty dates (Province):** rural property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty on amounts unpaid after July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid after October 31 — the FVRD/rural regime wording is stated here for the provincial rural system generally; confirm the current-year notice for a specific Furry Creek lot with the Surveyor of Taxes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/important-dates

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ housing in Furry Creek I can buy?** No. No source found places any age-restricted strata, seniors rental, retirement residence or subsidized seniors building in Furry Creek; its two named strata complexes — Oliver's Landing and Ocean's Crest — carry no age restriction in any source checked. https://getinvolved.slrd.bc.ca/sites/default/files/pdfs/planning/Zoning/Waterworks%20Master%20Plan%20REV%204%20-%20October%202021.pdf
- **Q: A listing called Oliver's Landing a great retirement spot. Is it 55+?** No — that is a seller's lifestyle remark, not a bylaw. Oliver's Landing is a 56-unit gated townhouse strata (built 2000–2001 in the phases listings show) whose current listings record pets with restrictions and no age restriction of any kind. https://www.zolo.ca/furry-creek-real-estate/25-whitecap-court
- **Q: If a Furry Creek strata adopted a 55+ bylaw later, what would the rules be?** Only a 55+ bylaw is valid anywhere in BC since Nov 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2); it would bind who may reside, not who may own, and Regulation 7.01 exemptions would apply to legacy residents, live-in caregivers, a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Where is the nearest seniors housing with care?** In Squamish: Shannon Falls Retirement Residence, 38225 Third Avenue, is registered with BC's Assisted Living Registry (class SN, seniors), and Westwinds at 38275 Third Ave provides 232 affordable rentals for low-income seniors (opened 2022). Both are in Squamish, not Furry Creek. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B1%5D=city%3ASquamish&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=desc and https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/hundreds-of-new-affordable-homes-for-seniors-open-in-squamish-887232529.html
- **Q: Can I add a suite or extra units for family or a caregiver?** Not under the provincial SSMUH rules — the Province has confirmed Furry Creek is exempt from the additional 3–6 unit densities, Bylaw No. 1887-2025 removed the added units from the FCR3 and CD3 zones, and statutory building schemes and covenants also apply. https://www.slrd.bc.ca/media/file/250129-ssmuh-area-d-amendment-bb-extension-update-rfd
- **Q: Can I rent out a Furry Creek strata unit long-term?** Strata rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022, so a strata cannot ban long-term rentals — but it can still restrict short-term rentals, and any tenant in a future 55+ strata would still have to meet the age bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What extra carrying costs are specific to Furry Creek?** A bundle of SLRD local services — water, sewer, dyking/drainage, fire protection, street lighting, solid waste and open spaces — levied on top of provincial rural property tax; get the current levy list from the SLRD before comparing carrying costs with Squamish. https://www.slrd.bc.ca/inside-slrd/bylaws?community=All&type=All&page=4
- **Q: What hazard checks matter for an older buyer here?** The expanded Howe Sound East Development Permit Areas (Bylaw No. 1717-2021) for landslide, slope/foundation stability, liquefaction, coastal and creek hazards — creek-fan lots carry dike and flood construction level requirements, which affect both building plans and insurance conversations. https://getinvolved.slrd.bc.ca/sites/default/files/pdfs/planning/staff-reports/Area%20D%20DPA%20Housekeeping%204th%20Reading%20Report-FULL.pdf
- **Q: How do I get around without driving?** No public transit service to Furry Creek was confirmed on an official source; BC Transit's Squamish system does not list a Furry Creek route, the catchment schools are in Squamish, and Highway 99 driving is the practical access — a central fact for a buyer planning to age in place. https://www.sd48seatosky.org/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Furry Creek was briefly given SSMUH densities, then had them taken back (2025).** Amendment Bylaw No. 1887-2025 removed the extra 3–6 unit densities that had briefly been added to the FCR3 and CD3 zones after the Province determined the community is not within an urban containment boundary — anyone quoting "you can build four units now" from a 2024 source is out of date. https://www.slrd.bc.ca/media/file/250129-ssmuh-area-d-amendment-bb-extension-update-rfd
- **Oliver's Landing is still building out, three decades in.** Development-permit documents for "Oliver's Landing at Furry Creek, Buildings 16, 17, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23" were issued for form-and-character review in Dec 2024–Mar 2025 under the BC Building Code 2024 — the final phase of the waterfront strata was still in the permit stream in 2025, on strata plan LMS4257 lands. https://getinvolved.slrd.bc.ca/media/file/olivers-landing-formcharacter-dp-schedule-2-site-plans
- **The whole community runs on SLRD local services, not a city.** Water, sewer, dyking, fire, street lighting and open-space maintenance at Furry Creek are each their own SLRD local service with its own bylaw (e.g., Dyking Bylaw No. 481, Sewer Bylaw No. 1619) — which is why two otherwise-similar Sea-to-Sky homes can carry different levy stacks. https://slrd.civicweb.net/document/149848/
- **A development application for Furry Creek has been in the SLRD process since 2021** — it was one of two Howe Sound East applications (with Britannia South/Tiger Bay) that drove the expanded hazard Development Permit Areas adopted in 2022, and the CD3 zone contemplates further build-out; applicant, unit counts and current status on an official source are UNCONFIRMED. https://getinvolved.slrd.bc.ca/sites/default/files/pdfs/planning/staff-reports/Area%20D%20DPA%20Housekeeping%204th%20Reading%20Report-FULL.pdf
- **Squamish quietly became the corridor's seniors-housing centre in 2022:** Westwinds (232 affordable low-income seniors rentals, 38275 Third Ave) opened in April 2022 beside the registered Shannon Falls Retirement Residence on the same street — a Furry Creek senior who needs to move to supported or subsidized housing is, in practice, moving to Third Avenue in Squamish. https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/hundreds-of-new-affordable-homes-for-seniors-open-in-squamish-887232529.html and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/shannon-falls-retirement-residence/plan-your-visit
