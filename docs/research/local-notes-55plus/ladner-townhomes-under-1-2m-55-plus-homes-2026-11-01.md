## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1/123.2; any bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). The same Act and age-bylaw rules apply to a bare land strata like South Pointe as to a condo strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** Under-55s can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; younger spouse/partner; children under 19 in the resident's care; adult children cared for before 19 (expanded May 1, 2023). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rental bylaws are still allowed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Bare land strata specifics (South Pointe, Laburnum Park Place):** the owner typically insures and maintains the house itself while the strata maintains common property (gates, internal roads, landscaping). Listing fee figures for South Pointe from different years conflict ($113/mo and $173/mo appear in sources from different listing dates) — treat both as dated; the current fee and what it covers come from the Form B and strata financials. https://plsrealestate.com/british_columbia/house/delta/R2435695-5359_jibset_bay and https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/5379-regatta-way
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600; OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400 (2024).** South Pointe's listing data records zoning CD98A — a site-specific comprehensive development zone, so additions are governed by the CD zone and the strata bylaws together, not the standard RS zones. "Seniors congregate housing" is a permitted principal use in the RA6 zone added in 2024. https://www.zolo.ca/ladner-real-estate/5371-regatta-way and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **Secondary suites and SSMUH:** the RS1 zone lists a secondary suite as an accessory use, and Delta's SSMUH amendments allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — but inside a bare land strata, the strata bylaws sit on top of the zoning, so a suite needs both to allow it. Delta legalized suites in 2010; Bylaw No. 8242 (2023) set a suite's floor area at less than 50% of the dwelling's gross floor area (excluding an attached garage). https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/218413/
- **Property transfer tax (general, Province):** 1% to $200,000; 2% on the portion over $200,000 up to $2,000,000; 3% above $2,000,000 — every purchase in this band sits in the 1% and 2% tiers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First time home buyers' program:** full exemption requires a fair market value of $835,000 or less, with a partial exemption below $860,000 — only the lower slice of this band can use it, and all other conditions must also be met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **FHSA and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits: UNVERIFIED** in this file — no live CRA page stating the limits was read, so no FHSA or HBP figure is stated.
- **Property taxes and deferment:** Delta's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026 (5% penalty after, a further 5% after September 2, 2026); BC's regular deferment program opens at 55 or older on a principal residence, and bare land strata lots are titled lots. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which Ladner townhome complexes are actually 55+?** Two verified bare land strata rancher communities: South Pointe (Neilsen Grove, plan NWS2528, built 1989, "Age restriction is 55+" per listings) and Laburnum Park Place (Delta Manor, detached ranchers described as a 55+ neighbourhood with MLS age-restriction data). No conventional townhouse complex in Ladner carries a verified 55+ bylaw. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3075824-5371-regatta-way-delta-bc-v4k-4r9 and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3048423-5246-laburnum-park-place-delta-bc-v4k-4s6/brochure
- **Q: Are South Pointe or Laburnum Park Place homes under $1.2M?** UNCONFIRMED. No source states either complex's price band, and this file makes no price claim for any complex — check the live feed at the time of search. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-townhomes-under-1-2m.html
- **Q: How is a bare land strata rancher different from a townhouse?** You own a lot and a detached house on it — your own front door, yard and garden — and pay a comparatively small bare land fee for gates, roads and common areas; the roof, exterior and house insurance are generally the owner's responsibility. In a conventional townhouse strata the building envelope is the strata's. Confirm the exact split in the bylaws and Form B. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/5379-regatta-way
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live at South Pointe?** Yes — a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident is exempt; caregivers, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a rancher there for a parent?** Ownership is not age-restricted; the bylaw governs residence. A 55+ parent can be the resident of a unit owned by a younger buyer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a South Pointe home?** Long-term rental bans are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022; the tenant must still meet the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Q: Is Hampton Cove 55+? It has a clubhouse.** No. No source states an age restriction for any Hampton Cove building, and the phase names and Hampton Club in the live page's copy are UNVERIFIED on any developer or strata source. A clubhouse is an amenity, not an age bylaw. https://www.zolo.ca/ladner-real-estate/5535-admiral-way/308
- **Q: Are pets and gardening allowed at South Pointe?** A listing records "pets allowed" and describes gardening as a reason buyers choose the complex; pet numbers and sizes are bylaw-specific — confirm in the registered bylaws. https://eugenek.ca/mylistings.html/SearchResults.form?_pg=8
- **Q: What flood questions should I ask?** Whether the lot is within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch (Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area) — South Pointe backs onto marsh and waterways — and how the strata's documents treat Ladner's dike-protected setting. Flood requirements are lot-specific; check DeltaMap and title. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer use the PTT exemption in this band?** Only at the lower end: the full exemption stops at a fair market value of $835,000 and the partial exemption at $860,000, with all other conditions met — most of this band sits above those ceilings. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Both verified 55+ rancher communities are 1980s stock.** Listing data dates South Pointe's homes to 1989, and the Laburnum Park Place listing likewise records built 1989 — a purchase in either is a house purchase (roof, drainage, envelope) plus a strata purchase, unlike Delta Green (2021) at the condo end. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3075824-5371-regatta-way-delta-bc-v4k-4r9 and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3048423-5246-laburnum-park-place-delta-bc-v4k-4s6/brochure
- **Laburnum Park Place's 55+ character is documented in the City's own record, not just listings.** Residents' 2009 letters to Council, filed on the City's civic record, describe the complex as housing "for 55 and over" and count eighteen homes for people over 55 on the street — a useful cross-check when a listing's age field is the only other source. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/46919/
- **Two eras of South Pointe listings give two different fees — neither is current.** $113/mo and $173/mo appear in sources from different years; anyone quoting a South Pointe fee without a date is quoting a stale number. The Form B is the only current source. https://plsrealestate.com/british_columbia/house/delta/R2435695-5359_jibset_bay
- **Ladner's new townhouse supply adds nothing to its 55+ supply.** Marina Garden Estates / Hampton Cove, Skye at Holly Park and Wildwood Ladner are all general-market family product in every source — the sub-areas' growth does not create new age-restricted stock. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/202969/ and https://skyehomes.ca/
- **No Ladner statistics exist to anchor this band.** SnapStats covers North Delta only, with no South Delta section and no price-band or townhome-only cut — any "typical Ladner townhome price / fee / size" figure for this band is UNVERIFIED. Source: the area research file, ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/ladner-townhomes-under-1-2m.md, Section 2.
