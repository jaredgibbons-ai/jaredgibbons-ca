## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. This is the rule The Greens' age bylaw sits under — and the reason Douglas Point's "adult orientated" marketing is not a restriction at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Plans: the Douglas NCP's designations are history.** The Douglas Neighbourhood Concept Plan process concluded in 1999 and its land use designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan — **Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026)** — so the in-force check for any Douglas lot is its Surrey 2050 OCP designation plus its zone under Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000. Never cite the replaced OCP Bylaw No. 18020 as current. Listings quoting a 1999 "Commercial/Residential" NCP designation (as the 172 Street assembly cards do) are quoting a superseded document. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/douglas-ncp and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Secondary suites in Surrey:** one secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area under Zoning By-law No. 12000; the old 90 m² Surrey suite cap is NOT a current rule — never state it. A feed card's "licenced revenue suite" wording (259 172A Street) is a claim to verify against City records. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Coach houses and garden suites in Surrey:** permitted in residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes; 35 m² minimum and 75 m² maximum, rising to a maximum of 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the principal dwelling has not achieved its maximum floor area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Short-term rentals in Surrey:** permitted — an owner must host from their principal residence and hold a City business licence at $358 per year (Zoning By-law No. 12000, Part 4 s. B.7(d); Business License By-law No. 13680, s. 60.1). Source: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **SSMUH:** Surrey's small-scale multi-unit housing framework was adopted July 8, 2024: a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, applying to eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **ALR at the north edge:** the plan area is bordered by the Agricultural Land Reserve to the north; an ALR parcel follows the ALC housing frame of one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). Whether a specific parcel is in the ALR: UNVERIFIED at parcel level. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Servicing caution:** a 2007 City corporate report stated a City sanitary sewer system serviced about half the lands within the Douglas neighbourhood, flowing to the Douglas sanitary pump station. That statement is from 2007; current parcel-level servicing (City water/sewer vs well/septic) is UNVERIFIED in this pass. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/RPT_2007-L001.pdf
- **Property taxes (city-wide):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **GST rebates on a NEW home (relevant only to new construction, not to a resale at The Greens):** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST rebate under Bill C-4 (royal assent Mar 13, 2026) rebates 100% of the GST on a newly constructed or substantially renovated home valued up to $1 million, phasing out between $1 million and $1.5 million for a maximum of $50,000, for agreements in the Mar 20, 2025–2031 window; the older GST/HST new housing rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. Home Buyers' Plan and First Home Savings Account dollar limits are UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: Douglas Point looks like a seniors complex — is it 55+?** No. A listing for a Douglas Point rancher states in terms "Adult orientated but NOT age restricted!" It is a gated rancher strata (built 2003) with no age bylaw — anyone can live there, which also means it offers a 55+ buyer no age-restricted resale pool. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3119233-34-17516-4-avenue-surrey-british-columbia-v3s0r9-879900/brochure
- **Q: Do both of us have to be 55 at The Greens?** The listing checked states the local form as "One of the owners must be 55+ yrs." Provincially, a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident is exempt in any case, as are live-in caregivers and legacy residents. The complex's own bylaw wording must still be confirmed in its Form B. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3118185-66-350-174-street-surrey-british-columbia-v3z2n8-1455000/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy at The Greens as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. Surrey separately permits short-term rental only from an owner's principal residence with a $358/yr City business licence. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are The Greens homes single-level?** Mixed in the listings checked: one unit is described as a "corner-unit rancher with full basement" and another as a two-storey townhome with the primary bedroom on the main floor. Main-floor primary living is the pattern, but a basement or upper floor is part of both listings — verify the floor plan unit by unit if stair-free living is the requirement. Sources: the Section 2 listings.
- **Q: Are pets allowed at The Greens?** A building directory records its bylaws as age restrictions with pets allowed with restrictions. Read the current bylaws on the Form B — pet rules are complex-specific and change by amendment. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/the-greens-at-douglas-350-174-street
- **Q: Can I price a Pacific Douglas home off the South Surrey medians?** No. SnapStats publishes medians at community level only — the S Surrey White Rock detached median ($1,628,000) and Condos & Townhomes median ($700,000) for September 2026 describe all nine sub-areas combined, and Pacific Douglas publishes no median of its own; its own September 2026 rows were 39 detached listings / 6 sold / 15% and 25 attached / 4 sold / 16% (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report, via the slug's research file ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/pacific-douglas-guide.md).
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Peace Arch Hospital, 15521 Russell Avenue, White Rock — a Fraser Health community hospital with emergency, surgery and maternity care, open 24/7, in the next municipality. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/white-rock/peace-arch-hospital
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
