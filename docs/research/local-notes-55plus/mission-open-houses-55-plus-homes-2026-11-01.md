## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** People under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; exemptions attach to a "specified resident." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed; live-in caregivers; and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Open-house disclosure:** BCFSA states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Superintendent's 2017 consultation report, provides that disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information about motivation, financial qualifications or needs. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers and http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Manufactured-home parks run on tenancy law, not strata law.** Pad tenancies fall under BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act: you own the home and rent the pad; pad rent may be raised only once in 12 months on at least three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026) plus a proportional share of changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Secondary suites in homes on this feed:** all Mission secondary suites must be registered with the City; a suite is a self-contained living space inside a single family home, suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes, and a new suite goes through the building permit process. A card saying "registered w. City" is listing text — verify it. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
- **Current plan: Official Community Plan Bylaw 6405-2026** replaces and repeals OCP Bylaw 6350-2025, keeping the same vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction with a Regional Context Statement added. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026 and https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan
- **Property taxes (for the sellers behind these open houses):** City of Mission 2026 taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a flat 10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026 — no staged 5% + 5% inside the city. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: The card shows two different times for the same open house — which is right?** UNVERIFIED — check before you go. On every labelled card read, the top block is exactly one hour later than the "Open House on..." line under it; listing text backed the block in five of six text-bearing cases and the line in one, so neither can be treated as authoritative from the page alone. Confirm with the listing brokerage. https://jaredgibbons.ca/mission-open-houses.html
- **Q: Does every listing on this page hold an open house, and is any of it 55+?** On the first page read, all 12 cards carried a Sunday, November 1, 2026 open-house block; whether every card in the full 48-listing feed is labelled is UNVERIFIED. The feed is all-ages — the verified 55+ complexes are Cherry Lane and Luther Place (Section 2), and a 55+ unit appears here only when its own listing is in the feed that weekend. https://jaredgibbons.ca/mission-open-houses.html
- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually BUY in Mission?** Two verified age-restricted strata: Cherry Lane (32165 7th Ave, 20 townhouses, 1989, NWS2971) and Luther Place (7525 Martin Place, NWS2729, 1988); plus a life lease at Cedar Valley Manor (occupancy interest, not ownership) and manufactured homes on rented pads at Mission Mobile Home Park (34519 Lougheed Hwy). Sources: Section 2.
- **Q: Can I take the train to a weekend open house?** Not on the regular schedule: the West Coast Express is a weekday commuter service (Mission City Station to Waterfront, about 75 minutes) with no regular weekend or statutory-holiday service. Weekend open houses are reached by car or BC Transit local bus. https://www.translink.ca/news/2025/november/all%20aboard%20translink%20celebrates%2030%20years%20of%20west%20coast%20express and https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/
- **Q: At an open house, what should I tell the host?** Use the visit for facts about the home. Rule 5-10(3) only excuses disclosure while the host is hosting or giving factual answers — once you discuss your motivation, finances or needs with the seller's licensee, you are sharing with the other side's representative. BCFSA requires licensees to inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download and https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata we see at an open house?** Yes — provincial rules exempt a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption), plus defined child and caregiver cases. In a manufactured-home park, the park's own rules govern instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: A home on the feed has a suite — what do I check?** Its registration. Mission requires every secondary suite to be registered with the City; an unregistered suite can be investigated and may require a decommission permit. Suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
- **Q: Is Oasis Country Estates a 55+ park if it shows up on the feed?** Not as a whole — listings split it into an "adult side" and a "family section" with no stated age number for the adult side: UNCONFIRMED as 55+. Mission Mobile Home Park's listings state 55+ outright. https://www.zolo.ca/mission-real-estate/41168-lougheed-highway/13
- **Q: Where is the hospital if I am moving for care proximity?** Mission Memorial Hospital, 7324 Hurd St, with the Mission Community Health Centre at 7298 Hurd St. The in-town ladder is in Section 2: independent strata → life lease → Chartwell / The Cedars rental and assisted → long-term care at The Residence in Mission. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Mission/mission-memorial-hospital
- **Q: How do I price what I saw?** Not off this feed and not off a monthly median alone: Mission's September 2026 attached median was $430,000 on only 10 sales (it was $642,500 in August on 12), SnapStats FVR September 2026 — small-count volatility. Price off the specific complex's own sales.
