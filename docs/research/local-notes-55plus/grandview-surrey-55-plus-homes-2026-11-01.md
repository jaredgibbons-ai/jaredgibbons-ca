## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. In Grandview this matters in the negative: the complexes are strata, but no source states any of them has a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw — The Loop's developer material, for example, states rentals allowed with no short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://storage.googleapis.com/precon-app.appspot.com/40b3001a-ef8b-402b-b8b3-506e6b28f0f2.pdf
- **Plans and zoning:** the current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900 (finally adopted June 15, 2026) — never cite the replaced Bylaw No. 18020 as current. Surrey's zoning bylaw is Zoning By-law No. 12000. Note the sibling Grandview Corners file in this series still cites Bylaw No. 18020 from its underlying research — that citation is superseded by this standing correction. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Secondary suites in Surrey:** a secondary suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area under Surrey Zoning Bylaw 12000; only one suite is permitted per dwelling unit, and a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing in Surrey:** the City adopted its SSMUH zoning changes on July 8, 2024 — a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. This affects the detached lots, not the strata lots inside existing complexes. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Strata paperwork is where an age bylaw would be proved:** for any strata purchase, the Form B (Information Certificate), the bylaws and rules, minutes, budget and depreciation report are where a complex's age bylaw is actually proved. If a Grandview listing ever claims 55+, the Form B and the registered bylaws are the proof, not the listing text. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers
- **GST on new homes (relevant to Grandview's presale and new-build shelf only — resale homes are GST-exempt):** the first-time home buyers' GST rebate (Bill C-4, royal assent Mar 13, 2026 per this program's verified position) rebates 100% of the GST to a maximum of $50,000 on a new home to $1M, phasing out to $1.5M, for purchases from Mar 20, 2025 to 2031; the older GST new housing rebate rebates 36% of the GST to a maximum of $6,300, phasing out between $350K and $450K. HBP and FHSA dollar figures are UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/news/2026/03/legislation-to-make-life-more-affordable-receives-royal-assent.html
- **Property taxes (city-wide):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

- **Q: A listing near 24 Ave and 160 St says "adult-oriented" / "seniors-friendly" — is it 55+?** Not on that wording. In BC a strata age restriction exists only as a registered 55+ bylaw under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1/123.2 — marketing language is not a bylaw. No Grandview complex checked states an age restriction at all; confirm any future claim on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. What would the rule be in a true 55+ strata nearby?** Provincially, a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption) is exempt, as are live-in caregivers, legacy residents, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children the resident cared for before they turned 19. That framework applies in the verified 55+ complexes in Sunnyside Park — it does not create a 55+ complex in Grandview, where no age bylaw was verified. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a Grandview townhome?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. A strata can still restrict short-term rentals, and The Loop's developer material states exactly that pattern: rentals allowed, no short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://storage.googleapis.com/precon-app.appspot.com/40b3001a-ef8b-402b-b8b3-506e6b28f0f2.pdf
- **Q: Is The Residence at Morgan Heights something I can buy into?** No. It is a registered assisted-living residence (class SN, operator Morgan Heights Care Ltd.) at 15955 27th Avenue offering assisted living and long-term residential care — rental/care housing, not strata ownership. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=pique%20newsmagazine&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=41&name=&address=&page=10&order=alr_residence_type&sort=asc and https://morganheightscare.ca/residential-care
