# Tsawwassen Lots & Land — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Scope note: this is a topic file, not an area file. The live page is a Tsawwassen-wide feed of building lots and vacant land in the City of Delta. Vacant land has no 55+ product as such: a bare lot carries no strata corporation, no age bylaw and no park rule, so Tsawwassen has ZERO verified 55+ ownership complexes on the lots-and-land page itself. Section 1 defines the topic's two land systems (serviced urban infill lots versus agricultural parcels on the Tsawwassen edges), Section 2 covers Tsawwassen's verified 55+ stock Tsawwassen-wide — the built product a 55+ buyer is choosing between building and buying — and Sections 3–5 are angled to what a 55+ buyer needs to know about buying land and building in Tsawwassen: zoning minimums, Agricultural Land Reserve rules, the dike and flood-construction frame, bluff hazards, servicing, and the single-level / caregiver-suite questions that drive most 55+ land purchases. No market figures are stated for this topic: SnapStats does not cover South Delta / Tsawwassen and publishes no vacant-land category.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. This is the rule a 55+ buyer who builds and later strata-titles, or who buys into a bare-land strata, will live under — it does not attach to a freehold lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://choa.bc.ca/resources/strata-property-act/legislative-updates/
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. The live-in caregiver exemption is the strata-side mechanism a 55+ buyer planning ahead for care should know; on a freehold lot there is no bylaw to exempt anyone from, and the caregiver-suite question is a zoning and ALR question instead (below). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Establish which land system you are buying in before anything else.** An RS- or RD-zoned urban lot runs on Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 minimums, while an Agriculture (A)-designated parcel also runs on Agricultural Land Commission rules — the same "land" search spans both. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Urban zoning minimums (Bylaw No. 7600):** minimum lot sizes for subdivision include RS4 at 610 m², RS5 at 550 m² (or 460 m² outside the Single Family Residential designation), RS6 at 390 m², RS7 at 335 m², RD1 at 836 m² and RD4 at 504 m², with zoning confirmed parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf
- **Bluff-shore zoning:** the RS3 Single Detached Residential 3 zone sheet describes a zone for single detached housing in the vicinity of Georgia Strait, with a minimum subdivision lot area of 4,000 m², lot width 30 m and average lot depth 30 m — the estate-scale standard that fits the English Bluff and Cliff Drive shore. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS3.pdf
- **Added units on urban lots:** Delta's zoning updates allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots under the provincially required Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing changes — added-unit permission is not the same as permission to subdivide, and subdivision still runs against the zone minimums above. For a 55+ buyer, this is the urban route to a suite or added dwelling on an existing lot. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Agricultural designation and reserve status:** farm land on the Tsawwassen edges is designated Agriculture (A) in the OCP (Bylaw No. 8400, 2024) — section 1.5.1 sets the designation's purpose as maintaining agricultural lands, and the Agriculture policies at section 2.6 apply to Delta's agricultural land. A specific parcel's reserve status is checked on the Agricultural Land Commission maps by PID or civic address. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Farm-zone subdivision:** in the A1 Agriculture zone of Bylaw No. 7600 (s. 10.10.8), subdivision is permitted only where it complies with the Official Community Plan Bylaw and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable, and the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision is 8 ha, with a home site severance exception under Agricultural Land Commission policy; A2 (s. 10.20.6) also sets an 8 ha minimum, and A3 (s. 10.30.6) does not permit subdivision. Subdivision of Agricultural Land Reserve land requires permission from the Agricultural Land Commission, apart from the limited cases in the Agricultural Land Reserve General Regulation where an approving officer may approve without the Commission. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/
- **Housing on reserve land:** a principal residence built without an Agricultural Land Commission application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence is permitted without an application at 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on larger parcels, where Delta bylaws also permit it. In Delta's A1 zone, the bylaw provides one farm house per lot, a farm house maximum of 500 m², a farm home plate maximum of 3,600 m², one secondary suite inside the farm house, and one accessory farm dwelling unit of maximum 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on lots over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **Bluff hazard area (SD2):** Development Permit Area SD2, "SD2 English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment)," Category "b" (protection of development from hazardous conditions), covers the west shore; the OCP states the waterfront area is subject to mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence, development affected by hazardous conditions is subject to conditions set by a suitably qualified Professional Engineer, and no building may sit within 7.5 m of the natural boundary of the sea. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Boundary Bay hazard area (SD3):** on the Boundary Bay foreshore and the Southlands, OCP hazard Development Permit Area SD3 provides a 2.9 m Geodetic Survey of Canada datum floor level, a 2.3 m Geodetic Survey of Canada datum minimum land surface elevation and a 7.5 m tidal setback — the official frame behind any Flood Construction Level question on a low-lying lot. A single-level rancher design does not remove the floor-level requirement; it has to be designed to it. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Streamside rules:** all properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area, so the setback check applies on farmland ditches as well as urban lots. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Water and septic on unserviced parcels:** the provincial GWELLS registry holds recorded wells (older wells may not appear); domestic groundwater use needs no licence, while non-domestic use, including irrigation, requires a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act; and Fraser Health requires a home not connected to a community sewer to use an onsite sewage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Taxes on a land purchase:** Delta's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. The Province's general property transfer tax rates are 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion greater than $200,000 and up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% of the portion greater than $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. GST treatment of a specific lot sale is UNVERIFIED in these notes — the live page's summary was not verified against a Canada Revenue Agency source; confirm with a CPA per transaction. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Tax relief that matters at 55+:** the Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+ — both are principal-residence programs, so they speak to the finished home, not to the vacant lot purchase itself. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a "55+ lot" in Tsawwassen?** No such product exists as land. A vacant lot carries no age restriction — the 55+ rule, where one exists, lives in a strata's registered bylaws (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1/123.2) or in a manufactured-home park's rules, and Tsawwassen has no verified 55+ park. A lot marketed to downsizers is still a general-market lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What 55+ homes CAN I buy in Tsawwassen instead of building?** Two verified strata ownership buildings, both condos in Tsawwassen Central: The Lexington (1150 54A St, 38 units, built 1988) and Tsawwassen Greene (1140 55 St, 27 units, built 1991). No verified 55+ ownership building was found in English Bluff, Tsawwassen East or Tsawwassen North, and there is no verified 55+ townhouse complex in Tsawwassen. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/the-lexington-1150-54a-street and https://www.realtor.ca/real-estate/29373447/204-1140-55-street-delta
- **Q: If I buy land and build a rancher, can I make my own home "55+"?** A single freehold house has no strata corporation, so there is no age-bylaw mechanism for it. If the build is later strata-titled (for example a bare-land strata), only a 55+ bylaw is legal in BC, and it would restrict who may live there, not who may own. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: How big a house can go on a reserve parcel, and can I add a caregiver dwelling?** A principal residence built without an Agricultural Land Commission application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. One additional detached residence is permitted without an application at 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on larger parcels, where Delta bylaws also permit it — that additional-residence route is the ALR version of the caregiver-suite question. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I subdivide a farm parcel near Tsawwassen to fund the build, or for family?** Only within tight limits: in the A1 zone a new lot needs a minimum of 8 ha, Official Community Plan compliance and approval under the Agricultural Land Commission Act where applicable, A3 zoning does not permit subdivision, and reserve land generally needs Agricultural Land Commission permission to subdivide. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/
- **Q: What extra rules apply to a bluff or beach lot?** On the west shore, Development Permit Area SD2 (English Bluff / Tsawwassen Escarpment) addresses mud flows, debris flows, erosion and land slip, with development subject to a Professional Engineer's conditions and a 7.5 m setback from the natural boundary of the sea; on Boundary Bay, SD3 sets a 2.9 m datum floor level, a 2.3 m minimum land surface elevation and a 7.5 m tidal setback. Confirm both with the City before writing, not from a listing's wording. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Can I add units on an urban lot instead of subdividing?** Delta allows up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots under the Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing changes — but that permission does not create new lots and does not waive a hazard or streamside permit area on the parcel. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: What servicing costs should I price into an unserviced parcel?** Well and septic: the provincial GWELLS registry holds recorded wells (older wells may not appear), domestic groundwater use needs no licence while non-domestic use requires a water licence, and Fraser Health requires a home not connected to a community sewer to use an onsite sewage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person. Parcel-level servicing capacity for any specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until confirmed with the City of Delta. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/tsawwassen-lots-land.md
- **Q: Are there market statistics to price a Tsawwassen lot against?** No figure is stated in these notes: SnapStats does not cover South Delta / Tsawwassen (its Delta coverage is North Delta only, a different community, not used here even as context) and publishes no vacant-land category, so land sale counts, price per acre and land days on market are UNVERIFIED. - **Q: What taxes do I carry into the budget on top of the lot price?** Property transfer tax in the general tiers (1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000), Delta's own tax calendar (2026 due date July 2, 2026, with 5% penalties after it and a further 5% after September 2, 2026), and GST only as a per-transaction CPA question — its treatment of a specific lot sale is UNVERIFIED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The two-system split is the defining fact of this topic.** An urban lot's potential is set by its Bylaw No. 7600 zone minimum (as low as 335 m² for a new RS7 lot) and the four-unit added-housing permission, while a farm parcel's potential is set by the 8 ha A1 subdivision minimum and the Agricultural Land Commission's housing and subdivision rules. The same search word — "land" — returns parcels whose buildable future differs by orders of magnitude. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **There is no Schedule D.1 in the current OCP, however often older material cites one.** The Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950; the current consolidated OCP, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), contains no area-plan schedules, and its Development Permit Areas live in Appendix B. A land document or listing that grounds a claim in "the Tsawwassen Area Plan, Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" is conflating two bylaws. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **The bluff standard is estate-scale by zone, before the hazard permit is even opened.** RS3's minimum subdivision lot area is 4,000 m² with a 30 m lot width — the English Bluff and Cliff Drive shore was never zoned for small-lot subdivision, and SD2's Professional Engineer conditions and 7.5 m sea setback then apply on top. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS3.pdf and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **A ditch can trigger a development permit area.** Streamside Protection and Enhancement applies to all Delta properties within 30 m of a stream, expressly including sloughs and ditches — on farmland, the drainage ditch at the parcel edge is a permit-area trigger, not just scenery. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **The built 55+ alternative is two small condo buildings, and the accessible-design alternative is rental.** Tsawwassen's entire verified 55+ ownership stock is 38 units at The Lexington plus 27 units at Tsawwassen Greene; the purpose-built adaptable/accessible seniors product (The Tides' 152 homes, all designed adaptable or accessible) is KinVillage rental. A 55+ buyer who wants a new single-level home with no stairs and cannot find it in that stock is exactly the buyer this land page serves — which is why the SD3 floor-level and SD2 setback numbers matter to the design, not just to the permit. https://canada.constructconnect.com/joc/news/projects/2025/11/seniors-find-new-homes-as-152-units-open-in-delta and ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/tsawwassen-central-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **The treaty-land boundary decides which rulebook a "Tsawwassen" parcel sits under.** Parcels on Tsawwassen Lands (724+ hectares under the Final Agreement) are under Tsawwassen First Nation jurisdiction over land management; City of Delta zoning, and every Bylaw No. 7600 figure in Section 3, does not describe them. Establish which side of that boundary a parcel is on before pricing anything. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
