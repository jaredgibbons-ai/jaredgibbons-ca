## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000** governs this pocket. Secondary suites: one per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, never inside a coach house or garden suite, habitable floor area at most 40% of the principal dwelling unit. Coach houses / garden suites: 35–75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 below maximum FAR, with one extra parking space. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **OCP: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900** (adopted Jun 15, 2026) is the current OCP — never cite the replaced Bylaw No. 18020 as current. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH):** Surrey allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. This applies to the detached lots around The Crescent, not to the strata lots inside it. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **ALR:** parcels in the Agricultural Land Reserve follow the ALC housing frame — one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). ALR status is a parcel-by-parcel check, live for the acreage lots in this pocket. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **RA-zone figures caution:** the live page's RA subdivision figures (4,050 m² minimum lot size and related dimensions) were NOT re-verified against the Part 12 bylaw text in the research pass (fetch 429, stopped) — UNVERIFIED against the bylaw text; do not quote them as bylaw fact. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/elgin-chantrell.md
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey-wide):** renting by the owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, and a business licence at $358 per year is required. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Property taxes:** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Elgin Chantrell?** One verified complex: The Crescent, 3500 144 St — a gated 57-home strata townhouse complex (plan LMS4241) whose listings consistently describe a 55+ community. Everything else in this sub-area's feed is detached or acreage housing with no age restriction stated. https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/15-3500-144-st-69975
- **Q: Is The Crescent a strata or freehold?** The building record and most listings say Freehold Strata, plan LMS4241; one listing records "Freehold NonStrata." That conflict is flagged in Section 2 — settle it on the Form B and strata plan, not from a listing. https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/15-3500-144-st-69975 and https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3104377-3500-144-street-33-surrey-bc-v4p-3j6/brochure
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live at The Crescent?** Yes, if the age bylaw is a standard 55+ bylaw — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children under 19 in the resident's care and certain adult children are also exempt. Confirm the registered bylaw wording on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy at The Crescent as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. An under-55 owner may not be able to live in the unit; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit at The Crescent?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed at The Crescent?** Listings describe the community as pet friendly. Confirm the specific pet bylaw (numbers, size) in the Form B and registered bylaws — listing text is not the bylaw. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3104377-3500-144-street-33-surrey-bc-v4p-3j6/brochure
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Is Elgin Chantrell an official Surrey neighbourhood?** No — it is a board sub-area name combining Elgin and Chantrell Creek. The City publishes no boundary for the combined name, and the board's street-level line is member-only, so compare a listing by its actual street and parcel, not the label. https://jaredgibbons.ca/elgin-chantrell.html
- **Q: Which school would my grandchildren attend if family visits turn into a move?** Schools confirmed in the pocket are Chantrell Creek Elementary (2575 137 St) and Elgin Park Secondary (13484 24 Ave), but SD36 catchments are address-based and no locator result for this pocket is on record — run the exact address before relying on a school. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/education/work/bc_k12_schools.csv
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Peace Arch Hospital, 15521 Russell Avenue, White Rock, serves the South Surrey side; travel time from this pocket is UNVERIFIED. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Services/maternal-fetal-infant-services/maternity-units
