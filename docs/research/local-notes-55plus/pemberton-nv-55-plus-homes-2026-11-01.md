# Pemberton Heights (Pemberton NV) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Pemberton Heights is a residential neighbourhood in the **District of North Vancouver** (not the City) — one of the neighbourhoods inside the District's Lions Gate community, at the Capilano River and Marine Drive. Its MLS name is "Pemberton NV"; the "NV" separates it from the Town of Pemberton in the Sea-to-Sky corridor. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf
- The neighbourhood is a level, grid-pattern bench of single-detached streets — West Keith Road, West 22nd Street and Pemberton Avenue are its search streets — above Marine Drive, with Norgate to the east, Lower Capilano / Woodcroft to the west, and Capilano IR No. 5 (X̱wemelch'stn), Squamish Nation land, immediately south across Marine Drive. https://partii-partiii.fng.ca/fng-gpn-ii-iii/pii/en/474475/1/document.do
- Capilano Elementary (K–7, 1230 West 20th St) sits in the neighbourhood and is an authorized IB World School offering the Primary Years Programme (authorized 2011); the catchment secondary school is Carson Graham Secondary (2145 Jones Ave), also an IB World School. SD44 catchments are address-based and were under review in 2025–26 tied to the new Cloverley elementary opening September 2026 — confirm by exact address. https://ibo.org/school/004909/ and https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/044 and https://www.sd44.ca/media/nvsd/nvsd/content-assets/documents/schools/Catchment-Review-Presentation-Boards-May-20-Revised-Graphics.pdf
- The R2 Marine–Willingdon RapidBus stops on Marine Drive at Pemberton Avenue and runs between Park Royal and Metrotown Station (extended September 7, 2026; roughly 5 a.m.–2 a.m., every 6–7 minutes at peak); TransLink's Route 240 also runs the Marine Drive corridor. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/ and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt240.pdf
- Everyday shopping is at the Lions Gate Village Centre (Capilano Road / Marine Drive) and Capilano Mall to the east; Park Royal is across the Capilano River in West Vancouver. http://parkroyal.ca/
- Recreation at the door: Sowden Park is a District park in the neighbourhood (rezoned to Community Park Zone in District Bylaw 6972, and on the District's designated-parks liquor list under Bylaw 8520, Schedule A), and the Lions Gate Community Recreation Centre (1733 Lions Gate Lane, opened June 4, 2022) offers a gymnasium, kitchen, multipurpose and art spaces — no pool — plus the NVDPL Lions Gate Express Library. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/council_reports/98357.pdf and https://www.nvrc.ca/sites/default/files/docs/About-Us/june_23_2022_commission_meeting_agenda_package_final.pdf and https://www.nvdpl.ca/lions-gate-express
- Where 55+ housing concentrates: **nowhere inside Pemberton Heights.** The bench is almost entirely freehold single-family houses; the only strata product is the new general-market Lions Gate Village stock at the southwest corner, and none of it is age-restricted — see Section 2.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Municipality: District of North Vancouver.** The District's OCP (Bylaw 7900) directs 75–90% of residential growth into four centres — Lynn Valley, Lynn Creek, Maplewood Village and Lions Gate Village — and preserves established low-density neighbourhoods elsewhere; Pemberton Heights is outside the centres, and Lions Gate Village Centre at its southwest corner is the growth node. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf and https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/regional-context-statement-north-vancouver-district-2026.pdf
- **District taxes:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; Council approved a 3.53% municipal tax increase for 2026; the 2026 total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value, and combined single-family utilities total $2,571 in 2026 (water $932.80; sewer/drainage $1,255.60). https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- **Suites and coach houses:** since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary; a secondary suite may be up to 49% of the house with no minimum size, and coach houses may be two storeys. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **SSMUH caution for the Lions Gate bench:** GEOweb classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop — but on March 2, 2026 District Council asked staff to pursue exempting the Lions Gate area from SSMUH, and the District's Bill 25 compliance amendments were still in preparation in spring 2026. Price a Pemberton Heights lot as a single-family lot unless a specific approval says otherwise. https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf and http://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-4-detached-residential.pdf and https://app.dnv.org/OpenDocument/Default.aspx?docNum=6702125
- **Hazard permit areas:** Schedule B of the District OCP establishes Development Permit Areas for Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard; lots near the Capilano River on the west edge warrant a GeoWeb flood/hazard check — the District's flood construction level data is modelled on a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-single-family-residential-1-5.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ condo or townhome I can buy in Pemberton Heights?** No — no verified 55+ ownership complex exists inside the sub-area. The nine buildings on the maintained North Vancouver 55+ strata list are all in other neighbourhoods (Lynn Valley, Lonsdale, Delbrook, Edgemont). The strata stock that does exist here — Park West, Belle Isle, Mason at Lions Gate Village — is general-market with no age bylaw in any source checked. https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/on-the-market/55-buildings-north-vancouver/
- **Q: Then why do downsizers buy on this bench?** The lots: the research file describes predominantly single-detached houses on the District's flattest residential bench — level, grid-pattern streets rather than sloped lots — so a rancher or a house with a main-floor primary suite gives single-level living without leaving the District. That is a housing-form choice, not a 55+ designation. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/pemberton-nv.md
- **Q: Is Pemberton Heights in the City or the District?** The District. It is one of the neighbourhoods in the District's Lions Gate community; the City of North Vancouver is a separate municipality to the east with its own council, OCP and tax rates. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere on the North Shore?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What subsidized seniors rental is closest?** Capilano House, 1551 Bridgman Ave, listed in BC Housing's Zone 1 seniors directory (May 2025) as a Housing Registry development at the bench's eastern edge; applications go through BC Housing's Housing Registry. Its age minimum and unit count are UNCONFIRMED in the directory extract, and its exact MLS sub-area (Pemberton NV vs Norgate) is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-1-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: Can I add a suite or coach house so a caregiver or adult child can live with me?** District zoning has permitted a house plus secondary suite plus coach house (three units) on single-family lots within the Urban Containment Boundary since June 2024. Do not price in full SSMUH density on top of that for a Lions Gate lot — Council voted in March 2026 to pursue exempting the Lions Gate area from SSMUH. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf and http://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-4-detached-residential.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital, and how do I get downtown without a car?** Lions Gate Hospital is at 231 East 15th St in the City of North Vancouver. Without a car: the R2 RapidBus stops at Pemberton Avenue on Marine Drive (Park Royal–Metrotown since September 7, 2026), and the SeaBus from Lonsdale Quay crosses to Waterfront Station in 12 minutes. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital and https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/ and https://www.translink.ca/translink/schedules-and-maps/seabus
- **Q: Are there flood or slope risks to check on the west edge?** Yes. The District designates Natural Hazard Development Permit Areas and publishes property-level reports through GeoWeb; Metro Vancouver warns the Capilano River below Cleveland Dam can rise more than 5 metres, with flow increasing to 413,000 litres per second or higher. Pull the GEOweb flood construction level for any river-adjacent lot before pricing a renovation. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-single-family-residential-1-5.pdf and https://metrovancouver.org/river-safety/about-the-capilano-river

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The growth node has a paper history most listings never mention.** Lions Gate Village began as the April 2013 Lower Capilano–Marine Village Implementation Plan, followed by a July 2015 Lions Gate Public Realm Strategy; the District's Belle Isle Park concept ties the village to the Capilano River trail system and a future Village Plaza and Village Green. The built result so far is three named strata projects (Park West, Belle Isle, Mason) — all general-market. https://app.dnv.org/OpenDocument/Default.aspx?docNum=4013488 and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/belle-isle-park-final-report.pdf
- **The District is actively trying to keep this bench out of provincial multiplex density.** A March 2, 2026 Council motion asked staff to pursue exempting the Lions Gate area from SSMUH on the basis that it is better suited to development supporting rapid transit — the opposite assumption from most Metro Vancouver benches, where SSMUH upside is the pitch. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-4-detached-residential.pdf
- **The land south of Marine Drive is an approved development front, not a static edge.** On October 29, 2024, Squamish Nation Council approved land uses for the Xwmélch'sten (Capilano) IR 5 Marine Drive site — mixed-use market residential, Nation housing, and a new Administrative and Community Service Centre. South-facing Pemberton Heights homes look across Marine Drive at that site. https://www.squamish.net/squamish-nation-council-approves-land-uses-for-three-sites-extends-moratorium-on-third-party-development-proposals/
- **The neighbourhood school is an IB school, and its catchment was just reviewed.** Capilano Elementary is an authorized IB World School (PYP, authorized 2011) inside the neighbourhood; SD44 ran a catchment review tied to the new Cloverley elementary opening September 2026, so a catchment printed in an older listing may already be stale — confirm by exact address. https://ibo.org/school/004909/ and https://www.sd44.ca/media/nvsd/nvsd/content-assets/documents/schools/Catchment-Review-Presentation-Boards-May-20-Revised-Graphics.pdf
- **Sowden Park carries two District paper trails.** It was rezoned to Community Park Zone (CP) in District Bylaw 6972 (with a Sowden Park Dedication Bylaw 7567 given readings in 2005), and it is on the District's list of designated parks where liquor consumption is permitted under Bylaw 8520, Schedule A. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/council_reports/98357.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/Bylaw%208520.pdf
- **The river below the dam is a genuine hazard, not scenery copy.** Metro Vancouver publishes that the Capilano River below Cleveland Dam can rise more than 5 metres with water moving faster than 21 km/h; the Hatchery in the regional park (~250,000 visitors a year) was reported closed to the public for construction starting October 1, 2026. https://metrovancouver.org/river-safety/about-the-capilano-river and https://www.pac.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/sep-pmvs/hatcheries-ecloseries/capilano-eng.html
- **SnapStats publishes no North Vancouver figures at all.** The GVR September 2026 report covers Burnaby, New Westminster, Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, Pitt Meadows and Maple Ridge only — any "Pemberton NV median" attributed to SnapStats does not exist; sub-area medians are Paragon-only. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/pemberton-nv.md
