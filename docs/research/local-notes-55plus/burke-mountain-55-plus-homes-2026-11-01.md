# Burke Mountain (Coquitlam) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. No Burke Mountain strata checked has used this power — the absence of a 55+ bylaw is the verified fact here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996** is Coquitlam's zoning bylaw. The small-scale residential zones are R-1, R-2 and R-3 (subdivision minimums 650 m² / 18.5 m width for R-1 and R-3; 465 m² / 12.5 m for R-2). https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) in Coquitlam:** the City's adopted rules allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the rules — the route by which a Burke Mountain house lot, not a strata, becomes a multi-generational or suite property. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Watercourse setbacks are doubled in Northeast Coquitlam:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 50 metres of the top of bank of any watercourse in Northeast Coquitlam (30 metres in the rest of Coquitlam); Zoning Bylaw s.519 sets flood protection setbacks from watercourses and s.523 covers riparian areas. The City is also securing a Partington Creek wildlife corridor (50 metres wide, expanding to a 100-metre buffer at an identified wildlife "hot spot," between Cedar Drive and Victoria Drive in its first phase). Creek-edge and corridor lots need lot-level checks before decks, additions or suites are promised. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits and https://www.coquitlam.ca/417/Northwest-Burke-Vision
- **Property tax dates that apply citywide:** Coquitlam property tax payment was due July 2, 2026 for the Jan 1–Dec 31, 2026 year, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after September 15, 2026, including unclaimed Home Owner Grants. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy on Burke Mountain?** None that any source verifies. Every named complex and listing stream checked — the new townhouse collections and the village-area strata stock — is general-market family housing with no age restriction, and there is no seniors building of any tenure on the mountain. A senior who wants a legal 55+ purchase in Coquitlam is looking at the Town Centre buildings or Hideaway Park in other sub-areas (see the Central Coquitlam file). (Building checks Nov 1, 2026; listing counts as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.)
- **Q: Isn't Robert Nicklin Place a Burke Mountain seniors building?** No, on both counts: the 164-unit Robert Nicklin Place at 3100 Ozada Ave sits by Lincoln Station, not on Burke Mountain (it surfaces in Burke searches because the provincial riding is named Coquitlam-Burke Mountain), and its tenant priority covers families with low incomes as well as seniors and people with disabilities — it is mixed affordable rental, not 55+. http://globalawards.news/new-community-to-include-164-rental-homes-open-for-families-and-seniors/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Coquitlam?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can a Burke Mountain house work as a multi-generational home instead?** That is the realistic in-area route: Coquitlam's SSMUH rules allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes, where the lot is connected to municipal water and sewer. On creek-edge lots, check the 50-metre Northeast Coquitlam watercourse DPA before assuming the extra unit fits. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId= and https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Q: When do the Burke Mountain Village shops and community centre actually open?** They are under construction, not open: the village centre is planned at more than 120,000 sq. ft. of retail, and the aquatic centre / community centre / library building broke ground on May 12, 2026 — its completion date is UNVERIFIED. Buy on what exists today; treat the village as a dated, sourced future amenity. https://burkevillage.ca/village-timeline/
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Eagle Ridge Hospital, 475 Guildford Way, Port Moody — the Fraser Health hospital on the Port Moody / Coquitlam border, reached by driving down off the mountain and across the city. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** There is no SkyTrain station on Burke Mountain; the nearest Millennium Line stations are Coquitlam Central, Lincoln and Lafarge Lake-Douglas, so a car-free senior here is bus-dependent — and the bus routes serving the mountain's interior streets are UNVERIFIED in the sources checked. Check the exact address's bus service before buying on a no-car plan. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/station/coquitlam-central/schedule
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay on Burke Mountain?** Not in a seniors building — there is none in the sub-area. The in-city ladder sits elsewhere in Coquitlam and the Tri-Cities: 55+ strata in the Town Centre pocket, the registered assisted living at Residences at Belvedere on Delestre Avenue, retirement and care on Dufferin Street, and two new S.U.C.C.E.S.S. 55+ affordable rental buildings under construction at 290 Laval Square and 443 Clayton Street. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026HMA0070-000764.pdf and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ACoquitlam&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=asc

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The two markets on the mountain are moving at different speeds (September 2026):** detached ran a 5% sales ratio (3 solds from 65 listings) while condos & townhomes ran 14% (11 solds from 77 listings), and 11 of the sub-area's 14 solds were strata. A senior downsizing INTO a Burke townhouse is buying into the liquid side of the mountain; a senior selling a Burke house is on the slow side — comp the two separately, against the Coquitlam medians (detached $1,455,000; condos & townhomes $625,000). (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.)
- **The village centre is a plan with a groundbreaking, not an amenity:** Burke Mountain Village's 120,000+ sq. ft. of retail, public plaza and combined aquatic centre / community centre / library are under construction in 2026 (groundbreaking May 12, 2026). Listings that present village shops or a pool as existing features are ahead of the facts — the only village-area facility confirmed open is the City-run Burke Mountain Discovery Centre at 3537 Princeton Avenue. https://burkevillage.ca/village-timeline/
- **Creek rules reach twice as far here as in the rest of the city:** Northeast Coquitlam's Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area extends 50 metres from the top of bank of any watercourse — double the 30-metre reach elsewhere in Coquitlam — and the Partington Creek wildlife corridor adds a secured 50-metre corridor (100 metres at the identified hot spot) between Cedar Drive and Victoria Drive. On a mountainside laced with Hyde, Smiling, Partington and Burke Mountain creeks, the buildable area of a lot is a survey question, not an assumption. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits and https://www.coquitlam.ca/417/Northwest-Burke-Vision
- **Names that sound like seniors housing are neither here nor 55+ (checked Nov 1, 2026):** Robert Nicklin Place is mixed affordable rental by Lincoln Station, and 55One is a mixed affordable rental tower in Burquitlam whose "55" is a street-number name (551 Emerson Street), not an age rule. Neither gives Burke Mountain a 55+ entry. http://globalawards.news/new-community-to-include-164-rental-homes-open-for-families-and-seniors/ and https://www.concertproperties.com/news/in-the-news/one-hundred-affordable-homes-open-coquitlam
- **The growth plan is measured in decades:** the Northwest Burke Vision covers about 400 hectares / 980 acres described by the City as the last large undeveloped area in Coquitlam, guiding growth over 30+ years on top of the Hyde Creek, Smiling Creek and Partington Creek plans adopted since the area was first defined for population growth in the early 1990s. A purchase here is a purchase inside an active, long construction frontier — with no seniors component named in any plan source checked. https://www.coquitlam.ca/417/Northwest-Burke-Vision
- **School claims need an address-level run, full stop:** SD43 names Smiling Creek Elementary and Coast Salish Elementary as Burke Mountain–area schools, but their street addresses are UNVERIFIED in the research and the only verified locator result in the base material is a City Centre address. Programs of Choice (French Immersion, Mandarin Bilingual, Montessori) are application/lottery entry, never catchment. http://sd43.bc.ca/cross-catchment
