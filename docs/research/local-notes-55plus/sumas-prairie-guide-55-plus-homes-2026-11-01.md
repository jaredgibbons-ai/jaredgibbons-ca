## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **There is no age bylaw on a freehold farm or rural house.** Strata age law only bites inside a strata corporation, and this sub-area has no verified strata stock (Section 2). A senior buying on the prairie faces flood, zoning and Agricultural Land Reserve rules — not a 55+ residency bylaw, and not a restricted resale pool. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **If the same buyer instead buys 55+ strata in urban Abbotsford, the provincial frame is this:** the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1/123.2, has allowed only 55-and-over residency bylaws since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44); the bylaw restricts residing, not owning; Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — since May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19; and strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since the same date, though short-term rental bylaws still apply and a tenant must still meet the age bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Flood is an active planning file, not a settled fix.** The watershed initiative was signed in April 2023 after the November 2021 floods; approximately $220 million in provincial funding since 2021 and $76.6 million for the Barrowtown Pump Station sit alongside $3.3 million for the initiative's design and assessment work — and the parcel-level facts (elevation, Flood Construction Level position, dyke/pump position) for any specific property are UNVERIFIED in this file and must be confirmed with the City before subjects come off. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/supporting-flood-mitigation-planning-sumas-prairie
- **ALR housing rules govern added homes on farm parcels.** Under Agricultural Land Commission housing rules, land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless the owner qualifies for an additional residence; the principal residence must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with a maximum of 1,000 m² of fill permitted for that residence; a secondary suite is permitted in the ALR only within the principal residence; and an additional detached residence may be up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. ALR status for any specific prairie parcel is UNVERIFIED — check parcel by parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Zoning frame: Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 applies city-wide.** The City's AgRefresh parcel-size work records a minimum subdivision lot area of 8.0 ha in the Agricultural One Zone (A1) and 16.0 ha in the Agricultural Two Zone (A2). The zone of any specific prairie parcel is a per-parcel check: UNVERIFIED for any individual parcel. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- **Suites and coach/garden suites in Abbotsford:** under Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014, a secondary suite must be less than 50% of the size of the principal unit, and garden suites and coach houses may occupy up to 15% of the lot area to a maximum of 100 m² — subject on ALR land to the ALC frame above. The Official Community Plan is Bylaw 2600-2016. Sources: the research program's rules-gaps pass, as recorded in the batch-12 research file for this area.
- **Manufactured-home parks, if a park purchase elsewhere in Abbotsford is involved:** tenancies fall under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act — the resident owns the home and rents the pad. A park's age rule is a park rule, not a strata bylaw. No park is verified in this sub-area (Section 2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- **Property taxes (Abbotsford side):** City of Abbotsford taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026. Prairie parcels on the Chilliwack / FVRD side of the split fall under that jurisdiction's tax regime instead — which regime applies is a per-parcel check, and no FVRD rural tax figure is stated in this file. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there really no 55+ condo, townhouse or park to buy on Sumas Prairie?** Correct, on every source checked — and this guide file agrees word-for-word with the sibling listings-page file: no age-restricted strata, freehold complex or manufactured-home park is verified in this sub-area, and its September 2026 attached board row is 0 listings / 0 solds. Abbotsford's buyable 55+ stock — 26 verified strata complexes, three parks and two life lease residences — is all in the urban city. See the sibling file and the Abbotsford West, Central Abbotsford and Poplar files in this set. (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report)
- **Q: The guide and the listings page disagree about the schools — which one is right?** The guide. It names Semá:th Elementary at 36321 Vye Road and Barrowtown Elementary at 5137 Tolmie Road, and both name/address pairs match the BC Ministry K-12 file exactly. The sibling listings page named "Upper Sumas Elementary" at the Vye Road address and gave a garbled Barrowtown address — listings-page defects for the rebuild list, not guide-page defects. Catchments remain address-based; no locator run for a prairie address is on record. https://www.abbyschools.ca/schools/catchments and https://jaredgibbons.ca/sumas-prairie-guide.html
- **Q: So how does a senior actually live on the prairie?** In a regular freehold farm or rural-residential house, on the sources checked. There is no age bylaw to qualify under, and no restricted buyer pool when the home is resold. The seniors-specific work is practical, not legal: single-level living in the existing house, a suite for a caregiver or family member where the ALR and zoning frames allow one, and the flood file on the specific parcel. Sources: Sections 2 and 3 above.
- **Q: What is being done about flooding, and should it decide my purchase?** It should be the first subject, not the last. The watershed initiative signed in April 2023 followed the November 2021 floods; the Province has provided approximately $220 million for Abbotsford and Sumas Prairie since 2021, including $76.6 million for Barrowtown Pump Station upgrades, plus $3.3 million for design and assessment work. Get the parcel's elevation, Flood Construction Level position and insurance position in writing before subjects come off. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/supporting-flood-mitigation-planning-sumas-prairie
- **Q: Can a second dwelling be built on a prairie farm parcel for family or a caregiver?** Where the parcel is in the ALR, the ALC frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² of floor area, a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional detached residence of 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (parcels over 40 ha); the City's suite cap (less than 50% of the principal unit) and coach/garden cap (15% of the lot to 100 m²) sit alongside it. Confirm the parcel's ALR status and zone first. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Does Sumas Prairie stop at the Abbotsford city line?** The board sub-area the guide covers is Abbotsford's, but the prairie and its watershed are planned jointly — the flood initiative's signatories include the City of Chilliwack alongside Abbotsford and three First Nations, and the physical floodplain spans Abbotsford and Chilliwack / FVRD jurisdiction. For any specific parcel, the municipality on the title and tax notice decides which bylaws, taxes and flood rules apply — check the parcel, not the prairie name. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/supporting-flood-mitigation-planning-sumas-prairie
- **Q: How far is the hospital from the prairie?** Abbotsford Regional Hospital and Cancer Centre is at 32900 Marshall Rd, in the urban city northwest of the prairie, and the Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 2692 Clearbrook Rd #100. No official source publishes drive times from prairie addresses — any minute figure is UNVERIFIED. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Abbotsford/abbotsford-regional-hospital-and-cancer-centre
- **Q: If I sell a prairie farm and buy 55+ in the city, can my 53-year-old spouse live with me?** Yes — Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption; the same regulation covers live-in caregivers and the defined children cases. That exemption matters only in the city purchase — a prairie house itself has no age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I subdivide a prairie parcel to create a smaller homesite for retirement?** Generally no, on the published minimums: A1 subdivision minimums are 8.0 ha and A2 minimums are 16.0 ha in the City's parcel-size work, so most prairie parcels cannot be split further — and the parcel's actual zone must be confirmed first. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- **Q: How did Sumas Prairie sell in September 2026 — is it a slow market?** No pace conclusion can be drawn: the board row is 2 detached listings with 0 solds (sales ratio NA) and 0 attached listings with 0 solds. Medians are published at community level only (Abbotsford detached $949,000; attached $497,500 in September 2026), expressly not as prairie figures — price a prairie property from its own farm and acreage comparables (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report).

- **The sub-area a buyer sees is wider than the prairie floor.** Cards labelled "Sumas Prairie" on the guide include Majuba Hill and Old Yale Road upland acreage — a hillside house on the fringe carries a different flood and slope profile than a farm parcel on the floor, even though both wear the same sub-area label.
