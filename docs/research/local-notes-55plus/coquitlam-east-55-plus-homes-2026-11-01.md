## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+ — and the "50 plus" wording in the 2018 Glen Eagles listing in Section 2) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996** carries Coquitlam's small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3 — minimum lot size for subdivision is 650 m² with 18.5 m width for R-1 and R-3, and 465 m² with 12.5 m width for R-2. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **SSMUH in Coquitlam:** the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing rules as adopted in Coquitlam allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the SSMUH rules. None of this infill is age-restricted. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Watercourse protection:** a Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 50 metres of the top of bank of any watercourse in Northeast Coquitlam, and within 30 metres in the rest of Coquitlam; Zoning Bylaw s.519 sets flood protection setbacks from watercourses and s.523 covers riparian areas — directly relevant to the creek- and ravine-edge lots on the east side. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** for this area (Zone 4 directory, May 2025), which is how the Coquitlam-North buildings listed in Section 2 fill units. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Property taxes:** City of Coquitlam property tax payment was due July 2, 2026, covering January 1 through December 31, 2026, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance of current taxes after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after September 15, 2026 (including unclaimed Home Owner Grants). https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Coquitlam East?** None that any source verifies inside the board's Coquitlam East sub-area. The verified 55+ ownership buildings on this side of the Tri-Cities — Marlborough House (Guildford Way) and Princess Gate (Princess Crescent) — sit in the North Coquitlam and Canyon Springs sub-areas, and the 55+ manufactured-home park, Hideaway Park, sits in Ranch Park. A 55+ buyer in Coquitlam East itself is buying a regular house or strata home with no age bylaw. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: A listing called Glen Eagles on Dufferin Street "50 plus". Can it still enforce that?** No — not as a 50+ bylaw. Any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022; only a bylaw the strata has amended to 55+ can still restrict who lives there. The only source found for Glen Eagles' restriction is a 2018 listing, so its current bylaw is UNCONFIRMED — read the strata's current bylaws and Form B before relying on any age claim for that building. https://s.realtyninja.com/static/media/listings/4753_la12c6202b_fs.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — a BC age bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: How does a manufactured home in Hideaway Park differ from a strata purchase?** In the park model the buyer owns the manufactured home and rents the pad — a current listing states a pad rental of approximately $750/month — rather than owning a strata lot and a share of common property. The park is also in the Ranch Park sub-area, not Coquitlam East. https://vancouvernashdom.com/coquitlam-mobile-homes-floating-homes-recreat/SearchResults.form/
- **Q: Can I add a suite or carriage home for a parent or caregiver?** That is the realistic ageing-in-place route on the east side's house lots: Coquitlam's SSMUH rules allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes — but lots not connected to municipal water or sewer fall outside the rules, and creek-edge lots can trigger the watercourse permit area. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Eagle Ridge Hospital, 475 Guildford Way in Port Moody — a Fraser Health community hospital with a 24/7 emergency department, on the Port Moody / Coquitlam border. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** Coquitlam Central Station (Millennium Line and West Coast Express) at Lougheed Highway and Pinetree Way is the area's rapid-transit link; the West Coast Express is a weekday commuter service, so local buses are the everyday option for the interior streets. Per-route bus numbers for those streets: UNVERIFIED. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/station/coquitlam-central/schedule
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay on the east side?** At the retirement-residence and care steps, yes — but across the sub-area line in Eagle Ridge CQ: Parkwood Manor (1142 Dufferin St) offers independent-living rental suites, and Dufferin Care Centre (1131 Dufferin St) is long-term care. Neither is a purchase, and neither is in Coquitlam East proper. https://cogirseniorliving.ca/retirement-home-coquitlam-british-columbia/venvi-parkwood-manor/ and https://www.retirementconcepts.com/locations/dufferin-care-centre/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The east side's seniors cluster hides behind a sub-area line.** Dufferin Street holds a retirement residence (Parkwood Manor, 1142), a care centre (Dufferin Care Centre, 1131) and an adult-oriented strata (Glen Eagles, 1150) within a few hundred metres of each other — and MLS classes all of it as Eagle Ridge CQ, so a search filtered to "Coquitlam East" will never show it. https://cogirseniorliving.ca/retirement-home-coquitlam-british-columbia/venvi-parkwood-manor/ and https://s.realtyninja.com/static/media/listings/4753_la12c6202b_fs.pdf
- **"Adult oriented" in an old listing can be a dead bylaw.** Glen Eagles was marketed as "50 PLUS" in 2018; since Nov 24, 2022 only 55+ bylaws survive in BC, so any pre-2022 listing language about 19+, 40+, 45+ or 50+ restrictions has to be re-verified against the strata's current bylaws before it is repeated. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **BC Housing does not use the board's map.** The Zone 4 seniors directory splits Coquitlam into just "North" and "South" — a building described as a "Coquitlam-North seniors building" (Johnson St, Pipeline Rd, Ozada Ave) is not thereby inside the Coquitlam East MLS sub-area. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **The strata sample here is too thin to price from.** Coquitlam East detached ran at a 14% sales ratio in September 2026 (28 listings, 4 solds) and condos & townhomes at 11% (9 listings, 1 sold) — one strata sale does not set a sub-area price. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.)
- **Creek setbacks are wider on this side of the city.** The watercourse development permit area reaches 50 metres from the top of bank in Northeast Coquitlam, versus 30 metres in the rest of Coquitlam — check it before pricing an addition or a suite on a ravine-edge lot. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Coquitlam taxes have two penalty dates.** 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to unpaid balances after July 2 and a further 5% after September 15, 2026, including unclaimed Home Owner Grants. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes
