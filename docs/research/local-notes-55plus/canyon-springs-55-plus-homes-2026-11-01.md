## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The Canyon Springs complex stands at 2665 Mountain Highway in Lynn Valley, in the District of North Vancouver — two buildings (four and five storeys) connected by a timber-and-steel structure, built by Polygon in 2015, near Lynn Canyon. https://rentitfurnished.com/vancouver/building/canyon-springs and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/canyon-springs-2665-mountain-hwy
- Mountain Highway is the north–south spine here: Lynn Valley Road crosses it at the Lynn Valley town centre a few blocks south, and Highway 1 (Upper Levels Highway) is immediately south of the town centre. The seniors cluster described in Section 2 spreads across three Lynn Valley pockets: Lynn Valley Road itself (Balmoral House), Cedar Village Crescent / Cedar Village Close to the east (Kirkstone Gardens, Cedar Garden, Cedarview Lodge), and Whiteley Court further east (Kiwanis Lynn Manor and Lynn Woods). Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- The recreation anchor is the Karen Magnussen Community Recreation Centre on Kirkstone Road, with the historic Molly Nye House beside the Lynn Valley Road seniors buildings — a current Balmoral House listing describes both as steps away. https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/960-lynn-valley-road/113
- Schools: School District 44 catchments are address-based, with elementary (K–7) and secondary (8–12) schools only and no middle tier; the catchment for a specific Lynn Valley address is UNCONFIRMED from the sources read — check each address with SD44. https://www.sd44.ca/school/canyonheights/Pages/default.aspx (SD44 school pages; catchment method per the Capilano area research file, sources read 2026-11-01)
- The District's Official Community Plan is Bylaw 7900; zoning is Zoning Bylaw 3210. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- Where the seniors housing actually is: Lynn Valley is one of the District's two real seniors clusters (the other is Edgemont Village). Within about a kilometre of the Canyon Springs building there is a verified 55+ strata on Lynn Valley Road (Balmoral House), a second 55+ strata on Cedar Village Crescent (Kirkstone Gardens), a publicly operated assisted-living residence and long-term care lodge on Cedar Village Close, and the Kiwanis society's two independent seniors buildings on Whiteley Court. The Canyon Springs building itself is **not** age-restricted — see Section 2. Sources: Section 2 below.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. Note the local nuance: building data for Canyon Springs records "rentals allowed w/restrictions" — any rental-restriction bylaw behind that wording is void; short-term limits may still apply. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/canyon-springs-2665-mountain-hwy
- **A non-profit's age rule is not a strata bylaw.** Kiwanis Lynn Manor's 65+ eligibility and Lynn Woods' District-bylaw age minimum are landlord/program rules set by the society, its funders and (for Lynn Woods) a District housing agreement — the Strata Property Act's 55+ framework does not govern them, which is how a 65+ threshold can lawfully exist next door to 55+ strata product. https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/lynn-manor/ and http://globalawards.news/affordable-housing-project-for-seniors-lowers-age-to-fill-units/
- **Zoning: District of North Vancouver Zoning Bylaw 3210, Section 525.** The R1 detached zone minimum lot is 4,000 m² (30 m width, 34 m depth); the R2 minimum is 660 m² (18 m width, 34 m depth), with modifiers down to R2(S1) at 335 m² / 10 m. An R2 principal building may include a secondary suite, and a coach house is a separate accessory dwelling unit. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Three units on a house lot:** since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary; the District's SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 4 units above 280 m² and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf
- **Hazard permit areas:** District OCP Bylaw 7900, Schedule B sets seven Development Permit Areas including Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard — live issues on Lynn Valley's creek and canyon-edge lots; the District publishes property-level GeoWeb reports. https://dnv-docs.simplicitycms.ca/documents/ocp-schedule-b-dpa.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/
- **Property taxes:** District of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; 2026 single-family utility fees total $2,571.20 (water $932.80, sewer/drainage $1,255.60, plus other charges). https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- **Assisted living and long-term care here are health-system products:** Cedar Garden is registered assisted living operated by Vancouver Coastal Health, and Cedarview Lodge is VCH long-term care — both accessed through VCH assessment, not the real-estate market. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ALangley&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Canyon Springs itself a 55+ building?** No. It is a general-market Polygon strata condominium built in 2015 at 2665 Mountain Hwy; building data records pet and rental restrictions only, with no age restriction stated anywhere in the sources checked. The 55+ buildings are its Lynn Valley neighbours, Balmoral House and Kirkstone Gardens. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/canyon-springs-2665-mountain-hwy
- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy near Canyon Springs?** Two verified 55+ strata buildings in Lynn Valley: Balmoral House (960 Lynn Valley Rd, built 2002, MLS-flagged "Senior Community: Yes") and Kirkstone Gardens (2020 Cedar Village Cres, built 1991, described in listings as an established 55+ community). Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: I'm 58 and low-income. Can I get into the Kiwanis buildings on Whiteley Court?** Not Lynn Manor on its stated terms — the society lists Lynn Manor as 65+. Lynn Woods next door is the newer subsidized/below-market building (106 one-bedroom apartments), and reporting says its age minimum was lowered from 65 (to 55 for the rent-geared-to-income homes), but the society's own pages read here do not state Lynn Woods' current age — confirm eligibility directly with the Kiwanis North Shore Housing Society. https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/lynn-manor/ and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2017-2021/2019MAH0181-002254.pdf
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** At Canyon Springs, building data records pets allowed with restrictions. At Balmoral House, a current listing mentions a "four-legged companion" in the suite description, but the building's pet bylaw itself is UNCONFIRMED from the sources read — check the strata bylaws for the specific building. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/canyon-springs-2665-mountain-hwy and https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/960-lynn-valley-road/113
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver, is the Vancouver Coastal Health hospital serving the North Shore; the North Vancouver Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 221 West Esplanade, North Vancouver. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/north-vancouver-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in Lynn Valley?** Through the publicly funded steps, yes: Cedar Garden (1250 Cedar Village Close) is VCH-operated registered assisted living and Cedarview Lodge (1200 Cedar Village Close) is VCH long-term care at 82 beds — both a few blocks from the Canyon Springs building, and both allocated by VCH assessment rather than bought or privately rented. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/cedar-garden/plan-your-visit and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/cedarview-lodge
- **Q: Why do searches for "Canyon Springs" also show Coquitlam homes?** Because the same name is a Coquitlam board sub-area and a North Vancouver building. The Coquitlam figures and listings have nothing to do with the District of North Vancouver complex — check the municipality on every result. Sources: the conflict note at the top of this file.

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The slug names a building, not a neighbourhood — and the building is not seniors housing.** "Canyon Springs" in North Vancouver is one 2015 Polygon condominium at the top of Mountain Highway. Its 55+ relevance is purely geographic: it stands in the middle of Lynn Valley's seniors cluster while having no age bylaw of its own. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/canyon-springs-2665-mountain-hwy
- **Two Cedar Village streets, two opposite products.** Cedar Village Crescent holds both Kirkstone Gardens (55+ strata condos) and Mountain Estates (family townhomes with a playground) — and Cedar Village Close, a different street a block away, holds the VCH assisted-living and long-term care buildings. A search result that says "Cedar Village" can be any of the three; the street suffix is the tell. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3115906-317-2020-cedar-village-crescent-north-vancouver-british-columbia-v7j3p5-729000/brochure and https://www.zolo.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate/1930-cedar-village-crescent/65 and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/cedar-garden/plan-your-visit
- **The Kiwanis age rules here are a policy story, not a bylaw story.** Lynn Woods opened as a 65+ building because the District wrote that age into its housing agreement; when the market-rate suites would not fill at that age, council lowered the thresholds (reported as 55 for rent-geared-to-income homes, 60 for low-income homes). The neighbouring Lynn Manor tower — operating since 1974 — still states 65+. Lesson: in non-profit housing the age on the door is set by agreements and can be renegotiated; in strata it is set by the Strata Property Act and cannot go below 55 at all. http://globalawards.news/affordable-housing-project-for-seniors-lowers-age-to-fill-units/ and https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/lynn-manor/
- **Lynn Valley's care beds are public, not private.** Unlike Edgemont Village (Amica) or West Vancouver (Amica, Cogir), the assisted-living and long-term care capacity beside Canyon Springs is operated by Vancouver Coastal Health itself — Cedar Garden and Cedarview Lodge share the 1200-block of Cedar Village Close and a phone exchange, and both fill through VCH assessment. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ALangley&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/cedarview-lodge
- **Balmoral House is the downsizer's Lynn Valley address.** Built in 2002 — decades newer than most North Shore 55+ strata stock — at 960 Lynn Valley Rd with the Karen Magnussen Recreation Centre and the Molly Nye House heritage site as its stated neighbours, it is the building a Lynn Valley house-seller downsizes into without leaving the valley. https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/960-lynn-valley-road/113
