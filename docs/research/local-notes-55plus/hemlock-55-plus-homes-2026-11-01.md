# Hemlock Valley (FVRD Electoral Area C) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**GEOGRAPHY FLAG, stated up front: Hemlock Valley is NOT in the City of Mission. "Hemlock" is a Fraser Valley Real Estate Board sub-area name that SnapStats reports under the community Mission, and listings carry "Mission, BC V0M 1A1" and "Agassiz, BC V0M 1A1" mailing addresses — but Hemlock Valley is unincorporated land in Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) Electoral Area C, governed by the FVRD's Hemlock Valley Official Community Plan (November 2019). It is the residential village at Sasquatch Mountain Resort (the former Hemlock ski area). Title, zoning, permits and taxes run through the FVRD and the Province — not the City of Mission and not the District of Kent. This file is titled and framed accordingly.** https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691 and https://jaredgibbons.ca/hemlock.html

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The plan area is based on the Controlled Recreation Area (CRA) established by the Province of British Columbia in 2016 as part of approval of the Hemlock Valley All Seasons Resort Master Plan, for what is now Sasquatch Mountain Resort; the CRA carries the licence of occupation that lets the resort developer use Crown land for the purposes in the Resort Master Plan. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- The village's streets, from the live page's listings, include Sakwi Creek Road, Edelweiss Drive, Mt Downing Road, Enzian Way and Snowflake Crescent. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hemlock.html
- Planning areas inside the OCP area: Hemlock West (the historic resort community), Hemlock East and Hemlock North, each to be detailed through neighbourhood plans as the resort expands. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- Distances, per the OCP: the largest nearby town is Agassiz in the District of Kent, approximately 38 km from Hemlock Valley; Mission is approximately 55 km west; the Village of Harrison Hot Springs sits at the southern end of Harrison Lake, south of the plan area. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- Community scale: the FVRD's Hemlock Valley Evacuation Guide describes Hemlock Valley, a designated place in FVRD Area C, as having approximately 200 dwellings — mainly vacation homes and short-term rentals — with Sasquatch Mountain Resort at the village's upper end. http://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD Evacuation Guide - Hemlock Valley.pdf
- The resort as the OCP records it: 4 ski lifts servicing 146.8 hectares of developed ski terrain on 35 named trails, on the south and southwest slopes, using a 100% natural snowpack; Comfortable Carrying Capacity approximately 1,150 skiers/boarders per day and Balanced Resort Capacity 1,640 guests per day. Existing accommodation base per the OCP: 226 resort residential units equating to 1,072 bed units, about 60% of them ski-in/ski-out or within comfortable walking distance of the base area. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- Access: the valley is reached by road from the Agassiz / Harrison Mills side — the FVRD evacuation guide's evacuation route runs past Sts'ailes First Nation, Harrison Mills and sites along that route. - Schools: there is no school in the valley. The OCP states the small permanent population will limit the viability of locating schools and medical services within the Controlled Recreation Area. The nearest schools confirmed in the BC Ministry K-12 file are in SD78 Fraser-Cascade (Kent Elementary, 7285 Mccullough Rd, Agassiz; Agassiz Elem-Secondary, Agassiz; Harrison Hot Springs Elementary) and SD75 Mission (Deroche Elementary, 10340 N Deroche Rd; Dewdney Elementary, 37151 Hawkins-Pickle Rd) — but which district and school a Hemlock Valley address is catchment-assigned to is UNVERIFIED. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- Transit: no public transit service to Hemlock Valley was confirmed on an official source: UNVERIFIED. Hospital and medical: no hospital or clinic in the valley was confirmed; the OCP treats medical services as outside services residents travel to. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates for this region: NOT here. The nearest verified 55+ ownership stock is in the City of Mission's urban core, approximately 55 km west (Section 2), and in Agassiz/Harrison-area communities under their own pages — none in the valley. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. No Hemlock Valley strata checked has adopted one — the candidates in Section 2 record no age restriction — so a 55+ buyer here is buying general-market resort product under ordinary strata rules. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw — the live question in a valley the FVRD describes as mainly vacation homes and short-term rentals, where the Enzian Way building's listings expressly allow VRBO and Airbnb. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/listing/r3080163-21000-enzian-way-601b-agassiz-hemlock-homes-for-sale-285000/brochure
- **Land use runs through the FVRD, not a city hall:** the Hemlock Valley Official Community Plan (November 2019) governs the plan area and the FVRD administers it; the OCP's Infrastructure & Services chapter covers water supply, sanitary sewer, utilities, stormwater, snow clearing and storage, solid waste and emergency/protective services — a resort-community system in the FVRD plan framework, not City of Mission services. Parcel-level servicing (which system a given lot is on, and its fees): UNVERIFIED. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **Hazard and permit overlays:** the OCP carries a Hazard & Risk Management chapter including a Wildfire Interface Hazard section, and Development Permit Areas in Schedule I that include protection of development from hazardous conditions. Parcel-level hazard status: UNVERIFIED. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **The expansion vision is a plan, not a bylaw promise:** the OCP and the provincially approved Resort Master Plan contemplate a phased build-out toward 19,969 bed units and a Balanced Resort Capacity of 16,641 visitors per day, with a proposed build-out distribution of 40% public (nightly-rental) bed units, 45% private bed units and 15% resident/employee deed-restricted housing — a long-range plan target, not built product and not today's tenure split (today's freehold/strata split: UNVERIFIED). https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **Property taxes are rural-area taxes, paid to the Province:** a property not located in a city, town, district or village is in a rural area, and its owner receives the property tax notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays the Province, not the FVRD. Under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11, rural taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty on any portion unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on any portion unpaid on October 31. (This is the rural regime — do NOT apply Mission's city tax rules here; Mission's flat 10% penalty applies only inside the City of Mission.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **Community organisation:** the Hemlock Valley Homeowners Association, established in 1980, is a non-profit that works on behalf of valley homeowners. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ housing in Hemlock Valley itself?** No — none was verified in any source. No age-restricted strata, seniors rental, care residence or subsidized seniors building with a valley address exists in the sources checked; the valley's named condo building and its strata townhouses record no age restriction, and one townhouse listing records "Age Restrictions: No" outright. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Hemlock Valley part of Mission?** No — for real estate reporting, SnapStats groups the "Hemlock" sub-area under the community Mission, and many listings carry a Mission mailing address, but Hemlock Valley is unincorporated FVRD Electoral Area C land governed by the FVRD's Hemlock Valley Official Community Plan; Mission is approximately 55 km west. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **Q: Can I live in Hemlock Valley year-round as a retiree?** Nothing in an age bylaw stops you — the stock is general-market. The practical limits are the OCP's own findings: no school and no medical service is viable inside the Controlled Recreation Area on its analysis, so everyday services mean the trip to Agassiz (approximately 38 km) — and there is no care residence in the valley to move into if your needs change. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **Q: Who do I pay property tax to on a Hemlock Valley property?** The Province: rural-area properties receive their tax notice from the Surveyor of Taxes Office and pay the Province, not the regional district or a municipality; rural taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid on October 31. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **Q: Can I rent my cabin out short-term when I'm not using it?** That is the valley's pattern — the FVRD describes the dwellings as mainly vacation homes and short-term rentals, and the Enzian Way building's listings state VRBO and Airbnb rentals are allowed. But long-term rental bans are the only ones voided province-wide; a strata can still restrict SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw, so any specific property's rental permission, strata rules and licensing still need parcel-level confirmation. http://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD Evacuation Guide - Hemlock Valley.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: My spouse is under 55 — does that matter here?** Not under any age bylaw, because no valley complex checked has one. The provincial spouse/partner exemption matters only inside a 55+ strata (such as the Mission complexes in Section 2), where a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident is exempt by regulation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Where would my grandchildren go to school if family lived here full-time?** There is no school in the valley — the FVRD plan states the permanent population is too small to make a school in the Controlled Recreation Area viable, so students travel to outside communities; the nearest schools confirmed in the BC Ministry K-12 file are in Agassiz (SD78) and at Deroche/Dewdney (SD75), but the catchment assignment for a specific Hemlock Valley address is UNVERIFIED. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** No hospital or clinic in the valley was confirmed, and the nearest hospital for a valley address is UNVERIFIED in this research — the OCP treats medical services as outside services residents travel to, with Agassiz approximately 38 km away the largest nearby town. Do not quote a hospital for this area without verifying the specific address. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **Q: How did Hemlock sell recently — is there a 55+ resale market to price against?** There is no seniors market here at all, and the general market is thin: the sub-area showed 13 detached listings with 0 sold and 9 Condos & Townhomes listings with 0 sold in September 2026, so no sales ratio or sub-area median exists for the month. The Mission community medians sometimes shown beside Hemlock figures are board context for a city market 55 km away, not Hemlock pricing evidence. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: Will the resort expansion create a seniors community eventually?** The plan's own build-out distribution proposes 15% resident/employee deed-restricted housing at full build-out — that is workforce housing in a long-range resort vision (19,969 bed units), not a 55+ or seniors designation, and none of it is built product today. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The mailing address lies about who governs you.** Listings in this sub-area carry both "Mission, BC V0M 1A1" and "Agassiz, BC V0M 1A1" mailing addresses — neither mailing town is the governing authority. Permits, zoning, the OCP and the evacuation plan are FVRD Electoral Area C; taxes go to the Province's Surveyor of Taxes. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hemlock.html and https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area
- **The tax penalty regime flips at the city boundary.** A Hemlock Valley property pays the rural two-step penalty (5% after July 2, a further 5% after October 31), while a property 55 km west inside the City of Mission pays Mission's single flat 10% penalty the day after the July 2 due date. Same board "community" in the stats table, two different tax systems. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01 and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **Most of the valley is not full-time housing at all.** The FVRD's own evacuation guide puts the valley at approximately 200 dwellings, mainly vacation homes and short-term rentals — a full-time retiree is joining a small permanent minority in a resort village whose services, snow clearing and emergency planning are built around that mix. http://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD Evacuation Guide - Hemlock Valley.pdf
- **The one condo building predates the modern resort.** The Enzian Way building was built in 1978 per listing data (1980 per the building directory — the sources conflict), decades before the 2016 Controlled Recreation Area and the current resort master plan; its amenity set (outdoor pool, hot tub, sauna, games room) and its short-term-rental-friendly rules are a 1970s ski-condo pattern, not a seniors-living pattern. https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/listing/r3080163-21000-enzian-way-601b-agassiz-hemlock-homes-for-sale-285000/brochure and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/hemlock-valley-21000-enzian-way
- **The build-out vision is longer than the plan itself.** The OCP/Master Plan vision — 19,969 bed units and 16,641 visitors per day, against today's 226 resort residential units and 1,640-guest balanced capacity — is a multi-phase transformation of the valley into a four-season resort community. Do not price a cabin as if the expanded resort, or any seniors component in it, already exists; the OCP proposes no 55+ designation at any phase. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **There is an evacuation plan because there has to be.** The FVRD publishes a Hemlock Valley Evacuation Guide whose route runs past Sts'ailes First Nation and Harrison Mills — a single-road mountain village with a wildfire interface hazard section in its OCP. For an older full-time resident, that road and that plan are part of the ownership picture in a way they are not in a city 55+ complex. http://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD Evacuation Guide - Hemlock Valley.pdf and https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
