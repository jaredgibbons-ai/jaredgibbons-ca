# Port Kells — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Port Kells is in the northeast corner of Surrey, on the south bank of the Fraser River, north and south of Highway 1, east of Highway 15 (176 Street), against the Langley border. It has two distinct halves: a large light-industrial district north of Highway 1, and a rural/low-density residential pocket ("South Port Kells") south of the highway. https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791
- "Port Kells" is a real board sub-area name in the Fraser Valley board's SnapStats reporting structure, and the City profiles it as one of Surrey's major industrial areas; it is not one of Surrey's six town centres, and for community planning it sits within the Guildford area's orbit, with its own land use plans. https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791 and https://flipbook-surrey-ca.cld.bz/2025-2029-Surrey-Financial-Plan/43/
- The residential sample is tiny by structure: the September 2026 SnapStats sub-area row for Port Kells showed 4 detached listings and 0 attached (condo/townhome) listings — consistent with a predominantly freehold, mostly industrial area. (Area research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/port-kells.md, citing stats/subarea-stats-sep2026.csv, read 2026-11-01.)
- Schools: Port Kells Elementary (K–7) at 19076 88 Ave, School District 36 (Surrey); the current school building opened in 1907 and is on the Canadian Register of Historic Places. For the test address 19076 88 Ave, the SD36 locator gives Port Kells Elementary and Ecole Salish Secondary. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/surrey and https://www.historicplaces.ca/en/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=11415
- Land use: North Port Kells is primarily light industrial zoning (IL) with manufacturing, construction and wholesale/distribution concentrations (roughly 610 businesses in the industrial area, per the City's profile); South Port Kells is rural/suburban residential and cluster housing under the adopted South Port Kells Land Use Plan, with ALR lands at its edges. https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791 and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ADOPTEDSOUTHPORTKELLSLANDUSEPLAN.pdf
- Transit and access: no rapid transit. The Barnston Island Ferry (free, provincial inland ferry) departs from the foot of 104 Ave in Port Kells across Parson's Channel; Highway 1, Highway 15 and Highway 17 / South Fraser Way run through or beside the area. Local bus route numbers and frequency: UNCONFIRMED from an official source in this pass. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry and https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791
- Recreation and shopping: no civic recreation centre and no retail centre in Port Kells itself; Tynehead Regional Park (Metro Vancouver) borders the area to the west, and the nearest full shopping is in Guildford and Langley's Walnut Grove. https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791
- Where 55+ housing concentrates: nowhere in Port Kells. No 55+ strata, rental, retirement residence, assisted-living residence, subsidized seniors building or manufactured-home park with a Port Kells address was verified in any source checked — see Section 2. The nearest seniors housing of any type is in Guildford/Fleetwood and the wider Surrey sub-areas covered in other files.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City plan: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900** ("Surrey Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2026, No. 21900") is Surrey's current OCP — the former OCP Bylaw No. 18020 is not the current bylaw and must not be cited as current. Area-level guidance for the residential pocket is the adopted South Port Kells Land Use Plan; the Anniedale-Tynehead Neighbourhood Concept Plan immediately south is in preparation. https://pub-e61c5ee2a8704225bfa8de0c280e8a2c.r2.dev/bylaws/082baa6293643c966caa6d2ce04a8a540be9ee7dc26a69bf824f78267b55e6ce.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ADOPTEDSOUTHPORTKELLSLANDUSEPLAN.pdf
- **ALR rules govern the south pocket's farm parcels.** The South Port Kells plan maps ALR boundary lands through the residential pocket; ALR status changes what a buyer can build and which residence rules (ALC Housing in the ALR: generally one residence per parcel, principal residence 500 m² or less, secondary suite only inside the principal residence) apply to the parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ADOPTEDSOUTHPORTKELLSLANDUSEPLAN.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Port Kells has its own DCC schedule.** Surrey publishes Port Kells-specific Development Cost Charges (2025 Port Kells Industrial DCCs and Commercial DCCs listed separately from city-wide rates) — a real line item for any industrial or commercial purchase with development intent, and a sign of how the City plans this area: as an employment district, not a residential one. https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791
- **Flood construction requirements are parcel-level.** Parcel-level flood construction level / floodplain requirements for South Port Kells lots are UNVERIFIED from the sources used in this pass — confirm against the City's floodplain mapping and the property's title before subject removal on a low-lying south-side lot.
- **City taxes (City of Surrey):** property taxes are due July 2 (notices sent end of May; if the due date falls on a weekend or statutory holiday, payment the next business day is without penalty), with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Port Kells?** None that any source verifies. There is no 55+ strata, co-op, life lease or manufactured-home park in Port Kells — the residential stock is freehold houses on suburban and rural lots in South Port Kells. A 55+ buyer here is buying a regular home with no age bylaw. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is there any seniors housing at all in Port Kells — rental or care?** None verified. No retirement residence, assisted-living residence or subsidized seniors building carries a Port Kells address in the Assisted Living Registry, BC Housing or operator sources checked; the registry's Surrey entries are in other sub-areas. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ASurrey&address=&name=&page=2&order=alr_residence_type&sort=desc
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Surrey?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I build a suite or second home on a South Port Kells acreage for family?** It depends on the parcel's zoning and, critically, whether it is in the ALR: the adopted land use plan shows ALR lands inside the residential pocket, and ALR parcels carry the ALC's residence limits (a secondary suite only inside the principal residence; additional residences only under the ALC's rules or with approval). Confirm ALR status and zoning before pricing in a second dwelling. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ADOPTEDSOUTHPORTKELLSLANDUSEPLAN.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Where would I actually shop, and where is care, if I live in South Port Kells?** Outside the area: Port Kells has no retail centre or recreation centre of its own, with the nearest full shopping in Guildford and Langley's Walnut Grove. Seniors services and care housing are likewise in the Guildford/Fleetwood/Whalley parts of Surrey covered in other files. https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** There is no rapid transit, and local bus route numbers/frequency for Port Kells were not confirmed from an official source in this pass — UNCONFIRMED. The one named transit service in the area is the free Barnston Island Ferry from the foot of 104 Ave, an on-demand barge crossing of Parson's Channel with set operating hours — a neighbourhood feature, not a commuting service. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry
- **Q: Is flooding a concern on a South Port Kells lot?** Treat it as a parcel-level check, not an area-wide answer: flood construction requirements for individual south-side lots are UNVERIFIED in this pass, and the right step is the City's floodplain mapping plus a title search for flood covenants before subject removal.
- **Q: Does the industrial side affect a home purchase south of the highway?** It shapes the area's planning: the City profiles Port Kells as a major industrial area (781 acres, roughly 610 businesses, mainly light industrial zoning) and levies its own Port Kells industrial/commercial DCCs, while Anniedale-Tynehead directly south is planned for further industrial, commercial and residential growth — so traffic and land use on the south edge will keep changing. https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791 and https://flipbook-surrey-ca.cld.bz/2025-2029-Surrey-Financial-Plan/43/
- **Q: Can I rent out a home I buy in Port Kells?** A freehold house here carries no strata rental bylaw at all; in a BC strata anywhere, long-term rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, though short-term rentals can still be restricted — and in a true 55+ building (none verified here) a tenant would still have to satisfy the age bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **One name, two unrelated markets.** North of Highway 1 you are in an IL-zoned industrial district with its own DCC schedule; south of it you are in a rural-residential pocket under a separate land use plan. Listings, stats and services barely overlap — a "Port Kells" search has to be split by highway side before it means anything. https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791
- **The board stats will mislead if read at face value.** The sub-area's September 2026 row (4 detached listings, 0 sales, 0 attached listings) is a sample too small to signal anything about demand — it mainly confirms there is almost no strata stock to trade. (Area research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/port-kells.md, citing stats/subarea-stats-sep2026.csv, read 2026-11-01.)
- **The ferry is small and on demand.** The Barnston Island Ferry at the foot of 104 Ave is a free, roughly 5-minute crossing on a small barge (capacity ~6 vehicles) within posted hours — neighbours use it to reach Barnston Island (which is in Metro Vancouver / electoral-area jurisdiction, not Surrey), not as a daily commute route. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry
- **The school is a heritage building.** Port Kells Elementary's current building opened in 1907 and is on the Canadian Register of Historic Places — the old village core around 88 Ave is the residential pocket's historic centre, in a sub-area otherwise defined by warehouses. https://www.historicplaces.ca/en/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=11415
- **Surrey-wide 55+ names will keep surfacing in Port Kells searches.** Sullivan Mews (East Newton), Westwood Estates (Queen Mary Park/Whalley) and the City Centre retirement residences are all real Surrey seniors products that search engines attach to Surrey queries generally — every one of them is in a different sub-area, and none makes Port Kells a 55+ area. Sources: Section 2, Checked list.
- **Anniedale-Tynehead is the change to watch.** The Neighbourhood Concept Plan in preparation directly south contemplates industrial, commercial and residential growth next to Port Kells — servicing, traffic and land values on the south edge move with that plan, not with anything happening inside Port Kells itself. https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791
