## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; sub-55 bylaws invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** Under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rental bylaws are still allowed. Surrey's own short-term rental rules add: owner-only hosting, owner living at the property as primary residence, business licence $358 per year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Strata paperwork is where a townhome is actually priced.** The Province's strata buying guidance directs buyers to the Form B information certificate, the Form F, strata bylaws and rules, fees and special levies — age restrictions, pet rules and rental restrictions live in those documents, not in the listing price. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/first-time-owners
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000; OCP: Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900** (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — never cite the replaced Bylaw No. 18020 as current. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **The $1.2M line sits inside the insured-mortgage range (CMHC):** homes between $500,000 and $1,499,999 require 5% on the first $500,000 plus 10% above it; mortgage loan insurance is required with less than 20% down, with premium rates from 0.6% to 4.5% of the total mortgage amount. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Property transfer tax:** 1% up to $200,000 and 2% above $200,000 up to $2,000,000 — a purchase under $1.2M meets only those two tiers. First-time buyer full exemption requires $835,000 or less (partial to $860,000); newly built full exemption threshold $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — so a townhome between $1,150,000 and $1,200,000 in this band is outside the newly built exemption on price alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Fee-range caution:** the live page FAQ's $250–$475 monthly strata fee range is the site's own copy, not confirmed against an official source in the research pass — take the fee from the specific unit's Form B, never from the page. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-townhomes-under-1-2m.html
- **Property taxes:** due July 2, 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **First-home programs:** FHSA and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits are UNVERIFIED in this file — no live CRA page stating the current limits was read in this pass.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which 55+ townhome complexes could appear on this page?** Eleven verified townhouse-form complexes (Section 2), from Glenwood Village in West Newton to Peninsula Village and The Crescent in South Surrey. The feed itself proved the point: its West Newton card at 213 7156 121 Street is a Glenwood Village rancher townhome. Whether a unit in any other named complex is listed under $1.2M right now is a live feed question — UNVERIFIED per complex in this research. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-townhomes-under-1-2m.html
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ townhome?** Yes — the provincial younger-spouse/partner exemption applies, along with the under-19 child, adult-child and caregiver exemptions in Regulation 7.01. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a 55+ townhome as an investment?** Ownership is not age-restricted; residency is. You could rent the unit to a 55+ tenant — long-term rental bans are void province-wide — but you could not live in it yourself until you qualify or an exemption applies. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What strata fee should I budget?** The one on the specific unit's Form B. The page FAQ's $250–$475 range is unverified site copy, and fees in the older 1988–1995 complexes that dominate this roster are set complex by complex. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-townhomes-under-1-2m.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/first-time-owners
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer avoid property transfer tax on a townhome here?** On price alone, in the lower part of the band: full exemption requires a value of $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 — townhomes above $860,000 are outside it on price, and the program's other conditions still have to be met either way. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: Are these Jared Gibbons' own listings?** No — the visible cards are other brokerages' listings, with Jared's contact block shown as site owner, not listing agent. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-townhomes-under-1-2m.html
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale pool?** Yes — the next buyer must be 55+, exempt, or buying without living in the unit. In the small complexes on this roster (Lakeview Estates, 20 homes) that effect is concentrated; in Chelsea Gardens or Peninsula Village the pool is deeper. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the 55+ townhouse complexes?** Complex by complex: Charleston Green listings state pets allowed; Carriage House (condo-form, so off this page) states no pets. Read the Form B for the specific complex — there is no Surrey-wide answer. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/listing/r3060854-37-6537-138-street-surrey-bc-v3w-0c4
- **Q: Why do some "55+" townhomes say only one occupant must be 55+?** Because that is how the bylaw is commonly written and how listings phrase it — The Estates at Manor Ridge states "1 occupant must be 55+" and Lakewood Heights states "only one occupant needs to meet the age requirement" — which works alongside, and is distinct from, the provincial spouse exemption. Confirm the exact bylaw wording on the Form B. ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/surrey-condos-under-800k-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: Is there a townhome-only market statistic for Surrey?** No. SnapStats combines condos and townhomes and publishes no price-band cut — anyone quoting a "Surrey townhomes under $1.2M median" is quoting a figure no permitted source publishes. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/surrey-townhomes-under-1-2m.md

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **This feed contains its own warning label.** The one 55+ card in the visible feed — a single-level rancher townhome at Glenwood Village — sits a few cards away from general-market family townhomes at similar prices. Two townhomes at the same price on this page can be open to completely different buyer pools, and only the strata documents say which is which. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-townhomes-under-1-2m.html
- **Surrey's 55+ townhouse stock is old, and that is the point.** The townhouse roster was built 1986–2003, with most complexes from 1988–1995 — rancher and duplex-style plans with main-floor primary bedrooms, built for downsizers before "55+" was the only legal age bylaw. Newer townhome complexes in the feed carry no age restriction in any source checked. ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/surrey-condos-under-800k-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **Boundary Park holds three of the eleven.** Lakeview Estates, Lakewood Heights and Lakewood Place all sit on West Boundary Drive at Boundary Park in the Newton/Panorama Ridge seam — two of them with near-identical names. A buyer who mixes up Heights and Place is comparing the wrong complex's fees and bylaws. ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/surrey-condos-under-800k-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **Chelsea Gardens is two products under one name.** A townhome buyer at 13888 70 Ave and a condo buyer at 13870 70 Ave are in the same LMS1416 gated community with potentially different sections and fee schedules — the Form B, not the complex name, settles which. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/13888-70-avenue/328
- **The page's ceiling is headroom, not a market.** The visible feed topped out at $799,900 on the cards read, against a $1,200,000 ceiling and a combined attached community median of $512,944 — the research file itself asks whether Surrey townhomes between $900K and $1.2M trade through this page at all, or whether the ceiling is mostly headroom. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/surrey-townhomes-under-1-2m.md
