## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://choa.bc.ca/resources/strata-property-act/legislative-updates/
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured home parks run on different law.** Pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and its Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003), not the Strata Property Act; the Regulation expressly contemplates parks where every site is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55, and a park landlord may refuse an assignment where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement in such a park. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019; OCP Bylaw No. 7060-2014.** Houses sit under RS-1 (s. 605 — minimum lot area 668.0 m², width 18.0 m, depth 27.0 m, maximum coverage 40%). http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **Suites and added units:** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-family and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws — the route for a West Central 55+ homeowner to add housing for a caregiver or family member on a detached lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Floodplain (south edge only):** along River Road / Port Haney the flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), no habitable floor space may be located below the flood construction level on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. Floodplain status is per-lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** for this area (Zone 4 directory, May 2025), with some buildings apply-direct to the society. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ housing in West Central itself?** No — none was verified in any source inside the 207 St–222 St working scope. Maple Ridge's 55+ market starts east of 222 Street (Fraserview Village and the 224 Street row, in East Central) plus the Val Maria park in Southwest Maple Ridge. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Are the two seniors towers on 222 Street in West Central or East Central?** Honestly unsettled: Maple Ridge Towers (11929 222 St) and Maple Towers (11841 222 St) sit on the seam, MLS sources place 223 Street addresses in West Central, and no official boundary exists for either sub-area. They are also subsidized rental, not purchases — the assignment question matters for the page, not for a buyer. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata (in the East Central buildings)?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption), with further defined exemptions for children and live-in caregivers. The co-op (60+) and Legion life leases east of here set their own occupancy terms — the strata exemptions do not automatically apply to them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC strata — every rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. In a manufactured-home park, assignment and renting run through the park rules and the MHPTA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Q: Can I add a suite to a West Central house for a caregiver or my parents?** Maple Ridge allows one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building (maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area), and eligible lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. Note the floodplain exception: s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation on floodplain properties at the south edge. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street — a Fraser Health community hospital with 24/7 emergency care serving Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows. The Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is in Haney Place Mall, 11900 Haney Place, open 7 days a week, 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/F/C/urgent-primary-care-centre---ridge-meadows
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or a buyer who purchases without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes at 55+?** The Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
