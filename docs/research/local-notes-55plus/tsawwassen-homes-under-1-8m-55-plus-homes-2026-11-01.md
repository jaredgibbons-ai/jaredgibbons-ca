## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why The Lexington's older "rentals not permitted" bylaw summary cannot be relied on without reading the current Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017** is Delta's zoning bylaw; seniors housing in Delta is typically delivered through site-specific comprehensive development zones — The Waterford's site is CD Zone No. 276 (seniors' congregate housing and congregate care). https://delta.civicweb.net/document/71542/
- **"Seniors congregate housing" is a named use in Delta's newer apartment zones.** The Apartment Residential 6 (RA6) zone added to Bylaw No. 7600 lists apartment building and seniors congregate housing as principal uses, for six-storey residential development along arterial and collector streets under the OCP's Neighbourhood Centres and Corridors designation. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future")** is Delta's current OCP — a single consolidated plan with no Tsawwassen area-plan schedule (the old schedules belonged to the superseded OCP, Bylaw No. 3950). https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing:** Delta's SSMUH rules allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide, subject to the bylaw and to any hazard or streamside permit area on the lot — relevant to a 55+ buyer in this band adding a suite or coach house. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Property taxes:** Delta's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Tax relief that matters at 55+:** the Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Will this under-$1.8M feed show me Tsawwassen's 55+ homes?** Mostly no. The band is framed as detached; Tsawwassen's verified 55+ ownership is two strata condo buildings in Tsawwassen Central (The Lexington, 1150 54A St; Tsawwassen Greene, 1140 55 St), which surface on condo searches, not on a detached band feed. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). At The Lexington the listing formulation is "one occupant must be 55+." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/1150-54a-street/201
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. Confirm the current position in the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or a buyer who purchases without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I add a suite or coach house to a Tsawwassen detached house for a caregiver or family member?** Delta's SSMUH rules allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide, subject to the zoning bylaw and to any hazard or streamside permit area on the lot. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, in Ladner — Fraser Health's community hospital for Delta with a 24/7 emergency department. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- **Q: Is Tsawwassen flood-protected?** The City of Delta is the diking authority and operates and maintains over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure — lot-level flood construction requirements still have to be checked parcel by parcel. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the Tsawwassen 55+ buildings?** Building by building: The Lexington's sources record one pet allowed but no dogs; Tsawwassen Greene's listing records one dog or cat to 12 inches at the shoulder; KinVillage's rental buildings do not allow pets. Confirm in the strata bylaws / tenancy documents. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/the-lexington-1150-54a-street and https://www.kinvillage.org/independent-living
- **Q: What if I want seniors rental instead of buying?** KinVillage: The Tides (152 homes — 106 subsidized, 46 market rental) and the independent-living courts, with eligibility including age 55 or older and subsidized rent at 30% of household gross income through BC Housing; The Waterford on 56 Street is the private all-inclusive option (106 independent suites plus 36 care suites). All rental/care — nothing to buy. https://www.kinvillage.org/independent-living and https://briacommunities.ca/communities/waterford/
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes at 55+?** The Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The two 55+ ownership buildings sit one block apart, and the building between them is not 55+.** The Lexington (1150 54A St) and Heron Place (1153 54A St) face each other on the same street; only The Lexington carries the 55+ bylaw, while Tsawwassen Greene (1140 55 St) is around the corner. Buyers routinely assume the whole 54A/55 Street cluster is age-restricted — it is not. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/the-lexington-1150-54a-street and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/heron-place-1153-54-a-street
- **KinVillage is mid-rebuild, in phases.** The Tides (152 homes) replaced the 86-home North Court building; Phase 2 at 1050 54A Street (143 more affordable seniors rental units plus a replacement community centre) is in the rezoning process as City File LU009921. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/new-affordable-seniors-housing-breaks-ground-in-delta and https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009921
- **The town centre itself is in a redevelopment process.** The Tsawwassen Town Centre Mall site (1219 56 Street, 5499 12 Avenue, 1236 55 Street) is the subject of a Century Group redevelopment application, City File LU009754 — the commercial heart of the sub-area will change around the 55+ buildings. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/237772/
- **Delta runs three seniors recreation facilities, and the Tsawwassen one is on the KinVillage campus.** The City lists the KinVillage Community Centre (5430 10th Ave) among its three seniors recreation facilities; KinVillage residents receive a free membership. https://www.delta.ca/city-hall/working-delta/volunteering/kinvillage-community-centre and https://www.kinvillage.org/independent-living
