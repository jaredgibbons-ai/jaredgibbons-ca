## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT THIS HOME TYPE

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw, and a tenant in a 55+ building must still meet the age bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **ALR housing frame — the rule that shapes retiring on the chicken farm itself.** On ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence; and at most one additional residence is permitted — up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. Local government bylaws may further regulate or restrict residences and may not be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. A separate detached retirement or caregiver house outside that frame is not a right, and subdividing ALR land to carve off a retirement lot states no general entitlement in any source used here — UNCONFIRMED as a route; it is an ALC/local-government check parcel by parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Chicken quota: UNVERIFIED in every particular in this file.** No BC Chicken Marketing Board or Province supply-management source was opened for this topic. The live page's own account — that meat-chicken production is regulated by the BC Chicken Marketing Board, that quota can transfer with registered premises or in some cases separately from the land, that both require an application and Board approval and premises certification, that quota is not included unless expressly stated in the Contract of Purchase and Sale, and that the Board generally allots transferred quota only where it is satisfied there is a Board-approved building with adequate premises — is recorded as SITE COPY ONLY, not verified fact. Verify any quota position with the Board directly before subjects are removed. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chicken-farms.html
- **Barn and environmental standards for a broiler operation: UNVERIFIED in this file** — barn construction, ventilation, manure-storage, mortality, groundwater-licensing and biosecurity requirements were not confirmed on an official source in this pass; the live page's buying checklist (ventilation, biosecurity, water and power, feed and manure systems, runoff compliance) is site copy only. Avian influenza / disease-control effects on a sale: likewise UNVERIFIED — no CFIA or Province source was opened. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chicken-farms.html
- **Farm classification does not transfer automatically.** BC Assessment farm classification is a voluntary, property-by-property program. Income thresholds: $2,500 if the operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha if larger; $10,000 if under 0.8 ha. Applications are due by October 31 for the next tax year, late applications are not accepted, and a buyer who plans to keep farming must file a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Manufactured-home pads follow tenancy law, not strata law.** Under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (the Paradise Park, Georgian Park, Maplewood Estates and Cottonwood Village model), the buyer owns the home and rents the site; pad rent may be raised only once every 12 months on at least three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for increases taking effect in 2026) plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Property transfer tax (general tiers):** 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% on the portion above $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion of value above $3,000,000. How PTT and GST split between land, barns, residence and quota on a specific chicken-farm purchase is UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-time-buyer and home-buyer program limits (FHSA / Home Buyers' Plan): UNVERIFIED in this file** — no live CRA source was read for those limits in this pass, so no figure is stated.

What 55+ homes can I buy near farm country?** In the verified roster: 35 strata complexes across the three farm-community cities — 26 in Abbotsford, 5 in Chilliwack and 4 in Langley City — plus 4 manufactured-home parks (Paradise Park, Georgian Park and Maplewood Estates in Abbotsford; Cottonwood Village in Chilliwack) and 2 life lease residences in Abbotsford (Garden Park Tower, Cascade Court). Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Can I keep backyard chickens at the hobby properties on this page instead of a commercial operation?** That is a municipal-bylaw and parcel question, not a quota question — and no municipal chicken-keeping bylaw was opened for this topic, so the permission position for any specific hobby property is UNCONFIRMED in this file. What is settled: a hobby property's coop or shed carries no quota, no board licensing position and no commercial production capacity as stated here — value it as residential/hobby land unless the contract and the board confirm otherwise. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chicken-farms.html
- **Q: Can I build a small retirement house on the chicken farm and sell the barns?** Only inside the ALR frame, where the parcel is in the ALR: one principal residence up to 500 m², a suite only inside that residence, and at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha), with local bylaws possibly more restrictive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata in Abbotsford or Chilliwack?** Yes — Regulation 7.01 exempts a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Royal Court's listing data states the rule itself: only one owner must be at least 55. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.zolo.ca/abbotsford-real-estate/31930-old-yale-road/215
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a 55+ unit for my parent, or hold it until I qualify?** Ownership is not age-restricted; residing is. The Province states under-55s can own in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in the unit, so a 55+ parent can be the resident of a unit you own. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: If the buyer keeps farming my place, does farm classification just continue?** No. Classification is voluntary and property-by-property; the buyer must file a General Application for Farm Classification with BC Assessment by October 31 after purchase, and the operation must keep meeting the income threshold for its size — $2,500 (0.8–4 ha), $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha, or $10,000 (under 0.8 ha). https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: In a park like Cottonwood Village, what do I own and what can the pad rent do?** You own the manufactured home and rent the pad under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act: increases only once a year, on at least three full months' notice, capped at 2.3% for 2026 plus the proportional levy/utility amount. Cottonwood Village sits on First Nations leased land, with listing-stated lease expiry in 2076 plus an option to extend — read the lease, not just the listing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes and https://bentruscottrealestate.com/manufactured-homes.html?utm_medium=paid&utm_source=fb&utm_id=52609658579346&utm_content=52609658626346&utm_term=52609658585146&utm_campaign=52609658579346

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **A coop is not a broiler barn.** The gap between this page's title ("Broiler Farms with Quota") and its feed (hobby homes with chicken coops) is the single biggest misread on the page: a coop carries no quota, no board position and no production capacity. Any genuinely commercial chicken deal has to be verified off the feed — contract wording plus the BC Chicken Marketing Board, whose rules are UNVERIFIED in this file. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chicken-farms.html
- **The chicken and broiler pages are the same sector with different feeds.** Both pages frame broiler = meat chicken, yet one surfaces hobby/residential cards and the other surfaces the generic poultry feed. A buyer who checks only one page gets only half the (already thin) picture — and neither feed, as read, contains a confirmed broiler operation. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chicken-farms.html and https://jaredgibbons.ca/broiler-farms-for-sale-bc.html
- **The 55+ stock and the farm stock never overlap in Abbotsford.** All 26 verified strata sit in Abbotsford West, Central Abbotsford and Poplar; the farm sub-areas (Bradner, Matsqui, Sumas Prairie and the rest) hold none. A retiring grower downsizes into the Clearbrook and Mill Lake corridors, not onto a farm road. ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/abbotsford-open-houses-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **Three "Maplewoods" in Abbotsford are three different tenures.** Maplewood Manor (32040 Tims Ave) is a buyable 55+ strata; Maplewood House (1919 Jackson St) is a Fraser Health-funded long-term care home; Maplewood Estates (2035 Martens St) is a 55+ manufactured-home park where you rent the pad. Search results blend all three — check the street before booking a viewing. https://maplewood.bc.ca/long-term-care/
- **Chilliwack's Spadina corridor holds a trap.** Ivy Green (45775 Spadina Ave) and Spadina Regency (45765 Spadina Ave) are genuine 55+ buildings; the third strata on the same street, Chilliwack Gardens at 45749 Spadina Ave, is stated in a current listing to have no age restrictions. - **Old directories still call Trethewey Tower 45+.** Sub-55 age bylaws have been unenforceable since Nov 24, 2022, and current MLS data for 32440 Simon Ave records "Restricted Age: 55+." Qualify against the current registered bylaws, not a directory page. https://geraldlucas.ca/mylistings.html/listing.r3052595-1602-32440-simon-avenue-abbotsford-v2t-5r3.106964680
- **Farm classification has a hard calendar edge.** Applications are due by October 31 for the next tax year and late applications are not accepted — a chicken-farm sale that closes in November with a buyer who misses the filing can lose classification for a full cycle. Diarize the date at subject removal, not at completion. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
