## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. This is why the 40+ and 45+ numbers on the park list in Section 2 cannot be read as strata-style age bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is the current-law reading behind Spadina Regency's listing line "rentals and pets are permitted," and it overrides any older "rentals not allowed" directory line for a Chilliwack strata (the directory line recorded against Mountainview Estates in the sibling research is one example). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000** is Chilliwack's zoning bylaw. Its Section 2 defines a secondary suite as an accessory dwelling unit of not more than 90 m² and less than 40% of the habitable floor space of the building, located within a building containing only 1 principal dwelling unit — the cap is the lesser of 90 m² and 40%. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Accessory dwelling units:** Section 4.06(1) of the same bylaw permits only 1 ADU per lot where the zone permits an ADU; the listed types are secondary suite, coach house and garden suite, and garden suites are capped at a maximum gross floor area of 100 m² by Section 4.06(1)(g). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Suites policy:** the City's Secondary Suites Policy permits the rental of secondary suites within any single detached dwelling in the City regardless of zone; a suite must be built with a building permit, meet the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new secondary suites are not permitted below the flood construction level under the Floodplain Regulation Bylaw. https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724
- **SSMUH:** Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395 is the City's small-scale multi-unit housing amendment to Bylaw No. 5000, allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing; Bylaw 5395 was adopted June 18, 2024. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3090/Signed%20Staff%20Report%20%2D%20RZ001761%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Short-term rentals:** the City regulates short-term rental operations under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf
- **City plan: Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500** is the current city-wide plan (the research file left the plan name UNVERIFIED; the sibling East Chilliwack research confirmed it against the City's OCP page — noted here as a cross-file update, not silently adopted). https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Flood:** flood hazard zoning exists in the bylaw structure (RS-FHA reserve zone, Section 12.02 of Bylaw No. 5000) and the City applies flood protection requirements and a Floodplain Regulation Bylaw; parcel-level Flood Construction Levels for Proper West lots: UNVERIFIED. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724
- **Property taxes:** City of Chilliwack property tax notices are mailed annually at the end of May and are due the first working day after July 1 of every calendar year; a 5% late payment penalty is applied for taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% for taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026 — a two-step penalty, unlike Mission's flat 10%. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually BUY in Chilliwack Proper West?** Two verified age-restricted strata buildings, both on Spadina Avenue: Ivy Green (45775 Spadina Ave — 24 condos, built 1994, LMS1336, no pets) and Spadina Regency (45765 Spadina Ave — concrete, built 1989, rentals and pets permitted per listings). Both carry a board label of "Chilliwack Downtown" — see the scope flag at the top of this file — and the better-known 55+ names (Knight Lodge, Mountainview Estates, Cottage Grove) are all outside this scope under their own sub-area labels. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Knight Lodge in Chilliwack Proper West?** No. It is a real 55+ building, but board data classifies 45660 Knight Road as Sardis West Vedder, in the heart of Sardis. If your search is set to Proper West, it should not appear; if it does, the search is drawing wider than the sub-area. https://www.zolo.ca/chilliwack-real-estate/45660-knight-road/203
- **Q: Spadina Regency says rentals and pets are permitted — in a 55+ building?** That is what its listings state, and it is consistent with current provincial law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022. The age bylaw still governs who may RESIDE there — a tenant must meet the 55+ bylaw or an exemption — and pet rules remain a valid bylaw matter, building by building (contrast Ivy Green, ten numbers away, where pets are not allowed). Confirm both against the registered bylaws and Form B. https://www.robbiejohal.com/listing/r3101299-45765-spadina-avenue-501-chilliwack-bc-v2p-1t5/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live at Ivy Green or Spadina Regency?** Yes in a strata — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent, or to move into later?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in this part of Chilliwack?** The in-area ladder is unusually complete for a small city: independent strata (Ivy Green, Spadina Regency) → subsidized independent rental for low-income seniors 55+ (Jubilee Place, 45440 Hodgins Ave, 36 units, veterans emphasis) → independent/assisted retirement rental in the hospital precinct (Chartwell Birchwood on Patten Ave, including cottage/townhouse suites; Chartwell Hampton House, 45555 Hodgins Ave; Chartwell Lynnwood, 9168 Corbould St) → registered assisted living (Birchwood and The Cascades, 45586 McIntosh Dr, both class SN in the Assisted Living Registry) — all within a few blocks of Chilliwack General Hospital at 45600 Menholm Rd. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Can a detached house here take a suite or a coach house for a caregiver or family?** One accessory dwelling unit per lot where the zone permits it — a secondary suite (capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space), a coach house, or a garden suite (capped at 100 m²) — under Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, ss. 2 and 4.06; the City's policy also permits renting a secondary suite in any single detached dwelling regardless of zone, with a building permit and flood-protection compliance. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724
- **Q: Is there a bus I can rely on if I stop driving?** BC Transit's Chilliwack system serves this side of the city, and route 52 Evans runs with timing points at Hodgins Ave at Ashwell Rd — the same intersection the hospital-precinct seniors cluster sits beside. Service levels and frequency: check the current BC Transit schedule before relying on them; no frequency figure is stated in any source used here. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=52
- **Q: Why are there no market numbers for this area?** Chilliwack is a CADREB market that SnapStats does not cover, and CADREB publishes statistics board-wide only with no sub-area tables — so any Chilliwack Proper West statistic is UNVERIFIED here rather than borrowed from a neighbouring board. https://www.cadreb.com/market/
- **Q: When are Chilliwack property taxes due, and what is the penalty?** Notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1 each year; for 2026, a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522

- **The board's labels and the page's geography name the same ground differently — in both directions.** Both Spadina 55+ buildings are labelled "Chilliwack Downtown" in board data while a house on the same street is labelled "Chilliwack Proper West," and the city's most-marketed 55+ building, Knight Lodge, is labelled "Sardis West Vedder." A buyer who searches by sub-area label alone will miss real stock on this page and be shown stock that belongs to another page. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r2939860-45775-spadina-avenue-302-chilliwack-bc-v2p-1t5/brochure and https://www.livingfraservalley.com/listing/r3086596-45726-spadina-avenue-chilliwack-bc-v2p-1t7/brochure and https://www.zolo.ca/chilliwack-real-estate/45660-knight-road/203
- **Two near-identical building names sit on opposite sides of this page's line.** Mountainview Estates (46966 Yale Rd, Proper East) and Mountview Apartments (46374 Margaret Ave, Proper East) are two different 55+ buildings with two different spellings — and both are EAST, despite Yale Road and Margaret Avenue sounding like this page's corridors. Neither belongs in Proper West copy. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/mountain-view-estates-46966-yale-road-chilliwack-east and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/mountview-apartments-46374-margaret-ave
- **One operator runs the whole hospital-corner care cluster.** Chartwell operates all three retirement residences at the Landing / hospital corner — Birchwood (45650 Patten Ave), Hampton House (45555 Hodgins Ave) and Lynnwood (9168 Corbould St) — and its own copy for each notes it is "on the same corner as two other Chartwell retirement residences," so a senior comparing the three is comparing suite types and service packages inside one operator, not three competitors. https://chartwell.com/bc/chilliwack/hampton-house and https://chartwell.com/bc/chilliwack/lynnwood and https://chartwell.com/bc/chilliwack/birchwood
- **The subsidized building on Hodgins was built for veterans first.** Jubilee Place (45440 Hodgins Ave) offers its 36 bachelor and one-bedroom units to seniors 55 and over on low fixed income "with emphasis on veterans and spouses" per the 2020 housing inventory — an eligibility emphasis a general seniors search will not surface, and one of the few subsidized seniors buildings in the city that sits on a named Proper West corridor rather than downtown or Sardis. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/Homelessness/2020%20Chilliwack%20Housing%20Inventory.pdf
- **Chilliwack's tax penalty is two steps, and this side of the city also carries flood paperwork Mission buyers won't expect.** A 5% penalty lands after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 4, 2026; and while the Proper West flat looks high and dry, the City's rules bar new secondary suites below the flood construction level and keep a flood hazard reserve zone in the bylaw — so the suite a buyer plans for a caregiver needs a flood-level check even on a downtown-side lot. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522 and https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724
