## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **The price band itself has no legal meaning.** "Under $1.8M" is a listing-feed filter, not a City, board or planning designation, and SnapStats publishes no price-band cut — there is no published count, share or median for South Surrey homes under $1,800,000 (South Surrey under-$1.8M area research file, Sections 1 and 2).
- **Property transfer tax at this band:** the Province's general rates are 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion above $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% above $2,000,000 — a purchase inside this band reaches only the 1% and 2% tiers. The first time home buyers' program gives a full exemption at a fair market value of $835,000 or less (effective April 1, 2024), and the newly built home exemption's full threshold is $1,100,000 with complete elimination at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000**, including the SSMUH framework: lots smaller than 280 m² may hold a maximum of 3 units, lots larger than 280 m² a maximum of 4 units, and eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop with 15-minute service a maximum of 6 units. The framework applies to eligible residential properties under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary. Zone names on older listings may be pre-2024: the former RF and RF-SS zones were replaced by R3 Urban Residential, RF-SD by R6 Semi-Detached, and the RM-D duplex zone was removed. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Plans:** the Semiahmoo Town Centre Plan borders the Sunnyside Park 55+ corridor; the City's plan page records it was updated to align with the King George Boulevard Bus Rapid Transit and the Surrey 2050 OCP designations — land-use pressure on the corridor's older low-rise stock should be read there. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/semiahmoo-town-centre-plan
- **OCP: "Surrey 2050" Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2026, No. 21900** is the current OCP — finally adopted June 15, 2026, replacing the 2013 OCP Bylaw No. 18020. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Strata purchase paperwork:** the Province's buyer path is Form B and Form F, strata bylaws and rules, fees and special levies — the Form B is where a complex's age bylaw is actually proved. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/first-time-owners
- **Property taxes (city-wide):** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **First-home programs:** the First Home Savings Account and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits are UNVERIFIED in this file — no live CRA page stating the current limits was read in this pass, so no figure is published here.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which South Surrey 55+ complexes are there, and are they under $1.8M?** Six strata complexes are verified: Peninsula Village, Semiahmoo Estates, Bishop's Green, St. Martin's Lane, the building at 13965 16 Ave, and The Crescent — two condo buildings and four townhouse complexes, all 1988–2000 stock in the listings checked. Whether any given unit is listed under $1,800,000 is a live-listing question: SnapStats publishes no price-band cut, so no band count or median for these complexes exists in a published source, and none is claimed here. Sources: Section 2 above; South Surrey under-$1.8M area research file, Section 2.
- **Q: Do both of us have to be 55?** Peninsula Village listings state the local form expressly — "One person 55+." Provincially, a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident is exempt in any case, as are live-in caregivers and legacy residents. Each complex's own bylaw wording must still be confirmed in its Form B. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3098715-2500-152-street-67-surrey-bc-v4p-1m8/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ complex as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, including the older "rentals not allowed" directory record for Semiahmoo Estates. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are these single-level homes, or will I have stairs?** Mixed by complex. The two condo buildings are apartment form; Bishop's Green and St. Martin's Lane are rancher townhouses with walkout basements — main-floor living, but not stair-free homes; The Crescent listings describe a main-floor primary bedroom with bedrooms upstairs for guests. Verify the floor plan unit by unit if true single-level living is the requirement. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/2533-152-street/98 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/3500-144-street/65
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Building by building, and the sources conflict in tone on this corridor: a directory records Semiahmoo Estates as pets not allowed, Peninsula Village listings state pets ok, and The Crescent listing checked allows two pets with restrictions. Read the individual complex's bylaws — do not carry one corridor building's pet rule into another. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/semiahmoo-estates-2239-152-street and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/3500-144-street/65
- **Q: How much property transfer tax applies under $1.8M?** On the Province's general tiers, 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000 and 2% of the portion above $200,000 up to $2,000,000 — a purchase inside this band never reaches the 3% tier, and a qualifying first-time exemption (full at $835,000 or less) or newly built exemption (full threshold $1,100,000) can reduce the bill at the band's lower end. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Where is the closest hospital?** Peace Arch Hospital, 15521 Russell Avenue, White Rock — in the next municipality, a short drive south on 152 Street from every corridor complex in Section 2. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/white-rock/peace-arch-hospital
- **Q: Is Amica White Rock in White Rock?** No — despite the name, its address is 15333 16th Avenue, Surrey, on the Surrey side of 16 Avenue. It is a rental care residence (independent living through licensed residential care, Assisted Living Registry class SN), not a strata you can buy. https://www.amica.ca/white-rock
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The under-$1.8M page's geography and the 55+ geography point in opposite directions.** The live page frames its band around Grandview Heights and the Semiahmoo Town Centre, but all six verified 55+ complexes are older 152 Street / 144 Street corridor stock in Sunnyside Park, King George Corridor and Elgin Chantrell — no 55+ complex was verified in Grandview at all (South Surrey under-$1.8M area research file, Section 1; Section 2 above).
- **South Surrey's best-known 55+ complex and its Sunnyside neighbours are in different MLS sub-areas.** Peninsula Village (2500 152 St, 95 townhomes) is filed under King George Corridor while Bishop's Green (2533 152 St), a few blocks north on the same street, is filed under Sunnyside Park — a buyer searching one sub-area's page misses half the corridor. https://storage.ubertor.com/cl2464/listing/Listing_780_extra/128267.pdf and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/2533-152-street/98
- **The community figures silently include another city.** "S Surrey White Rock" medians (detached $1,628,000; Condos & Townhomes $700,000, September 2026) combine South Surrey with the City of White Rock — White Rock held the community's largest attached inventory (193 active listings) — so a "South Surrey" statistic quoted from SnapStats is partly a White Rock statistic (SnapStats FVR September 2026, via the South Surrey under-$1.8M research file).
- **No new 55+ strata supply is being added in South Surrey in any source checked.** The verified complexes are 1988–2000 stock (16 Ave building 1988/89, Semiahmoo Estates 1990/91, Peninsula Village circa 1991–1993, Bishop's Green early 1990s, St. Martin's Lane 1995, The Crescent 2000) — the pool turns over listing by listing while the growth areas add general-market product.
