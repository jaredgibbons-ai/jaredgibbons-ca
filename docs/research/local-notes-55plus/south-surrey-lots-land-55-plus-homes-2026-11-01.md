# South Surrey Lots & Land — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Scope note:** this file covers South Surrey for the lots-and-land topic slug — a property-type filter over the whole S Surrey White Rock board community, spanning both municipalities it contains. **The land angle, stated plainly: there is no 55+ strata stock on raw land, and no such product as a "55+ lot."** A vacant lot carries no age restriction; a 55+ rule can only arise later, from a strata's registered bylaws if the build is strata-titled, or from park rules if the land becomes a manufactured-home park. The 55+ content of this file is therefore (a) the zoning, ALR and siting rules that decide what a 55+ buyer can build on a South Surrey lot, and (b) the built South Surrey 55+ roster the land buyer is weighing a build against. Municipal frames are kept separate: City of Surrey rules for Surrey lots, City of White Rock rules (Zoning Bylaw 2024 No. 2506; OCP Bylaw 2017 No. 2220) for White Rock lots. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT THIS HOME TYPE

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. This rule does not attach to a freehold lot — it is the rule a 55+ buyer who builds and later strata-titles, or who buys into a bare-land strata like Sea Ridge Bays, will live under. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it,** with Regulation 7.01 exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. The live-in caregiver exemption is the strata-side mechanism a 55+ buyer planning ahead for care should know; on a freehold lot there is no bylaw to exempt anyone from, and the caregiver-suite question is a zoning and ALR question instead (below). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw. **Surrey short-term rentals:** permitted, owner-only from the principal residence, City business licence $358/yr. White Rock's STR rule: UNVERIFIED in this pass. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Start with the zone (Surrey):** Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000 is the current zoning bylaw, and a lot's zone is confirmed parcel by parcel in the City's COSMOS map. The live page's old-to-new zone mapping (RF→R3, RH→R1, RQ→R2, one-acre→RA) is site copy — Bylaw 12000's currency is confirmed in this program, the mapping itself was not re-read from the bylaw. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf and https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html
- **Added units on urban lots (Surrey SSMUH), adopted July 8, 2024:** inside the Urban Containment Boundary, eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² may hold 3 units on a lot of 280 m² or less, 4 units on a larger one, and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, with exemptions including properties not connected to community water and sewer. The live page adds that a triplex/fourplex (houseplex) is allowed only in the R3 zone, on a lot with a lane or two road frontages, with no secondary suite — that houseplex detail is site copy in the research pass. For a 55+ buyer, this is the urban route to a rancher plus a suite or added dwelling on one lot. Added-unit permission is not the same as permission to subdivide. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html
- **Secondary suites (Surrey):** Surrey permits only one suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, and never inside a coach house or garden suite. The old 90 m² Surrey suite cap is NOT a current rule — never state it. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Coach houses and garden suites (Surrey):** 35 m² minimum and 75 m² maximum, rising to a maximum of 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the principal dwelling has not achieved its maximum floor area — the detached caregiver-dwelling route on an urban or acreage lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Acreage lots (Surrey RA), site copy flagged:** the live page states a new RA lot needs at least 4,050 m², 50 m wide and 60 m deep under Bylaw 12000, Part 12 — carried as site copy; not re-read from the bylaw in the research pass. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html
- **White Rock lots:** White Rock adopted Zoning Bylaw No. 2506 on June 27, 2024 to incorporate SSMUH (up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 over 280 m², up to 6 within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop). The live page's RS-1 minimum new lot dimensions (742 m², 18 m wide, 30.5 m deep) are site copy — not re-read from the bylaw in the research pass. A White Rock lot never answers to Surrey's Bylaw 12000. Source: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md and https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html
- **ALR parcels:** subdivision of Agricultural Land Reserve land requires permission from the Agricultural Land Commission, apart from limited approving-officer cases in the Agricultural Land Reserve General Regulation; a parcel's reserve status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address. Housing on reserve land: one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha) — that additional-residence route is the ALR version of the caregiver-dwelling question. Whether a specific South Surrey parcel is in the ALR: UNVERIFIED at parcel level. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Subdivision process (Surrey), site copy flagged:** the live page states a subdivision goes through City planner review, a Preliminary Layout Approval (PLA) letter listing conditions, 12 months to meet them (an extension can be requested for a fee), conditions that can include road dedication, covenants, tree permits and development cost charges, and a registered land surveyor's final plan to the Land Title Office. No City subdivision page was opened in the research pass, so the PLA timeline is UNVERIFIED beyond site copy. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html
- **Plans:** the current plan is the **Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026)**, replacing Bylaw No. 18020 — never cite 18020 as current. White Rock lots sit under White Rock's OCP Bylaw 2017 No. 2220 instead. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments and https://www.whiterockcity.ca/514/Resources-Documents
- **Servicing:** the live page states that if a new lot would use a well or septic field, Fraser Health testing must be done and the application cannot proceed if standards are not met, and that some acreage land is unserviced — site copy; parcel-level servicing for any specific lot is UNVERIFIED. Fraser Health requires a home not connected to a community sewer to use an onsite sewage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person; the provincial GWELLS registry holds recorded wells (older wells may not appear). "Serviced lot" and "subdivision potential" claims in feed cards are listing copy only. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems and https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html
- **Owner-builder and GST, site copy flagged:** the live page states an owner building their own home needs a BC Housing Owner Builder Authorization, and sketches GST treatment for vacant-land sales. Neither BC Housing nor CRA material was opened in the research pass, so both points are UNVERIFIED beyond site copy — questions for the buyer's own advisors, not advice from this file. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html
- **Taxes on a land purchase:** Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. The Province's general PTT rates are 1% up to and including $200,000, 2% on the portion above $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% above $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Tax relief that matters at 55+:** the Province's regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities) — a principal-residence program, so it speaks to the finished home, not to the vacant lot purchase itself. FHSA and HBP dollar limits are UNVERIFIED in this file — no live CRA page was read in this pass. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a "55+ lot" in South Surrey?** No such product exists as land. A vacant lot carries no age restriction — the 55+ rule, where one exists, lives in a strata's registered bylaws (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1/123.2) or in a manufactured-home park's rules, and it attaches only when a strata is created or a park operates. A lot marketed to downsizers is still a general-market lot. The one genuine land-plus-55+ case in South Surrey is Sea Ridge Bays, where the lot itself is a bare-land strata lot in an existing 55+ corporation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://realtylink.org/en/print/property/263120958
- **Q: What 55+ homes CAN I buy in South Surrey instead of building?** Six verified strata complexes (Peninsula Village, Semiahmoo Estates, Bishop's Green, St. Martin's Lane, the building at 13965 16 Ave, and The Crescent), plus Sea Ridge Bays (bare-land strata manufactured homes, own the lot) — and, flagged as a roster tension, The Greens at Douglas in Pacific Douglas, verified in this batch but not yet in the consolidated city-wide roster. No 55+ pad-rent park exists in South Surrey. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: If I buy land and build a rancher, can I make my own home "55+"?** A single freehold house has no strata corporation, so there is no age-bylaw mechanism for it. If the build is later strata-titled (for example a bare-land strata), only a 55+ bylaw is legal in BC, and it would restrict who may live there, not who may own. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: How many homes can go on a South Surrey lot?** In Surrey, inside the urban containment boundary, eligible lots under 4,050 m² may hold 3 units (280 m² or less) or 4 units (larger), with up to 6 for some lots within 400 m of frequent bus service; units can include a secondary suite (capped at 40% of the principal dwelling) or a coach/garden suite (35–75 m², up to 120 m² on RA/R2/R2-O/R3 lots). In White Rock, Bylaw No. 2506 carries the same 3 / 4 / 6 SSMUH tiers. Which count a specific lot supports is a COSMOS and zoning check on that parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: Can I add a caregiver suite or a second dwelling on an acreage lot?** On a Surrey RA lot, the coach house / garden suite route runs to 120 m² where the bylaw conditions are met. On an ALR parcel, the frame is the ALC's: a secondary suite only inside the principal residence, and one additional detached residence at 90 m² or less (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² or less (larger parcels), where Surrey bylaws also permit it. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: How do I find a lot's zone?** On the Surrey side, look the lot up on the City's COSMOS map and read its zone in Surrey Zoning Bylaw 12000; White Rock lots sit under White Rock Zoning Bylaw No. 2506, adopted June 27, 2024. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: What if the lot has no city sewer or water?** The page's guide copy states a new lot on a well or septic field needs Fraser Health testing before a subdivision can proceed, and acreage servicing varies lot by lot — parcel-level servicing for any specific lot is UNVERIFIED in this research, so confirm services with the City and Fraser Health before writing. SSMUH exemptions include properties not connected to community water and sewer, so servicing also decides the unit count. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: Are there market statistics to price a South Surrey lot against?** No land figure is stated in these notes: SnapStats publishes no lots/land category, so land sale counts and land prices per acre are UNVERIFIED. Price land off land sales, not house medians — the community's house medians ($1,628,000 detached; $700,000 attached, September 2026) describe built homes across all nine sub-areas (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report, via the slug's research file).
- **Q: What taxes do I carry into the budget on top of the lot price?** PTT in the general tiers (1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000), Surrey's tax calendar (due July 2; 5% penalty after it, a further 5% after September 2), and GST only as a per-transaction CPA question — its treatment of a specific lot sale is UNVERIFIED here. Once the home is built and is your principal residence, the Province's property tax deferment regular program (55+) becomes relevant. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool if I buy into the built roster instead?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. A freehold lot you build on carries no such restriction on resale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The two-system split is the defining fact of this topic.** An urban lot's potential is set by its Bylaw No. 12000 zone and the SSMUH added-unit framework (3 units under 280 m², 4 units above it, up to 6 near a frequent bus stop), while a reserve parcel's potential is set by the Agricultural Land Commission's housing and subdivision rules (a 500 m² principal-residence cap, a 90 m² / 186 m² additional-residence limit, Commission permission to subdivide). The same search word — "land" — returns parcels whose buildable future differs by orders of magnitude. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **This one page sells land under two different bylaw regimes.** A White Rock lot answers to Zoning Bylaw No. 2506 (adopted June 27, 2024) and a Surrey lot to Bylaw No. 12000 — minimum-lot, unit-count and suite answers differ across the municipal seam that the feed does not show. Source: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **South Surrey's only land-form 55+ product is a strata, not a park.** Sea Ridge Bays buyers own their lots as bare-land strata lots and pay a strata fee — there is no pad rent and the MHPTA does not apply — while Surrey's three genuine 55+ pad-rent parks are all in Newton / Fleetwood. A "mobile home on land you own" listing in South Surrey and a "mobile home in a park" listing elsewhere in Surrey are different legal purchases sharing a search term. https://realtylink.org/en/print/property/263120958 and ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/surrey-mobile-homes-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **South Surrey's built 55+ supply is old, which is why 55+ buyers end up on a land page.** The verified South Surrey complexes are 1988–2002 stock (16 Ave building 1988/89, Semiahmoo Estates 1990/91, Peninsula Village circa 1991–1993, Bishop's Green early 1990s, St. Martin's Lane 1995, The Crescent 2000) — no new age-restricted strata project in South Surrey appears in any source checked, so a buyer who wants a new single-level home is pushed toward building on a general-market lot.
