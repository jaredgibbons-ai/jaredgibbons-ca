## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. In Holly this is a checking rule: with no verified 55+ strata in the sub-area, any "55+" claim for a local complex has to be proven from the registered bylaws, not a listing write-up. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017** assigns zoning parcel by parcel; the urban Ladner ladder in Holly includes RS5 (550 m² subdivision minimum, or 460 m² outside the SFR designation), RS6 (390 m²), RS7 (335 m²), RD1 duplex (836 m²) and RD4 duplex (504 m²), with RS1–RS3 and RD2–RD3 at 4,000 m² in lower-density areas. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **Seniors congregate housing in Delta's zoning:** the Apartment Residential 6 (RA6) zone added to Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 in 2024 lists "seniors congregate housing" as a permitted principal use for six-storey apartment development under the OCP's Neighbourhood Centres and Corridors designation. No seniors congregate or RA6 project in Holly appears in any source checked — the 60B Street applications are townhouse developments. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/ and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/219618/
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future")**, adopted July 8, 2024, is Delta's consolidated OCP; the former East Ladner Area Plan (Schedule B.1 of the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950) that once covered this part of Ladner was consolidated away in 2024. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Suites and SSMUH — the local aging-in-place route:** the RS1 zone lists a secondary suite as an accessory use to a single detached dwelling (45% maximum lot coverage, one single detached dwelling per lot), and Delta's SSMUH amendments allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies. In a sub-area with no 55+ buildings, a suite or coach house for a caregiver or family member is the practical substitute. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Non-market rental eligibility is program-based, not age-based, at Ladner Willows.** The City guide routes Ladner Willows applications through the BC Housing Registry with no stated age requirement, in contrast to the Evergreen Lane and Marina Park buildings, where the same guide states an explicit "Age Requirement: 55+." That contrast is how a reader can tell which Delta buildings are actually age-restricted. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
- **Property tax deferment at 55+:** BC's regular deferment program lets a homeowner 55 or older during the current year defer annual property taxes on a principal residence as a provincial loan; Delta's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026 (5% penalty after, a further 5% after September 2, 2026), and the City tax page links the deferment and Home Owner Grant programs. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ housing I can buy in Holly?** No verified stock. No strata complex in Holly has a public source stating a 55+ bylaw, and the sub-area's new supply (Skye at Holly Park, 3- and 4-bedroom townhomes on 60B Street) is family product. Ladner's verified 55+ purchases are Delta Green (51 Street, Ladner Elementary sub-area) and South Pointe (Neilsen Grove). https://skyehomes.ca/
- **Q: Is Ashley Green a seniors complex? It looks like downsizer housing.** No source says so. Listings for Ashley Green (4756 62 St) market the homes to families — "steps to Holly Elementary," "ready for the next family" — with no age restriction stated anywhere. It is a bare land strata of detached-style homes that downsizers happen to like; liking is not a bylaw. https://wahi.com/ca/en/real-estate/bc/greater-vancouver/delta/east-ladner/1-4756-62-street-delta-v4k4v8-british-columbia-canada
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. If we buy in a Ladner 55+ building elsewhere, can we both live there?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What is Ladner Willows, and is it seniors housing?** It is a 146-unit non-market rental building at 5625 Ladner Trunk Road, owned and operated by Red Door Housing Society, for "families, seniors, and individuals with disabilities" per the City — mixed-household affordable rental applied for through the BC Housing Registry, with no 55+ age requirement in the City's guide. It is a rental option that seniors can apply for, not a 55+ building, and there is nothing to buy. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009635 and http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
- **Q: How do older owners age in place in Holly, then?** Through the house itself: Delta's SSMUH amendments allow secondary suites, duplexes, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies, and the RS1 zone lists a secondary suite as an accessory use. Confirm the parcel's zone on the Bylaw No. 7600 zoning maps before pricing in a suite. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Q: Is Holly behind dikes?** Ladner is low land protected by the City's dike and drainage system — over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure and 31 drainage pump stations. Any lot within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch is also in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. Flood requirements are lot-specific; check DeltaMap. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/drainage/flood-protection
- **Q: What transit and seniors services serve Holly?** Ladner Exchange (routes 601 to Bridgeport Station, 609 via Ladner Trunk Road and Arthur Drive) is the hub; no bus route with a stop inside Holly itself was verified. The free Delta Seniors Bus, home-based at McKee Seniors Recreation Centre (5155 47 Ave), serves seniors across Delta, and McKee runs programs for adults 50+. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2021-06/SocialServicesInventory%20CS.pdf
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes at 55+ on a Holly house?** Yes, if it is your principal residence — BC's regular deferment program opens at 55 or older during the current year (also surviving spouses and persons with disabilities). Delta's tax page links the program. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Holly's growth edge is townhouses, and the applications are public.** City of Delta material names townhouse development applications on 60B Street at Holly's east edge — 4726, 4740 and 4752 60B Street plus a portion of 6100 Ladner Trunk Road, and a Bassi Properties application at 4739 and 4749 60B Street — on top of the Skye at Holly Park project already selling from 4760 60B Street. A 55+ buyer waiting for a Holly seniors project is waiting for something no application currently proposes. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/219618/ and https://skyehomes.ca/
- **The sub-area name follows a school, and the school is the amenity.** Holly Elementary (4625 62 St) is the one civic anchor physically inside the sub-area; the area research file could not verify a park inside Holly by official City name — UNVERIFIED. Seniors services (McKee, Ladner Leisure Centre, the library) are all in central Ladner, west of the sub-area. https://hy.deltasd.bc.ca/
- **East Ladner's plan history explains the farm edge.** This part of Ladner was planned under the East Ladner Area Plan (Schedule B.1) of the old OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, until the 2024 OCP (Bylaw No. 8400) consolidated the area plans into one Land Use Plan — older documents and listings that cite "East Ladner Area Plan" policies are citing a repealed schedule. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Ladner Willows replaces existing rental townhouses.** The City's project page states the existing townhouses on the 5625 Ladner Trunk Road site would be demolished for the 146-unit building and that the applicant must follow Delta's Tenant Relocation Policy — neighbours and renters watching the east edge should expect a construction phase, not an addition to standing stock, first. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009635
- **The "no bus inside Holly" gap is documented, not assumed.** The area research file records a bus route with a stop inside Holly itself as UNVERIFIED after checking TransLink's Ladner timetables (601, 609, 618) — a car-free 55+ buyer should test the actual walk to a Ladner Trunk Road stop before buying, rather than trusting a "transit nearby" listing line. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- **Catchments are address-based at the farm edge too.** The Holly Elementary / Delta Secondary result in the research file is for the school's own address (4625 62 St) only; east-edge addresses near the farmland can fall differently, and each must be run in the SD37 School Locator. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Delta's own housing guide is the fastest way to check any "seniors" claim in this sub-area.** The City of Delta Affordable Housing Guide (April 2026) lists every non-market building in Ladner with its eligibility in one table — buildings with a stated "Age Requirement: 55+" (Evergreen Court, Evergreen Plaza, Marina Park) versus buildings with none (Ladner Willows, Evergreen Downs, Lynden Court). If a Holly building is not in that table with a 55+ line, and no strata bylaw source exists, the 55+ claim is unproven. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
