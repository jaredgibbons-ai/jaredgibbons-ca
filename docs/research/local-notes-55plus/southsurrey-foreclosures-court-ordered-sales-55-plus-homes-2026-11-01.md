## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT THIS HOME TYPE

### The foreclosure process — verified in this program (BC Laws, Supreme Court Civil Rules, Rule 21-7)

- **Step 1 — the petition:** "A proceeding for foreclosure of the equitable right to redeem mortgaged property or for redemption must be started by petition" in the Supreme Court of British Columbia — Rule 21-7 (1). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Parties:** in a Rule 21-7 proceeding, all persons whose interest in or claim to the mortgaged property is sought to be extinguished and all persons against whom any relief is sought must be made respondents — Rule 21-7 (2); a petitioner may also join a claim arising out of the mortgage or collateral security and a person liable to pay the mortgage debt — Rule 21-7 (3). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Later interests:** if the petitioner registers a certificate of pending litigation against the mortgaged property, a person who subsequently registers or files an interest in a land title office need not be served with the petition, is bound by an order made in the proceeding, and may file a response to petition — Rule 21-7 (4). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Information notice:** a petitioner who serves the petition must include with it an informational notice for foreclosure proceedings in Form 125 — Rule 21-7 (4.1). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Powers of the court:** in a Rule 21-7 proceeding the court may make a final order of foreclosure — Rule 21-7 (5)(a); order that a respondent must, within a redemption period that the court may fix, pay to the petitioner what is due under the mortgage and for costs, and that in default of payment the respondent is to be foreclosed of the respondent's equity of redemption — Rule 21-7 (5)(b); and determine summarily, or order that an account be taken of and that a registrar certify, the amount due — Rule 21-7 (5)(c). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **The redemption period has no fixed length in the Rule text read:** it is the period the court fixes in its order under Rule 21-7 (5)(b); the passage read states no set length. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Property types:** in process terms, any mortgaged property can be the subject of a Rule 21-7 proceeding, which by its terms covers "mortgaged property" without a property-type limit. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03

### The 55+ overlay and buyer costs

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now** (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; sub-55 bylaws invalid since Nov 24, 2022), the bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit rather than who may own it, and Regulation 7.01 exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. Strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022. A court process changes who holds title; nothing in the verified Rule 21-7 text read changes a strata's age bylaw — who may live in a 55+ unit bought through this page is still governed by the bylaw and the exemptions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Buyer costs — property transfer tax:** the Province's general PTT rates are 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% on the portion above $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% on the portion above $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion of value above $3,000,000. GST treatment for a court-ordered purchase, and any PTT exemption position on a specific purchase: UNVERIFIED in this pass. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How does a BC foreclosure start?** By petition in the Supreme Court of British Columbia under Rule 21-7 (1); everyone whose interest in the property is sought to be extinguished must be made a respondent, and the petitioner must serve the court's foreclosure information notice, Form 125, with the petition. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: How long is the redemption period?** There is no fixed length in the Rule text read in this program — the redemption period is the period the court fixes in its order under Rule 21-7 (5)(b). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: What can the court order at the first stage of a South Surrey foreclosure?** Under Rule 21-7 (5) the court may make a final order of foreclosure, order payment of what is due within a court-fixed redemption period with foreclosure in default, or determine or have certified the amount due. Do not assume every file is at an early, reversible stage — the first order can already be final. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: Can someone outbid my accepted offer at the approval hearing?** UNVERIFIED as a stated process rule in this file — the bid-rights wording in Rule 13-5 and the PD-66 practice direction remain UNVERIFIED program-wide under the standing position, including the live page's 9:45 a.m. sealed-bid account. Treat a competing bid at the approval stage as a real possibility to plan for, and get the bid instructions for the specific file from the seller's counsel, confirmed by your own lawyer. https://jaredgibbons.ca/southsurrey-foreclosures-court-ordered-sales.html
- **Q: If I buy a unit in a 55+ complex through a court-ordered sale, does the 55+ bylaw still apply to me?** The verified process text read in this program does not touch strata bylaws: who may LIVE in the unit is governed by the complex's 55+ bylaw and the Regulation 7.01 exemptions (spouse/partner, caregiver, legacy cases), whoever holds title. At Sea Ridge Bays the same strata frame applies; in a pad-rent park (none verified 55+ in South Surrey) the equivalent question would be answered by the park rules, not the Strata Property Act. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a 55+ unit through this page and rent it out?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside — and long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC strata. Two catches: the tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

SnapStats publishes no foreclosure cut, and its all-property community figures (detached at a 102% sale-to-list ratio, Condos & Townhomes at 99% in September 2026) are not foreclosure figures. Price against the general market in the property's own sub-area, not a discount myth (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report, via the slug's research file).

- **South Surrey's 55+ supply comes in two titled forms on this page's ground, and a court process treats each the same way at the title level but not at the occupancy level.** Conventional strata (Peninsula Village, The Crescent and the rest of the roster — a strata lot whose occupancy is governed by the 55+ bylaw) and bare-land strata (Sea Ridge Bays — a titled lot, no pad tenancy). There is no verified 55+ pad-rent park in South Surrey, so the MHPTA home-versus-pad split that complicates court sales elsewhere in Surrey does not arise here. https://realtylink.org/en/print/property/263120958 and ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/surrey-mobile-homes-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
