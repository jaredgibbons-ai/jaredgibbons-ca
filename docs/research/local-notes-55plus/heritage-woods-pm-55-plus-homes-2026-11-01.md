## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, whatever older building profiles (including Cobalt Sky's) still print. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: City of Port Moody Zoning Bylaw, 2018, No. 2937.** Single-detached homes sit in RS zones and multi-residential homes in RT and RM zones. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/
- **OCP: Port Moody Official Community Plan Bylaw 2026, No. 3522** (current OCP). Its Chapter 14, Section 14.3 covers Heritage Woods with the Heritage Mountain grouping, with policies to retain the existing residential and forested character of the grouping. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733
- **Secondary suites:** Port Moody's secondary suite guide states the City amended its Zoning Bylaw in 2004 to allow secondary suites in all single-detached residential zones (except manufactured home parks), and in 2018 to permit suites in the duplex zone; a suite may not be stratified. https://api.ghdcdn.com/portmoody-edocs/v1/eDocs/Get?docnumber=632150
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing:** SSMUH, including coach houses and accessory dwelling units, is permitted in RS and RT zones outside the transit-oriented areas; under the June 2024 Zoning Bylaw amendment (Amendment Bylaw No. 94, 2024, No. 3460), the City must allow up to four units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², and up to six units on such lots within 400 m of frequent bus stops. The City adopted A Guide to SSMUH on July 22, 2025. http://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
- **Creek and slope rules that bite on this hillside:** lots with a watercourse need a riparian assessment and Zoning Bylaw Section 5.4 setbacks, and environmentally sensitive and steep slope development permit areas can apply. No floodplain or ALR land was confirmed in Heritage Woods. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
- **Manufactured-home parks:** none inside Heritage Woods was verified on an official source: UNCONFIRMED for this sub-area.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ housing I can buy in Heritage Woods?** No — not on any source checked. No Heritage Woods strata is verified to carry a 55+ bylaw, and the sub-area's strata supply is thin to begin with: just 6 active condos & townhomes listings against 18 detached listings in September 2026. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/cobalt-sky-55-hawthorne-drive (complex check) — listing counts as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: Is there a Heritage Woods retirement residence?** No Port Moody residence by that name is registered or listed. A website called "Heritage Woods Senior Living" publishes generic US-style retirement content with no Port Moody address; BC's Assisted Living Registry and the Zone 4 directory list no such residence here. https://www.heritagewoodsseniorliving.com/benefits-for-seniors-with-independent-living-communities/ and https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I own a unit in a 55+ building without living there?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted anywhere in BC — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a Heritage Woods townhome?** Long-term rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022 — Cobalt Sky's building profile still showing "rentals allowed w/restrictions" predates the change in effect. A strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/cobalt-sky-55-hawthorne-drive
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the townhome complexes?** Cobalt Sky's building sources record pets allowed with restrictions. Discovery Ridge's current listings do not state a pet rule in the copy checked — pet policy there is UNCONFIRMED; check the Form B. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/cobalt-sky-55-hawthorne-drive and https://vancouvernashdom.com/port-moody-heritage-woods-townhouses/SearchResults.form/
- **Q: Can I add a coach house or suite to a Heritage Woods house for a parent or caregiver?** SSMUH — including coach houses and accessory dwelling units — is permitted on qualifying RS and RT lots outside the transit-oriented areas, and secondary suites are allowed in all single-detached residential zones (except manufactured home parks); a suite may not be stratified. Hillside lots may also trigger steep-slope or riparian permit requirements first. http://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/ and https://api.ghdcdn.com/portmoody-edocs/v1/eDocs/Get?docnumber=632150
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Eagle Ridge Hospital is in Port Moody at 475 Guildford Way (Fraser Health), down the hill from this area. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- **Q: How do residents get to SkyTrain?** TransLink buses serve the north shore and connect to Moody Centre Station and Coquitlam Central Station; the OCP supports added transit service to the north shore. Specific route numbers serving Heritage Woods: UNVERIFIED. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction elsewhere shrink my resale buyer pool if I buy into one later?** Mechanically, yes, for any BC 55+ strata: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. That trade-off is built into every BC 55+ strata since the 2022 law change confined age bylaws to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Detached demand led Port Moody here in September 2026 — attached did not.** Heritage Woods detached homes ran a 22% sales ratio (18 listed, 4 sold) while its 6 attached listings recorded no sales that month — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report.
- **The OCP protects this hillside's character on paper.** Chapter 14, Section 14.3 policies for the Heritage Mountain grouping aim to retain the existing residential and forested character — a reason no seniors tower or care campus is planned for this sub-area in the OCP. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733
- **Port Moody's seniors-housing action is all happening down the hill.** Council declined a Kyle Seniors Housing proposal at second reading on Oct 14, 2025 (5-1), then directed staff to investigate affordable seniors housing opportunities after defeating a St. George Street / Mary Street rezoning — all Moody Centre items, with nothing proposed for Heritage Woods. https://sam4pomo.beehiiv.com/p/monthly-update-october-2025
- **Kyle Centre, the older-adult hub serving the city, is being replaced.** The 1977 Kyle Centre at 125 Kyle Street is at end of life; its redevelopment's first phase — conceptual design for a proposed 39,000 sq ft facility, plaza and expanded Kyle Park — began in summer 2025, funded with $515,000 from the Community Amenity Contribution Reserve, with room kept for potential future affordable housing at the west end of the City-owned lands under a separate project. https://www.portmoody.ca/news-notices-and-community-matters/posts/city-launches-plan-to-redevelop-kyle-centre-area-conceptual-design-for-new-facility-is-first-step/ and http://www.portmoody.ca/city-government/city-projects/kyle-centre-redevelopment/
- **Both commute doors matter on this hillside.** David Avenue runs east into Coquitlam as well as west into Port Moody, so Heritage Woods buyers working in either direction use the neighbourhood differently — and neither route involves a SkyTrain station in the sub-area itself. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733
- **A single house listing here was flagged "Senior Community: No."** MLS data carried in a current Heritage Woods rental listing (119 Aspenwood Dr) explicitly records "Senior Community, No" for the property — a small, concrete example of the sub-area's non-seniors character in the board's own data. https://www.zolo.ca/port-moody-real-estate/119-aspenwood-drive
