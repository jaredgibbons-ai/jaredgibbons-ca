# Delta Manor — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Delta Manor is a Paragon (MLS) sub-area name, not an official City of Delta neighbourhood name; it covers the central Ladner grid around Delta Secondary School and Memorial Park, under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024). https://delta.civicweb.net/document/137892/ and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Ladner is one of Delta's three communities (Ladner, Tsawwassen, North Delta); Ladner and Tsawwassen together are South Delta. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Delta Secondary School is at 4615 51 Street and Memorial Park at 5010 47 Avenue — the two anchors of this sub-area. https://de.deltasd.bc.ca/ and http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/list-of-parks/lists/all-parks/memorial-park
- McKee Seniors Recreation Centre is at 5155 47 Avenue, at the Memorial Park edge of the sub-area; the City lists it as a seniors recreation facility operated by the McKee Seniors Society, opened in 2005. http://www.delta.ca/city-hall/working-delta/volunteering/mckee-seniors-recreation-centre
- The Evergreen Lane seniors-rental cluster (Section 2) sits on Evergreen Lane immediately beside Delta Secondary and the Ladner Lawn Bowling / McKee area — BC Housing describes Evergreen Court as "near the Ladner Village core," and a location map places Delta Secondary about 280 metres southwest of it. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/new-affordable-rental-homes-coming-for-seniors-in-delta and https://mapcarta.com/W699925749
- Ladner Leisure Centre, the main Ladner recreation facility, is at 4600 Clarence Taylor Crescent. http://delta.ca/parks-recreation/sport-recreation/indoor-facilities/ladner-leisure-centre
- Ladner Pioneer Library (Fraser Valley Regional Library) is at 4683 51st Street. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations
- Delta Hospital, Fraser Health's community hospital for Delta with 24/7 emergency care, is at 5800 Mountain View Blvd, beside the Ladner Exchange bus loop. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- Transit: TransLink route 601 runs South Delta to Bridgeport Station (Canada Line) via Ladner Exchange; route 618 is the Ladner North loop via Ladner Trunk Road, Central Avenue, Westminster Avenue and Crescent Drive; route 609 runs Ladner Exchange to South Delta Exchange via Arthur Drive. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt618.pdf
- Flood context: Ladner is low land protected by the City of Delta's flood protection system — over 63 kilometres of dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection, 31 drainage pump stations and roughly 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure maintained by the City. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates in this sub-area: there is no verified 55+ ownership stock here (see Section 2). The 55+ supply is the non-market rental cluster on Evergreen Lane, beside Delta Secondary and McKee Seniors Recreation Centre.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017** assigns zoning parcel by parcel on its zoning maps; the urban Ladner ladder in and around Delta Manor includes RS5 (550 m² subdivision minimum, or 460 m² outside the SFR designation), RS6 (390 m²), RS7 (335 m²), RD1 duplex (836 m²) and RD4 duplex (504 m²). https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **Seniors congregate housing is a named use in Delta's apartment zones.** The City's Apartment Residential 6 (RA6) zone, added to Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 in 2024 for six-storey apartment development under the OCP's Neighbourhood Centres and Corridors designation, lists "seniors congregate housing" as a permitted principal use alongside apartment buildings. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future")** is Delta's adopted OCP, adopted July 8, 2024; it consolidated the former area plans (the Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.3 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950) into a single Land Use Plan. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/ and http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Secondary suites and small-scale multi-unit housing:** the RS1 zone lists a secondary suite as an accessory use to a single detached dwelling, and Delta's SSMUH amendments allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Property tax deferment at 55+:** BC's Property Tax Deferment Program (regular program) lets a homeowner who is 55 or older during the current year defer annual property taxes on a principal residence as a provincial loan; the City of Delta's tax page links the program and the Home Owner Grant. Delta's 2026 tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Subsidized seniors rental runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry and the City guide.** The City of Delta Affordable Housing Guide (April 2026) is the City's own list of non-market, co-op and subsidized assisted living options, including the SAFER (Shelter Aid for Elderly Renters) rent supplement. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ condo I can buy in Delta Manor?** No verified one. The sub-area's big strata pocket, Sunningdale on 53 Street, states no age restriction in its sources, and no other Delta Manor strata has a public source stating a 55+ bylaw. Ladner's verified 55+ ownership buildings are Delta Green (Ladner Elementary sub-area) and South Pointe (Neilsen Grove sub-area) — see those files. https://rennie.com/listings/53-4768-street-sunningdale-302-delta
- **Q: What 55+ housing does exist here, then?** Non-market rental on Evergreen Lane: Evergreen Court (130 below-market homes, 55+) and Evergreen Plaza (138 units, 55+), both beside Delta Secondary. They are rentals with eligibility and application processes — there is nothing to buy in either building. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Ladner?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: How do I apply for Evergreen Court or Evergreen Plaza?** Evergreen Court applications run online through Affordable Housing Societies (evergreencourt@affordablehousingsocieties.ca per the City guide); Evergreen Plaza applications go directly to the building (Delta Senior Housing Association, 604-940-2774 per the City guide). Both are below-market rentals, so income and eligibility rules apply on top of the 55+ age requirement. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
- **Q: Is there a seniors centre in the sub-area?** Yes — McKee Seniors Recreation Centre at 5155 47 Avenue, beside Memorial Park. The McKee Seniors Society newsletter gives annual membership for 50+ at $30 (McKee) and lists the centre's programs for adults 50+. https://www.mckeesociety.com/s/McKee-February-2026-Newsletter.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/city-hall/working-delta/volunteering/mckee-seniors-recreation-centre
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day, and is there a seniors bus?** Ladner Exchange, beside Delta Hospital, is the hub: route 601 to Bridgeport Station (Canada Line), plus local loops 609 and 618. Separately, the City runs a free Delta Seniors Bus service for seniors, home-based at McKee Seniors Recreation Centre in Ladner. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2021-06/SocialServicesInventory%20CS.pdf
- **Q: Is Delta Manor in a floodplain?** Ladner is low land behind the City's dike and drainage system — over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure and 31 pump stations. Flood construction requirements are lot-specific; the City directs owners to DeltaMap for parcel-level flood information. https://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/drainage/flood-protection
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes here at 55+?** Yes, if the home is your principal residence — BC's regular deferment program is open at 55 or older during the current year (also to surviving spouses and persons with disabilities). Delta's tax page links the program; the City's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The "seniors cluster" on Evergreen Lane mixes 55+ and family buildings under near-identical names.** Evergreen Court (4625) and Evergreen Plaza (4603) are 55+; Evergreen Downs (4600), on the same lane, is family housing with no age requirement in the City guide. A buyer or renter searching "Evergreen" has to check the exact building, not the street name. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
- **Evergreen Court is the newest seniors stock in Ladner.** BC Housing's release describes 130 below-market homes — 38 accessible and 92 adaptable — built as a Canada / BC / City of Delta / Affordable Housing Societies partnership, with construction expected to complete in winter 2024 at the time of the release. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/new-affordable-rental-homes-coming-for-seniors-in-delta
- **The sub-area's seniors infrastructure is civic, not strata.** McKee Seniors Recreation Centre (5155 47 Ave, opened 2005), the Ladner Lawn Bowling Club area beside Delta Green, Ladner Leisure Centre (4600 Clarence Taylor Crescent) and Ladner Pioneer Library (4683 51 St) all sit within the central Ladner grid — the services are here even though the 55+ ownership stock is not. http://www.delta.ca/city-hall/working-delta/volunteering/mckee-seniors-recreation-centre and http://delta.ca/parks-recreation/sport-recreation/indoor-facilities/ladner-leisure-centre
- **Delta's free Seniors Bus is based in this sub-area.** The City's service directory states the Delta Seniors Bus offers a free transportation service for seniors in Delta and that its home base is McKee Seniors Recreation Centre in Ladner. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2021-06/SocialServicesInventory%20CS.pdf
- **Delta has a Seniors Support Coordinator.** The City's Social Service Provider Directory (2021) describes a coordinator who connects people ages 50+ to services and resources, including housing choices and help with government forms. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2021-06/SocialServicesInventory%20CS.pdf
- **Streamside rules bite on central Ladner lots.** Any property within 30 m of a stream — including sloughs and drainage ditches — falls in Delta's Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area, which can limit additions, coach houses and rebuilds. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **School catchments are address-based even in a sub-area named for centrality.** For the sample address 4600 53 Street, the SD37 School Locator returns Ladner Elementary (K–7) and Delta Secondary (8–12); other Delta Manor addresses must each be checked in the locator — relevant to grandparents buying with family use in mind. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
