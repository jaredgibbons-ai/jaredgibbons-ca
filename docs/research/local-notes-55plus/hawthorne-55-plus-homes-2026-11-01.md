## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata corporation may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. In Hawthorne this law currently has nothing to attach to — no strata in the sub-area carries a verified age bylaw at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; exemptions attach to a "specified resident." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents lawfully in place when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** Since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may enforce a residential rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rental bylaws are still allowed. This applies to Hawthorne's general-market stratas (Ladner Pointe, Green Gables, The Pointe) exactly as it does to a 55+ building. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017**, assigned parcel by parcel on the bylaw's zoning maps. The urban Ladner ladder includes RS5 (subdivision minimum 550 m², or 460 m² outside the SFR designation), RS6 (390 m²), RS7 (335 m²), RD1 duplex (836 m²) and RD4 duplex (504 m²). https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **Suites and small-scale multi-unit housing — the practical "55+" tool in Hawthorne.** The RS1 zone lists a secondary suite as an accessory use to a single detached dwelling, and Delta's SSMUH amendments allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — i.e., a downsizer here ages in place by adding a suite or coach house, not by buying into an age-restricted complex. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future")** is Delta's adopted OCP covering Ladner, including Hawthorne. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **"Seniors congregate housing" is a named principal use in Delta's RA6 apartment zone** under Bylaw No. 7600 (six-storey apartment development along arterial/collector streets under the OCP's Neighbourhood Centres and Corridors designation) — the zoning route a future Hawthorne seniors building would use; none exists in the sub-area today. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **Streamside permit area:** all properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area — a live constraint on adding a coach house or suite on Hawthorne lots near Ladner's sloughs and ditches. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Property taxes and 55+ relief:** Delta's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026; the City's tax page links the provincial Home Owner Grant and Property Tax Deferment programs. Provincially, the regular deferment loan program covers homeowners 55 and older, and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) covers seniors 65+. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
- **Subsidized seniors housing for Ladner runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** (Zone 3 — Richmond and West Delta directory, May 2025), which covers Marina Park and the Evergreen Lane developments south of Hawthorne. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ building in Hawthorne itself?** No verified one. Every Hawthorne strata complex checked (Ladner Pointe, Green Gables, The Pointe, Keira Gardens, Central Park, Central Park West, Oak Glenn Terrace) is general-market in every source found. Ladner's verified 55+ ownership buildings are Delta Green (51 Street, Ladner Elementary sub-area), Laburnum Park Place (Delta Manor) and South Pointe (Neilsen Grove). https://en.youlive.ca/delta-building/13200-delta-green and https://realtylink.org/en/print/property/263117720
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata in Ladner?** Yes — provincial rules exempt a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Caregivers, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in Delta Green or Laburnum Park for a parent, or to hold?** Ownership is not age-restricted in BC — the 55+ bylaw governs who may reside, not who may own. A 55+ parent can be the resident of a unit owned by a younger buyer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a Hawthorne condo long-term?** Strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022, so a long-term ban cannot be enforced in Hawthorne's general-market buildings either; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and in a 55+ building the tenant would still have to meet the age bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: If there's no 55+ complex, how do downsizers actually stay in Hawthorne?** Through the house stock: the RS zones allow a secondary suite as an accessory use (RS1), and Delta's SSMUH rules allow duplexes, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — subject to the streamside permit area on lots within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch. Confirm the specific parcel's zone on the Bylaw No. 7600 maps before pricing that potential in. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Q: Where do Hawthorne seniors actually go for programs?** McKee Seniors Recreation Centre, 5155 47 Avenue in Ladner (south of the sub-area): the McKee Seniors Society's newsletter states City of Delta seniors centres provide programs and activities for people aged 50+, with McKee annual membership listed at $30, and the same newsletter describes the Delta Seniors Bus as a free curbside service within Delta for Delta residents 65+. https://www.mckeesociety.com/s/McKee-August-2026-Newsletter.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, Delta, V4K 3V6 — Fraser Health's community hospital for Delta with 24/7 emergency care, located beside Ladner Exchange, south of Hawthorne. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- **Q: What transit serves Hawthorne day to day?** Route 618, the Ladner North loop from Ladner Exchange, runs through Hawthorne via Central Avenue in one direction and returns via Crescent Drive and 57 Street; its timetable points peak-hour riders to routes 606 and 608 (Ladner Ring). Route 601 connects Ladner Exchange to Bridgeport Station (Canada Line). https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt618.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- **Q: Is Hawthorne in a floodplain?** Ladner is low land protected by the City's dike and drainage system — Delta operates and maintains over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure. Flood construction requirements are lot-specific; confirm them with the City for the parcel. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: Can a 55+ Hawthorne homeowner defer property taxes?** The provincial regular deferment program is a low-interest loan for homeowners 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities) on a principal residence; seniors 65+ can also claim the additional home owner grant (total $845 in this region). Delta's tax page links both programs. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Ladner's 55+ map is drawn by listing sub-areas, not by the village as a whole.** The three verified 55+ ownership communities each sit in a different Paragon sub-area — Delta Green in Ladner Elementary, Laburnum Park Place in Delta Manor, South Pointe in Neilsen Grove — and Hawthorne, between them, has none. A search filtered to "Hawthorne" will return zero 55+ product even though Ladner has real stock a few blocks south and north. Sources: the MLS sub-area fields cited in Section 2.
- **The newest 55+ ownership product in Ladner is Delta Green (built 2021)** — a 24-unit adaptable-unit strata by Mastermind Developments beside the Ladner Lawn Bowling Club, Ladner Pioneer Library and McKee Seniors Centre — and its developer markets that seniors-services cluster, not the building alone, as the product. It is in the Ladner Elementary sub-area, south of Hawthorne. https://en.youlive.ca/delta-building/13200-delta-green
- **Hawthorne's own new supply is townhouses, not seniors housing.** The research file's one Hawthorne-specific development is Wildwood Ladner, a presale of 40 four-bedroom townhomes by Shato Holdings Ltd. at 4900 Central Avenue — family product in the middle of the sub-area. https://wildwoodladner.com/
- **The seniors centre's membership age is 50+, not 55+.** McKee Seniors Recreation Centre (5155 47 Ave) runs programs for adults 50+ with a $30 annual membership (McKee, per the society's August 2026 newsletter) — five years earlier than the strata age line most buyers have in mind. https://www.mckeesociety.com/s/McKee-August-2026-Newsletter.pdf
- **Delta's seniors bus is a separate, free layer.** The McKee newsletter describes the Delta Seniors Bus as a free curbside service within Delta for Delta residents 65+ — distinct from TransLink's 618/601 buses that serve Hawthorne's streets. https://www.mckeesociety.com/s/McKee-August-2026-Newsletter.pdf
- **The sub-area name is a school and a park, not a housing brand.** Unlike Delta Manor (Laburnum Park) or Neilsen Grove (South Pointe), "Hawthorne" in Ladner names Hawthorne Elementary (5160 Central Ave) and Hawthorne Park (5151 56 St) — no developer or strata has ever branded a Hawthorne seniors community, which is why searches for one come back with Port Coquitlam's Hawthorne campus and Central Saanich's Hawthorne Village instead. https://ha.deltasd.bc.ca/ and https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/hawthorne-park
