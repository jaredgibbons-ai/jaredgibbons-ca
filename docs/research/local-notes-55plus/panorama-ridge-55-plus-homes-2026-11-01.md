# Panorama Ridge, Surrey — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Panorama Ridge sits inside the City of Surrey's South Newton plan area in the Newton community: the plan area that contains it covers the lands north of Highway 10, east of King George Boulevard and west of 152 Street, with commercial nodes at Panorama Heights (152 St / Hwy 10) and Sullivan Heights (64 Ave / 144 St). No stand-alone City boundary for "Panorama Ridge" is published — UNVERIFIED as an official line. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- Nearby areas: Sullivan Station (north), East Newton (northwest), West Newton (west), and the Serpentine lowlands and Delta (south, across Highway 10). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- The 55+ stock is not spread across the ridge: it clusters on W Boundary Drive at Boundary Park, at the sub-area's west edge, in the Lakewood Place and Lakewood Heights townhouse complexes. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- SD36 schools in the area: Panorama Ridge Secondary, 13220 64 Ave; Colebrook Elementary, 5404 125a St; McLeod Road Traditional, 6325 142 St. Catchments are by home address through the SD36 School Locator — for the test address 13220 64 Ave the locator returned North Ridge Elementary and École Panorama Ridge Secondary. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- Transit: routes 321 (White Rock / Newton via 152 St and King George Blvd), 322 (Newton / Scottsdale via 132 St and 64 Ave) and 324 (Newton / Surrey Central via 132 St) run on the area grid, and the R1 RapidBus runs on King George Boulevard west of the area. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2026-schedules/april/surrey-p170.pdf
- Main road corridors: King George Boulevard north to Surrey Central; Highway 10 east to Langley/Cloverdale and west toward Delta; 152 Street and 64 Avenue. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- Health care: no hospital is inside the sub-area; Surrey Memorial Hospital, 13750 96 Ave, and the Surrey-Newton Urgent and Primary Care Centre, 6830 King George Blvd, serve the area. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Surrey/surrey-newton-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
- Land caution that shapes this sub-area: the plan area includes fish-bearing creeks — Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek and Hyland Creek tributaries — so riparian setback rules can apply to creek-side lots, and the Serpentine lowlands lie south of Highway 10. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000** governs the South Newton lands (R-series residential zones, with RA at the rural edges toward Colebrook); the Panorama Ridge research records an R3 minimum subdivision lot area of 660 m² at Part 26 Section F of the bylaw for this area. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **Plans: the South Newton NCP no longer governs.** A City update endorsed land-use and road-network changes for South Newton, and the South Newton NCP was then rescinded by the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan update — land use now follows the OCP and Zoning Bylaw 12000. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH):** Surrey allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; one secondary suite per house under the BC Building Code. This affects the detached streets around the Boundary Park complexes, not the strata lots inside them. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Strata vs freehold in this sub-area:** the verified base stock is predominantly freehold detached houses; the two verified 55+ complexes are strata townhouse product under the Strata Property Act, with monthly fees, levies and shared common-property obligations. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Property taxes (city-wide, not area-specific):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Panorama Ridge?** Two strata townhouse complexes verified in this research, both on W Boundary Drive at Boundary Park: Lakewood Heights (6211 W Boundary Drive) and Lakewood Place (6061 W Boundary Drive). No 55+ condo building, rental building, retirement residence or subsidized complex was verified inside the sub-area. https://sothebysrealty.ca/en/property/british-columbia/region-fraser-valley/surrey-real-estate/3076580/6211-w-boundary-drive-no13/ and https://www.realtyexecutives.com/propertydetails/23-6061-boundary-west-drive-surrey-british-columbia-v3x2a6-canada-r3078728-lid279223779
- **Q: Do both of us have to be 55?** At Lakewood Heights, listings state only one occupant needs to meet the age requirement. Provincially, a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption) is exempt in any case — confirm the complex's own bylaw wording in the Form B. https://sothebysrealty.ca/en/property/british-columbia/region-fraser-valley/surrey-real-estate/3076580/6211-w-boundary-drive-no13/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ complex as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is Lakeview Estates on the same street part of Panorama Ridge?** Its homes sit on W Boundary Drive, but MLS listings file Lakeview Estates (6090 W Boundary Drive) under West Newton, not Panorama Ridge — expect to find its listings on the West Newton 55+ page, not this one. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6090-w-boundary-drive/104
- **Q: Is Cedars at Panorama Ridge a seniors residence I can move into?** No. BC's Assisted Living Registry records 6811 135A St as a supportive-recovery residence (class SU), not a seniors residence — the "Panorama Ridge" in its name is a location label, not a seniors-housing offer. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ASurrey&address=&name=&page=2&order=alr_residence_type&sort=desc
- **Q: Are these rancher-style homes, or will I have stairs?** The verified Boundary Park product is two-storey townhouse form — the Lakewood Heights listing describes a 3-bed, 3-bath, 2,230 sq ft end unit, and the Lakewood Place listing describes a triplex-style end unit with three bedrooms up. A buyer who needs single-level living should verify the floor plan unit by unit; no verified single-level 55+ complex was found in this sub-area. https://sothebysrealty.ca/en/property/british-columbia/region-fraser-valley/surrey-real-estate/3076580/6211-w-boundary-drive-no13/ and https://thelunders.com/recip.html/1289584.search?_pg=3
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What should I check on a Panorama Ridge lot or townhouse near the creeks?** The plan area includes fish-bearing creeks (Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek, Hyland Creek tributaries), so riparian setback rules can limit building envelopes on creek-side lots — check City mapping and development permit areas before valuing an addition or rebuild. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Q: Where is the closest hospital and urgent care?** Neither is inside the sub-area: Surrey Memorial Hospital is at 13750 96 Ave and the Surrey-Newton Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 6830 King George Blvd. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Surrey/surrey-newton-urgent-and-primary-care-centre

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **All of Panorama Ridge's verified 55+ ownership stock is on one short drive.** Lakewood Place (6061) and Lakewood Heights (6211) on W Boundary Drive are the whole verified list — there is no 55+ condo tower, rental building or care residence inside the sub-area to fall back on if neither complex has a listing. https://sothebysrealty.ca/en/property/british-columbia/region-fraser-valley/surrey-real-estate/3076580/6211-w-boundary-drive-no13/ and https://www.realtyexecutives.com/propertydetails/23-6061-boundary-west-drive-surrey-british-columbia-v3x2a6-canada-r3078728-lid279223779
- **The same drive is split between two MLS sub-areas.** Lakeview Estates at 6090 W Boundary Drive is filed under West Newton while its two neighbours are filed under Panorama Ridge — a buyer searching only one sub-area's 55+ page will miss a third of the Boundary Park cluster. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6090-w-boundary-drive/104
- **"Ideal place for seniors" in a listing is not an age bylaw.** Panorama Mews (5867 129 St) uses that phrase for a main-floor-layout townhouse, but no source states an age restriction for the complex — only a filed 55+ bylaw (or an MLS restricted-age field) makes a complex 55+. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r2989114-18-5867-129-street-surrey-bc-v3x-0j4
- **The sub-area's namesake care-sounding building is a recovery residence.** Cedars at Panorama Ridge (6811 135A St) is class SU (supportive recovery) in the provincial Assisted Living Registry — Panorama Ridge has no registered seniors assisted-living residence of its own. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ASurrey&address=&name=&page=2&order=alr_residence_type&sort=desc
- **The governing plan changed under this sub-area.** The South Newton NCP was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update, so a seller quoting South Newton NCP densities or designations is quoting a document that is no longer in effect; the OCP and Zoning Bylaw 12000 are the live sources. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **The newest townhouse stock here is family product, not seniors product.** Nora (12830 62 Ave, 2022) and Steller Panorama (5858 132 St, 2022) added 81 townhouses in 2022 with no age restriction found in any source — Panorama Ridge's 55+ supply is still the older Boundary Park complexes. https://www.condosurrey.com/nora-12830-62-avenue-surrey and https://condos.ca/surrey/steller-panorama-5858-132-st
