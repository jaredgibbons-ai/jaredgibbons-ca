# East Central, Maple Ridge — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**This file adapts the program's earlier east-central-maple-ridge-guide notes to the live page slug `east-central`; the building research is the same verified stock. Plain finding up front: East Central holds nearly all of Maple Ridge's 55+ stock — the Fraserview Village strata pocket south of downtown, the 224 Street retirement row, and the 119 Avenue / 229–230 Street pocket — including the city's only verified 55+ strata buildings, its 55+ manufactured-home park, a 60+ housing co-operative (note the age bar) and Legion life leases.**

- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates: three pockets — (1) the Fraserview Village pocket south of the downtown core around 116 Avenue and 225–227 Streets (four 55+ strata buildings sharing one amenity building, plus Fraserwood beside them); (2) the 224 Street retirement row at the downtown core (Sunwood at 12241, Chartwell Willow at 12275, Legion Gardens at 12127); and (3) the 119 Avenue / 229–230 Street pocket east of downtown (Maple Ridge Seniors Village at 22141 119 Ave, Haney Pioneer Village Co-op at 11830 230 St, Alouette Retirement Mobile Home Park at 12868 229 St, Greystone Manor at 11657 Ritchie Ave). Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Unlike most Maple Ridge sub-areas, the 55+ product here is real and dense — East Central carried 120 active Condos & Townhomes listings in September 2026, the largest attached inventory of any Maple Ridge sub-area (SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, cited in the research file). ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/east-central.md
- Health: Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, Maple Ridge — a Fraser Health community hospital with 24/7 emergency care serving Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows; the Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 121-11900 Haney Place. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://choa.bc.ca/resources/strata-property-act/legislative-updates/
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **SOURCE CONFLICT — FLAG, do not smooth over:** building sources for Rosewood Court (Fraserview Village) still record its bylaws as including "rentals not allowed." Under the provincial change above, a residential rental-restriction bylaw has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 regardless of what an older bylaw set or a building-data site still displays. Have the strata's current bylaws and a Form B reviewed rather than rely on either the building-data summary or a listing. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/rosewood-court-22611-116-ave
- **Manufactured home parks run on different law.** Pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and its Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003), not the Strata Property Act. The Regulation expressly contemplates parks where every site is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 (or two or more tenants, at least one of whom has reached 55), per the Human Rights Code s. 10(2)(b)(i) permitted age requirements — and a park landlord may refuse an assignment or sublease where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement in such a park. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Co-op and life-lease age rules are contract/membership rules, not strata bylaws.** Haney Pioneer Village's 60+ threshold and the Legion life leases' terms are set by those organizations' own documents; the provincial strata exemptions (spouse, caregiver, legacy) do not automatically apply to them — read the occupancy agreement or life lease itself. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019; OCP Bylaw No. 7060-2014.** Houses sit under RS-1 (s. 605 — minimum lot area 668.0 m², width 18.0 m, depth 27.0 m, maximum coverage 40%); Town Centre parcels carry Town Centre commercial, multi-family and comprehensive development zones under the same bylaw. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **Transit-Oriented Area Designation Bylaw No. 8001-2024** covers the Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station TOAs inside the Town Centre plan area — the provincially driven density framework around the two transit anchors nearest the 55+ cluster. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- **Suites and added units:** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-family and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Floodplain (south edge only):** along River Road / Port Haney the flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), a covenant on title identifies construction in the floodplain, no habitable floor space may be located below the flood construction level on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. The Fraserview Village pocket is north of the river flats; floodplain status is per-lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** for this area (Zone 4 directory, May 2025), with some buildings apply-direct to the society — the directory gives the route per building. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What exactly am I buying at Fraserview Village?** A freehold strata lot in one of four buildings (Rosewood Court, Emerald Manor, The Willows, Castlemount) that share one amenity building — indoor pool, hot tub, recreation/exercise centre, clubhouse, workshop and a rentable guest suite — with strata fees covering access. Unit counts and strata plan numbers per building are UNCONFIRMED in the sources checked; confirm on the Form B. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: The co-op says 60+, not 55+. Is that a mistake?** No. Haney Pioneer Village Co-operative (11830 230 St, 84 townhouse units) is a 60+ co-op in every source — CHF BC and the Seniors Resource Directory both state 60+. It is also a share-purchase co-op, not a strata: different age bar, different legal form, different resale rules. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Q: What is a life lease at Legion Gardens?** A purchased leasehold interest for life under the Legion's own terms — you buy the right to live in the suite (Legion Gardens: 56 suites from 1994, per the Legion case study), not a strata lot or freehold title. Resale and buy-back terms are in the Legion's documents and are UNCONFIRMED from public sources — read them before valuing a unit. https://dev.ibabchyperarticles.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Seniors-Housing-Development-2010.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption), with further defined exemptions for children and live-in caregivers. Note this is the STRATA rule; the co-op and life-lease buildings set their own occupancy terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC strata — every rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, whatever an older building summary still shows (Rosewood Court's included). The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. In the Alouette park, renting and assignment run through the park rules and the MHPTA instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Q: What does the Alouette park actually cost to carry?** Listing-stated figures for one current listing: pad rental fee $713.31, city taxes $457.37, metered water billed quarterly — plus the park's stated rules (no dogs, indoor cat only, 2 residents per unit, "no financing permitted in this community"). All listing-stated: confirm against the tenancy agreement and park rules. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3067037-12868-229th-street-18-maple-ridge-bc-v2x-6r1/brochure
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street — a Fraser Health community hospital with 24/7 emergency care serving Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows. The Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is in Haney Place Mall, 11900 Haney Place, open 7 days a week, 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/F/C/urgent-primary-care-centre---ridge-meadows
- **Q: What transit serves the 55+ cluster?** The Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station (West Coast Express) are the Town Centre anchors — the City's Transit-Oriented Area Designation Bylaw No. 8001-2024 covers both TOAs inside the Town Centre plan area, which is the ground the Fraserview / 224 Street cluster sits beside. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- **Q: Is the Fraserview pocket in the floodplain?** The City's floodplain rules bite at the south edge (River Road / Port Haney: flood construction level 6.7 m geodetic, no habitable floor space below it, no suites on floodplain properties under s. 402.8(h)). The Fraserview pocket is north of the river flats; floodplain status is per-lot — check the specific lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Building by building: The Willows' listing records 2 cats or 2 dogs, or 1 of each; the Alouette park listing states no dogs, indoor cat only; Chartwell Willow (rental) is pet-friendly. Confirm in the bylaws / park rules for the specific purchase. Sources: Section 2 above.

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Four strata buildings, one amenity building.** Fraserview Village's 55+ buildings share a single amenity building (pool, hot tub, clubhouse, workshop, guest suite) at the village address 22610 116 Ave in the Seniors Resource Directory — a buyer comparing fees across the four buildings is partly paying for the same shared facility four different ways. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/MRN/180831/MRNN180831_Z38.pdf
- **The 222 Street edge is genuinely ambiguous.** Maple Ridge Towers (11929 222 St) and Maple Towers (11841 222 St) sit on the seam between this sub-area and West Central; MLS sources place 223 Street addresses in "West Central," so the two subsidized towers' sub-area assignment is UNCONFIRMED and they are carried in this file for their cluster, with the flag stated. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **The Legion runs a small housing system, not one building.** Branch 88 owns and operates two rental facilities (Earl Haig and Legion House) and two life-lease facilities (Legion Gardens and Legion Manor) — and Legion Manor's street address does not appear in any source checked, so it cannot be mapped from public sources alone. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/MRN/190830/MRNN190830_Z31.pdf
- **A directory ghost sits on the retirement row.** "Panorama by ECRA," 12148 224 St, appears in the 2018 Seniors Resource Directory and nowhere current — a buyer handed an old directory will ask for a building that no 2026 source confirms exists. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/MRN/180831/MRNN180831_Z38.pdf
- **The Town Centre plan quietly governs the downtown half of this sub-area.** There is no City plan named "East Central" — the downtown part of the MLS sub-area is the City's Town Centre Area Plan ground, whose boundaries were expanded in the plan update to incorporate River Bend. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
