## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. Most homes in this price band are freehold, where strata age law does not apply at all — there is no age-restriction mechanism of the strata kind on a freehold detached lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **ALR rules are the rules that bite at this band.** Properties in the Agricultural Land Reserve are subject to the Agricultural Land Commission Act and ALR regulations, ALC approval may be required before the City can approve related development applications, and the City directs owners to check ALR status on the City Webmap; an ALC application may be required for a new house, coach house or suite, for placing fill or removing soil, for non-farm uses, and for subdivision or a boundary adjustment. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **ALC house-size rules on ALR land:** the ALC's housing guidance caps a principal residence at a total floor area of 500 m² or less (with no more than 1,000 m² of fill), permits a secondary suite in the ALR only when it is located within the principal residence, and limits an additional residence to up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm Home Plate:** the OCP includes Development Permit Area 4 – Farm Home Plate, to reduce the long-term impact of residential uses on agricultural land, and a Development Permit is required when the Farm Home Plate regulations in the Zoning Bylaw are not met. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Development Permit Areas — wording conflict flagged:** the live page describes "Development Permit Area 2 (Hillside and Upland)" for upland estates. The OCP Schedule D list names D-2 as "Hillside Environment" and carries a separate D-5 "Hillside Development" — the live page's DPA2 name and single-area framing are UNVERIFIED against the current OCP list and should not be copied. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-homes-over-1-8m.html and https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Suites and accessory dwellings (city-wide, Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, ss. 2, 4.06, 5.04):** a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling, one accessory dwelling unit is permitted per lot, garden suites are capped at 100 m², and the bylaw's parking rules sit at s. 5.04. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Property transfer tax at this band:** the Province's general PTT rates are 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% on the portion above $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% on the portion above $2,000,000, with a further 2% tax on the residential portion of value above $3,000,000. First-time-buyer and newly-built exemption thresholds: UNVERIFIED in this file — those figures are not stated here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Property taxes:** City of Chilliwack property tax notices are mailed annually at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; a 5% late payment penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% penalty to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **Cultus Lake tenure and rural servicing: UNVERIFIED as general statements.** The live page states Cultus Lake includes both leasehold homes inside Cultus Lake Park and freehold homes in the Lindell Beach / Cultus Lake East side, and that most rural acreages use private septic and private wells or rural water systems — no official source confirming either pattern was opened in the underlying research; tenure and servicing are per-parcel checks on title and with the City. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-homes-over-1-8m.html

- **Q: Is there a 55+ complex in Chilliwack with homes over $1.8M?** None verified. A home over $1.8M in Chilliwack is, on the sources checked, a general-market home with no strata age bylaw. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: How many Chilliwack homes are for sale over $1.8M right now?** That figure cannot be stated from the sources used here. No source publishes a price-band cut for Chilliwack, CADREB publishes statistics board-wide only, and SnapStats does not cover the city; the live page's own feed is the only place the current band count can be seen, and it changes as listings come and go. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-homes-over-1-8m.html and https://www.cadreb.com/market/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Does that matter at this price level?** Only if the home is in a 55+ strata — and none in this band is verified as one. In a 55+ strata, Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); on a freehold acreage or house there is no strata age bylaw at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can ALR rules affect a Chilliwack acreage over $1.8M?** Yes, where the parcel is in the Agricultural Land Reserve: ALR properties are subject to the Agricultural Land Commission Act and regulations, ALC approval may be required before the City can approve related development, and an ALC application may be required for a new house, coach house or suite, fill or soil removal, a non-farm use, or a subdivision or boundary adjustment; check a parcel on the City Webmap. The ALC's floor-area caps are stated in Section 3. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What permit areas matter for a hillside or farm property in this band?** The City's 2050 Official Community Plan (Bylaw 2025, No. 5500) governs, including its Schedule B neighbourhood plans and Schedule D development permit areas — D-2 Hillside Environment, D-5 Hillside Development and D-4 Farm Home Plate among them; which areas apply is a per-parcel check with the City. Note the current OCP list does not use the live page's "DPA2 (Hillside and Upland)" name. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Q: How much property transfer tax will I pay on a home over $1.8M?** Under the Province's general PTT rates — 1% on the first $200,000, 2% on the portion above $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% on the portion above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion of value above $3,000,000 — a home in this band is taxed mostly in the 2% tier, with only the value above $2,000,000 in the 3% tier. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact tax before you offer; exemption thresholds are UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Can a caregiver or family member live in a second dwelling on an acreage?** The ALC permits one additional residence without an application where its size rules are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — and City bylaws must also permit it; a secondary suite in the ALR is permitted only within the principal residence. There is no strata-style caregiver exemption because there is no strata. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: On a Cultus Lake property, what should I check first?** Tenure, on title. The live page itself flags a leasehold/freehold split at the lake, and that split is UNVERIFIED in this file — the title search is the check that settles it for a specific property. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-homes-over-1-8m.html
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Chilliwack General Hospital, 45600 Menholm Rd — a Fraser Health community hospital with emergency and acute care, on the valley floor in the urban core; the Chilliwack Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at #104 - 7955 Evans Road. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital
- **Q: If I buy a general-market acreage now and need care later, what is in Chilliwack?** The registered assisted-living residences and retirement residences are in the urban core and Sardis (Chartwell Birchwood, Hampton House and Lynnwood in the hospital precinct; The Cascades on McIntosh Drive; Waverly Seniors Village on south Young Road), and the subsidized seniors buildings are downtown/central — the care ladder exists in the city, just not in the hillside or acreage pockets. Sources: Section 2 above.
