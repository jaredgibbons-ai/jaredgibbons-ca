## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** People under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents who were living in the unit when the bylaw passed; live-in caregivers; and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted, and a tenant in a 55+ townhome must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Pet and rental specifics are complex-by-complex.** The live page's generalization that most newer family townhome complexes allow two pets and permit long-term rentals is UNVERIFIED as a blanket claim — read the specific strata's registered bylaws and Form B. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-townhomes-under-1-2m.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **No townhome-only statistic exists in the permitted source.** SnapStats splits attached product only as Condos & Townhomes combined (Abbotsford median $497,500, September 2026) — that combined median is condo-weighted context and must never be quoted as a townhome price (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report).
- **Zoning frame:** Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 governs; its framework defines "Congregate Housing" (added by Bylaw 3249-2022) as the seniors supportive-housing use class, and s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings — suites are not a strata-townhome feature, and no suite claim is made for any complex in Section 2. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-abbotsford-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/7217/8514/3396/d9117e5011287c79b090adc71e32c234_Appendix_B_-_Draft_ZB_Amendments.pdf and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **The governing plan:** Abbotsforward 2050, the City's Official Community Plan (Bylaw 2600-2016, as updated). https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2016b2600schedulee
- **Manufactured-home pads follow tenancy law, not strata law.** Under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, pad rent can be increased only once every 12 months, on at least three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026) plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **FHSA and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits: UNVERIFIED** — no live CRA page stating those limits was read for this file, so no FHSA or HBP figure is stated here.
- **Property taxes:** Abbotsford taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

- **Q: What is the median townhome price in Abbotsford?** SnapStats does not publish one — it combines condos and townhomes, and the combined Abbotsford median was $497,500 in September 2026. - **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ townhome?** Yes, in law: Regulation 7.01 exempts a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident, and The Wedgewood's own listing data states only one resident needs to be over 55. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.zolo.ca/abbotsford-real-estate/2626-countess-street/106
- **Q: I want no stairs. Which 55+ townhome stock is rancher form?** Downes Road Estates is the verified rancher-form option — a gated bare land strata of rancher-style homes (plan LMS2380). Whether any specific unit in the other townhome complexes is master-on-main is a per-listing question: UNCONFIRMED at complex level. https://www.estateblock.com/abbotsford/16-32250-downes-rd-518712
- **Q: Can I rent out my 55+ townhome?** For a long-term tenancy, yes: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022. Your tenant must still meet the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. Stall availability and any extra charge are not stated in the sources — confirm with the strata. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3085200-61-32959-george-ferguson-way-abbotsford-bc-v2s-7w9
