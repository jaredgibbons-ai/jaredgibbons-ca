## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why the Manor Ridge directory line "rentals not allowed" cannot be read at face value. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Open-house representation rule:** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report, provides that a disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Representation duties generally:** BCFSA states that in BC a licensed real estate professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party to the transaction. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Identification or sign-in for open-house visitors:** no BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source found states an identification or sign-in requirement — UNVERIFIED as a rule; an individual host may still ask, on the seller's instructions (slug's research file, Section 4).
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000** governs Cloverdale, including the small-scale multi-unit housing framework: up to 3 units on lots under 280 sq m, up to 4 units on lots over 280 sq m, and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. **OCP: "Surrey 2050" Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2026, No. 21900**, finally adopted June 15, 2026, replacing the 2013 OCP Bylaw No. 18020. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **Short-term rentals in Surrey:** a short-term rental is under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at $358 per year is required before operating — on top of any strata short-term rental bylaw. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Property taxes (city-wide):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **First-home programs:** the First Home Savings Account and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits are UNVERIFIED in this file — no live CRA page stating the current limits was read in this pass, so no figure is published here.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Cloverdale — and will I find them at an open house?** Two strata complexes verified in this research: Carriage House (17695 58 Ave, a 1987 condo building in downtown Cloverdale) and The Estates at Manor Ridge (6140 192 St, a 1989 townhouse complex). Whether either holds advertised open houses in a given week is a feed question, not a stock question — the open-house feed's first page read for the slug's research was Clayton and Cloverdale general-market townhouses, detached houses and a condo, with no 55+ complex named on it. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/17695-58-avenue/102 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8 and https://jaredgibbons.ca/cloverdale-open-houses.html
- **Q: Do I need my own agent to attend a Cloverdale open house?** No official source requires it. Rule 5-10(3) excuses disclosure of representation where a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or answering general factual questions — but the moment you share your motivation, financial qualifications or needs, that changes, and BCFSA requires unrepresented consumers to be told the risks of dealing with a licensee who represents the other party. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download and https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: How do I know the open-house time on the page is current?** Check it carefully and confirm it: on the labelled cards read for the slug's research, the card's top time block (labelled PST) is exactly one hour later than the "Open House on..." line under it, and the time inside the listing text agrees with the block in four of five text-bearing cases and with neither in the fifth — confirm the time with the listing brokerage before you go. Which of the stated times is authoritative is UNVERIFIED. https://jaredgibbons.ca/cloverdale-open-houses.html
- **Q: Does every listing on the Cloverdale open-houses page hold an open house?** Not on the evidence of the first page: 11 of the 12 cards carried an open-house time block and one Clayton townhouse card carried no open-house label or time at all in the page text, so treat an unlabelled card as a listing to confirm privately, not as an open house. https://jaredgibbons.ca/cloverdale-open-houses.html
- **Q: Do I have to sign in or show ID at an open house?** No BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source states an identification or sign-in requirement for open house visitors — UNVERIFIED as a rule; an individual host may still ask, on the seller's instructions. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata we see listed?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). At Manor Ridge, listings state 1 occupant must be 55+ in terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8
- **Q: Can I have a pet in Cloverdale's 55+ complexes?** The two complexes are opposites in the sources checked: Carriage House listings state "No pets permitted," while Manor Ridge listings allow 1 pet with restrictions. There is no Cloverdale-wide pet rule — read the individual complex's bylaws before you tour. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/17695-58-avenue/102 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ complex as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, including any older "rentals not allowed" record for Manor Ridge. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **This feed's own cards disagree with themselves about the time, by exactly one hour.** On every labelled entry read for the slug's research (12 entries across 11 labelled cards), the card's top time block is exactly one hour later than the "Open House on..." line directly under it — and no labelled card's listing text matched the "Open House on..." line. The same one-hour pattern was found on the Langley and Tsawwassen open-house feeds in this program; Cloverdale's feed was checked on its own cards. https://jaredgibbons.ca/cloverdale-open-houses.html
- **One card in twelve on an "open houses" page is not an open house.** A Clayton townhouse card on the first page carried no open-house label or time at all in the page text — the same card appears unlabelled on the Clayton area page's feed. https://jaredgibbons.ca/cloverdale-open-houses.html
- **The 55+ stock and the open-house feed barely intersect.** Cloverdale's verified 55+ ownership supply is two 1980s complexes (Carriage House, Manor Ridge); the feed's first page was general-market Clayton and Cloverdale product. A 55+ buyer who shops only this feed can tour all weekend without seeing an age-restricted home, and a seller in a 55+ complex should not assume this feed is where their buyer comes from. Sources: Section 2 above and https://jaredgibbons.ca/cloverdale-open-houses.html
- **Cloverdale's seniors rental sits one block from its seniors condo.** Bethshan Gardens (17528 59 Ave, society-owned rental) and Carriage House (17695 58 Ave, strata ownership) are both in the downtown core — neither is an open-house product, but both are the actual seniors housing a Cloverdale open-house weekend is usually a first step toward. http://bethshangardens.org/
- **The city-wide Surrey 55+ notes understate Cloverdale.** The Surrey city-wide file in this series lists Manor Ridge as Cloverdale's only verified complex; Carriage House was verified separately in this series from three independent listings (R3164723, R3080712, R3089060) that all state 55+ in terms.
- **Sellers: open-house traffic is not a measured market statistic here.** The community context behind a Cloverdale open-house weekend is a detached market at 98% sale-to-list with 19 days on market and an attached market at 96% with 32 days in September 2026 — price to those segments, not to open-house traffic, which no official source measures (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via the slug's research file).
