# Vancouver Heights (Burnaby) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**READ THIS FIRST — THE NAME LIES: Vancouver Heights is in the CITY OF BURNABY, not the City of Vancouver.** It is a North Burnaby neighbourhood east of Boundary Road, on and below the Capitol Hill rise, with Hastings Street as its spine. The City of Vancouver neighbourhood across Boundary Road is Hastings-Sunrise. Burnaby bylaws, Burnaby property taxes, Burnaby schools (School District 41) and Burnaby market statistics apply here — nothing City of Vancouver does. The City of Burnaby's planning name for the Hastings Street village centre in this neighbourhood is the "Heights Urban Village." Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01) and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The neighbourhood frame: Boundary Road to the west, Gamma Avenue to the east, Hastings Street to the south, and Burrard Inlet to the north — a quiet residential neighbourhood in North Burnaby, officially known as Vancouver Heights and commonly called "The Heights" or "Burnaby Heights." https://pinskymortgages.ca/burnaby-heights/
- Hastings Street is the spine and the shopping street: the Heights commercial district runs along Hastings (roughly Boundary Road to Gamma Avenue) as a street-front strip of independent shops, grocers, delis and restaurants rather than a mall, and the City has processed mixed-use redevelopment proposals on Hastings — for example at 4472 Hastings Street at Willingdon Avenue — to strengthen the Heights Urban Village. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/FileStream.ashx?DocumentId=69510
- Albert Street runs parallel to Hastings one block north and carries much of the area's low-rise apartment stock and its civic cluster; McGill Street, further north toward the inlet slope, is where the area's one seniors residence sits (Section 2). Sources: the building and amenity entries below.
- Schools are School District 41 (Burnaby). Two SD41 elementary schools sit on Pandora Street inside the sub-area: Confederation Park Elementary (4715 Pandora St, K–7) and Rosser Elementary (4375 Pandora St, K–7), per the Province's school directory for District 41. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- Secondary catchment — settled fact for this area: **ALPHA Secondary (8–12), NOT Burnaby North.** The SD41 locator run for the sample in-area address 4375 Pandora St (range 4303–4375 Pandora St) returned Rosser Elementary and Alpha Secondary; the second in-area sample, 4715 Pandora St, returned Confederation Park Elementary and Alpha Secondary. Burnaby North Secondary (751 Hammarskjold Dr) is a different school located in North Burnaby — location is not catchment. Catchments for other Heights addresses: UNCONFIRMED — run the exact address. (SD41 School Locator, mybaragar.com, runs recorded in the area research file, 2026-11-01.)
- The west-end civic cluster, all within the sub-area: Confederation Park (250 Willingdon Ave), Eileen Dailly Leisure Pool & Fitness Centre (240 Willingdon Ave), the McGill Branch of Burnaby Public Library (4595 Albert St), and the Confederation Seniors' Centre (4585 Albert St) — a recreation centre, not housing (see Section 2). https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/confederation-park and http://www.burnaby.ca/confederation55+
- Transit: TransLink's R5 Hastings St RapidBus runs along Hastings through the Heights, linking Burrard Station in downtown Vancouver with SFU's Burnaby Mountain campus, at least every 10 minutes at peak and 15 minutes off-peak, with in-area stops including Hythe, Holdom and Kensington. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Route_Diagrams/213/rR5.pdf
- Transit improved in September 2026: on September 7, 2026, TransLink extended the R2 Marine–Willingdon RapidBus from Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown Station via Hastings Street and Willingdon Avenue, serving the Heights with peak service every six to seven minutes. There is no SkyTrain station in this sub-area. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/
- Health care: there is no hospital in the sub-area; the city's hospital is Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St, south across the city. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- Where the seniors housing actually is: exactly one place — Seton Villa, 3755 McGill St, at the west end of the sub-area near Boundary Road. Every other Burnaby seniors building a search returns sits south of Hastings, east of Gamma Avenue, or further south in the city (see the Checked list in Section 2).

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**Vancouver Heights has ZERO verified 55+ ownership complexes.** That is the headline of this page, stated plainly: no strata building with an address inside this sub-area was found in any source checked carrying a verified 55+ age-restriction bylaw. The sub-area does have attached stock — SnapStats recorded 22 condos & townhomes active and 1 sold here in September 2026 — but it is general-market stock on and near Hastings and Albert Streets, not age-restricted stock. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.) The one verified seniors building in the sub-area is a non-profit retirement residence you rent into, not buy into: Seton Villa.

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- None verified in this sub-area. Do not pad: a 55+ buyer who wants to own in Vancouver Heights is buying an ordinary detached house or a general-market strata unit with no age bylaw — see the Checked list below for what the in-area strata stock actually is.

### Verified 55+ rental (not ownership)

- None verified as general-market 55+ rental inside this sub-area. The BC Housing Zone 5 seniors directory's Burnaby-North section lists no general 55+ rental building with a Vancouver Heights address; its one in-area entry is Seton Villa, listed as "housing with supports" (below). https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076

### Retirement residences and care (rental/care — NOT strata ownership)

- **Seton Villa Retirement Centre — 3755 McGill St, Burnaby, V5C 1M2.** The sub-area's one verified seniors residence, and it IS in Vancouver Heights: the Canadian Register of Historic Places entry for Overlynn — the heritage mansion on the same lot at 3755 McGill St — states the property "is situated in the residential neighbourhood of Vancouver Heights" and "is now part of a large senior citizens development known as Seton Villa"; Heritage Burnaby records the same address under "Historic Neighbourhood: Vancouver Heights." https://www.historicplaces.ca/fr/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=3799 and https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark520
- What it is: a 19-storey non-profit retirement centre opened in 1974, operated by Action Line Housing Society, registered in BC's Assisted Living Registry (class SN — Seniors and Persons with Disabilities). The operator describes a model that blends independent living, supportive housing and assisted living, with all-inclusive living: homemade meals, weekly housekeeping and laundry, and 24-hour on-site health care. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ABurnaby&f%5B1%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASupportive%20Recovery&name=&address=&order=alr_residence_type&sort=asc and https://www.setonvilla.com/home and https://www.setonvilla.com/about-us
- Size: BC Housing's Zone 5 seniors directory lists Seton Villa at 133 bachelor and 56 one-bedroom units (189 units), non-profit managed, "housing with supports," applications taken directly by the residence — the directory edition located for this research is dated January 2021, so treat the count as directory-stated, not current-verified. https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076
- Placement note — read before using any other Burnaby 55+ notes file: the sibling notes file for Capitol Hill BN deliberately does NOT count Seton Villa, because no source assigns 3755 McGill St to the Capitol Hill MLS sub-area, while the heritage sources above assign it to Vancouver Heights. It is counted here, once, and nowhere else. Its minimum resident age: UNCONFIRMED — the operator markets the building to seniors and it is registered as a seniors residence, but no source checked states a 55+ (or any) age threshold.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. In Vancouver Heights this law is academic for in-area buying — no in-area strata carries a verified 55+ bylaw — but it governs any 55+ purchase a Heights downsizer makes elsewhere. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026** is the current bylaw — adopted June 9, 2026 and in effect July 1, 2026, replacing the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742). The 1965 bylaw is SUPERSEDED — never cite it as current. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/bylaws/zoning-bylaw
- **R1 SSMUH zoning:** Burnaby consolidated its single- and two-family districts into the R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing district in June 2024 — R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. On the Heights' detached streets, that — not a 55+ complex — is where new ground-oriented downsizer supply comes from. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP: the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan** was adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP Bylaw, and its land use map now takes precedence over older neighbourhood community-plan maps where they conflict. The Hastings corridor through this neighbourhood is planned as the Heights Urban Village; community plans are adopted by Council as policy guidelines for rezoning and development applications. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- **Property taxes:** City of Burnaby property taxes for 2026 were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
- **Tax deferment exists as a provincial route:** the City's property-tax pages direct owners who are unable to pay to the provincial property tax deferment programs (gov.bc.ca/propertytaxdeferment) for eligibility and applications. Program eligibility details: UNCONFIRMED from the sources read for this file — check the provincial page, not a listing summary. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/additional-information
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing** — the Housing Registry and the Zone 5 (Burnaby and New Westminster) seniors directory, whose edition located for this research is dated January 2021. Seton Villa takes applications directly as a Section 2 (direct-apply) development in that directory. https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Vancouver Heights in Vancouver?** No — it is a City of Burnaby neighbourhood in North Burnaby, east of Boundary Road. The City of Vancouver (Hastings-Sunrise) is across Boundary Road to the west. Burnaby taxes, Burnaby bylaws and School District 41 apply, and its market statistics are reported under Burnaby. https://pinskymortgages.ca/burnaby-heights/
- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Vancouver Heights?** None that any source verifies. No strata building inside the sub-area carries a verified 55+ age bylaw, and the area's one seniors residence (Seton Villa) is rental, not ownership. A 55+ buyer here buys an ordinary home with no age restriction — or buys into a 55+ strata in another Burnaby sub-area. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Seton Villa in Vancouver Heights or on Capitol Hill?** Vancouver Heights. The Canadian Register of Historic Places and Heritage Burnaby both place 3755 McGill St — the Seton Villa lot, including the Overlynn mansion on it — in the Vancouver Heights neighbourhood. Neighbourhood names in North Burnaby overlap loosely (its own operator also calls the setting "Burnaby Heights"), which is why the Capitol Hill notes file leaves it uncounted and this file counts it once. https://www.historicplaces.ca/fr/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=3799 and https://www.setonvilla.com/home
- **Q: Can I buy a suite at Seton Villa?** No. It is a non-profit residence operated by Action Line Housing Society — independent/supportive suites and assisted living that you rent into or are placed into, including 29 publicly subsidized assisted-living suites recorded by Fraser Health. There is no strata ownership to purchase. https://www.setonvilla.com/about-us and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/8/C/assisted-living-at-seton-villa
- **Q: I use a wheelchair — can Seton Villa take me?** Not in the existing tower, on Fraser Health's record: its subsidized assisted-living suites "cannot accommodate wheelchairs or scooters," and residents must be able to move around with or without mobility aids; resident pets are also not permitted. That limit is exactly what the operator's new building addresses — see the next answer. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/8/C/assisted-living-at-seton-villa
- **Q: What is being built beside Seton Villa?** A new 48-unit, fully wheelchair-accessible building, built in partnership with CMHC, BC Housing and the City of Burnaby: construction began in 2025, with occupancy slated for late spring 2027, per the operator. The existing tower is also in year one of a five-year rejuvenation funded by BC Housing. https://www.setonvilla.com/about-us
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Burnaby?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — a BC age bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Which secondary school serves the Heights — Burnaby North?** No. The SD41 locator results recorded for two in-area Pandora Street addresses both returned **Alpha Secondary** (with Rosser Elementary for 4375 Pandora St and Confederation Park Elementary for 4715 Pandora St). Burnaby North Secondary is simply the other secondary school located in North Burnaby. Confirm the catchment for a specific address with the district — boundaries do not follow MLS sub-area lines. (SD41 School Locator runs recorded in the area research file, 2026-11-01; school addresses: https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041)
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** RapidBus, not SkyTrain — there is no station in the sub-area. The R5 runs on Hastings at least every 10 minutes at peak between downtown Vancouver and SFU, and since September 7, 2026 the R2 also runs along Hastings and down Willingdon Avenue to Metrotown. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects and https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The seniors tower stands on a heritage estate — and the sources disagree on its date.** Seton Villa's lot at 3755 McGill St includes Overlynn, the C.J. Peter mansion: the Canadian Register of Historic Places dates the house to 1909 (built for $75,000, one of the first houses in Vancouver Heights), while the operator's own history page dates Overlynn to 1912 and says it later served as a school for girls (Seton Academy) before Action Line Housing Society bought the property in 1970 and opened Seton Villa in 1974. The 1909 vs 1912 build year is a genuine source conflict — do not publish either year as settled. https://www.historicplaces.ca/fr/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=3799 and https://www.setonvilla.com/about-us
- **The wheelchair answer flips in 2027.** Today, Fraser Health records that Seton Villa's existing tower cannot accommodate wheelchairs or scooters; the operator's new 48-unit fully wheelchair-accessible building — construction began 2025, occupancy slated for late spring 2027 — is being built on the same site specifically to expand accessibility, alongside a five-year BC Housing-funded rejuvenation of the existing tower. A buyer screening Seton Villa for a parent in 2026 and one screening it in 2027 are looking at two different buildings. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/8/C/assisted-living-at-seton-villa and https://www.setonvilla.com/about-us
- **The "55+" building on Albert Street is a gym-and-clubs centre, not a home.** Confederation Seniors' Centre (4585 Albert St) is the City's 55+ recreation centre for the area — $22.60/year single-centre membership effective September 1, 2026 — and it is the amenity that makes the west end of the Heights workable for older residents who buy ordinary homes here. http://www.burnaby.ca/confederation55+
- **Its replacement is deferred.** The City's planned new Confederation Park Community Centre — a 3-level facility beside Eileen Dailly Pool and the McGill Branch library that would retain both and replace the 1971 seniors centre — has been deferred, per the City's project page. The current centre, pool and library are the offer, not a future one. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/confederation-park-community-centre
- **One street, two elementary schools.** Pandora Street carries both in-area elementaries, and the SD41 locator splits it by address range: 4375 Pandora St returns Rosser Elementary, while 4715 Pandora St returns Confederation Park Elementary — both feeding Alpha Secondary in the runs recorded. Repeating "the Pandora Street school" without an address is an error waiting to happen. (SD41 School Locator runs recorded in the area research file, 2026-11-01.)
- **The corridor's new strata supply is aimed at everyone, not seniors.** The mixed-use projects moving through the Heights Urban Village — the 37-home proposal at 4269 Hastings St recorded in the area research file and the 38-residence Siena presale at 4451 Hastings St (spring 2028 completion in its listing copy) — are general-market homes over retail. Nothing in any source checked frames new Heights product as 55+ or age-restricted. https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/4451-hastings-street/305
- **Burnaby replaced its entire zoning bylaw in 2026.** The Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (adopted June 9, 2026, in force July 1, 2026) repealed and replaced the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 — and the Burnaby 2050 OCP (adopted December 9, 2025) replaced the 1998 OCP above it. Any Heights document, summary or old listing that cites the 1965 zoning bylaw or the 1998 OCP as current is out of date twice over. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **2026 tax dates have already passed.** Burnaby's 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date — a buyer completing in the Heights this fall inherits that calendar, not Vancouver's. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
