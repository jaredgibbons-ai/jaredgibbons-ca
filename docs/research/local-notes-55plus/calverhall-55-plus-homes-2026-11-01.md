## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why the "rentals not allowed" line in Ridge Park Gardens' building data (Section 2) does not reflect current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 6700, Ground Oriented (GO) Zone** (adopted December 8, 2025): a lot of 280 m² or less allows up to 3 units, a lot over 280 m² allows up to 4 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 designation and up to 6 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 2 or Residential Level 4 designations; the December 2025 adoption increased permitted units from 3 to 4 on nearly 4,300 residential properties City-wide. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf and https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/HTO-Year-2-Progress-Report-July-1,-2025-June-30,-2026.pdf
- **Suites and coach houses (City):** the City legalized secondary suites in all single-family dwellings in 1993; one suite is permitted per single-family property, the owner must reside at the property, and a suite may be combined with an accessory coach house where the front lot line is at least 10 m, with a Development Permit and a Building Permit. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Permits-Inspections/building-permits/secondary-suites
- **City OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2014, No. 8400** governs land use in the City, amended December 8, 2025 (Amendment Bylaw 2025, No. 9114) for provincial housing requirements; a Calverhall-specific plan is UNVERIFIED (none confirmed). https://www.cnv.org/Community-Environment/Housing/Provincial-Housing-Legislation-Updates
- **Streamside permits:** the City's Streamside Protection Development Permit Area covers land within 15 metres of the top of bank of a watercourse or within 10 metres of the top of a ravine bank; the City stormwater plan notes a creek channel from the Calverhall – Cloverly neighbourhoods. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Building/Land-Use-Approvals/Development-Permits/Streamside-Development-Permit-Areas
- **City taxes 2026:** due July 2, 2026; residential municipal-general rate 1.89202 and total residential rate 3.34028 per $1,000 of assessed value, plus a separate residential storm drainage rate of 0.10697 per $1,000. City utility fee totals for a Calverhall home: UNVERIFIED. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/City-Finances/2026/2026_TAX_RATE_SUMMARY_CITY_OF_NORTH_VANCOUVER-2.PDF

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

**Q: Is Calverhall in the City or the District of North Vancouver?**
A: The City. The City's Integrated Stormwater Management Plan names "the Calverhall – Cloverly neighbourhoods" as City neighbourhoods, and City zoning (Bylaw No. 6700) and City tax rates apply — a District-based tax or zoning estimate will misprice the file. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Living-City/Integrated-Stormwater-Management-Plan.pdf

**Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Calverhall?**
A: None were verified — there is no confirmed condo building, townhome complex or age-restricted product inside the area. The nearest verified 55+ strata is Ridge Park Gardens, 2059 Chesterfield Ave in Central Lonsdale (42 units, built 1995, current listings call it a 55+ community), west of Grand Boulevard and outside Calverhall. https://www.zolo.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate/2059-chesterfield-avenue/411

**Q: Building data for Ridge Park Gardens says "rentals not allowed." Can I rent out a unit there?**
A: As a matter of provincial law, yes for long-term rentals: every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

**Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?**
A: Yes — the May 1, 2023 expansion of Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children under 19 in the resident's care, adult children the resident cared for before 19, and live-in caregivers are also exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

**Q: Is there subsidized seniors rental in or near Calverhall?**
A: None inside Calverhall. The nearest non-profit option is Kiwanis Towers, 170 2nd St W in Lower Lonsdale, labelled 55+ by the Kiwanis North Shore Housing Society, which states most of its communities offer below-market rental housing. https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/kiwanis-tower/

**Q: Where do seniors actually gather near here?**
A: The new Silver Harbour Seniors' Activity Centre, 2325 St Georges Ave, opened in 2026 beside the new Harry Jerome Community Recreation Centre (130 East 23rd St, opened July 25, 2026) — 70+ programs and services for adults 55+, in Central Lonsdale west of Calverhall. It is an activity centre, not housing. https://www.cnv.org/City-Hall/About/Capital-Projects/Past-Projects/Silver-Harbour and https://www.cnv.org/City-Hall/News-Room/News-Releases/2026/6/3/New-HJCRC-and-Silver-Harbour-Seniors%E2%80%99-Activity-Centre-to-Open-July-25,-2026

**Q: Can a Calverhall house lot hold a suite, coach house or more units?**
A: Under the City's GO Zone (adopted December 8, 2025): up to 3 units on a lot of 280 m² or less, up to 4 units on a larger lot in Low Rise Neighbourhood 1, and up to 6 units in Low Rise Neighbourhood 2 or Residential Level 4 designations. Separately, one secondary suite is permitted per single-family property (owner must reside there), combinable with a coach house where the lot qualifies. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf and https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Permits-Inspections/building-permits/secondary-suites

**Q: Where is the hospital and the nearest assisted living?**
A: Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, is the Vancouver Coastal Health hospital serving the North Shore. The registry's nearest assisted-living residence to Calverhall is Chartwell Churchill House, 150 West 29th St in Upper Lonsdale — outside the area. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B1%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=desc

**Q: What were City property taxes for 2026?**
A: Due July 2, 2026; total residential rate 3.34028 per $1,000 (municipal-general 1.89202), plus storm drainage at 0.10697 per $1,000. The City illustrates an average residential property assessed at $1,387,684 paying $4,790 in total 2026 property taxes. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/City-Finances/2026/2026_TAX_RATE_SUMMARY_CITY_OF_NORTH_VANCOUVER-2.PDF and http://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/City-Finances/2026/2026-Property-Tax-Info-Sheet_web-final.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The municipality error is the area's standing trap.** Calverhall reads like a District Seymour/Lynn name, but every governing document is the City's — City zoning Bylaw No. 6700, City OCP Bylaw No. 8400, and a 2026 total residential tax rate of 3.34028 per $1,000 (District rate: 3.28195). Files priced off District figures are wrong from the first page. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/City-Finances/2026/2026_TAX_RATE_SUMMARY_CITY_OF_NORTH_VANCOUVER-2.PDF
- **The seniors infrastructure beside Calverhall is brand-new — and it is not housing.** Silver Harbour's new activity centre at 2325 St Georges Ave was completed in August 2026 (its previous building dated to 1973, at 144 East 22nd St), paired with the new Harry Jerome centre that opened July 25, 2026. A buyer told "a new seniors building just opened by Calverhall" is hearing about a recreation centre. https://www.cnv.org/City-Hall/About/Capital-Projects/Past-Projects/Silver-Harbour and https://silverharbourcentre.com/?s=
- **(December 8, 2025)** The GO Zone quietly re-priced Calverhall lots: permitted units rose from 3 to 4 on nearly 4,300 City properties and up to 6 per lot on about 600 — a house lot's value now turns on its OCP designation, not the old single-family zoning. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/HTO-Year-2-Progress-Report-July-1,-2025-June-30,-2026.pdf
- **The City legalized suites in 1993 — decades before most BC municipalities.** One suite per single-family property, owner must reside on site; that long-standing rule is why so many Calverhall-era City houses already carry a legal suite a buyer can use for family or a caregiver. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Permits-Inspections/building-permits/secondary-suites
- **"Cloverly" vs "Cloverley" will break your searches.** The City stormwater plan spells the adjoining neighbourhood "Cloverly"; SD44 and the Province spell the new school "Cloverley Elementary." Search both spellings in City documents or you will miss records. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026INF0050-001056 and https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Living-City/Integrated-Stormwater-Management-Plan.pdf
- **The nearest 55+ strata predates the current law's framing.** Ridge Park Gardens (1995, Central Lonsdale) is marketed in 2026 listings as a 55+ community with "one owner must be 55+" in an earlier listing — the current provincial exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents) are broader than that listing shorthand suggests. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3025512-2059-chesterfield-avenue-407-north-vancouver-bc-v7m-2p4/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
