## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT THIS HOME TYPE

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01: a strata may restrict residents to 55+, and any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw governs residency, not ownership:** under-55s can own a lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in it; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — younger spouses/partners, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rental bylaws remain allowed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Manufactured-home parks** run under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (BC), not strata law: the buyer owns the home and rents the site, pad rent may rise once every 12 months on 3 full months' notice by the capped formula (2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026; 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Consumer-protection frame for anything the homepage advertises (BCFSA):** a licensed real estate professional must give consumers information about commissions and fees, inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, warn unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party, and work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Homepage ranking language is page content, not a verified rule or figure:** the area section's "South Surrey's #1 ranked REALTOR®" wording and the footer's "Top 1% of All Realtors in the Fraser Valley" line are recorded in the research file as page statements, UNVERIFIED as worded against board data in that pass. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/homepage.md

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where do I find 55+ homes from the homepage?** There is no 55+ section or filter named on the homepage in the page text read — the working route is Search by Area: pick one of the 11 communities, then narrow by property type, knowing the verified 55+ depth differs sharply by community (Section 2). https://jaredgibbons.ca/
- **Q: Which of the homepage's 11 communities has the most 55+ choice?** Abbotsford: 26 verified strata complexes, three 55+ parks and two life leases on the standing rosters, ahead of Surrey (15 strata) and Langley (11 strata). Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: The homepage feed shows a listing I like. Is it 55+?** Not necessarily — the feed as read is a general current-listings feed mixing property types and communities, and it repeated one card. Age restriction is a per-building fact, confirmed from the building's bylaws and Form B, never from its presence in the feed. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/homepage.md
- **Q: My partner is under 55. Can we live in a 55+ building together?** Yes — a younger spouse or partner of a specified resident is a required provincial exemption, as are children in defined cases and live-in caregivers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I buy a 55+ unit as an investment and rent it out?** Ownership is not age-restricted and long-term rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022 — but any tenant must still meet the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be banned by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Q: Does the homepage publish market statistics I can budget from?** No market statistics appear on the homepage in the text read — no median, benchmark or days-on-market figure; the only dollar figures are individual listing prices inside the feed cards. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/homepage.md
- **Q: Are the reviews on the homepage a rating I can rely on?** They are excerpts: the research pass recorded ten 5-star Google review excerpts, several truncated, and did not check their completeness or any overall rating against Google — UNVERIFIED as a rating. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/homepage.md
- **Q: I need assisted living later. Does buying a 55+ strata unit lead into that?** No — care residences (Section 2) are a separate rental/Fraser Health category, not a stage of a strata purchase. A 55+ strata purchase buys independent living only, plus whatever service plan that specific building carries (Mayfair Terrace in Port Coquitlam is the program's example of a strata with a mandatory service plan). Source: ~/workspace/local-notes/port-coquitlam-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: What does it cost to work with the realtor on the homepage?** No commission figure appears on the homepage; under the BCFSA framework, commission and fee information is given to the consumer at the outset of dealings, off-page. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
