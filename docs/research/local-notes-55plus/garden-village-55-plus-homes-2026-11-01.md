# Garden Village (Burnaby) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Garden Village is in southwest Burnaby, west of Metrotown toward Boundary Road, around Central Park and Patterson Station (Expo Line). The name comes from the historic Garden Village subdivision of 1953 — a 140-acre site planned for 500 modern homes — inside what the City now treats as the Central Park historic neighbourhood / Maywood planning area. The exact MLS sub-area boundary streets are UNVERIFIED. Sources: the area research file (Heritage Burnaby; sources read 2026-11-01) and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- Housing stock is detached houses with very little strata: in September 2026 the sub-area recorded ZERO attached listings and zero attached sales. Sources: the area research file (SnapStats GVR September 2026).
- Schools in and beside the sub-area: Windsor Elementary and Moscrop Secondary; the SD41 locator for the sample address 5455 Rumble St returns Nelson Elementary and Burnaby South Secondary — catchments are address-based. Burnaby is School District 41 — 41 elementary and 8 secondary schools, no middle-school tier (K–7 / 8–12). https://burnabyschools.ca/about-sd41-overview/ and https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- The Rumble Street Urban Trail runs along the sub-area's south side — the flat walking route at the foot of the South Slope streets. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/walking-hiking-and-biking/rumble-street-urban-trail
- Health: the nearest urgent care is the Metrotown UPCC at 102-4555 Kingsway, east of the sub-area; the nearest hospital is Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/burnaby/metrotown-urgent-and-primary-care-centre and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- Where the seniors housing actually is: two spots, both at the edges of daily life here — Courtyard Terrace assisted living on Burke St by Central Park/Patterson Ave, and the Fair Haven Homes Society campus at Sussex Ave and Rumble St on the sub-area's south edge. Both are rental/care. There is no 55+ strata to buy.

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**No verified 55+ strata ownership complex was found in this sub-area.** Garden Village's verified seniors stock is one assisted-living residence, one long-term care home and one non-profit 55+ rental building — nothing a buyer can purchase.

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- None verified in this sub-area (and almost no strata stock of any kind: zero attached listings and zero attached sales in September 2026, per the area research file).

### Verified 55+ rental (not ownership)

- No standalone market-rental 55+ building was verified in this sub-area. The one independent-living 55+ rental here is the non-profit Fair Haven building below, which is income-limited and so is listed under Subsidized seniors rental.

### Retirement residences and care (rental/care — NOT strata ownership)

A secondary source citing Fraser Health's service information states Fraser Health funds 47 of its suites — a mixed private-pay and publicly funded residence. Rental/care only. https://www.parkplaceseniorsliving.com/find-a-location/british-columbia/lower-mainland/courtyard-terrace/ and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ABurnaby&f%5B1%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASupportive%20Recovery&name=&address=&order=alr_residence_type&sort=asc
- **Fair Haven Burnaby Lodge — 7557 Sussex Ave, V5J 3V6.** Long-term care home at the corner of Sussex Ave and Rumble St, operated by the Fair Haven Homes Society: 100 publicly subsidized beds (Fraser Health). The campus sits at the sub-area's south edge (the Garden Village / South Slope edge); it is counted here as the campus's Rumble St frontage is the sub-area side, but a boundary-strict reading could place it at the edge — flagged, not hidden. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/8/F/fair-haven-homes---burnaby-lodge

### Subsidized seniors rental (non-profit, income-based — not ownership)

- **Fair Haven Burnaby Apartments (Fair Haven Homes Society, Sussex Ave at Rumble St).** Independent-living rental for seniors 55 and over (tenants with disabilities 50 and over): a three-storey building of 55 barrier-free one-bedroom units of about 650 sq ft, opened March 1999, built in partnership with BC Housing — 33 units are subsidized on a rent-geared-to-income basis and 22 are market-priced. Eligibility per the society: applicants must be over 55, able to care for themselves, and have income within the BC Housing Seniors Rentals Income Limits. Rental only — nothing to buy. The apartment building's own civic address was not separately confirmed in the sources opened (the campus address, 7557 Sussex Ave, is the Lodge's listing address) — the Apartments' street number is UNCONFIRMED. https://fairhaven.bc.ca/apartments/burnaby-apartments/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **How the 55+ test works at Fair Haven is different from a strata:** the Fair Haven Apartments' age rule is a tenancy eligibility rule set by the non-profit society with BC Housing (over 55, self-care capable, income within the BC Housing Seniors Rentals Income Limits) — it is not a Strata Property Act bylaw, and it comes with an income test a strata bylaw never has. https://fairhaven.bc.ca/apartments/burnaby-apartments/
- **Zoning bylaw — note the name change.** Research briefs for Burnaby sometimes still cite the "Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742)". The City states the new **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026** was adopted June 9, 2026, replaced the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965, and took effect July 1, 2026. Use the 2026 bylaw. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/bylaws/zoning-bylaw
- **R1 SSMUH zoning:** Burnaby consolidated its single- and two-family districts into the R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing district in June 2024 — 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; multi-unit lots may be stratified. On Garden Village's 1953-era detached lots, SSMUH infill is the only mechanism adding smaller ground-oriented homes; none of it is age-restricted. Patterson Station also puts the sub-area's east side in a SkyTrain transit-oriented area. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP: the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan** was adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP, and takes precedence over older community-plan maps. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Property taxes:** Burnaby's 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date; the City's 2026–2030 Financial Plan approved a 2.9% tax increase (about $61 on the average home). The detached single-family water utility charge is $652.33, with a 5% discount if paid by the due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Garden Village?** None that any source verifies. There is no age-restricted strata building in the sub-area — and almost no strata at all (zero attached listings and sales in September 2026). A 55+ buyer here is buying a detached house with no age bylaw; the verified seniors buildings (Fair Haven, Courtyard Terrace) are rental and care. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: What is the Fair Haven building at Sussex and Rumble?** Two things on one non-profit campus: the Burnaby Apartments — 55 one-bedroom independent-living rental units for people 55+ (33 subsidized rent-geared-to-income, 22 market-priced, opened 1999) — and Burnaby Lodge, a 100-bed publicly subsidized long-term care home. Neither can be bought; the apartments are applied for through the society/BC Housing route, and the Lodge through Fraser Health. https://fairhaven.bc.ca/apartments/burnaby-apartments/ and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/8/F/fair-haven-homes---burnaby-lodge
- **Q: Who qualifies for a Fair Haven apartment?** Per the society: you must be over 55 (50+ for tenants with disabilities), able to care for yourself, and have income within the BC Housing Seniors Rentals Income Limits — so it is an income-tested tenancy, not an open-market rental and not an ownership product. https://fairhaven.bc.ca/apartments/burnaby-apartments/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Burnaby?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — a BC age bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I add a suite or coach house for a parent or caregiver instead?** That is the realistic route in this sub-area: Burnaby's R1 SSMUH zoning allows 3–4 units on most house lots (up to 6 near frequent transit), suites are part of the permitted forms, and multi-unit lots may be stratified. What a specific lot can hold depends on its size and transit proximity — check the lot against the City's R1 rules. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day?** Patterson Station (Expo Line) serves the sub-area at Central Park, and the park itself plus the Rumble Street Urban Trail give flat walking routes — a genuine plus for a senior household, even though the housing itself is ordinary detached stock. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/walking-hiking-and-biking/rumble-street-urban-trail
- **Q: Where is the hospital and urgent care?** The Metrotown UPCC (102-4555 Kingsway) is just east of the sub-area; the nearest hospital is Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/burnaby/metrotown-urgent-and-primary-care-centre and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in Garden Village?** Partly: assisted living exists in-sub-area at Courtyard Terrace on Burke St (with a mix of private-pay and Fraser Health-funded suites), and long-term care at Fair Haven Burnaby Lodge (100 subsidized beds) on the south edge — both accessed by application, not purchase. https://www.parkplaceseniorsliving.com/find-a-location/british-columbia/lower-mainland/courtyard-terrace/ and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/8/F/fair-haven-homes---burnaby-lodge

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The neighbourhood name is a 1953 subdivision brand.** "Garden Village" was the marketing name of a 1953 subdivision — 140 acres planned for 500 modern homes — whose name outlived the sales office and now labels the MLS sub-area around Central Park's southwest side. Sources: the area research file (Heritage Burnaby; sources read 2026-11-01).
- **One society runs the whole local seniors ladder.** The Fair Haven Homes Society's Sussex Ave campus pairs the 55+ rental apartments (1999) with a 100-bed care home on the same corner — a household can, in principle, move from its independent apartment to the Lodge without leaving the neighbourhood, which is rare in Burnaby's southwest. https://fairhaven.bc.ca/apartments/burnaby-apartments/ and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/8/F/fair-haven-homes---burnaby-lodge
- **The Fair Haven apartments are barrier-free by design:** all 55 units are barrier-free one-bedrooms of about 650 sq ft — a 1999 non-profit build that anticipated accessibility standards most market buildings of its era did not. https://fairhaven.bc.ca/apartments/burnaby-apartments/
- **Metrotown's seniors centre is not this sub-area's.** Search results for "Burnaby 55+" surface the Bonsor 55+ Centre (6533 Nelson Ave) and The Poppy Residences (5291 Grimmer St) — both are Metrotown addresses. Garden Village's own seniors infrastructure is Fair Haven and Courtyard Terrace only. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities
- **Burnaby replaced its entire zoning bylaw in 2026.** The Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (adopted June 9, 2026, in force July 1, 2026) repealed and replaced the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 — documents or summaries that still cite the 1965 bylaw are out of date. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Burnaby taxes have two penalty steps.** 2026 property taxes were due July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date, and the detached water charge ($652.33) carries a 5% early-payment discount. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
