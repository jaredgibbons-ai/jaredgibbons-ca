## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Norgate is a flat, self-contained post-war subdivision in the District of North Vancouver, between Capilano Road (west) and Pemberton Avenue (east), and between Marine Drive (south) and the Welch Street / Upper Levels corridor (north); the exact street bounds are UNVERIFIED from an official District document — the description follows the subdivision's street pattern in the research file. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/norgate.md

- The neighbourhood elementary school is Norgate Xwemélch'stn Community Elementary (K–7, 1295 Sowden St), an authorized IB World School offering the Primary Years Programme; the catchment secondary school is Carson Graham Secondary (2145 Jones Ave). https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/044 and https://www.sd44.ca/school/norgate/About/
- The southern edge, along Welch Street, faces Capilano Indian Reserve No. 5 (X̱wemelch'stn), Squamish Nation land, where the Nation exercises its own land-use and zoning control; Welch Strip Park is the District park on the municipal side of that edge. https://partii-partiii.fng.ca/fng-gpn-ii-iii/pii/en/474475/1/document.do and https://www.dnv.org/parks-trails-recreation/find-all-district-parks-map

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Municipality: District of North Vancouver — not the City.** Taxes, permits and bylaws are the District's. District 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value, and combined single-family utilities total $2,571 in 2026 (water $932.80; sewer/drainage $1,255.60). https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- **District OCP (Bylaw 7900)** directs 75–90% of growth to four centres and preserves established low-density neighbourhoods; Norgate's nearest growth node is the Lions Gate Village Centre at Capilano Road and Marine Drive, planned under the April 2013 Lower Capilano–Marine Village Implementation Plan. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf and https://app.dnv.org/OpenDocument/Default.aspx?docNum=4013488
- **Suites and coach houses:** since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary; a secondary suite may be up to 49% of the house with no minimum size, and coach houses may be two storeys. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **SSMUH caution for this bench:** the District's GEOweb classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop — but a March 2, 2026 District Council motion asked staff to pursue exempting the Lions Gate area, which includes Norgate, from SSMUH. Verify the specific lot on GEOweb before relying on density. https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf and http://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-4-detached-residential.pdf
- **Reserve-edge rule:** homes on Welch Street face Capilano IR No. 5; Squamish Nation Council approved land uses for the reserve's Marine Drive site on October 29, 2024 — mixed-use market residential, Nation housing, and a new Administrative and Community Service Centre — so change on that edge is governed by the Nation, not the District. https://www.squamish.net/squamish-nation-council-approves-land-uses-for-three-sites-extends-moratorium-on-third-party-development-proposals/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ condo or townhome I can buy in Norgate?** No — no verified 55+ ownership complex exists inside the sub-area. The maintained North Vancouver 55+ strata list names nine buildings and none is in Norgate; the closest are in Central Lonsdale (Ridge Park Gardens, Sundale, Vista 29), Delbrook (Queensbrook), Edgemont (Edgemont Villa, Highland House) and Lynn Valley (Balmoral House, Kirkstone Gardens). https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/on-the-market/55-buildings-north-vancouver/
- **Q: Why do downsizers look at Norgate houses if there is no 55+ product?** The housing form: the research file describes a subdivision of small post-war detached ranchers and bungalows on flat lots and crescents — one of the few level, single-storey pockets on the North Shore. A rancher gives single-level living without a strata age bylaw, but it is a general-market house, not 55+ housing. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/norgate.md
- **Q: Is Norgate in the City or the District of North Vancouver?** The District. Norgate Park is a District park and District council documents count Norgate among the District's Lower Capilano neighbourhoods — the distinction changes the tax rates, bylaws and permit desk that apply. http://dnv.org/ and https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/council_reports/211146.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere on the North Shore?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What subsidized seniors rental is closest to Norgate?** Capilano House, 1551 Bridgman Ave, listed in BC Housing's Zone 1 seniors directory (May 2025) as a Housing Registry development on the bench east of the subdivision. Applications for Registry buildings go through BC Housing's Housing Registry; the directory extract does not state its age minimum or unit count — both UNCONFIRMED here. https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-1-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th St in the City of North Vancouver — the North Shore's hospital, operated by Vancouver Coastal Health, with emergency and critical care. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** The R2 RapidBus on Marine Drive at the subdivision's south edge runs Park Royal to Metrotown (extended September 7, 2026), roughly 5 a.m.–2 a.m. with 6–7 minute peak frequency, and connects at Lonsdale Quay to the SeaBus, which crosses to Waterfront Station in 12 minutes. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/ and https://www.translink.ca/translink/schedules-and-maps/seabus
- **Q: Can I add a suite or coach house to a Norgate rancher for a caregiver or family member?** District zoning has permitted a house plus secondary suite plus coach house (three units) on single-family lots within the Urban Containment Boundary since June 2024 — but the District has also asked to exempt the Lions Gate area from broader SSMUH density, so confirm the specific lot's status on GEOweb before designing around it. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf and http://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-4-detached-residential.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The "City of North Vancouver" label in some listings for this bench is wrong.** The research file's official sources put Norgate in the District; a buyer who budgets on City tax rates or asks the City permit desk about a Norgate lot is working from the wrong municipality's numbers. District 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026 with 5% + 5% penalties, and the total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- **The land across Welch Street is not District land and never will be planned by the District.** It is Capilano IR No. 5 (X̱wemelch'stn). In October 2024 Squamish Nation Council approved mixed-use market residential, Nation housing and a new Administrative and Community Service Centre for the reserve's Marine Drive site — the southern outlook of Welch Street homes is an active development front. https://www.squamish.net/squamish-nation-council-approves-land-uses-for-three-sites-extends-moratorium-on-third-party-development-proposals/
- **Original Norgate Park houses are reported to hide their heating in the floor slab.** A heritage-society account puts originals at roughly 900–1,600 sq ft, many built slab-on-grade with radiant in-floor hot-water heating — UNVERIFIED from official sources, but where that system survives, its condition is a real inspection item because the pipes are embedded in concrete. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/norgate.md
- **The recreation centre for this bench has no pool.** The Lions Gate Community Recreation Centre (1733 Lions Gate Lane, opened June 4, 2022) is District-owned and NVRC-operated with a gymnasium, kitchen, multipurpose and art spaces, the NVDPL Lions Gate Express Library and Capilano Community Services Society — and no pool. https://www.nvrc.ca/sites/default/files/docs/About-Us/june_23_2022_commission_meeting_agenda_package_final.pdf and https://www.nvdpl.ca/lions-gate-express
- **The Spirit Trail's route past Norgate was still a studied option, not a built fact.** The North Shore Spirit Trail is planned as a 35 km accessible greenway from Horseshoe Bay to Deep Cove; the District's 2013 route report studied a Welch Strip option to connect Park Royal/Ambleside and Kings Mill Walk via a planned railway crossing at Mackay Creek. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/spirit-trail-planning-report-2013%20(1).pdf
- **East-edge lots need a creek check, not an assumption.** Mackay Creek runs along the subdivision's east side, and the District's OCP Schedule B establishes Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard Development Permit Areas; the District's GEOweb flood construction levels are modelled on a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-single-family-residential-1-5.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/
- **SnapStats publishes no North Vancouver figures at all.** The GVR September 2026 report's communities are Burnaby, New Westminster, Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, Pitt Meadows and Maple Ridge — so any "Norgate median price" quoted from SnapStats does not exist; sub-area medians are Paragon-only. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/norgate.md
