# Champlain Heights (Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Champlain Heights is a residential neighbourhood in southeast Vancouver, inside the Killarney local area, centred on Kerr Street and East 54th Avenue above the Fraser River. The City lists "Champlain Heights" as a recognised smaller area, but it is not one of the City's 22 distinct local areas. The City publishes no street-by-street written boundary for it; exact board boundary roads are UNVERIFIED. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/killarney.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- Kerr Street is the neighbourhood's north–south spine, with East 49th Avenue to the north, East 54th Avenue through the middle and SE Marine Drive / Marine Way along the south. https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/killarney-community-centre.aspx
- The shopping anchor is at the southeast corner of East 54th Avenue and Kerr Street: a 1999 City report describes the Champlain Mall site at 3200 East 54th Avenue, in the northwesterly part of Champlain Heights, zoned CD-1 under By-law Number 4550 (approved at a public hearing April 20, 1970), with a public library in the mall. https://council.vancouver.ca/990727/ub1a.htm
- Schools (Vancouver School Board, SD39; catchments are address-based — check the VSB locator for any specific home): Champlain Heights Elementary, 6955 Frontenac Street, is inside the neighbourhood; Cook Elementary (3340 East 54th Avenue), MacCorkindale Elementary (6100 Battison Street) and Weir Elementary (2900 East 44th Avenue) are in the wider Killarney area, with Killarney Secondary at 6454 Killarney Street. SD39 has no middle-school tier (K–7 / 8–12). https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods and https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
- The Vancouver Public Library's Champlain Heights Branch, 7110 Kerr Street, is inside the neighbourhood itself. Killarney Community Centre, 6260 Killarney Street (indoor pool, ice rink, fitness centre), serves the area from just north. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/killarney.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/killarney-community-centre.aspx
- Everett Crowley Park, 8200 Kerr Street, adjoins the neighbourhood to the west. SOURCE DIFFERENCE: the City's Killarney page describes it as 40 hectares; the research file notes the Park Board dataset figure is 38.08 hectares. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/killarney.aspx
- Transit is bus only — there is no SkyTrain station in Champlain Heights. Route 49 runs on East 49th Avenue (Metrotown Station to UBC), route 26 serves the Killarney area to Joyce Station, and route 100 runs on Marine Drive (Marpole Loop to 22nd Street Station). https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt049.pdf
- Where the seniors housing actually is: it clusters at the south end of the Kerr Street corridor and the neighbourhood's south-east streets — the Salvation Army's Southview Terrace / Southview Heights at 7252 Kerr Street, the Royal Arch Masonic Home long-term care home at 7850 Champlain Crescent, and Champlain Place (a BC Housing directory seniors development) at 3307 East 58th Avenue. All are rental, supportive or care housing — none is something a buyer can purchase. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Just south of the neighbourhood, across Marine Way in Fraserlands / River District, is a second seniors site (the Kopernik Foundation lands on Rosemont Drive). Because board sub-area lines there are UNVERIFIED, that site is written up in the Fraserview VE notes, not here.

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**No verified 55+ strata ownership complex was found in this sub-area.** Champlain Heights' verified seniors housing is a supportive/assisted-living residence, a long-term care home and one subsidized seniors development — all rental or care, nothing to buy.

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- None verified in this sub-area. The townhouse complexes the neighbourhood is known for (for example Champlain Village and Marine Woods) are general-market family complexes; no source checked states an age restriction for any strata complex inside Champlain Heights.

### Verified 55+ rental (not ownership)

- None verified as a market-rental 55+ apartment building in this sub-area. The rental seniors housing that does exist here is the supportive residence and the subsidized development below.

BC's Assisted Living Registry lists **Southview Heights** at the same address as a Registered assisted-living residence, class SN (seniors), legal operator The Governing Council of the Salvation Army in Canada. Rental/supportive and assisted living — nothing to buy. Suite counts for either program: UNCONFIRMED. - **Royal Arch Masonic Home — 7850 Champlain Crescent, V5S 4C7.** A long-term care home, not housing for sale: Vancouver Coastal Health describes it as a three-storey care home with 151 subsidized beds, in operation since 1982, operated by the Royal Arch Masonic Homes Society (a non-profit organization) under the Ministry of Health's Continuing Care Program for people who meet "complex care" criteria. The operator's own site confirms the same 151 beds, the 1982 start and its location "in the Champlain Heights area of southeast Vancouver." Admission is through the long-term care system, not a purchase or a rental application to the home. https://www.ramh.ca/ and http://www.vch.ca/en/location/royal-arch-masonic-home

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Tenure is the Champlain Heights rule that actually bites: leasehold.** The neighbourhood mixes freehold houses with strata townhouses/condos and leasehold complexes from its original planned build-out, and tenure must be confirmed complex by complex on title. A general reference states the majority of the Champlain Heights area is leasehold property — pre-paid 99-year leases from the City of Vancouver expiring in the 2080s. Lease terms (expiry, rent, prepaid vs monthly) for any specific complex: UNCONFIRMED — read them off the title and lease documents. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/killarney.aspx and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Champlain_Heights
- **House-street zoning: R1-1 District Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575** — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex, 464 m² for a 5-unit multiple dwelling, 557 m² for 6 to 8 units; a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units. Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **How the City zones seniors housing:** Vancouver's Zoning and Development By-law has long defined congregate housing as a facility providing residential units for persons aged 55 or over with shared dining and support services, and the seniors sites in this neighbourhood sit in site-specific CD-1 zones (Southview at 7252 Kerr; the Champlain Mall CD-1 dates to 1970). The City states Council actively encourages supportive, assisted living and affordable rental housing for seniors, but City staff do not manage these facilities or their rental processes; the Seniors Services Society maintains the Seniors Housing Directory of BC. https://council.vancouver.ca/previous_years/990727/p4.htm and https://vancouver.ca/people-programs/find-supportive-housing-for-seniors.aspx
- **Citywide plan:** the Vancouver Official Development Plan replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026; the East Fraser Lands Official Development Plan (River District) applies at Killarney's south edge, not inside Champlain Heights itself. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/east-fraser-lands-river-district-documents-and-policies.aspx
- **Tax calendar:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax requires an annual property status declaration; the tax is 3% of assessed taxable value for homes vacant for the 2025 reference year, and the 2025 payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Champlain Heights?** None that any source verifies. There is no age-restricted strata building in the sub-area; the verified seniors addresses (Southview on Kerr Street, the Royal Arch Masonic Home, Champlain Place) are supportive rental, assisted living, long-term care and subsidized rental. A 55+ buyer here is buying a regular house, townhome or condo with no age bylaw — in a neighbourhood where much of the multi-family stock is leasehold, so the tenure check matters more than the age check. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Can I buy into Southview Terrace or the Royal Arch Masonic Home?** No. Southview Terrace is a Salvation Army independent supportive seniors residence and Southview Heights is registered assisted living — both rental/care at 7252 Kerr Street. The Royal Arch Masonic Home is a 151-bed publicly subsidized long-term care home; you reach it through the Vancouver Coastal Health long-term care process, not a purchase. https://southviewterracesa.ca/ and https://www.ramh.ca/
- **Q: Is Champlain Heights freehold?** Not entirely — and this is the question that decides value here. The planned community mixes freehold detached houses, strata townhouses/condos and leasehold complexes on pre-paid 99-year City of Vancouver leases expiring in the 2080s. Confirm the tenure and the lease terms for the specific complex on title before pricing anything. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Champlain_Heights and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/killarney.aspx
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Vancouver?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — a BC age bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: How do I get into the subsidized seniors housing here?** Champlain Place (3307 East 58th Avenue) is listed in BC Housing's Zone 6 seniors directory (May 2025), which is the directory BC Housing publishes for seniors and adults-with-disabilities housing in Vancouver; subsidized seniors housing in the city is applied for through BC Housing's Housing Registry or directly with the non-profit operator, depending on the development's section in that directory. The City of Vancouver points seniors to the Seniors Services Society's Seniors Housing Directory of BC for the full list. https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-6-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/people-programs/find-supportive-housing-for-seniors.aspx
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** Buses only: the 49 on East 49th Avenue to Metrotown Station and UBC, the 26 to Joyce Station, and the 100 along Marine Drive. There is no SkyTrain station in or planned for the neighbourhood in the sources checked. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt049.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** There is no hospital in Champlain Heights. The health service confirmed inside the wider south side is the South Community Health Centre, 6405 Knight Street, which provides public and community health services for South Vancouver, west of the neighbourhood. The nearest hospital confirmed on a health-authority page for this sub-area: UNVERIFIED in the sources checked — do not quote one without checking. https://www.vch.ca/en/location-service/home-health-south-community-health-centre
- **Q: Is Champlain Heights in the Fraser River floodplain?** The house streets sit on the south slope above the river; the City's designated floodplain covers the Fraser River coastal floodplain at the river's edge, south of the neighbourhood's house streets. Whether a specific parcel is in the floodplain: UNVERIFIED — check the parcel in the City dataset. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **This is a planned leasehold community, not an organically grown suburb.** Champlain Heights was one of the last areas of Vancouver to be urbanized: planned in the early 1970s as a showcase community of curved roads and cul-de-sacs mixing housing types and incomes, and developed in the early 1980s on a large tract of City-owned land — which is why so much of it sits on pre-paid 99-year City leases expiring in the 2080s. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Champlain_Heights and https://council.vancouver.ca/020115/RR1.htm
- **Three "Southview" names, two different products.** Southview Terrace (independent supportive) and Southview Heights (assisted living) share the Salvation Army campus at 7252 Kerr Street — a site City documents once called Southview Lodge. Southview Gardens at 3240 East 58th Avenue is an unrelated general-market rental complex. https://southviewterracesa.ca/ and https://council.vancouver.ca/20040224/ph5.htm and https://storeys.com/coromandel-properties-woodbourne-capital-southview-gardens-vancouver-civil-suit/
- **The long-term care home is as old as the neighbourhood.** The Royal Arch Masonic Home has operated since 1982 — the same build-out era as the planned community around it — with all 151 beds publicly subsidized and in single rooms per the care-home reporting summarized by VCH. https://www.ramh.ca/ and http://www.vch.ca/en/location/royal-arch-masonic-home
- **The mall's zoning is older than the neighbourhood.** The Champlain Mall CD-1 (By-law No. 4550) was approved in 1970, before the planned community was built out; the mall parcel at East 54th and Kerr has carried a public library inside it since that era. https://council.vancouver.ca/990727/ub1a.htm
- **Seniors housing here has deep community-plan support, in low-rise form.** The City's Victoria-Fraserview/Killarney Community Vision recorded very strong support (84–87%) for housing designated for seniors in the area — the policy backdrop for the Kerr Street and East 58th Avenue seniors buildings. https://council.vancouver.ca/020115/RR1.htm
- **Even the park's size is stated two ways.** City web copy calls Everett Crowley Park 40 hectares; the Park Board dataset figure in the area research is 38.08 hectares. Quote either with its source, not as a single settled number. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/killarney.aspx
