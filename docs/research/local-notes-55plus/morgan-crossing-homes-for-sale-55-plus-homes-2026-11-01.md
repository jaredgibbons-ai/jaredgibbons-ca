## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. At Morgan Crossing this matters in the negative: the complexes are strata, but no source states any of them has a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. Building records for the Morgan Crossing complexes record rentals allowed (15850 26 Ave) and rentals allowed with restrictions (The Morgan) — consistent with the post-2022 position, and unit-specific in any case. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/morgan-crossing-15850-26-avenue
- **City plan — Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026)** is Surrey's current Official Community Plan, per the program's standing correction; the former OCP Bylaw No. 18020 is superseded and must not be cited as current. The Grandview Heights General Land Use Plan remains the area-plan frame named in Section 1. Sources: program standing notes and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/grandview-heights-general-land-use-plan
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000** (cited in City corporate reports as "Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000, as amended") is the zoning bylaw governing these strata lots. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r128-proposed-amendments-surrey-zoning-law-1993-no-12000
- **Strata paperwork is where an age bylaw would be proved:** for any strata purchase, the Province's buyer paperwork path is the Form B information certificate, Form F, strata bylaws and rules, fees and special levies — strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report, and strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. If a Morgan Crossing listing ever claims 55+, the Form B and the registered bylaws are the proof, not the listing text. The live page likewise advises reading the depreciation report and the last two years of strata minutes closely in buildings of this 2008–2011 era, looking for envelope, roof and elevator work coming up. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing and https://jaredgibbons.ca/morgan-crossing-homes-for-sale.html
- **First-time buyer frame the page itself uses:** the page states many Morgan Crossing condos are priced under $835,000, in the context of BC's first-time home buyer exemption (exempting the first $500,000, saving up to $8,000, partial to $860,000, per the page). Those thresholds are page copy — verify eligibility and current thresholds for your own purchase before relying on them. https://jaredgibbons.ca/morgan-crossing-homes-for-sale.html

- **Q: A listing says "adult-oriented" / "seniors-friendly" near 24 Ave and 160 St — is it 55+?** Not on that wording. In BC a strata age restriction exists only as a registered 55+ bylaw under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1/123.2 — marketing language is not a bylaw. No Morgan Crossing complex checked states an age restriction at all; confirm any future claim on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. What would the rule be in a true 55+ strata nearby?** Provincially, a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption) is exempt, as are live-in caregivers, legacy residents, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children the resident cared for before they turned 19. That exemption framework applies in the verified 55+ complexes in Sunnyside Park — it does not create a 55+ complex at Morgan Crossing, where no age bylaw was verified. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a Morgan Crossing condo?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. A strata can still restrict short-term rentals. Building records for these complexes record rentals allowed or allowed with restrictions, and card text on the live page includes "Pets & rentals allowed" for one unit — listing text for that unit only, not a village-wide rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation and https://jaredgibbons.ca/morgan-crossing-homes-for-sale.html

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Morgan Crossing's story is a walk-to-shops story, not a seniors story.** The built product is 2008–2011 condos above and beside the village shops (listing text describes homes "atop the shops at Morgan Crossing" with "60 other shops and restaurants" — the exact current tenant count and mix are UNVERIFIED), aimed in the page's own framing at first-time buyers and downsizers who want strata living without an age rule. https://jaredgibbons.ca/morgan-crossing-homes-for-sale.html
- **One Croydon Drive card is board-labelled "Morgan Creek."** Of the cards read in full, all but one were labelled Grandview Surrey; the exception (410 15745 Croydon Drive) was labelled Morgan Creek Surrey despite sitting in the same complex cluster. A buyer filtering by sub-area name alone will both miss village units and import golf-course-community units. Search by address and complex name as well. https://jaredgibbons.ca/morgan-crossing-homes-for-sale.html
- **The same building answers to two names in current sources.** 15850 26 Avenue is "Axis at Morgan Crossing" in the live page's table and in one listing, and "Arc at Morgan Crossing" / "The Arc At Morgan Crossing" in current feed text for the same address. Strata documents, not marketing names, settle which name the corporation uses — check the Form B. https://jaredgibbons.ca/morgan-crossing-homes-for-sale.html and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/15850-26-avenue/353
- **"Sunnyside Heights" is not "Sunnyside Park."** Sunnyside Heights is a Grandview Heights neighbourhood plan area inside the GLUP; Sunnyside Park Surrey is the separate MLS sub-area down the 152 Street corridor where South Surrey's verified 55+ strata stock (Semiahmoo Estates, Bishop's Green, St. Martin's Lane) actually sits. A search that merges the two names puts another sub-area's 55+ complexes on a Morgan Crossing page. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/grandview-heights-general-land-use-plan
- **The buildings' age is the value question here, not an age bylaw.** These are 2008–2011 buildings — 15 to 17 years old per the page — so envelope, roof and elevator horizons in the depreciation report and the contingency reserve balance decide value more than finishes. That is the page's own buying advice, and it is the opposite diligence from a 55+ purchase, where the bylaw check comes first. https://jaredgibbons.ca/morgan-crossing-homes-for-sale.html
- **The care option inside the plan area carries a neighbourhood name, not a "55+" label.** The Residence at Morgan Heights (15955 27 Ave) is registered as a seniors (SN) assisted-living residence at the GLUP's north edge — a buyer searching only for "55+" product will miss it, and a buyer searching for ownership will be misled by it. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=pique%20newsmagazine&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=41&name=&address=&page=10&order=alr_residence_type&sort=asc
