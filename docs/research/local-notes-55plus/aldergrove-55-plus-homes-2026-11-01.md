# Aldergrove, Township of Langley — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Aldergrove is a community in the Township of Langley; the Township states its eastern boundary is 276 Street bordering the City of Abbotsford and its southern boundary is the United States border. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx
- The Aldergrove Core Area is centred on Fraser Highway and 272 Street, bounded by Bertrand Creek to the north and east, 29 Avenue to the south and 270 Street to the west; wider community boundaries are UNVERIFIED. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/resources/get-involved/ocp-update/Att-B---Aldergrove-CP---Redlined.pdf
- The two verified 55+ ownership complexes sit in opposite corners of the community: Creekside Villa at 27435 29A Ave in the core, beside Bertrand Creek, and Pioneer Park, a manufactured-home co-operative at 27111 0 Ave at the south edge by the US border. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Schools physically in Aldergrove (SD35): Parkside Centennial Elementary, 3300 270 Street; Shortreed Community Elementary, 27330 28 Avenue; Coghlan Fundamental Elementary, 4452 256 Street; North Otter Elementary, 5370 248 Street; Betty Gilbert Middle School, 26845 27 Avenue; Aldergrove Community Secondary, 26850 29 Avenue. Catchments are address-based through the SD35 School Locator. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
- Recreation: the Aldergrove Credit Union Community Centre, 27032 Fraser Highway, is the Township's recreation complex in Aldergrove — pools plus the Otter Co-op Outdoor Experience waterpark, an ice arena, a fitness centre and an indoor walking track. Aldergrove Library (Fraser Valley Regional Library) is at 26770 29 Avenue. https://www.tol.ca/en/parks-recreation/aldergrove-community-centre.aspx
- Transit: TransLink route 503 (Aldergrove/Langley – Fraser Hwy Express) runs from 272 Street at Fraser Highway via Langley Centre to Surrey Central Station; BC Transit route 21 (Aldergrove Connector) links Aldergrove with Abbotsford from the same stop. No SkyTrain or West Coast Express station is in Aldergrove, and TransLink's 503 timetable shows roughly 70 minutes end-to-end to Surrey Central on early-morning trips, stretching to about 90 minutes at peak. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt503.pdf
- Health care: Langley Memorial Hospital, 22051 Fraser Highway, Langley, is the nearest hospital confirmed on a health authority source; the long-term care home inside Aldergrove itself is Jackman Manor (Section 2). https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Langley/langley-memorial-hospital
- Much of the Township's land is within the Agricultural Land Reserve, and Bertrand Creek bounds the core area to the north and east — both matter to the rural lots around the 55+ complexes. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx

### Verified 55+ manufactured-home co-operative (own the home, co-op land — not strata, not a rented pad)

- **Pioneer Park — 27111 0 Ave (Aldergrove, at the US border).** A 55+ manufactured-home co-operative. MLS listings for homes in the park state "Pioneer Park is 55+", that both occupants must be 55 in one listing's wording ("Strictly 55plus (both occupants must be 55)"), and that a buyer must be accepted by the co-op and purchase co-op shares — one listing states shares of $9,200, refunded upon sale, with $510/month co-op fees and 2 pets allowed with no size restrictions. Those dollar figures are listing-stated for specific homes at specific dates — confirm the current share price, monthly co-op fee and occupancy rule with the co-op before writing. This is neither a strata purchase nor a standard pad-rental park: the land is held through the co-operative. https://murphyrealestategroup.ca/recip.html/1272399.search/SearchResults.form and https://murphyrealestategroup.ca/recip.html/1272399.search/SearchResults.form?_pg=3

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. Note the live example in Aldergrove data: an older listing for a Creekside Villa unit described it as a "45+ rancher" while its own MLS property data recorded "Restricted Age: 55+" — under current law only the 55+ form of bylaw can be valid, and the registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/27435-29a-avenue/29
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. Read this against Pioneer Park's listing wording that both occupants must be 55: a co-operative's occupancy rules are set by the co-op, not by strata law, so the provincial strata exemptions do not automatically answer a Pioneer Park occupancy question — ask the co-op. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://murphyrealestategroup.ca/recip.html/1272399.search/SearchResults.form
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Township zoning: Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500** governs Aldergrove. Its Section 110.1 zone ladder, as read out in Township council reports, runs from R-1B/R-1C urban lots at 464.5 m² minimum up to SR-2 suburban lots at 8,094 m² minimum, with rural zones beyond the core; MLS data for Creekside Villa records its zoning as RM-2. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf and https://fraservalleyhomesold.ca/mylistings.html/listing.r3112002-33-27435-29a-avenue-langley-v4w-3m4.108533948
- **The Township's bylaw has its own "seniors' housing" definition:** a Township council report varying Zoning Bylaw 2500 for a seniors' housing project records the bylaw definition's maximum seniors' housing dwelling-unit size as 70 m² (753 ft²) — Township zoning treats seniors' housing as a defined use separate from a strata's 55+ age bylaw, and the two should not be conflated on a page. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=866EA2E9-916E-485E-9075-F59C90961D19&folderId=47498C260508154601958591
- **Plans:** the Aldergrove Community Plan and the Aldergrove Core Area Plan govern the community, within the Township's Official Community Plan. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/resources/get-involved/ocp-update/Att-B---Aldergrove-CP---Redlined.pdf and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/community-planning.aspx
- **ALR check before any rural-lot assumption:** much of the Township's land is within the Agricultural Land Reserve, and the Township directs applicants to check a property's ALR designation and watercourses in GeoSource before applying. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- **Property taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes and utility fees were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to current-year taxes unpaid at midnight July 2, 2026, with a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026, and utility fees are now billed separately. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Aldergrove?** Two verified ownership options: strata rancher townhouses at Creekside Villa (27435 29A Ave, 55 units, LMS361), and manufactured homes in the Pioneer Park co-operative (27111 0 Ave), where the purchase includes co-op shares and a co-op acceptance step. No 55+ rental building or subsidized complex was verified inside Aldergrove. https://fraservalleyhomesold.ca/mylistings.html/listing.r3112002-33-27435-29a-avenue-langley-v4w-3m4.108533948 and https://murphyrealestategroup.ca/recip.html/1272399.search/SearchResults.form?_pg=3
- **Q: How is Pioneer Park different from a normal manufactured-home park?** It is a co-operative, not a pad-rental park: listings state the buyer must be accepted by the co-op and must purchase co-op shares ($9,200 in one listing, refunded upon sale), and pays a monthly co-op fee ($510 in the same listing) rather than pad rent to a landlord. Confirm the current figures and rules with the co-op — they are listing-stated, not a published tariff. https://murphyrealestategroup.ca/recip.html/1272399.search/SearchResults.form?_pg=3
- **Q: Do both of us have to be 55 at Pioneer Park?** One listing states the rule in its strictest form — "Strictly 55plus (both occupants must be 55)" — while another listing for the same park states only that the park is 55+ and that occupancy is limited to 2 occupants who are 55+. Because a co-op sets its own occupancy rules, get the co-op's current rule in writing before writing an offer; do not rely on the strata exemptions. https://murphyrealestategroup.ca/recip.html/1272399.search/SearchResults.form and https://murphyrealestategroup.ca/recip.html/1272399.search/SearchResults.form?_pg=3
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live at Creekside Villa?** For the strata complex, yes — provincial strata rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a Creekside Villa unit for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit at Creekside Villa?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** At Creekside Villa, MLS data records pets allowed with restrictions, and listings state 2 pets maximum with a shoulder-height limit of 14 inches. At Pioneer Park, listings state 2 pets allowed with no size restrictions. Confirm the current rule for the specific complex before writing. https://fraservalleyhomesold.ca/mylistings.html/listing.r3112002-33-27435-29a-avenue-langley-v4w-3m4.108533948 and https://murphyrealestategroup.ca/recip.html/1272399.search/SearchResults.form
- **Q: Is there long-term care in Aldergrove if I need it later?** Yes — Jackman Manor, 27477 28 Ave, is an 88-bed long-term care home operated by the Aldergrove Lions Seniors Housing Society in partnership with Fraser Health. Admission is through Fraser Health assessment, not by purchase or market rent. https://www.seniorsadvocatebc.ca/quickfacts/location/85553/ and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Langley/jackman-manor
- **Q: How do I get to Surrey or Abbotsford without driving?** TransLink route 503 runs from 272 Street at Fraser Highway to Surrey Central Station via Langley Centre (roughly 70–90 minutes end-to-end depending on time of day, per TransLink's timetable), and BC Transit route 21 connects Aldergrove with Abbotsford from the same stop. There is no SkyTrain in Aldergrove. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt503.pdf
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes for the strata: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. At Pioneer Park the pool is further narrowed by the co-op acceptance and share purchase steps in its listings. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://murphyrealestategroup.ca/recip.html/1272399.search/SearchResults.form?_pg=3

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Aldergrove's two 55+ ownership products run on two different legal systems.** Creekside Villa is a freehold strata under the Strata Property Act (plan LMS361); Pioneer Park is a housing co-operative where you buy the home plus co-op shares and the co-op approves the buyer. Fees, resale steps and occupancy rules are not interchangeable between the two, and neither is a pad-rental park of the kind the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act governs. https://fraservalleyhomesold.ca/mylistings.html/listing.r3112002-33-27435-29a-avenue-langley-v4w-3m4.108533948 and https://murphyrealestategroup.ca/recip.html/1272399.search/SearchResults.form?_pg=3
- **The "Creekside" name is used twice in the core, for different complexes.** Creekside Villa (27435 29A Ave) is the verified 55+ complex backing onto Bertrand Creek; Creekside Estates (27125 31A Ave) is a separate townhouse complex with no age restriction found in any source. A name-only search will mix them.
- **Old Creekside Villa listings say "45+" — that cannot be a valid strata bylaw today.** One older listing called a unit a "large 45+ rancher" while recording "Restricted Age: 55+" in its property data; since Nov 24, 2022, BC strata age bylaws below 55 are invalid, so only the 55+ reading can govern. https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/27435-29a-avenue/29 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Pioneer Park sits on 0 Avenue — the border road.** Its address, 27111 0 Ave, puts the co-op at the community's south edge on the US border, a rural setting apart from the Fraser Highway core where Creekside Villa and Jackman Manor sit; the two 55+ options are not interchangeable locations. https://murphyrealestategroup.ca/recip.html/1272399.search/SearchResults.form and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx
- **The Township bylaw's "seniors' housing" is a zoning term, not an age bylaw.** Zoning Bylaw 2500 defines seniors' housing with a maximum unit size (70 m² in the bylaw definition, per a Township council report) — a project can be zoned seniors' housing without being a 55+ strata, and Creekside Villa's 55+ status comes from its strata bylaw, not its RM-2 zoning. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=866EA2E9-916E-485E-9075-F59C90961D19&folderId=47498C260508154601958591
- **The seniors housing most directories attach to "Langley" is not in Aldergrove.** The Langley Lions Housing Society campus, including The Maple (101 independent seniors' homes, 5460 203rd St), is in Langley City — a buyer searching "Aldergrove seniors housing" will be shown Langley City product that is a separate community and a separate market. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/opening-more-seniors-housing-in-langley
- **Aldergrove's care home predates its 55+ strata.** Jackman Manor opened in 1982, a decade before Creekside Villa was built in 1992 — the community's seniors infrastructure started with non-profit care, and the ownership downsizer product is the later addition. https://www.seniorsadvocatebc.ca/quickfacts/location/85553/ and https://fraservalleyhomesold.ca/mylistings.html/listing.r3112002-33-27435-29a-avenue-langley-v4w-3m4.108533948
