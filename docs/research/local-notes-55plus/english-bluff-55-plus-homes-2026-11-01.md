# English Bluff, Tsawwassen (Delta) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44, Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022), the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1/123.2, permits a strata age bylaw only at 55 or older; lower thresholds (19+, 40+, 45+) are invalid. On this shore that law is mostly theoretical — there is no verified strata stock for it to apply to. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** People under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed; live-in caregivers; and, from May 1, 2023, a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children cared for by the resident before age 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rental bylaws remain allowed. Relevant only if a buyer later looks at Central's strata buildings — there is no strata rental stock on the bluff. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **The rule that actually governs this shore is a hazard permit area, not a housing rule.** Development Permit Area SD2, "SD2 English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment)," Category "b" in OCP Bylaw No. 8400, covers this waterfront: the OCP states the area is subject to mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence, and ocean-frontage slopes require protection of existing vegetation and tree cover. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SD2 building rules:** no habitable floor lower than the Flood Construction Level (unless a lesser elevation is set provincially); no building within 7.5 m of the natural boundary of the sea or within 7.5 m of the inboard toe of any dike; a restrictive covenant acknowledging flood risk is required; and development affected by hazardous conditions is subject to conditions set by a suitably qualified Professional Engineer. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, RS zones.** The RS3 Single Detached Residential 3 zone sheet describes a zone for single detached housing in the vicinity of Georgia Strait, with a minimum subdivision lot area of 4,000 m², lot width 30 m and average lot depth 30 m; zoning is parcel by parcel. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS3.pdf
- **Suites and added units:** Delta's Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing rules allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide, subject to the bylaw and to any hazard or streamside permit area — on the bluff, the SD2 conditions sit on top of that permission, which is the practical route to a caregiver or family suite here. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Foreshore is not part of the lot.** Delta states foreshore and water use is interjurisdictional and the foreshore below the natural boundary is Crown land administered through FrontCounter BC — upland ownership does not include the beach below the natural boundary. https://www.delta.ca:443/building-development/development-planning-approvals/development-considerations
- **Property taxes and 55+ relief:** Delta's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. Provincially, the regular property tax deferment loan program covers people 55 and older, and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) covers seniors 65+. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ complex on English Bluff itself?** No verified one. Research found no strata complex, retirement residence, seniors rental or manufactured-home park inside the sub-area; the housing is freehold detached homes under Bylaw No. 7600 RS zones. A 55+ purchase in Tsawwassen means Tsawwassen Central (The Lexington or Tsawwassen Greene). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/the-lexington-1150-54a-street
- **Q: If I buy a house here at 60, can anyone stop a younger family member living with me?** There is no strata and no age bylaw on a freehold detached lot, so no strata age rule applies at all. The provincial 55+ framework (residency not ownership, spouse/caregiver/child exemptions) only bites inside a strata corporation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I add a suite for a caregiver or family member?** Possibly. Delta allows up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots under its SSMUH rules, but an English Bluff lot also sits in Development Permit Area SD2, so the hazard conditions and a Professional Engineer's requirements govern what can actually be built. Confirm with the City for the specific lot. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: What does the bluff designation do to insurance and renovations?** The OCP itself documents mud flow, debris flow, erosion, land slip and subsidence hazards for this shore, requires a Flood Construction Level, 7.5 m setbacks from the sea's natural boundary, and a registered restrictive covenant acknowledging flood risk — expect a lender or insurer to find all of that in a search, and budget for the geotechnical report. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Can I clear trees to open up the view?** Not freely. The SD2 justification requires protection of existing vegetation and tree cover on the ocean-frontage slopes; removal can need approvals and can affect the slope itself. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Is the beach in front of a waterfront house private?** No. The foreshore below the natural boundary is Crown land, and Delta notes foreshore use is interjurisdictional (federal Fisheries Act, provincial Water Sustainability Act and Riparian Areas Regulation, and Delta bylaws). https://www.delta.ca:443/building-development/development-planning-approvals/development-considerations
- **Q: Where would I go if I needed care later without leaving Tsawwassen?** The ladder is in Central: independent strata living (The Lexington, Tsawwassen Greene), independent/assisted rental at the KinVillage campus (including 68 publicly subsidized assisted-living suites) and The Waterford (106 independent suites, 36 licensed care suites), then KinVillage West Court's 100 publicly subsidized long-term care beds. None of it is on the bluff. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/7/B/assisted-living-at-residence-at-kinvillage and https://briacommunities.ca/communities/waterford/
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd in Ladner (Fraser Health), is the nearest hospital; Fraser Health also lists a South Delta After-Hours Urgent and Primary Care Centre at 1826–4949 Canoe Pass Way in Tsawwassen. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- **Q: What transit serves the bluff day to day?** The 614 English Bluff community shuttle loops via 1 Avenue, English Bluff Road and 12 Avenue from South Delta Exchange, and the 604 runs peak service via English Bluff Road to Bridgeport Station. It is a shuttle-and-peak-bus pattern, not all-day frequent service — check the current TransLink timetable for the exact trip. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt603.pdf
- **Q: Can a 55+ buyer defer property taxes on a bluff house?** The provincial regular deferment program is open to homeowners 55 and older on their principal residence (a low-interest provincial loan; surviving spouses and persons with disabilities also qualify), and the City's tax page links the program. Confirm eligibility and current interest terms with the Province before relying on it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The OCP growth map deliberately bypasses the bluff.** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 focuses Tsawwassen growth on the Urban Centre, the 56 Street corridor, Southlands and Tsawwassen Springs — all outside English Bluff — and no named major project inside English Bluff was verified. The shore's character is protected by geography and the SD2 permit area, not by a heritage designation. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **The lot-size floor here is estate scale.** The RS3 zone that fits this shore sets a 4,000 m² minimum subdivision lot area; the wider Bylaw 7600 ladder runs from RS7 at 335 m² up to 4,000 m² for RS1/RS2/RS3 and RD2/RD3 — which is why there is no small-lot strata product on the bluff to age-restrict in the first place. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS3.pdf
- **Beach access is public, at set points.** Fred Gingell Park's stairs run from the upper park down to the beach, and the foreshore itself is Crown land below the natural boundary — a waterfront lot does not come with exclusive beach. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search?page=3 and https://www.delta.ca:443/building-development/development-planning-approvals/development-considerations
- **The restrictive covenant runs with the land.** SD2's guidelines require a covenant acknowledging the land may be subject to flooding, registered as part of development — a buyer will see it on title, and a seller should expect questions about it. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta's flood system behind areas like this is large and actively maintained:** over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure, operated by the City as diking authority. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **The elementary school is the sub-area's namesake anchor, not a housing complex.** English Bluff Elementary at 402 English Bluff Road is the landmark the Paragon name tracks (the name also matches TransLink's "604 English Bluff" route); there is no equivalent named residential complex in the sub-area. http://eb.deltasd.bc.ca/ and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt603.pdf
