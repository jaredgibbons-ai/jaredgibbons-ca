# West Newton, Surrey — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- West Newton is the Newton community land west of King George Boulevard toward the Delta border (Scott Road / 120 Street side), in the City of Surrey, including the Highway 99 industrial area at the south. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newton
- King George Boulevard is the east edge: East Newton is across it. Scottsdale / North Delta is west across Scott Road, Bear Creek Green Timbers is north, and the Serpentine & Nicomekl lowlands are south. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newton
- The Newton Town Centre at King George Blvd and 72 Ave is the City's designated town centre at the area's east edge; the Newton Recreation Centre is at 13730 72 Ave and the Newton Library at 13795 70 Ave. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/newton-town-centre-plan
- SD36 schools: T.E. Scott Elementary, 7079 148 St, is inside the plan area on the King George side; Bear Creek Elementary, 13780 80 Ave, is at the north edge; Frank Hurt Secondary, 13940 77 Ave, is the area secondary east of King George Blvd. Catchments are by home address through the SD36 School Locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- Transit: the R1 RapidBus runs on King George Boulevard (Newton Exchange to Guildford Exchange); the R6 Scott Road RapidBus runs on the area's west edge between Scott Road Station and Newton Exchange; routes 323 (via 124 St) and 364 (via 64 Ave) run through the area. No SkyTrain station is inside West Newton. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/R6/direction/0/round-trip
- Health care: Surrey Memorial Hospital, 13750 96 Ave; Surrey-Newton Urgent and Primary Care Centre, 6830 King George Blvd. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Surrey/surrey-newton-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
- Where the 55+ housing actually concentrates: the Scottsdale / 121 Street corridor at the west edge (Glenwood Village, 7156 121 St; Lakeview Estates on W Boundary Drive at the Boundary Park edge), one small manufactured-home park on King George Boulevard itself (Newton Mobile Home Park, 6571 King George Blvd), and one subsidized seniors complex on 75A Avenue (PICS, 12075 75A Ave). Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

Its W Boundary Drive address sits at the Boundary Park edge it shares with the Panorama Ridge complexes in the Panorama Ridge notes — the MLS sub-area for this complex is West Newton, not Panorama Ridge. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6090-w-boundary-drive/104 and https://condos.ca/surrey/6090-boundary-drive-west-surrey/unit-104-R2940514

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. An older building source for Glenwood Village at 7156 121 St describes it as designed for adults "45+" — that wording predates the current law; the MLS restricted-age field for the complex now records 55+, and the registered bylaws and Form B are what govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/204-7156-121-st-42603
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. Note the live conflict this creates in West Newton data: MLS building data for Glenwood Village still records "Rentals Not Allowed" in its strata-restrictions field — that field reflects an old bylaw record, not the current provincial law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/204-7156-121-st-42603
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000** governs West Newton — residential zones R1–R6 in the neighbourhood portion and industrial zones in the Highway 99 industrial area at the south of the plan area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newton
- **Plans:** within the West Newton NCP set, the City states West Newton South was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan update and is no longer in effect; the Newton Town Centre Plan covers the King George Blvd / 72 Ave core. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newton
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH):** Surrey allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; one secondary suite per house under the BC Building Code; properties not connected to community water and sewer are among the exemptions. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Pad tenancy at Newton Mobile Home Park runs under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not strata law:** when a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser, and the park owner's consent process and pad-rent increase formula in that Act apply — see the province-wide mobile-home notes for the full rules. Surrey also has a Manufactured Home Park Policy for park redevelopment, and Surrey's Tenant Protection Bylaw, 2025, No. 21788 does NOT apply to property governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_reg_21788.pdf
- **Flood check for south-end streets:** the south end of West Newton falls toward the Serpentine & Nicomekl lowlands, the dyke-protected floodplain south of Newton, while the residential upland portion is outside that lowland floodplain — confirm a specific lot against City flood mapping. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- **Property taxes (city-wide, not area-specific):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Glenwood Village Heights the same as Glenwood Village?** No — and the difference is the whole point. Glenwood Village, 7156 121 St (NWS2768, 1988), is the verified 55+ gated complex. Glenwood Village Heights, 6875 121 St (NWS3423), states "NO AGE RESTRICTIONS" in its current listings. Check the strata plan number on the listing and the Form B, not just the name. https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/204-7156-121-st-42603 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6875-121-street/132
- **Q: What 55+ homes can I buy in West Newton?** Two strata townhouse complexes verified in this research — Glenwood Village (7156 121 St) and Lakeview Estates (6090 W Boundary Drive, 20 homes) — plus manufactured homes on rented pads at Newton Mobile Home Park (6571 King George Blvd, 34 sites), where you own the home and rent the site. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3075595-204-7156-121-street-surrey-bc-v3w-0j6 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6090-w-boundary-drive/104 and https://www.mhbo.com/mobile-home-park/83905-newton-mobile-home-park-6571-king-george-blvd-surrey-bc-v3w-4z4
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: The listing says rentals are not allowed at Glenwood Village. Is that still true?** Not for long-term rentals. Every strata rental-restriction bylaw in BC became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, even where MLS building data still records the old restriction. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and the strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/204-7156-121-st-42603
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: How does buying at Newton Mobile Home Park differ from buying a strata townhouse?** You buy the manufactured home and rent the pad it sits on — there are no strata fees, the purchase is subject to park approval, and your ongoing land cost is pad rent under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act rather than a strata fee under the Strata Property Act. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6571-king-george-boulevard/6 and https://www.mhbo.com/mobile-home-park/83905-newton-mobile-home-park-6571-king-george-blvd-surrey-bc-v3w-4z4
- **Q: Is the PICS housing on 75A Avenue something I apply for, or buy?** Apply — you cannot buy it. PICS states Guru Nanak Niwas is 77 independent-living units, all subsidized by BC Housing, for residents over 55 with a low income; the assisted-living side at the same address has 49 publicly subsidized units. https://pics.bc.ca/free-programs-services/senior-care-facility/ and https://www.seniorsadvocatebc.ca/quickfacts/location/1866/
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Complex-by-complex. MLS data for Glenwood Village records pets allowed; listings at Newton Mobile Home Park state one pet is allowed, subject to park rules. Confirm the current bylaw or park rule for the specific complex before writing. https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/204-7156-121-st-42603 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6571-king-george-boulevard/6
- **Q: What transit serves the 121 Street complexes?** Two RapidBus corridors bracket West Newton — the R1 on King George Boulevard to Surrey Central Station and the R6 on Scott Road to Scott Road Station, both connecting to the Expo Line; routes 323 and 364 run through the area streets. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/R6/direction/0/round-trip
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Two complexes on the same street carry almost the same name and opposite age rules.** Glenwood Village (7156 121 St, NWS2768) is 55+; Glenwood Village Heights (6875 121 St, NWS3423) advertises no age restrictions. A search filtered on the name alone will mix the two — the strata plan number is the reliable separator. https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/204-7156-121-st-42603 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6875-121-street/132
- **Older descriptions of Glenwood Village say "45+".** A building source describes the 7156 121 St complex as designed for adults 45+ — a pre-2022 description. Since Nov 24, 2022, a BC strata age bylaw below 55 is invalid, and the complex's MLS restricted-age field now records 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Boundary Park's 55+ complexes are split between two sub-areas.** Lakeview Estates (6090 W Boundary Drive) is filed by MLS under West Newton, while Lakewood Place and Lakewood Heights on the same drive are filed under Panorama Ridge — the same park-edge cluster appears on two different 55+ pages depending on the complex. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6090-w-boundary-drive/104
- **West Newton's subsidized seniors housing and its assisted living share one address and one operator.** At 12075 75A Ave, PICS runs both the 77-unit subsidized independent-living building (Guru Nanak Niwas, over-55, low income) and a 49-unit publicly subsidized assisted-living residence — the local ladder from independent to assisted living without leaving the complex. https://pics.bc.ca/free-programs-services/senior-care-facility/ and https://www.seniorsadvocatebc.ca/quickfacts/location/1866/
- **The King George Boulevard park is West Newton's only verified 55+ manufactured-home park, and it is small.** Newton Mobile Home Park has 34 home sites — turnover, not a large resale pool, is the supply. https://www.mhbo.com/mobile-home-park/83905-newton-mobile-home-park-6571-king-george-blvd-surrey-bc-v3w-4z4
- **West Newton South is a rescinded plan.** The City states that part of the West Newton NCP set was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update — density claims for lots near the 55+ complexes should be checked against the current OCP and Zoning Bylaw 12000, not the old plan. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newton
