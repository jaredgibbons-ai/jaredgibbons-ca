# East Newton, Surrey — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- East Newton is the Newton community land east of King George Boulevard, in the City of Surrey; the City's East Newton plan material is bounded by King George Highway to the west, 76 Avenue to the south, 152 Street to the east and Newton Athletic Park to the north. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- King George Boulevard is the dividing street: West Newton is across it to the west, Panorama Ridge / Sullivan is south, and Fleetwood Tynehead is northeast. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- The local pocket name inside East Newton is Chimney Hill, carried by Chimney Hill Elementary and Chimney Heights Park. https://www.surreyschools.ca/schools/chimneyhill/
- SD36 schools in the area: Chimney Hill Elementary, 14755 74 Ave; M.B. Sanford Elementary, 7318 143 St; Tamanawis Secondary, 12600 66 Ave; Panorama Ridge Secondary, 13220 64 Ave — school names and addresses confirmed against the BC Ministry of Education K-12 schools file, 2025/2026. https://www.surreyschools.ca/schools/chimneyhill/
- Newton Athletic Park, 7395 128 St, sits at the north edge of the plan area; the Newton Recreation Centre and Newton branch of Surrey Libraries are at the 13795 70 Ave town-centre site, west of King George Boulevard. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/newton-athletic-park
- Transit: the R1 RapidBus runs on King George Boulevard between Newton Exchange and King George Station via Surrey Central Station; route 321 runs via 152 St and King George Blvd; route 324 runs via 132 St. No SkyTrain station is inside East Newton. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/R1/direction/0/round-trip
- Health care: Surrey Memorial Hospital, 13750 96 Ave; Surrey-Newton Urgent and Primary Care Centre, 6830 King George Blvd. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Surrey/surrey-newton-urgent-and-primary-care-centre

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000** governs East Newton; MLS data for Chelsea Gardens records its zoning as RM45. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf and https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/201-13870-70-ave-60859
- **Plans:** the East Newton North NCP was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan update and East Newton South's land-use designations were superseded by that update, with the NCP still guiding public amenities and infrastructure — read the current OCP and Zoning Bylaw 12000, not just the old NCP documents. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH):** Surrey allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; the BC Building Code permits one secondary suite per house. This applies to the detached streets around the 55+ complexes, not to the strata lots inside them. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Manufactured-home parks in Surrey carry a City policy layer on top of the provincial Act:** the City's Manufactured Home Park Policy brochure tells park residents the property owner must give 12 months' notice of a final moving date, can only give that notice after receiving all permits and approvals, and must pay $20,000 on or before the move-out date. Surrey's Tenant Protection Bylaw, 2025, No. 21788 does NOT apply to property governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act — park residents rely on the provincial Act and the City policy, not that bylaw. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_reg_21788.pdf
- **Property taxes (city-wide, not area-specific):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in East Newton?** Two strata complexes verified in this research — Chelsea Gardens (13870 / 13888 70 Ave, LMS1416) and Charleston Green (6537 138 St, NWS2625) — plus one non-strata option, Sullivan Mews (15020 66A Ave), where you buy a membership, not a strata lot. https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/201-13870-70-ave-60859 and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/listing/r3060854-37-6537-138-street-surrey-bc-v3w-0c4 and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3058951-15020-66a-avenue-49-surrey-bc-v3s-2a5/brochure
- **Q: Why does Sullivan Mews say no mortgages are possible?** Because, per its listing, it is not a strata — the purchase is a membership, so there is no strata title for a lender to register against, and the listing states a purchase must be cash. Confirm the current membership and financing terms in the membership documents before writing an offer. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3058951-15020-66a-avenue-49-surrey-bc-v3s-2a5/brochure
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in Chelsea Gardens or Charleston Green as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is Arbourside Court something I can buy?** No. Arbourside Court, 13751 74 Ave, is an independent retirement living residence with supportive services — its own site describes senior apartments with rents, operating since 2001. It is a place to rent with services, not strata ownership. https://www.arbourside.com/
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the 55+ complexes?** It is complex-by-complex. MLS data for Chelsea Gardens records pets allowed with restrictions; Charleston Green listings state pets allowed; Sullivan Mews listings state 1 small pet and a maximum of 2 occupants. Confirm the specific pet bylaw in the Form B and registered bylaws. https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/201-13870-70-ave-60859 and https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/57-6537-138-st-1173642 and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3058951-15020-66a-avenue-49-surrey-bc-v3s-2a5/brochure
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. That trade-off is built into every BC 55+ strata since the 2022 law change confined age bylaws to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day from the 70 Avenue cluster?** The R1 RapidBus on King George Boulevard links Newton Exchange and King George Station via Surrey Central Station for the Expo Line; there is no SkyTrain station in East Newton itself. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/R1/direction/0/round-trip
- **Q: Where is the closest urgent care?** The Surrey-Newton Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 6830 King George Blvd, and Surrey Memorial Hospital is at 13750 96 Ave. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Surrey/surrey-newton-urgent-and-primary-care-centre

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Chelsea Gardens is two addresses and two housing forms under one name.** Apartment condos sit at 13870 70 Ave and ground-level townhouse-style homes at 13888 70 Ave, in one gated LMS1416 community — a buyer comparing two Chelsea Gardens listings may be comparing different building forms and fee schedules, not just different units. https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/201-13870-70-ave-60859 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/13888-70-avenue/328
- **The "55+" label on the King George Boulevard manufactured-home parks near East Newton does not hold up.** Crispen Bays (7790 King George Blvd) and Crestway Bays (8220 King George Blvd) are listed by a park directory as all-ages, and Crestway listings call the park family-oriented — a buyer who assumes every Newton park is seniors-only will be wrong on these two. https://www.mhbo.com/browse/mobile_home_parks/bc/surrey
- **Sullivan Mews' monthly fee is not a strata fee.** Its listing states the $540 monthly amount covers taxes, heat, electricity, management, insurance and water because the purchase is a membership — comparing that figure line-by-line against a strata fee at Chelsea Gardens misleads. Listing-stated — confirm current amounts in the membership documents. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3058951-15020-66a-avenue-49-surrey-bc-v3s-2a5/brochure
- **The retirement residence on 74 Avenue runs on a 65+ basis, not 55+.** Building sources describe Arbourside Court as geared to adults 65 and older — a 58-year-old downsizer who qualifies for the strata complexes does not automatically qualify there. https://www.condosurrey.com/buildings/view/30779/arbourside-court/surrey/east-newton/13751-74-ave
- **The old East Newton plans are partly dead documents.** The City states East Newton North was rescinded and East Newton South's designations were superseded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update — a seller quoting an old NCP density for a lot near these complexes is quoting a superseded plan. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- **Surrey's 2025 Tenant Protection Bylaw does not cover manufactured-home parks.** Bylaw No. 21788, adopted for rental-building redevelopment, expressly does not apply to property governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act — park residents' protection is the provincial 12-month notice and $20,000 compensation framework plus the City's Manufactured Home Park Policy. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_reg_21788.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf
