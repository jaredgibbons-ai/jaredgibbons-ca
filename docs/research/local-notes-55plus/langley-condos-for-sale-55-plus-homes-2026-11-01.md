## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; any bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). This is the test for Highgate Terrace: "age restrictions" without a number cannot be rounded up to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** Under-55s can own but may not be able to reside; exemptions attach to a "specified resident." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** as of Nov 24, 2022; stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals. Parkway Estates and Parkway II still carry "rentals not allowed" in building records — that line cannot be relied on for a long-term rental. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Two municipalities, two zoning bylaws:** Langley City Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 governs the City condos; Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs the Township complexes. Neither creates a 55+ status — the age rule comes from the strata bylaw, park rule or co-op rule. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/schedule-zoning-bylaw-2025-no-3300pdf
- **Suites / SSMUH, by municipality:** City — Bylaw 3284 (June 17, 2024): RS1/RS2 permit 4 units, 6 within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; City suite cap is the lesser of 100 m² and 40%, owner must reside on the lot. Township — Bylaw 6020 (Nov 18, 2024): 3 units on lots ≤280 m², 4 on lots to <4,050 m², no 6-unit tier; Township suite cap 120 m².
- **Langley City short-term rentals: UNVERIFIED** on any City page in this program — flagged, not filled, because it matters to investor math on condos.
- **Manufactured-home parks run on park rules, not strata law:** age rules at Cedar Creek, Fernridge and Pineridge are park rules on a leasehold pad, and Pioneer Park's are co-op rules; pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003), which recognises a park reserved for tenants who have reached 55. Pad rent increases are capped at 2.3% for 2026 plus a proportional levy/utility amount, once per 12 months on 3 full months' notice. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Property taxes — two calendars:** Township taxes and utility fees were due Thursday, July 2, 2026 (5% penalty at midnight July 2, 2026, further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026); the City also sets a July 2 due date, with its penalty rate UNCONFIRMED on the page cited. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx and https://www.langleycity.ca/PayPropertyTaxes
- **Tax relief at 55+:** regular property tax deferment is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities); the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which verified 55+ condos can I buy in Langley?** Four condo-form buildings: Parkway Estates (5360 205 St), Parkway II (5363 206 St) and Glenmont Park (5375 205 St) in Langley City, and Waterfront at Bedford Landing (23215 Billy Brown Rd) in Fort Langley. The other 11 verified Langley 55+ ownership products are townhouses, park homes or a co-op — not condos. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: What is the median price of a Langley condo?** SnapStats does not publish one — it combines condos and townhomes, whose combined Langley median was $611,000 in September 2026 (699 listings, 128 sold). Do not quote that as a condo figure (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report).
- **Q: The feed showed a 55+ unit on 53a Avenue — is that a fifth verified condo?** UNCONFIRMED as a named complex. The card at 205 20600 53a Avenue describes a 55+ community in listing text, but no building at that address was verified on a strata or manager source in this pass. Treat it as a lead to verify against the strata documents for that unit, not as a roster entry. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-condos-for-sale.html
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ condo?** Yes — an exemption is required for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children and caregivers are exempt in defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy for a parent or as an investment?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. Under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a 55+ parent can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ condo?** Long-term rental bans are void since Nov 24, 2022, whatever a Parkway building record still shows. The tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals; Langley City STR status is UNVERIFIED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the Langley City 55+ condos?** Strict end of the market: Parkway Estates and Parkway II record pets not allowed. Glenmont Park's pet rule in the sources cited here: UNCONFIRMED — a listing in the City file describes no pets, so confirm in the bylaws. Waterfront at Bedford Landing records pets allowed with restrictions (cats and dogs OK, limit two). https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/parkway-estates-5360-205-street and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/waterfront-at-bedford-landing-23215-billy-brown-road
- **Q: What should I check before buying any Langley condo, 55+ or not?** The live page's own checklist is the diligence core: strata bylaws, depreciation report, recent strata minutes, Form B information certificate and contingency reserve fund — plus, on newer Willoughby buildings, the exact home-warranty position from the documents, not the listing. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-condos-for-sale.html
- **Q: Does "Langley" here mean the City or the Township?** Both — and it matters: different zoning bylaws (City No. 3300 vs Township No. 2500), different tax notices, and the 55+ condos split 3 City / 1 Township. Confirm the municipality on title. Sources: Section 3 above.
- **Q: Is SkyTrain a current amenity for a Langley City 55+ condo?** No — it is a 2029 fact. All eight stations are under construction with operation expected in late 2029, ending at Langley City Centre. Price today's commute on today's bus network. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0022-000515

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Langley's verified 55+ condos are old, central and few.** Three of the four are 1988–1990 Langley City buildings within a few blocks on 205/206 Street; the fourth is a 2009 Fort Langley building. There is no verified new-build 55+ condo in Langley — the new Willoughby towers dominating condo searches are general-market. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **The condo page cannot be priced off its own board data.** SnapStats has no condo-only cut, so every "Langley condo median" a buyer sees elsewhere is either the combined $611,000 attached figure relabelled, or invented. The research file states the limitation openly; this file follows it (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report).
- **Fee inclusions change the real monthly cost in the City cluster.** Parkway II listings state strata fees include heat, hot water and electricity, and Glenmont Park listings state electricity/gas/hot water included — in 1988–1990 buildings, an inclusive fee can beat a newer unit's lower headline fee plus separate utilities. Listing-stated; the strata documents govern. https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/5363-206-street/210
- **Two Langley seniors names are traps in condo searches.** "The Maple" (subsidized rental, 5460 203rd St) is not "Maple Manor" (general-market rental, 20117/20119 56th Ave), and "Sunridge Gardens" is a rental residence, not condos for sale. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/opening-more-seniors-housing-in-langley and https://centurygroup.ca/seniors-living/
- **The "rentals not allowed" lines on the Parkway buildings are legally dead for long-term rentals.** Both carry that line in public data, and all such bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022 — the surviving restrictions are the age bylaw and any short-term rental rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Most of Langley's 55+ ownership is not condo form at all.** Eleven of the fifteen verified products are townhouse strata, park homes or a co-op — a buyer who searches only "condos" misses Walnut Grove's four townhouse complexes, Brookswood's three parks and Aldergrove's co-op entirely. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **The Langley City 55+ cluster sits on the future SkyTrain corridor.** The extension runs along Fraser Highway to 203 Street in Langley City, in service expected 2029 — the 205/206 Street cluster is in the core that corridor serves, while no station serves Willoughby, Walnut Grove or Fort Langley directly. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/rapid-transit-projects/surrey-langley-skytrain
