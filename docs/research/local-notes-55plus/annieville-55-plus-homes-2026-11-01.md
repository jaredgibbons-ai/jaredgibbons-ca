# Annieville — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. This is the rule that decides Delta Cedars and Kennedy Gate: an unstated "age restriction" in an old building is not evidence of a valid 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. Building sources for Delta Cedars and Kennedy Gate still list "rentals not allowed"; that wording is out of date against this law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017** applies parcel by parcel. Zone sheets read for this area include RD3 at a 4,000 m² minimum lot, RS6 at 390 m² and RS1 at 4,000 m²; the full ladder runs RS1/RS2/RS3/RD2/RD3 at 4,000 m², RS4 at 610 m², RS5 at 550 m² or 460 m², RS6 at 390 m², RS7 at 335 m², RD1 at 836 m² and RD4 at 504 m², and there is no RD5 zone. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS6.pdf
- **Seniors congregate housing is a named use in Delta's apartment zones.** The City's Apartment Residential 6 (RA6) zone, added to Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 in 2024 for six-storey apartment development under the OCP's Neighbourhood Centres and Corridors designation, lists "seniors congregate housing" as a permitted principal use alongside apartment buildings. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future")** is Delta's adopted OCP, adopted July 8, 2024; it consolidated the former area plans into a single Land Use Plan, and carries the ND3 Annieville Development Permit Area for the River Road village and the ND1 Fraser River Escarpment Development Permit Area on the river frontage in Appendix B. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/ and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Secondary suites and small-scale multi-unit housing:** the RS1 zone lists a secondary suite as an accessory use to a single detached dwelling, and Delta's SSMUH amendments allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — the route by which an Annieville house lot adds a suite for a parent or caregiver. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Property tax deferment at 55+:** BC's Property Tax Deferment Program (regular program) lets a homeowner who is 55 or older during the current year defer annual property taxes on a principal residence as a provincial loan; the City of Delta's tax page links the program and the Home Owner Grant. Delta's 2026 tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility and http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Subsidized seniors rental runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry.** BC Housing's Zone 2 directory (North Delta, Langley and Surrey, May 2025) is the directory covering this area; none of its Delta–North seniors buildings sits in Annieville. https://www.bchousing.org//publications/Zone-2-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ condo I can buy in Annieville?** No verified one. Delta Cedars (8635 120 St) and Kennedy Gate (8921 117A St) are the buildings buyers assume are age-restricted, and their sources do say an age restriction exists — but neither source states the age, and any age below 55 has been unenforceable since Nov 24, 2022. Get the strata's current bylaws before treating either building as 55+. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/delta-cedars-8635-120-street
- **Q: What seniors housing does exist here, then?** Columbus Lodge at 8850 118A Street — a 31-unit seniors independent-living rental with meals included, run by the Columbus Charities Association (Knights of Columbus). It is a rental with services, not a purchase, and its exact age threshold is UNCONFIRMED in the sources. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/NDR/250717/NDRN250717_A05.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Delta?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, so the "rentals not allowed" wording still shown for Delta Cedars and Kennedy Gate does not hold. Two catches remain where a valid 55+ bylaw exists: the tenant still has to satisfy the age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is there a seniors centre in the sub-area?** Yes — Kennedy Seniors Recreation Centre at 11760 88 Avenue, billed by the City as recreation for 50+, operated with the Kennedy Seniors Society. It sits in the same cluster as Columbus Lodge. http://www.delta.ca/services/seniors/seniors-recreation-centres/lists/seniors-recreation-centres/kennedy-seniors-recreation-centre
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day, and is there a seniors bus?** Routes 312 (via 112 Street) and 391 (via 116 Street) both run between Scottsdale Exchange and Scott Road Station (Expo Line). Separately, the City runs a free Delta Seniors Bus for Delta residents aged 65+ — note the 65+ threshold, not 55+ — with curbside pick-up/drop-off inside Delta, medical appointments given priority, and booking up to 7 days ahead. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt312.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/seniors-services/seniors-bus
- **Q: Is the River Road side a hazard area?** The frontage sits in the ND1 Fraser River Escarpment Development Permit Area; the OCP lists mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence for that area, so slope-side and riverfront lots need geotechnical review in the subject list. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Can I add a suite or coach house for family on an Annieville house lot?** Delta's SSMUH rules allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — confirm the parcel's zone sheet under Bylaw No. 7600 before promising a configuration. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes here at 55+?** Yes, if the home is your principal residence — BC's regular deferment program is open at 55 or older during the current year (also to surviving spouses and persons with disabilities). Delta's tax page links the program; the City's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility and http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The "age restriction" on the two Kennedy-cluster stratas may legally no longer exist.** Building sources for Delta Cedars and Kennedy Gate say an age restriction is in the bylaws but never give the number. Since Nov 24, 2022 the only enforceable strata age bylaw in BC is 55+; a 19+, 40+ or 45+ bylaw in either building is void, and a listing or building page repeating "age restrictions" does not tell you which case you are in. The strata's filed bylaws are the only proof. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The seniors centre's age is 50+, the seniors bus's age is 65+, and neither makes the housing 55+.** Kennedy Seniors Recreation Centre is billed as recreation for 50+; the Delta Seniors Bus is for residents 65+. Three different age thresholds operate within a few blocks in this cluster — check the one that applies to the service you mean to use. http://www.delta.ca/services/seniors/seniors-recreation-centres/lists/seniors-recreation-centres/kennedy-seniors-recreation-centre and http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/seniors-services/seniors-bus
- **Columbus Lodge is run by a charity, not a strata or a care chain.** The operator is the Columbus Charities Association (the Knights of Columbus organization, colchar.org), and the City directory lists it as seniors housing with meals and a residents' bus — an independent-living rental model, not assisted living and not a purchase. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2021-06/SocialServicesInventory%20CS.pdf and https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/NDR/250717/NDRN250717_A05.pdf
- **North Delta has no subsidized assisted living at all.** The City's Affordable Housing Guide (April 2026) states there are two assisted living facilities in Delta — Augustine House in Ladner and the Residences at KinVillage in Tsawwassen, both accessed through Fraser Health. A buyer who will need assisted living later is looking at a move to South Delta, not a building in this sub-area. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
- **The big-lot pattern on the river side is an infill pattern, not a seniors pattern.** A City bylaw notice for 10604 River Road proposed rezoning from RD3 to RS6 for a two-lot subdivision under Bylaw No. 7600 — the large river-side parcels are being split into small house lots, which is where new ground-level housing in Annieville actually comes from. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/224579/
- **School catchments are address-based even beside the elementary school.** The SD37 locator result for 9240 112 Street is Annieville Elementary and Delview Secondary, but Annieville's east-side addresses can fall to other schools — relevant to grandparents buying with family use in mind. Check each address in the locator. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
