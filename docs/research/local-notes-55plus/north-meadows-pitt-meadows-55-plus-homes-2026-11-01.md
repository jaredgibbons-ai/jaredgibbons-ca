## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. This matters in North Meadows in reverse: a strata here (for example at The Fairways) could lawfully adopt a 55+ bylaw in future by ¾ vote of the owners, but none checked has one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://choa.bc.ca/resources/strata-property-act/legislative-updates/
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Short-term rentals are PROHIBITED city-wide in Pitt Meadows.** Short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in the City's Zoning Bylaw, per the City's zoning bylaw amendments staff report. Do not price any North Meadows property — including the boat-access Pitt Lake cabins carried in this sub-area's listing feed — on short-term-rental income. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011.** The rural zones that cover most of this sub-area set large minimum lot areas for subdivision: A-1 General Agricultural 8 ha (one farm house per lot to a maximum gross floor area of 500 sq m), A-2 Large Lot Agricultural 16 ha, RR-2 4 ha, RR-3 2 ha, RR-4 1 ha (the RR zones are for land outside the Agricultural Land Reserve), and RS Large Lot Residential 0.2 ha. Any specific parcel's actual zone and ALR status are per-parcel: UNVERIFIED here. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 - Consolidated Version_0.pdf
- **Suites and second dwellings:** a garden suite is permitted in the A-1, A-2, RR-1 to RR-5 and RS zones, one per lot, 33 sq m to 90 sq m, and a property may have a garden suite or a secondary suite, not both. On ALR land the Agricultural Land Commission also allows one additional detached residence without an application where there is one existing residence when construction begins, to a maximum of 90 sq m on properties 40 ha or less. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 - Consolidated Version_0.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Plans:** the Official Community Plan "I See Pitt Meadows 2040" covers the whole city including this sub-area, and the North Lougheed Area Plan, adopted by OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3060, 2026, covers approximately 50 ha north of Lougheed Highway between Meadows Gardens Golf Course and Harris Road, with development to proceed gradually and further planning, technical studies and Council approvals required. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/homes-development/zoning-land-use/north-lougheed-area-plan
- **Flood:** over 95% of the Pitt Meadows land base lies within the floodplain for the Fraser River and, to a smaller degree, the Pitt River, per the City's 2020 Business Plan — this is the settled current figure; older ~85%/~86% figures are superseded and must not be used. The city is almost entirely protected by 60 km of dikes; the northern drainage areas (Alouette Pump Station catchment, Fenton, and the Pitt Polder catchment) are drained by floodboxes and pump stations because of low elevation. Flood construction levels are governed by the City's Flood Construction Bylaw 2384. 78% of the city's land falls within the Agricultural Land Reserve per the same Business Plan, with property-level ALR status confirmed per address. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- **Wells and septic on rural lots:** whether a given North Meadows lot is on municipal water/sewer or on a well and septic system is not confirmed per lot on an official source — UNVERIFIED. Well records can be checked in the provincial GWELLS application, and an onsite sewerage system must comply with the Sewerage System Regulation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- **City taxes:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026 under the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026, with a 5% penalty added after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 1, 2026. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** for this area (Zone 4 directory, May 2025) — and that directory's Pitt Meadows entries are the Advent Road and Ford Road co-ops, neither in this sub-area. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

Its board-level sub-area classification is UNCONFIRMED from public sources; on the City's geography it is not a North Meadows building. https://thewesbrooke.com/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. If I buy in the city's 55+ strata in Central Meadows, can we both live there?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a 55+ unit for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building, or run a short-term rental on a Pitt Meadows acreage or Pitt Lake cabin?** Two different answers. In a strata, long-term rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022, but the tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption. Short-term rentals are prohibited city-wide in Pitt Meadows — short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in the Zoning Bylaw — so no property here, cabin included, can be priced on short-term-rental income. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
- **Q: Can an acreage lot here have a second dwelling for a caregiver or family member?** A garden suite is permitted in the A-1, A-2, RR and RS zones, one per lot at 33 to 90 sq m (a lot may have a garden suite or a secondary suite, not both), and on ALR land the Agricultural Land Commission allows one additional detached residence to 90 sq m on properties 40 ha or less where the conditions are met. Per-lot zone and ALR status must be confirmed for the specific parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: How does transit work here if I give up driving?** Thinly. The verified service is on the south edge: route 701 via Harris Road to Pitt Meadows Station and the 719/722 service on Wildwood Crescent and Hammond Road. The R3 RapidBus stop at Harris Road and route 791 are UNVERIFIED at timetable level in this program — do not plan around them without checking TransLink's current timetables. No route serving the rural north itself was confirmed, and there is no West Coast Express station in the sub-area. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt701.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt719.pdf
- **Q: What should I check about flood risk on a North Meadows home?** The City states over 95% of Pitt Meadows is in the floodplain, protected by about 60 km of dikes, and the north side is low Pitt Polder land drained by floodboxes and pump stations. Ask for the parcel's flood construction level under Flood Construction Bylaw 2384, its dike and drainage details, and your insurer's flood terms before subject removal. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The "North Meadows" label reaches Pitt Lake.** Listings labelled "North Meadows PI" include boat-access-only cabins on Debeck Creek at Pitt Lake, far north of the urban edge — so a saved search on this sub-area name returns golf condos, farm acreages and off-grid-style cabins in one feed. Check the street and the access, not the label. https://jaredgibbons.ca/north-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.html
- **Advent Road is a trap for 55+ searchers.** The city's 55+ pocket (Meadowland Village strata and the Meadowlands co-op) is on Advent Road south of the highway in Central Meadows — while Highland Park Elementary, also on Advent Road at 18961, sits in this sub-area north of the highway. The same street name appears in both sub-areas; the civic address and which side of Lougheed Highway it sits on decide which file a building belongs in.
- **The one buyable 55+ complex in the city has disagreeing published numbers.** Meadowland Village in Central Meadows is described as 26 homes in listing text and a "20 Unit Development" in a building source, with the build year given as both 1988 and 1989 — carried here as flagged conflicts, not resolved figures. A North Meadows buyer cross-shopping it should work from the strata plan and Form B, not listing sheets.
- **The north side is pump-station country.** The Pitt Polder catchments sit low enough that drainage runs through floodboxes and pump stations behind the dikes, and the Codd Island Wetlands in the rural north are undiked floodplain wetlands — flood due diligence here is about the specific catchment and lot, not a city-wide yes/no. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- **The North Lougheed Area Plan is new and gradual.** The plan for about 50 ha between Meadows Gardens Golf Course and Harris Road was adopted by OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3060, 2026, but the City states development will proceed gradually with further planning, technical studies and Council approvals required — a designation to know about, not a project to price in. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/homes-development/zoning-land-use/north-lougheed-area-plan
- **September 2026 attached counts underline how small this market is:** North Meadows recorded 9 active Condos & Townhomes listings and 2 sold (22% sales ratio), and its detached shelf was 1 active listing against 2 sold — a 200% ratio that is a small-numbers artefact of those tiny counts, not a demand signal (September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report).
