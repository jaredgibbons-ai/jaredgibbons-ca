# Edmonds BE (Burnaby) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Edmonds BE is the southeast Burnaby town centre around Kingsway at Edmonds St, served by Edmonds Station on the Expo Line. The Edmonds Community Plan, adopted March 2025, divides the town centre into the Central, South, Station, West, Northwest and East Edmonds districts. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-04/Edmonds-Community-Plan.pdf and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- Schools: Edmonds Community Elementary, 7651 18th Ave (the SD41 locator returns Edmonds plus Byrne Creek for the area); Twelfth Avenue Elementary, 7622 12th Ave; Byrne Creek Community Secondary, 7777 18th St. Burnaby is School District 41 — 41 elementary and 8 secondary schools, no middle-school tier (K–7 / 8–12), with address-based catchments. https://burnabyschools.ca/about-sd41-overview/ and https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- Edmonds Community Centre, 7433 Edmonds St, includes the **Edmonds Centre for 55+** — a City seniors activity centre (recreation, not housing). https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/edmonds-community-centre
- Health: the Edmonds Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at #201-7315 Edmonds St; the nearest hospital is Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/burnaby/edmonds-urgent-and-primary-care-centre and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- Where the seniors housing actually is: Edmonds has the deepest seniors stock of any Burnaby sub-area in this batch — an independent-living rental residence (Mulberry PARC, Acorn Ave), three registered assisted-living residences (Southoaks Crescent, Rosewood St, 17th Ave), three long-term care homes (Cumberland St, 4th St, New Vista Place) and a large subsidized seniors rental cluster (New Vista Place / Mary St). ALL of it is rental or care. None of it is strata ownership.

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**No verified 55+ strata ownership complex was found in this sub-area.** Despite Edmonds' reputation for seniors buildings, every verified seniors building here is a rental residence, an assisted-living residence, a long-term care home or subsidized non-profit housing. A buyer cannot purchase in any of them — do not let a 55+ page imply otherwise.

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- None verified in this sub-area.

### Verified 55+ rental (not ownership)

- No standalone market 55+ rental apartment building was verified in this sub-area. The independent-living rental product here is a retirement residence (Mulberry PARC, below), and the big independent seniors rental cluster is subsidized (New Vista, below).

- **Rosewood by Nikoniko — 7868 Rosewood St, V5E 2H1.** A small assisted-living community, registered in BC's Assisted Living Registry (class SN), operator BTN Senior Care Management LP; the operator describes a small assisted-living community run on Japanese values of hospitality (omotenashi). https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ABurnaby&f%5B1%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASupportive%20Recovery&name=&address=&order=alr_residence_type&sort=asc

- **Normanna (Normanna Rest Home) — 7725 4th St, V3N 5B6.** Non-profit long-term care home with 100 publicly subsidized beds, owned and operated by the Norwegian Old People's Home Association. Care only. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/0/4/normanna-rest-home
- **New Vista Care Home — 7232 New Vista Place, V3N 1B5.** Long-term care home for 240 residents, part of the New Vista Society's Edmonds campus (the society's resident welcome guide states the 240-resident figure). ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Assisted living here is registry housing, not strata:** Nikkei Home, Rosewood by Nikoniko and Liberty Place are registered with BC's Assisted Living Registry (class SN); subsidized suites in registered residences are allocated through Fraser Health, and the long-term care homes (George Derby, Normanna, New Vista Care Home) are publicly subsidized beds accessed through Fraser Health — none of these routes involves a purchase. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ABurnaby&f%5B1%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASupportive%20Recovery&name=&address=&order=alr_residence_type&sort=asc
- **Zoning bylaw — note the name change.** Research briefs for Burnaby sometimes still cite the "Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742)". The City states the new **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026** was adopted June 9, 2026, replaced the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965, and took effect July 1, 2026. Use the 2026 bylaw. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/bylaws/zoning-bylaw
- **R1 SSMUH zoning:** Burnaby consolidated its single- and two-family districts into the R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing district in June 2024 — 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; multi-unit lots may be stratified. Edmonds also sits in a SkyTrain transit-oriented area, where the provincial framework allows substantially taller residential buildings near the station. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP and town centre plan: the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan** was adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP, and takes precedence over older community-plan maps; the Edmonds Community Plan itself was adopted in March 2025. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan and http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-04/Edmonds-Community-Plan.pdf
- **Property taxes:** Burnaby's 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date; the City's 2026–2030 Financial Plan approved a 2.9% tax increase (about $61 on the average home). The detached single-family water utility charge is $652.33, with a 5% discount if paid by the due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Edmonds is known for seniors buildings — what 55+ homes can I actually buy there?** None that any source verifies as an age-restricted strata. Edmonds' seniors buildings are real but they are rental and care: Mulberry PARC (independent-living rental), Nikkei Home, Rosewood by Nikoniko and Liberty Place (assisted living), George Derby, Normanna and New Vista Care Home (long-term care), and New Vista Place / Ernest Winch Tower (subsidized rental). A buyer in Edmonds is purchasing a regular condo, townhome or house with no age bylaw. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: What is Mulberry PARC, and can I buy a unit there?** Mulberry PARC, 7230 Acorn Ave, is an independent-living RENTAL retirement residence run by PARC Retirement Living — you lease a suite with residence services; there is no strata title to buy. https://parcliving.ca/mulberry-parc/
- **Q: How do I get into the New Vista seniors buildings?** By application, not purchase: New Vista Place and Ernest Winch Tower (7210 and 7216 Mary St) house 348 independent seniors, the majority in rent-geared-to-income homes, under BC Housing programs — the route in is the BC Housing registry/application process and the society, not a listing. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/more-than-150-affordable-homes-coming-to-burnaby
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Burnaby?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — a BC age bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is there culturally specific seniors care in Edmonds?** Yes, twice over: Nikkei Home (6680 Southoaks Crescent) is assisted living run by the Nikkei Seniors Health Care and Housing Society, and Rosewood by Nikoniko (7868 Rosewood St) is a small assisted-living community run on Japanese hospitality values — both registered with BC's Assisted Living Registry. Normanna (7725 4th St) is the Norwegian community's non-profit care home. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ABurnaby&f%5B1%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASupportive%20Recovery&name=&address=&order=alr_residence_type&sort=asc and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/0/4/normanna-rest-home
- **Q: What transit and daily services does a senior here actually use?** Edmonds Station (Expo Line) anchors the town centre, and the Edmonds Community Centre at 7433 Edmonds St houses the City's Edmonds Centre for 55+ for programs and activities — recreation space, not housing. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/edmonds-community-centre
- **Q: Where is the hospital and urgent care?** The Edmonds UPCC is in the town centre at #201-7315 Edmonds St for urgent and primary care; the nearest hospital is Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/burnaby/edmonds-urgent-and-primary-care-centre and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in Edmonds?** This is Edmonds' real strength: the in-area ladder runs from independent subsidized rental (New Vista Place / Ernest Winch Tower) and independent-living rental (Mulberry PARC), through assisted living (Nikkei Home, Rosewood, Liberty Place), to long-term care (George Derby, 300 beds; Normanna, 100 beds; New Vista Care Home, 240 residents) — every step rental or publicly funded, none of it purchased. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/3/A/george-derby-centre and https://newvista.bc.ca/Brochures/Welcome-Guide-for-Residents-and-Families.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Edmonds' seniors depth is a non-profit story, not a market one.** The cluster exists because societies built here over decades — the New Vista Society's care home and towers, the Norwegian Old People's Home Association's Normanna (100 beds), the Nikkei society's assisted living, and the George Derby Care Society's 300-bed centre on a site with veterans' care history back to 1947/1948. That is why the stock is application-based: it was never built to sell. https://georgederby.ca/newsite/about-us/ and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/0/4/normanna-rest-home
- **The town centre is mid-reinvention.** The Edmonds Community Plan was adopted in March 2025 with six districts, and the Kingsway + Edmonds master plan contemplates replacing Hall Towers (331 rental units, tenants not displaced) and adding about 1,400 purpose-built rental and roughly 1,000 market strata homes on the City/BC Housing site — future general-market supply, none of it designated 55+. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/kingsway-edmonds-conceptual-master-plan and http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-04/Edmonds-Community-Plan.pdf
- **Watch for the "Derby Manor" name in directories.** One directory gives it an address on 16th Ave while a City zoning bylaw titles it at 7550 Cumberland St — the George Derby Centre's own address. Until a primary source separates the two, treat any "Derby Manor, Burnaby" listing as UNCONFIRMED and do not double-count it as an extra care home. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/3/A/george-derby-centre
- **The same society runs both a seniors-only cluster and a mixed building.** New Vista operates the 348-home independent seniors cluster (New Vista Place / Ernest Winch Tower) and the Eunice Oh Residence (7898 18th Ave), which mixes families, seniors and individuals — the operator's name on a building does not make it a 55+ building. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/more-than-150-affordable-homes-coming-to-burnaby
- **Burnaby replaced its entire zoning bylaw in 2026.** The Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (adopted June 9, 2026, in force July 1, 2026) repealed and replaced the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 — documents or summaries that still cite the 1965 bylaw are out of date. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Burnaby taxes have two penalty steps.** 2026 property taxes were due July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date, and the detached water charge ($652.33) carries a 5% early-payment discount. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
