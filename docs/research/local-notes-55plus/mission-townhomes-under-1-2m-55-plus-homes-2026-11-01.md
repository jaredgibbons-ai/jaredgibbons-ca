## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2: a strata may restrict residents to 55 or older; any age bylaw below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** Under-55s can own a lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; exemptions attach to a "specified resident." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. Cherry Lane's "Family Exemptions" listing wording points at this framework — confirm the registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; SHORT-TERM rental bylaws are still allowed. Directory lines saying "rentals not allowed" at Cherry Lane / Cedarbrook Estates cannot be relied on for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Secondary suites are not a townhome feature in Mission:** City rules state suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes; a suite is a self-contained space inside a single family home, must be registered with the City, and a new suite goes through the building permit process. A "townhome with suite income" claim on this feed needs parcel-level verification. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
- **Manufactured-home parks run on tenancy law, not strata law** — Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act: own the home, rent the pad; increases once in 12 months on three full months' notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026) plus a proportional share of levy/utility changes. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020** is Mission's zoning bylaw. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning
- **Current plan: Official Community Plan Bylaw 6405-2026** replaces and repeals OCP Bylaw 6350-2025, same vision/framework/mapping with a Regional Context Statement added; it carries an Urban Residential Townhouse designation among its designations. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026 and https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan
- **Property taxes:** City of Mission 2026 taxes due Thursday, July 2, 2026, flat 10% penalty on outstanding current balances on July 3, 2026 — a single step, no 5% + 5% inside the city; diking levies ride on top of the municipal rate. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/tax-rates

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which townhomes on this page are actually 55+?** Only two complexes in Mission are verified 55+ strata, and both are townhouses: Cherry Lane (32165 7th Ave, 20 units, 1989, NWS2971) and Luther Place (7525 Martin Place, NWS2729, 1988), both recording "Restricted Age: 55+" in listings. Every other townhome complex named on the feed is general-market or UNCONFIRMED — see Section 2. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/cherry-lane-32165-seventh-ave
- **Q: Is there a median price for a Mission townhome under $1.2M?** No such figure exists. SnapStats publishes no townhome-only split and no price-band cut — its combined Condos & Townhomes community median was $430,000 in September 2026 on 10 sales ($642,500 in August on 12), which mixes condos in and swings month to month. Price a townhome off its own complex. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: Why does a detached rancher show up on a townhomes page?** Because the feed is a site filter, not a form check: Heritage Park Place cards describe detached ranchers inside a strata subdivision. Confirm whether a unit is a townhouse, a detached strata lot or a bare-land strata lot, and read the strata plan and bylaws, before comparing it to freehold product. https://jaredgibbons.ca/mission-townhomes-under-1-2m.html
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live at Cherry Lane or Luther Place?** Yes — a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident (or legacy-exemption holder) is exempt by regulation; children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a 55+ townhome now for a parent, or to move into later?** Ownership is not age-restricted — residing is. You can buy now; a 55+ parent can be the resident, and you could not live there yourself until you meet the bylaw or fit an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a 55+ townhome, or add a suite for income?** Renting: long-term rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022, but the tenant must meet the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. Suites: Mission does not permit secondary suites in townhouses or rowhomes at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
- **Q: What pets are allowed in the 55+ townhomes?** Cherry Lane records 1 cat or 1 small dog (15 in. at the shoulder); Luther Place records pets welcome with restrictions. Confirm against the registered bylaws for the specific unit. Sources: Section 2.
- **Q: How is the commute from a Mission townhome?** By West Coast Express: five weekday morning trains from Mission City Station (5:25–7:25 a.m.) reach Waterfront in 75 minutes, five afternoon returns, no weekend or holiday service — a real weekday asset and nothing outside those windows. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital, and what if I need care later?** Mission Memorial Hospital, 7324 Hurd St. Both 55+ townhome complexes sit on the central bench, in the same few blocks as Cedar Valley Manor (life lease), Chartwell Cedarbrooke and Carrington House (rental/assisted) and The Cedars on Hurd St — the ladder is local. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Mission/mission-memorial-hospital
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale pool?** Yes, mechanically: the next buyer must be able to live there under the bylaw (55+, exempt spouse/partner, caregiver, legacy) or buy without living in it — and Mission's attached market is thin (10 attached sales community-wide in September 2026), so complex-level evidence matters more than community medians. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

- **Feed names are listing text, not verification.** Spencer Estates, Cherryville, 58 on Cherry Hill, Black Bear Estates and Archer Green all appear only inside listing cards on this feed; none was confirmed on a developer or strata source in this research. https://jaredgibbons.ca/mission-townhomes-under-1-2m.html
