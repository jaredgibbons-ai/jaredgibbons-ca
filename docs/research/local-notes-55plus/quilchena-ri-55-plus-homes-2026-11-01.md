## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). In Quilchena this matters mostly as a filter: the pocket has no strata stock for such a bylaw to attach to. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** People under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed; live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** Since Nov 24, 2022 no strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw; stratas may still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500, Section 8.1 (Single Detached RS1/RS2)** is the pocket's documented zoning; minimum lot areas by subzone include 360 m² (RS1/B, RS2/B), 550 m² (RS1/E), 828 m² (RS1/F) and 2,000 m² (RS1/G), and a secondary suite is a permitted secondary use. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=24187
- **Plan: Richmond's 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000,** is the city-wide plan; the Laurelwood Sub-Area Plan is the City sub-area plan for the Granville Avenue / Railway Avenue bend. No approved major project specific to the Quilchena pocket was confirmed. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm and https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/laurelwood562.pdf
- **Suites / SSMUH — the actual "55+ strategy" in a house pocket:** the City allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit such as a coach house per lot on covered lots, with Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (RSM) zoning applied under Bill 44 and provincial minimums of three units on lots 280 m² or less and four units on larger lots. A suite or coach house is how a caregiver or family member lives on a Quilchena lot — there is no age-restricted building to buy instead. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Flood — Richmond is a dike-protected island city:** Richmond is made up of islands within the Fraser River floodplain at an average of 1 metre above sea level; the flood protection system includes 49 km of dikes, is designed for a 1:500 event (0.2% chance in any given year), and dike raising from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres is under way. All lands in Richmond are designated flood plain under Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw No. 8204, which sets flood construction levels (FCLs) habitable floors must meet unless exempt. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** (Zone 3: Richmond and West Delta directory, May 2025) — a Quilchena senior who needs subsidized rental applies there and is matched city-wide, not to a building in the pocket. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Quilchena?** None — zero verified stock. No age-restricted strata building, and in fact no named strata building of any kind, was confirmed inside the pocket on any official, developer or strata source. What you can buy in Quilchena is a freehold detached house. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=24187
- **Q: Is there any seniors rental or subsidized housing in the pocket?** None verified inside it. BC Housing's Zone 3 directory puts its west Richmond seniors buildings on Blundell Road and Railway Avenue (Rosewood Towers, 6220 Blundell Rd; Rosewood Village, 8280 No. 2 Rd; Ukrainian Village, 8720 Railway Ave) — all south of the pocket, applied for through the Housing Registry. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: Rosewood Towers shows up when I search "Quilchena seniors." Is it in Quilchena?** UNCONFIRMED. Rosewood Towers (6220 Blundell Rd) and Rosewood Manor care home (6260 Blundell Rd) are real BC Housing / care stock on the Blundell corridor, but no source places them inside the Quilchena pocket around Moresby Drive, and Quilchena has no published boundary to settle it. Do not market them as Quilchena buildings. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: How do seniors actually age in place in Quilchena?** In their own houses, using the suite rules: the RS1/RS2 zones list a secondary suite as a secondary use, and the City allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit such as a coach house per lot on covered lots — space for a caregiver or family member on the same lot. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. If we buy into a 55+ strata elsewhere in Richmond, can we both live there?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in the defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. Under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a 55+ parent can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Is Quilchena in a floodplain?** All of Richmond is designated flood plain under Bylaw No. 8204, behind a 49 km dike system designed for a 1:500 event, with dike raising from 3.5 m to 4.7 m under way; habitable floors must meet the lot's flood construction level unless exempt — a real cost item on any Quilchena rebuild or major renovation. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital and the community centre?** Richmond Hospital, 7000 Westminster Highway, is the city's hospital, with the City Centre UPCC at 110, 4671 No. 3 Road; the pocket's community centre is Thompson Community Centre, 5151 Granville Avenue, across Granville Avenue to the north. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/health/about-bc-s-health-care-system/heath-care-partners/hospitallist.pdf and https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/centres/thompson.htm
- **Q: What transit serves the pocket?** Only the wider-network route is confirmed: the 401 (One Road) via Westminster Highway and No. 1 Road between Richmond-Brighouse Station and Steveston. A bus stop inside the pocket itself is UNVERIFIED — do not promise walk-to-bus in listing copy. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt401.pdf
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in Quilchena?** No care home or assisted-living residence with a pocket address was verified. The nearest named care stock on a public source is Rosewood Manor long-term care, 6260 Blundell Rd (155 residents, by referral through Richmond Continuing Health Services, per an older Richmond seniors directory), and Richmond's registered retirement residences are in the City Centre. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/RIR/131023/RIRN131023_Z36.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Quilchena" is a school name doing duty as a neighbourhood name:** the City publishes no Quilchena boundary and no Quilchena plan — the plan for the Granville / Railway bend is Laurelwood. Marketing that draws a firm "Quilchena" boundary is inventing one; the honest anchors are Quilchena Elementary, Moresby Drive, Granville Avenue and Railway Avenue. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/laurelwood562.pdf
- **The pocket's lot sizes swing by a factor of five by subzone:** RS1/RS2 subdivision minimums run from 360 m² (RS1/B, RS2/B) to 2,000 m² (RS1/G) — two houses on the same street can carry completely different suite, coach-house and redevelopment math. Confirm the subzone before pricing either. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=24187
- **Richmond's seniors system is directory-driven, not storefront-driven:** the west Richmond subsidized stock (Rosewood Towers, Rosewood Village, Ukrainian Village) fills through BC Housing's Housing Registry under the Zone 3 directory — a senior downsizing out of a Quilchena house applies once to the Registry and is matched across the city, which is why none of these buildings advertises vacancies like a market rental. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **The dike is part of the utility bill:** Richmond's 2026 typical single-family metered utility total of $2,251 per year includes a $342 flood protection line — the dike system is a standing annual charge on every Richmond home, Quilchena included. https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/finance/utility-fees/utilityrates.htm
- **Richmond's two money dates:** 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% on any outstanding balance after Tuesday, September 1, 2026; flat-rate utilities were due March 31, 2026 with a 10% discount only if paid by the due date. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- **No SkyTrain, no bus exchange, no shopping centre:** nothing in the sources read puts rapid transit or a retail centre inside the pocket — the trade for Quilchena's quiet house streets is that every service a senior uses daily (hospital, library main branch, community centre) sits outside the pocket, in the City Centre or across Granville Avenue. https://rpl.yourlibrary.ca/about/hours-locations and https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/centres/thompson.htm
