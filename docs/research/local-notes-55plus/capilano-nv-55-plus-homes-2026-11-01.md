# Capilano (North Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- This file covers the Capilano Road corridor in the District of North Vancouver: the residential streets on both sides of Capilano Road between the Upper Levels Highway (Highway 1) and the Cleveland Dam, around the Capilano River canyon. Anchors on the corridor are Capilano Suspension Bridge Park at 3735 Capilano Road and the Cleveland Dam at the north end, in Capilano River Regional Park. Exact boundary streets: UNVERIFIED. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01), and https://www.capbridge.com/faq/
- Naming split that matters for this file: District and school sources use "Upper Capilano" for the plateau north of Highway 1 (Canyon Heights, Capilano Highlands, Grousewoods) and "Lower Capilano / Lions Gate" for the Marine Drive end south of the highway. The seniors housing verified for this area is not on Capilano Road itself — it is one ridge east, in **Edgemont Village** (Highland Boulevard / Connaught Crescent), which the research file treats as the corridor's east-side centre: the Capilano Library, 3045 Highland Boulevard, is described by the North Vancouver District Public Library as "located in the heart of Edgemont Village." https://www.nvdpl.ca/capilano-library and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- Schools beside the corridor: Canyon Heights Elementary, 4501 Highland Boulevard; Highlands Elementary, 3150 Colwood Drive; Cleveland Elementary, 1255 Eldon Road; Handsworth Secondary, 1033 Handsworth Road. School District 44 catchments are address-based (K–7 elementary, 8–12 secondary, no middle tier) — check each address with SD44. Capilano Elementary, 1230 West 20th Street, carries the name but sits in the City of North Vancouver lowlands south of the corridor. https://www.sd44.ca/school/canyonheights/Pages/default.aspx and https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/044
- Transit: TransLink route 236 runs the length of Capilano Road toward Grouse Mountain and Lonsdale Quay; route 232 runs via Capilano Road, Edgemont Boulevard and Highland Boulevard; route 246 crosses the corridor via Capilano Road and Montroyal Boulevard with downtown trips over the Lions Gate Bridge. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt236.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt246.pdf
- Recreation and health: the Delbrook Community Recreation Centre, 851 West Queens Road, is the nearest NVRC full-service centre; Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver, is the North Shore hospital. https://www.nvrc.ca/facilities-fields/locations-hours/courts and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital
- Where the seniors housing actually is: in Edgemont Village, on and around Highland Boulevard — one verified 55+ strata (Edgemont Villa, 3151 Connaught Cres), a second building listed as 55+ by a North Shore guide (Highland House, 3088 Highland Blvd), and one full-service rental residence with assisted living and long-term care (Amica Edgemont Village, 3225 Highland Blvd). No seniors housing of any type was verified on Capilano Road itself or on the canyon-side streets. Sources: Section 2 below.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Zoning: District of North Vancouver Zoning Bylaw 3210, Section 525.** The R1 detached zone minimum lot is 4,000 m² (30 m width, 34 m depth); R2 is 660 m² (18 m width, 34 m depth), with modifiers R2(S1) 335 m² / 10 m, R2(S2) 1,100 m² / 24 m and R2(S3) 4,000 m² / 30 m. An R2 principal building may include a secondary suite; a coach house is a dwelling unit accessory to and detached from the principal dwelling. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Three units on a house lot:** since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary. The District's SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop. For a corridor homeowner, the suite/coach house is the realistic ageing-in-place mechanism, since the corridor itself has no seniors buildings. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf
- **OCP: District of North Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900**, which names Edgemont as one of the District's six Village Centres — the planning basis for the village's denser, walkable form that the seniors buildings sit in. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **Hazard permit areas:** OCP Bylaw 7900, Schedule B sets seven Development Permit Areas including Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard — canyon-edge and ravine lots on this corridor are exactly what they were written for, and the District publishes property-level GeoWeb reports including modelled flood construction levels. https://dnv-docs.simplicitycms.ca/documents/ocp-schedule-b-dpa.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/
- **Assisted living is not an age-bylaw product:** Amica Edgemont Village's assisted-living suites are registered under BC's Assisted Living Registry and its long-term care floor is licensed care — admission is the operator's/health system's decision, not a strata bylaw question. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ALangley&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc
- **Property taxes:** District of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 residential municipal rate is 1.83419 and the total residential rate 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value; combined single-family utilities are $2,571.20 in 2026. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in the Capilano area?** One solidly verified building: Edgemont Villa, 3151 Connaught Cres in Edgemont Village — 20 freehold strata condos, built 2003, described as 55+ across multiple listings. Highland House (3088 Highland Blvd) is listed as 55+ by one North Shore building guide, but no second source confirming its bylaw was found — verify before treating it as the second option. There is nothing age-restricted on Capilano Road itself. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is there a rental option if I don't want to buy?** Yes, one: Amica Edgemont Village at 3225 Highland Blvd offers independent living suites on a rental basis, with assisted living, long-term care and memory care in the same residence. There is no general 55+ rental apartment building in the area. https://www.amica.ca/edgemont-village
- **Q: Can I add a suite or coach house for a parent or caregiver on a Capilano house lot?** Generally yes in form: District zoning has permitted three units total (principal dwelling, secondary suite, coach house) on single-family lots within the Urban Containment Boundary since June 2024. The lot's zone, size, and any creek/slope/wildfire permit area decide the specific case — canyon-edge lots need the GeoWeb check first. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day?** From Edgemont Village, routes 232 and 246 run via Highland and Edgemont Boulevards (246 reaches downtown over the Lions Gate Bridge); route 236 runs the Capilano Road corridor itself to Lonsdale Quay and Grouse Mountain. The SeaBus at Lonsdale Quay crosses to Waterfront Station in 12 minutes. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt236.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt246.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver (Vancouver Coastal Health); the North Vancouver Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 221 West Esplanade, North Vancouver. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/north-vancouver-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in the area?** At the rental-residence step, yes — Amica Edgemont Village runs independent living through long-term care and memory care under one roof in Edgemont Village. There is no publicly funded long-term care home inside this sub-area; the District's VCH care homes are in Lynn Valley (Cedarview Lodge) and the hospital district. https://www.amica.ca/edgemont-village/care-and-support/assisted-living
- **Q: Is Edgemont Village part of "Capilano"?** For housing searches, effectively yes: the District's OCP names Edgemont a Village Centre immediately east of the corridor, the Capilano Library sits in it, and every verified seniors building for the Capilano side is there. But note the trap in reverse — Capilano Elementary (1230 W 20th St) and Capilano House (1551 Bridgman Ave) are City-side, south of the highway, and are not this area's buildings. https://www.nvdpl.ca/capilano-library and https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/044

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The corridor's traffic is the area's defining fact, and the seniors buildings avoid it.** Capilano Road carries all Grouse Mountain and Suspension Bridge traffic; Grouse Mountain's address is 6400 Nancy Greene Way, at the top of Capilano Road, the only road up. Edgemont Village, one ridge east, gets the walkability without the tour-bus route at the door — which is precisely where the 55+ stock sits. https://www.grousemountain.com/contact and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt236.pdf
- **Edgemont Villa is tiny, and that cuts both ways.** With only 20 condos (built 2003), listings are rare and the building's resale story is a handful of sales; a buyer set on it is waiting for a specific unit, not choosing from a market. https://storage.ubertor.com/nshomes.myubertor.com/content/featuresheet_289_document/46715.pdf
- **The same crescent holds the area's newest general-market condos.** Crescentview at Edgemont (3121 Connaught Cres, completed 2023, 22 condos) stands beside Edgemont Villa — a downsizer who does not need the 55+ bylaw has a brand-new alternative on the same street, and a seller at Edgemont Villa competes with it. https://www.vancouvernewcondos.com/properties/crescentview-north-vancouver/
- **Highland House is the file's open verification item.** It appears as a 55+ building in exactly one source (a realtor's building guide) and nowhere else public that this research could find — no building-data page, no listing text. If it is genuinely 55+, it is the area's second purchase option; if the guide is stale, it is not. Form B and the filed bylaws settle it in one step. https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/on-the-market/55-buildings-north-vancouver/
- **The reservoir is upstream of the whole corridor.** The Cleveland Dam (completed 1954, 91 m high) holds back Capilano Lake, the drinking-water reservoir above these homes, and the Capilano River Hatchery below the dam (completed 1971) normally draws about 250,000 visitors a year — context for both the hazard-permit checks and the weekend traffic. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/capilano-river-regional-park and https://www.pac.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/sep-pmvs/hatcheries-ecloseries/capilano-eng.html
- **Amica Edgemont is the District's west-side care anchor.** It is the only Assisted Living Registry entry on the District's west side in this research — the other District registrations are in Lynn Valley (Cedar Garden) — so the Capilano/Edgemont side's assisted-living capacity is one private residence. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ALangley&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc
