# Connaught Heights (New Westminster) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Connaught Heights is the west-end residential neighbourhood of New Westminster, on the bench above the Fraser River around 22nd Street Station (Expo Line). The City's Official Community Plan treats the station area as a Frequent Transit Development Area; London Street and Ninth Avenue are its inner streets, with Marine Way at the south edge and the 20th/22nd Street corridors framing it. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports and https://www.newwestcity.ca/housing/affordable-housing/connaught-heights-small-sites/sb_expander_articles/1840.php
- The housing stock is detached houses on RS-1 lots — in September 2026 the sub-area's entire listed stock was detached: 15 listings, 0 sales, and 0 condos & townhomes (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report). There is no strata building in the area to carry an age restriction.
- Schools: for the sample address 2201 London Street, the district pattern is Connaught Heights Elementary (which hosts a Montessori K–5 program), Fraser River Middle School and New Westminster Secondary; School District 40 catchments are address-based, so run the exact address through the district before relying on it. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/
- Park: Grimston Park is the neighbourhood park. The City runs its park and facility listings citywide rather than by neighbourhood. http://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/parks/community-parks
- Retail is minimal inside the neighbourhood — the City, in its own Connaught Heights small-sites housing consultation, describes the area as having limited local shops and services, with residents using 22nd Street Station and the commercial nodes outside the area. https://www.newwestcity.ca/housing/affordable-housing/connaught-heights-small-sites/sb_expander_articles/1840.php
- Transit: 22nd Street Station (Expo Line) is the area's anchor — one stop pattern into downtown New Westminster one way and toward Metrotown the other — supplemented by local buses on the Burnaby–New West network. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2024-schedules/september/burnaby-new-west-p155.pdf
- Health: Royal Columbian Hospital, 330 East Columbia Street in Sapperton, is New Westminster's hospital — a Fraser Health regional referral and teaching hospital. Its redevelopment is the largest health project in the city: the new 388-bed Jim Pattison Acute Care Tower opened to patients May 31, 2026, with Phase 3 upgrades to the existing buildings expected to complete in 2029. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/New-Westminster/royal-columbian-hospital and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/accessing-health-care/capital-projects and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/news/2026/May/Opening-the-new-Jim-Pattison-Acute-Care-Tower-at-Royal-Columbian-Hospital
- The public library's main branch is downtown at 716 6th Avenue — outside the sub-area; the library system's site is the starting point for branch and program information. https://www.nwpl.ca/

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**Connaught Heights has NO verified 55+ housing of any tenure — no 55+ strata, no 55+ rental, no retirement residence, no assisted-living registration and no subsidized seniors building with a Connaught Heights address was found in any source checked.** The neighbourhood's listed stock is detached houses. New Westminster's seniors buildings are elsewhere in the city (see the Checked list for where they actually are). Do not market anything in this sub-area as 55+.

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- None. There is no strata complex in the sub-area (0 condos & townhomes listed, as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report).

### Verified 55+ rental (not ownership)

- None. No purpose-built 55+ rental building with a Connaught Heights address appears in any source checked, including BC Housing's Zone 5 directory for Burnaby and New Westminster. https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076

### Retirement residences and care (rental/care — NOT strata ownership)

- None in the sub-area. BC's Assisted Living Registry lists exactly two registered seniors assisted-living residences in New Westminster — Thornebridge Gardens, 649 8th Avenue, and Victoria Heights, 230 Ross Drive — and neither is in Connaught Heights. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANew%20Westminster&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=asc

### Subsidized seniors rental (non-profit, income-based — not ownership)

- None in the sub-area. Citywide context: the City's own reporting counts 1,257 units of non-market housing in New Westminster in 2023, including 60 units of supportive seniors housing and 599 units for independent seniors — but those buildings sit in other neighbourhoods (Uptown, Downtown, Glenbrooke side), not in Connaught Heights. https://www.ubcm.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/New Westminster.pdf
- The one live housing file inside the area is not seniors housing: the City has consulted on small-sites affordable housing at 2035 London Street and 2038 Ninth Avenue — a general affordable-housing process, and the Ninth Avenue site carries a Crown Land Grant for parks and recreation use, which complicates any housing use. Nothing seniors-specific is attached to either site in the City's material. https://www.newwestcity.ca/housing/affordable-housing/connaught-heights-small-sites/sb_expander_articles/1840.php

### Checked and NOT 55+ (do not put these on a 55+ page)

- **The detached-house stock itself.** RS-1 houses with secondary suites and laneway/carriage houses are the whole market here — general-market family housing, with no age restriction mechanism attached to a freehold house lot. (Research statement: sources checked Nov 1, 2026.)
- **Thornebridge Gardens — 649 8th Avenue.** New Westminster's best-known retirement residence sits by Moody Park/Uptown, roughly across the city from Connaught Heights — it surfaces in "New Westminster seniors" searches and gets mentally filed under whatever neighbourhood the searcher lives in. It is not in this sub-area (see the Moody Park file). https://www.verveseniorliving.com/thornebridge-gardens/
- **Victoria Heights — 230 Ross Drive.** The city's other registered assisted-living residence (operator Good Samaritan Canada) is in Victoria Hill, east of the Quay — not in Connaught Heights. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANew%20Westminster&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=asc

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. Note the practical point for this area: an age bylaw can only exist inside a strata corporation, and Connaught Heights has no strata stock — a freehold house cannot be "55+" by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001.** Connaught Heights houses sit in the Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series, s.310) — RS-1 sets a minimum site area of 557.40 m² (6,000 sq ft) and a floor space ratio of 0.5. The RS districts allow secondary suites, and laneway / carriage houses are regulated under the same bylaw — the realistic "downsizer" tool kit in this area is a suite or a laneway house on a house lot, not a condo. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Plan: Our City 2041, the Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 7925, 2017** — the 22nd Street Station area is designated a Frequent Transit Development Area, the plan's growth node for the west end. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- **Transit-oriented development pressure:** provincial transit-oriented development rules apply around 22nd Street Station, and Marine Way land assemblies at the area's south edge have been marketed on that basis (listing material has claimed up to 20 storeys / 5.0 FSR potential). Those figures are listing-stated, not verified against the City's adopted bylaws — treat them as UNCONFIRMED and check any specific site with the City before pricing a house on assembly value.
- **Flood:** the City's floodplain study divides the city into four zones — Zone 1 Queensborough, Zone 2 the west-end Fraser River industrial lands, Zone 3 the Quay waterfront, and Zone 4 the Brunette River industrial lands. The Connaught Heights residential bench sits above the Zone 2 industrial flats; floodplain status is a parcel check, not a neighbourhood assumption. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- **City taxes:** New Westminster 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applying July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 3, 2026 on unpaid current taxes. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/whats-happening/index/main/175/site_post/page10.php

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a 55+ home in Connaught Heights?** No — there is none. The sub-area has no strata building, no co-op, no retirement residence and no seniors rental; its entire September 2026 listed stock was detached houses (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report). No verified 55+ strata ownership was found anywhere in New Westminster in the sources checked — the city's seniors stock is rental and care, in other neighbourhoods.
- **Q: I want to downsize but stay in the west end. What actually works here?** The house-based routes: an RS-1 house with a secondary suite (for family or a caregiver), or a lot that can take a laneway/carriage house under Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 — RS-1 minimum site area is 557.40 m² with FSR 0.5, and suites and laneway houses are regulated in the same bylaw. Confirm the specific lot's eligibility with the City. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Q: Is there a retirement residence near Connaught Heights?** Not in the sub-area. The city's two registered assisted-living residences are Thornebridge Gardens (649 8th Ave, by Moody Park/Uptown) and Victoria Heights (230 Ross Dr, Victoria Hill) — both a drive or SkyTrain ride east. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANew%20Westminster&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=asc
- **Q: Is there subsidized seniors housing in the west end?** None verified in Connaught Heights. The City counts 599 independent-seniors non-market units citywide (2023), but they are in other neighbourhoods; applications for Housing Registry buildings run through BC Housing, and the in-area small-sites process at 2035 London St / 2038 Ninth Ave is general affordable housing, not seniors housing. https://www.ubcm.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/New Westminster.pdf and https://www.newwestcity.ca/housing/affordable-housing/connaught-heights-small-sites/sb_expander_articles/1840.php
- **Q: Will the station area change the neighbourhood?** The OCP designates the 22nd Street Station area a Frequent Transit Development Area, and provincial TOD rules plus Marine Way assemblies point to taller forms at the edges over time. What is approved for any given site is a City check; listing claims of 20 storeys / 5.0 FSR are UNCONFIRMED against adopted bylaws. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- **Q: My spouse is under 55 — if we buy into a 55+ strata elsewhere in the city, can we both live there?** Yes, wherever a genuine 55+ strata exists: provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases — under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a suite in a Connaught Heights house?** Suites are a zoning question here, not a strata one: the RS districts in Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 allow secondary suites, and laneway/carriage houses are regulated under the same bylaw. (The province-wide voiding of strata rental bylaws doesn't apply — there is no strata.) https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Q: Which hospital serves the area, and is it being rebuilt?** Royal Columbian Hospital, 330 East Columbia Street — and yes: the 388-bed Jim Pattison Acute Care Tower opened May 31, 2026 as Phase 2 of the redevelopment, with Phase 3 renovations expected to complete in 2029. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/accessing-health-care/capital-projects
- **Q: Is Connaught Heights in the floodplain?** The City's flood study puts the west-end Fraser industrial lands in Zone 2; the residential bench above them is a different elevation. Floodplain status is parcel-specific — ask the City about the exact lot rather than assuming either way. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- **Q: When are New Westminster taxes due, and what are the penalties?** 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and a second 5% applies September 3, 2026 to unpaid current-year taxes. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/whats-happening/index/main/175/site_post/page10.php

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **A "Heights" with no seniors housing at all:** every New Westminster seniors headline — Thornebridge Gardens, Dunwood Place, Legion Manor, the 599 independent-seniors units the City counts — belongs to another neighbourhood. Connaught Heights' seniors live in ordinary houses, which is why nothing here can honestly be sold as a 55+ home. https://www.ubcm.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/New Westminster.pdf
- **The City's own housing file for this area is two small lots — and one of them is park-grant land:** the small-sites consultation covers 2035 London Street and 2038 Ninth Avenue, and the Ninth Avenue site carries a Crown Land Grant for parks and recreation. Whatever is eventually built there is general affordable housing; no seniors component is stated in the City's material. https://www.newwestcity.ca/housing/affordable-housing/connaught-heights-small-sites/sb_expander_articles/1840.php
- **The elementary school is a choice-program draw:** Connaught Heights Elementary hosts a Montessori K–5 program — one reason young families compete for the same RS-1 houses a downsizer is eyeing, and a resale point for sellers. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/
- **Minimal retail is a planning fact, not an impression:** the City's consultation material itself notes the area's limited shops and services. For a car-free senior buyer, the practical centre of the neighbourhood is 22nd Street Station, not a high street. https://www.newwestcity.ca/housing/affordable-housing/connaught-heights-small-sites/sb_expander_articles/1840.php
- **Assembly speculation is priced into some Marine Way listings:** marketing for assembled sites at the south edge claims transit-oriented densities (up to 20 storeys / 5.0 FSR) that no source checked confirms in an adopted City bylaw. A buyer paying an "assembly premium" for a Connaught Heights house should verify the site's actual status with the City first. (Listing claims treated as UNCONFIRMED.)
