# Cultus Lake North — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

Jurisdiction note: the north end of Cultus Lake is largely Cultus Lake Park, governed by the Cultus Lake Park Board, inside FVRD Electoral Area H, south of the City of Chilliwack — it is not a City of Chilliwack neighbourhood, per the area research file. Sibling files: Cultus Lake East (provincial park shore plus the one age-restricted manufactured-home park on Columbia Valley Highway) and Cultus Lake South (Lindell Beach) cover the same lake — complexes are filed in one sub-area file only and cross-referenced in the others, never double-counted.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. At the north end this is a cross-shopping rule: neither the Park lease lots nor the Lakeside condominium carries any age bylaw in the sources checked. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The strata bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in strata exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. None of these exemptions applies to a Park Board lease — lease terms are the Park Board's own. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide in strata.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Tenure (the defining rule at the north end):** homes inside Cultus Lake Park sit on leased lots administered by the Cultus Lake Park Board under the Cultus Lake Park Act — a buyer purchases the home and the leasehold interest, not freehold land. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/ and https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/cultus-lake/widgets/132550/faqs
- **Park zoning: FVRD Cultus Lake Park Zoning Bylaw No. 1375, 2016** zones the Park lease lands; zones in this area include R-1 Suburban Residential and R-2 Urban Residential. R-1 allows one single-detached dwelling per lot; R-2 allows one single-detached dwelling plus one secondary suite. The bylaw tables state no minimum lot area figure for R-1/R-2, so minimum lot area and frontage are UNVERIFIED. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Area-H-Zoning-Bylaw-1375-Cultus-Lake-Park-Amended.pdf
- **Plan / OCP: PlanCultus, the Cultus Lake Park Plan, Bylaw No. 1080, 2016**, is the land use plan for Cultus Lake Park and designates the existing residential neighbourhoods as Residential, with single-family residential use and, subject to Park bylaws, secondary suites or coach houses. Area H land outside the Park falls under the FVRD Official Community Plan for Electoral Areas E and H, Bylaw No. 1115. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/ and https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/CLP-Plan-Bylaw-1080-hig-res.pdf
- **Lease charges and rural taxes:** Park lease lots pay Park Board lease charges rather than a standard municipal tax bill; the current lease fee schedule and any FVRD service levies for a specific lot are UNVERIFIED. For non-Park Electoral Area H land, taxes are provincial rural taxes under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act — due July 2, with a 5% penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid on October 31 — the FVRD rural regime, which differs from the City of Chilliwack's own dates. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/new-resident-information/ and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **Hazards frame:** the FVRD Evacuation Guide for Area H lists flooding, landslide and slope stability, interface fire, debris slides and flash flooding among local hazards, and the FVRD provides a Flood and Debris Control service in Area H. ALR land inside the Park / north end is UNVERIFIED. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/FVRD-Area-H-Update-with-Emergency-and-Evacuation-Plans-May-14-2025.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ home I can buy at the north end of Cultus Lake?** Nothing verified. The north end offers Park Board leasehold homes and cabins (no age bylaw) and the 21-home Lakeside condominium (no age restriction in any source checked). The lake's only age-restricted product, the Cultus Lake Village manufactured-home park, is board-classified in Cultus Lake East and its own age threshold is UNCONFIRMED because its listings conflict between 45+ and 55+. https://www.lakesidecultuslake.com/ and https://www.zolo.ca/cultus-lake-real-estate/3942-columbia-valley-road/28
- **Q: Are homes at the north end on leased land?** Yes, inside the Park. Homes inside Cultus Lake Park sit on leased lots administered by the Cultus Lake Park Board, and the Park operates under the Cultus Lake Park Act. The exception is Lakeside at Cultus Lake, which the developer describes as freehold waterfront land. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/ and https://altureproperties.com/2017/06/05/lakeside-cultus-lake-takes-location-everything/
- **Q: Can I add a suite to a Park lease lot?** It depends on the zone: under FVRD Zoning Bylaw No. 1375, 2016, R-1 allows one single-detached dwelling per lot, while R-2 allows one single-detached dwelling plus one secondary suite — and PlanCultus contemplates secondary suites or coach houses subject to Park bylaws. Confirm the lot's zone and the current lease terms with the Park Board. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Area-H-Zoning-Bylaw-1375-Cultus-Lake-Park-Amended.pdf and https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/
- **Q: Which school is located at Cultus Lake?** Cultus Lake Swílhcha Community School, K to Grade 5, at 71 Sunnyside Blvd, is the only public school located at the lake. For the sample address 71 Sunnyside Blvd, the SD33 School Locator returns Cultus Lake Swílhcha Community School (elementary), Mount Slesse Middle (with the locator note that Mount Slesse students must attend G.W. Graham Secondary), and G.W. Graham Secondary. Catchments for other addresses are UNVERIFIED — run the exact address. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/school/03333009 and https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- **Q: Is bus service year-round?** No. Route 56 Cultus Lake runs between Chilliwack (Cottonwood Mall / Promontory area) and the Cultus Lake Marina stop via Vedder Road and Columbia Valley Highway in summer months only, and the Village Centre Plan states there are no year-round transit services to Cultus Lake Park. Year-round living at the north end means driving. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview?route=56 and https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/7.-a-2024-08-06-Village-Centre-Plan-Draft-Report.pdf
- **Q: What is planned for the Village Centre?** Village Centre redevelopment is a Park Board request-for-proposals process with a preferred proponent in negotiations; it is not a verified developer presale project, and no new 55+ product is attached to it in any source checked. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/
- **Q: What hazards should a buyer note at the north end?** The FVRD Area H Evacuation Guide lists flooding, landslide and slope stability, interface fire, debris slides and flash flooding among local hazards. Price insurance and emergency planning accordingly. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/FVRD-Area-H-Update-with-Emergency-and-Evacuation-Plans-May-14-2025.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Chilliwack General Hospital, 45600 Menholm Rd, Chilliwack, and the Chilliwack Urgent and Primary Care Centre, #104 - 7955 Evans Road, Chilliwack. There is no hospital or care home at the lake. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital
- **Q: Where do I shop and borrow books day to day?** The Village Centre plaza at Columbia Valley Highway and Sunnyside Boulevard is the Park's commercial area, and a Park Board committee agenda names Cultus Lake Superette as its grocery and convenience store (street address UNVERIFIED). There is no Fraser Valley Regional Library branch at Cultus Lake; the nearest is Sardis Library, 5819 Tyson Road, Chilliwack. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/2023.02.13-Village-Center-Planning-and-Development-Committee-Agenda-Full.pdf and https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/
- **Q: Do Park lots pay property taxes?** Park lease lots pay Park Board lease charges rather than a standard municipal tax bill; the current lease fee schedule and per-lot FVRD levies are UNVERIFIED from an official source in this pass — get the lot's current charges from the Park Board before writing. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/new-resident-information/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The north end's only condominium markets itself on scarcity, not age.** Lakeside at Cultus Lake's own site states only 5 of its 21 homes remain, with a private dock and a boat slip for each home — a recreational product aimed at weekend and second-home buyers, with no age restriction anywhere in its material. https://www.lakesidecultuslake.com/
- **There are no SnapStats numbers for anywhere at Cultus Lake.** The SnapStats FVR September 2026 report contains no Chilliwack section at all, and neither does the GVR report — any inventory, sales-ratio or median figure quoted for this sub-area has no source in the named stats program and should be treated as UNVERIFIED. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/cultus-lake-north.md
- **The Park is self-funded, which is why the lease charges matter.** The Park Board's community material records that Cultus Lake Park receives no local, provincial or federal funding and is entirely self-sustaining, with more than 1,500 residential leaseholders — lease charges are the Park's operating revenue, not a tax bill by another name. https://www.admiraal.ca/cultus-lake?p=5
- **The lake's sewage servicing is being rebuilt in stages, north side first.** The FVRD's North Cultus wastewater treatment plant was commissioned in April 2025 under the FVRD Liquid Waste Management Plan (approved 2019), for Class A effluent plus phosphorus removal — 2025 was its first operational year, and it serves the lake's north side. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/news/news-archives/2025/north-cultus-wastewater-treatment-plant-comes-online.html and https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/wwtp
- **"Cultus Lake North" is an emergency-planning name as much as a real estate one.** The FVRD uses "Cultus Lake North" as a community location name in its Area H Evacuation Guide; it is not a municipal neighbourhood name, and no official MLS boundary for it is published by the Park Board or the FVRD. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/FVRD-Area-H-Update-with-Emergency-and-Evacuation-Plans-May-14-2025.pdf
- **The school at the lake only goes to Grade 5.** Cultus Lake Swílhcha Community School is K–5; from Grade 6 the locator pattern for the school's own address runs to Mount Slesse Middle and then G.W. Graham Secondary in Chilliwack — relevant to grandparents hosting family, and to why no family-driven or age-driven school premium exists at the north end. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/school/03333009 and https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
