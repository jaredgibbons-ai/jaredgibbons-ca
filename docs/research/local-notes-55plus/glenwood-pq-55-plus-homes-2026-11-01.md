- Hyde Creek Recreation Centre at 1379 Laurier Avenue is the north-side recreation centre; Evergreen Park at 3500 Cedar Drive is listed in the City Parks Directory on the north side. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/recreation-parks/parks-trails/parks-directory?page=1

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. In Glenwood this is the test every "seniors-style" complex here fails: none is verified to hold such a bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why older building profiles for Glenwood complexes still showing "rentals allowed w/restrictions" are out of date on the rental point. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw, 2008, No. 3630.** Glenwood's detached homes sit under the RS-1 to RS-4 zones (minimum lot widths from at least 9.5 m in RS-4 to at least 30 m in RS-3); townhouse zones RTh1, RTh2 and RTh3 exist in the bylaw. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/3630-zoning-bylaw-2008pdf
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2013, No. 3838** governs the area; no Glenwood-specific plan or major project was confirmed in it. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- **Secondary suites:** the City permits secondary suites only in the RS-1, RS-2, RS-3 and RS-4 zones, and a building permit is required — the practical route to housing a parent or caregiver in Glenwood's detached stock. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/secondary-suites
- **Suite utility-charge exemptions that matter to seniors:** the City waives 50% of the extra suite utility levy when the suite is occupied by a parent or grandparent of the owner, and 100% when a paid caregiver (minimum $150/month) lives in the suite to care for a permanently disabled owner or family member; applications are annual, due by Dec 31. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/utility-bills/secondary-suite-exemptions
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing:** the City permits 3 to 6 SSMUH units on RS-zoned lots without rezoning, depending on lot size, location and transit proximity. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Manufactured-home parks:** none inside Port Coquitlam's city limits on any source found, so no park rules apply in Glenwood.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Shaughnessy Park a 55+ complex?** No. Re-verified for this file: building sources for 3655 Shaughnessy St list only pet and rental restrictions, listings show 3–4 bedroom family townhomes, and no source states an age restriction of any kind. It does not belong on a 55+ page. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/shaughnessy-park-3655-street
- **Q: Is there any 55+ housing I can buy in Glenwood?** No — not on any source checked. Port Coquitlam's one verified 55+ strata is Mayfair Terrace downtown (2266 Atkins Ave, Central Port Coquitlam). https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/mayfair-terrace-2266-atkins-ave
- **Q: The Arden is marketed to downsizers. Does that make it 55+?** No. Its listing copy targets first-timers, downsizers and investors together, with rentals and pets allowed — marketing language, not a bylaw. The only valid strata age bylaw in BC is a 55+ bylaw under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, and no source shows one for this building. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r2895626-305-1990-westminster-avenue-port-coquitlam-bc-v3b-1e8 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in PoCo?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a Glenwood condo or townhome?** Long-term rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022 — building profiles still showing rental restrictions predate the change. A strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I add a suite in a Glenwood house for a parent or caregiver?** Secondary suites are permitted in the RS-1 to RS-4 zones with a building permit, and the City halves the extra suite utility levy when the suite is occupied by a parent or grandparent of the owner, and waives it entirely when a paid caregiver lives in the suite to care for a permanently disabled owner or family member. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/secondary-suites and https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/utility-bills/secondary-suite-exemptions
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Shaughnessy Park?** Building sources record pets allowed with restrictions. Confirm the current bylaw wording and any size or number limits on the Form B for the specific unit. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/shaughnessy-park-3655-street
- **Q: Where is the nearest seniors housing if I need care later?** The in-city care options are downtown: Hawthorne Seniors Care Community (2111 Hawthorne Ave, assisted living and long-term care through Fraser Health) and the registered assisted-living residences on Kelly Avenue. None are in Glenwood. https://hawthornecare.com/ and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3APort%20Coquitlam&f%5B1%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASupportive%20Recovery&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=desc
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Eagle Ridge Hospital at 475 Guildford Way in Port Moody is the Tri-Cities hospital; Royal Columbian Hospital in New Westminster is the larger regional hospital. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- **Q: Is Glenwood in a floodplain?** A significant portion of Port Coquitlam is within the Fraser and Pitt River floodplain behind dykes; Glenwood sits on the north side, and lot-level floodplain status is UNVERIFIED and must be checked with the City. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/port-coquitlam-reminds-residents-prepare-possible-flooding

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **There is no SkyTrain coming to Port Coquitlam on any funded timeline.** TransLink's Transport 2050: 10-Year Priorities commits only to an "exploratory business case" for a Millennium Line extension to Port Coquitlam, still simply "underway" in the Mayors' Council's Feb 19, 2026 work plan — no route, stations, funding or dates approved. Glenwood's transit is buses to Coquitlam Central and Port Coquitlam stations. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/about-translink/governance-and-board/council-minutes-and-reports/2026/february/report--2026-mc-budget--feb-2026.pdf
- **The West Coast Express is commuter-only.** Port Coquitlam Station's service is five trains in each direction on weekdays (mornings toward Vancouver, evenings toward Mission), with no weekend or holiday service, per TransLink's timetable effective Jan 5, 2026 — do not price a Glenwood home off all-day rail service. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
- **Glenwood detached homes sat in September 2026.** The sub-area recorded 39 active detached listings and just 1 sale that month (3% sales ratio), the slowest detached pace in Port Coquitlam, while attached homes moved at 17% (41 listed, 7 sold) — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report.
- **Flood works are underway elsewhere in the city.** The City's Cedar Drainage Pump Station, part of the Pitt River dike system, is being rebuilt with construction scheduled summer 2026 to summer 2027 — relevant background when a buyer is checking lot-level flood status anywhere in PoCo. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/capital-projects/cedar-drainage-pump-station-0
- **Port Coquitlam bills utilities flat, not metered.** Water and sewer are flat annual fees billed separately from property taxes, due March 31 — a simpler cost line for a downsizer budgeting a Glenwood house than metered billing elsewhere. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/utility-bills
