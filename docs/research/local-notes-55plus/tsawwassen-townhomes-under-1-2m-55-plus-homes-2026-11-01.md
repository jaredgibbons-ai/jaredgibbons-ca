## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. No Tsawwassen townhome complex checked in this program carries such a bylaw — which is why this band contains no 55+ townhome. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://choa.bc.ca/resources/strata-property-act/legislative-updates/
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. (Relevant here to the two Central condo buildings in Section 2, not to any townhome in the band.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. The live page's per-complex pet and rental claims for this band are UNVERIFIED — no strata or manager source for them was read in this program. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **The band's one verifiable price rule is property transfer tax.** The Province's general PTT rates are 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion greater than $200,000 and up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% of the portion greater than $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — so every purchase in this band is taxed entirely in the 1% and 2% tiers, and the 3% tier and the further 2% do not arise. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-time buyer PTT exemption — only the band's lower slice can use it.** The full exemption requires a fair market value of $835,000 or less, with a partial exemption above $835,000 and below $860,000, and all the program's other buyer and property conditions must also be met — so a purchase at the band's upper end cannot use the program at all, and two townhomes on the same page can sit on opposite sides of it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **FHSA and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits: UNVERIFIED in these notes.** No dollar limit for either program is stated here, because neither was read off a live CRA page in this pass. Do not copy a limit from the live page or from calculator marketing copy.
- **North tenure rule — read the lease, not the summary.** At Boardwalk, the developer states homes are purchased with a 99-year leasehold on Tsawwassen Lands, where Tsawwassen First Nation is self-governing with jurisdiction over land management. The live page's further leasehold details — that the lease is prepaid by the developer, that owners pay no monthly ground rent, and that property taxes are set to match City of Delta rates — are UNVERIFIED here: the developer page read confirms the 99-year leasehold only. The head lease and sublease documents are the diligence set. (Developer source: https://ownboardwalk.com/) and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- **Delta-side planning frame:** the Delta Official Community Plan 2024, Bylaw No. 8400, is the City's adopted consolidated OCP covering Tsawwassen — it contains no area-plan schedules (the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, superseded), its Development Permit Areas live in Appendix B, and Delta's zoning bylaw is Zoning Bylaw No. 7600. Seniors housing in Delta is typically delivered through site-specific comprehensive development zones (The Waterford's site is CD Zone No. 276). http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/71542/
