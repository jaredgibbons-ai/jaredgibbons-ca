# Coquitlam West (City of Coquitlam) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Coquitlam West is the City of Coquitlam's south-west side. The City's planning name for the same ground is Southwest Coquitlam, which includes the Burquitlam and Lougheed Town Centre station areas and the Austin Avenue corridor; the board name "Coquitlam West" is the GVR sub-area name in the SnapStats GVR September 2026 report. Exact sub-area boundary lines: UNVERIFIED — the City describes Southwest Coquitlam by its station areas and corridor, not by boundary roads, in the sources read. http://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1968
- In local and board usage the sub-area takes in Burquitlam / Lougheed at its north end and the Maillardville streets to the south — listing systems file Maillardville-area addresses such as Dunlop Street, Delestre Avenue and Cayer Street (all V3K postcodes) under Coquitlam West, and a neighbourhood profile groups Coquitlam West with Harbour Chines, Harbour Place, Chineside, Central Coquitlam and Maillardville as the city's central-west neighbourhoods. Treat the southern edge as listing-system usage, not a confirmed boundary. https://vancouvernashdom.com/coquitlam-coquitlam-west-houses/SearchResults.form/?_pg=4 and https://faithwilson.com/neighbourhoods/maillardville-central-west/?sub_area_community=Coquitlam+West&market_trend=R3BH-9w
- Roads: North Road, Clarke Road, Austin Avenue and Lougheed Highway serve Coquitlam West. Transit: Burquitlam Station (Millennium Line, Evergreen Extension) and Lougheed Town Centre Station (Millennium and Expo Lines). http://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1968
- Schools: School District 43 catchments are address-based. For the sample address 575 Poirier Street (Coquitlam Public Library Poirier branch), the SD43 School Locator result is Parkland Elementary, École Montgomery Middle and Centennial Secondary; Burquitlam-side addresses can feed different schools — check the exact address. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- Library: Coquitlam Public Library Poirier branch, 575 Poirier Street, is in this part of the city; the City Centre branch, 1169 Pinetree Way, serves Coquitlam as a whole. https://www.coqlibrary.ca/
- Health care: Eagle Ridge Hospital, 475 Guildford Way, Port Moody, is the Fraser Health hospital on the Port Moody / Coquitlam border; Royal Columbian Hospital in New Westminster serves the south-west side by SkyTrain — which hospital is closer for a specific Coquitlam West address: UNVERIFIED. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- Where the seniors stock actually sits: there is no 55+ strata cluster here (see Section 2). The seniors housing that does exist is (1) a care cluster in Maillardville — Alderson Avenue, Cartier Avenue and Delestre Avenue; (2) subsidized and affordable rental buildings scattered through Burquitlam and Maillardville — Emerson Street, Cottonwood Avenue, Dogwood Street, Brunette Avenue, Schoolhouse Street and King Albert Avenue, per BC Housing's directory; and (3) two 55+ affordable rental buildings under construction in 2026 — Clayton Street in Burquitlam-Lougheed and Laval Square in Maillardville. Sources: the entries in Section 2 below.

What this sub-area has instead is care, subsidized rental, and 55+ rental under construction, set out below. Do not pad a Coquitlam West 55+ page with ownership product that is not here.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. (In this sub-area the rule matters mostly as a negative: no strata here was verified to hold such a bylaw.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996** is Coquitlam's zoning bylaw, and the Citywide Official Community Plan is Bylaw No. 3479, 2001; the City's growth page explains the OCP gives every property a land-use designation while the Zoning Bylaw sets permitted uses, height and density. https://www.coquitlam.ca/323/How-to-Plan-for-Growth
- **Planning change specific to this sub-area:** the Transit-Oriented Areas Update and the Southwest Shoulders and Corridors review apply here — Council-adopted OCP and Zoning amendments creating medium-density housing and townhousing opportunities around the Burquitlam and Lougheed Town Centre stations and along Austin Avenue. That is the planning context in which the older house streets around the stations are being assembled and rezoned. http://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1968
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) in Coquitlam:** up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the rules. Small-scale zones are R-1, R-2 and R-3 under Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 (subdivision minimums 650 m² / 18.5 m width for R-1 and R-3; 465 m² / 12.5 m for R-2). https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Watercourse setbacks:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam; Zoning Bylaw s.519 sets flood protection setbacks and s.523 covers riparian areas. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** for this area (Zone 4 directory, May 2025) or directly to the non-profit operator where the directory says so — it is application and waitlist housing, not a purchase, and several Coquitlam — South entries also accept couples under 55 and people with disabilities, per the directory's codes, so "seniors directory" does not mean every unit is age-restricted to 55+. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Property tax dates that apply citywide:** Coquitlam property tax payment was due July 2, 2026 for the Jan 1–Dec 31, 2026 year, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after September 15, 2026, including unclaimed Home Owner Grants. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Coquitlam West?** None that any source verifies. There is no age-restricted strata building and no 55+ manufactured-home park with a Coquitlam West address in the sources checked; the city's verified 55+ ownership buildings are in the Town Centre pocket (Marlborough House, Princess Gate, Burlington Estates) and its 55+ park is in Ranch Park — both in the Central Coquitlam file. A listing that markets a Burquitlam or Maillardville home as "55+" should be asked for the registered bylaw or park rule that says so. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026HMA0070-000764.pdf
- **Q: Is Wildwood Park a 55+ park?** No. Wildwood Park at 201 Cayer St is marketed in its own listings as a family-oriented park with a playground, pool, tennis court and clubhouse, on leasehold non-strata pads. Its appearance in 55+ searches is a search artefact, not an age rule. https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/listing/r3091187-201-cayer-street-212-coquitlam-maillardville-homes-for-sale-288880/brochure
- **Q: What 55+ housing is coming to Coquitlam West?** Rental, not ownership: 158 affordable homes for adults 55+ with low to moderate incomes, under construction at the Province's June 29, 2026 announcement — 116 homes in a six-storey building at 443 Clayton St (Burquitlam-Lougheed, walking distance to Lougheed Station) and 42 homes in a three-storey building at 290 Laval Square beside the Maillardville Community Centre — both to be owned and operated by S.U.C.C.E.S.S. Opening dates are not stated in the release: UNCONFIRMED. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026HMA0070-000764.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes, where a 55+ strata is involved — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. Note the subsidized buildings in Section 2 run on their own program rules — BC Housing's directory records "couples under 55 accepted" for several Coquitlam — South entries, which is a program code, not the strata exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted in a strata — the bylaw governs who may reside, and a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. In this sub-area the question is academic for ownership, because no 55+ strata was verified here; it applies to the Town Centre buildings in the Central Coquitlam file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a strata unit in Coquitlam West?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022; a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. In a manufactured-home park such as Wildwood or Mill Creek Village, rentals are a park-rule and tenancy question, not a strata question — get the park's rules in writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: How do I get into the subsidized seniors buildings?** Through BC Housing's Housing Registry or directly to the non-profit operator, as BC Housing's Zone 4 directory directs for each building — Cottonwood and Dogwood are BC Housing-managed; 55One's 100 funded homes are operated by 43 Housing Society; Chateau De Ville, Le Chateau Place and Mountain View Suites are non-profit entries. These are income-based rentals with waitlists, not purchases. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf and https://www.concertproperties.com/news/in-the-news/one-hundred-affordable-homes-open-coquitlam
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Eagle Ridge Hospital, 475 Guildford Way, Port Moody, is the Fraser Health hospital on the Port Moody / Coquitlam border; Royal Columbian Hospital in New Westminster is the other hospital serving the south-west side, reachable by SkyTrain from Lougheed Town Centre Station. Which is closer depends on the specific address: UNVERIFIED per address. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- **Q: If I need care later, what is in this sub-area?** A Maillardville cluster: assisted living at Residences at Belvedere (750 Delestre Ave — private-pay plus 60 Fraser Health funded suites, by case-manager referral), long-term care at Foyer Maillard (1010 Alderson Ave, 125 publicly subsidized beds, bilingual French/English) and Cartier House (1419 Cartier Ave), plus the Belvedere Care Centre on Alderson Ave. All are rental/care — none is a purchase. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/1/B/assisted-living-at-residences-at-belvedere and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/2/3/foyer-maillard---long-term-care and https://www.parkplaceseniorsliving.com/find-a-location/british-columbia/lower-mainland/cartier-house/
- **Q: Is a house lot here a redevelopment play?** Possibly, and it has to be checked lot by lot: the adopted Southwest Shoulders and Corridors amendments create medium-density and townhousing opportunities around Burquitlam and Lougheed Town Centre stations and along Austin Avenue, on top of the citywide SSMUH rules (up to 3–4 units on house lots). Whether a specific lot is inside an amended area is a City confirmation, not a listing claim. http://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1968 and https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The sub-area's 55+ story is a pipeline story, not a resale story:** until the two S.U.C.C.E.S.S. buildings open, a 55+ buyer who wants to own in Coquitlam has to shop the Town Centre pocket or Ranch Park, while Coquitlam West's seniors supply is entirely rental and care — 158 new 55+ rental homes under construction at once (116 at 443 Clayton St + 42 at 290 Laval Square) is the largest seniors-housing change in this part of the city in the sources checked. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026HMA0070-000764.pdf
- **Wildwood Park is the trap in every Tri-Cities park search:** it is large, established (over 30 years, per its operator's description), amenity-rich and affordable-looking — and family-oriented, with a playground in its own listing copy. Any 55+ page, filter or shortlist that includes Wildwood Park at 201 Cayer St is wrong, and the error usually comes from search engines grouping it with the genuinely 55+ Hideaway Park across the city. https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/listing/r3091187-201-cayer-street-212-coquitlam-maillardville-homes-for-sale-288880/brochure and http://www.wildwoodmanufacturedhomes.com/
- **Two Dogwoods, two cities:** BC Housing's directory lists a "Dogwood" at 710 Dogwood St in Coquitlam's south list, while Port Coquitlam has Dogwood Manor and Dogwood Place on Seymour St, run by the Port Coquitlam Senior Citizens' Housing Society. Applications, waitlists and operators are different — a caller asking for "Dogwood" without the city will be routed wrong. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **55One is not a seniors building, despite its name and directory placement:** the "55" in 55One is its address — 551 Emerson St. Its 100 funded affordable homes are for families, seniors and individuals with low or moderate incomes, inside a 308-suite market rental tower, operated by 43 Housing Society. It appears in BC Housing's seniors/disabilities directory because seniors can apply there, not because it is age-restricted. https://www.concertproperties.com/news/in-the-news/one-hundred-affordable-homes-open-coquitlam
- **Maillardville's care cluster is a French-language asset found nowhere else in BC care:** Foyer Maillard (1010 Alderson Ave) is operated by a French-Canadian non-profit society, provides care in a bilingual French/English environment, and was described at its rebuild as the only care facility in BC offering French long-term care services — with 125 publicly subsidized beds, all single rooms, a few blocks from the Belvedere assisted-living and care buildings on Alderson / Delestre. For a French-speaking senior, this cluster is the reason to look at Maillardville specifically. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/2/3/foyer-maillard---long-term-care and https://resosante.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/NR_Foyer_Maillard_groundbreaking_Dec_5_2014.pdf
