# Deep Cove (North Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Deep Cove is the waterfront village at the District of North Vancouver's far eastern edge, on the shore of Indian Arm at the foot of Mount Seymour, built around Gallant Avenue, Deep Cove Road and Panorama Drive. It is one of the six Village Centres named in the District's Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900 (with Lions Gate, Queensdale, Maplewood, Parkgate and Edgemont). Exact boundary streets: UNVERIFIED; the confirmed anchors are the village core on lower Gallant Avenue, Panorama Park on Panorama Drive, and the Cove Cliff / Banbury Road streets above the village. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf and https://www.nvrc.ca/facilities-fields/locations-hours/community-recreation-centres/panorama-park-picnic-shelter
- One road in and out: Deep Cove Road / Gallant Avenue and Dollarton Highway run west to Mount Seymour Parkway, Phibbs Exchange and Highway 1; there is no second road connection north up Indian Arm for cars. The District has managed village parking formally for a decade — a Parking and Access Implementation Plan endorsed in 2015 and a further Parking Review implementation plan to Council on April 22, 2024 covering on-street pay parking, resident parking and time-limited parking. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/regional-maps/2026/september-2026/north-and-west-vancouver-map.pdf and https://app.dnv.org/OpenDocument/Default.aspx?docNum=6278173
- Schools in the village area: Cove Cliff Elementary (K–7), 1818 Banbury Road, and Seycove Secondary Community School (8–12), 1204 Caledonia Avenue. School District 44 catchments are address-based, with no middle tier; Seycove's family of schools also includes Dorothy Lynas and Sherwood Park elementaries on the Parkgate side, so a street west of the village may not feed Cove Cliff — check each address with SD44. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/044
- Panorama Park, on Panorama Drive in front of the Cove, is the waterfront park — beach access, open green space, picnic shelter, Deep Cove Yacht Club and Indian Arm views — and the eastern access point for the Baden Powell Trail and the Quarry Rock lookout. Deep Cove Park is at 1991 Rockcliff Road, north of the village core. https://www.nvrc.ca/facilities-fields/locations-hours/community-recreation-centres/panorama-park-picnic-shelter and https://www.dnv.org/parks-trails-recreation/find-all-district-parks-map
- There is no library branch inside Deep Cove in the sources read; the District library's Parkgate side is the nearest branch direction. The hospital is Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver — the far end of the District from the Cove. https://www.nvdpl.ca/locations and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital
- Where the seniors housing actually is: **not in Deep Cove.** No seniors housing of any type — no 55+ strata, no retirement residence, no assisted living, no long-term care, no subsidized seniors building — was verified with an address inside Deep Cove. The nearest verified seniors housing is west along Mount Seymour Parkway in the Seymour/Parkgate area (Cedar Springs PARC; the Mount Seymour Lions housing buildings). Sources: Section 2 below.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. In Deep Cove this is academic — there is no verified strata stock for a bylaw to attach to — but it governs the buildings elsewhere in the District that a Cove downsizer would buy into. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Zoning: District of North Vancouver Zoning Bylaw 3210, Section 525.** The R1 detached zone minimum lot is 4,000 m² (30 m width, 34 m depth); R2 is 660 m² (18 m width, 34 m depth), with modifiers down to R2(S1) at 335 m² / 10 m. An R2 principal building may include a secondary suite; a coach house is a separate accessory dwelling unit. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Three units on a house lot:** since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary. In Deep Cove, where there is no seniors building at all, this suite/coach-house capacity is the entire local ageing-in-place mechanism: a caregiver suite, a family member's coach house, or rental income from the same lot. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **Hazard permit areas:** OCP Bylaw 7900, Schedule B sets seven Development Permit Areas including Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard — Deep Cove's forested slope, creek and shoreline lots can fall in several at once, which bears directly on adding that suite or coach house; the District publishes property-level GeoWeb reports, including modelled flood construction levels for a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m. https://dnv-docs.simplicitycms.ca/documents/ocp-schedule-b-dpa.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/
- **Village-centre planning:** the District's Livable Deep Cove project covers the lower Gallant Avenue pedestrian area; in the District's September 2022 engagement, 818 online surveys were completed and 82% of respondents supported change on lower Gallant. It is a public-realm project, not a housing project — no seniors housing component is confirmed in it. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/livable-deep-cove-survey-2022.pdf
- **Property taxes:** District of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; combined single-family utility fees are $2,571.20 in 2026 (water $932.80; sewer/drainage $1,255.60). https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Deep Cove?** None that any source verifies. There is no age-restricted strata building, no retirement residence and no seniors rental in Deep Cove. A 55+ buyer here buys a regular detached house; the District's verified 55+ purchase options are in Edgemont Village (Edgemont Villa) and Lynn Valley (Balmoral House, Kirkstone Gardens). Sources: Section 2 above and the Capilano and Canyon Springs local-notes files in this series (researched 2026-11-01).
- **Q: Is Cedar Springs PARC in Deep Cove?** No — it is at 3633 Mt Seymour Parkway in the Parkgate/Seymour area, which its operator describes as a 10-minute drive from Deep Cove. It is the nearest independent-living rental residence, across from Parkgate Village mall and the Parkgate Community Centre. https://parcliving.ca/blog/the-best-senior-living-in-north-vancouver-cedar-springs-parc/
- **Q: How does a senior age in place in Deep Cove?** In their own house, with two District tools: zoning that has allowed a principal dwelling plus a secondary suite plus a coach house (three units total) on single-family lots within the Urban Containment Boundary since June 2024 — room for a caregiver or family member on the lot — and private in-home care services that market Deep Cove coverage. Neither is a "55+ home"; both are how it is actually done here. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf and https://www.homeinstead.ca/home-care/can/bc/west-vancouver/3022/service-area/deep-cove-bc/in-home-care/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. If we buy into a 55+ strata elsewhere in the District, can we both live there?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** Local buses connect the village terminus on Panorama Drive / Gallant Avenue to Phibbs Exchange via Dollarton Highway and Mount Seymour Parkway; since September 7, 2026 the R2 RapidBus runs one-seat from Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown every 6–7 minutes at peak. There is no SkyTrain or SeaBus near the Cove — Phibbs is the connection point, and the village itself is a single-road community. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/regional-maps/2026/september-2026/north-and-west-vancouver-map.pdf and https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver — across the District from Deep Cove, reached via the single road out, Mount Seymour Parkway and Highway 1. The North Vancouver Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 221 West Esplanade, North Vancouver. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/north-vancouver-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in Deep Cove?** Not in a care building — there is none at any step: no assisted living, no long-term care, no memory care in the Cove. The ladder (Amica Edgemont Village's continuum in Edgemont; VCH's Cedar Garden assisted living and Cedarview Lodge long-term care in Lynn Valley) is a move west across the District. https://www.amica.ca/edgemont-village and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/cedarview-lodge
- **Q: Does the village parking regime affect a home purchase?** Yes, on the core streets: the District has run a formal parking-and-access program for Deep Cove since 2015, with a further implementation plan in April 2024 covering pay, resident and time-limited parking — summer visitor pressure around Panorama Park and the Quarry Rock trailhead is a managed, priced feature of village-core addresses, not a surprise. https://app.dnv.org/OpenDocument/Default.aspx?docNum=6278173

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Deep Cove is the District's seniors-housing blank spot.** Every other District village centre in this research series has at least one verified seniors product (Edgemont: a 55+ strata plus Amica; Lynn Valley: two 55+ stratas plus VCH care). Deep Cove has zero — no registry entry, no directory entry, no complex. A page that implies otherwise would be inventing stock. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **The "nearest" residence markets the Cove, not the other way around.** Cedar Springs PARC's own location writing sells Deep Cove as a scenic outing 10 minutes from its Parkgate address — the Cove is the amenity in that relationship, which tells a buyer exactly where the housing actually is. https://parcliving.ca/blog/the-best-senior-living-in-north-vancouver-cedar-springs-parc/
- **The Mount Seymour Lions buildings are the quiet non-profit option west of the Cove.** Lions Manor (4290 Naughton Ave) and Lions Plaza (800 Apex Ave), run by the Mount Seymour Lions Housing Society, appear in BC Housing's seniors/disabilities directory for the Seymour side — the closest subsidized seniors housing to Deep Cove, and easy to miss because neither carries a Deep Cove or Parkgate name. https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-1-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **One road means one evacuation and access story.** Deep Cove Road / Dollarton Highway is the only vehicle connection; combined with Schedule B creek, slope and wildfire permit areas overlapping on the forested lots, the practical senior-living questions here are access, hazard and services — not buildings. https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/ and https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/regional-maps/2026/september-2026/north-and-west-vancouver-map.pdf
- **Foreshore claims are unverified until the title says so.** No Deep Cove foreshore, dock or moorage tenure fact is confirmed in the sources for this file — waterfront listings that promise moorage should be treated as UNVERIFIED until title and District records confirm them, which matters more here than in most sub-areas because so much of the stock touches Indian Arm. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
- **The village's current District project adds no homes.** Livable Deep Cove is a lower-Gallant pedestrian/public-realm project (82% support in the District's 2022 engagement survey) — worth knowing so a buyer does not mistake construction in the village core for a coming seniors or condo project. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/livable-deep-cove-survey-2022.pdf
