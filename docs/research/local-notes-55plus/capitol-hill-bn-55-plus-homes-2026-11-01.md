# Capitol Hill BN (Burnaby) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Capitol Hill BN is the hilltop residential area north of Hastings St in north Burnaby, centred on Capitol Hill Reservoir Park, 229 N Hythe Ave. The exact MLS sub-area boundary streets are UNVERIFIED — no City source checked states them. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
- Schools: Capitol Hill Elementary (Holdom Ave) and Burnaby North Secondary, 751 Hammarskjold Dr. The SD41 locator for a west-side sample address, 4715 Pandora St, returns Confederation Park Elementary and Alpha Secondary — catchments are address-based, so a west-edge address does not carry the hilltop catchment. Burnaby is School District 41 — 41 elementary and 8 secondary schools, no middle-school tier (K–7 / 8–12). https://burnabyschools.ca/about-sd41-overview/ and https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- Amenities on the west side: Confederation Park, 250 Willingdon Ave; Eileen Dailly Leisure Pool, 240 Willingdon Ave; the McGill Branch library, 4595 Albert St. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/confederation-park and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/city-departments/burnaby-public-library
- Transit: the R5 Hastings RapidBus serves Hastings St (at least every 10 minutes at peak), and the R2 RapidBus was extended on September 7, 2026 from Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown via Hastings St and Willingdon Ave. There is no SkyTrain station in or beside this sub-area. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects and https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/
- Where the seniors housing actually is: nowhere verified inside this sub-area. Capitol Hill BN is detached-house stock on a hill; the one well-known seniors residence on its edge, Seton Villa on McGill St, could not be placed inside the MLS sub-area from the sources checked (see Section 2), and the "55+" facility on Albert St is a City recreation centre, not housing.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Zoning bylaw — note the name change.** Research briefs for Burnaby sometimes still cite the "Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742)". The City states the new **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026** was adopted June 9, 2026, replaced the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965, and took effect July 1, 2026. Use the 2026 bylaw. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/bylaws/zoning-bylaw
- **R1 SSMUH zoning:** Burnaby consolidated its single- and two-family districts into the R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing district in June 2024 — 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; multi-unit lots may be stratified. On Capitol Hill's detached streets, that is the rule set behind new duplex, multiplex and coach-house forms — the realistic "downsizer supply" here is new SSMUH product, not a 55+ complex. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP: the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan** was adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP, and takes precedence over older community-plan maps. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Property taxes:** Burnaby's 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date; the City's 2026–2030 Financial Plan approved a 2.9% tax increase (about $61 on the average home). The detached single-family water utility charge is $652.33, with a 5% discount if paid by the due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy on Capitol Hill?** None that any source verifies. No age-restricted strata building with an address inside the Capitol Hill BN sub-area was found in any source checked — the hill's housing stock is detached houses (plus general-market apartments on Hastings St). A 55+ buyer here is buying an ordinary home with no age bylaw, in a quiet hilltop neighbourhood. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Seton Villa on McGill St part of Capitol Hill?** UNCONFIRMED. Seton Villa (3755 McGill St) is a real non-profit assisted-living residence — 19 storeys, opened 1974, 29 publicly subsidized suites per Fraser Health — but its own operator describes it as being in Burnaby Heights, another source places it in Vancouver Heights, and no source checked assigns it to the Capitol Hill MLS sub-area. Treat it as a nearby north Burnaby option, not a Capitol Hill building. https://www.setonvilla.com/home and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/8/C/assisted-living-at-seton-villa
- **Q: Is the Confederation Seniors' Centre a place to live?** No — it is a City recreation centre for people 55+ at 4585 Albert St in Confederation Park, with memberships ($22.60/year single-centre, $56.45/year multi-centre, effective September 1, 2026) and society-run clubs. It is an amenity for residents of the area, not housing. http://www.burnaby.ca/confederation55+
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Burnaby?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — a BC age bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: If there's no 55+ complex, what do downsizers buy here?** In practice, the newer ground-oriented product on the hill's detached lots: Burnaby's R1 SSMUH zoning (in place since June 2024) allows 3–4 units on most former single-family lots — up to 6 near frequent transit — and multi-unit lots may be stratified, so duplexes, multiplexes and coach houses are the new supply. None of it carries an age restriction. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** This sub-area has no hospital; the nearest is Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St, south of the hill across Canada Way. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** Buses, at RapidBus quality on the edges: the R5 on Hastings St runs at least every 10 minutes at peak, and since September 7, 2026 the R2 runs from Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown via Hastings and Willingdon. There is no SkyTrain serving Capitol Hill. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects and https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/
- **Q: Does the hilltop location matter for daily living?** Yes, practically: this is a hilltop neighbourhood north of Hastings St centred on a reservoir park — the flat, walkable seniors amenities (the Confederation 55+ centre, Eileen Dailly pool, McGill library) sit on the west side around Willingdon Ave and Albert St, not up on the hill's residential streets. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/confederation-park and http://www.burnaby.ca/confederation55+

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The best-known "Capitol Hill area" seniors residence may not be in Capitol Hill at all.** Seton Villa (3755 McGill St, opened 1974) is described by its own operator as a "hidden gem in Burnaby Heights," and a Jane's Walk source places it in Vancouver Heights. Neighbourhood names in north Burnaby overlap loosely; the MLS sub-area assignment for that address is UNCONFIRMED, which is why it is not counted in this sub-area's stock. https://www.setonvilla.com/home and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/seton-villa
- **Seton Villa has a hard mobility limit worth knowing:** Fraser Health records that its subsidized assisted-living suites cannot accommodate wheelchairs or scooters, and resident pets are not permitted — a real screening fact for anyone considering it as the "stay nearby" option. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/8/C/assisted-living-at-seton-villa
- **The park at the centre of the hill is a working reservoir.** Capitol Hill Reservoir Park (229 N Hythe Ave) is the landmark the sub-area is named around — a reservoir site, which is part of why the hilltop stayed low-density residential. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
- **The planned Confederation Park Community Centre is deferred.** The City's project to rebuild facilities at Confederation Park has been deferred — so the west-side amenity set (Eileen Dailly Leisure Pool, 240 Willingdon Ave; the Confederation 55+ centre) is the current, not future, offer. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/confederation-park-community-centre
- **Burnaby replaced its entire zoning bylaw in 2026.** The Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (adopted June 9, 2026, in force July 1, 2026) repealed and replaced the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 — documents or summaries that still cite the 1965 bylaw are out of date. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **The school catchment flips at the west edge.** The SD41 locator puts a Pandora St address on the west side into Confederation Park Elementary and Alpha Secondary, not Capitol Hill Elementary and Burnaby North — on a boundary street, run the exact address through the locator before repeating a school claim. https://burnabyschools.ca/about-sd41-overview/
- **Burnaby taxes have two penalty steps.** 2026 property taxes were due July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date, and the detached water charge ($652.33) carries a 5% early-payment discount. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
