Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**GEOGRAPHY FLAG, stated up front: Lake Errock is NOT in the City of Mission and NOT in the District of Kent. Listings carry "Mission" mailing addresses, the live page is titled "Mission BC Waterfront," and SnapStats groups the "Lake Errock" sub-area under the board community Mission — but Lake Errock is an unincorporated community in Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) Electoral Area C, on the lake of the same name near Harrison Mills. The FVRD is the local government; City of Mission and District of Kent bylaws, zoning and tax rules do NOT apply here. This file is titled and framed accordingly.** https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/areacocp?sort_by=recent&tool=forum_topic and https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/areacocp and https://jaredgibbons.ca/lake-errock.html

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. It matters here precisely because Lake Errock does have strata product — Tapadera Estates (LMS648) and the Morris Valley estate stratas — and none of them checked has adopted a 55+ bylaw; a listing for Tapadera states "no age restrictions" outright. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://kw.com/property/14600-Morris-Valley-Road-31-Mission-BC-V0M-1A1/2042579860092792
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** People under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; exemptions attach to a "specified resident." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; SHORT-TERM rental bylaws are still allowed — the live question in a recreation community like Tapadera, whose listings state rentals are allowed. Confirm any specific strata's short-term rules in its bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Land use runs through the FVRD, not a city hall:** the Electoral Area C Official Community Plan, Bylaw 1747, 2024 (adopted September 18, 2025), covers Lake Errock and includes a Lake Errock Neighbourhood Plan, replacing OCP Bylaw No. 0020, 1998; FVRD zoning applies lot by lot (Tapadera's own development guide refers to the Consolidated Zoning Bylaw No. 1638, 2021 for Holiday Park requirements — a guide, not the bylaw itself). A specific lot's designation, permit areas and permitted dwellings: UNVERIFIED — confirm with the FVRD for the PID. https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/areacocp and https://designrr.s3.amazonaws.com/kate.vannes_at_gmail.com_150177/katevannesgmailcom_Tapadera-GuidetoStrataLotDevelopment-May20251777056451.pdf
- **Manufactured-home parks in unincorporated areas run on the BC Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, province-wide** — it applies to any pad tenancy in the corridor regardless of FVRD jurisdiction: own the home, rent the pad; increases once in 12 months on three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026) plus a proportional share of levy/utility changes. Bare-land strata lots at Tapadera are NOT pad tenancies — owners there own the strata lot under strata law, a different tenure from the Lougheed Highway parks. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **ALR and hazards:** any Lake Errock-area parcel in the Agricultural Land Reserve follows the ALC frame of one principal residence to a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha); hillside/lakeshore lots may sit in geological-hazard or riparian development permit areas (the former OCP carried both; Bylaw 1747's areas for a specific lot are UNVERIFIED). One feed card claims a lot is "Out of the ALR" in listing text — that is not verification. Check both overlays by PID. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Property taxes are rural-area taxes, paid to the Province:** a property not in a city, town, district or village is in a rural area; its owner receives the notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays the Province, not the FVRD, Mission or Kent. Under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11, rural taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty on any portion unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on any portion unpaid on October 31. (Do NOT apply Mission's flat 10% city penalty here.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The mailing address lies about who governs you.** Listings here carry "Mission, BC" mailing addresses — neither Mission nor Kent is the governing authority. Permits, zoning, the OCP and the Neighbourhood Plan are FVRD Electoral Area C (Bylaw 1747, 2024, adopted September 18, 2025 — a brand-new plan replacing a 1998 bylaw), and taxes go to the Province's Surveyor of Taxes. https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/areacocp and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area
- **The tax penalty regime flips at the city boundary.** A Lake Errock property pays the rural two-step penalty (5% after July 2, a further 5% after October 31), while a property inside the City of Mission pays Mission's single flat 10% the day after the July 2 due date. Same board "community" in the stats table, two different tax systems. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01 and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **The feed borrows complexes from two other communities.** Tapadera Estates' own listings call it a Harrison Mills community, and the Lougheed Highway park address in this feed is the Dewdney-Deroche corridor's Oasis ground — both surface here because of board-line drawing, not because either sits on the lake. Price and diligence follow the actual address and jurisdiction, not the page label. https://kw.com/property/14600-Morris-Valley-Road-31-Mission-BC-V0M-1A1/2042579860092792 and https://jaredgibbons.ca/lake-errock.html
- **Owning the land vs renting the pad splits this corridor in two.** At Tapadera (bare-land strata, LMS648) owners own their strata lots under strata law with strata fees; in the Lougheed Highway manufactured-home parks residents own only the home and hold a pad tenancy under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. Same Morris Valley / Highway 7 drive, opposite tenure risk. https://www.tapaderaestates.net/ and http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- **The new OCP is newer than most listing text.** Bylaw 1747, 2024 was adopted September 18, 2025 and includes a dedicated Lake Errock Neighbourhood Plan — listing cards still citing the old plan era, or zoning labels like "R-2," predate or bypass it. The bylaw's Lake Errock schedules had not been read in this research: UNVERIFIED at lot level, so confirm with the FVRD. https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/areacocp
- **Building lots here sell their paperwork, not just their dirt.** Feed cards for Malcolm Road and Bayview Road lots cite perc tests, geotechnical hazard assessments, surveys and riparian assessments as already completed — the value is in whether those reports are current, transferable and matched to the lot's permit areas, none of which listing text verifies. https://jaredgibbons.ca/lake-errock.html
