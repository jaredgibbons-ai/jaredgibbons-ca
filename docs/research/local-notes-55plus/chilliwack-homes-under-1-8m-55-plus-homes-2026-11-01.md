## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw, and a tenant in a 55+ building must still meet the age bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home parks run on tenancy law, not strata law.** Cottonwood Village tenancies fall under BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act: the resident owns the home and rents the pad; pad rent may be raised only once in 12 months on at least three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026; 2.2% for 2027) plus a proportional share of changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. Cottonwood Village also sits on First Nations leased land with a listing-stated lease expiry in 2076 plus an option to extend — a tenure layer strata law does not have. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000.** Section 2 caps a secondary suite at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling; Section 4.06(1) permits only 1 accessory dwelling unit per lot (secondary suite, coach house or garden suite; garden suites capped at 100 m² gross floor area); ADU parking is set at s.5.04. Chilliwack's SSMUH amendment is Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, adopted June 18, 2024. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **ALR housing frame, where a parcel in this band is in the Agricultural Land Reserve:** one principal residence at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. Whether a specific parcel is in the ALR: UNVERIFIED at parcel level. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** the Province's general rates are 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% on the portion above $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% on the portion above $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion of value above $3,000,000 — a home under $1.8M is taxed in the 1% and 2% tiers only. First-time-buyer and newly-built exemption thresholds: UNVERIFIED in this file — no exemption figure is stated. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Property taxes:** City of Chilliwack notices are mailed annually at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; a 5% late payment penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **Short-term rentals:** the City regulates short-term rental operations under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141 — separate from the strata position above, which governs what a strata bylaw may do. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf
- **Leasehold pockets:** the live page states some detached homes in this band are leasehold (naming Cedarbrook / Shxwhá:y Village lands, Cultus Lake Park and certain gated Sardis subdivisions); no official source confirming that list was opened in the research pass, so the list is UNVERIFIED as stated and tenure remains a per-title check. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-homes-under-1-8m.html

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually BUY in Chilliwack?** Five verified age-restricted strata complexes — Ivy Green and Spadina Regency on Spadina Avenue downtown, Mountainview Estates in Proper East, Knight Lodge and Cottage Grove on the Sardis side — plus homes in Cottonwood Village, the city's one verified 55+ manufactured-home park, where you buy the home and rent the pad. Mountview Apartments is a flagged conflict, not a verified sixth complex. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: How many Chilliwack homes are for sale under $1.8M right now?** That figure cannot be stated from the sources used here. - **Q: Three buildings share Spadina Avenue — are they all 55+?** No. Ivy Green (45775) is 55+ with no pets; Spadina Regency (45765) is 55+ with rentals and pets permitted; Chilliwack Gardens (45749) states "no age restrictions" in its own listing. Check the civic number and the bylaws, not the street. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a Chilliwack 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent, or to move into later?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, and Spadina Regency's listings state rentals are permitted. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals; the City's own Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141 also applies. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Building by building: Ivy Green and Mountainview Estates record no pets; Spadina Regency's listings state pets are permitted; Knight Lodge records pets allowed with restrictions; Cottonwood Village allows 1 pet. Confirm against the registered bylaws or park rules for the specific unit. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: In Cottonwood Village, what am I buying and what do I pay monthly?** The manufactured home itself; the pad is rented under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act on First Nations leased land (listing-stated lease expiry 2076, option to extend). Pad rent can rise only once a year, on three full months' notice, within the provincial cap (2.3% for 2026; 2.2% for 2027) plus the proportional levy/utility amount. The current pad fee for a specific home: confirm in the park's tenancy documents — UNCONFIRMED here. https://bentruscottrealestate.com/manufactured-homes.html?utm_medium=paid&utm_source=fb&utm_id=52609658579346&utm_content=52609658626346&utm_term=52609658585146&utm_campaign=52609658579346 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Can a detached home in this band take a suite or a coach house for a caregiver or family?** One accessory dwelling unit per lot where the zone permits it — a secondary suite (capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling), a coach house, or a garden suite (capped at 100 m²) — under Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, ss. 2 and 4.06. On an ALR parcel the ALC frame is tighter: a suite only inside the principal residence, and one additional residence at 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in Chilliwack?** The in-city ladder runs: independent strata (the five complexes in Section 2) → subsidized independent rental (Jubilee Place, 36 units, 55+ low-income with veterans emphasis; The Paramount, 65+ income-tested) → independent/assisted retirement rental in the hospital precinct (Chartwell Birchwood, Hampton House, Lynnwood) → registered assisted living (Birchwood, The Cascades, Waverly Seniors Village, Sto:lo Elders Lodge) — all within the urban city, close to Chilliwack General Hospital at 45600 Menholm Rd. Sources: Section 2 above.
