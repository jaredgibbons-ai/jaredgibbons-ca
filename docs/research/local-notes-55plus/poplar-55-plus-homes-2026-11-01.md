# Poplar — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Poplar" is the board (Paragon) sub-area name; the City of Abbotsford's own demographic profile name for the same ground is "South Poplar" (profile area 5), and no Poplar-specific neighbourhood plan is listed in the OCP — the area is planned under the Abbotsforward 2050 Official Community Plan (Bylaw 2600-2016, as updated). https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/2020%20McKee%20Profile%20-%20spreads%20final.pdf and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- The sub-area sits south of Highway 1 around Huntingdon Road, King Road and Gladwin Road, west of the Marshall Road / McCallum Road area and east toward Clearbrook Road; exact board boundary lines are not published in text form — they are shown in the City's interactive WebMap. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap
- This is a mostly detached-house and rural-edge sub-area: Poplar carried only 30 attached listings and 3 attached sales in September 2026 (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report), and the base guide records that low-rise condo buildings concentrate north of Highway 1 along South Fraser Way rather than inside Poplar. The two verified 55+ strata complexes in Section 2 are the exception, not the pattern.
- The hospital is immediately north across Highway 1: Abbotsford Regional Hospital and Cancer Centre, 32900 Marshall Road (emergency, surgery, maternity, 24 hours). The Abbotsford Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 100-2692 Clearbrook Road. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Abbotsford/abbotsford-regional-hospital-and-cancer-centre
- BC Transit Central Fraser Valley routes through or beside Poplar: route 3 (Clearbrook / Huntingdon, via the Gladwin and King Road area), route 5 (Clearbrook / Bourquin via Hospital, on Marshall Road at the east edge), route 6 (Gladwin Road corridor) and route 12 (UFV / Bourquin via Jackson Street and McCallum Park and Ride). There is no SkyTrain or West Coast Express in Abbotsford. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf
- Schools physically in the sub-area: South Poplar Traditional Elementary (32746 Huntingdon Road), Jackson Elementary (33165 King Road) and Abbotsford Traditional School, middle/secondary (2272 Windsor Street); SD34 locator runs for both elementary addresses return Jackson Elementary → Abbotsford Middle → Abbotsford Senior Secondary for the regular stream (SD34 School Locator, run 2026-11-01). https://southpoplar.abbyschools.ca/ and https://jackson.abbyschools.ca/school-name-history-15
- The south edge is rural: lots toward Huntingdon Road are in or beside the Agricultural Land Reserve, and Poplar drains toward the Sumas Prairie system, so floodplain status is a lot-by-lot WebMap check (Zoning Bylaw Schedule E), not an area-wide answer. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

Poplar has exactly two verified 55+ strata (ownership) complexes and one verified 55+ manufactured-home park. Everything else seniors-oriented in the sub-area is care housing (Section 2, below). Buyers who want more 55+ strata choice in Abbotsford will find it in Abbotsford West and Central Abbotsford (see those files).

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- **Central Heights Manor — 33535 King Rd.** Freehold strata plan NWS3075, 3 levels, built 1990; listings describe a 55+ community, and building-source data records "No Pets or Rentals" in its bylaws summary (on rentals, see the provincial rule in Section 3 — strata rental-restriction bylaws are void). Name trap: several listing feeds mislabel this building's sub-area as "Central Abbotsford"; the building directory's Abbotsford building list places it in Poplar, and listing sources agree on the King Road address — it is filed here. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/buildings/abbotsford/poplar and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3077455-307-33535-king-road-abbotsford-bc-v2s-6z5 and https://www.zolo.ca/abbotsford-real-estate/33535-king-road/208
- **Crown Point — 1450 McCallum Rd.** Gated townhouse complex, freehold strata plan NWS2984, built 1989, zoning RM30; an MLS data sheet describes a "sought-after 55+ gated community" and records the pet bylaw as cats/dogs allowed, number limit one, with restrictions. https://www.cotala.com/tours/85537/FullPublic.pdf?i=1703196650

### Verified 55+ manufactured-home park (you own the home, rent the pad — not strata)

- **Maplewood Estates (also listed as Maplewood Mobile Home Park) — 2035 Martens St.** Listings for homes in this park use both names for the same address and describe an "Adult Community — 55+": a small pet is allowed and the buyer must be approved by the park owner before purchase. Pad rent and site count: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3097221-26-2035-martens-street-abbotsford-british-columbia-v2t6m3-299900/brochure

### Retirement residences and care (rental/care — NOT strata ownership)

- **Maplewood House — 1919 Jackson St.** Long-term care home run by the Maplewood Care Society: 78 beds (all publicly funded; 76 single-occupancy rooms in the Seniors Advocate data), opened 1989, accredited, funded primarily through Fraser Health with all admissions coordinated through Fraser Health — there is nothing to buy and no private application route to the home itself. https://maplewood.bc.ca/long-term-care/ and https://www.seniorsadvocatebc.ca/quickfacts/location/93664/
- A new Fraser Health long-term care community is planned for the vacant lot beside Maplewood House at the same 1919 Jackson Street address: five storeys, replacing the 109 beds at Cottage and Worthington Pavilion (Central Abbotsford) and adding 91 new beds, plus a stand-alone child care facility for 49 children; Fraser Health's project note expected construction to begin in 2025 with completion in 2027. https://thebeat.fraserhealth.ca/news/2023/Jun/new-long-term-care-community-coming-to-abbotsford

### Verified 55+ rental / subsidized seniors rental

- None verified inside Poplar. No source checked records a 55+ rental building or a subsidized seniors rental building in this sub-area; the closest verified subsidized stock is the Cottage and Worthington Pavilions on Cottage Lane in Central Abbotsford (see the Central Abbotsford file), and those beds are slated to move to the new 1919 Jackson Street care community in this sub-area when it opens. https://thebeat.fraserhealth.ca/news/2023/Jun/new-long-term-care-community-coming-to-abbotsford

### Checked and NOT 55+ (do not put these on a 55+ page)

- **Kings Crossing — 1641 King Cres.** A new development of 20 townhomes by Rockwood Homes Inc., selling with completion expected in 2026; the developer source states no age restriction. It is Poplar's newest townhouse stock and it is general market, not 55+. https://thekingscrossing.ca/contact-us/
- **"Maplewood" name collisions (three different buildings):** the 55+ strata in Abbotsford West is Maplewood *Manor* (32040 Tims Ave); the care home here is Maplewood *House* (1919 Jackson St); the manufactured park here is Maplewood *Estates* (2035 Martens St). Only the first and third are buyable, and they are in different sub-areas — a buyer searching "Maplewood Abbotsford" will get all three mixed together. Sources: the building entries in this file and the Abbotsford West file.
- No other Poplar complex marketed as 55+ could be verified from a public source; any additional "55+" claim for a Poplar address is UNCONFIRMED until a strata plan, MLS data sheet or manager source states the age restriction.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **In a BC strata, "55+" is the only age restriction that still exists.** The Strata Property Act (ss. 123.1/123.2, via Bill 44) has allowed only 55-and-over residency bylaws since Nov 24, 2022; older buildings' 19+, 40+ or 45+ bylaws are invalid, which is why any pre-2022 document for Central Heights Manor or Crown Point that shows a different age should be re-read against the current registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Owning is not the same as living in the unit.** The Province's guidance: an under-55 person can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it — the bylaw controls occupancy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Who is exempt (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who lived in the unit when the bylaw passed (legacy), live-in caregivers, and — after the May 1, 2023 expansion — the qualifying resident's younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in that resident's care, and adult children the resident cared for before age 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void in every BC strata** (since Nov 24, 2022), so the "No Rentals" line in Central Heights Manor's older bylaws summary cannot be enforced as a long-term rental ban; short-term rental bylaws remain enforceable, and a tenant must still meet the 55+ residency bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Maplewood Estates runs on the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not the Strata Property Act.** BC's page states the Act covers most manufactured home tenancies: the buyer owns the home, rents the site, and the park owner must approve a purchaser (the park's listings say exactly that). Site rent may be increased only once every 12 months, with at least three full months' notice, capped at the annual provincial limit (2.3% for increases effective in 2026; 2.2% announced for 2027) plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **If the plan is to age in a Poplar house instead:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014, s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings (City reports state the criteria include a minimum 400 m² lot and 12.0 m frontage) — the suite is the usual route to a caregiver or family member on the same lot. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **Rural south-edge lots:** where the A1 (Agricultural One) zone applies, the minimum subdivision lot area is 8.0 ha, and ALR land adds Agricultural Land Commission Act and ALR Use Regulation controls on subdivision and added dwellings — relevant if a buyer hopes to split a Huntingdon Road parcel for family. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/content/coa/coabylaws/z/zoning/?xsl=/templates/browse.xsl and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Tax mechanics:** Abbotsford tax is the BC Assessment value multiplied by a rate per $1,000 (Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3727-2026); taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes and https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How much 55+ choice is there in Poplar, honestly?** Two strata complexes (Central Heights Manor, 33535 King Rd; Crown Point, 1450 McCallum Rd) and one manufactured-home park (Maplewood Estates, 2035 Martens St). That is the whole verified buyable 55+ stock in the sub-area — Abbotsford West has fifteen verified 55+ strata complexes, so most 55+ buyers who want selection end up looking there. Sources: this file, Section 2, and the Abbotsford West file.
- **Q: My partner is 50. Can we live at Crown Point?** Yes, under the provincial exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement — Strata Property Regulation 7.01 covers that case, along with legacy residents and live-in caregivers. Crown Point's own MLS data records its pet bylaw (one cat or dog, with restrictions); confirm the age-bylaw wording in the registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.cotala.com/tours/85537/FullPublic.pdf?i=1703196650
- **Q: I read Central Heights Manor is "in Central Abbotsford." Which is it?** Poplar, on the sources checked: the building directory's Poplar building list carries 33535 King Rd, and the Central Abbotsford label in some feeds is a data error that has propagated. The strata plan (NWS3075) is the stable identifier. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/buildings/abbotsford/poplar
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit at Central Heights Manor? Its bylaws say no rentals.** Not on that bylaw's strength: BC voided all strata rental-restriction bylaws on Nov 24, 2022, so a long-term tenancy is permitted despite the older summary — but your tenant must still satisfy the 55+ residency bylaw or hold an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What's different about buying in Maplewood Estates?** You buy the home, not land: the site is rented under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, the park owner must approve you as a buyer (the listings state this), one small pet is allowed, and pad rent can rise only once a year on three full months' notice within the provincial cap (2.3% for 2026) plus the proportional levy/utility share. It is a tenancy relationship, with none of the Strata Property Act's Form B, depreciation report or strata-meeting machinery. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3097221-26-2035-martens-street-abbotsford-british-columbia-v2t6m3-299900/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Can I buy under 55 and hold the unit until I qualify?** Yes — ownership is not restricted, only residing is. An under-55 buyer can own at Central Heights Manor or Crown Point but could not live there until meeting the bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Is there any 55+ rental building in Poplar if I don't want to buy?** None verified. The sub-area's seniors housing beyond ownership is care housing (Maplewood House long-term care), which is Fraser Health-funded and admission-based, not a rental application. The nearest verified subsidized seniors residences are the Cottage and Worthington Pavilions in Central Abbotsford — themselves scheduled to be replaced by the new care community rising at 1919 Jackson St in this sub-area. https://maplewood.bc.ca/long-term-care/ and https://thebeat.fraserhealth.ca/news/2023/Jun/new-long-term-care-community-coming-to-abbotsford
- **Q: Does the floodplain touch Poplar homes?** Poplar drains toward the Sumas Prairie system, which the City describes as low-lying and subject to Nooksack River overflow flooding; that description belongs to Sumas Prairie itself, and a specific lot's floodplain status is only settled by the Zoning Bylaw Schedule E map in the City WebMap — check it before subject removal on any low-lying or rural lot. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse and https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap
- **Q: How do I get to the hospital from King Road without a car?** BC Transit route 5 (Clearbrook / Bourquin via Hospital) runs Marshall Road at the sub-area's east edge, and route 12 serves Jackson Street toward Bourquin Exchange; the hospital at 32900 Marshall Road is immediately north of Poplar across Highway 1. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The City doesn't call this area "Poplar."** Its official profile name is "South Poplar" (profile area 5); "Poplar" is the real estate board's label. A buyer reading City documents, census profiles or demographic reports should search "South Poplar" or find nothing. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/2020%20McKee%20Profile%20-%20spreads%20final.pdf
- **Three "Maplewoods," three tenures, two sub-areas.** Maplewood Manor (Tims Ave, Abbotsford West) is a buyable 55+ strata; Maplewood House (Jackson St, here) is a Fraser Health-funded care home with 78 beds; Maplewood Estates (Martens St, here) is a 55+ manufactured-home park where you rent the pad. Search results blend all three — check the street before booking a viewing. Sources: this file, Section 2, and the Abbotsford West file.
- **Poplar is about to hold the city's biggest care construction.** The new long-term care community beside Maplewood House at 1919 Jackson St will hold the 109 Cottage/Worthington beds plus 91 new ones — 200 beds on one Poplar site — with a 49-space child care facility built alongside; Fraser Health expected completion in 2027. For a 55+ buyer, that is the sub-area's care backstop arriving on a fixed schedule. https://thebeat.fraserhealth.ca/news/2023/Jun/new-long-term-care-community-coming-to-abbotsford
- **The "Central Abbotsford" mislabel on Central Heights Manor is structural, not a typo in one listing.** The same feed error recurs across portals; the building directory's Poplar list and the King Road address are the reliable anchors, and the strata plan number (NWS3075) never changes. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/buildings/abbotsford/poplar
- **The Traditional school pathway runs through this sub-area.** South Poplar Traditional Elementary is one of only two feeder schools for Abbotsford Traditional School (Grades 6–12), and feeder students are automatically registered there — a draw for grandparents buying near family, and a detail the regular catchment (Jackson Elementary) does not capture. https://ats.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/registration/atms
- **Poplar's name is literal.** The community was named for the poplar trees planted on top of the hill, and the Old South Poplar School is on the Abbotsford Community Heritage Register — the rural character at the south edge, where A1 zoning sets an 8.0 ha subdivision minimum, is policy, not an accident of development timing. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/2006%20Commission%20-%20PRC%2028%20Abbotsford%20Community%20Heritage%20Register%20Addition....pdf and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/content/coa/coabylaws/z/zoning/?xsl=/templates/browse.xsl
