# Ambleside (West Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Ambleside is the District of West Vancouver's municipal town centre and waterfront: the Marine Drive commercial core around 14th–18th Streets, Ambleside Park and the Argyle Avenue waterfront, and the residential streets between the water and the Upper Levels Highway. The District's Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 4985, 2018, names the Ambleside Municipal Town Centre as the District's town centre, with Park Royal immediately east as the separate regional shopping centre. Exact street-by-street sub-area boundaries: UNVERIFIED — no District source read publishes them. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01), and https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- The waterfront from 13th to 18th Streets is publicly owned under the District's Waterfront Guiding Principles, and the District announced on March 31, 2025 that the final home on the Ambleside waterfront, 1444 Argyle Avenue, had been acquired — the last of 32 lots acquired since 1975 — to form part of Brissenden Waterfront Park. https://westvancouver.ca/government-administration/major-projects/ambleside-waterfront-park-implementation-project
- The civic cluster sits inside Ambleside: the West Vancouver Community Centre and Aquatic Centre at 2121 Marine Drive, West Vancouver Memorial Library at 1950 Marine Drive, and the Seniors' Activity Centre at 695 21st Street. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/facilities/community-centre and https://westvancouver.ca/arts-culture/west-vancouver-memorial-library and https://westvancouver.ca/news/come-shape-future-ambleside-attend-community-workshop
- Schools inside the sub-area: Hollyburn Elementary (K–7), 1329 Duchess Avenue, and West Vancouver Secondary (Grades 8–12), 1750 Mathers Avenue, plus West Vancouver Inglewood Secondary, 1735 Inglewood Avenue. School District 45 catchments are address-based; run each address in the SD45 locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/045 and https://westvancouverschools.ca/admissions/student-admissions-guidelines/
- Transit: TransLink route 250 runs on Marine Drive through Ambleside between Horseshoe Bay, Dundarave, Park Royal and downtown Vancouver via the Lions Gate Bridge; Park Royal Exchange, immediately east, is the western terminus of the R2 Marine–Willingdon RapidBus, which since September 7, 2026 runs one-seat through Lonsdale Quay and Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt250.pdf and https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/
- Where the seniors housing actually is: Ambleside holds West Vancouver's whole seniors-housing cluster. The 55+ strata condos are on Duchess Avenue (Westview Terrace, 1390 Duchess Ave) and Marine Drive (Lincoln Gardens, 2110 Marine Dr), with Tudor Gardens at 843 22nd St on the west edge. The rental/care residences line the same few blocks: Hollyburn House at 2095 Marine Drive, Kiwanis Manor at 959 21st Street, Westerleigh PARC at 725 22nd Street, Amica West Vancouver at 659 Clyde Avenue at the east end by Taylor Way, and Inglewood Care Centre at 725 Inglewood Avenue. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Boundary caution at both ends: sources variously describe the 22nd Street strip as Ambleside's west end or as the edge of Dundarave (PARC describes Westerleigh as sitting "on the edge of the Dundarave neighborhood"; a Tudor Gardens listing calls it "the village of Dundarave"), and the Clyde Avenue / Taylor Way end shades into Park Royal. Because the sub-area boundary is UNVERIFIED, the 22nd Street and Clyde Avenue buildings are carried in this file with that flag. https://parcliving.ca/blog/the-best-senior-living-location-in-west-vancouver-bc/ and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3120687-101-843-22nd-street-west-vancouver-british-columbia-v7v4c1-739900/brochure

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why the "rentals not allowed" lines still shown in older building data for Ambleside buildings (Section 2) do not reflect current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010.** Houses sit in the RS single-family dwelling zones; Section 200 states minimum site areas of 8,094 m² in RS1, 1,625 m² in RS2, 975 m² in RS3, 731 m² in RS4 and 488 m² in RS5, and lists secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses in the single-family zones; apartment and mixed-use form concentrates in the town-centre blocks. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018**, with the Ambleside Centre Local Area Plan as the area-specific plan — implemented by provincial order effective April 7, 2026 after the District missed the Province's December 31, 2025 adoption deadline. Town-centre and adjacent lots carry plan expectations that did not exist under the prior OCP alone. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026HMA0038-000377
- **Foreshore Development Permit:** the District requires one for work within the Foreshore Development Permit Area — waterfront property or other properties at risk of coastal flooding — which applies to Ambleside's Bellevue Avenue and waterfront-edge properties. An Environmental Development Permit is required within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Assisted living is not an age-bylaw product:** Hollyburn House, Amica West Vancouver and the Kiwanis Manor assisted-living suites are registered under BC's Assisted Living Registry; admission runs through the operator (and Vancouver Coastal Health for publicly funded care such as Inglewood), not under the Strata Property Act. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=may5&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=41&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc&f%5B0%5D=city%3AWest%20Vancouver
- **Property taxes:** District of West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes
- **Additional school tax:** at Ambleside price points the Province's additional school tax can apply — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Ambleside?** Three verified age-restricted strata buildings: Westview Terrace (1390 Duchess Ave, 25 units, described in a current listing as a 55+ building), Tudor Gardens (843 22nd St, listing-stated 55+) and Lincoln Gardens (2110 Marine Dr, 44 units, listed as 55+ in a North Shore building guide). Everything else seniors-branded in Ambleside is rental or care. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Duchess Avenue has several older buildings showing "age restrictions" — are they all 55+?** No — not on the sources checked. Building data for the Westerlies (1480 Duchess), Pacific Horizons (1427 Duchess), Sunset Mariner (1455 Duchess) and Wedgewood Terrace (1730 Duchess) records an unspecified "age restriction" without stating 55+, and Wedgewood Terrace is a co-op (title: shares in cooperative), not a strata at all. Since Nov 24, 2022 the only enforceable strata age bylaw is 55+, but the exact bylaw in each building has to be read in its own strata documents. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/westerlies-1480-duchess-ave and https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3101405-1730-duchess-avenue-203-west-vancouver-bc-v7v-1p9/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Building data says "rentals not allowed" at Tudor Gardens and Lincoln Gardens. Can I rent out a unit?** Yes, as a matter of provincial law: every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, so the stale MLS wording does not bind. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the 55+ buildings?** It is building-by-building: a Tudor Gardens listing states no pets, while building data for Lincoln Gardens records pets allowed with restrictions. Check the specific building's bylaws — the two verified 55+ buildings differ on this. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3120687-101-843-22nd-street-west-vancouver-british-columbia-v7v4c1-739900/brochure and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/lincoln-gardens-2110-marine-drive
- **Q: Is there below-market seniors rental in Ambleside?** Yes — Kiwanis Manor at 959 21st St, run by the non-profit Kiwanis North Shore Housing Society as 55+ independent living; the society states most of its seniors communities offer below-market rental housing and applications are accepted year-round. The same address also carries a registered assisted-living residence (see the conflict note in Section 2). https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/kiwanis-manor/
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver, is the Vancouver Coastal Health hospital serving the North Shore, including West Vancouver; the North Vancouver Urgent and Primary Care Centre at Suite 200, 221 West Esplanade, North Vancouver, is the urgent-care option. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/north-vancouver-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** Route 250 on Marine Drive runs to downtown Vancouver via the Lions Gate Bridge, and Park Royal Exchange at Ambleside's east edge is the western terminus of the R2 RapidBus to Metrotown via Lonsdale Quay and Phibbs Exchange (since September 7, 2026, every 6–7 minutes at peak). Inside Ambleside itself, the Community Centre, library, Seniors' Activity Centre and both 55+ strata clusters are within a few flat blocks of Marine Drive. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt250.pdf and https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in Ambleside?** The in-area ladder is unusually complete: independent strata (Westview Terrace, Tudor Gardens, Lincoln Gardens), independent rental (Westerleigh PARC, Kiwanis Manor), retirement residences with assisted living (Hollyburn House, Amica West Vancouver), and long-term care (Inglewood Care Centre, 230 publicly funded beds). The publicly funded steps are allocated through Vancouver Coastal Health, not bought. https://www.seniorsadvocatebc.ca/quickfacts/location/75989/ and https://cogirseniorliving.ca/retirement-home-west-vancouver-british-columbia/venvi-hollyburn-house

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Ambleside holds the District's entire seniors cluster.** Every verified 55+ strata, retirement residence, assisted-living registration and long-term care bed in West Vancouver found in this research sits within the Ambleside flat or its immediate edges — the hillside neighbourhoods above the highway (Canterbury, Chartwell) have none. Sources: Section 2 above and the Assisted Living Registry's West Vancouver list. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=may5&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=41&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc&f%5B0%5D=city%3AWest%20Vancouver
- **The Inglewood site is about to be rebuilt into a much bigger seniors campus.** On September 21, 2026 the District approved bylaws and a development permit for the Inglewood Campus of Care redevelopment at 725 Inglewood Ave: Phase 1 builds a 364-bed publicly funded long-term care facility (existing residents move into it, then the current building is demolished), and Phase 2 adds 200 independent living units and 161 seniors rental housing units. Tenant placement and wait lists are Baptist Housing's, not the District's. https://westvancouver.ca/business-development/development-applications/inglewood-campus-care
- **One address, two products — and the sources genuinely conflict.** 959 21st St is described by its non-profit owner as 55+ independent living and by the provincial registry as a registered assisted-living residence under a different legal operator (Dundarave Assisted Living Residence Partnership, with Park Place Seniors Living publishing the assisted-living page). Anyone marketing or buying around Kiwanis Manor should establish which component a specific suite belongs to. https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/kiwanis-manor/ and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=may5&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=41&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc&f%5B0%5D=city%3AWest%20Vancouver
- **Hollyburn House has changed brands without moving.** The 2095 Marine Drive residence is now marketed as Venvi Hollyburn House under Cogir Senior Living; older material brands it Revera. The Assisted Living Registry's legal operator name (WC Operating (British Columbia - 1) GP Inc) matches neither brand — brand names churn, the registry entry is the stable record. https://cogirseniorliving.ca/retirement-home-west-vancouver-british-columbia/venvi-hollyburn-house
- **"Age restrictions" in MLS building data is not a synonym for 55+.** On Duchess Avenue alone, four buildings carry that generic flag with no age stated anywhere public, and one of them (Wedgewood Terrace) is a housing co-op rather than a strata. In a post-2022 market the only enforceable strata age bylaw is 55+, but a page that calls every age-flagged building "55+" would be inventing the number for at least four Ambleside buildings. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/wedgewood-terrace-1730-duchess-ave and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The waterfront is finished assembling.** With the March 31, 2025 acquisition of 1444 Argyle Avenue — the last of 32 waterfront lots acquired since 1975 — the public waterfront from Ambleside Park to John Lawson Park is complete as Brissenden Waterfront Park, which is the setting the Marine Drive and Duchess Avenue buildings sell against. https://westvancouver.ca/government-administration/major-projects/ambleside-waterfront-park-implementation-project
- **The town centre's density framework arrived by provincial order, not by council.** The District missed the December 31, 2025 deadline for the Ambleside Centre Local Area Plan; a provincial order implementing it took effect April 7, 2026, and the District must file progress reports with the Province every three months through 2026 and 2027 on the remaining housing directives. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026HMA0038-000377
