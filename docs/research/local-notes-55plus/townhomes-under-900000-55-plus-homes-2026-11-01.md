## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; sub-55 bylaws invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). The rule is provincial — it applies identically in Surrey, Langley and any other municipality the feed reaches. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** Under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rental bylaws are still allowed — and municipal short-term rental rules then differ by city on top of the strata position, so a cross-region buyer must check both layers in the actual municipality. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Strata paperwork is where a townhome under $900K is actually priced.** The Province's strata buying guidance directs buyers to the Form B, the Form F, strata bylaws and rules, fees and special levies — age restrictions, pet rules and rental restrictions live in those documents, not in the listing price. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/first-time-owners
- **Municipal bylaws in the confirmed geography:** Surrey — Zoning By-law No. 12000 and Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026; never cite the replaced No. 18020). Langley City — Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300; Township of Langley — Zoning Bylaw No. 2500. A rule quoted for one municipality does not transfer to the other, and nothing in this file states a Chilliwack rule, because no Chilliwack feed scope or source was verified. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **The $900,000 line sits inside the insured-mortgage range (CMHC):** homes between $500,000 and $1,499,999 require 5% on the first $500,000 plus 10% above it; insurance is required with less than 20% down, with premium rates from 0.6% to 4.5% of the total mortgage amount. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Property transfer tax across this band:** 1% up to $200,000 and 2% above $200,000 up to $2,000,000 — a purchase under $900,000 meets only those two tiers. First-time buyer full exemption requires $835,000 or less (partial to $860,000), so part of this band can qualify on price and part (above $860,000) cannot; the newly built full exemption threshold of $1,100,000 covers any qualifying new townhome on this page on price alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Property taxes** are municipal and differ by city and due date — Surrey's are due July 2 with 5% penalties after July 2 and after September 2; no Langley or Chilliwack date is stated here because none was verified for this page's file. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **First-home programs:** FHSA and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits are UNVERIFIED in this file — no live CRA page was read in this pass.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which areas does this feed actually cover?** Confirmed in the visible feed: Surrey (Clayton, West Newton) and Langley City. The editorial's wider claim — the Fraser Valley and Metro Vancouver "from Surrey to Chilliwack" — is UNVERIFIED as feed scope; no card from Chilliwack or any other community appeared in the page text read. https://jaredgibbons.ca/townhomes-under-900000.html
- **Q: How many townhomes are for sale under $900,000 right now?** No sourced number exists — no cross-region band count is published, SnapStats has no price-band cut and no townhome-only split, and no pagination count appeared in the page text read. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/townhomes-under-900000.md
- **Q: Can a 55+ townhome really come up under $900K?** The band's own feed says the form exists in it: its West Newton card is a rancher-style townhome in Glenwood Village, a verified 55+ complex (NWS2768, 86 homes). That is feed evidence for one card at reading — not a promise that a 55+ unit is listed under the ceiling on any given day. https://jaredgibbons.ca/townhomes-under-900000.html
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ townhome from this page?** Yes — the provincial younger-spouse/partner exemption applies, along with the under-19 child, adult-child and caregiver exemptions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55 and like a 55+ unit's price. Can I buy it?** You can own it; you may not be able to live in it. You could rent it to a 55+ tenant (long-term rental bans are void province-wide) or hold it for a 55+ parent — but writing an offer assuming you can move in is the classic error this band sets up, because restricted and open product sit side by side at the same prices. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What down payment does a townhome in this band need?** Two tiers: 5% on the first $500,000 plus 10% on the portion above $500,000, plus the insurance premium if buying with less than 20% down (CMHC premium rates 0.6% to 4.5% of the total mortgage amount). https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer avoid property transfer tax here?** On price alone, partly: full exemption to $835,000, partial to $860,000, on the first $500,000 of price — townhomes above $860,000 in this band are outside it on price, and the program's other conditions still apply either way. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: Are these Jared Gibbons' own listings?** No — the visible cards are other brokerages' listings, with Jared's contact block as site owner, not listing agent. https://jaredgibbons.ca/townhomes-under-900000.html
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale pool?** Yes — the next buyer must be 55+, exempt, or buying without living in the unit. In a cross-region search that is easy to forget, because the restriction follows the complex, not the price band. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Two townhomes, same price, one 55+ and one not — what else differs?** Management and monthly cost: the visible feed itself includes both amenity-rich complexes (a Clayton Rise card describing pool, clubhouse and gym) and a self-managed complex (Longlea Estates, per its listing text) — fees and reserve positions can differ as much as the age rule does. Read both Form Bs. https://jaredgibbons.ca/townhomes-under-900000.html
