# The Crest (Burnaby) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**The Crest has ZERO verified 55+ ownership stock — no 55+ strata and no 55+ manufactured-home park was verified.** What the sub-area does have is a genuine 55+ rental: Derby Manor, on the George Derby campus at its south edge. A buyer cannot purchase in any seniors building here — do not let a 55+ page imply otherwise.

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- None verified in this sub-area. The research file confirms no condo building or townhome complex name in The Crest on a developer or strata source, and no source checked states any age restriction for the low-rise strata stock near The Crest Centre and along 10th Avenue: age restriction UNCONFIRMED / not verified as 55+ for all of it.

### Verified 55+ rental (not ownership)

- **Derby Manor (The Derby Manor) — 8601 16th Ave, V3N 0G1.** A verified 55+ independent-living rental: the operator's own site states "The Derby Manor offers 122 affordable non-profit rental units for individuals 55+," in a building completed in 2017, owned and managed by the George Derby Care Society on land the Society provided. The Province's December 10, 2015 construction release gives the mix: 95 one-bedroom suites, 14 one-bedroom-plus-den suites and 13 two-bedroom suites, with hospitality and support services including two daily meals and snacks, light housekeeping, laundry services, social and recreational activities and 24/7 on-site emergency response. Rental only — nothing to buy. https://derbymanor.ca/ and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2015MNGD0084-002065
- How Derby Manor tenancy works, from the operator's own FAQ: residents hold a monthly Occupancy Agreement rather than a lease; the monthly fee is set and includes a basic package of services (you cannot rent without the package); and there is an additional monthly charge of $650 for a second person occupying a suite. Operator-stated terms — confirm the current fee schedule with the Society before relying on them. https://derbymanor.ca/
- Amenities the operator lists: courtyards and walkable grounds, a dining area, BBQ facility, multi-purpose room, hair salon and fitness centre, with a calendar of cultural events. The operator's own description also invites visitors to "visit our Show Suite in the Crest Mall" — the building markets directly to this sub-area. http://www.derbymanor.ca/

### Retirement residences and care (rental/care — NOT strata ownership)

- **George Derby Centre — 7550 Cumberland St, V3N 3X5.** Long-term care home with 300 publicly subsidized beds, operated by the George Derby Care Society under contract with Fraser Health, on the same campus as Derby Manor — the Province's release describes Derby Manor as "an addition to the existing campus of care site at George Derby Centre." The site has a veterans' care history going back to 1947/1948; the current centre opened in May 1988. Care only — access through Fraser Health. Cumberland Street addresses carry The Crest board label in current listings (see Section 1); the Edmonds file also covers this campus, because the two sub-areas meet here — it is one campus, not two buildings. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/3/A/george-derby-centre and https://georgederby.ca/newsite/about-us/ and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2015MNGD0084-002065
- No assisted-living residence with a Crest address appears in BC's Assisted Living Registry results for Burnaby — the registered Burnaby residences (including Nikkei Home, Rosewood by Nikoniko and Liberty Place) are on the Edmonds side or elsewhere in the city, not in this sub-area. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ABurnaby&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=asc

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. Derby Manor's 55+ rule is a rental operator's eligibility rule, not a strata bylaw — the Act matters here for the general-market strata a senior might buy instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Zoning bylaw — the NEW Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026.** The City states the new Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 was adopted June 9, 2026, replaced the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965, and took effect July 1, 2026. Do not cite the 1965 bylaw as current. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/bylaws/zoning-bylaw
- **R1 SSMUH zoning:** Burnaby consolidated its single- and two-family districts into the R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing district in June 2024 — 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; permitted forms include single-family homes, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. The Crest's housing stock is predominantly detached houses on these streets, so this — not a 55+ strata — is the in-area downsizing and multi-generational route. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP and plans: the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan** was adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP, and takes precedence over older community-plan maps where they conflict; the Edmonds Community Plan covers the town centre plan area west of The Crest (via the area research file). https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Property taxes:** Burnaby's 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date; the detached single-family water utility charge is $652.33, with a 5% discount if paid by the due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in The Crest?** None that any source verifies as an age-restricted strata. The sub-area is predominantly detached houses (14 detached listings against 6 condos & townhomes listings in September 2026), its low-rise strata stock carries no verified age restriction, and its one true 55+ building — Derby Manor — is rental. A buyer here is purchasing a regular house, townhome or condo with no age bylaw. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.)
- **Q: What is Derby Manor, and can I buy a unit there?** Derby Manor, 8601 16th Ave, is a 122-unit affordable non-profit RENTAL residence for individuals 55+, built in 2017 by the George Derby Care Society on its care campus. You hold a monthly Occupancy Agreement, not a lease or a title, and the set monthly fee includes a basic services package (meals, housekeeping, laundry, activities, 24/7 emergency response per the Province's project release). There is no strata title to buy. https://derbymanor.ca/ and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2015MNGD0084-002065
- **Q: Can a couple move into Derby Manor if only one of us is 55+?** The operator's own site describes the units as "for individuals 55+" and charges an additional $650/month for a second person in a suite, but no source checked states the Society's rule for a younger spouse or partner — that eligibility detail is UNCONFIRMED. Ask the Society directly before counting on it; the strata-law spouse exemption in Section 3 governs strata bylaws, not a non-profit rental's own admission rules. https://derbymanor.ca/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Burnaby?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — a BC age bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can a Crest house work as a multi-generational home instead of a 55+ purchase?** That is the realistic in-area route: Burnaby's R1 SSMUH district allows 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots and 6 near frequent transit, in forms including secondary suites, and multi-unit lots may be stratified and sold individually. Confirm lot area and the 400 m transit test for the specific lot. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: Where is the hospital and urgent care?** Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St, is the city's hospital; the Edmonds Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at #201-7315 Edmonds St, west of the sub-area. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** There is no SkyTrain station inside The Crest — Edmonds Station on the Expo Line, west of the sub-area, is the link — so a car-free senior here is bus-dependent for the last leg. Check the exact address's bus service before buying on a no-car plan. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in The Crest?** The campus at the sub-area's south edge is built for exactly that ladder: independent 55+ rental at Derby Manor (122 units), then long-term care next door at George Derby Centre (300 publicly subsidized beds, Fraser Health access). Assisted living sits on the Edmonds side of the city (Nikkei Home, Rosewood by Nikoniko, Liberty Place — see the Edmonds file). Every step is rental or publicly funded, none of it purchased. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2015MNGD0084-002065 and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/3/A/george-derby-centre

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The Crest's seniors stock exists because a veterans' care society built it.** The George Derby Care Society provided the land (valued at $3.5 million in the Province's release) for Derby Manor on its existing campus, where veterans' care has run since 1947/1948 and the current 300-bed centre opened in May 1988. That non-profit origin is why the 55+ stock here is application-based rental: it was never built to sell. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2015MNGD0084-002065 and https://georgederby.ca/newsite/about-us/
- **Derby Manor's tenancy is not a normal lease.** The operator's FAQ states there is no lease agreement — residents sign a monthly Occupancy Agreement; the fee cannot be unbundled from the basic services package; and a second occupant costs an additional $650/month. A senior comparing it against a market rental or a strata purchase is comparing three different legal arrangements, not three prices. https://derbymanor.ca/
- **One campus, two street addresses — and a directory trail that confused earlier research.** Derby Manor is 8601 16th Ave; the George Derby Centre is 7550 Cumberland St; the Province's release ties them as one campus of care site. Listings, bylaws and directories cite the campus by either frontage, which is how "Derby Manor" previously surfaced as an UNCONFIRMED duplicate in Edmonds research — the two addresses are the same site at the Crest / Edmonds meeting point. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2015MNGD0084-002065 and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/3/A/george-derby-centre
- **The operator markets the building from inside the sub-area's own mall.** Derby Manor's description invites visitors to "visit our Show Suite in the Crest Mall" — The Crest Centre plaza the sub-area is named for doubles as the 55+ rental's sales front, which is why the building is well known locally despite being rental-only. http://www.derbymanor.ca/
- **Read the September 2026 sub-area numbers with the small-sample warning attached:** 2 detached solds on 14 listings (14%) and 2 attached solds on 6 listings (33%) is two sales each way — price off a longer window and East Burnaby / Edmonds comparables, and against the Burnaby community medians (detached $1,825,000; condos & townhomes $686,000), not one month. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.)
- **Burnaby replaced its entire zoning bylaw in 2026.** The Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (adopted June 9, 2026, in force July 1, 2026) repealed and replaced the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 — documents or summaries that still cite the 1965 bylaw are out of date. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
