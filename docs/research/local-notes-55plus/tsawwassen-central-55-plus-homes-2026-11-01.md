# Tsawwassen Central, Delta — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Tsawwassen Central is a Paragon (FVREB MLS®) sub-area name, not an official City of Delta neighbourhood name; the City publishes no boundary for it. Its working centre is the junction of 56 Street and 12 Avenue, around the Tsawwassen Town Centre Mall site (site addresses 1219 56 Street, 5499 12 Avenue and 1236 55 Street). https://delta.civicweb.net/document/237772/
- The planning frame is the Tsawwassen community and the Tsawwassen Urban Centre under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), which consolidated the former area plans into a single Land Use Plan; the old Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- 56 Street is the main north–south street; 12 Avenue is the east–west street through the centre; 54A Street and 55 Street carry most of the central condo stock named in Section 2. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- South Delta Recreation Centre is at 1720 56 St, beside South Delta Exchange at 56 Street and 18 Avenue; Tsawwassen Library (Fraser Valley Regional Library) is at 1321A 56 St. http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/sport-recreation/indoor-facilities/south-delta-recreation-centre
- School District 37 (Delta) catchments are address-based; for the sample address 1299 56 St the SD37 School Locator result is Cliff Drive Elementary (K–7), 5025 12 Ave, and South Delta Secondary (8–12), 750 53 St, with no middle-school category. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates: this sub-area is the concentration point for all of Tsawwassen. The verified 55+ ownership buildings (The Lexington, Tsawwassen Greene), the KinVillage seniors campus and The Waterford retirement residence all sit within a few blocks of 54A Street, 55 Street, 56 Street and 10–12 Avenues — sources in Section 2. No verified 55+ ownership stock was found in English Bluff, Tsawwassen East or Tsawwassen North (see those files).

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- **The Lexington — 1150 54A Street (Tsawwassen Central).** Freehold strata, plan NWS2700, built 1988, 38 units, self-managed, at 54A Street and 11 Avenue. Building sources record it as an adult-oriented community with an age restriction of 55+, one pet allowed but no dogs, and rentals not permitted in the building's own bylaw summary; amenities listed are a workshop, storage locker and recreation room. Current MLS listings state the rule as "Age restriction 55+, one occupant must be 55+," with a community room, bike room and workshop. Read the rental line against Section 3: provincial law voided strata rental-restriction bylaws in 2022, so confirm the current position in the Form B and strata documents. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/the-lexington-1150-54a-street and https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/1150-54a-street/201
- **Tsawwassen Greene — 1140 55 Street (Tsawwassen Central).** Freehold strata condo, built 1991 (one building source lists 1990), 27 units in the building source. Current MLS listing text (MLS R3091035) states "Age restricted 55+," fees include secure underground parking and a storage locker, and one dog or cat is allowed to 12 inches at the shoulder. Listing-stated pet rule — confirm in the strata bylaws. https://www.realtor.ca/real-estate/29373447/204-1140-55-street-delta and https://sothebysrealty.ca/en/property/british-columbia/region-fraser-valley/delta-real-estate/2914521/1140-55-street-no204/

### Verified 55+ rental (not ownership)

- **The Tides at KinVillage — 1070 54A Street.** The first phase of the KinVillage campus redevelopment: 152 homes in total, made up of 106 subsidized homes for seniors with low incomes and 46 market rental suites for moderate incomes, all designed adaptable or accessible, with a 450-square-metre day program on the main floor; owned and operated by the KinVillage Association. Rental only — nothing to buy. https://canada.constructconnect.com/joc/news/projects/2025/11/seniors-find-new-homes-as-152-units-open-in-delta and https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/new-affordable-seniors-housing-breaks-ground-in-delta
- **KinVillage independent-living courts — campus around 54A Street and 10 Avenue.** KinVillage's own application page lists its independent-living buildings as North Court (86 units, 464–550 sq ft — the building the City's Phase 2 report records as demolished and replaced through The Tides and the Phase 2 site), South Court (11 units, 430–550 sq ft) and Vidal Court (30 units, primarily 578 sq ft, some to 813 sq ft; BC Housing's directory gives Vidal Court's address as 5492 11 Ave). KinVillage states it provides subsidized rental and market rental housing for independent older adults, that subsidized rent is 30% of household gross income through BC Housing, that eligibility includes being age 55 or older (or a person with a disability who can live independently), and that pets and smoking are not allowed in the buildings. Rental only. https://www.kinvillage.org/independent-living and https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

Publicly subsidized long-term care is accessed through a Fraser Health case manager — it is not a housing product for buyers. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/kinvillage and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/7/5/kinvillage-west-court

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why The Lexington's older "rentals not permitted" bylaw summary cannot be relied on without reading the current Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017** is Delta's zoning bylaw; seniors housing in Delta is typically delivered through site-specific comprehensive development zones — The Waterford's site is CD Zone No. 276 (seniors' congregate housing and congregate care), and the KinVillage Phase 2 application would rezone 1050 54A Street from CD289 to a new comprehensive development zone. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/71542/ and https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009921
- **"Seniors congregate housing" is a named use in Delta's newer apartment zones.** The Apartment Residential 6 (RA6) zone added to Bylaw No. 7600 lists apartment building and seniors congregate housing as principal uses, for six-storey residential development along arterial and collector streets under the OCP's Neighbourhood Centres and Corridors designation. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future")** is Delta's current OCP; the KinVillage Phase 2 page confirms that proposal's consistency with the OCP's Neighbourhood Centre and Corridors (NC) designation, which is intended for multi-unit residential and mixed uses in neighbourhood centres and along major corridors. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009921 and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing:** Delta's SSMUH rules allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide, subject to the bylaw and to any hazard or streamside permit area on the lot — relevant to a 55+ buyer adding a suite or coach house on a central detached lot. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Property taxes:** Delta's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026; the City's tax page links the provincial Home Owner Grant and Property Tax Deferment programs. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Tax relief that matters at 55+:** the Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
- **Subsidized seniors rental runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** (Zone 3 — Richmond and West Delta directory, May 2025), which is how the KinVillage subsidized units are filled. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where is the actual buyable 55+ stock in Tsawwassen?** Here, in Central. The two verified age-restricted strata ownership buildings in all of Tsawwassen are The Lexington (1150 54A St, 38 units, built 1988) and Tsawwassen Greene (1140 55 St, 27 units, built 1991); no verified 55+ ownership building was found in English Bluff, Tsawwassen East or Tsawwassen North. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/the-lexington-1150-54a-street and https://www.realtor.ca/real-estate/29373447/204-1140-55-street-delta
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Listings for both Central buildings phrase the bylaw as one occupant needing to be 55+. Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/1150-54a-street/201
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in The Lexington or Tsawwassen Greene as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, even where an older building summary (like The Lexington's) still says rentals are not permitted. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Building by building. The Lexington: one pet allowed, no dogs, per the building source. Tsawwassen Greene: one dog or cat to 12 inches at the shoulder, per current listing text. KinVillage's rental buildings allow no pets at all. Confirm the current bylaw in the Form B before writing an offer around a pet. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/the-lexington-1150-54a-street and https://www.realtor.ca/real-estate/29373447/204-1140-55-street-delta and https://www.kinvillage.org/independent-living
- **Q: Is KinVillage something I can buy into?** No. Everything on the KinVillage campus is rental or care: subsidized and market rental apartments (The Tides, South Court, Vidal Court), 68 publicly subsidized assisted-living suites, and West Court's 100 publicly subsidized long-term care beds. https://www.kinvillage.org/independent-living and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/7/5/kinvillage-west-court
- **Q: How do I get into the subsidized KinVillage units?** Through BC Housing. KinVillage states subsidized rent is 30% of household gross income, eligibility includes being 55 or older and under its published income ceiling, and applications for subsidized housing go through BC Housing (604-433-2218 / 1-800-257-7756); the market-rental suites at The Tides are applied for separately, with KinVillage advertising affordable market rents starting from $1,700 for a one-bedroom (operator-stated figure — confirm current rents with KinVillage). https://www.kinvillage.org/independent-living
- **Q: Where is the hospital and urgent care?** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd in Ladner, is the nearest hospital (Fraser Health). In Tsawwassen itself, Fraser Health lists a South Delta After-Hours Urgent and Primary Care Centre at 1826–4949 Canoe Pass Way. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/7/8/Service---South-Delta-After-Hours-Urgent-and-Primary-Care-Centre
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** South Delta Exchange, at 56 Street and 18 Avenue beside South Delta Recreation Centre, is the hub: route 601 runs to Boundary Bay and Bridgeport Station, route 602 to Tsawwassen Heights and Bridgeport Station, and the 609 links the exchange to Ladner Exchange via 52 Street and Canoe Pass Way. There is no SkyTrain in South Delta — Bridgeport Station on the Canada Line is the rail connection. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt602.pdf
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. That trade-off is built into every BC 55+ strata since the 2022 law change confined age bylaws to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The two 55+ ownership buildings sit one block apart, and the building between them is not 55+.** The Lexington (1150 54A St) and Heron Place (1153 54A St) face each other on the same street; only The Lexington carries the 55+ bylaw, while Tsawwassen Greene (1140 55 St) is around the corner. Buyers routinely assume the whole 54A/55 Street cluster is age-restricted — it is not. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/the-lexington-1150-54a-street and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/heron-place-1153-54-a-street
- **KinVillage is mid-rebuild, in phases.** The Tides (152 homes) replaced the 86-home North Court building, whose residents were offered relocation into the new building; Phase 2 at 1050 54A Street (143 more affordable seniors rental units plus a replacement community centre) is in the rezoning process as City File LU009921. Expect construction activity on the campus for years. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/new-affordable-seniors-housing-breaks-ground-in-delta and https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009921
- **The town centre itself is in a redevelopment process.** The Tsawwassen Town Centre Mall site (Thrifty Foods site; 1219 56 Street, 5499 12 Avenue, 1236 55 Street) is the subject of a Century Group redevelopment application, City File LU009754 — the commercial heart of the sub-area will change around the 55+ buildings. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/237772/
- **The Waterford's zoning tells you why it exists.** Its site-specific CD Zone No. 276 permits seniors' congregate housing and a seniors' congregate care facility — the 106 independent suites and 36 licensed care suites are a purpose-built continuum on one town-centre site, not a converted condo building. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/71542/ and https://briacommunities.ca/communities/waterford/
- **A north-side address can carry a Ladner school catchment.** SD37 catchments are address-based and do not follow the marketing sub-areas — a Canoe Pass Way sample address in Tsawwassen North returned Ladner Elementary and Delta Secondary. In Central, always run the exact address in the SD37 locator rather than assuming Cliff Drive / South Delta Secondary. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Delta runs three seniors recreation facilities, and the Tsawwassen one is on this campus.** The City lists the KinVillage Community Centre (5430 10th Ave) among its three seniors recreation facilities, operated by societies with volunteer-delivered programs; KinVillage residents receive a free membership. https://www.delta.ca/city-hall/working-delta/volunteering/kinvillage-community-centre and https://www.kinvillage.org/independent-living
- **Flood protection is a city-wide system, not a neighbourhood feature.** The City of Delta is the diking authority and operates and maintains over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure — lot-level flood construction requirements still have to be checked parcel by parcel. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
