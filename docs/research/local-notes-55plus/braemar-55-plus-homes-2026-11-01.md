# Braemar (District of North Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

Note on the header: Braemar is in the District of North Vancouver, not West Vancouver — it is one of the neighbourhoods in the District's North Lonsdale–Delbrook planning area, alongside Delbrook, Norwood/Queens, Upper Delbrook and Carisbrooke. It is filed with the West Vancouver batch because that is how the assignment grouped it, but every municipal fact below is District of North Vancouver. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/Council_Reports/418109.pdf

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Braemar sits around Mahon Avenue and Delbrook Avenue, between West Queens Road in the south and the Princess Park side in the east. Exact official boundaries are UNVERIFIED — the District's North Lonsdale–Delbrook Official Community Plan, Bylaw 6750, covers the planning area under the District OCP, Bylaw 7900. Nearby areas: Delbrook (west and south), Carisbrooke (east), Upper Delbrook (north-west), Upper Lonsdale (east, uphill) and Central Lonsdale (south). https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/Council_Reports/418109.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt249.pdf
- Housing form: detached houses on the District's detached residential zones (R1/R2 under Zoning Bylaw 3210, Section 525). No named condo building, townhome complex or seniors facility inside Braemar is confirmed on an official or developer source — see Section 2. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- The area's anchor institution is a school, not housing: École Braemar Elementary, 3600 Mahon Avenue, offering English, Early French Immersion and Late French Immersion. For the school's own address range, the SD44 locator returns École Braemar Elementary (K–7) and Carson Graham Secondary (8–12), 2145 Jones Avenue; other Braemar addresses are address-specific. https://braemar.sd44.ca/ and https://www.sd44.ca/registration/french-immersion/
- Parks: Princess Park, at the 3500-block of Princess Avenue on the area's east side, offers tennis courts, playgrounds, a picnic shelter and forested walking trails forming part of the Lynn Valley Link. The Mosquito Creek and MacKay Creek ravines west of the area are protected through the plan's natural-environment development permit areas. https://www.nvrc.ca/facilities-fields/locations-hours/community-recreation-centres/princess-park and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- Recreation: the Delbrook Community Recreation Centre, 851 West Queens Road, south of the area, is the nearest NVRC full-service centre with main and leisure pools and indoor racquet facilities. https://www.nvrc.ca/facilities-fields/locations-hours/courts
- Transit: TransLink route 249 (Delbrook / Lonsdale Quay) runs via Delbrook Avenue and West Queens Road through the area to Lonsdale Quay, where the SeaBus crosses to Waterfront Station in 12 minutes, every 15 minutes during the day; route 241 runs via Lonsdale Avenue and the Lions Gate Bridge to downtown Vancouver. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt249.pdf and https://www.translink.ca/translink/schedules-and-maps/seabus
- Health care: Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver, is the Vancouver Coastal Health hospital serving the North Shore; the North Vancouver Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 221 West Esplanade, North Vancouver. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/north-vancouver-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
- Where the seniors housing actually is, relative to Braemar: down the hill and west — the 55+ ownership building is Queensbrook on West Queens Road in Delbrook, the new affordable rental is Hollyburn Delbrook at 600 West Queens Road, and the Kiwanis seniors rentals are at Whiteley Court in the Capilano area. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**No verified 55+ strata, 55+ rental, retirement residence, care home or subsidized seniors building exists inside Braemar in any source checked.** The pocket is detached houses. What follows is the nearest North Shore stock, clearly labelled as outside the sub-area.

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- None inside Braemar.
- **Nearest, outside the sub-area — Queensbrook, 678 West Queens Road (Delbrook, same North Lonsdale–Delbrook planning area).** The nearest verified age-restricted strata ownership building to Braemar. Built 1997, 3 storeys, listed in building data under the Delbrook sub-area; listings describe a boutique 22-unit building and a "55+ community." Building sources record age restrictions, pets allowed, and rentals allowed; listings record cats and dogs OK with a two-pet limit, secured parking, storage, a clubhouse, workshop, exercise centre and bike room. Confirm the registered bylaw wording in the strata documents — the 55+ label here rests on listings plus the building-data age-restriction flag. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/queensbrook-678-west-queens-road and https://www.zolo.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate/678-west-queens-road/207

- **Nearest Kiwanis seniors rentals, outside the sub-area — Whiteley Court, Capilano area, District of North Vancouver.** Kiwanis North Shore Housing Society provides independent living apartments at below-market rates for adults 55+ on the North Shore; its District of North Vancouver buildings are Lynn Manor, 2555 Whiteley Court (applicants must be 65 or older), and Lynn Woods, 2551 Whiteley Court (60 or older for its rent-geared-to-income deep-subsidy units, per the society's application material). ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. (Queensbrook's building data already records rentals allowed, consistent with this.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **District zoning: Zoning Bylaw 3210, Section 525 (Bylaw 8799).** The R1 Rural and Suburban Detached Residential Zone has minimums of 4,000 m² / 30 m width / 34 m depth; the R2 Urban Detached Residential Zone has minimums of 660 m² / 18 m / 34 m, with modifiers R2(S1) at 335 m² / 10 m, R2(S2) at 1,100 m² / 24 m and R2(S3) at 4,000 m² / 30 m. R2 allows a principal dwelling plus a secondary suite in the principal building and a coach house — the stay-in-place alternative to a 55+ building. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Three units on single-family lots:** since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary; the District's SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf
- **Plans:** the North Lonsdale–Delbrook Official Community Plan, Bylaw 6750, is the local plan, under the District of North Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900. The planning area's one purpose-built seniors-adjacent project in the sources is the Delbrook Lands redevelopment at 600 West Queens Road (Bylaws 8397–8399, 2019; built as Hollyburn Delbrook rental housing). https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/Council_Reports/418109.pdf and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024HOUS0030-000268/
- **Hazards:** District OCP Schedule B establishes Development Permit Areas for Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard — lots near the Mosquito Creek and MacKay Creek ravines west of Braemar warrant a GeoWeb property check before pricing a reno or suite addition. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **Taxes and utilities:** District of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value, and combined single-family utilities are $2,571.20 in 2026 (water $932.80, sewer/drainage $1,255.60). https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Braemar?** None inside the pocket — no verified 55+ strata exists in Braemar; the housing is detached freehold houses. The nearest verified 55+ ownership building is Queensbrook, 678 West Queens Road in Delbrook — 22 units, built 1997, in the same North Lonsdale–Delbrook planning area. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/queensbrook-678-west-queens-road
- **Q: Is Queensbrook actually 55+, or just "adult-oriented" marketing?** Building data records an age restriction for the strata, and current listings describe it as a "55+ community." Under BC law since Nov 24, 2022, the only age bylaw a strata can hold is 55+ — but confirm the registered bylaw and its exact wording in the strata documents before relying on the listing label. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/queensbrook-678-west-queens-road and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live at Queensbrook?** Yes, if the building's 55+ bylaw follows the provincial model — the regulation requires an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a Queensbrook unit for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit at Queensbrook?** Building data records rentals allowed, and in any case long-term strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022. The tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/queensbrook-678-west-queens-road and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Queensbrook?** Yes — building and listing sources record pets allowed; a current listing records cats and dogs OK, number limit two, with restrictions. Confirm size/other limits in the bylaws. https://www.zolo.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate/678-west-queens-road/206
- **Q: Is there any new seniors housing coming near Braemar?** The Delbrook Lands project at 600 West Queens Road is the one: approved in 2019 as rental housing plus a seniors' care facility, and funded/announced in 2024 as Hollyburn Delbrook — 86 affordable rental homes for independent seniors, individuals and families, run by Hollyburn Community Housing Society. It is not 55+-only, and the care-facility component's status is UNCONFIRMED. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024HOUS0030-000268/ and https://app.dnv.org/OpenDocument/Default.aspx?docNum=4100529
- **Q: If I stay in my Braemar house, can I add a suite or coach house?** Yes — the R2 zone allows a principal dwelling plus a secondary suite and a coach house, and since June 2024 three units total are permitted on single-family lots within the Urban Containment Boundary, subject to the hazard development permit areas near the creek ravines. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver — the Vancouver Coastal Health hospital for the North Shore, south of Braemar via Lonsdale Avenue. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital
- **Q: What transit serves Braemar for a senior giving up driving?** Route 249 runs on Delbrook Avenue and West Queens Road to Lonsdale Quay and the SeaBus (12 minutes to Waterfront Station); route 241 runs via Lonsdale Avenue over the Lions Gate Bridge downtown. The Delbrook Community Recreation Centre at 851 West Queens Road is on that corridor. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt249.pdf and https://www.translink.ca/translink/schedules-and-maps/seabus

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Braemar is a North Vancouver pocket filed in buyers' minds as one Delbrook market:** the District plans it inside the North Lonsdale–Delbrook area (Bylaw 6750) with Delbrook, Norwood/Queens, Upper Delbrook and Carisbrooke, and the area's only 55+ building, Queensbrook, is actually addressed in Delbrook on West Queens Road. A "Braemar 55+" search that stays inside the pocket returns nothing — the stock is one street-corridor over. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/Council_Reports/418109.pdf and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/queensbrook-678-west-queens-road
- **The planning paper trail promised more than got built:** the 600 West Queens Road site was described in its 2019 rezoning as rental housing *and a seniors' care facility*, but the funded 2024 project is an 86-home affordable rental building with no care component described. Buyers told "a care home is coming to Delbrook" are working from the older document. https://app.dnv.org/OpenDocument/Default.aspx?docNum=4100529 and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024HOUS0030-000268/
- **Somerset Green is the trap across the municipal line:** the 143-unit seniors' condominium a District report cites sits in the *City* of North Vancouver, south of West 29th Street. It shows up in "North Vancouver 55+" lists and in District planning documents, but it is not a Braemar or Delbrook building. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/council_reports/512271.pdf
- **Queensbrook is genuinely small:** 22 units, built 1997, backing onto Belhouse Park per listings, a short walk to the Delbrook Community Rec Centre and the Mosquito Creek Trail — the scale is why Braemar-area downsizers wait years between listings there. (Listing-stated amenities; verify on the Form B.) https://www.zolo.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate/678-west-queens-road/206
- **The Kiwanis age ladder is not uniform:** the society's West Vancouver/Vancouver-side buildings take 55+, but the District of North Vancouver buildings nearest Braemar — Lynn Manor on Whiteley Court — require 65+, and Lynn Woods' deep-subsidy units require 60+. A 58-year-old Braemar renter does not qualify for the nearest Kiwanis building. https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Housing-Application-Form-March-2025.pdf
- **French Immersion is the pocket's real amenity story, and it cuts both ways for seniors:** École Braemar's English/Early/Late French Immersion programs draw family buyers to these streets, which supports resale of a detached house — but it also means the surrounding stock stays family housing, with no seniors product built inside the pocket. https://braemar.sd44.ca/ and https://www.sd44.ca/registration/french-immersion/
- **District context:** the District of North Vancouver's 2021 Census population was 88,168 in 32,700 occupied dwellings — a large municipality in which this planning area's entire verified 55+ ownership supply is one 22-unit building from 1997. https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/dp-pd/prof/index.cfm?Lang=E and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/queensbrook-678-west-queens-road
