## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. A map filter that still offers "40+" or "45+" strata categories is offering a bylaw class that no longer has effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **The ALR layer the page tells readers to turn on matters, at set numbers:** a parcel's Agricultural Land Reserve status is checked on the Agricultural Land Commission's maps by PID or civic address, and housing on ALR land is capped at one principal residence of 500 m² or less plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (larger parcels), where local bylaws also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **What each municipality's own GIS map shows — parcel, zoning, OCP, floodplain and overlay layers, and the exact setback or easement figures for any lot: UNVERIFIED in this program at municipal level.** The page's GIS section is general site copy covering "most municipal GIS maps," and no individual municipality's GIS was opened and verified layer-by-layer for this file. https://jaredgibbons.ca/map-search.html and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/map-search.md
- **Transfer tax, a figure a map-search user is usually budgeting around:** the Province's general property transfer tax rates are 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion greater than $200,000 and up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% of the portion greater than $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000, based on fair market value at registration unless an exemption applies. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Who a listing's agent works for, for a user who finds a home through a search and contacts its brokerage:** real estate trading services in BC are regulated by BCFSA, which states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Feed freshness for the map (how often the underlying listing data updates): UNVERIFIED** — no update interval or timestamp states it on the page text read for this file. https://jaredgibbons.ca/map-search.html
