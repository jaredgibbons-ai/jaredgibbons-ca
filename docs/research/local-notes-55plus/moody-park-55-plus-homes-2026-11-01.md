# Moody Park (New Westminster) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Moody Park is the Uptown New Westminster neighbourhood centred on the park of the same name. The working bounds used in the area research are roughly Sixth Avenue to Eighth Avenue and Eighth Street to Tenth Street — the City publishes no single official street-by-street boundary for it, and MLS board data draws its own line through the Eighth Street / Eighth Avenue corner (see Section 2), so precise bounds are UNVERIFIED. http://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/parks/community-parks
- The park block carries the civic anchors: Moody Park Arena at 701 Eighth Avenue, the outdoor pool and spray park in the park, and — at 620 Eighth Street, at the park's east edge — Century House, the City's seniors centre (not housing; see Section 2). https://www.newwestcity.ca/facilities and https://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/facilities/century-house/1000
- Just north of the park edge: the təməsew̓txʷ Aquatic and Community Centre at 65 East Sixth Avenue, opened May 2024, and the New Westminster Public Library's Main Branch at 716 Sixth Avenue. https://www.newwestcity.ca/facilities and https://www.nwpl.ca/
- Schools: for the sample address 1714 Eighth Avenue, the district pattern is École Lord Tweedsmuir Elementary (Early French Immersion), Fraser River Middle School and New Westminster Secondary. The replacement New Westminster Secondary at 820 Sixth Street opened January 11, 2021 and is reported above capacity — family demand around the park is a real pricing factor for houses here. School District 40 catchments are address-based. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/ and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2021EDUC0003-000034.htm
- Health: Royal Columbian Hospital, 330 East Columbia Street in Sapperton, is the city's Fraser Health regional referral and teaching hospital; its 388-bed Jim Pattison Acute Care Tower opened to patients May 31, 2026, with Phase 3 upgrades expected to complete in 2029. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/New-Westminster/royal-columbian-hospital and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/accessing-health-care/capital-projects
- The strata stock around the park is small, older low-rise buildings on Eighth Street and Eighth Avenue — The Diplomat (707 Eighth St), Moody Gardens (777 Eighth St), The Doncaster (620 Eighth Ave) and Fraser Gardens (1169 Eighth Ave) — and none of them is age-restricted (see Section 2). https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/the-diplomat-707-eighth-street and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/fraser-gardens-1169-eighth-avenue

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**Moody Park has NO verified 55+ strata ownership — no complex in the sub-area carries a verified age restriction.** What the area does have is seniors RENTAL and CARE stock: a registered retirement residence at its edge (Thornebridge Gardens) and a subsidized seniors tower (Lions Moody Park Tower), plus the city's seniors centre. A 55+ buyer looking to purchase will not find an age-restricted strata here.

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- None. The four named strata around the park were each checked — no age restriction appears for any of them in any source checked (see the Checked list).

### Verified 55+ rental (not ownership)

- None as a market-rental 55+ building. The area's seniors rental is the subsidized tower below.

### Retirement residences and care (rental/care — NOT strata ownership)

- **Thornebridge Gardens Retirement Residence — 649 8th Avenue.** A Verve Senior Living retirement residence offering Independent Living, Independent Supportive Living, Assisted Living and respite stays; pet-friendly, with concierge and 24/7 emergency response, a fitness centre, restaurant and bar/bistro. It is registered in BC's Assisted Living Registry (operator Thornebridge Gardens Holdings Ltd., class SN — Seniors and Persons with Disabilities). ComfortLife records it as opened 2007 under private ownership and describes the location as the "heart of Uptown New Westminster." Rental and care only — you cannot buy in. PLACEMENT CONFLICT, flagged: the building sits at the Moody Park / Uptown edge, and board data draws the line through its own block — a listing for a unit at 620 Eighth Avenue (The Doncaster, same block) is classed "Uptown NW," a Diplomat listing describes 707 Eighth Street as being in "the Uptown NW neighbourhood" with Moody Park across the street, and Moody Gardens listings likewise read "Uptown NW," while a Fraser Gardens listing at 1169 Eighth Avenue is classed "Moody Park." Thornebridge Gardens' own board sub-area is therefore UNCONFIRMED; it is filed here as the Moody Park-area retirement residence with the conflict stated, and it is not counted as in-area ownership under any reading. https://www.verveseniorliving.com/thornebridge-gardens/ and https://www.comfortlife.ca/retirement-homes/thornebridge-gardens-new-westminster/394 and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANew%20Westminster&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=asc and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r2946177-209707-eighth-street-new-westminster-bc-v3m-3s6

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. (The Diplomat's recorded position — rentals allowed — is consistent with this.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001.** Moody Park houses sit in the Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series, s.310) — RS-1 minimum site area 557.40 m² (6,000 sq ft), floor space ratio 0.5 — and the RS districts allow secondary suites, with laneway / carriage houses regulated under the same bylaw. The park-edge low-rise strata sit in their own multi-family districts under the same bylaw. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Plan: Our City 2041, the Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 7925, 2017**, covers Uptown and Moody Park. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- **Heritage:** parts of Uptown carry heritage protections and the area includes listed historic places (Century House, 1958, is on the Canadian Register of Historic Places) — a buyer planning exterior work on an older Moody Park house should check the City's heritage register for the specific property. https://www.historicplaces.ca/en/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=16771 and https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage/heritage-research-and-resources/sb_expander_articles/683.php
- **Flood:** the City's floodplain study zones cover Queensborough (Zone 1), the west-end Fraser industrial lands (Zone 2), the Quay waterfront (Zone 3) and the Brunette River industrial lands (Zone 4) — the Moody Park residential bench is not among the four zones, but floodplain status remains a parcel-level check with the City. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- **City taxes:** New Westminster 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applying July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 3, 2026 on unpaid current taxes. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/whats-happening/index/main/175/site_post/page10.php
- **Citywide non-market context:** the City reports 1,257 units of non-market housing in 2023, including 60 supportive-seniors units and 599 independent-seniors units — the stock that Lions Moody Park Tower and its peers belong to, all of it rental. https://www.ubcm.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/New Westminster.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a 55+ condo in Moody Park?** No — no strata in the sub-area carries a verified age restriction. The Diplomat, Moody Gardens, The Doncaster and Fraser Gardens were each checked and none is 55+. What Moody Park offers a 55+ buyer is an ordinary condo or house purchase in a park-side location — with no age bylaw attached. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/the-diplomat-707-eighth-street
- **Q: What is Thornebridge Gardens — can I buy a suite there?** No. It is a Verve Senior Living retirement RESIDENCE at 649 Eighth Avenue — independent living through assisted living and respite, rented with services, and registered as an assisted-living residence with the Province. Nothing there is strata ownership. https://www.verveseniorliving.com/thornebridge-gardens/ and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANew%20Westminster&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=asc
- **Q: Is Thornebridge Gardens actually in Moody Park?** Its board sub-area is UNCONFIRMED. It stands at 649 Eighth Avenue at the park's edge; the operator's directory describes it as "heart of Uptown New Westminster," and listing data classes its own block (620 Eighth Ave) as Uptown NW while classing 1169 Eighth Avenue as Moody Park. Say "Moody Park / Uptown edge" and you are telling the truth either way. https://www.comfortlife.ca/retirement-homes/thornebridge-gardens-new-westminster/394
- **Q: What is Lions Moody Park Tower, and how does someone get in?** An 80-unit non-profit seniors rental tower at 525 Eighth Street, listed by the City as Senior Citizens housing. Admission is by application with a waitlist — a resident oral history describes applying repeatedly before getting a unit. It is not sold and not market rental. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/2013_Information_Bulletin.pdf and https://blogs.ubc.ca/storiesnotatoms/2015/03/19/margo-p/
- **Q: Is Century House a housing option? My parents keep mentioning it.** No — Century House (620 Eighth St) is the City's seniors CENTRE: programs and membership for adults 50+ ($25/year on the current City page), in a 1958 building that is itself a registered historic place. It is a reason to live near Moody Park, not a place to live. https://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/facilities/century-house/1000
- **Q: Are pets and rentals allowed in the park-side condos?** It varies by building and none of it is age-related: The Diplomat's recorded bylaws say pets not allowed, rentals allowed — and since Nov 24, 2022 no BC strata can enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw anyway, though short-term rentals can still be restricted. Always confirm in the current bylaws and Form B. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/the-diplomat-707-eighth-street and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: If I buy into a genuine 55+ strata elsewhere, can my younger spouse live with me?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can a Moody Park strata vote itself 55+ later?** Legally yes — 55+ is the only age threshold allowed since Nov 24, 2022, adopted by ¾ vote, with legacy protection for people already lawfully living there. But none of the park-side stratas has done so in any source checked, and a possible future bylaw is not a reason to pay a 55+ premium today. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Is Moody Park in the floodplain?** The City flood study's four zones are Queensborough, the west-end industrial lands, the Quay waterfront and the Brunette industrial lands — the Moody Park bench is not one of them. Confirm the individual parcel with the City before purchase. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- **Q: What changed nearby that affects daily life here?** The təməsew̓txʷ Aquatic and Community Centre (65 E Sixth Ave) opened in May 2024 just north of the park, and New Westminster Secondary (820 Sixth St, opened Jan 11, 2021) is above capacity — the area's amenity base is new and its schools are full, which supports both livability and resale demand for houses. https://www.newwestcity.ca/facilities and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2021EDUC0003-000034.htm

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Three different "seniors" buildings within two blocks, none of them for sale:** Thornebridge Gardens (rental retirement residence with care), Lions Moody Park Tower (subsidized rental, application and waitlist) and Century House (a seniors centre, not housing at all). Moody Park looks like a 55+ hub on a map search; every one of its seniors buildings is rental, care or not-housing. https://www.verveseniorliving.com/thornebridge-gardens/ and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/2013_Information_Bulletin.pdf and https://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/facilities/century-house/1000
- **The board's Moody Park/Uptown line runs through one intersection:** listings class The Diplomat (707 Eighth St) and Moody Gardens (777 Eighth St) as "Uptown NW" with the park "directly across the street," The Doncaster (620 Eighth Ave) as Uptown NW, and Fraser Gardens (1169 Eighth Ave) as Moody Park. A search filtered to either name will miss buildings on the same corner — search both, and judge by the street address. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r2946177-209707-eighth-street-new-westminster-bc-v3m-3s6 and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r2958907-1169-eighth-avenue-106-new-westminster-bc-v3m-2r7/brochure
- **Even the small building's basic numbers conflict:** Fraser Gardens is 17 units in strata data and 18 in bccondosandhomes, built 1982 in listing data and 1983 in bccondosandhomes. On 1980s low-rise stock, the depreciation report and the Form B settle questions that listing databases cannot. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/fraser-gardens-1169-eighth-avenue
- **The seniors centre predates the suburbs around it:** Century House was built in 1958 and is described in City planning material as one of the first seniors centres of its type in Canada — the park's seniors infrastructure is institutional and long-established, which is part of why the area attracts older buyers even without a single 55+ strata. https://www.historicplaces.ca/en/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=16771
- **The subsidized tower's paper trail is thin:** Lions Moody Park Tower's 80 units and seniors use are City-verified (2013 Information Bulletin), but the operator's name comes through garbled in that document and the civic address rests on a places listing — anyone publishing details about this building should re-verify them with the City or BC Housing first. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/2013_Information_Bulletin.pdf
- **Dunwood Place's unit count depends on which directory you open:** 193 units in the City's 2009 inventory vs 191 capacity in the 2026 Senior Living Magazine directory — a standing example, one neighbourhood over in Glenbrooke North, of why seniors-housing numbers need a primary source. https://newwestcity.ca/database/rte/Housing%20web%20update%20-%20Non-Market%20details.pdf and https://www.seniorlivingmag.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/SEP-OCT-2026-MAGAZINE_INSPIRED.reduced-size.pdf
