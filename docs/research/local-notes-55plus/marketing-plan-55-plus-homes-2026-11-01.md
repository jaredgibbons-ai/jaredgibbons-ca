## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**This page has no area and no complexes. There is no verified 55+ ownership stock "on" the marketing-plan page, and none is invented for it. What a 55+ seller brings to this page is a home inside a complex verified in one of this program's area files. The entries below are the legal FORMS that home can take — each illustrated by one complex already verified in a sibling file — because the form, not the marketing, decides how the sale works: who may buy, what documents govern, and how the resale pool is restricted.**

### The forms a 55+ seller may be selling out of

- **Strata (ownership) — the standard form.** The seller owns a strata lot; the age rule is the strata's registered 55+ bylaw, proved by the Form B and the registered bylaws, not by listing copy. Example verified in this program: Chelsea Gardens, 13870 / 13888 70 Ave, Surrey (East Newton) — freehold strata, plan LMS1416, gated, MLS building data records "Restricted Age: 55+." https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/201-13870-70-ave-60859 and ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/east-newton-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **Membership (NOT strata).** The seller holds a membership interest, not a strata lot. Example: Sullivan Mews, 15020 66A Ave, Surrey — its own listings state "This is not Strata. It is a membership therefore not mortgages possible," with a 55+ age restriction, no smoking, 1 small pet and a maximum of 2 occupants. A buyer who cannot use a conventional mortgage is a marketing fact the seller has to price around, not a detail to discover at subject removal. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3058951-15020-66a-avenue-49-surrey-bc-v3s-2a5/brochure
- **Manufactured home on a rented pad (own the home, not the land).** The seller owns the home and assigns a pad tenancy under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act; the age rule runs through park rules and park approval, not a strata bylaw. Example: Westwood Estates, 134 Street, Surrey — listings describe a "55+ age restricted" gated park; current listings disagree on the civic address (8224 vs 8254 134 Street) and on pad rent, both flagged in the Surrey files. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3121759-103-8224-134-street-surrey-british-columbia-v3w4v1-249800/brochure and ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/surrey-condos-under-800k-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **Life lease and co-operative (buy-in interests — NOT strata ownership, NOT fee simple).** A life-lease seller transfers an occupancy interest under the lease agreement (example: Cedar Valley Manor, 8358 Cedar St, Mission, where the operator states the resident does not own the unit or the building and the entrance fee is returned at termination less a set percentage). A co-op member transfers shares/membership under the co-op's own rules (example: St. Andrew's Place, 7365 Cedar St, Mission, a 55+ co-op). Neither is marketed or documented like a strata resale. https://www.missionseniors.ca/our-communities/cedar-valley-manor/ and https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/ and ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/mission-homes-for-sale-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md

- **Any home marketed as "55+" on listing language alone.** This program repeatedly found the label failing in both directions: complexes whose own listings state "no age restrictions" in terms (Highgrove, 15055 20 Ave, Surrey; Heritage Park Place, 33751 7th Ave, Mission), and directories still showing rental bans that have been void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. A seller whose building was sold to them as "40+" or "45+" no longer has an enforceable age restriction to market — and a seller in a true 55+ building has the only age restriction BC law still allows. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. For a seller, this widens the buyer pool beyond resident-buyers: an under-55 buyer may purchase for a qualifying parent, or to hold, without residing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. These exemptions are part of the resale pool a seller is actually selling into: a couple where only one partner is 55+ is not excluded. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. A seller can therefore market a 55+ unit to an investor-buyer for a long-term tenancy (the tenant must still meet the age bylaw or an exemption), even where the strata's own documents still print a rental ban. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **How the age rule is proved in a sale:** the governing document is the strata's registered bylaw set, read with the Form B Information Certificate — not the seller's memory, the previous listing, or a building directory. This program's files found listing and directory text that was stale or conflicting (build years, tenure, addresses, pad rents) in complex after complex; each conflict is flagged in the relevant sibling file rather than smoothed over. Sources: the sibling files in ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/
- **Non-strata forms run on different law, and the sale documents change with the form.** Manufactured-home parks: the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act — the seller assigns the pad tenancy, and park approval and park rules govern the buyer. Membership and life-lease interests: the organisation's own membership / lease documents — the provincial strata exemptions do not automatically apply, and Sullivan Mews' listings state conventional mortgages are not possible on a membership purchase. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3058951-15020-66a-avenue-49-surrey-bc-v3s-2a5/brochure and https://www.missionseniors.ca/our-communities/cedar-valley-manor/
- **Representation and fee disclosure at the listing appointment:** BCFSA requires a licensee to give consumers information about commissions and fees, to act for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction (not both), and to disclose duties owed to clients and non-clients at the outset of dealings; dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances. The marketing-plan page states no fee — so the fee discussion is an appointment item, not a page answer. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **What the page does NOT promise, stated so a seller does not over-read it:** no package tiers, no timeline or launch calendar, no statement of who pays for photography, staging or advertising, no video/drone/floor-plan/staging/print components named, no open-house practice, and no performance reporting are stated anywhere in the page text. All are UNVERIFIED from the page and are listing-appointment questions. https://jaredgibbons.ca/marketing-plan.html
- **Tax relief a 55+ seller may already be using — and must settle on sale:** the Province's property tax deferment regular program is for people 55 or older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities); a deferment loan is a charge against the property that has to be accounted for when the home sells. The additional home owner grant (for seniors 65+) is a separate, principal-residence item. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: I am selling a 55+ home, not buying one. Why does the buyer pool shrink?** Because the age bylaw travels with the unit: the next occupant must be 55+, fit an exemption (younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy resident, or a child in the defined care cases), or buy without living in the unit. That is the mechanical trade-off built into every BC 55+ strata since Nov 24, 2022 — pricing and exposure have to be set for that pool, not for the general market. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can someone under 55 buy my 55+ unit?** Yes, as an owner. The bylaw governs residing, not owning — the Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them. An under-55 child buying for a 55+ parent, and an investor buying to rent to a qualifying tenant, are both real buyer categories for a 55+ listing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: The buyers are a couple — he is 58, she is 49. Can they live in my unit?** Yes. Provincial rules exempt a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). The exemption is in the Regulation, not a favour the strata grants — but confirm the couple's position against the registered bylaws before subjects are removed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: My strata documents say rentals are not allowed. Does that kill investor interest?** No — for long-term rentals the document is stale law: every strata rental-restriction bylaw in BC became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and the strata can still restrict short-term rentals, but a long-term tenancy is not barred by the old bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What does the marketing plan actually include for my home?** On the page's own text: a pricing and presentation strategy aligned with your goals, starting from a home evaluation, then professional photography, strong online listing exposure, targeted digital and social promotion, and polished listing materials — inside a wider process of prep and staging guidance, launch timing, showing strategy, offer negotiation and follow-through to completion. Deliverables in detail, platforms, quantities and timelines are not stated on the page: UNVERIFIED — ask for the per-property list before signing. https://jaredgibbons.ca/marketing-plan.html
- **Q: What does it cost, and who pays for photography and promotion?** The page states no fee, commission, package price or cost allocation: UNVERIFIED from the page. BCFSA requires a BC licensee to give consumers information about commissions and fees, so that information is provided in the listing conversation. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers and https://jaredgibbons.ca/marketing-plan.html
- **Q: I live in a manufactured-home park / a membership complex / a life lease. Is selling the same process?** No. A park sale assigns a pad tenancy under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and runs through park rules and approval; a membership sale (Sullivan Mews) transfers a membership its listings state cannot be conventionally mortgaged; a life-lease sale (Cedar Valley Manor) transfers an occupancy interest whose exit value is set by the lease — the operator states the entrance fee is returned at termination less a set percentage. Each form has its own buyer pool, documents and timeline. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3058951-15020-66a-avenue-49-surrey-bc-v3s-2a5/brochure and https://www.missionseniors.ca/our-communities/cedar-valley-manor/
- **Q: I bought when the building was "45+". Can I still sell it as age-restricted?** Not as a strata age restriction: any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, and only a 55+ bylaw is enforceable. Marketing a sub-55 restriction that no longer exists misstates the product — check the registered bylaws and the Form B before the listing describes any age rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I have deferred my property taxes since turning 55. Does that affect my sale?** The deferment program's regular stream is for homeowners 55 or older, and a deferment is a provincial loan secured against the home — it has to be accounted for in the sale's closing adjustments rather than discovered by the buyer's lawyer. Tell your agent and your lawyer it is in place at the listing appointment. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility
- **Q: Is this plan only for South Surrey and Langley sellers?** The page frames it that way — title and copy address South Surrey and Langley. Whether the identical plan is offered in the other areas Jared serves is UNVERIFIED from this page; the page's own route to specifics is a call or text to 604-928-1361 to walk through a plan for a specific property. https://jaredgibbons.ca/marketing-plan.html

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The page promises an approach, not a deliverables list — and says so by omission.** No testimonial, sold example, result statistic or case study appears in the page's body copy as read; it argues from approach only. A seller comparing agents on this page is comparing a stated method (evaluation → pricing/presentation strategy → photography → online exposure → digital/social promotion → polished materials), and the specifics live in the listing conversation the page asks for. https://jaredgibbons.ca/marketing-plan.html
- **The single most valuable sentence in a 55+ listing is one the seller cannot write themselves.** Whether a building is truly 55+ is settled by its registered bylaws and Form B — and this program found the opposite error in both directions across the market: genuine 55+ complexes a city-wide file had missed, and prominently marketed complexes whose own listings state "no age restrictions." Verifying the bylaw before the marketing is written is the difference between exposure to the right pool and a listing that has to be corrected mid-campaign. - **Form decides the exit before marketing does.** A strata lot sells with a Form B; a park home sells with a tenancy assignment and park approval; a membership sells (per its own listings) for cash with no conventional mortgage; a life lease exits on the lease's own refund formula. The marketing plan that fits one form mis-prices and mis-promises on another — the form check is step one of any 55+ listing, ahead of photography. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3058951-15020-66a-avenue-49-surrey-bc-v3s-2a5/brochure and https://www.missionseniors.ca/our-communities/cedar-valley-manor/
