## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **The age rule a land buyer eventually meets is provincial strata law.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in a unit, not who may own it, and Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19. None of this attaches to a parcel until a strata exists. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** (since Nov 24, 2022) for any strata later created on purchased land; short-term rental restrictions remain allowed by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Housing in the Agricultural Land Reserve — the governing frame for much of the rural land on a feed like this:** land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless it qualifies for an additional residence; a principal residence built without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; and one additional detached residence is permitted without an application at 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, or 186 m² or less on larger parcels, where local bylaws also permit it. Local governments may regulate further but cannot be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Checking a parcel's reserve status:** a parcel's ALR status is checked on the Agricultural Land Commission's maps by PID or civic address. Whether any specific parcel in this page's feed is in the ALR: UNVERIFIED at parcel level in this program. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Subdividing reserve land:** subdivision of ALR land requires permission from the Agricultural Land Commission, apart from the limited cases in the Agricultural Land Reserve General Regulation where an approving officer may approve without the Commission. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/
- **Manufactured-home park siting and age rules:** pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and its Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003); the Regulation contemplates parks where every site is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 (or two or more tenants, at least one of whom has reached 55), and a park landlord may refuse an assignment or sublease where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement in such a park. Whether a specific parcel is zoned to permit a manufactured-home park at all is a municipal zoning question: UNVERIFIED at parcel level in this program. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Transfer tax on a land purchase:** the Province's general property transfer tax rates are 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion greater than $200,000 and up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% of the portion greater than $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000; the tax is based on fair market value (land and improvements) on the day it is registered at the Land Title Office, unless an exemption applies. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on a land purchase: UNVERIFIED for any specific parcel** — whether GST applies depends on the seller and the parcel's history; confirm with the buyer's legal professional before writing. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/land.md
- **Zoning and servicing for any specific feed parcel: UNVERIFIED at parcel level** — municipal water/sewer versus well/septic, development timing and development cost are parcel questions, and listing text is not a municipal confirmation. https://jaredgibbons.ca/land.html
- **Who the seller's agent works for:** real estate trading services in BC are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA), which states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party — relevant when a buyer contacts a land listing's brokerage directly. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is this page only Fraser Valley land?** No — that is the page's title, not its feed. The first cards read for this file include land in Quesnel and Fort St. John (both "Contact for address," no price displayed), Gibsons and Squamish, alongside Langley, Surrey and Mission parcels, and every card read is labelled "Land" and "Status: Active." https://jaredgibbons.ca/land.html
- **Q: Can I buy a lot and make it a "55+ home"?** Not by age-restricting the lot. A freehold lot and the house built on it carry no age bylaw — an enforceable 55+ restriction exists only inside a strata corporation's bylaws, in a manufactured-home park's rules, or in a co-operative/life-lease organization's own documents. A 55+ buyer of land is buying location and build rights, not an age-restricted community. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Q: How many homes can I build on ALR land for myself and family?** Generally one principal residence of 500 m² or less in total floor area (with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction), with a secondary suite permitted only inside that residence; one additional detached residence may also be permitted without an ALC application, capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on larger parcels, where local bylaws also permit it. More than that, or a larger residence, requires ALC approval. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: How do I check whether a lot is in the Agricultural Land Reserve?** On the Agricultural Land Commission's maps, by PID or civic address; subdivision of reserve land requires ALC permission apart from limited approving-officer cases in the ALR General Regulation. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/
- **Q: What property transfer tax applies on a land purchase?** The general rates are 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% of the portion over $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000, calculated on fair market value at registration unless an exemption applies. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Does GST apply to the lot I want?** UNVERIFIED for any specific parcel in this file — GST treatment depends on the seller and the parcel's history and must be confirmed with the buyer's legal professional before an offer is written. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/land.md
- **Q: What were land prices in September 2026?** UNVERIFIED as a market figure — SnapStats publishes no land category (its splits are Detached versus Condos & Townhomes only, for exactly 14 communities, medians at community level only), so no land median, band or share is stated in this file. The priced cards read on the live page ran from $299,900 to $2,500,000 — a feed snapshot at reading, not a statistic. https://jaredgibbons.ca/land.html (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report)
- **Q: Can I place a manufactured home on a lot and call it a 55+ park home?** A manufactured home on your own lot is a freehold purchase with no park age restriction; the 55+ park form exists only inside a park, where you own the home, rent the pad, and the park's age rule runs through its rules and assignment consent under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and Regulation. Whether a given lot's zoning permits a manufactured home at all is parcel-specific and UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/maple-ridge-mobile-homes-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: If I later sell, does buying land in the ALR limit my buyer pool?** The ALR frame travels with the parcel: the 500 m² principal-residence cap, the suite-inside-the-residence rule and the 90 m² / 186 m² additional-residence caps bind the next owner the same way, and subdivision needs ALC permission — so the resale pool is buyers who accept reserve rules, which is a parcel fact to price in, not a surprise to discover. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

- **The ALR suite rule surprises downsizers.** On reserve land a secondary suite is permitted only INSIDE the principal residence — the detached caregiver cottage many 55+ buyers picture is the "additional residence," capped at 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (larger parcels), and only where local bylaws also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Fill is capped too, not just floor area.** A principal residence built in the ALR without an ALC application allows no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction — a servicing and site-prep limit that affects sloped or low parcels before design work starts. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
