Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

The condos and townhomes this page covers are the strata homes around that node. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7910-0319-00.pdf
- Three names are in play and they are not interchangeable: "Grandview Corners" is the retail node; "Grandview Heights" is the City's planning name for the wider community; and the board sub-area is called "Grandview Surrey" in the listing feed. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/grandview-heights-general-land-use-plan and https://jaredgibbons.ca/grandview-corners-condos-townhomes-for-sale.html

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. In Grandview this matters in the negative: the complexes are strata, but no source states any of them has a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw — The Loop's developer material, for example, states rentals allowed with no short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation and https://storage.googleapis.com/precon-app.appspot.com/40b3001a-ef8b-402b-b8b3-506e6b28f0f2.pdf
- **City zoning — confirmed from surrey.ca: Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000.** City corporate reports and planning reports cite it in the form "Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000, as amended" — that is the zoning bylaw governing Grandview Heights strata lots. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r128-proposed-amendments-surrey-zoning-law-1993-no-12000
- **City plan — confirmed from surrey.ca: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 18020** (the OCP document is headed "BYLAW NO. 18020 | OCTOBER 20, 2014"), with the Grandview Heights General Land Use Plan as the area plan frame under it. Where a neighbourhood plan and the OCP conflict, the underlying research records the OCP as the current rule — at least one Grandview neighbourhood plan (Morgan Heights) has since been rescinded through the Surrey 2050 OCP update, with development now led by the Official Community Plan. https://flipbook-surrey-ca.cld.bz/Official-Community-Plan-Bylaw-No-18020/111/ and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/morgan-heights-ncp
- **Strata paperwork is where an age bylaw would be proved:** for any strata purchase, the Province's buyer paperwork path is the Form B information certificate, Form F, strata bylaws and rules, fees and special levies — strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report, and strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. If a Grandview listing ever claims 55+, the Form B and the registered bylaws are the proof, not the listing text. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Lock-off suites are zone-specific, not complex-wide:** Surrey allows lock-off suites in townhouses in the RM-15, RM-23 and RM-30 zones in transit-oriented and frequent-bus areas, per the live page's summary of City rules — the City's zoning page itself was not re-read in the underlying research, so treat the zone list as UNVERIFIED at source level and confirm the specific complex's zone before relying on it. https://jaredgibbons.ca/grandview-corners-condos-townhomes-for-sale.html
- **ALR check on the east side:** the Grandview Heights plan area includes Agricultural Land Reserve land in its east portion; parcel-level status is checked in the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Grandview Heights?** None verified. No strata complex inside the Grandview GLUP area carries a stated 55+ age restriction in any source checked — The Loop, Imperial, Finestra and The Boroughs are all general-market townhome product. A buyer who needs a true 55+ strata in South Surrey is looking west, to the 152 Street corridor complexes in Sunnyside Park and King George Corridor. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/2350-165-street/55 and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/grandview-heights-general-land-use-plan
- **Q: A listing near 24 Ave and 160 St says "adult-oriented" / "seniors-friendly" — is it 55+?** Not on that wording. In BC a strata age restriction exists only as a registered 55+ bylaw under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1/123.2 — marketing language is not a bylaw. No Grandview complex checked states an age restriction at all; confirm any future claim on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Is The Residence at Morgan Heights something I can buy into?** No. It is a registered assisted-living residence (class SN, operator Morgan Heights Care Ltd.) at 15955 27th Avenue offering assisted living and long-term residential care — rental/care housing, not strata ownership. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=pique%20newsmagazine&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=41&name=&address=&page=10&order=alr_residence_type&sort=asc and https://morganheightscare.ca/residential-care
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. What would the rule be in a true 55+ strata nearby?** Provincially, a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption) is exempt, as are live-in caregivers, legacy residents, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children the resident cared for before they turned 19. That exemption framework applies in the verified 55+ complexes in Sunnyside Park — it does not create a 55+ complex in Grandview, where no age bylaw was verified. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a Grandview townhome?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. A strata can still restrict short-term rentals, and The Loop's developer material states exactly that pattern: rentals allowed, no short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation and https://storage.googleapis.com/precon-app.appspot.com/40b3001a-ef8b-402b-b8b3-506e6b28f0f2.pdf
- **Q: Are the Grandview townhomes single-level / good for aging in place?** The verified product form is multi-storey: The Loop's listings describe 3-storey townhomes, Finestra's plans are three-storey townhomes with roof decks, and Imperial's listings describe 3-storey townhomes — main-floor-primary rancher product of the Bishop's Green / St. Martin's Lane kind (in Sunnyside Park) is not the Grandview form. Verify the floor plan unit by unit. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/2350-165-street/55 and https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/finestra-surrey-presale-townhomes/ and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/15665-mountain-view-drive/53
- **Q: Which schools are in the area, and are they my catchment schools?** Edgewood Elementary (16666 23 Ave), Sunnyside Elementary (2828 159 St), Pacific Heights Elementary (17148 26 Ave) and Grandview Heights Secondary (16987 25 Ave) are physically in the area; SD36 catchment is set by exact address in the School Locator, and a school being nearby does not make it the catchment school — The Loop's developer material names Edgewood Elementary and Grandview Heights Secondary as its catchments, which is a developer statement for that complex only. https://www.surreyschools.ca/grandviewheights and https://storage.googleapis.com/precon-app.appspot.com/40b3001a-ef8b-402b-b8b3-506e6b28f0f2.pdf
- **Q: Where is the closest hospital?** Peace Arch Hospital in White Rock — the Sunnyside Park notes in this series give its address as 15521 Russell Avenue, White Rock, a short drive south-west of the Grandview node. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/white-rock/peace-arch-hospital
- **Q: Does buying a general-market Grandview home carry any resale age issue?** No age bylaw was verified for any Grandview complex, so the buyer pool is the general market — the opposite trade-off from a true 55+ strata, where the next buyer must be able to reside under the bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Grandview's growth story is a family-townhome story, not a seniors story.** The GLUP dates to 2005 and the built-out product around the node is newer 3-storey townhome complexes (The Loop spanning a full city block at 2350 165 St; Finestra's 123 townhomes at 18 Ave and 168 St) — no new age-restricted strata project inside the GLUP area appears in any source checked. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans and https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/finestra-surrey-presale-townhomes/
- **"Sunnyside Heights" is not "Sunnyside Park."** Sunnyside Heights is a Grandview Heights neighbourhood plan area inside the GLUP; Sunnyside Park Surrey is the separate MLS sub-area down the 152 Street corridor where South Surrey's verified 55+ strata stock (Semiahmoo Estates, Bishop's Green, St. Martin's Lane) actually sits. A search that merges the two names puts another sub-area's 55+ complexes on a Grandview page. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/grandview-heights-general-land-use-plan
- **The one manufactured-home community on 160 Street states the opposite of 55+.** Breakaway Bays (1840 160 St) is marketed in its current listing as a "NO AGE RESTRICTION community" — and MLS files it under King George Corridor in any case. Proximity to the 160 Street corridor does not make a community a Grandview 55+ option on either test. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/1840-160-street/337
- **The aquatic centre and the secondary school are the node's real anchors.** Grandview Heights Aquatic Centre (16855 24 Ave, 10-lane 50-metre pool plus leisure pool) sits beside Grandview Heights Secondary (16987 25 Ave) — both verified City/district facilities, and both are family infrastructure, which matches the general-market buyer pool the complexes were built for. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/SPRING-2026-Aquatics-Drop-in-Schedule.pdf and https://www.surreyschools.ca/grandviewheights
- **A neighbourhood plan name on a listing may already be rescinded.** The Morgan Heights NCP — one of the GLUP's six neighbourhood plan areas — has since been rescinded through the Surrey 2050 OCP update, with development now led by the Official Community Plan; where a plan and the OCP conflict, the OCP is the current rule. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/morgan-heights-ncp
- **The care option inside the plan area carries a neighbourhood name, not a "55+" label.** The Residence at Morgan Heights (15955 27 Ave) is registered as a seniors (SN) assisted-living residence — a buyer searching only for "55+" product will miss it, and a buyer searching for ownership will be misled by it; it is care housing at the GLUP's north edge. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=pique%20newsmagazine&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=41&name=&address=&page=10&order=alr_residence_type&sort=asc
