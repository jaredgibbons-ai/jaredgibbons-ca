## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home parks run on a different statute.** Pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA) — the law that applies where a tenant rents a pad site but owns the manufactured home. Age rules in a park are park rules/policies, not strata bylaws — get them in writing from the park, with the tenancy agreement. Surrey's own policy brochure states the property owner must give 12 months' notice of a final moving date using a Notice to End Tenancy, which can only be given after the owner receives all their permits, and the City's Manufactured Home Park Policy requires an affordable housing program for residents when a park is redeveloped. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf
- **Membership complexes are neither strata nor freehold purchase in the usual sense.** Sullivan Mews' own listings state it is a membership, not a strata, and that mortgages are not possible — financing, resale and transfer rules come from the membership documents, not the Strata Property Act. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3058951-15020-66a-avenue-49-surrey-bc-v3s-2a5/brochure
- **Short-term rentals in Surrey:** under 90 consecutive days per patron counts as short-term; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at $358 per year is required before operating — strata bylaws are a separate, building-level check on top. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Property transfer tax and first-time buyers in this band:** general PTT is 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value and 2% on the portion above $200,000; under the first time home buyers' program the full exemption requires a fair market value of $835,000 or less (effective April 1, 2024) and exempts the tax on the first $500,000, with a partial exemption only above $835,000 and below $860,000, subject to the program's other conditions. Because this band caps at $800,000, a qualifying first-time buyer anywhere in the band sits under the full-exemption value ceiling. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000**, including SSMUH zones RA to R6: up to 3 units on lots under 280 sq m, up to 4 units on lots over 280 sq m, up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. This affects detached streets around the complexes, not the strata / membership / park lots inside them. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **OCP: "Surrey 2050" Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2026, No. 21900** is the current OCP — finally adopted June 15, 2026, replacing the 2013 OCP Bylaw No. 18020. Older neighbourhood concept plans have been rescinded into it in areas such as South Newton — a seller quoting an old NCP designation may be quoting a document no longer in effect. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many Surrey condos under $800K are 55+?** That count cannot be stated from any published source: SnapStats publishes no price-band cut and no condo-only cut, and the live feed is the only place the current band supply can be seen. What can be stated is the building list: five verified 55+ condo-form buildings city-wide (Fleetwood Gardens, Carriage House, Semiahmoo Estates, 13965 16 Ave, and Chelsea Gardens' apartment section) — a unit in one of those buildings is 55+; a unit anywhere else in the feed needs its own age check (Surrey condos under-$800K area research file, Section 2; Section 2 above).
- **Q: Does the $800K cap cover the typical Surrey condo?** The cap sits above the middle of the combined attached market: the board-community Surrey Condos & Townhomes median sale price was $512,944 in September 2026 — $287,056 below the cap — but that median mixes townhomes with condos, so treat it as context for where the cap sits, not as the price of a typical condo (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via the research file).
- **Q: Why do so few 55+ listings show up in a condos-under-$800K search?** Form, not price: ten of Surrey's fifteen verified 55+ strata complexes are townhouse complexes, which a condos-only feed excludes by type before price is ever applied; the membership complex (Sullivan Mews) and the three manufactured-home parks are also outside a strata-condo feed. The 55+ condo supply is the five buildings in Section 2. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Do both of us have to be 55?** It varies by complex in its own words: Lakewood Heights and Peninsula Village listings state only one occupant needs to meet the age requirement, and Manor Ridge listings state 1 occupant must be 55+. Provincially, a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident is exempt in any case — confirm the building's bylaw in its Form B. https://sothebysrealty.ca/en/property/british-columbia/region-fraser-valley/surrey-real-estate/3076580/6211-w-boundary-drive-no13/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** In a strata, ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside; the Province states under-55s can own but may not be able to live in the unit. In a park or the membership complex, the equivalent question is answered by the park rules / membership documents, not the Strata Property Act. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit, or run an Airbnb?** Long-term: yes in a strata — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022; the tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption. Short-term: Surrey allows it only for an owner living at the property as their primary residence, with a $358-per-year business licence, and the strata can still restrict short-term rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Q: Is Sullivan Mews a cheap way into a Surrey "condo"?** It is not a condo and not a strata: its own listings state the purchase is a membership, that mortgages are not possible, with a 55+ age restriction, no smoking, 1 small pet and a maximum of 2 occupants. Read the membership documents before pricing it against strata units in this band. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3058951-15020-66a-avenue-49-surrey-bc-v3s-2a5/brochure
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Complex by complex across the city: Carriage House listings state no pets permitted; Fleetwood Gardens records pets allowed with restrictions; Manor Ridge allows 1 pet with restrictions; Sullivan Mews allows 1 small pet; Westwood Estates allows large dogs with park approval, no vicious breeds. There is no Surrey-wide pet rule for 55+ housing. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/17695-58-avenue/102 and https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/311-16068-83-ave-1729077
- **Q: As a first-time buyer under $800K, what is my transfer-tax position?** A qualifying first-time buyer anywhere in this band sits under the $835,000 full-exemption value ceiling: the program exempts the tax on the first $500,000 of the purchase price, subject to its other conditions (principal residence; property 0.5 hectares or smaller) — confirm the buyer's own position with a legal professional before writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** In a strata, mechanically yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. In the parks and the membership complex, the resale pool is set by the park's / membership's own transfer and age rules. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Surrey's 55+ supply comes in four different legal forms, and a condo search shows only one of them.** Strata (15 complexes), membership (Sullivan Mews — no strata lot, no conventional mortgage per its own listings), pad-rent manufactured homes (three parks — MHPTA, not strata law), and care/subsidized rental (Amica, Arbourside, Bethshan, PICS). The form decides the financing, the documents and the exit, before the floor plan matters. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3058951-15020-66a-avenue-49-surrey-bc-v3s-2a5/brochure and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf
- **The city-wide file in this series undercounts the city by five complexes.** Chelsea Gardens, Charleston Green, Glenwood Village, Carriage House and Fleetwood Gardens are all verified in the sub-area files (or in this pass) but absent from the city-wide calibration file's list of ten — including its statement that Fleetwood had no verified 55+ strata condo building, which Fleetwood Gardens (BCS1550, "Age Restricted 55+" in MLS building data) contradicts. This file follows the sub-area evidence. https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/311-16068-83-ave-1729077
- **Two complexes on one Newton street carry almost the same name and opposite age rules.** Glenwood Village (7156 121 St, NWS2768) is 55+; Glenwood Village Heights (6875 121 St, NWS3423) advertises "NO AGE RESTRICTIONS." A name-filtered condo or townhouse alert will mix them — the strata plan number is the reliable separator. https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/204-7156-121-st-42603 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6875-121-street/132
- **One short Newton drive holds three 55+ complexes filed under two different sub-areas.** Lakewood Place (6061) and Lakewood Heights (6211) on W Boundary Drive are filed under Panorama Ridge, while Lakeview Estates (6090), between them, is filed under West Newton — a buyer searching one sub-area's page misses part of the Boundary Park cluster. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6090-w-boundary-drive/104
- **Two Surrey parks share almost the same name, in two different communities.** Westwood Court is the Fleetwood park at 8670 156 Street; Westwood Estates is the Newton park on 134 Street, whose own listings disagree on 8224 vs 8254 as the civic address. An alert set on one name does not cover the other park. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/8670-156-street/9
- **Surrey's 55+ strata supply is old, with one exception.** Fourteen of the fifteen verified complexes are 1986–2000 stock in the listings checked (Lakeview Estates 1986, Carriage House 1987, Charleston Green and Glenwood Village 1988, Manor Ridge 1989, the South Surrey corridor and Boundary Park complexes 1990s, The Crescent 2000) — Fleetwood Gardens, built 2005, is the only verified 55+ strata building in the city newer than 2000 in this research. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **A care-sounding name in Newton is a recovery residence, not seniors housing.** Cedars at Panorama Ridge (6811 135A St) is class SU (supportive recovery) in the provincial Assisted Living Registry — Surrey's clearest example of why a name or "seniors" marketing language is never a substitute for the registry class or the filed bylaw. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ASurrey&address=&name=&page=2&order=alr_residence_type&sort=desc
