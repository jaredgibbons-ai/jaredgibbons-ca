# Port Moody — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves. No earlier 55+ file exists for Port Moody in this series — this file is researched fresh from City of Port Moody, Fraser Health, BC Housing and listing sources, on top of the batch-14 city research file.

**ZERO VERIFIED 55+ OWNERSHIP STOCK — stated plainly:** no age-restricted strata, manufactured-home park or other buyable 55+ complex in Port Moody was verified in any source checked in this pass. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. In Port Moody this law currently has nothing verified to attach to — no strata in the city carries a confirmed age bylaw in any source checked. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Port Moody short-term rentals (researched fresh):** the City brought forward Zoning Bylaw Amendment No. 3482 and Business Licensing Amendment No. 3483 (Short-Term Rentals) to a public hearing / opportunity for public input on February 4, 2025. The specific licensing terms and principal-residence rules those bylaws set are UNVERIFIED in this pass — the hearing notice was read, the adopted bylaw text was not — so no Port Moody STR rule is stated beyond the existence of that 2025 bylaw process. https://www.portmoody.ca/news-notices-and-community-matters/posts/notice-of-public-hearing-and-opportunity-for-public-input-short-term-rentals-bylaws-feb-4-2025/
- **OCP and zoning (current numbers):** OCP = Port Moody 2050, Bylaw, 2026, No. 3522 (adopted April 28, 2026; repeals the 2014 OCP, No. 2955). Zoning = Zoning Bylaw, 2018, No. 2937. Heritage: Moody Centre and the Ioco townsite are heritage conservation areas, where a heritage alteration permit can be required for new construction, reconstruction or changes on included properties. https://www.portmoody.ca/news-notices-and-community-matters/posts/port-moody-s-new-official-community-plan-will-serve-as-a-roadmap-for-the-continued-evolution-of-the-community-over-the-next-25-years/ and https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/
- **SSMUH and suites:** in June 2024 Council approved Zoning Bylaw Amendment No. 94, 2024, No. 3460 to comply with Bill 44: up to four dwelling units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², and up to six units on lots in that size band within 400 m of frequent bus stops (15 minutes or less), with minimum parking eliminated in the six-unit case; transit-oriented areas are exempt from the SSMUH rules in the City's framing in the research file. Secondary suites: Port Moody's own guide states suites were permitted in all single detached residential zones in 2004 (except manufactured home parks), extended to the duplex zone in 2018 with the maximum-size restriction removed; the guide's older text also states a suite may not exceed 90 m² and 40% of the building's floor space and may not be stratified — guide text, not a fresh bylaw read; confirm a specific lot against the current consolidated Zoning Bylaw No. 2937. A Guide to SSMUH was adopted July 22, 2025. http://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/ and https://api.ghdcdn.com/portmoody-edocs/v1/eDocs/Get?docnumber=632150
- **Manufactured-home pad rules:** no park was verified in Port Moody, so this is the provincial frame only: under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act framework, a pad rent can be increased once every 12 months on three full months' notice, by at most the annual limit — for 2026, 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Port Moody property taxes:** for 2026, taxes were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding amounts after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after September 15, 2026; utilities are billed separately, with the 2026 utility bill due February 27, 2026. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/property-taxes-and-utility-fees/property-taxes/
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry (Zone 4)** for the Section 1 developments (Inlet Centre, Moody's Landing), and directly to the operator for Section 2 (Legion Kinsmen Manor). BC Housing's Seniors' Supportive Housing program, for context, serves low-income British Columbians aged 55 or older (and people of any age with a disability), with tenants generally paying 50% of gross income as rent — program-level rules; whether either Port Moody development is an SSH building is UNVERIFIED from the directory alone. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf and https://www.bchousing.org/housing-assistance/housing-with-support/seniors-supportive-housing

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ home I can buy in Port Moody?** No verified one. No strata, townhouse complex or manufactured-home park in the city was found carrying a stated 55+ age restriction in any source checked. A senior who wants to buy and stay in the Tri-Cities under a 55+ bylaw is, on this series' evidence, looking at Mayfair Terrace in downtown Port Coquitlam (46 strata units, built 2000, with a mandatory service plan) — a different city, sharing the same hospital. Sources: Section 2 above and ~/workspace/local-notes/port-coquitlam-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md.
- **Q: Is 55 Klahanie Drive a 55+ building?** No — 55 is its street number. The Electronic Ave buildings at that address were built in 2023 and their listings describe general-market condos with resort-style amenities and pets / rentals with restrictions. No age restriction is stated for the building in any source checked. https://www.zolo.ca/port-moody-real-estate/55-klahanie-drive/231
- **Q: My spouse is under 55 / I'm under 55 buying for a parent — how would the exemptions work if I buy 55+ nearby?** Under the provincial rules that govern any BC 55+ strata: a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident is exempt (with defined child and caregiver exemptions, expanded May 1, 2023), and ownership itself is never age-restricted — an under-55 buyer can own a unit that a 55+ parent resides in. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a Port Moody condo long-term?** Strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022, so a long-term ban cannot be enforced; short-term rentals are a separate question — a strata can still restrict them, and Port Moody ran its own short-term rental bylaw process in 2025 whose adopted terms are UNVERIFIED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.portmoody.ca/news-notices-and-community-matters/posts/notice-of-public-hearing-and-opportunity-for-public-input-short-term-rentals-bylaws-feb-4-2025/
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** In the city: Eagle Ridge Hospital, 475 Guildford Way — the Tri-Cities hospital, with a 24/7 emergency department, diagnostics, inpatient and outpatient care, rehabilitation and surgery specialties. Long-term care sits on the same campus at Eagle Ridge Manor. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- **Q: What seniors housing does exist in Port Moody, if I rent?** Non-profit: Inlet Centre (101 Noons Creek Dr) and Moody's Landing (2330 St. Johns St), both applied for through BC Housing's Housing Registry per the Zone 4 directory; and Legion Kinsmen Manor (2909 Hope St), a non-profit seniors manor noted "market rent only" in the directory, applied for directly to the Port Moody Senior Hsg Society. None of it is ownership. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** Two SkyTrain stations in the city (Moody Centre, which also has the West Coast Express and a bus loop, and Inlet Centre), per TransLink's regional connections diagram. The West Coast Express is described on that diagram as commuter rail in weekday peak hours; specific frequencies and travel times are UNVERIFIED in this series and are not quoted. https://maps.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/system-maps/fwc26_regional_transit_connections.pdf
- **Q: Can a Port Moody house take a suite, coach house or more units, if I downsize into part of it?** Suites have been permitted in single detached zones since 2004 (duplex zone added 2018), and the 2024 SSMUH amendment allows up to four units on most 280–4,050 m² lots (six near frequent bus stops), with coach houses / accessory dwelling units in the RS and RT zones outside the transit-oriented areas in the research file's reading — but creek lots can need a riparian assessment and hillside lots can fall in steep-slope / environmentally sensitive development permit areas, and transit-oriented areas are exempt from SSMUH. Lot-by-lot confirmation against the consolidated zoning bylaw is required. http://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
- **Q: How are Port Moody homes selling, in board terms?** The board's Port Moody community (which groups Anmore and Belcarra in) sold at a $1,685,000 detached median (135 listings, 15 sold, 33 days) and a $715,000 Condos & Townhomes median (192 listings, 29 sold, 29 days) in September 2026; attached supply concentrates in Port Moody Centre (120 of 192 attached listings). Small-month caution: roughly 12–16 detached sales a month means the detached median swings widely ($1,570,000–$2,108,000 across the 13 months). Per-sub-area medians are not published. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report.)
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in Port Moody?** The in-city ladder is short: independent non-profit rental (Inlet Centre, Moody's Landing, Legion Kinsmen Manor), then long-term care at Eagle Ridge Manor on the hospital campus (publicly subsidized, accessed through Fraser Health; its own Fraser Health pages state both 65 and 75 beds — see the conflict flag in Section 2). No private retirement residence or registered assisted living residence in Port Moody was verified in this pass — the Tri-Cities' verified assisted living residences in this series are in Port Coquitlam. Sources: Section 2 above.
