## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The feed is city-wide, but the attached stock it draws from is not evenly spread: in September 2026 East Central held 120 active Condos & Townhomes listings and West Central 78 — about seven in ten of Maple Ridge's 286 attached listings — with Cottonwood at 34, Albion 18 and Southwest 8 (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report).
- The Town Centre is the city's planned condo core: the Town Centre Area Plan update expanded the plan boundaries to incorporate River Bend, consolidated the land use designations, and added a Town Centre Core designation supporting higher densities in a transit-oriented downtown anchored by the Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station; Transit-Oriented Area Designation Bylaw No. 8001-2024 covers the Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station TOAs. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- Haney Place Exchange routes include the R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus, which runs from Coquitlam Central Station to Haney Place, and Port Haney Station (22300 River Road) runs West Coast Express trains on weekdays between Waterfront (downtown Vancouver) and Mission City. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Route_Diagrams/194/r748.pdf and https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- Amenities in the core: Maple Ridge Leisure Centre, 11925 Haney Place, has an indoor pool, fitness centre, gymnasium and sport courts; Maple Ridge Public Library (Fraser Valley Regional Library), 130–22470 Dewdney Trunk Road, is the only FVRL branch in Maple Ridge; the Town Centre shopping centres listed in the City's downtown guide are Haney Place Mall, 11900 Haney Place, Valley Fair Mall, 22709 Lougheed Highway, and Maple Ridge Square Shopping Mall, 22441 Dewdney Trunk Road. https://www.mapleridge.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/maple-ridge-leisure-centre and https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods/be-downtown
- School District 42 (Maple Ridge–Pitt Meadows) serves the city; it has no middle tier — elementary is Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary is Grades 8 to 12 — and catchments are address-based through the district's School Locator. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- Health care: Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, Maple Ridge, is the community hospital for Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows with 24/7 emergency care; the Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is inside Haney Place Mall, 121–11900 Haney Place, open 7 days a week, 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital
- Floodplain at the south edge of the core: the Port Haney / River Road edge sits along the Fraser River — the flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), a covenant on title identifies construction in the floodplain, and no habitable floor space may be located below the flood construction level on affected lots. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates: almost all of Maple Ridge's verified 55+ ownership stock is in East Central — the Fraserview Village pocket around 116 Avenue and 225–227 Streets, plus the Alouette park and Haney Pioneer co-op east of downtown — with St. George's Village co-op on Dewdney Trunk Road at the Cottonwood edge and Val Maria park on Lougheed Highway in the southwest. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why building sources for Rosewood Court still showing "rentals not allowed" cannot be read at face value. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/rosewood-court-22611-116-ave
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019** contains the land-use and development regulations for every property in Maple Ridge (verified on the City's planning pages Nov 1, 2026 — the number is 7600-2019, not 7600-2020). https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/land-development/planning-application-forms-procedures
- **City plan: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 7060-2014** is the OCP bylaw cited on the City's own OCP chapter documents (verified Nov 1, 2026 — not 7601-2020). https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/01ocp-chapter-1201402201612431396
- **Secondary suites:** one secondary suite per lot, inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not in the Floodplain Area, with a building permit required; eligible single-detached and duplex lots can have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH rules. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Manufactured home parks run on different law.** Pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and its Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003), not the Strata Property Act. The Regulation expressly contemplates parks where every site is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 (or two or more tenants, at least one of whom has reached 55), and a park landlord may refuse an assignment or sublease where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement in such a park. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Co-op and life-lease age rules are contract/membership rules, not strata bylaws.** St. George's Village's 55-year membership qualification, Haney Pioneer's 60+ bar and the Legion life leases' terms are set by those organizations' own documents; the provincial strata exemptions do not automatically apply to them. https://www.stgeorgesvillage.ca/_files/ugd/070889_39221c746a9b483980e20a3596cdb580.pdf and https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Property transfer tax and the first-time program in this band:** the Province publishes general PTT at 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion greater than $200,000 and up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% of the portion greater than $2,000,000; the first time home buyers' program's full exemption requires a fair market value of $835,000 or less (effective April 1, 2024) with the exemption on the first $500,000 of price, and a partial exemption above $835,000 and below $860,000 — subject to all the program's other conditions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **FHSA and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits: UNVERIFIED** — no live CRA page was read for this file, so no FHSA or HBP limit is stated here.
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** for this area (Zone 4 directory, May 2025), with some buildings apply-direct to the society — the directory gives the route per building. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

- **Q: What exactly is Fraserview Village, and what do I actually buy?** A cluster of separate strata buildings around a shared amenity building with an indoor pool, hot tub, exercise/recreation centre, clubhouse, workshop and a rentable guest suite. You buy a strata lot in one building; each building has its own bylaws — verify the specific building's age bylaw on the Form B. https://vancouvernashdom.com/maple-ridge-east-central-apartments/SearchResults.form/feed/feed/feed/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a Fraserview building?** Yes — in a strata, provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. This provincial guarantee applies to strata; the co-ops and the Legion life leases set their own household rules. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a Fraserview unit as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Building sites say Rosewood Court doesn't allow rentals. Can I rent out my unit or not?** The building-data summary is stale against provincial law: every residential rental-restriction bylaw in BC became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: a tenant must still satisfy the building's 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and the strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/rosewood-court-22611-116-ave and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Q: Are there 55+ condos toward Cottonwood or in the Town Centre towers?** No verified ones. Toward Cottonwood the verified 55+ ownership option is St. George's Village, a townhouse co-operative on Dewdney Trunk Road (membership shares, not a strata condo), and the Town Centre towers (Era, Inspire, Brickwater) carry no 55+ bylaw in any source checked. https://www.stgeorgesvillage.ca/_files/ugd/070889_39221c746a9b483980e20a3596cdb580.pdf and https://vancouvernashdom.com/maple-ridge-apartments-for-sale/SearchResults.form/?_pg=6
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer use the PTT exemption on a condo in this band?** In principle, yes — the program's full exemption runs to a fair market value of $835,000 (exempting the first $500,000 of price), with a partial exemption to $860,000, provided the buyer meets all the program's personal and property conditions; the exemption is claimed through the buyer's legal professional on the PTT return. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Fraserview?** Building by building. Rosewood Court records pets allowed with restrictions; a listing for The Willows states the building is pet friendly and allows 2 cats or 2 dogs (or 1 of each). Confirm the specific building's pet bylaw on the Form B. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/rosewood-court-22611-116-ave
- **Q: What does buying at the Alouette park actually cost each month?** You own the manufactured home and rent the pad under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. A current listing states the pad rental fee at $713.31 plus city taxes of $457.37 and quarterly metered water, with park rules including no dogs, indoor cat only, 2 residents per unit, and no financing permitted in the community (all listing-stated — confirm in the tenancy agreement and park rules). https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3067037-12868-229th-street-18-maple-ridge-bc-v2x-6r1/brochure

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Maple Ridge's 55+ condo supply is one village, not a city-wide market.** Four separate strata corporations at Fraserview Village share one amenity building — two buildings a block apart can carry different pet and other bylaws while sharing the same pool, so price and rules must be compared building by building, not by the "Fraserview Village" name. https://vancouvernashdom.com/maple-ridge-east-central-apartments/SearchResults.form/feed/feed/feed/ and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/rosewood-court-22611-116-ave
- **The $800K cap sits above the attached market's middle, but that proves nothing about any one building.** The Maple Ridge condos & townhomes median sale price was $525,000 in September 2026 — $275,000 below the cap — though that median mixes townhomes with condos and swung from $640,000 in August 2026 on small sold counts (33–47 solds a month) (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report).
- **The newest downtown condo stock is not seniors stock.** The Town Centre's recent growth (Era, Inspire, Brickwater and the Transit-Oriented Area framework under Bylaw No. 8001-2024) is general-market product; the 55+ strata supply is still the 1990s Fraserview Village buildings. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- **A Legion branch is one of the biggest seniors-housing operators in the city.** Royal Canadian Legion Branch 88 — described in the Legion's own case study as one of the largest Legions in Canada, with over 2,600 members — runs two life-lease buildings (Legion Gardens, Legion Manor) plus two seniors' rental buildings (Earl Haig and Legion House), with veterans placed first but all buildings open to everyone. https://dev.ibabchyperarticles.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Seniors-Housing-Development-2010.pdf
- **The "55+" park on 229 Street and the family complex with almost the same name are different places.** Alouette Retirement Mobile Home Park (12868 229 St) is the verified 55+ park; Alouette Estates (11870 232 St) is a general-market townhouse complex with no age restriction in any source. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3067037-12868-229th-street-18-maple-ridge-bc-v2x-6r1/brochure and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3105915-24-11870-232-street-maple-ridge-british-columbia-v2x6s9-711888/brochure
- **East Central's attached depth cuts both ways for a 55+ seller.** With 120 active Condos & Townhomes listings in September 2026, a Fraserview seller competes not only with the other village buildings but with every newer general-market condo downtown (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report).
