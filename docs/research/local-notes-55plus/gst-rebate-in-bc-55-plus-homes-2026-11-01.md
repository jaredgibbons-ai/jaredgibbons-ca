# GST Rebate in BC — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

This file is a TOPIC page (slug `gst-rebate-in-bc`), not a neighbourhood. It is framed around the federal GST rebates as they apply to a 55+ buyer in BC — most importantly, whether a resale 55+ strata purchase attracts GST at all, and what the new first-time home buyers' rebate does and does not do for a downsizer. All dollar figures below were read on live Canada.ca / CRA pages in this pass (Nov 1, 2026); any figure that could not be read there is marked UNCONFIRMED. Every fact carries its source. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The rebate rules are federal and provincial, not municipal: the GST/HST rebates are administered by the Canada Revenue Agency under Part IX of the Excise Tax Act, and in BC only the 5% GST applies (BC has no HST). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- The geographic boundary that matters for this topic is new versus resale, not city versus city: the rebates apply to newly constructed or substantially renovated homes, and the region's verified 55+ ownership stock (Section 2) is overwhelmingly resale strata built in the 1980s–2000s, to which no GST rebate applies because resale homes are not charged GST in the first place. Source: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/gst-rebate-in-bc.md
- Where new 55+ product would even arise in this region: UNCONFIRMED — no source in this research identifies a newly built age-restricted strata project currently selling in the Fraser Valley communities covered by the sibling files; the newest verified 55+ stock found in the program is rental (Kew Lifestyles at Montrose, Port Coquitlam, completed 2023). Source: ~/workspace/local-notes/port-coquitlam-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- BC's own tax layer sits beside the federal rebate: property transfer tax (PTT) is a provincial tax on registration at the Land Title Office, separate from GST, with its own first-time buyer program. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT THIS HOME TYPE

- **The first-time home buyers' (FTHB) GST/HST rebate is law.** Amendments to Part IX of the Excise Tax Act in Bill C-4 introduced it, and Bill C-4 received royal assent on March 13, 2026. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Amount and thresholds (read on the CRA page in this pass):** together with the GST/HST new housing rebate, the FTHB rebate provides 100% of the GST (or the federal part of the HST) on a newly constructed or substantially renovated single unit residential complex or residential condominium unit valued up to $1 million; between $1 million and $1.5 million the rebate is gradually reduced; no FTHB rebate is available at $1.5 million or more. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Maximum in dollars (read on the CRA page in this pass):** eligible individuals could get a rebate of up to $50,000 — consistent with 100% of the 5% GST on a $1 million home in BC. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/news/newsroom/tax-tips/tax-tips-2026/first-time-buyers-can-save-more-on-new-homes-the-first-time-home-buyers-gst-hst-rebate-is-available-now.html
- **Who counts as a first-time home buyer (CRA):** at least 18 years old, a Canadian citizen or permanent resident, and must not have lived in a home — in or outside Canada — that they or their spouse or common-law partner owned or jointly owned as a primary place of residence in the calendar year of the purchase or in the preceding four calendar years. A long-time homeowner downsizing into a 55+ home therefore does not qualify on these terms as stated. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property and timing conditions (CRA):** the buyer must meet the existing new housing rebate's eligibility criteria (or would meet them if that rebate's ceiling were $1.5 million instead of $450,000); must have entered a purchase and sale agreement with a builder on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031 (for an owner-built home, construction must begin on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031); construction must begin before 2031 and be substantially completed before 2036; the buyer must be the first to occupy the home after substantial completion; and the buyer must not have previously applied for an FTHB rebate. The rebate also generally covers a home bought on land leased from the builder and a share in a co-operative housing corporation entitling the buyer to occupy a new unit. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **The existing GST/HST new housing rebate (CRA):** generally 36% of the GST (or federal part of the HST) paid on a newly constructed or substantially renovated single unit residential complex or condominium unit, to a maximum rebate of $6,300; for homes valued between $350,000 and $450,000 the rebate is gradually reduced, and no rebate is available at $450,000 or more. This rebate is not limited to first-time buyers, but it is limited to new or substantially renovated homes. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Claiming (CRA):** the application time limit is usually within two years of taking ownership or finishing construction; a builder can credit the rebate against the amount payable when ownership transfers; where the builder does not credit it, the buyer applies directly to the CRA through a CRA account or by mailing Form GST190 (houses purchased from a builder), and an owner-builder who started construction on or after March 20, 2025 applies using Form GST191. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/news/newsroom/tax-tips/tax-tips-2026/first-time-buyers-can-save-more-on-new-homes-the-first-time-home-buyers-gst-hst-rebate-is-available-now.html
- **BC property transfer tax, first-time buyers' program (Province):** a qualifying buyer is exempt from PTT on the first $500,000 of the purchase price where the property is used only as a principal residence, has a fair market value of $835,000 or less, is 0.5 hectares or smaller and contains only residential improvements; a partial exemption may apply between $835,000 and $860,000. General PTT rates are 1% up to $200,000, 2% on the portion up to $2,000,000, and 3% above that, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **UNCONFIRMED figures — not read on a live CRA page in this pass:** the RRSP Home Buyers' Plan figures stated on the live site page (a $60,000 withdrawal limit, 90-day holding rule, 15-year repayment, and a five-year repayment start for 2022–2025 withdrawals), the first-time home buyer's tax credit figures ($10,000 credit, up to $1,500 relief), and the FHSA lifetime contribution limit. The CRA's Home Buyers' Plan page failed to load in the research pass (HTTP 403), so none of those figures is adopted here. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/gst-rebate-in-bc.md
- **BC strata age law still frames the purchase itself:** only 55+ age bylaws are valid in BC strata (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, Regulation 7.01, since Nov 24, 2022), the bylaw governs who may reside rather than who may own, and rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: I'm downsizing from a house I've owned for years into a new 55+ condo. Do I get the $50,000 rebate?** On the CRA's stated definition, no: a first-time home buyer must not have lived in a home they or their spouse or common-law partner owned as a primary residence in the purchase year or the preceding four calendar years. A long-time owner does not meet that test. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Does GST even apply when I buy a resale unit in a 55+ building?** The rebates — and the GST they refund — attach to newly constructed or substantially renovated homes; the live topic page notes resale homes are not subject to GST, and the verified 55+ stock in Section 2 is resale stock. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/gst-rebate-in-bc.md
- **Q: I'm not a first-time buyer, but I'm buying a brand-new home. Is there any rebate?** Yes, potentially the long-standing new housing rebate: generally 36% of the GST paid, to a maximum of $6,300, gradually reduced between $350,000 and $450,000 and unavailable at $450,000 or more — figures read on the CRA publication in this pass. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: How much GST can a qualifying first-time buyer in BC get back on a new home?** Up to $50,000: 100% of the GST on a new or substantially renovated home valued up to $1 million, gradually reduced between $1 million and $1.5 million, and nothing at $1.5 million or more. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/news/newsroom/tax-tips/tax-tips-2026/first-time-buyers-can-save-more-on-new-homes-the-first-time-home-buyers-gst-hst-rebate-is-available-now.html
- **Q: We signed a presale before March 20, 2025. Does the FTHB rebate apply?** On the CRA's stated conditions, no: the purchase and sale agreement with the builder must have been entered into on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031, however new the building is when it completes. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Does buying a share in a seniors co-op count for the FTHB rebate?** The CRA states the rebate is also generally available where the buyer purchased a share in a co-operative housing corporation that entitles them to occupy a newly constructed or substantially renovated residential unit — the "newly constructed or substantially renovated" condition still applies, which an established resale co-op would not meet on these terms. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: How and when do I claim?** Usually within two years of taking ownership or finishing construction: the builder can credit the rebate at closing (Form GST190 filed jointly), or you apply directly to the CRA through a CRA account or by mailing Form GST190 — Form GST191 for an owner-built home. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/news/newsroom/tax-tips/tax-tips-2026/first-time-buyers-can-save-more-on-new-homes-the-first-time-home-buyers-gst-hst-rebate-is-available-now.html
- **Q: Can the GST rebate and BC's PTT exemption stack on one purchase?** They are separate federal and provincial programs: the BC first-time program exempts PTT on the first $500,000 at a fair market value of $835,000 or less, with a partial exemption to $860,000, and nothing in either program's conditions as read in this pass bars claiming both. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: What about the Home Buyers' Plan and the first-time home buyer's tax credit amounts I've seen quoted?** UNCONFIRMED in this file: the dollar figures for those programs were not read on a live CRA page in this pass (the CRA Home Buyers' Plan page failed to load), so they are not stated here rather than repeated from secondary sources. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/gst-rebate-in-bc.md
- **Q: Does the rebate change who can live in a 55+ building?** No — it is a tax measure only. Residency is still governed by the strata's 55+ bylaw and the Regulation 7.01 exemptions for spouses, children and caregivers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The headline rebate mostly misses the 55+ market it is marketed beside.** The FTHB rebate needs both a first-time buyer (no owned primary home in the purchase year or prior four years) and a new home — while the region's verified 55+ ownership stock is resale buildings from the 1980s–2000s bought largely by downsizers. Both conditions failing at once is the normal case for this page's audience, on the CRA's stated terms. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and Section 2 above.
- **The signing date, not the completion date, decides a presale.** A presale signed before March 20, 2025 is outside the FTHB rebate no matter when the building completes; construction must also begin before 2031 and be substantially completed before 2036. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **The old rebate's ceiling is far below local new-home prices.** The new housing rebate disappears entirely at $450,000 — a threshold the CRA publication states plainly — so for a non-first-time buyer purchasing new construction in this region, the $6,300 maximum is largely theoretical. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **The rebate became law after it became available.** Bill C-4 received royal assent on March 13, 2026, yet eligibility reaches back to agreements entered into on or after March 20, 2025 — the CRA notes builders could not credit the rebate before royal assent, so early eligible buyers apply directly to the CRA. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/news/newsroom/tax-tips/tax-tips-2026/first-time-buyers-can-save-more-on-new-homes-the-first-time-home-buyers-gst-hst-rebate-is-available-now.html
- **The phase-out formula itself is not stated on the CRA pages opened.** The live site page works a $1.25 million example on a straight-line basis (50%, up to $25,000); the CRA publication says only that the rebate is "gradually reduced" between $1 million and $1.5 million — the straight-line mechanism is UNCONFIRMED. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/gst-rebate-in-bc.md
- **A co-op share can be a "home" for this rebate — but only a new one.** The CRA's co-operative-share wording matters in a region with seniors co-ops (St. George's Village, Pioneer Park), yet both of those are established communities, not new construction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and Section 2 above.
