# Simon Fraser Univer. (SFU / UniverCity) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- This area is the top of Burnaby Mountain in the City of Burnaby: the Simon Fraser University (SFU) Burnaby campus plus the UniverCity residential community beside it. The board sub-area is listed as "Simon Fraser Univer." in Jared's area list; SnapStats prints the full name as "Simon Fraser University SFU" (September 2026). A separate neighbouring sub-area, "Simon Fraser Hills," covers the Forest Grove side of the mountain and is not this area. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.
- UniverCity is a planned community on about 65 hectares, approved by the City of Burnaby in 1996 (Official Community Plan and zoning amendments), envisioned at up to 4,536 residential units in two neighbourhoods south and east of campus, housing up to 10,000 residents at build-out; it is built out in four neighbourhoods — West Highlands, East Highlands, High Street and the Slopes. https://univercity.ca/about-us/history/
- University High Street, in the Cornerstone/High Street core, is the community's main street (grocery, pharmacy, liquor store, cafés and restaurants). (Developer source via the research file: https://www.skytraincondo.ca/presale/arbour-at-sfu-burnaby/)
- TransLink's R5 Hastings St RapidBus terminates at the SFU Exchange, running to Burrard Station in downtown Vancouver at least every 10 minutes at peak and 15 minutes off-peak; the 145 bus connects SFU Exchange to Production Way–University Station on the Millennium Line. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Route_Diagrams/213/rR5.pdf
- The community is ringed by the Burnaby Mountain Conservation Area (578 hectares, 34 multi-use trails over 28 km, summit 366 m); SFU transferred more than 320 hectares of university land to the City to be included in it as part of the UniverCity arrangement. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/walking-hiking-and-biking/burnaby-mountain-conservation-area-trails and https://univercity.ca/about-us/history/
- Schools are School District 41: University Highlands Elementary sits inside UniverCity, and Burnaby Mountain Secondary (8800 Eastlake Drive) is the public secondary school on the mountain. Catchment quirk from the research file: a locator run for the secondary school's own address returned Cameron Elementary, not University Highlands — verify by exact address. https://universityhighlands.burnabyschools.ca/parkingstudent-drop-off/
- Where 55+ housing concentrates: nowhere on the mountain. No verified 55+ strata, rental, retirement, assisted-living or subsidized seniors building exists in UniverCity or on the SFU campus in any source checked; every Burnaby assisted-living residence in the provincial registry sits at the bottom of the mountain (Section 2). The community's housing was built from the early 2000s onward for a university population — faculty, staff, students and investors — per the research file.

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**This area has ZERO verified 55+ ownership stock — and a tenure point that matters more than the age point: UniverCity homes are LEASEHOLD, not freehold. The land remains under SFU ownership and SFU Community Trust leases fully serviced, subdivided parcels to developers through 99-year lease agreements, so a buyer acquires a leasehold interest in a strata unit on SFU's prepaid ground lease — not freehold strata ownership of land. No UniverCity building is described in these notes as freehold. https://univercity.ca/about-us/history/**

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- None verified. No UniverCity or SFU-area building carries a sourced 55+ age restriction in any source checked — and, separately, no UniverCity home is freehold ownership at all (see the tenure note above).

### Verified 55+ rental

- None verified on Burnaby Mountain. SFU's own housing on campus (including Graduate and Family Housing in UniverCity) is university housing for students and families, not seniors rental — see Checked and NOT below.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. A leasehold strata in UniverCity is still a strata corporation under the Act — but no UniverCity strata is verified as having a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** People under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — since May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. For UniverCity specifically, verify any building's rental rules in its own bylaws — occupancy demand on the mountain is heavily student-driven (flagged as general practice guidance in the research file, UNVERIFIED per building). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **The ground lease is the governing document.** SFU Community Trust leases parcels to developers on 99-year leases and the land remains SFU's; before anything else, a buyer confirms the lease commencement date, remaining term, whether rent is prepaid for the full term, and any rent-review or end-of-term provisions. Lender treatment of leasehold (some lenders cap mortgage terms against the remaining lease term) is general practice guidance — UNVERIFIED against any specific lender. https://univercity.ca/about-us/history/
- **City OCP: Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan**, adopted December 9, 2025, replacing the 1998 OCP Bylaw; UniverCity itself is governed by its own OCP framework dating to the 1996 approvals. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Zoning: Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815), in effect July 1, 2026**, which replaced the former Zoning Bylaw 1965 (adopted June 9, 2026). Most house lots citywide are now in the R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing District; under Part 6 s.3.1 a secondary suite is allowed inside a primary dwelling unit in SSMUH, rowhouses and townhouses (one per primary unit, at least 32.52 m² / 350 ft², never stratified or subdivided from the primary unit). The 1965 bylaw is superseded — do not cite it as current. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://tradeschoolsandpermitinfo.ca/guides/burnaby-building-permit-guide
- **SSMUH:** R1 lots rezoned in 2024 can build 3 to 6 units depending on lot area and proximity to a frequent transit network stop, with secondary suites counting toward the total — citywide context; UniverCity's own parcels are developed under its 1996 OCP/lease framework, not lot-by-lot SSMUH. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Taxes:** City of Burnaby property taxes for 2026 were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date. A leasehold owner still receives a tax bill for the unit — confirm how taxes and any ground-lease charges are split in the specific building's documents. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ building at SFU / UniverCity I can buy into?** No verified one — and nothing at UniverCity is freehold in the first place. All market homes there are leasehold strata units on SFU's 99-year ground leases, and no building carries a sourced 55+ age restriction. A buyer who needs a true 55+ ownership product has to look off the mountain. https://univercity.ca/about-us/history/
- **Q: What does leasehold actually mean for my purchase?** The land stays SFU's. SFU Community Trust leases serviced parcels to developers for 99 years; you buy the home/strata lot interest for the lease term. There are no conventional fee-simple residential lots inside UniverCity, so comparisons between buildings come down to unit size, floor and outlook — and to the remaining lease term in the building's documents. https://univercity.ca/about-us/history/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we live in a 55+ strata (off the mountain)?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt categories. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. A 55+ parent can be the resident; an investor's tenant must also satisfy the bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a UniverCity unit?** Long-term rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022, and UniverCity has a deep student rental market — but read the specific strata's bylaws and the ground lease documents, and verify rental rules building by building before pricing a unit as an investment (per-building rules UNVERIFIED in the sources checked). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is the gondola coming? Should I pay for it?** No — do not price it as current transit. TransLink states the Burnaby Mountain Gondola (Production Way–University Station to the SFU bus exchange, about seven minutes) is not yet approved or funded; it still needs inclusion in a future investment plan plus TransLink Board and Mayors' Council approval. Current transit is the R5 RapidBus and the 145 bus. https://www.translink.ca/news/2023/november/translink%20seeking%20feedback%20on%20burnaby%20mountain%20gondola
- **Q: Where is the nearest seniors care if I age in place here?** Off the mountain. The Assisted Living Registry's Burnaby seniors residences are all in North Burnaby, South Burnaby or Edmonds (Seton Villa, Dania Manor, Nikkei Home and others listed in Section 2); there is no assisted-living residence, long-term care home or retirement residence in UniverCity. The nearest hospital for the mountain: UNCONFIRMED in the sources checked for this file — Burnaby Hospital is the city's hospital, but no source read for this file states it, so it is not asserted here. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=intlblog_06252018&f%5B0%5D=city%3ABurnaby&name=&address=&order=alr_residence_type&sort=asc
- **Q: Does the 55+ resale-pool trade-off apply here?** Only in a real 55+ strata elsewhere: the next buyer must be 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. In UniverCity the equivalent resale constraint is different — the buyer pool is limited by the leasehold term and lender comfort with it, not by an age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://univercity.ca/about-us/history/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The whole community is a land-lease deal from 1996.** SFU kept the land, leased parcels to developers on 99-year terms, and in exchange transferred more than 320 hectares to the City for the Burnaby Mountain Conservation Area that now surrounds the homes — the trails at the door exist because of the same arrangement that makes the homes leasehold. https://univercity.ca/about-us/history/
- **The market is deep, attached and slow-turning.** The sub-area listed 91 condos & townhomes with 6 sales in September 2026 (7% sales ratio) against just 5 detached listings — a seller here competes against dozens of near-identical units in a handful of buildings, which is a pricing problem no age restriction creates or solves. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
- **The elementary catchment does not do what the map suggests.** University Highlands Elementary sits inside UniverCity and was designed so most families walk to it in under 5 minutes, yet the SD41 locator run in the research file for 8800 Eastlake Drive (Burnaby Mountain Secondary's own address) returned Cameron Elementary — catchment is by exact address, not by proximity, on the mountain as everywhere else. https://universityhighlands.burnabyschools.ca/parkingstudent-drop-off/
- **The gondola is a business case, not a project.** Council endorsed the route in 2022 and it sits in the Mayors' Council 10-Year Access for Everyone plan, but TransLink's own status is "not yet approved or funded" — roughly 25,000 daily trips to the mountain still run on buses and Gaglardi Way. https://www.translink.ca/news/2023/november/translink%20seeking%20feedback%20on%20burnaby%20mountain%20gondola
- **Burnaby rewrote its zoning bylaw under this community mid-2026.** Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815) took effect July 1, 2026, replacing the 1965 bylaw, and the Burnaby 2050 OCP was adopted December 9, 2025 — any UniverCity document or listing still citing the 1965 bylaw or the 1998 OCP as "current" citywide law is out of date (UniverCity's own 1996 framework still governs the community itself). https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Childcare, not seniors care, is the on-mountain support infrastructure.** The UniverCity Childcare Centre and Verdant Early Childhood Centre operate in the community alongside SFU Childcare — the service buildings on the mountain were built for young families and students, which is the practical reason a 55+ buyer finds no care ladder here. https://univercity.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/UniverCity-Contacts-.pdf
