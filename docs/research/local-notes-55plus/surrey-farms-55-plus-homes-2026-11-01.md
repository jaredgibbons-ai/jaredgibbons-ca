## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. A farm parcel is not a strata, so this law creates no age rule on ALR land — it applies only to the strata complexes in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **ALR housing rules — the rules that actually govern a Surrey farm home:** one principal residence per parcel without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m², with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction; a secondary suite only inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence at a maximum of 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha, with further residences only by ALC approval where necessary for farm use. Local governments may further regulate or restrict ALR residences, and replacing an existing principal residence has its own ALC conditions, including ALC approval to live in the old residence while a new one is built. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **ALR short-term accommodation:** whole-house short-term rental of a principal residence is not permitted outright in the ALR and requires ALC approval; an additional residence may be used for short-term accommodation only as agri-tourism accommodation on a property that is actively farmed and holds farm classification under the Assessment Act. Surrey's own layer sits on top: owner-host only, primary residence, $358-per-year business licence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Farm classification (BC Assessment Class 9):** minimum income thresholds of $2,500 (operation 0.8–4 ha), $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha, or $10,000 (operation under 0.8 ha), with applications due by October 31 for the next assessment and tax year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Urban density rules do not transfer onto a farm parcel:** Surrey's SSMUH framework (3 units under 280 m², 4 over 280 m², up to 6 near a frequent bus stop) applies to eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary — it is not a route to extra dwellings on ALR land, where the ALC rules govern. Surrey's urban suite rule (one suite, at most 40% of the principal unit, never inside a coach house) is likewise the urban contrast to the ALR suite-inside-the-house rule. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Manufactured-home parks run under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act**, not strata law, and Surrey's Manufactured Home Park Policy brochure states the owner must give 12 months' notice of a final moving date, only after receiving all permits. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf
- **Parcel-level ALR status check:** UNVERIFIED in this program — a direct re-open of the ALC's ALR maps page returned a 429 rate-limit error and was not retried, so no map-check method is stated as verified here; the live page's own ALR search tool is the site's feature and its data sources were not verified (Surrey farms area research file, Sections 1 and 4).
- **OCP: "Surrey 2050" Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2026, No. 21900** is the current OCP — finally adopted June 15, 2026, replacing the 2013 OCP Bylaw No. 18020. City zoning generally: Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a 55+ farm in Surrey?** No such product was found in any source checked. Age restrictions in BC attach to strata bylaws (or park / membership rules), and a Surrey farm parcel is freehold ALR land under the ALC residence frame — one principal residence (≤500 m²) plus at most one small additional residence. A 55+ buyer can buy a farm at any age; the age rule only appears if they instead buy into one of the fifteen strata complexes in Section 2. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Where are Surrey's farms?** There is no single farm belt in the sources used here: the live feed's three listings at reading sat in Sullivan Station (two, on the Colebrook side) and Fleetwood Tynehead (one), and the page's own related links point to Cloverdale Farms, South Surrey Farms and Port Kells Acreages as separate pockets (Surrey farms area research file, Section 1).
- **Q: How big can the farmhouse be, and can I add a suite or a second home?** Principal residence capped at 500 m² (1,000 m² fill limit) without an ALC application; a suite only inside that residence; one additional detached residence at 90 m² or less (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² or less (larger parcels), with City bylaws also required to permit it and anything beyond that needing an ALC application showing the residence is necessary for farm use. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I live in the old house while I build the new one?** That needs ALC approval — replacing a principal residence has its own ALC conditions, and demolishing and removing the old residence first is the route that does not require that approval. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I Airbnb the farmhouse or the second dwelling?** Not by right: whole-house short-term rental of a principal residence in the ALR requires ALC approval, an additional residence can be used for short-term accommodation only as agri-tourism accommodation on an actively farmed, farm-classified property, and Surrey separately requires an owner-host living at the property as their primary residence with a $358-per-year business licence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Q: What 55+ homes can I buy in Surrey if I give up the farm idea?** Fifteen strata complexes (Section 2): five condo buildings and ten townhouse complexes across South Surrey (6), Newton (6), Cloverdale (2) and Fleetwood (1) — plus the Sullivan Mews membership complex and three 55+ manufactured-home parks, which are different tenures, not strata. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Do both of us have to be 55 in those complexes?** It varies in their own words: Lakewood Heights and Peninsula Village listings state only one occupant needs to meet the age requirement; Manor Ridge states 1 occupant must be 55+. Provincially, a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident is exempt in any case. https://sothebysrealty.ca/en/property/british-columbia/region-fraser-valley/surrey-real-estate/3076580/6211-w-boundary-drive-no13/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What keeps a Surrey farm's taxes at farm rates?** BC Assessment farm classification: meet the income thresholds in Section 3 for the operation's size, with the application deadline of October 31 for the next tax year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Are the Surrey market numbers farm prices?** No. SnapStats publishes no farm category; the residential context for the board community Surrey in September 2026 was a detached median of $1,215,000 and a Condos & Townhomes median of $512,944, and farm-specific statistics are UNVERIFIED (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via the Surrey farms research file).
- **Q: What should I check on a creek-side Surrey farm parcel?** The South Newton plan area's farm pockets include fish-bearing creeks (Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek, Hyland Creek tributaries), so riparian setback rules can limit building envelopes — check City mapping and development permit areas before valuing an addition, a replacement house or the additional residence. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Surrey farm trading is thin and parcel-specific, not a board category.** The city-wide farm feed showed 3 listings at reading, and the acreage-associated Port Kells sub-area recorded 4 detached listings and 0 sales in September 2026 — with a feed that thin, the competition for a farm seller is not a deep market, and production history, water position, buildings and ALC approvals in place are what a buyer's lender and lawyer will price (Surrey farms area research file, Sections 2 and 6).
- **The downsizer's real choice in Surrey is farm-freehold versus urban-strata, with nothing in between.** There is no 55+ park on farm land, no 55+ bare-land strata and no age-restricted acreage product in any source checked — a senior who wants land keeps full freehold obligations under ALC rules; a senior who wants the age-restricted community gives up the land for one of the fifteen urban complexes. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **A suite in the barn is the classic Surrey farm mistake.** Urban Surrey allows a suite in a house and, on urban lots, a separate coach house; ALR land allows the suite only inside the principal residence, and the detached second dwelling is the size-capped additional residence. Pricing a parcel on "suite in the shop" income prices a use the ALC frame does not permit. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
