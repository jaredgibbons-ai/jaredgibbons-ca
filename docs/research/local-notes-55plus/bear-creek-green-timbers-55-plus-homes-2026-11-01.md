## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000** governs this ground. Secondary suites: one secondary suite may be permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite, and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf and (area research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/bear-creek-green-timbers.md)
- **Plans:** the current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — never cite the replaced Bylaw No. 18020 as current. Neighbouring Newton plan material records that older NCP designations were superseded by that update, so density claims for lots here should be checked against the current OCP and Zoning Bylaw 12000. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH):** Surrey allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. This applies to the detached streets of this sub-area, not to any strata complex. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Coach houses and garden suites:** all Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow a coach house or garden suite; the unit must be at least 35 m² and at most 75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR, smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access for a coach house, and one extra parking space is required. (Area research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/bear-creek-green-timbers.md)
- **Property taxes (city-wide, not area-specific):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Bear Creek Green Timbers?** None verified. No age-restricted strata, membership complex or manufactured-home park with an address in this sub-area was found in any source checked. The verified Surrey supply is in the neighbouring sub-areas listed at the end of Section 2 — Fleetwood, Queen Mary Park, West Newton and East Newton are the closest clusters. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: The feed shows lots of condos and townhouses here — aren't some of them 55+?** Not in any source checked. The named attached complexes in the feed — Kings Landing / Kings Landing II, Trails at Bear Creek Park, Carlingwood, and the Bear Creek building at 13680 84 Ave — carry no stated age restriction in the sources checked; the Bear Creek building's recorded restrictions are pets and rentals only. A listing that reads "downsizer-friendly" or "quiet" is not an age bylaw. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/bear-creek-13680-84-avenue and https://jaredgibbons.ca/bear-creek-green-timbers.html
- **Q: Is Bear Creek Villa a place I can buy into?** No. Bear Creek Villa, 8233 140 St, is a rental retirement residence offering supportive independent living beside Bear Creek Park — you rent a suite with services there; there is no strata lot to buy, and no source checked states a numeric age threshold for it. https://www.bearcreekvilla.com/en_us/neighborhood.php
- **Q: I saw a 55+ gated rancher complex on 141 Street — is that in this area?** That is Camberley Green, 10172 141 St, and listing sources file it under Whalley, not Bear Creek Green Timbers — even though it sits near the Green Timbers ground. Search for it under Whalley, and confirm the board filing on the listing itself before relying on the sub-area label. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/10172-141-street/107
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata (in a neighbouring area)?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** In a strata, ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. That trade-off is built into every BC 55+ strata since the 2022 law change confined age bylaws to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Will SkyTrain change the search here?** The Surrey Langley SkyTrain project names Green Timbers Station at 140 Street and Fraser Highway, on the western edge of Green Timbers Urban Forest, with an anticipated in-service date of 2029 — a plan fact, not operating service today, and no verified 55+ complex sits at that station. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/stations/ and https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/rapid-transit-projects/surrey-langley-skytrain
- **Q: Where is the closest hospital care?** Surrey Memorial Hospital is at 13750 96 Ave, and the Surrey-Newton Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 6830 King George Blvd. Bear Creek Villa's own site describes Surrey Memorial as a short drive from its 140 Street site. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Surrey/surrey-newton-urgent-and-primary-care-centre and https://www.bearcreekvilla.com/en_us/neighborhood.php

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **This sub-area is a hole in Surrey's 55+ map, surrounded by supply on three sides.** Surrey's verified roster — 15 strata complexes, the Sullivan Mews membership complex and 3 manufactured-home parks — clusters in South Surrey, Cloverdale, Fleetwood and the Newton sub-areas, but inside Bear Creek Green Timbers itself there is no verified 55+ ownership product at all. A "55+ in Bear Creek Green Timbers" search that returns strata listings is returning neighbouring sub-areas' stock. Source: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/surrey-condos-under-800k-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **The one 55+ complex on 141 Street is filed under a different sub-area.** Camberley Green (10172 141 St, gated 55+, about 40 rancher townhomes in the listings checked) is filed by listing sources under Whalley — a buyer who searches only this sub-area's page will miss it, and a buyer who assumes the Green Timbers name puts it here will mis-file it. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/10172-141-street/107
- **The "Bear Creek" name is used three different ways on the same few blocks.** Bear Creek the building (13680 84 Ave, general-market strata — pets and rentals restrictions only, no age rule), Bear Creek Villa (8233 140 St, rental retirement residence), and Into Action – Bear Creek (13222 87B Ave, a supportive-recovery residence, class SU, not seniors housing). Only one of the three is seniors housing, and none of them is something you can buy. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/bear-creek-13680-84-avenue and https://www.bearcreekvilla.com/en_us/neighborhood.php and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=buffer&f%5B0%5D=city%3ASurrey&f%5B1%5D=ha%3AFraser%20Health&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASupportive%20Recovery&page=2&name=&address=&order=alr_residence_type&sort=asc
- **The sub-area's detached market was one of Surrey's slowest in September 2026.** Bear Creek Green Timbers recorded a 5% detached sales ratio (5 solds on 93 listings) and an 8% Condos & Townhomes ratio (3 solds on 37 listings) — a downsizer selling a house here to buy into a neighbouring 55+ complex is selling into that thin detached market, and medians are published only at community level (Surrey detached $1,215,000; attached $512,944) (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file).
- **The coming station is named for the forest, not for a housing cluster.** Green Timbers Station (140 Street and Fraser Highway, anticipated 2029) is described by the project as serving the Jim Pattison Outpatient Care and Surgery Centre, the BC RCMP E-Divisional Headquarters and Surrey Memorial Hospital — health and civic destinations, on the forest's western edge — not a new seniors project, and no new age-restricted strata project inside this sub-area appears in any source checked. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/stations/
- **The City and the board draw this ground differently, and both are "right."** The City places Bear Creek Park in Whalley and does not list Bear Creek Green Timbers among its seven communities at all; the board treats it as a single Surrey sub-area. School, plan and tax claims quoted from a City page will use City community names that do not match the listing feed's label. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
