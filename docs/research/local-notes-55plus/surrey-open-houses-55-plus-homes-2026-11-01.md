# Surrey (City-Wide) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Scope note:** this file covers the City of Surrey as a whole, for the city-wide slug. Complexes that also have their own sub-area 55+ notes (Panorama Ridge, Sunnyside Park, East Newton, West Newton) are listed here once, at city level, with their community named — the detail lives in those files and is not repeated in full.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The City of Surrey names seven communities: City Centre, Cloverdale, Fleetwood, Guildford, Newton, South Surrey and Whalley. A city-wide 55+ search has to be run community by community — there is no single Surrey 55+ district. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- Where the verified 55+ stock concentrates, by community: **South Surrey** holds the largest cluster (the 152 Street corridor in Sunnyside Park and King George Corridor, plus Elgin Chantrell); **Newton** holds the townhouse complexes at Boundary Park and on 66A Avenue and the two gated manufactured-home parks on 134 Street and 156 Street; **Cloverdale** holds Manor Ridge on 192 Street; **Fleetwood** holds the Westwood Court manufactured-home park as well as general townhouse stock with no verified 55+ strata condo building in this pass. **City Centre, Whalley and Guildford:** no 55+ strata complex was verified in any source checked in this pass — UNCONFIRMED as 55+ concentrations; the SkyTrain-corridor condo stock there is general-market in the listings checked. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- The manufactured-home parks are a Surrey-wide pattern of their own: the verified 55+ parks sit on the Newton/Fleetwood flats (134 Street and 156 Street), on pad-rent land — you own the home, you rent the pad. Sources: Section 2 below.
- Main corridors that tie the clusters together: King George Boulevard and 152 Street (South Surrey / Newton), Highway 10 (Newton / Cloverdale), Fraser Highway (Fleetwood / Cloverdale, with the future Surrey–Langley SkyTrain on this corridor in City planning material), and 192 Street (Cloverdale). https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- School District 36 (Surrey) serves the whole city; catchments are address-based through the SD36 School Locator, and schools are elementary Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary Grades 8 to 12. https://www.surreyschools.ca/grandviewheights
- Health care: Surrey Memorial Hospital, 13750 96 Ave (City Centre), serves most of the city; Peace Arch Hospital, 15521 Russell Avenue, White Rock, serves South Surrey; the Surrey-Newton Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 6830 King George Blvd. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Surrey/surrey-newton-urgent-and-primary-care-centre and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/white-rock/peace-arch-hospital
- Transit spine: SkyTrain (Expo Line) serves City Centre/Whalley only; the R1 RapidBus runs on King George Boulevard through Newton to South Surrey; local buses plus the Newton and Fleetwood grids serve the 55+ clusters in Section 2 — none of the verified Newton, Fleetwood, Cloverdale or South Surrey 55+ complexes is on a SkyTrain line. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2026-schedules/april/surrey-p170.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home parks run on a different statute.** Pad tenancies in BC manufactured-home parks are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA) — the law that applies where a tenant rents a pad site but owns the manufactured home; where both the pad and the dwelling are rented, the Residential Tenancy Act applies instead. Age rules in a park are park rules/policies, not strata bylaws — get them in writing from the park, with the tenancy agreement. https://www.keyhomes.ca/listings/55-mobile-home-park-british-columbia and http://logancrowder.ca/guides/mhp-redevelopment-guide.pdf
- **Surrey's manufactured-home park redevelopment policy:** the City's own policy brochure states that if you own your manufactured home and pay pad rent, the MHPTA gives you rights; the property owner must give 12 months' notice of a final moving date using a Notice to End Tenancy, which can only be given after the owner receives all their permits, and the City has a Manufactured Home Park Policy (which the brochure states is not a law, but guidelines Council uses) requiring an affordable housing program for residents when a park is redeveloped. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf
- **Membership complexes are neither strata nor freehold purchase in the usual sense.** Sullivan Mews' own listings state it is a membership, not a strata, and that mortgages are not possible — financing, resale and transfer rules come from the membership documents, not the Strata Property Act. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3058951-15020-66a-avenue-49-surrey-bc-v3s-2a5/brochure
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000** governs the whole city, including its small-scale multi-unit housing zones RA to R6: up to 3 units on lots under 280 sq m, up to 4 units on lots over 280 sq m, and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. This affects detached streets around the 55+ complexes, not the strata / membership / park lots inside them. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Plans:** Surrey 2050 is the current Official Community Plan framework; older neighbourhood concept plans have been rescinded into it in areas such as South Newton — a seller quoting an old NCP designation may be quoting a document no longer in effect. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **Property taxes (city-wide):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which Surrey community should I search first for 55+ homes?** South Surrey, for depth: Peninsula Village, Semiahmoo Estates, Bishop's Green, St. Martin's Lane, the 13965 16 Ave building and The Crescent are all there. Newton is next (the Boundary Park trio and Sullivan Mews, plus the Westwood Estates park), then Cloverdale (Manor Ridge) and Fleetwood (Westwood Court park). No 55+ strata complex was verified in City Centre, Whalley or Guildford in this pass. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: What is the cheapest-form 55+ living in Surrey — and what is the catch?** The manufactured-home parks (Westwood Court, Fleetwood; Westwood Estates, Newton) are the detached, single-level form at park prices in the listings checked — the catch is tenure: you own the home and rent the pad monthly under the MHPTA, the pad rent in listings is unit- and date-specific (two Westwood Estates listings checked showed $822/mo and $935/mo), and a park redevelopment runs through the City's manufactured-home park policy and a 12-month notice regime, not strata law. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/8670-156-street/9 and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf
- **Q: Is Sullivan Mews a strata? Can I get a mortgage on it?** No, and its own listings say so: "This is not Strata. It is a membership therefore not mortgages possible," with a 55+ age restriction, no smoking, 1 small pet and a maximum of 2 occupants in the listing checked. Read the membership documents before pricing it against strata bungalows. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3058951-15020-66a-avenue-49-surrey-bc-v3s-2a5/brochure
- **Q: Do both of us have to be 55?** It varies by complex in its own words: Lakewood Heights listings state only one occupant needs to meet the age requirement, Manor Ridge listings state 1 occupant must be 55+, and Peninsula Village listings state one person 55+. Provincially, a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident is exempt in any case — confirm the complex's bylaw or park rule in its own documents. https://sothebysrealty.ca/en/property/british-columbia/region-fraser-valley/surrey-real-estate/3076580/6211-w-boundary-drive-no13/ and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ complex as an investment or for a parent?** In a strata, ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside; the Province states under-55s can own but may not be able to live in the unit. In a manufactured-home park or a membership complex, the equivalent question is answered by the park rules / membership documents, not the Strata Property Act — ask there, in writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit?** In a strata, long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022; the tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. In the parks, rental rules are park rules — the sources checked do not state a Westwood Court or Westwood Estates rental rule, so that is UNCONFIRMED until the park office states it in writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** It is complex by complex across the city: Manor Ridge listings allow 1 pet with restrictions; Peninsula Village listings state pets ok (one listing: 2 pets, size limit stated in the listing); Sullivan Mews listings allow 1 small pet; Westwood Estates listings allow large dogs with park approval, no vicious breeds. There is no Surrey-wide pet rule for 55+ housing. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8 and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3114342-60-8254-134-street-surrey-british-columbia-v3w6m2-218800/brochure
- **Q: Where are the hospitals for each cluster?** Surrey Memorial Hospital (13750 96 Ave) for the Newton, Fleetwood, Cloverdale, Guildford and City Centre clusters; Peace Arch Hospital (15521 Russell Ave, White Rock) for the South Surrey cluster; and the Surrey-Newton Urgent and Primary Care Centre at 6830 King George Blvd sits between the Newton and South Surrey clusters. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Surrey/surrey-newton-urgent-and-primary-care-centre and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/white-rock/peace-arch-hospital
- **Q: Does gated mean 55+?** No. Woodgrove (2588 152 St) is marketed as a quiet gated community with no age restriction stated anywhere checked, while Peninsula Village, Bishop's Green and the two Westwood parks are both gated **and** age-restricted in their listings. The gate is a form; the age bylaw or park rule is the verification. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/2588-152-street/61
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** In a strata, mechanically yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. In the parks and the membership complex, the resale pool is set by the park's / membership's own transfer and age rules, which must be read in those documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Surrey's 55+ supply comes in three different legal forms, and a city-wide search mixes them without labelling them.** Strata (Peninsula Village, Manor Ridge, the Lakewood complexes), membership (Sullivan Mews — no strata lot, no conventional mortgage per its own listings), and pad-rent manufactured homes (Westwood Court, Westwood Estates — MHPTA, not strata law). The form decides the financing, the documents and the exit, before the floor plan matters. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3058951-15020-66a-avenue-49-surrey-bc-v3s-2a5/brochure and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf
- **One short Newton drive holds three 55+ complexes filed under three different sub-areas.** Lakewood Place (6061) and Lakewood Heights (6211) on W Boundary Drive are filed under Panorama Ridge, while Lakeview Estates (6090), between them, is filed under West Newton — a buyer searching one sub-area's page misses a third of the Boundary Park cluster. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6090-w-boundary-drive/104
- **Two Surrey parks share almost the same name, in two different communities.** Westwood **Court** is the Fleetwood park at 8670 156 Street; Westwood **Estates** is the Newton park on 134 Street (whose own listings disagree on 8224 vs 8254 as the civic address). A listing alert set on one name does not cover the other park. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/8670-156-street/9 and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3121759-103-8224-134-street-surrey-british-columbia-v3w4v1-249800/brochure
- **The city-wide page's own topic — open houses — is where 55+ stock actually surfaces.** Because the verified complexes are small and turn over rarely, a Surrey open-house run grouped by the City's seven communities (City Centre, Cloverdale, Fleetwood, Guildford, Newton, South Surrey, Whalley) is the practical way buyers catch a Boundary Park, Manor Ridge or 152 Street listing the weekend it opens; the open-house feed itself carries no 55+ filter in any source checked — UNCONFIRMED as a feed feature, so the age check still has to be done complex by complex from Section 2. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities and https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-open-houses.html
- **Surrey's newest housing growth is not adding verified 55+ strata supply.** The complexes in Section 2 are 1989–2000 stock in the listings checked (Manor Ridge 1989, Peninsula Village circa 1991–1993, Sullivan Mews and the Lakewood complexes 1990s, The Crescent 2000) — no new age-restricted strata project anywhere in the city appears in any source checked in this pass, so Surrey's 55+ ownership supply is a fixed, aging pool that turns over listing by listing. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 above.
- **A care-sounding name in Newton is a recovery residence, not seniors housing.** Cedars at Panorama Ridge (6811 135A St) is class SU (supportive recovery) in the provincial Assisted Living Registry — Surrey's clearest example of why a name, a directory category or "seniors" marketing language is never a substitute for the registry class or the filed bylaw. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ASurrey&address=&name=&page=2&order=alr_residence_type&sort=desc
