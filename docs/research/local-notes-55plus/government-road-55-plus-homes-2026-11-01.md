## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Government Road is the residential and light-industrial corridor in north-east Burnaby running south of Lougheed Highway, between the Lougheed Town Centre / Cameron lands to the east and Burnaby Lake and the Sperling side to the west. It is named for Government Road itself and anchored by École Seaforth Elementary at 7881 Government Rd. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041

The confirmed anchors are Government Road itself, Seaforth Elementary on it, Stoney Creek Community Elementary at 2740 Beaverbrook Cres on the area's east side, and Burnaby Lake Regional Park at its west and south-west side. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **City zoning: the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026** is the current bylaw — adopted June 9, 2026 and in effect July 1, 2026. (The old 1965 zoning bylaw is not current; do not cite it as such.) https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/bylaws/zoning-bylaw
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing (R1 SSMUH District, in place since June 2024):** R1 house lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. Government Road also carries industrial and office (Lake City) parcels that are not R1 — confirm the zone parcel by parcel before pricing any corridor lot on housing rules. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Plans:** the Lochdale Urban Village Community Plan, for the urban village north of the area across Lougheed Highway, was adopted by Council on July 25, 2022 — it is not this sub-area's plan. The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- **Property taxes:** City of Burnaby property taxes for 2026 were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date. Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan, about $61 for the average home. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
- **Utilities:** Burnaby's 2026 residential utility notice covers annual water, annual sewer use, a cross-connection device charge where applicable, and a residential garbage toter fee; the detached single-family dwelling water charge is $652.33, rates carry a 5% discount if paid by the due date, and for single-family homes sewer is billed as a sewer parcel tax on the property tax notice. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing.** The Housing Registry and the Zone 5 (Burnaby and New Westminster) seniors directory are the application route for non-profit seniors buildings elsewhere in the city; the directory edition located for this research is dated January 2021, and it lists nothing in this sub-area. https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ condo or townhouse I can buy in Government Road?** No verified one. The named complexes on the corridor — Sandlewood on Government Street and Discovery Place on Carrigan Court — are general-market, with no age restriction stated in any source checked. A buyer who needs the 55+ bylaw itself has to look outside this sub-area. https://vancouvernashdom.com/burnaby-north-government-road-apartments/SearchResults.form/?_pg=2 and https://vancouvernashdom.com/burnaby-north-townhouses-for-sale-above-700000/SearchResults.form/?_pg=2
- **Q: Is 115 Place a 55+ co-op?** No. BC Housing's Zone 5 directory (January 2021) notes "50 & older" for its towers — not 55+ — the co-op is a mixed co-op community preserved as affordable housing, and Cardston Court is at the Lougheed Town Centre / Cameron side, east of this sub-area. https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076 and https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/province-finances-affordable-co-op-housing-in-burnaby
- **Q: Where is the seniors centre for this side of Burnaby?** The Temporary Cameron 55+ Centre, 110-9855 Austin Avenue inside The City of Lougheed Shopping Centre — one of 4 Burnaby centres specializing in programs for adults 55+, with a seniors lounge and the Seniors' Society programs, open while the Cameron complex is rebuilt. It is recreation and library services, not housing. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/cameron-recreation-complex
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules exempt a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St — across the city to the west. No hospital or urgent and primary care centre is located inside this sub-area. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day without a car?** Route 110 runs on Government Road itself, linking Lougheed Station and Production Way–University Station to the east with Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station and Metrotown Station to the west — two Millennium Line station groups on one bus route. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt110.pdf
- **Q: Can a Government Road house lot add units?** If the lot is R1, yes — 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 on larger lots, and 6 on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, with stratification allowed. Corridor parcels in industrial or office zones follow their own zone, so confirm the parcel's zone first. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: How did detached homes do here in September 2026?** None sold — 24 detached listings and 0 detached solds, so the detached sales ratio is not applicable for the month; the attached side recorded 23 listings and 3 solds (13%). Sub-area median prices: UNVERIFIED — SnapStats publishes medians at community level only. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The civic centre is mid-rebuild, and the replacement is big.** The old Cameron Community Centre and Library closed August 18, 2024; the replacement carries an estimated total construction cost of about $267 million, with a new library, aquatic facility, gymnasium, weight room, indoor running track and dedicated seniors spaces in the 213,000 sq ft mass-timber building, whose timber structure reached topping-off during construction. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2024-07-23/council-approves-award-contract-advance-construction-replacement-cameron and https://www.grahambuilds.com/news/mass-timber-construction-topping-off-cameron-community-centre-and-library-burnaby-bc/
- **The temporary centre opened September 3, 2024.** The Temporary Cameron Community Centre and Library, inside The City of Lougheed Shopping Centre (9855 Austin Ave), opened its doors on September 3, 2024, with the 55+ seniors lounge and programs, and stays open until the new facility does. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2024-09-03/temporary-cameron-community-centre-now-open
- **The park at the west end just got a 25-year guarantee.** The City of Burnaby and Metro Vancouver signed a 25-year lease agreement for Burnaby Lake Regional Park (announced February 5, 2025), covering the 360-acre park Metro Vancouver has funded and managed since 1978 — the green edge of this sub-area is secured, not awaiting a land decision. http://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-02-05/city-burnaby-and-metro-vancouver-sign-burnaby-lake-regional-park
- **The zoning bylaw itself is new.** The Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026. Any document still citing the 1965 bylaw as current is out of date. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **The city plan above it is new too.** The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan was adopted December 9, 2025 and replaces the 1998 OCP; the neighbouring Lochdale Urban Village plan across Lougheed was adopted July 25, 2022. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- **2026 tax dates have already passed.** Burnaby's 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date; the 2026–2030 Financial Plan's increase was 2.9%, about $61 for the average home. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
