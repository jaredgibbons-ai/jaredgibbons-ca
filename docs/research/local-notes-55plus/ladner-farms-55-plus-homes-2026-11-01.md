# Ladner Farms (Delta) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Plain finding up front: the 55+ angle on Ladner farm and ALR land is thin, and this file says so with sources. No 55+ strata, manufactured-home park, co-operative, rental or care complex was verified on Ladner farm land or in the Ladner Rural / East Delta farm belt. What a 55+ farm buyer actually faces here is ALR housing law — house-size caps and a tightly limited second dwelling — not an age-restricted community. Ladner's verified 55+ complexes are all in the village, listed in Section 2 for contrast.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **The strata age-bylaw rules, for the record — with no local strata to apply them to.** Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; age bylaws below 55 invalid since Nov 24, 2022, under Bill 44); the bylaw restricts residency, not ownership; Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19; and residential rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, while short-term restrictions remain allowed. A farm buyer gets none of this machinery, because a farm purchase is freehold land, not a strata lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **ALR housing caps are the rules that actually bind a 55+ farm buyer.** Land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **The second-dwelling rule is the ageing-in-place rule on a farm.** The ALC permits one additional residence without an ALC application where its conditions are met — detached, with a total floor area of 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and Delta's bylaws must also permit it. That is the legal route to a caregiver's or family member's dwelling on a Ladner farm; anything larger needs an ALC application. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Delta zoning for the belt: the A1 Agriculture zone** of the Delta Zoning Bylaw (Part 10, Agriculture Zones, as amended by Bylaw 8285, 2023) is the zone intended for farming and related uses; the OCP designates the same land Agriculture (A) under Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), s. 1.5.1. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Farm classification is a continuing obligation, not a one-time label.** BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income requirements at farm gate prices are $2,500 if the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha, and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha. A downsizing buyer who stops producing can lose the classification and its tax treatment. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Water and septic are diligence items with legal teeth:** under the Water Sustainability Act, domestic groundwater use does not need a licence, but non-domestic use including irrigation requires a water licence, and well records are searched in the provincial GWELLS registry; a home not on community sewer must use an onsite system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, with only an Authorized Person designing, installing or inspecting it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Property taxes:** Delta's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026; the Province's regular property tax deferment program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities). https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ farm community or seniors farm complex in Ladner?** No — none was verified on any source. Farm parcels are freehold, strata farm product is not a Delta pattern, and ALR residences cannot be stratified. Ladner's verified 55+ complexes (Delta Green, South Pointe) are in the village. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: I'm 55+ and buying a farm to downsize onto. What limits the house I can build?** On ALR land, a principal residence built without an ALC application is capped at 500 m² total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. Confirm the parcel's ALR status on the ALC maps before designing anything. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can my adult child or a caregiver live in a second dwelling on the farm?** The ALC permits one additional residence without an application where its size rules are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels, detached — and Delta's bylaws must also permit it. There is no strata-style caregiver exemption here because there is no strata; the land rules are the whole answer. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: If I stop farming, do I keep farm-class taxes?** Not automatically: BC Assessment requires a producing farm to keep meeting minimum gross farm income thresholds that scale with the operation's size ($2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that; $10,000 under 0.8 ha). Plan the production, or lease the land to someone who produces, before assuming the classification survives. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Does Westham Island have any seniors housing?** None was verified in any source — Westham Island is farm land within the Ladner Rural side of this topic, and no 55+ complex, park or care home with a Westham Island address appears in the sources checked. The island's housing is farm freehold. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md
- **Q: Where is the hospital for a farm-belt address?** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, in Ladner village — Fraser Health's community hospital for Delta with a 24/7 emergency department. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes at 55+ on a farm property?** The Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. Farm classification is a separate BC Assessment matter and is not affected by deferment. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
- **Q: Is the farm belt flood-protected?** Delta is the diking authority and operates and maintains over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure — the low-lying farm belt sits behind that system, and lot-level flood construction requirements still have to be checked parcel by parcel. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The topic is wider than the village name suggests.** The live feed for this page also carried East Delta parcels on Ladner Trunk Road under the Agri-Business label — a buyer searching "Ladner" gets the whole Ladner-side farm belt (Ladner Rural plus East Delta plus Westham Island), not just land near the village. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md
- **Ladner's newest 55+ ownership building is in the village, and it is recent.** Delta Green at 4686 51 Street was built in 2021 — 24 strata units, plan EPS8064 — and its 2018 public hearing record shows the 55+ category was in the proposal from the start. The village, not the belt, is where Delta approves age-restricted strata. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/179807/
- **Delta's 2024 OCP designates the belt Agriculture (A) in a single consolidated plan.** Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) superseded the old OCP Bylaw No. 3950 and its schedules; farm land's designation purpose is stated as maintaining agricultural lands (s. 1.5.1), with Agriculture policies at s. 2.6. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Seniors congregate housing is a named principal use in Delta's RA6 apartment zone** — the zoning route for future purpose-built seniors buildings along Delta's arterial and collector corridors, added to Zoning Bylaw No. 7600. It applies to corridor apartment land, not to the A1 farm belt. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
