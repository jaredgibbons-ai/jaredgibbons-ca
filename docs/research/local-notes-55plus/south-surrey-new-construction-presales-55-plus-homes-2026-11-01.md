## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **A presale has no strata bylaws in force yet in the way a completed strata does — the disclosure statement governs.** As the live page states it (site copy, attributed there to BCFSA presales information): under BC's Real Estate Development Marketing Act the developer must give the buyer a disclosure statement before signing, and the buyer can cancel by written notice within 7 days. Those specifics were not opened on a BCFSA or BC Laws page in the research pass: verify the current REDMA rules on BCFSA's site before relying on any summary. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-new-construction-presales.html and https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **If a project were marketed as 55+, the restriction would have to survive into the registered bylaws.** In a completed BC strata, only a 55+ age bylaw is legal (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; sub-55 bylaws invalid since Nov 24, 2022) — so any presale age promise worth relying on must appear in the disclosure statement's proposed bylaws, not in a brochure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Strata age bylaws restrict who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it,** with Regulation 7.01 exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers, a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19 (expanded May 1, 2023). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** in strata since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **New-home tax frame as the live page states it (site copy only, UNVERIFIED on an official source in the research pass):** a 2-5-10 warranty (2 years materials and labour, 5 years building envelope, 10 years structure), the BC Newly Built Home Exemption (no property transfer tax on a qualifying new principal residence up to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000) and the federal First-Time Home Buyers' GST Rebate (up to 100% of the 5% GST on a new home up to $1 million, maximum $50,000). Verify each against the Province and the Canada Revenue Agency for a specific purchase. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-new-construction-presales.html
- **General property transfer tax rates (the frame behind the exemptions):** 1% up to and including $200,000, 2% on the portion above $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Surrey small-scale stock rules that touch the "new construction" edge:** SSMUH allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; a secondary suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Market-data limit:** SnapStats does not split new construction or presales from resale, and the live page itself states FVREB does not report new construction separately — any presale-only market figure is UNVERIFIED. The whole-market context (September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via the research file) is 593 Condos & Townhomes listings at a $700,000 combined median. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-new-construction-presales.md

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a brand-new 55+ presale I can buy in South Surrey?** Not on the verified record: no developer source checked for Harlowe, Calais, Rockford or any other in-scope project states a 55+ age restriction. Have a lawyer read the disclosure statement inside that window and verify the current REDMA rules with BCFSA — the research pass did not open those rules on an official page, so they are carried here as site copy only. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-new-construction-presales.html
- **Q: Can I plan a move around "estimated completion early 2028"?** No. That wording is the developer's estimate in Harlowe's listing text; the disclosure statement's date range and amendment history govern.
