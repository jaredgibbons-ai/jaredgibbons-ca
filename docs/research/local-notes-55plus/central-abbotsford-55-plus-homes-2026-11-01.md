# Central Abbotsford — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Central Abbotsford" is the board's sub-area name; the City does not use it as an official neighbourhood name. The City plans that cover the core are the City Centre Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 2913-2019) and the Historic Downtown Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 2920-2019). https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/community-planning/neighbourhood-plans
- The City's own description of the core: a roughly rectangular City Centre bordered approximately by Peardonville Road and Mill Lake to the south, Ware Street, Mill Lake Road and Horn Street to the east, Parkview Street and Tims Street to the west, and a line roughly parallel to George Ferguson Way and Maclure Road to the north, centred on South Fraser Way. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/HDNP%2BCommercial%2BMarket%2BStudy.pdf
- Abbotsford West is west of here, Abbotsford East to the east, Poplar to the northwest. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/community-planning/neighbourhood-plans
- Where the 55+ stock sits: two pockets — the Gladwin Road / Mill Lake edge (Regency Park, Summerset Place) and the George Ferguson Way–McCallum Road corridor (Horizon Place, Cloud Croft Manor, Oakhurst Park, Alameda Court) — plus the Menno Place care campus around Marshall Road and Primrose Street (Section 2). This sub-area also holds the city's deepest general attached stock: 256 attached listings and 35 attached sales in September 2026, the most of any Abbotsford sub-area (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report).
- The hospital is at this sub-area's east edge: Abbotsford Regional Hospital and Cancer Centre, 32900 Marshall Rd; the Abbotsford Public Health Unit is at 104-34194 Marshall Road. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Abbotsford/abbotsford-regional-hospital-and-cancer-centre
- Transit: Bourquin Exchange is the central area's BC Transit hub; Central Fraser Valley routes serving Abbotsford include 1 (Highstreet/UFV), 2 (Highstreet/McMillan), 5 (Clearbrook/Bourquin via Hospital), 12 (UFV/Bourquin) and 31 (Abbotsford/Mission). Abbotsford is BC Transit territory — no SkyTrain or West Coast Express station in the city. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf
- Schools are SD34; locator runs for central addresses return the Abbotsford Senior family — 33130 Bevan Ave returns Godson Elementary → Abbotsford Middle → Abbotsford Senior Secondary, and 32161 Dormick Avenue returns Dormick Park Elementary → Abbotsford Middle → Abbotsford Senior Secondary (SD34 School Locator, run 2026-11-01). Catchments are address-specific; run the exact address before relying on one. https://www.abbyschools.ca/policy-18-school-catchment-areas

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **BC law now permits exactly one kind of strata age bylaw: 55+.** Amendments to the Strata Property Act (ss. 123.1/123.2, brought in by Bill 44) took effect Nov 24, 2022; a strata may restrict residents to people 55 and older, and every younger age bylaw (19+, 40+, 45+) became invalid the same day. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **It is a residency rule, not an ownership rule.** Provincial guidance is explicit that someone under 55 may own a lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in it; the bylaw bites on who occupies the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Statutory exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** a person who was already living in the unit when the bylaw was adopted keeps the right to stay (legacy); a live-in caregiver is exempt; and since the May 1, 2023 expansion, so are a younger spouse or partner, a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Strata rental bans are gone.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may enforce a residential rental-restriction bylaw — they are void without any vote or filing — although a strata can still prohibit or limit short-term rentals, and any tenant in a building like Regency Park must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Georgian Park is tenancy law, not strata law.** Manufactured home park tenancies fall under BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act: the resident owns the home and rents the pad, pad rent may be raised only once in 12 months on at least three full months' written notice, and the raise is capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026) plus a proportional share of changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Two neighbourhood plans steer what gets built around the 55+ stock.** The City Centre Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 2913-2019) and Historic Downtown Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 2920-2019) sit under the Abbotsforward 2050 OCP; the OCP framework calls for a new mixed-use centre between the hospital and Mill Lake Park and a Primary Transit Corridor on South Fraser Way between Clearbrook and McCallum Road — the corridor most of this sub-area's 55+ buildings sit on or near. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea and https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/city-begins-implementing-updated-official-community-plan
- **Small-scale multi-unit rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014, s.140):** the bylaw's small-scale provision allows 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on lots over 280 m² in the stated conditions, and s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings — relevant to a senior selling a central detached house, where the infill value is part of the price conversation. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **The City's zoning vocabulary for seniors projects:** the Zoning Bylaw framework defines "Congregate Housing" as an independent living setting with common dining and amenity areas (added by Bylaw 3249-2022) — the use category under which supportive seniors projects are processed. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-abbotsford-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/7217/8514/3396/d9117e5011287c79b090adc71e32c234_Appendix_B_-_Draft_ZB_Amendments.pdf
- **Property taxes:** Abbotsford taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: At Regency Park, does every owner have to be 55?** No — the listing data for both towers words it as a one-owner rule: "One owner must be 55+" at 3150 Gladwin and "55+ age (for 1 owner)" at Tower 2, 3170 Gladwin. That also lines up with the provincial exemption for a younger spouse or partner. Confirm the wording in the registered bylaws for the specific tower. https://www.zolo.ca/abbotsford-real-estate/3150-gladwin-road/201 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm 52. Can I buy in Horizon Place and move in when I turn 55?** You can buy now — the age bylaw restricts residing, not owning. You could not live in the unit until you meet the bylaw or fit an exemption (for example, as the spouse or partner of a resident who qualifies). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in one of these buildings?** For a long-term tenancy, yes: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void across BC since Nov 24, 2022. Your tenant still has to meet the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and bylaws can still restrict short-term rentals. Georgian Park is different — it is a manufactured home park whose own rules, per listings, do not allow rentals of the homes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Which of the central buildings allow a pet?** From listing sources: Cloud Croft Manor allows one lap-sized dog or two cats; Willband Creek Estates is described as pet friendly; Parkside Place allows one cat and no dogs; Regency Park Tower 2 records no pets. Summerset Place is unresolved — listing sources conflict — so treat pets there as UNCONFIRMED until the registered bylaws are read. Sources: the building entries in Section 2.
- **Q: In Georgian Park, what am I buying and what do I pay monthly?** You buy the manufactured home and rent the pad under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act — one listing quoted $782/month pad rent for a specific home (listing-stated; confirm in the tenancy documents). Pad rent can only be raised once a year, on three full months' notice, within the provincial cap (2.3% for 2026) plus the proportional levy/utility amount. https://www.zolo.ca/abbotsford-real-estate/3300-horn-street/55 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Is there RV parking anywhere in the central 55+ stock?** Yes — Oakhurst Park on George Ferguson Way lists RV parking among its amenities, along with a clubhouse. Confirm stall availability and any extra charge with the strata; availability is not stated in the sources. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3085200-61-32959-george-ferguson-way-abbotsford-bc-v2s-7w9
- **Q: How close is the 55+ stock to the hospital, really?** The Marshall Road care cluster (Menno Hospital, The Mayfair) sits on the same corridor as Abbotsford Regional Hospital at 32900 Marshall Rd, at the sub-area's east edge; the Mill Lake buildings (Regency Park, Summerset Place) are on the west side of the sub-area, across town from the hospital by central-Abbotsford standards. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Abbotsford/abbotsford-regional-hospital-and-cancer-centre
- **Q: What happens to my resale buyer pool in a 55+ building?** It narrows by design: the next owner must be able to reside under the bylaw — 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, a caregiver, or a legacy case — or purchase without living there. Every BC 55+ strata carries that trade since the 2022 amendments. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: If I buy a strata unit now and need care later, what's in this sub-area?** The Menno Place campus covers the steps in one location — independent supportive living (Menno Pavilion, Primrose Gardens), assisted living (Menno Assisted Living, Terrace East) and residential care (Menno Home, Menno Hospital) — and The Mayfair on Marshall Road offers independent living rental; publicly subsidized beds at Cottage and Worthington Pavilions are being replaced by the new Fraser Health long-term care community at 1919 Jackson Street (in Poplar), expected complete in 2027. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/ABB/260226/ABBNZ260226.pdf and https://thebeat.fraserhealth.ca/news/2023/Jun/new-long-term-care-community-coming-to-abbotsford
- **Q: What transit serves the central buildings day to day?** Bourquin Exchange is the hub; BC Transit Central Fraser Valley routes through the central area include 5 (Clearbrook/Bourquin via Hospital) and 12 (UFV/Bourquin), and route 31 links Abbotsford to Mission. There is no SkyTrain or West Coast Express in Abbotsford. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Two of the "Central Abbotsford" names buyers search for are not actually in this sub-area's verified stock.** Central Heights Manor on King Road is routinely labelled Central Abbotsford in feeds but belongs to Poplar, and Parkside Place on Murray Avenue is filed here only because the building directory says Central while some feeds say West — sub-area labels in listing data are inconsistent, and the registered strata plan is the reliable identifier (Parkside Place is LMS118; Horizon Place is NWS2684; Oakhurst Park is NWS1894). https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/buildings/abbotsford/poplar and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/buildings/abbotsford/central-abbotsford?sortby=status_sync%7Cdesc&page=4
- **The subsidized care beds beside the hospital are scheduled to move.** Fraser Health's new long-term care community at 1919 Jackson Street (Poplar) will replace the 109 beds at Cottage and Worthington Pavilion and add 91 more; construction was expected to begin in 2025 with completion in 2027. A senior counting on the Cottage Lane pavilions as the long-term plan should know the replacement is already in procurement/construction. https://thebeat.fraserhealth.ca/news/2023/Jun/new-long-term-care-community-coming-to-abbotsford
- **Cloud Croft Manor's build year disagrees with itself across sources.** Directories say 1976; MLS listings say 1975. It is a small 18-unit building from the mid-1970s either way — the oldest verified 55+ strata in this sub-area — but a depreciation report and Form B, not a listing sheet, settle which year the strata uses. https://www.rew.ca/buildings/areas/abbotsford-bc/page/3
- **Alameda Court has two civic addresses in listings — 2277 and 2279 McCallum Rd.** Buyers searching only one number will miss listings filed under the other. https://www.rodfriesen.com/55-plus-condos-in-abbotsford
- **The central area is where Abbotsford's attached market actually turns over.** Central posted the city's fastest attached sales ratio (14%) and largest attached sales count (35) in September 2026, with 256 attached listings — deep selection and real turnover at the same time, which is the context for pricing any of the Section 2 buildings (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report).
- **Mill Lake is the dividing amenity.** The Gladwin Road/Ware Street buildings (Regency Park's two towers, Summerset Place) sell against Mill Lake Park access from Bevan Ave and Emerson St and the Centennial Outdoor Pool at 2310 Emerson St, while the George Ferguson Way buildings sell against the Bourquin Exchange and Clearbrook Library at 32320 George Ferguson Way — two different daily-life pitches inside one sub-area. https://www.abbotsford.ca/parks-playgrounds and https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/
- **The OCP's growth arrow points at the hospital–Mill Lake gap.** Abbotsforward 2050 plans a new mixed-use centre between the hospital and Mill Lake Park, so the blocks around Cottage Lane and Marshall Road — where The Mayfair and the subsidized pavilions already sit — are the sub-area's designated growth seam, not a quiet backwater. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/city-begins-implementing-updated-official-community-plan
