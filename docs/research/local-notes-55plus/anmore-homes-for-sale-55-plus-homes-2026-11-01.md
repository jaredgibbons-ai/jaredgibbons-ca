# Anmore — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Plain finding up front: Anmore has ZERO verified 55+ ownership stock. No 55+ strata, manufactured-home park, co-operative, life lease, purpose-built 55+ rental, retirement residence, assisted-living residence or subsidized seniors building inside the Village of Anmore was verified on any source checked in this pass — including the research file, BC's Assisted Living Registry searches, and BC Housing seniors-program material. Anmore is a village municipality of detached and estate housing north of Port Moody, and its board statistics are grouped inside the Port Moody SnapStats community, expressly NOT Anmore figures. A senior who needs a 55+ complex, care or subsidized housing will be looking in Port Moody, Coquitlam or Port Coquitlam, not in the village. What Anmore does offer a 55+ buyer is a detached house that can be adapted — and, since mid-2024, a secondary suite and/or coach house on many lots for a caregiver or family member.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. In Anmore this is mostly theoretical — no 55+ strata was verified — but it governs any small strata a buyer does find: ask for the registered bylaw, not the listing word "adult-oriented." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; exemptions attach to a "specified resident." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed; live-in caregivers; and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw; stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Village zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 568-2017** (consolidated August 2022 in the Village's zoning checklist) is the Anmore zoning bylaw cited in Village documents; the Village describes its Zoning Bylaw as the primary bylaw regulating land use, density and building size and siting. https://anmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Checklist-Zoning-INF.pdf and https://anmore.com/building-development/community-planning/
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 532-2014**, now being amended — draft Amendment Bylaw No. 726-2025 addresses provincial housing legislation and the Metro 2050 regional context statement; the Village states planning for a new OCP is expected to begin in 2027. Treat 726-2025 as draft/amendment material, not as a settled new OCP, until adoption is confirmed: adoption status UNCONFIRMED in this pass. https://anmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Draft-Official-Community-Plan-Bylaw-Amendment-No.-726-2025.pdf and https://anmore.com/building-development/community-planning/official-community-plan/official-community-plan-update/
- **SSMUH / suites and coach houses — the aging-in-place rule that actually applies in Anmore:** the Village's OCP amendment material states that, as required by the Province and as a result of an amendment to the Anmore Zoning Bylaw, SSMUH in the form of secondary suites is now permitted on single-family lots across the Village that previously allowed only single-detached residential, and that properties can have between two to three units in various configurations depending on lot size with the addition of a coach house, also permitted in many residential zones. https://anmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Draft-Official-Community-Plan-Bylaw-Amendment-No.-726-2025.pdf
- **Suite/coach size caps in the Village checklist:** a secondary suite is capped at 90 m² or 40% of the floor area of the principal dwelling; a coach house is capped at 100 m² on parcels under 4,047 m² and 130 m² on parcels over 4,047 m²; combined suite + coach gross floor area is stated at 180 m². Confirm the specific lot against the current consolidated bylaw — the checklist states it is a convenience document, not the bylaw. https://anmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Checklist-Zoning-INF.pdf
- **Tax relief at 55+:** the Province's regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Anmore?** None that are verified as 55+ complexes. There is no verified 55+ strata, park, co-op or life lease in the village. What a 55+ buyer buys in Anmore is a general-market detached or estate house — and then relies on the house itself, and on suite/coach-house rights, for aging in place. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is there a seniors building, care home or subsidized seniors building in the village if I need help later?** None verified. No retirement residence, assisted-living residence or subsidized seniors building with an Anmore address was found in the registry, BC Housing or research-file sources checked. Care would mean moving to a neighbouring Tri-Cities community. Sources: Section 2 above. https://www.bchousing.org/housing-assistance/housing-with-support/seniors-supportive-housing
- **Q: Can I add a suite or coach house for a caregiver or adult child?** In many cases, yes in principle: the Village states secondary suites are now permitted on single-family lots across the Village under its SSMUH zoning amendment, and a coach house is also permitted in many residential zones, giving two to three units depending on lot size and configuration. Caps in the Village checklist are 90 m² / 40% for a suite and 100 m² or 130 m² for a coach house depending on parcel size. Confirm the exact lot with the Village before valuing it. https://anmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Draft-Official-Community-Plan-Bylaw-Amendment-No.-726-2025.pdf and https://anmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Checklist-Zoning-INF.pdf
- **Q: A listing calls an Anmore home "adult-oriented" or says it has strata fees — is it 55+?** Not on that wording alone. Under current BC law only a registered 55+ strata bylaw counts, and no Anmore complex was verified with one in this pass. Strata fees appear on ordinary Anmore listings as well. Ask for the registered bylaws and Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.zolo.ca/anmore-real-estate/3295-sunnyside-road/29
- **Q: My spouse is under 55 — would that matter if we did find a 55+ strata nearby?** Provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children and live-in caregivers are exempt in defined cases. That is a strata rule — it does not create a 55+ complex in Anmore where none exists. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Eagle Ridge Hospital in Port Moody (475 Guildford Way) — a Fraser Health community hospital with 24/7 emergency, described as serving Anmore among its communities. There is no hospital in Anmore itself. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital#.XSqzNyUTGEc
- **Q: Can I get by without a car in Anmore?** Plan on driving. Anmore has no SkyTrain station (the nearest Millennium Line stations are Moody Centre and Inlet Centre in Port Moody), specific Anmore bus routes are UNVERIFIED in this pass, and the Village itself notes a limited number of routes in and out shared with Buntzen Lake recreation traffic. https://maps.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/system-maps/fwc26_regional_transit_connections.pdf and https://anmore.com/living-in-anmore/emergency-preparedness/
- **Q: What is a typical Anmore home worth, so I can compare downsizing?** No Anmore median is published. SnapStats medians are community-level only, Anmore recorded 0 detached solds in September 2026, and the Port Moody community figures group Belcarra and Port Moody proper — do not quote them as Anmore prices. Price an Anmore home off its own village comparables (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats).
- **Q: Can I defer property taxes at 55+ in Anmore?** The Province's regular deferment program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), subject to the program's other conditions — it is a provincial program, not a village one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
- **Q: Does Buntzen Lake traffic affect daily life for an older resident?** Yes, seasonally: BC Hydro manages lake parking by reservation and capacity, the Village enforces parking/traffic bylaws on surrounding streets including near Sunnyside Road, and the Village notes limited routes in and out shared with recreation visitors. Check current Village notices before a summer showing or move. https://anmore.com/2026/05/05/buntzen-lake-traffic-and-parking-in-anmore/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Anmore's "55+ search" is really an aging-in-place search.** Because there is no complex to buy into, the diligence shifts to the house: single-level living, suite/coach potential under the SSMUH amendment, lot access in winter, and distance to Port Moody services. The research file makes the same pivot — its named complexes are UNVERIFIED and its verified facts are schools, the lake and the village frame. https://jaredgibbons.ca/anmore-homes-for-sale.html
- **The SSMUH change is recent and the OCP is still catching up.** Suites were added by a mid-2024 zoning amendment, the Housing Needs Report was completed in 2024, and draft OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 726-2025 was still working through council process in 2026 material — a buyer relying on a suite should confirm the current consolidated zoning for the lot, not a 2023-era summary. https://anmore.com/building-development/community-planning/official-community-plan/official-community-plan-update/
- **The board label can put a Port Moody house on the Anmore page.** The feed itself carried 30 Birch Wynd — a Port Moody civic address — under the "Anmore" sub-area label. Municipality, taxes and services follow the civic address and title, not the board label. https://jaredgibbons.ca/anmore-homes-for-sale.html
- **Zero solds is a pricing warning, not a bargain signal.** Anmore's sub-area row showed 28 detached listings and 0 solds in September 2026 — a thin, slow-turning segment inside the Port Moody community figures, where one estate sale can move nothing publishable because no Anmore median exists at all (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats).
- **The two schools physically in the village stop at middle school.** Anmore Elementary and Eagle Mountain Middle are in the village; secondary is in Port Moody (Heritage Woods Secondary is the name in the program's work). For a grandchild-visiting or caregiver-family plan, catchments are still address-based and UNVERIFIED for any specific Anmore address in this program.
- **No care ladder exists inside the village boundary.** Unlike Port Coquitlam (strata with a service plan, then assisted rental, then long-term care in one downtown cluster), Anmore has no rung at all — the first move for care is also a move out of the village. That is the single most important planning fact in this file. Sources: Section 2 above.
