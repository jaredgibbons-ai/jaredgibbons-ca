## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Steveston is the district of south-west Richmond, inside the City of Richmond, BC. The City plans the whole district as the Steveston planning area under the Steveston Area Plan (Schedule 2.4 of the Official Community Plan, within the City of Richmond 2041 OCP framework, Bylaw 9000); the City does not use "Steveston North" or "Steveston South" as planning names. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/steveston566.pdf and https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
- Board geography: the listing board splits the district into Steveston North, Steveston South and Steveston Village. The board's exact boundaries for the three are not published on an official source; in listing practice the north/south split is generally drawn at Steveston Highway — UNVERIFIED as an official boundary. https://jaredgibbons.ca/steveston-homes-for-sale.html
- Heritage core: the Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area sits at the district's south-west waterfront; properties inside it are subject to Heritage Alteration Permit requirements under the Steveston Area Plan, and protected heritage buildings and other resource types such as docks and landscape features are identified in the Official Community Plan. The Gulf of Georgia Cannery at 12138 4th Avenue is a national historic site. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://parks.canada.ca/culture/designation/lieu-site/gulf-of-georgia
- Main roads: Moncton Street through the village, No. 1 Road north to Richmond centre, Steveston Highway east to Highway 99, Railway Avenue north–south at the east side of Steveston, and No. 2 Road north from the village. Parks and waterfront: Garry Point Park, a 39-acre waterfront park at the mouth of the Fraser River; Imperial Landing Waterfront Park on the historic BC Packers cannery site; the West Dyke Trail along the west dike; Steveston Community Park, 4011 Moncton Street (30 acres, with the Steveston Community Centre, pool and library in the park complex); Richmond Public Library's Steveston Branch (Easthope Hub) is at 4320 Moncton Street. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails-cycling/trails-map/steveston.htm
- Schools confirmed against the BC Ministry K-12 file (SD38 Richmond), by name and street address: Lord Byng Elementary, 3711 Georgia St; Westwind Elementary, 11371 Kingfisher Dr; John G. Diefenbaker Elementary, 4511 Hermitage Dr; Robert A. McMath Secondary, 4251 Garry St; Hugh Boyd Secondary, 9200 No. 1 Rd; Steveston-London Secondary, 6600 Williams Rd (at the district's east side). There is no SkyTrain station in Steveston. Other route numbers serving Steveston Exchange: UNVERIFIED — not confirmed on an official timetable in this pass. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt401.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. No Steveston strata verified in Section 2 carries such a bylaw at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500.** Steveston houses sit in Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), with secondary suite rules in Section 5.4 (a suite must be at least 33.0 m² and no more than the lesser of 40% of the dwelling's floor area or 110 m²) and Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL added under provincial Bill 44 legislation; village commercial and mixed-use zones named by the City include Steveston Commercial (CS3) and Commercial Mixed Use (ZMU32) – Steveston Village. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **Heritage:** homes inside the Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area need a Heritage Alteration Permit for exterior changes under the Steveston Area Plan — the rule that most often surprises a buyer planning a renovation near the village core. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **Flood:** Steveston fronts the South Arm of the Fraser River behind the City dike system — Richmond is a low-lying island city in the Fraser River floodplain protected by a perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations, designed for a 1:500 flood event with dike raising from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres — and the City's Steveston Island Dike project addresses the Steveston waterfront and harbour. The Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction level requirements in Richmond. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://www.letstalkrichmond.ca/StevestonIslandDike and https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Property taxes (city-wide Richmond rule):** property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** (Zone 3: Richmond and West Delta directory, May 2025); assisted living at Austin Harris runs through Vancouver Coastal Health assessment instead — two different doors for the two Steveston subsidized options. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf and https://successbc.ca/assisted-living/services/austin-harris-residence/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Steveston?** None that any source verifies as age-restricted. Steveston has no verified 55+ strata ownership building — the buying options are general-market houses, townhomes and village condos with no age bylaw. Richmond's verified 55+ ownership stock is Quilchena Green in Terra Nova (and Covenant Court in Woodwards on listing evidence) — see the Richmond city-wide file in this batch. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Then where do Steveston seniors actually live, if not in a 55+ condo?** In the three rental/care residences at the village edge — Wisteria Place (107 independent-living suites, Garry St), The Maple Residences (144 non-profit independent-living rental units, Chatham St) and Austin Harris Residence (50 affordable assisted-living suites, Moncton St) — or in their own houses, using a secondary suite for a caregiver or family member under Bylaw 8500 Section 5.4. https://optimaliving.ca/2021/10/wisteria-place-opens-its-doors-to-new-residents-and-the-richmond-community/ and https://themapleresidences.com/ and https://successbc.ca/assisted-living/services/austin-harris-residence/
- **Q: A listing says a Steveston unit is "perfect for downsizing seniors." Is it a 55+ building?** No — that phrase is marketing, and the clearest example (The Village, 4211 Bayview St) aims the same sentence at "young families" too, with no age restriction in any source. Only a registered strata bylaw makes a building 55+, and no Steveston building's sources state one. https://vancouvernashdom.com/richmond-open-houses-condos/SearchResults.form/?_pg=8
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Does that matter in Steveston?** For a general-market Steveston purchase, no age bylaw applies at all. The provincial spouse/partner exemption matters only inside a true 55+ strata (for example Quilchena Green in Terra Nova): provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption, with defined exemptions for children and live-in caregivers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: How do I get into Austin Harris Residence?** Not by applying to the building as a rental: it is an Independent Living BC affordable assisted-living residence operated by S.U.C.C.E.S.S., residents pay 70% of after-tax income, and the operator directs clients to the Vancouver Coastal Health Home and Community Care Services Team for registration and assessment. https://successbc.ca/assisted-living/services/austin-harris-residence/ and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/austin-harris-residence
- **Q: Can I rent out a Steveston condo I buy?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. A strata can still restrict short-term rentals, which matters in a waterfront village with visitor demand — check the building's short-term rental bylaw in the strata documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is Steveston in a floodplain?** Yes — it fronts the South Arm of the Fraser River in the Richmond floodplain, behind the City perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations; Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction levels, the City is raising dikes from 3.5 m to 4.7 m, and the Steveston Island Dike project addresses this waterfront specifically. Confirm the flood construction level for the exact lot before pricing in a rebuild or addition. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **Q: Can I renovate a village house freely?** Not if it sits inside the Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area: exterior changes need a Heritage Alteration Permit under the Steveston Area Plan. Check heritage status before subject removal, not after. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **Q: Which school will my grandchildren / a resale buyer get?** SD38 catchments are address-based with no middle-school tier, and the district locator returned no result for a Moncton Street village sample address — so no catchment should be promised for a village listing until SD38 confirms the exact address. The district's schools physically serving the area include Lord Byng, Westwind and Diefenbaker elementaries and McMath and Hugh Boyd secondaries, confirmed against the BC Ministry K-12 file. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** Buses from Steveston Exchange on Chatham Street — route 401 runs via No. 1 Road to Richmond–Brighouse Station for Canada Line connections. There is no SkyTrain station in Steveston, so every rail trip starts with a bus ride. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt401.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Steveston's seniors housing is all at the village edge, and all of it is rental.** Wisteria Place (Garry St), The Maple Residences (Chatham St) and Austin Harris (Moncton St) stand within a few blocks of Steveston Community Park — the district built its seniors supply beside the community centre, pool and library, not as strata product a buyer can purchase. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **The newest seniors building grew out of the Buddhist temple site.** Wisteria Place (107 suites) was made a reality with the Steveston Buddhist Temple as a key partner alongside the Wisteria Senior Health and Housing Society, with Optima Living as operator — the temple still neighbours the building by Steveston Park. https://optimaliving.ca/2021/10/wisteria-place-opens-its-doors-to-new-residents-and-the-richmond-community/
- **The Maple Residences is a veterans' project.** Its 144 units are owned and managed by the Steveston 284 Seniors' Housing Society, formed by the Army, Navy and Air Force Veterans Unit 284 — the ANAF clubhouse shares the No. 1 Road and Chatham Street site. https://www.steveston.bc.ca/documents/201111_VillagerNewsletter_NOVEMBER_FINAL._001.pdf
- **"Steveston North" and "Steveston South" are listing names, not City names.** The City plans one Steveston planning area under the Steveston Area Plan and designates the core as a Heritage Conservation Area; the north/south split at Steveston Highway is listing practice, UNVERIFIED as an official boundary — which is why the same No. 2 Road seniors building (Covenant Court, Woodwards) keeps getting filed under Steveston in searches. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **The village catchment gap is real.** The SD38 locator returned no result for 4011 Moncton Street because the address falls outside its listed ranges for Moncton Street — a listing that names a catchment school for a village home without a district confirmation is guessing. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
