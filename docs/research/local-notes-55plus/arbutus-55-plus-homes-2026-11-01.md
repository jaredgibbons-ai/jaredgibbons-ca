# Arbutus Ridge (Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Arbutus Ridge is an official City of Vancouver local area — one of the City's 22 distinct areas — in the middle of the west side, between Shaughnessy, Dunbar and Kerrisdale, with Kitsilano to the north. The City publishes no street-by-street written boundary for it in the sources read, so exact boundary roads are UNVERIFIED. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/arbutus-ridge.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- Schools located inside the area on the Vancouver School Board (SD39) list: Carnarvon Elementary, 3400 Balaclava Street, and Trafalgar Elementary, 4170 Trafalgar Street. Catchments are address-based and checked in the VSB locator; SD39 has no middle-school tier (K–7 elementary, 8–12 secondary). https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods and https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
- The Arbutus Greenway's temporary path runs 8.5 km along the east side of Arbutus Street and West Boulevard between Fir Street and Milton Street, along the area's east side. Nearby frequent transit corridors named by the City are the #99 Broadway, #25 King Edward, #41 West 41st, #49 West 49th and #14 Arbutus / West Boulevard routes; no SkyTrain station sits inside Arbutus Ridge. https://vancouver.ca/streets-transportation/explore-the-arbutus-greenway.aspx
- Arbutus Ridge has no community centre or library branch of its own in the sources read — the City lists the Dunbar, Kitsilano and Kerrisdale community centres and library branches as the area's local services. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/arbutus-ridge.aspx
- Where the seniors housing actually is: a single cluster at the area's south end, on two adjoining streets — Amica Arbutus Manor at 2125 Eddington Drive and Arbutus Care Centre at 4505 Valley Drive. One further residence, at 2799 Yew Street on the area's west side, carries a neighbourhood-attribution conflict and is flagged in Section 2. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

The operator's own description states the residence has served the Arbutus Ridge area since 1974. Nothing here is for sale — you rent a suite and choose a care level. https://www.amica.ca/arbutus-manor and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=May5&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=41&f%5B0%5D=city%3AVancouver

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City of Vancouver Seniors Housing Strategy (approved July 23, 2024):** the City's first seniors housing strategy, approved unanimously by Council, with a Seniors Housing Rezoning Policy intended to enable more housing options for seniors across all neighbourhoods, a Resident Relocation Policy adding protections for seniors in community care and assisted living residences facing redevelopment, and updated design guidelines. This is the policy route by which new seniors residences get built in house-only areas like Arbutus Ridge. https://vancouver.ca/people-programs/seniors-housing-strategy.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/seniors-housing-strategy-address-needs-of-older-adults-july-2024.aspx
- **The City does not run seniors housing.** Council encourages supportive, assisted and affordable rental housing for seniors, but City staff do not manage the facilities or their rental processes; the City directs seniors to the Seniors Services Society and its Seniors Housing Directory of BC. https://vancouver.ca/people-programs/find-supportive-housing-for-seniors.aspx
- **Zoning:** the house streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units, with minimum site areas from 306 m² to 557 m² by unit count. None of that infill is age-restricted. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Tax calendar:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; owners must also file the annual Empty Homes Tax declaration — the tax is 3% of assessed taxable value for homes declared or deemed vacant for the 2025 reference year. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Arbutus Ridge?** None that any source verifies. There is no age-restricted strata building in the area; the verified seniors buildings are a rental retirement residence (Amica Arbutus Manor) and a long-term care home (Arbutus Care Centre). A 55+ buyer in Arbutus Ridge is buying a regular house, townhouse or condo with no age bylaw. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Can I buy into Amica Arbutus Manor on Eddington Drive?** No. It is a rental retirement residence — independent living, registered assisted living and respite stays — operated by Amica Arbutus Manor Inc. You rent a suite; there is nothing to purchase. https://www.amica.ca/arbutus-manor and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=May5&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=41&f%5B0%5D=city%3AVancouver
- **Q: How do I get into Arbutus Care Centre if I need long-term care?** Through Vancouver Coastal Health, not through a realtor or the building's front desk: it is a long-term care home under VCH with 152 of its 156 beds publicly funded (as of March 31, 2025), so admission runs on health-authority eligibility and waitlists. https://www.seniorsadvocatebc.ca/quickfacts/location/91224/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Vancouver?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — a BC age bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I add a suite or a laneway house so a parent or caregiver can live with me?** That is the realistic ageing-in-place route here: under the R1-1 schedule a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and the provincial age-bylaw exemptions separately allow a live-in caregiver to reside in a 55+ strata unit. What a specific lot can hold depends on its size — check the lot against the R1-1 schedule. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day?** Buses only — there is no SkyTrain in Arbutus Ridge. The City names the #14 Arbutus / West Boulevard route on the area's east side and the #99, #25, #41 and #49 as the nearby frequent corridors, and the Arbutus Greenway gives a walking and cycling route along the same east edge. https://vancouver.ca/streets-transportation/explore-the-arbutus-greenway.aspx
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** There is no hospital inside Arbutus Ridge in the sources read, and the nearest hospital's name and address were not confirmed on a health-authority page in this research — UNCONFIRMED. The in-area health asset is care, not acute care: the Arbutus Care Centre long-term care home on Valley Drive. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/arbutus-ridge.aspx
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in the area?** Partly. The in-area ladder is two steps: independent / assisted rental living at Amica Arbutus Manor (and at the Yew Street residence flagged in Section 2), then long-term care at Arbutus Care Centre. There is no subsidized independent seniors rental step in between inside Arbutus Ridge. https://www.amica.ca/arbutus-manor and https://www.seniorsadvocatebc.ca/quickfacts/location/91224/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The two care buildings are neighbours, not a coincidence of naming.** Amica Arbutus Manor (2125 Eddington Drive) and Arbutus Care Centre (4505 Valley Drive) sit on adjoining streets at the area's south end — Eddington Drive is a short street off the Valley Drive side — so the area's whole seniors-care supply is one small cluster, not a spread of options. https://www.amica.ca/arbutus-manor and https://www.seniorsadvocatebc.ca/quickfacts/location/91224/
- **The Manor predates most of the neighbourhood's current houses' renovations:** the operator states Amica Arbutus Manor has provided senior living with access to Arbutus Ridge's golf courses, shopping and parks since 1974. Do not confuse that 1974 date with the age of any specific suite or building system — the operator does not state a rebuild date. https://www.amica.ca/arbutus-manor
- **Arbutus Care Centre is changing hands.** Vancouver Coastal Health has announced it will purchase the property at 4505 Valley Drive and assume operational responsibility from the AgeCare / Revera operation recorded by the Seniors Advocate — a buyer or family checking the home should confirm who operates it on the day they enquire. https://www.seniorsadvocatebc.ca/quickfacts/location/91224/ and https://www.agecare.ca/communities/arbutus-care-centre/
- **The Yew Street residence has had three names in the sources.** The Assisted Living Registry still lists the operator as Seasons Retirement Communities (Arbutus) GP Inc., while the building is currently marketed as Chartwell Arbutus; a search by either name lands on the same 2799 Yew Street address. https://chartwell.com/bc/vancouver/arbutus and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=May5&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=41&f%5B0%5D=city%3AVancouver
- **There is no in-area community centre, pool or library branch.** The City routes Arbutus Ridge residents to the Dunbar, Kitsilano and Kerrisdale community centres, the Kitsilano and Kerrisdale arenas / pool and those neighbourhoods' library branches — a real, if small, lifestyle trade against neighbouring areas with their own centres. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/arbutus-ridge.aspx
- **New seniors supply here would arrive by rezoning, not by right.** The City's 2024 Seniors Housing Strategy created a Seniors Housing Rezoning Policy precisely to enable more seniors housing in neighbourhoods across the city; the R1-1 house zoning itself permits no seniors residence form. https://vancouver.ca/people-programs/seniors-housing-strategy.aspx and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
