# Seymour NV — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Seymour NV is the MLS sub-area inside the District of North Vancouver's Seymour community — the largest community area in the District, lying east of the Seymour River. The "NV" distinguishes it from other Seymours; Blueridge, Windsor Park, Indian River and Parkgate are separate MLS sub-areas within the same District community. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/Council_Reports/SLP_2003_6.0.pdf
- The community's spine is Mount Seymour Parkway, with Riverside Drive, Berkley Road and Carnation Street as its search streets; Parkgate Village, at the base of Mount Seymour Road, is one of the District's six Village Centres under the OCP. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- Schools: Seymour Heights Elementary (K–7, 2640 Carnation St) serves the Seymour Heights pocket, Blueridge Elementary (K–7, 2650 Bronte Dr) serves Blueridge, and Dorothy Lynas Elementary (K–7, 4000 Inlet Cres) is the French Immersion school for the Raven Woods / Dollarton-side pockets; the catchment secondary school is Windsor Secondary (931 Broadview Dr). An SD44 locator run on 2640 Carnation St (range 2524–2720 Carnation St) returned Seymour Heights Elementary and Windsor Secondary; all other addresses remain address-specific — confirm with SD44. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/044 and https://dorothylynas.sd44.ca/
- Parkgate Community Centre (3625 Banff Court — fitness centre, gymnasium, pickleball and badminton courts) anchors Parkgate Village beside the Parkgate branch of the North Vancouver District Public Library (3675 Banff Court); Ron Andrews Community Recreation Centre (931 Lytton St, next to Windsor Secondary) adds a pool, racquetball/squash and fitness facilities. http://www.nvrc.ca/facilities-fields/community-recreation-centres and https://www.nvdpl.ca/locations
- Parkgate Village shopping centre (3650 Mount Seymour Parkway) has 36 stores shadow-anchored by a 37,000 sq ft Safeway, plus a Shoppers Drug Mart and BlueShore Financial branch. https://www.bentallgreenoakleasing.com/pdfs/Brochure/921/2742193
- Transit is buses on Mount Seymour Parkway — routes 211 Seymour, 212 Deep Cove, 214 Blueridge and 215 Indian River — connecting to Phibbs Exchange for the R2 RapidBus (Park Royal to Metrotown since September 7, 2026) and downtown routes. Road access is the Parkway to Highway 1 and the Ironworkers Memorial Second Narrows Crossing. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/regional-maps/2026/september-2026/north-and-west-vancouver-map.pdf and https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/
- Where 55+ housing concentrates: **there is no 55+ ownership concentration in Seymour NV.** The one seniors-specific building inside the sub-area is a rental retirement residence at Parkgate (Cedar Springs PARC, Section 2); the strata clusters at Parkgate and along the Parkway are general-market.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Municipality: District of North Vancouver — Seymour Local Plan.** The Seymour Local Plan manages slow, limited growth to protect the area's forested character and maintains the predominantly single-family character of Seymour Heights, Blueridge, Riverside East and Windsor Park; it contemplates townhouse development only on assembled sites on the north side of Mount Seymour Parkway (3200–3500 blocks) at 0.6–1.2 FSR. District-wide, Parkgate is one of six Village Centres and the OCP's 2030 housing-mix target is 55% detached / 45% attached. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/Council_Reports/SLP_2003_6.0.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **District taxes:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; Council approved a 3.53% municipal tax increase for 2026 (2.03% services plus 1.5% infrastructure renewal); the 2026 total residential rate is $3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value, with utility fees billed separately from the tax notice. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- **Suites, coach houses and multiplexes — in flux, check lot by lot:** under Zoning Bylaw 3210 as amended by Bylaw 8698 (June 2024), most single-family lots may hold a main house, a secondary suite and a coach house; December 2025 Ground-Oriented Housing rules permit up to four homes on more than 4,000 eligible District properties, and in April 2026 Council voted against adopting the six-home near-transit tier. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/new-provincial-housing-legislation-fact-sheet.pdf and http://docs.dnv.org/documents/3-1-multiunit-housing.pdf
- **Hazard permit areas:** the District designates Natural Hazard Development Permit Areas for slope and creek hazards and provides property-level reports through GeoWeb; after the January 19, 2005 Berkley Escarpment landslide the District began proactive hazard management, and its Landslide Risk Assessment was updated in 2026. Sloped and creek-adjacent Seymour lots should be checked before purchase. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/bgclandslideriskassessment2026updatereportfinal.pdf and http://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-1-district-landslide.pdf
- **Planning is done in consultation with the Tsleil-Waututh Nation:** the Seymour community is also home to the Nation, and District planning for the area east of the Seymour River is done in consultation with it. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/Council_Reports/SLP_2003_6.0.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ condo or townhome I can buy in Seymour NV?** No — no verified 55+ ownership complex exists inside the sub-area. The nine buildings on the maintained North Vancouver 55+ strata list are all west of the Seymour River (Lynn Valley, Lonsdale, Delbrook, Edgemont). Seymour's strata clusters — Parkgate Place, Banff Court, Raven Woods, McCartney Lane — carry no age restriction in any source checked. https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/on-the-market/55-buildings-north-vancouver/
- **Q: What seniors housing IS in Seymour?** One building: Cedar Springs PARC at 3633 Mt Seymour Pkwy, a rental independent-living retirement residence across from Parkgate Village — monthly rental with meals, activities and services, in a LEED Gold building. There is no strata title and nothing to buy. https://parcliving.ca/blog/the-best-senior-living-in-north-vancouver-cedar-springs-parc/
- **Q: Is the Mount Seymour Park co-op a seniors co-op?** No. Its own profile describes "people and families of all ages and stages of life" in 57 townhouse units (1–4 bedrooms) with member volunteer duties. A third-party directory listed it under independent living, but the co-op's own description governs: it is all-ages co-operative housing, not 55+, and a co-op share is not a strata purchase. https://www.readkong.com/page/mount-seymour-park-housing-co-operative-chf-bc-3337889
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere on the North Shore?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: If I stay in a Seymour house, can I add a suite or coach house for a caregiver?** Generally yes on the base rules: since June 2024 most District single-family lots may hold a house, a secondary suite and a coach house, and December 2025 Ground-Oriented Housing rules allow up to four homes on eligible properties — but hazard Development Permit Areas and servicing can cap a specific Seymour lot, so verify on GeoWeb before marketing multiplex value. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/new-provincial-housing-legislation-fact-sheet.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital, and what if I need care later?** Lions Gate Hospital (231 East 15th St, City of North Vancouver) is the North Shore hospital. The registered assisted-living residences in the sources checked are all on the west side of the District/City (Edgemont, Cedar Village, Upper Lonsdale) — a move up the care ladder from Seymour usually means leaving the sub-area. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ABurnaby&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=asc
- **Q: How is transit for someone who no longer drives?** Parkway buses (211, 212, 214, 215) run to Phibbs Exchange for the R2 RapidBus and downtown connections — workable from Parkgate, thin from the upper residential streets, which are car-oriented subdivisions. There is no SkyTrain or RapidBus stop inside the sub-area. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/regional-maps/2026/september-2026/north-and-west-vancouver-map.pdf
- **Q: What natural hazards should I check before buying on a Seymour street?** Slope and creek hazards. The District maps Natural Hazard Development Permit Areas, provides property-level GeoWeb reports, and updated its Landslide Risk Assessment in 2026 — pull the report for any lot near the Seymour River, a creek ravine, or a steep bank before subject removal. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/bgclandslideriskassessment2026updatereportfinal.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **A directory error puts a "seniors community" inside the recreation centre.** A third-party independent-living directory lists "Parkgate Community Centre, 3625 Banff Ct — 90 suites" as a housing option. That address is the NVRC community centre (fitness centre, gymnasium, courts) beside the library branch — nobody lives there. The actual Parkgate seniors building is Cedar Springs PARC on the Parkway. https://www.seniorhome.ca/blog/independent-living-north-vancouver-2026 and http://www.nvrc.ca/facilities-fields/community-recreation-centres
- **The ski hill's numbers come from two different authorities.** BC Parks states the Mt Seymour ski area operates inside the provincial park (established 1936) under an 81-hectare controlled recreation area with 39 runs, 12 lit for night skiing; the resort operator publishes a 330 m vertical drop from a 1,265 m summit and average annual snowfall over 1,000 cm. The Province also requires drivers to obey winter tire and chain signs on designated routes from October 1 to April 30 — a real factor on upper Seymour streets on the snow line. https://bcparks.ca/mount-seymour-park/ and http://www.mtseymour.ca/ and http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/driving-and-cycling/traveller-information/seasonal/winter-driving/enforcement
- **There are two golf courses at the Parkway, with opposite tenures.** Northlands Golf Course (3400 Anne Macdonald Way) is District-owned and public — 18 holes, par 71, designed by Les Furber, open since 1996/97 — while Seymour Golf & Country Club (3723 Mount Seymour Parkway, established 1953) across the Parkway is private. Cedar Springs PARC sits next to the private club. https://www.northlandsgolfcourse.com/ and http://www.seymourgolf.com/
- **The one density door in Seymour is a single parkway strip.** The Seymour Local Plan contemplates townhouses only on assembled sites on the north side of Mount Seymour Parkway, 3200–3500 blocks, at 0.6–1.2 FSR, while directing the rest of the community to remain single-family — owners on that strip hold the sub-area's only plan-supported assembly value. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/Council_Reports/SLP_2003_6.0.pdf
- **Seymour guards a regional asset, which is why growth is capped.** The Local Plan recognises Seymour's regional role as guardian of much of the region's Green Zone, and the community is also home to the Tsleil-Waututh Nation, with District planning east of the Seymour River done in consultation with the Nation. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/Council_Reports/SLP_2003_6.0.pdf
- **The elementary catchment split is settled for exactly one address range.** An SD44 locator run confirmed 2524–2720 Carnation St → Seymour Heights Elementary / Windsor Secondary. The Seymour Heights vs Blueridge elementary boundary matters to family buyers and remains address-specific everywhere else — do not generalise the one run. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/seymour-nv.md
- **SnapStats publishes no North Vancouver figures at all.** The GVR September 2026 report covers Burnaby, New Westminster, Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, Pitt Meadows and Maple Ridge only — any "Seymour NV median" attributed to SnapStats does not exist; sub-area medians are Paragon-only. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/seymour-nv.md
