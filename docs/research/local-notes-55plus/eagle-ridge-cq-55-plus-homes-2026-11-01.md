## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. No Eagle Ridge strata checked has used this power — the absence of a 55+ bylaw is the verified fact here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why the "rentals allowed w/restrictions" lines in the Eagle Ridge Court and Greyhawke Estates building records have to be read against current law and the registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/eagle-ridge-court-1170-lansdowne-drive
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996** is Coquitlam's zoning bylaw. The area's small-scale residential zones are R-1, R-2 and R-3 (subdivision minimums 650 m² / 18.5 m width for R-1 and R-3; 465 m² / 12.5 m for R-2); the specific zone of any one complex in Section 2: UNCONFIRMED from the sources checked. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) in Coquitlam:** the City's adopted rules allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the rules. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Watercourse setbacks:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam — relevant on creek-edge house lots, not to the corridor strata buildings. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Property tax dates that apply citywide:** Coquitlam property tax payment was due July 2, 2026 for the Jan 1–Dec 31, 2026 year, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after September 15, 2026, including unclaimed Home Owner Grants. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Eagle Ridge?** None that any source verifies. The sub-area had 13 condos & townhomes listed in September 2026 — a strata-heavy mix — but every named complex checked (Eagle Ridge Court, Eagleridge Villas, Greyhawke Estates, Maya) is general-market. The nearest verified 55+ purchase stock in Coquitlam is the Town Centre cluster on Guildford Way / Princess Crescent / Burlington Drive (Central Coquitlam file). (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file; building checks Nov 1, 2026.)
- **Q: Is Eagle Ridge Court an adult or seniors building?** No age restriction appears in its building record — the restrictions listed are pets allowed with restrictions and rentals allowed with restrictions, and the complex was built in 1981 as a general complex. The name is the neighbourhood's name, not an age designation. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/eagle-ridge-court-1170-lansdowne-drive
- **Q: Is this the same market as Upper Eagle Ridge or Westwood Plateau?** No. SnapStats carries Eagle Ridge and Upper Eagle Ridge as separate sub-areas (Upper Eagle Ridge recorded 3 detached listings and 0 solds in September 2026, against Eagle Ridge's 8 listings and 2 solds), and Westwood Plateau sits above both. Complexes upslope — Greyhawke Estates is listed as Westwood Plateau in current listing data — should not be priced or marketed as Eagle Ridge without an address check. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.) https://www.zolo.ca/coquitlam-real-estate/1495-lansdowne-drive/140
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in one of the Lansdowne complexes?** Long-term rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022, whatever a building database still prints ("rentals allowed w/restrictions" at Eagle Ridge Court, for example); short-term rentals can still be restricted — Eagleridge Villas' data records a 3-month short-term minimum. Confirm the building's current registered bylaws before counting on income. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/eagle-ridge-court-1170-lansdowne-drive
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** On the area's own main road: Eagle Ridge Hospital, 475 Guildford Way, Port Moody, at the west end of Guildford Way on the municipal border. It is a hospital, not a seniors residence — there is no care or retirement building attached to it in the sources checked. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- **Q: Where do older adults here actually gather?** The City's 50+ programming runs through its two pavilions; the north-side one is Glen Pine Pavilion, 1200 Glen Pine Court, by City Hall — the City describes the pavilions as adult-oriented centres focused on programming for older adults, with activity groups run with the Glen Pine 50 Plus Society. https://coquitlam.ca/455/Glen-Pine-Pavilion and https://www.coquitlam.ca/1159/50-Plus-Services
- **Q: Can a house lot here work for ageing in place with family?** That is the in-area route: Coquitlam's SSMUH rules allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes, where the lot is connected to municipal water and sewer. Confirm the zone and services for the specific lot. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in Eagle Ridge?** Not in a seniors building — there is none in the sub-area. The ladder is citywide: 55+ strata in the Town Centre pocket, subsidized seniors rental on the Johnson / Pipeline side of North Coquitlam (Friendship Manor, Ozada Village, Robert Nicklin Place in the BC Housing directory), and retirement / care elsewhere in the Tri-Cities. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The hospital is the area's namesake and its biggest search trap (checked Nov 1, 2026):** "Eagle Ridge" searches return the hospital first, and the hospital sits in Port Moody (475 Guildford Way), not Coquitlam. A senior who buys here for "the Eagle Ridge community" is buying beside a hospital — genuinely useful — but there is no retirement residence, assisted living or seniors housing attached to that name anywhere in the sub-area. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- **The same street carries both the zero and the cluster (checked Nov 1, 2026):** Guildford Way runs from Eagle Ridge Hospital in the west to Marlborough House (3098 Guildford Way) in the Town Centre pocket in the east — one of Coquitlam's three verified 55+ strata buildings. Same road, different sub-areas: a Guildford Way address alone does not tell you which file a building belongs in; the civic number does. https://www.zolo.ca/coquitlam-real-estate/3098-guildford-way/118
- **Listings and sales point in opposite directions here (September 2026):** strata listings outnumbered detached listings 13 to 8, yet the strata segment recorded 0 solds while detached sold 2 of 8 (25%) — a senior downsizer shopping this sub-area is shopping the segment that, in that month, was not transacting at all (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file).
- **One address, two sub-areas (checked Nov 1, 2026):** Greyhawke Estates at 1495 Lansdowne Drive is recorded as an Eagle Ridge-area townhouse complex in building data and as a Westwood Plateau home in current listing data. The boundary between Eagle Ridge, Upper Eagle Ridge and the Plateau is not settled by any City source in the research — any comp set for the Lansdowne corridor has to state which side of that unsettled line it used. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/greyhawke-estates-1495-lansdowne-drive and https://www.zolo.ca/coquitlam-real-estate/1495-lansdowne-drive/140
- **BC Housing's map will mislead an Eagle Ridge search (May 2025 directory):** the Zone 4 seniors directory splits Coquitlam into "North" and "South" only, so its Johnson St / Pipeline Rd / Ozada Ave entries file under "Coquitlam — North." One Johnson Street condo listing is board-labelled Eagle Ridge CQ, which makes the import tempting — but no source places Friendship Manor, Ozada Village or Robert Nicklin Place inside Eagle Ridge itself, so they stay out of this file as UNCONFIRMED for the sub-area. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf and https://vancouvernashdom.com/coquitlam-apartments-for-sale-from-400000-to-600000/SearchResults.form/?_pg=19
- **The seniors centre for this side of the city is a pavilion, not a residence (City source, read Nov 1, 2026):** Glen Pine Pavilion (1200 Glen Pine Court) is one of the City's two hubs for 50+ programming, with the Glen Pine 50 Plus Society running activity groups — services for older adults exist here even though seniors housing does not. https://coquitlam.ca/455/Glen-Pine-Pavilion and https://www.coquitlam.ca/1159/50-Plus-Services
