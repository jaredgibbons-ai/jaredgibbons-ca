# Altamont (West Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Altamont is a residential hillside area of the District of West Vancouver, above Marine Drive between Dundarave to the east and Caulfeild to the west. The District does not use "Altamont" as a named neighbourhood in its Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 4985, 2018 — its village centres are Dundarave, Caulfeild Village and Horseshoe Bay, with Ambleside as the Municipal Town Centre — so street-by-street boundaries for Altamont are UNVERIFIED. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- The streets that frame the area are Marine Drive along the waterfront below, Queens Avenue climbing the hillside, and the avenues above them below the British Properties edge; nearby areas are Dundarave (east), Caulfeild (west), the British Properties (north, uphill), Ambleside (south-east) and Chelsea Park (north-west, uphill). https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- Housing form: detached houses on hillside lots in the RS single-family zones of Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010. No condo building, townhome complex or seniors facility inside Altamont is confirmed on an official or developer source — see Section 2. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- Transit at the edges, not inside: TransLink route 251 (Queens / Park Royal), operated as West Vancouver Blue Bus, runs on the Queens Avenue corridor below the hillside, and route 250 runs on Marine Drive between Horseshoe Bay, Dundarave, Park Royal and downtown Vancouver via the Lions Gate Bridge. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt251.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt250.pdf
- Schools: School District 45 (West Vancouver Schools) catchments are address-based and no Altamont catchment is confirmed — SD45 directs families to the school locator on its admissions page. SD45 has no middle tier: elementary is K–7, secondary is 8–12. Nearby schools are Irwin Park Elementary (K–7), 2455 Haywood Avenue in Dundarave, and Westcot Elementary (K–7) at the foot of the British Properties. https://westvancouverschools.ca/admissions/student-admissions-guidelines/ and https://westvancouverschools.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Location-Address.pdf
- Health care: Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver, is the Vancouver Coastal Health hospital serving the North Shore including West Vancouver; the North Vancouver Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at Suite 200, 221 West Esplanade, North Vancouver. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/north-vancouver-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
- The District's seniors hub is downhill in Ambleside: the West Vancouver Seniors' Activity Centre, 695 21st Street, offers programs, services and amenities geared toward 55+ adults, including the Garden Side Bistro, fitness, arts and learning programs and a seniors' shuttle bus. It is a program centre, not housing. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/facilities/seniors-activity-centre and https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-programs-services/seniors-services

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**No verified 55+ strata, 55+ rental, retirement residence, care home or subsidized seniors building exists inside Altamont in any source checked.** The pocket is detached houses only. What follows is the nearest West Vancouver stock, clearly labelled as outside the sub-area, because that is where an Altamont downsizer actually lands.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why the "rentals not allowed" lines in older building data for Tudor Gardens and Wedgewood Terrace must be re-read against current law and the building's current bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **District zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010.** Altamont houses sit in the RS single-family zones; Section 200 states minimum site areas of 8,094 m² in RS1, 1,625 m² in RS2, 975 m² in RS3, 731 m² in RS4 and 488 m² in RS5, and lists secondary suites and a detached secondary suite (coach house) among permitted uses. The bylaw's ground-oriented rules allow 4 dwellings per lot, or 6 if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop — the legal route to staying in the area with a suite or coach house rather than moving to a 55+ building. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf and https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf
- **Density rules on the lower streets are in transition.** On July 21, 2025, the Province directed West Vancouver to amend its OCP to provide increased density in the single-family and duplex areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave (and at Park Royal–Taylor Way) by December 31, 2025 — Altamont sits directly above Dundarave, so verify infill rules per property. On December 1, 2025, Council adopted OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 5406, 2025, bringing the OCP into alignment with the 2024 Interim Housing Needs Report. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698 and https://westvancouver.ca/business-development/housing/official-community-plan-housing-policy-update
- **OCP context on seniors housing:** the District's own Canada Community-Building Fund reporting states West Vancouver's population has aged faster than the region's — seniors were about one in five residents in 2001 and nearly one in three in 2021, with a median age of 51 in 2021 — and the 2018 OCP's housing theme expressly aims to provide "more and ongoing seniors and rental housing options," while noting seniors are among those unable to find accessible, single-level options close to amenities and transit. That is the policy backdrop: demand is documented, supply inside pockets like Altamont is not. https://www.ubcm.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/West%20Vancouver.pdf and https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- **Hazard permits on hillside lots:** an Environmental Development Permit is required for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank, and a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit for work within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface; a Foreshore Development Permit applies to waterfront/coastal-flood-risk property below the hillside. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Taxes:** District of West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026. At Altamont price points the Province's additional school tax can also apply — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. Water and sewer are metered and billed quarterly. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Altamont?** None inside the pocket — no verified 55+ strata (or any strata) exists in Altamont in any source checked; the housing is detached freehold houses. The nearest verified 55+ ownership buildings are downhill in Ambleside and Dundarave: Westview Terrace, 1390 Duchess Ave, and Tudor Gardens, 843 22nd St. https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/on-the-market/55-plus-buildings-west-vancouver/ and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/tudor-gardens-843-22-street
- **Q: Is there a 55+ rental building near Altamont?** Yes, in Ambleside: the Kiwanis North Shore Housing Society's West Vancouver buildings (Kiwanis Manor, 959 21st St; The Court, 2151 Gordon Ave; The Cypress, 999 21st St; The Terrace, 975 21st St) are independent-living rentals for 55+, below-market, with income/asset eligibility and preference for North Shore residents. Rental only — nothing to buy, and pets are not permitted in Kiwanis seniors communities. https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/kiwanis-manor/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata like Tudor Gardens?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent or as an investment?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Building data says Tudor Gardens has "rentals not allowed." Is that still true?** Not as a long-term rental ban — every strata rental-restriction bylaw in BC became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. Confirm the building's current bylaws and Form B rather than relying on older building-data lines. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/tudor-gardens-843-22-street
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the nearest 55+ options?** It splits by building: Tudor Gardens is recorded as no pets, and Kiwanis seniors communities do not permit pets; Westview Terrace's pet position is UNCONFIRMED from the sources checked — confirm in the bylaws/Form B for the specific building. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/tudor-gardens-843-22-street and https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/kiwanis-manor/
- **Q: If I stay in my Altamont house, can I add a suite or coach house instead of moving?** That is the route the zoning contemplates: secondary suites and detached secondary suites are permitted uses in the RS single-family zones, and the ground-oriented rules allow up to 4 dwellings per lot (6 within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop), subject to the lot's zone and permits — including the wildfire-interface and watercourse development permits that apply on this hillside. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf and https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver — the Vancouver Coastal Health hospital serving the North Shore, including West Vancouver. The North Vancouver Urgent and Primary Care Centre (Suite 200, 221 West Esplanade) is the urgent-care option. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in West Vancouver?** The in-District ladder is: registered assisted-living residences at Amica West Vancouver (659 Clyde Ave), Amica Lions Gate (Keith Rd) and Hollyburn House (2095 Marine Dr), then long-term care at Inglewood Care Centre (725 Inglewood Ave, 230 publicly funded beds). All are in the Ambleside/Park Royal side of the District, downhill from Altamont — none is in the pocket itself. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3AArmstrong&f%5B1%5D=city%3AMasset&f%5B2%5D=city%3AWest%20Vancouver&f%5B3%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=asc and https://www.seniorsadvocatebc.ca/quickfacts/location/75989/
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day from Altamont?** The confirmed service is at the edges: route 251 on Queens Avenue and route 250 on Marine Drive, both West Vancouver Blue Bus routes to Park Royal. Most Altamont streets are a hill walk or a drive from those corridors, and the Seniors' Activity Centre runs its own shuttle bus for 55+ members downhill in Ambleside. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt251.pdf and https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-programs-services/seniors-services

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The whole District has only three buildings a North Shore guide lists as 55+-bylaw strata** — Westview Terrace (1390 Duchess Ave), Tudor Gardens (843 22nd St) and Lincoln Gardens (whose address the guide page does not state — UNCONFIRMED here). A buyer searching "Altamont 55+" is really searching Ambleside and Dundarave; the hillside pockets contribute no supply at all. https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/on-the-market/55-plus-buildings-west-vancouver/
- **The demand side is documented by the District itself:** seniors rose from about one in five West Vancouver residents in 2001 to nearly one in three in 2021, and the median age hit 51 in 2021 — a decade older than the regional median — yet the OCP still describes the District as mostly detached, single-family housing with limited other forms. https://www.ubcm.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/West%20Vancouver.pdf
- **The biggest seniors project in the District's history is the Inglewood redevelopment, and it is not in Altamont.** On September 21, 2026, Council approved bylaws and a development permit to redevelop Inglewood Care Centre at 725 Inglewood Ave in two phases: Phase 1, a 364-bed publicly funded long-term care facility; Phase 2, 200 independent-living units and 161 seniors rental housing units. The District is not involved in tenant placement — Baptist Housing handles resident inquiries. https://westvancouver.ca/business-development/development-applications/inglewood-campus-care
- **"Age restrictions" on a building-data site is not the same as verified 55+.** Wedgewood Terrace (1730 Duchess Ave) is recorded with age restrictions but no stated threshold in the sources checked; since Nov 24, 2022 any BC strata age bylaw below 55 is invalid anyway, so the registered bylaw — not the database line — is what decides who can live there. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/wedgewood-terrace-1730-duchess-ave and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Kiwanis Manor carries two different descriptions in two official-ish sources** — independent 55+ rental on the operator society's site, registered assisted living in BC's Assisted Living Registry under a partnership name. Anyone referring a client there should confirm which product the building actually offers before describing it. https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/kiwanis-manor/ and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3AArmstrong&f%5B1%5D=city%3AMasset&f%5B2%5D=city%3AWest%20Vancouver&f%5B3%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=asc
- **The seawalk below the hillside is the flat walking route:** the Centennial Seawalk runs roughly 1.7 km east from Dundarave Park (150 25th Street, with 50+ metres of sandy beach, playground, washrooms and a seasonal concession) to Ambleside — relevant to any senior buyer weighing a hillside house against a flat Ambleside condo. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/dundarave-park
- **The District runs a dedicated 55+ service layer downhill:** the Seniors' Activity Centre at 695 21st Street runs the Garden Side Bistro, the SACS on 21st shop, rehabilitation/fitness programs and a seniors' shuttle bus — services an Altamont senior uses by driving or bussing down to Ambleside, not services in the pocket. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-programs-services/seniors-services
