# Mid Meadows (Pitt Meadows) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Mid Meadows" is the Paragon/MLS sub-area name for the Bonson Road lands in the south-east of Pitt Meadows. The City of Pitt Meadows does not publish an official boundary for it; in City geography the same ground is the Bonson Road neighbourhoods — North Bonson and South Bonson — plus Osprey Village on Fraser Way, all south of the CPKC rail line along Hammond Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- The spine is Bonson Road, running south from Hammond Road to the Fraser River dike; Barnston View Road serves South Bonson, and Fraser Way serves Osprey Village at the east side. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-and-facilities-list
- The stock is family housing: detached houses in North and South Bonson, and townhome/duplex product in South Bonson and Osprey Village — including Nature's Walk by Onni in South Bonson. No age-restricted complex of any tenure was found anywhere in the sub-area (see Section 2). https://onni.com/blog/introducing-natures-walk/
- Two elementary schools sit inside the sub-area: Davie Jones Elementary, 12030 Blakely Road, and Edith McDermott Elementary, 12178 Bonson Road. School District 42 catchments are address-based with no middle-school tier (elementary K–7, secondary 8–12) — the SD42 School Locator sample for 10932 Barnston View Road returns Davie Jones Elementary and Pitt Meadows Secondary, so which of the two elementaries applies is a house-by-house check. https://elementary.sd42.ca/daviejones/ and https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- Parks and rec are the area's strength: Pitt Meadows Athletic Park, 11431 Bonson Road; the South Bonson Community Centre, 10932 Barnston View Road; and Shoreline Park, 51 Bonson Road, on the Fraser River dike at the foot of Bonson Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/pitt-meadows-athletic-park and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-and-facilities-list
- Transit: no West Coast Express station in the sub-area — Pitt Meadows Station is in Central Meadows. The in-area service is the 719/722 circular bus routes tying Bonson Road and Fraser Way to Meadowtown and the Harris Road core. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt719.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt722.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain
- Shopping and services: Osprey Village's shops on Fraser Way are the in-area retail node; the nearest full grocery stores are in Central Meadows (Meadowvale's Save-On-Foods and Meadowtown's Real Canadian Superstore, 19800 Lougheed Highway). The city's only library branch is also in Central Meadows, at 200-12099 Harris Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/dmlive-155673-v1-gpra_report_to_pitt_meadows_20190405_v2_pdf.pdf and https://fvrl.bc.ca/locations/pitt-meadows-public-library
- Health: no hospital in Pitt Meadows. The nearest is Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, Maple Ridge, a Fraser Health community hospital with 24/7 emergency care; the Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 121-11900 Haney Place, Maple Ridge. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/F/C/urgent-primary-care-centre---ridge-meadows
- Flood setting: the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of Pitt Meadows is in the floodplain, behind roughly 60 km of dikes; South Bonson sits between the dike-protected riverfront and the City's drainage systems (Area 3 is the Kennedy area system). https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**Mid Meadows has NO verified 55+ housing of any tenure — no 55+ strata, no 55+ rental, no retirement residence, no assisted-living registration and no subsidized seniors building with a Bonson Road, Fraser Way or Osprey Village address was found in any source checked.** The city's 55+ stock is concentrated in the Central Meadows Town Centre pocket (see that file). Do not market anything in this sub-area as 55+.

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- None. No strata complex in Mid Meadows carries a verified age restriction in any source checked.

### Verified 55+ rental (not ownership)

- None. No purpose-built 55+ rental building with a Mid Meadows address appears in any source checked, including BC Housing's Zone 4 seniors directory — its Pitt Meadows entries are the Advent Road and Ford Road co-ops, neither of which is in this sub-area. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

### Retirement residences and care (rental/care — NOT strata ownership)

- None in the sub-area. BC's Assisted Living Registry shows a single registered seniors assisted-living residence for the whole City of Pitt Meadows — The Wesbrooke, 12000 190A Street — which is in the Central Meadows Town Centre pocket, not in Mid Meadows. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&name=&address=&f%5B0%5D=ha%3AFraser%20Health&order=alr_residence_type&sort=desc&page=4

### Subsidized seniors rental (non-profit, income-based — not ownership)

- None in the sub-area. No non-profit or BC Housing seniors building with a Mid Meadows address was verified in the sources checked, including the BC Housing Zone 4 directory. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

### Checked and NOT 55+ (do not put these on a 55+ page)

- **Cedar Downs — 12525 190A Street.** A general-market strata on the north side of Lougheed Highway that listing data classes as "Mid Meadows" even though it sits on the 190A Street corridor, far from Bonson Road — no age restriction appears in any source checked. It is included here because the board puts it in this sub-area; it is not 55+ stock, and its placement shows how little the board's Mid Meadows matches the City's Bonson geography. (Research statement: sources checked Nov 1, 2026.)
- **The rest of the Bonson Road stock.** Detached houses and townhomes across North Bonson, South Bonson and Osprey Village were checked as a class: family product, no age restriction found for any named complex. There is no manufactured-home park in the sub-area in any source checked (the city's one park co-op, Meadow Highlands, is on Ponderosa Blvd in Central Meadows — and it is not verified 55+ either; see the Central Meadows file). https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-and-facilities-list

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. This matters in Mid Meadows in reverse: a Bonson Road strata could lawfully adopt a 55+ bylaw in future (¾ vote of the owners), but none checked has one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011** governs Mid Meadows; the Bonson lands sit under the RS and R-series Urban Residential zones and multi-family zones in Osprey Village, with the same citywide suite and SSMUH rules as the rest of Pitt Meadows. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 - Consolidated Version_0.pdf
- **Plan: the Official Community Plan "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," Bylaw No. 2864, 2020** (adopted September 27, 2022) governs the Bonson neighbourhoods. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- **Suites:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 allows one secondary suite per principal dwelling unit, 33 to 90 m², inside the same building where the zone permits it, OR one garden suite per lot in the zones listed in the City's garden suite guide — a lot cannot have both. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH):** lots 280 m² or less allow up to 3 units, lots over 280 m² up to 4 units, and lots over 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent transit bus stop up to 6 units; the City's SSMUH Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 2986 was adopted June 25, 2024. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf
- **Flood and dikes:** the City states over 95% of Pitt Meadows is in the floodplain behind roughly 60 km of dikes. In South Bonson the Fraser River dike is the neighbourhood's front yard — Shoreline Park sits on it — and dike/drainage status is a standing due-diligence item on any purchase here. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- **City taxes:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026 under the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after August 1, 2026; the bylaw also sets a residential drainage levy of $0.1082 per $1,000 of assessed value. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a 55+ home in Mid Meadows?** No — there is no verified 55+ strata, co-op or age-restricted complex of any kind in the sub-area. The closest verified 55+ purchase is Meadowland Village, 18960 Advent Road, in Central Meadows. (Research statement: directories, registry and listing sources checked Nov 1, 2026.)
- **Q: Is Osprey Village a seniors village?** No. Osprey Village is a retail village on Fraser Way surrounded by family townhomes; no building there carries a verified age restriction, and BC Housing's seniors directory lists no Mid Meadows address at all. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: Is Nature's Walk age-restricted? It looks like a downsizer product.** No age restriction appears in the developer's material or any source checked — Onni describes Nature's Walk as a family townhome/duplex community in South Bonson. Ground-floor living is not the same as a 55+ bylaw. https://onni.com/blog/introducing-natures-walk/
- **Q: Is there a retirement residence or assisted living on Bonson Road?** No. The City of Pitt Meadows has exactly one registered assisted-living residence — The Wesbrooke, 12000 190A Street, in the Town Centre pocket of Central Meadows. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&name=&address=&f%5B0%5D=ha%3AFraser%20Health&order=alr_residence_type&sort=desc&page=4
- **Q: Is there any subsidized seniors housing in Mid Meadows?** None verified. BC Housing's Zone 4 seniors directory covers Pitt Meadows, and its in-city entries are the Advent Road and Ford Road co-ops — neither is in Mid Meadows, and the Ford Road co-op is not seniors-only in the first place. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: Could a Bonson Road strata become 55+ later?** Legally yes — a strata may adopt a 55+ bylaw by ¾ vote, and 55+ is the only age threshold allowed since Nov 24, 2022. People already living there lawfully when such a bylaw passed would be protected as legacy residents. But that is a possibility, not a feature: never buy in Mid Meadows on the strength of a future bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: If I buy a house here and my adult child or grandchild lives with me, are there age rules?** In Mid Meadows, no — no complex here has an age bylaw. The question only bites in a 55+ strata elsewhere, where provincial exemptions cover a younger spouse/partner, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: How does transit work here if I give up driving?** There is no West Coast Express station in the sub-area; the 719/722 buses loop Bonson Road and Fraser Way to Meadowtown and the Harris Road core, where Pitt Meadows Station and the R3 RapidBus run. For a car-free 55+ buyer, that two-step trip is the practical difference between Mid Meadows and the Town Centre pocket. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt719.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain
- **Q: What should I check about flood risk on a South Bonson home?** The City states over 95% of Pitt Meadows is in the floodplain, protected by about 60 km of dikes — ask for the parcel's flood and drainage information from the City, and confirm flood insurance terms before subject removal. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **Q: Which elementary school would visiting — or resident — grandchildren attend?** It splits by address between Davie Jones Elementary (12030 Blakely Rd) and Edith McDermott Elementary (12178 Bonson Rd), with Pitt Meadows Secondary for grades 8–12; run the exact address through the SD42 School Locator rather than assuming the closer school. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The board's "Mid Meadows" and the City's Bonson geography are different maps:** listing data even puts Cedar Downs, at 12525 190A Street north of Lougheed Highway, in "Mid Meadows," while the Bonson Road lands a kilometre south share the label. When a listing says "Mid Meadows," check the street, not the label. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- **This is the one Pitt Meadows sub-area where the 55+ answer is simply "nothing":** the city's whole 55+ cluster — one strata, one co-op, the retirement residence, the seniors centre — sits in the Town Centre pocket in Central Meadows. A 55+ search filtered to Mid Meadows will return family townhomes wearing downsizer styling, not age-restricted homes. (Research statement: sources checked Nov 1, 2026.)
- **Two elementary schools inside one small sub-area, split address-by-address:** Davie Jones and Edith McDermott are both on the Bonson side, and the catchment line between them does not follow "north vs south" intuition — the locator sample for a South Bonson address (10932 Barnston View Rd) came back Davie Jones. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **The dike is the neighbourhood amenity:** Shoreline Park at the foot of Bonson Road (51 Bonson Rd) sits on the Fraser River dike, and the Athletic Park and South Bonson Community Centre cluster on Bonson Road — the recreation is genuine, but the dike underneath it is flood infrastructure first. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-and-facilities-list and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **No station, despite the city having one:** Mid Meadows is the residential area most Pitt Meadows buyers picture — and it is a bus loop (719/722), not a walk, away from the West Coast Express. Central Meadows, not Mid Meadows, holds the station, the library, the rec centre and the seniors centre. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain
