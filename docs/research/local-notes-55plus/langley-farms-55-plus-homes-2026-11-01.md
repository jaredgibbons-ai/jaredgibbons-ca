# Langley Farms (Township and City of Langley) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Plain finding up front: the 55+ angle on Langley farm and ALR land is thin, and this file says so with sources. No 55+ strata, manufactured-home park, co-operative, rental or care complex was verified on Langley farm land or inside the board's rural farm sub-areas (Salmon River, Campbell Valley, County Line Glen Valley, Otter District) as a farm-belt product. What a 55+ farm buyer actually faces here is ALR housing law — house-size caps and a tightly limited second dwelling — not an age-restricted community.**

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- This is a topic page, not a neighbourhood: farm land, working farms and agricultural land across Langley, principally the Township of Langley, where the farm belt sits; Langley City is urban and holds no farm belt. The Township states 235 square kilometres of the Township is within the Agricultural Land Reserve. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx
- There is no single MLS sub-area for farms; the board's rural Langley sub-areas in the SnapStats data are Salmon River, Campbell Valley, County Line Glen Valley and Otter District, alongside the named communities (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, cited in the Langley Farms research file, ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/langley-farms.md).
- The farm corridors are the rural roads of those districts — Fraser Highway's rural stretches, 56 Avenue and Rawlison Crescent on the Salmon River upland, 240 Street / 232 Street as valley edges, and the Glen Valley, Campbell Valley and Otter roads — not an urban street grid. A specific parcel's reserve status is checked on the Agricultural Land Commission maps by PID or civic address. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- Where Langley's verified 55+ housing actually is, for contrast: in the urban communities, not the farm belt — Creekside Villa in the Aldergrove core, the Langley City downtown cluster (Huntsfield Green, the Parkway buildings, Glenmont Park), Willow Park Estates in Willoughby, and the Brookswood manufactured-home parks. None of those sits on farm land, and none is filed under this topic. Sources: the Langley-wide notes in ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/langley-foreclosures-court-ordered-sales-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md, Section 2.
- Schools, recreation and shopping are those of the nearest community to the parcel (Fort Langley, Walnut Grove, Aldergrove, Murrayville or Langley City) — the farm belt itself contains no school, recreation centre or shopping centre confirmed on an official source in the farms research file. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx
- Health: Langley Memorial Hospital, 22051 Fraser Highway, is the Fraser Health community hospital serving Langley, on the urban side of the belt. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Langley/langley-memorial-hospital

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **The strata age-bylaw rules, for the record — with no local strata to apply them to.** Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; age bylaws below 55 invalid since Nov 24, 2022, under Bill 44); the bylaw restricts residency, not ownership; Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19; and residential rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, while short-term restrictions remain allowed. A farm buyer gets none of this machinery, because a farm purchase is freehold land, not a strata lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **ALR housing caps are the rules that actually bind a 55+ farm buyer.** Land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **The second-dwelling rule is the ageing-in-place rule on a farm.** The ALC permits one additional residence without an ALC application where its conditions are met — detached, with a total floor area of 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and Township bylaws must also permit it. That is the legal route to a caregiver's or family member's dwelling on a Langley farm; anything larger needs an ALC application. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Township zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 2500** applies across the Township; Township material names Rural Zones RU-1 and RU-2 and Suburban Residential Zones SR-1, SR-2 and SR-3. A general RU-zone minimum parcel size from direct bylaw text was not confirmed in the farms research file — UNCONFIRMED. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/public-notices/public-notices-2026/2026-07-20-Public-Notice.pdf
- **Farm classification is a continuing obligation, not a one-time label.** BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income requirements at farm gate prices are $2,500 if the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha, and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha. A downsizing buyer who stops producing can lose the classification and its tax treatment. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Water and septic are diligence items with legal teeth:** under the Water Sustainability Act, domestic groundwater use does not need a licence, but non-domestic use including irrigation requires a water licence, and well records are searched in the provincial GWELLS registry; a home not on community sewer must use an onsite system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, with only an Authorized Person designing, installing or inspecting it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Property taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes and utility fees were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to current-year taxes unpaid at midnight July 2, 2026, with a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026, and utility fees are now billed separately. Farm classification, above, is separate from the municipal tax notice and is where the larger tax exposure sits. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ farm community or seniors farm complex in Langley?** No — none was verified on any source. Farm parcels are overwhelmingly freehold, strata farm product is not a Langley pattern, and ALR residences cannot be stratified. The 55+ complexes that exist in Langley are urban strata and manufactured-home parks in Aldergrove, Brookswood, Willoughby and Langley City. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: I'm 55+ and buying a farm to downsize onto. What limits the house I can build?** On ALR land, a principal residence built without an ALC application is capped at 500 m² total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. Confirm the parcel's ALR status on the ALC maps before designing anything. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can my adult child or a caregiver live in a second dwelling on the farm?** The ALC permits one additional residence without an application where its size rules are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels, detached — and Township bylaws must also permit it. There is no strata-style caregiver exemption here because there is no strata; the land rules are the whole answer. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: If I stop farming, do I keep farm-class taxes?** Not automatically: BC Assessment requires a producing farm to keep meeting minimum gross farm income thresholds that scale with the operation's size ($2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that; $10,000 under 0.8 ha). Plan the production, or lease the land to someone who produces, before assuming the classification survives. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Is the manufactured-home park on Fraser Highway a seniors park?** The large one, Langley Grove Estates, is explicitly not: its listings state no age restrictions and all ages welcome. The Aldergrove park on Fraser Highway states the same. A rural highway address does not make a park a 55+ park. https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/24330-fraser-highway/52
- **Q: Can I buy a farm parcel and later strata-title a seniors rancher development on it?** No source supports that route: ALR residences cannot be stratified, and a non-ALR rural parcel would still need Township zoning and subdivision approval — no such 55+ farm development exists or was proposed in any source checked. Treat any pitch of that kind as UNCONFIRMED until the ALC and Township positions are in writing. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Do I need a water licence for the farm?** Domestic groundwater use is exempt; non-domestic use, including irrigation, requires a licence under the Water Sustainability Act. Search GWELLS for the parcel's well record — older wells may not appear in the registry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- **Q: What about flood risk on valley farm land?** The Township's documented dyke system is on the Fraser River at the Salmon River mouth, and the Township directs applicants to check a property's designations and watercourses in GeoSource; township-wide floodplain mapping was not confirmed in the farms research file, so parcel-level status is a check, not an assumption. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/salmon-river-dike-upgrades.aspx
- **Q: Where would I actually go if I need care while living on a Langley farm?** To a community care home, reached through Fraser Health — for example Jackman Manor in Aldergrove, described by Fraser Health as care for elders who can no longer live safely or independently at home. There is no care option on farm land itself. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Langley/jackman-manor

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **235 km² is the context for everything on this page.** That is the area of the Township the Township itself states is in the Agricultural Land Reserve — most Langley farm parcels are reserve land with ALC housing and use rules layered over Township zoning, which is why "55+ farm" product does not exist here in strata form. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx
- **The suite rule on ALR land is narrower than in town.** In the Township's urban areas a suite or accessory unit is a zoning question; on ALR land a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence, and a detached second dwelling is capped at 90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size. Buyers transferring an urban "coach house" assumption onto a farm parcel will over-read their options. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **The farm belt's one big manufactured-home community markets itself on the opposite promise.** Langley Grove Estates sells "all ages welcome" and resort-style amenities on a rural Fraser Highway site — the belt's manufactured-home supply is aimed at families and downsizers alike, with no age gate. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3111178-150-3665-244-street-langley-british-columbia-v2z1n1-210000/brochure
- **SnapStats publishes no farm category.** The farms research file is explicit that the report's Langley and rural sub-area figures are residential figures used as context only — there are no official farm-sale counts, price-per-acre figures or farm days-on-market in that source, and any such number on a farm page is UNVERIFIED. (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, cited in ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/langley-farms.md.)
- **A Langley farm listing can carry a manufactured home without being a "mobile home" purchase.** Parcels like 24183 Fraser Hwy pair a manufactured home with barns and acreage and are priced on land value — the dwelling is incidental, the ALR land is the asset, and none of the park-tenancy or strata-age analysis applies. https://www.zillow.com/homedetails/24183-Fraser-Hwy-Langley-BC-V2Z-2L3/2071787377_zpid/
- **The Township's own seniors-housing zoning definition never touches the belt.** As documented in the Aldergrove 55+ notes, Zoning Bylaw 2500 defines "seniors' housing" as a use with a maximum dwelling-unit size — an urban project tool. No seniors-housing project on farm land was verified under it in any source checked. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=866EA2E9-916E-485E-9075-F59C90961D19&folderId=47498C260508154601958591
