# Canyon Heights (District of North Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Canyon Heights is a neighbourhood in the District of North Vancouver (not the City of North Vancouver) — an Upper Capilano neighbourhood on the Highland Boulevard corridor, west of Edgemont Village and east of the Capilano River, at the foot of Grouse Mountain. Exact boundary streets are UNVERIFIED; the confirmed anchor is the Highland Boulevard corridor, with Canyon Heights Elementary at 4501 Highland Boulevard inside the area. https://www.sd44.ca/school/canyonheights/Pages/default.aspx
- Nearby areas: Edgemont Village (east), Capilano Highlands (north), Forest Hills (north-east), Grouse Woods (north-west, across the Capilano River side) and Queensdale (south-east). The District's Official Community Plan names Edgemont, immediately east, as one of its six Village Centres — Edgemont is the area's walk-to retail, and it is a different neighbourhood from Canyon Heights. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- Housing form is the defining fact for this file: the area is detached houses under the District's R1 and R2 detached residential zones, between the Capilano River and the Mosquito / Mackay creek ravines. One neighbourhood description puts Canyon Heights at only 1% townhomes and 1% condos, with most homes single-family houses — there is effectively no strata stock here for a 55+ bylaw to attach to. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf and https://www.realestatevalley.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate.php?p=1
- Schools: School District 44, elementary (K–7) and secondary (8–12) only, no middle tier. For the sample address 4501 Highland Boulevard, the SD44 School Locator result is Canyon Heights Elementary (K–7) and Handsworth Secondary (8–12); Highlands Elementary, 3150 Colwood Drive, and Cleveland Elementary, 1255 Eldon Road, sit beside the area, and catchments are address-based — check each address with SD44. https://www.sd44.ca/school/canyonheights/Pages/default.aspx and https://handsworth.sd44.ca/
- Library: the Capilano Library, 3045 Highland Boulevard, is described by the North Vancouver District Public Library as "located in the heart of Edgemont Village" — east of Canyon Heights on the same boulevard. https://www.nvdpl.ca/capilano-library
- Recreation: the Delbrook Community Recreation Centre, 851 West Queens Road, south-east of the area, is the nearest NVRC full-service centre, with a main pool and leisure pool and indoor racquet facilities. Capilano River Regional Park (Metro Vancouver), west of the area on the Capilano River, offers the Capilano Pacific Trail and the Coho Loop Trail. https://www.nvrc.ca/facilities-fields/locations-hours/courts and https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/capilano-river-regional-park
- Transit: TransLink route 246 (Downtown / Highland) runs via Marine Drive, Capilano Road, Ridgewood Drive, Edgemont Boulevard, Highland Boulevard and Montroyal Boulevard through the area, crossing the Lions Gate Bridge downtown-bound; route 232 (Grouse Mountain / Phibbs Exchange) also runs via Highland Boulevard, Edgemont Boulevard and Capilano Road, and route 236 runs on Capilano Road on the west side. There is no SkyTrain, SeaBus or West Coast Express station in or near the sub-area — the SeaBus at Lonsdale Quay is a drive or bus ride away. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt246.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt232.pdf
- Health care: Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver, is the Vancouver Coastal Health hospital serving the North Shore — a 268-bed facility providing a full range of general and many specialized acute care services, including emergency and critical care. The North Vancouver Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 2nd floor, 221 West Esplanade, North Vancouver. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/north-vancouver-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
- Where the 55+ stock sits: nowhere inside the sub-area — see Section 2. The nearest seniors residence of any kind is at the area's south-east edge in Edgemont Village, on the same Highland Boulevard.

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**No verified 55+ ownership stock exists in Canyon Heights.** No age-restricted strata building, townhome complex or manufactured-home park with a Canyon Heights address was verified in any source checked — the underlying area research likewise confirms no named condo buildings or townhome complexes in Canyon Heights on any official or developer source, and the housing stock is predominantly freehold detached. A 55+ buyer who wants to own in this neighbourhood is buying a detached house (with suite / coach-house options under Section 3), not an age-restricted community. Do not pad a Canyon Heights 55+ page with product from Edgemont, Lynn Valley or Lonsdale.

Neither is in Canyon Heights or Edgemont. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B1%5D=city%3AVernon&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=desc

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. (In Canyon Heights this is a framework rule only — no strata in the sub-area was verified to hold such a bylaw, because no strata stock was verified at all.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **District zoning: Zoning Bylaw 3210, Section 525** sets the detached zones that cover Canyon Heights: the R1 zone minimum lot is 4,000 m² with 30 m width and 34 m depth, and the R2 zone minimum lot is 660 m² with 18 m width and 34 m depth, with modifiers R2(S1) at 335 m² / 10 m, R2(S2) at 1,100 m² / 24 m and R2(S3) at 4,000 m² / 30 m; an R2 principal building may include a secondary suite, and a coach house is a dwelling unit accessory to and detached from the principal dwelling unit. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Three units on a detached lot:** since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary. That suite-plus-coach-house form is the closest thing Canyon Heights offers to in-neighbourhood downsizing or a live-in caregiver / family arrangement: it is created by the owner under zoning, not bought as a 55+ product. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **SSMUH classification:** the District's GEOweb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop; provincial rules set 3 units on lots of 280 m² or smaller, 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on larger lots within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop. Lot-by-lot status is a District GeoWeb check. https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf
- **Official Community Plan: Bylaw 7900** covers this area, including its Development Permit Areas in Schedule B — seven DPAs including Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard. A Canyon Heights property can fall in one of these areas along the Capilano River side and on steeper ground, and the District publishes property-level GeoWeb reports, including modelled flood construction levels for a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m. https://dnv-docs.simplicitycms.ca/documents/ocp-schedule-b-dpa.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/
- **District housing-needs context:** the District's 2024 Housing Needs Report treats seniors as one of its emerging housing issues and states the District's stock is comprised largely of single-family homes (49%), a form that may not adequately serve the aging population, with greater diversity facilitating aging in place — the policy backdrop to Canyon Heights having houses to adapt, but no seniors product to buy. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/CDNV_DISTRICT_HALL%236399184-v1-DNV_Housing_Needs_Report_Final_.pdf
- **Property tax dates:** District of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 residential municipal rate is 1.83419 and the total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value. District single-family utility fees in 2026 include water at $932.80 and sewer/drainage at $1,255.60, with combined single-family utilities of $2,571. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Canyon Heights?** None in an age-restricted community — no verified 55+ strata, townhome complex or manufactured-home park exists in the sub-area in any source checked. What a 55+ buyer can buy here is a freehold detached house, with no age rule on who lives in it, and with suite / coach-house potential under District zoning. Buyers who need a true 55+ strata product have to look outside the sub-area. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf and https://www.realestatevalley.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate.php?p=1
- **Q: Is Amica Edgemont Village in Canyon Heights?** No — it is at 3225 Highland Boulevard in Edgemont Village, the neighbourhood immediately east of Canyon Heights, on the same boulevard. It is also rental/care (independent living, registered assisted living, long-term care and memory care), not a purchase. It is the nearest seniors residence to Canyon Heights, which is why it gets attached to the name — keep the two neighbourhoods separate. https://www.amica.ca/edgemont-village
- **Q: Can a Canyon Heights house hold a suite for a caregiver or family member?** Yes, under zoning rather than any age rule: an R2 principal building may include a secondary suite, a coach house is a separate accessory dwelling unit, and since June 2024 three units total (principal, suite, coach house) are permitted on single-family zoned lots within the Urban Containment Boundary. There is no 55+ test on any of it — occupancy is the owner's arrangement. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55 — does that matter for buying a house here?** No. A detached freehold house has no age bylaw at all. The spouse / partner exemption in Strata Property Regulation 7.01 matters only inside a 55+ strata, and no such strata was verified in Canyon Heights. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: If I buy a strata unit elsewhere on the North Shore, can I rent it out?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022 — but if the building is a 55+ strata, the tenant still has to satisfy the age bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver — Vancouver Coastal Health's 268-bed North Shore hospital, with emergency and critical care, general surgery, orthopedics, cardiac care, diagnostics, palliative care, rehabilitation and neurosurgery among its services. The North Vancouver Urgent and Primary Care Centre (221 West Esplanade, 2nd floor) is the urgent-care option. Both are in the Lonsdale area, a drive or bus ride south of Canyon Heights — no hospital or UPCC is inside the sub-area. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/north-vancouver-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
- **Q: Is there subsidized seniors housing I can apply for from Canyon Heights?** Yes, but it is not in Canyon Heights: the District's Kiwanis North Shore Housing Society buildings are in Lynn Valley — Lynn Manor (205 units, operated since 1974) and the newer Lynn Woods at 2555 Whiteley Court (106 one-bedroom apartments for low- and middle-income seniors). Apply through the society / BC Housing route for those buildings; the current age threshold is UNCONFIRMED after the reported post-opening change from 65 to 55 for the rent-geared-to-income homes — confirm with the society. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/new-affordable-rental-homes-for-seniors-coming-to-north-vancouver and http://globalawards.news/affordable-housing-project-for-seniors-lowers-age-to-fill-units/
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day without a car?** Buses only: route 246 runs from Highland Boulevard / Montroyal Boulevard directly downtown over the Lions Gate Bridge, and route 232 links Highland and Edgemont to Grouse Mountain and Phibbs Exchange; route 236 runs on Capilano Road. There is no rapid transit in the sub-area, so a car-light 55+ buyer should test the exact stop, frequency and hill walk for the specific street, not the neighbourhood name. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt246.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt232.pdf
- **Q: What hazard or permit checks matter on a Canyon Heights lot?** Creek, slope and wildfire checks: OCP Bylaw 7900 Schedule B sets Streamside Protection, Creek Hazard, Slope Hazard and Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Areas, and the District's GeoWeb publishes property-level reports including modelled flood construction levels for a 2100 horizon plus 0.6 m. River-side and steeper lots on the west and ravine edges are where this bites; check the specific lot before pricing a renovation, suite or coach house. https://dnv-docs.simplicitycms.ca/documents/ocp-schedule-b-dpa.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay on the North Shore?** The nearest rung is Amica Edgemont Village at the sub-area's edge (independent living through registered assisted living, long-term care and memory care, rental/care). The Registry also lists North Vancouver seniors residences outside this area — Chartwell Churchill House (150 West 29th St) and Cedar Garden Assisted Living (1250 Cedar Village Close, Vancouver Coastal Health) — and Lions Gate Hospital is the North Shore hospital. Nothing in that ladder is inside Canyon Heights itself. https://www.amica.ca/edgemont-village and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B1%5D=city%3AVernon&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=desc and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Canyon Heights 55+" is a house search wearing a strata label:** with only about 1% townhomes and 1% condos in the neighbourhood description and no named strata complex confirmed on any official or developer source, any listing, portal filter or page that presents a Canyon Heights "55+ community" is describing, at most, a detached house marketed to older buyers. There is no age bylaw, amenity building or strata behind it. https://www.realestatevalley.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate.php?p=1 and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Highland Boulevard crosses the answer:** the same boulevard holds Canyon Heights Elementary at 4501 Highland Boulevard inside the sub-area, the Capilano Library at 3045 Highland Boulevard in the heart of Edgemont Village, and Amica Edgemont Village at 3225 Highland Boulevard. Three "Highland Boulevard" seniors-and-services landmarks, two different neighbourhoods — a buyer searching by street alone will merge Canyon Heights and Edgemont into one place. https://www.sd44.ca/school/canyonheights/Pages/default.aspx and https://www.nvdpl.ca/capilano-library and https://www.amica.ca/edgemont-village
- **The downsizing product here is one the owner creates:** since June 2024 a Canyon Heights lot within the Urban Containment Boundary can hold a principal house, a secondary suite and a coach house — so the realistic 55+ move inside this neighbourhood is often selling and buying a house that already has (or can take) a suite / coach house for a caregiver, adult child or rental income, priced off the zoning facts in Section 3, not off any "senior-friendly" listing language. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **The District itself flags the mismatch:** its 2024 Housing Needs Report names seniors as an emerging housing issue and says a stock comprised largely of single-family homes (49% District-wide) may not adequately serve the aging population. Canyon Heights is that pattern in concentrated form — high-value detached houses, strong schools and trails, and no purpose-built seniors housing of any tenure inside the boundary. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/CDNV_DISTRICT_HALL%236399184-v1-DNV_Housing_Needs_Report_Final_.pdf
- **The subsidized seniors address people cite for "North Vancouver" is in Lynn Valley, and its age rule moved after opening:** Lynn Woods (2555 Whiteley Ct, 106 units, Kiwanis North Shore Housing Society) opened with a District-set minimum age of 65, then the District agreed to lower it — to 55 for the rent-geared-to-income homes in the report read — after units sat empty. Anyone quoting a fixed "65+" or "55+" rule for that building without checking the society's current terms is quoting a superseded version. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/new-affordable-rental-homes-for-seniors-coming-to-north-vancouver and http://globalawards.news/affordable-housing-project-for-seniors-lowers-age-to-fill-units/
- **Trail access and hazard maps are the same map here:** the Capilano River Regional Park trails (Capilano Pacific Trail, Coho Loop) that sell the west side of the neighbourhood run through the same river / creek terrain that puts west-edge and ravine lots into the OCP's creek, slope and wildfire Development Permit Areas. Price the amenity and the permit check together — the District's GeoWeb report for the specific lot settles both. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/capilano-river-regional-park and https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/
- **District, not City — and it changes the paperwork:** Canyon Heights is in the District of North Vancouver, so its zoning (Bylaw 3210), OCP (Bylaw 7900), tax rates and GeoWeb are District instruments; the City of North Vancouver's bylaws and rates, which govern Lonsdale and the hospital area, do not apply to a Canyon Heights purchase even though both share "North Vancouver" addresses and Lions Gate Hospital. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf
