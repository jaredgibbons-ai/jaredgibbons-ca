# Communities — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

This file is a UTILITY/HUB page (slug `communities`), not a neighbourhood. It is framed region-wide: how a 55+ buyer chooses between the Fraser Valley and Lower Mainland communities on jaredgibbons.ca/communities.html. Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT THIS HOME TYPE

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, and Strata Property Regulation 7.01, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** People under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents who were lawfully living in the unit when the bylaw passed; live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw, though short-term rental bylaws remain allowed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Manufactured-home parks run under a different Act.** In a pad-rental park the buyer owns the home and rents the site under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (BC); the park's 55+ rule is a park designation in its tenancy documents, not a strata bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Pad-rent increases are capped by formula:** once every 12 months, on 3 full months' notice — 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027, the proportional amount being the change in local government levies plus regulated park utility fees divided by the number of sites in the park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Co-operative and life-lease tenures are neither strata nor freehold:** a co-op buyer purchases shares/membership and a life-lease buyer purchases a lease interest — the documents for that specific community govern, and neither tenure is confirmed from the strata rules above. UNCONFIRMED for any specific community without its own documents. Sources: the co-op and life-lease entries in Section 2 above.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which community has the most 55+ homes to choose from?** Abbotsford, on this program's verified counts: 26 strata complexes, three 55+ parks and two life leases, ahead of Surrey's 15 strata complexes. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: I want South Surrey / White Rock. What is actually there?** The verified ownership stock is on the Surrey side: 15 Surrey strata complexes in total, clustered in Sunnyside Park, the King George Corridor and Newton, plus the flagged Sea Ridge Bays bare-land strata. A building named for White Rock may still be in Surrey, and the municipality decides which bylaws and tax calendar apply. Source: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/surrey-homes-for-sale-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption; children and live-in caregivers are exempt in defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I buy in a 55+ building before I turn 55?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. An under-55 buyer can own the lot but may not be able to live in it until they qualify or an exemption applies. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Is a manufactured-home park the cheapest way into a 55+ community?** No price comparison is stated here — no source in this research publishes a verified price or pad-rent comparison across tenures, so any "cheapest" claim is UNCONFIRMED. What is verifiable is the cost structure: in a park you own the home and rent the pad, and pad rent can rise once a year by the capped formula in Section 3. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Can I rent out a 55+ strata unit I buy?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant must still satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Q: Does the hub page's community advice cover Mission?** No — Mission has no block on the live communities page, a gap recorded in the research file for this slug. Mission's own verified 55+ pockets are covered in its sibling files. https://jaredgibbons.ca/communities.html and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/communities.md
- **Q: A listing says 45+ or "adult oriented." Is that the same as 55+?** No. Since Nov 24, 2022 a strata age bylaw below 55 is invalid, and in the sibling files 45+ and 40+ labels belong to parks or older marketing — Country Park Village (45+) and Rainbow Estates (40+) in Chilliwack are not 55+ stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and Section 2 above.
- **Q: Where should I look if I need care later, not just a smaller home?** Retirement residences and care residences are a separate, rental category in every sibling file — they are not a later stage of a strata purchase, and access to publicly funded care runs through Fraser Health, not a sale. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Are the prices in the communities page's examples current?** No figure from the hub page is adopted here: its two prose price examples are undated illustrations with no source cited on the page, flagged UNVERIFIED as market figures in the research file. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/communities.md

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The deepest 55+ market is the furthest practical one for many buyers.** Abbotsford's 26 verified strata complexes are more than Surrey and Langley combined on the standing rosters — the community choice for a 55+ buyer is often really a choice between choice itself (Abbotsford) and location (South Surrey), because the stock does not exist in equal depth in both. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Two Surrey complexes with near-identical names are different stratas.** Lakewood Heights (6211 W Boundary Dr) and Lakewood Place (6061 W Boundary Dr) sit near a third Boundary Drive complex, Lakeview Estates (6090 W Boundary Dr) — a search on one name mixes the others, and each has its own bylaws and fees. Source: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/surrey-homes-for-sale-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **A 60+ co-op hides inside Maple Ridge's 55+ results.** Haney Pioneer Village is 60+, not 55+ — a buyer searching "55+ Maple Ridge" will meet it and be a decade short of its rule if they are 55. Source: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/maple-ridge-homes-for-sale-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **All four of Maple Ridge's verified 55+ strata buildings are in one development area,** Fraserview Village in East Central — the rest of the city verified no 55+ strata stock in its sibling files. Source: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/maple-ridge-homes-for-sale-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **Port Coquitlam's single 55+ strata building carries a second mandatory monthly charge:** listing information for Mayfair Terrace states a required base service plan on top of the strata fee, so its monthly cost is not comparable to a plain strata fee elsewhere without reading the service agreement. Source: ~/workspace/local-notes/port-coquitlam-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **The newest 55+ stock in the Tri-Cities is rental, not strata.** Kew Lifestyles at Montrose in Port Coquitlam completed in 2023 as purpose-built rental; no new age-restricted strata project appears in the sources checked for that city. Source: ~/workspace/local-notes/port-coquitlam-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **Flagged names travel between files.** Carriage Lane is a flagged conflict in the Abbotsford research and also appears as a family townhouse name in Maple Ridge's checked-and-not list — it is counted as 55+ nowhere in this program. Sources: the Abbotsford and Maple Ridge sibling files in ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/
