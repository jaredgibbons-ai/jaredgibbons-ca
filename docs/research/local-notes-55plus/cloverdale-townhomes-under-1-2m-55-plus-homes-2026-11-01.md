## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why the Manor Ridge directory line "rentals not allowed" cannot be read at face value. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Property transfer tax in this band:** the general tiers are 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000; 2% of the portion greater than $200,000 and up to and including $2,000,000; 3% of the portion greater than $2,000,000 — every purchase in this band is taxed entirely in the 1% and 2% tiers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First time home buyers' program:** the full exemption requires a fair market value of $835,000 or less, with a partial exemption above $835,000 and below $860,000 — so only the lower slice of this band can use the program at all, and a purchase at the band's upper end cannot; all the program's other buyer and property conditions also apply. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Strata document framework:** BC strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal, and the provincial strata-housing pages are the official route for the rules on depreciation reports, contingency reserve funds and strata governance that a townhome buyer will meet in the document package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000** governs Cloverdale, including the small-scale multi-unit housing framework: up to 3 units on lots under 280 sq m, up to 4 units on lots over 280 sq m, and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. **OCP: "Surrey 2050" Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2026, No. 21900**, finally adopted June 15, 2026, replacing the 2013 OCP Bylaw No. 18020. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **Short-term rentals in Surrey:** a short-term rental is under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at $358 per year is required before operating — on top of any strata short-term rental bylaw. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Property taxes (city-wide):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **First-home programs:** the First Home Savings Account and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits are UNVERIFIED in this file — no live CRA page stating the current limits was read in this pass, so no figure is published here.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ townhome in Cloverdale in this band's product type?** One complex matches the form: The Estates at Manor Ridge (6140 192 St, a 1989 townhouse complex, listings state "1 occupant must be 55+"). The community's other verified 55+ complex, Carriage House, is a condo building, not a townhouse, so this townhome feed will never surface it. No price is stated for Manor Ridge in this file — whether a given Manor Ridge listing sits under the band's ceiling is a live-feed question. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/17695-58-avenue/102
- **Q: Most of this feed is Clayton product. Is any of it 55+?** Not in any source checked. The cards read for the slug's research were Clayton-labelled townhouses, and no source checked states an age restriction for any Clayton complex — Clayton's attached stock is general-market in the sources checked. A Clayton listing that reads "downsizer-friendly" is not an age bylaw (slug's research file, Sections 1 and 4).
- **Q: Do both of us have to be 55 at Manor Ridge?** No — its listings state 1 occupant must be 55+. Provincially, a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption) is exempt in any case — confirm the complex's own bylaw wording in its Form B. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer use the PTT exemption on a townhome in this band?** Only at the band's lower end: the full exemption requires a fair market value of $835,000 or less, with a partial exemption running to $860,000, and all the program's personal and property conditions must also be met — most of this band sits above those ceilings, so two townhomes on the same page can sit on opposite sides of the program. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: What PTT do I budget on a townhome under $1.2M?** The two lower tiers only: 1% to $200,000 and 2% on the portion up to $2,000,000 — the 3% tier and the further 2% over $3,000,000 do not arise in this band. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Can I have a pet at Manor Ridge?** Its listings allow 1 pet with restrictions in the listing checked. The community's other 55+ complex is the opposite — Carriage House listings state "No pets permitted" — so there is no Cloverdale-wide pet rule; read the individual complex's bylaws. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/17695-58-avenue/102
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a Manor Ridge townhome as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ townhome complex?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, including any older "rentals not allowed" record for Manor Ridge. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What should I check in the strata package for a townhome in this band?** Read the depreciation report and Form B before removing conditions, and remember strata disputes in BC can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal; garage configuration and driveway-apron parking are set by the complex's own strata plan and bylaws, so verify them in that package rather than from listing copy — the live page's strata-fee range and garage/apron claims are UNVERIFIED on any official or developer source in the slug's research. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool at Manor Ridge?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
