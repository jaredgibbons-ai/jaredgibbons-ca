# Bridgeport (Richmond) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Bridgeport is Richmond's north riverfront area on Lulu Island, around Bridgeport Station on the Canada Line — Bridgeport Road, River Road, Great Canadian Way and Van Horne Way — across the North Arm of the Fraser River from Vancouver. The City plans this ground as "Bridgeport Village," the northern village of the City Centre Area Plan; "Bridgeport" on its own is not a City planning-area name. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/station/bridgeport/map and https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/_4_-_Cary_Tsai_-_9100_9120_Bridgeport_Rd77457.pdf
- Character, stated openly: this is a heavily industrial and commercial area, not a conventional residential neighbourhood. The City Centre Area Plan describes Bridgeport as an industrial area in transition with an incomplete street grid and former rail alignments, and the plan prohibits residential use in the Urban Centre T5 and General Urban T4 (Areas A and B) designations that cover most of the village core. The residential stock board sources file under "Bridgeport RI" sits outside that core: the River Drive waterfront masterplans west of the station (Parc Riviera, Northview Estates) and houses and townhouses on the Bridgeport Road corridor to the south. https://Richmond.Ca/__shared/assets/specific_map_transect_bridgeport23854.pdf and https://www.zolo.ca/richmond-real-estate/10311-river-drive/84
- Nearby areas: Sea Island and YVR across the Middle Arm to the north-west (via the airport branch at Bridgeport Station), Brighouse / City Centre to the south along No. 3 Road, West Cambie and East Cambie to the south-east, and Vancouver across the North Arm to the north. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/station/bridgeport/map
- Transit — the area's defining feature: Bridgeport Station is the Canada Line junction station where the Richmond–Brighouse branch, the YVR–Airport branch and the Vancouver leg to Waterfront all meet, and a regional bus exchange (routes 403, 430 Express, 601, 352, 604 among them). TransLink's published SkyTrain timetable effective September 7, 2026 gives 26 minutes from Richmond–Brighouse Station to Waterfront Station; a Bridgeport-specific published minute figure is UNVERIFIED. https://RICHMOND.CA/__shared/assets/210_public23846.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt991.pdf
- Entertainment anchor: River Rock Casino Resort sits at Bridgeport Station on the Great Canadian Way / River Road side, shown on TransLink's station entrance map; its civic address on an official source is UNVERIFIED. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/skytrain-accessible-entrance-maps/bridgeport_station_elevator_map.pdf
- Schools: School District 38 (Richmond) catchments are address-based with no middle-school tier (elementary K–7, secondary 8–12). The village core itself is designated non-residential in the City Centre Area Plan; listings for the River Drive complexes name Robert J. Tait and R.C. Talmey elementaries as nearby schools, but the SD38 locator result for any specific Bridgeport address is UNVERIFIED — check the exact address. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments and https://www.zolo.ca/richmond-real-estate/10311-river-drive/84
- Library and health care: the nearest full library branch listed by Richmond Public Library is the Brighouse (Main) Branch, 100-7700 Minoru Gate, south in the City Centre; the nearest hospital is Richmond Hospital, 7000 Westminster Highway (Vancouver Coastal Health), and Richmond City Centre Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at #110, 4671 No. 3 Road. https://rpl.yourlibrary.ca/about/hours-locations and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/richmond-city-centre-urgent-and-primary-care-centre/plan-your-visit
- Where the seniors housing actually is: **nowhere verified in Bridgeport.** There is no age-restricted strata, no 55+ rental, no retirement residence and no subsidized seniors building with a Bridgeport address in any source checked — the plan prohibition on residential use in the village core is the structural reason, and the River Drive and corridor product that does exist is general-market and family-marketed. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **The City's own seniors threshold is 55.** Richmond's 2019 Housing Needs and Co-Location staff report discusses seniors' housing need with seniors defined as individuals aged 55 years and older. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/_2_HousingNeeds_CoLocationOpp_PLN_071619_54160.pdf
- **Zoning — why there is no seniors stock in the core:** Bridgeport Village land use is set by the City Centre Area Plan transects — Urban Centre T5 (residential prohibited; office, hotel, institutional, retail, restaurant and entertainment uses) and General Urban T4 with Industrial Reserve overlays (residential prohibited; light industry base use). Any future seniors building in Bridgeport would first need a plan or zoning change in the village, or a corridor site like the Bridgeport Road RTM3 townhouse rezoning at 10760–10780 Bridgeport Road and 3033–3111 Shell Road. https://Richmond.Ca/__shared/assets/specific_map_transect_bridgeport23854.pdf and https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/_2_Application_1082009BCLtd_BridgeportRd_PLN_07041850976.pdf
- **Airport rules overlay everything here:** the City Centre Area Plan map states Richmond's Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development (ANSD) Policy applies throughout Bridgeport Village; a City data sheet places sites here in Aircraft Noise Sensitive Use Areas 1B and 2 and in Flood Construction Level Area A; and the City identifies Bridgeport Station as a Transit-Oriented Area under Bill 47 while stating that Federal Airport Zoning Regulations and their maximum heights govern over the TOA heights here. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/_1_-_8320-8440_Bridgeport_Rd___8351_Sea_Island_Way_PH_07171747799.pdf and https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Flood — a riverfront area inside a dike-protected island city:** Richmond is made up of islands within the floodplain of the Fraser River at an average of 1 metre above sea level; the City's flood protection system includes 49 km of dikes and 39 drainage pump stations, is designed to handle a 1:500 flood event, and dikes are being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise. New construction is subject to flood construction levels and dike setbacks under the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204, and City data sheets place Bridgeport Village sites in Flood Construction Level Area A. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm and https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Property taxes and utilities:** City of Richmond property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. Richmond's 2026 typical single-family metered utility total is $2,251 per year, including a $342 flood protection line. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm and https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/finance/utility-fees/utilityrates.htm
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** (Zone 3: Richmond and West Delta directory, May 2025) — with no Bridgeport building in it. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Bridgeport?** None that any source verifies. There is no age-restricted strata anywhere in the sub-area — the village core prohibits residential use under its T5 and T4 (Areas A and B) plan designations, and the homes that do exist (River Drive masterplans, Bridgeport Road corridor) are general-market. A 55+ buyer in Bridgeport is buying a regular condo, townhome or house with no age bylaw. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Parc Riviera has a pool, clubhouse and one-level townhomes pitched at downsizers. Is it 55+?** No. Building sources record Parc Riviera's restrictions as pets allowed and rentals allowed, with no age restriction, and its listings market daycare and playground amenities to families in the same breath as downsizer floorplans. "Downsizer-friendly" in a listing is not an age bylaw. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/parc-riviera-10011-river-drive and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3105336-18-10133-river-drive-richmond-british-columbia-v6x0k8-968000/brochure
- **Q: Is Bridgeport Court the Bridgeport 55+ building?** It is neither Bridgeport nor 55+ on the sources checked: listings place 3051 Airey Drive in West Cambie, and building sources record only pet and rental restrictions for it. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3048878-3051-airey-drive-306-richmond-bc-v6x-3x6/brochure and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/bridgeport-court-3051-airey-drive
- **Q: Could a seniors building be built in Bridgeport Village in future?** Not under the current plan in the core: the T5 and T4 (Areas A and B) designations covering most of the village prohibit residential use outright, so any residential — seniors or otherwise — starts with a plan amendment, not a building permit. The corridor south of the village is where the City has actually rezoned for homes (the RTM3 townhouse site on Bridgeport Road / Shell Road). https://Richmond.Ca/__shared/assets/specific_map_transect_bridgeport23854.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. If we buy into a 55+ strata elsewhere in Richmond, can we both live there?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in the defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. Under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a 55+ parent can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Q: What do the airport rules mean for a home here?** The ANSD Policy applies throughout Bridgeport Village, City data sheets place sites in Aircraft Noise Sensitive Use Areas 1B and 2, and federal Airport Zoning Regulation heights govern over the station area's transit-oriented heights — noise-sensitive design and disclosure are part of every purchase file in this area, for seniors housing as much as any other. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/_1_-_8320-8440_Bridgeport_Rd___8351_Sea_Island_Way_PH_07171747799.pdf
- **Q: Is Bridgeport in a floodplain?** Yes — City data sheets place village sites in Flood Construction Level Area A under Bylaw 8204, the River Drive complexes sit directly behind the North Arm dike, and city-wide Richmond sits in the Fraser River floodplain behind 49 km of dikes designed for a 1:500 event, with dike raising from 3.5 m to 4.7 m under way. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in Bridgeport?** No retirement residence, assisted-living residence or care home with a Bridgeport address was verified — the care step means leaving the area for Richmond's City Centre, Steveston or Hamilton residences. The offset is access: Bridgeport Station puts the Canada Line junction and the regional bus exchange at the area's door for getting to services elsewhere. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B1%5D=city%3ARichmond&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc and https://RICHMOND.CA/__shared/assets/210_public23846.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The newest homes near the village are marketed under a different neighbourhood's name.** Northgate, the 19 townhouses completed in 2025 on the City's Bridgeport Road Corridor rezoning site (3033 Shell Road), are described in listings as an East Cambie community — the same ground the City's file calls the Bridgeport Road Corridor. A buyer searching only "Bridgeport" misses them, and a buyer searching "Bridgeport seniors" finds nothing at all, because there is nothing to find. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3106501-8-3033-shell-road-richmond-british-columbia-v6x0b8-1059000/brochure and https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/_2_Application_1082009BCLtd_BridgeportRd_PLN_07041850976.pdf
- **The plan promises a community centre and library that are not site-specific yet.** The City Centre Area Plan states a Community Centre (North) and Library Lending Services are to be situated in Bridgeport, Aberdeen or Capstan Village or within 400 m of the village centre — a plan commitment spanning three villages, not a Bridgeport facility a buyer can walk to today. https://Richmond.Ca/__shared/assets/specific_map_transect_bridgeport23854.pdf
- **The station area's height rules are federal, not municipal.** At Bridgeport Station — a designated Transit-Oriented Area where provincial heights would normally apply — the City states Federal Airport Zoning Regulations govern over the TOA heights, because this is the YVR flight-path side of Richmond. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **The village core is still being assembled parcel by parcel.** Current City applications include RZ 24-043066 at 9100 and 9120 Bridgeport Road (School & Institutional Use to Auto-Oriented Commercial) — commercial, not residential, change is what is actually moving through the village. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/_4_-_Cary_Tsai_-_9100_9120_Bridgeport_Rd77457.pdf
- **Bridgeport Court's build year is not settled in the sources:** listing data says 1994, building sources say 1995. Minor — but it is the kind of detail that should be checked against the strata plan before it is repeated in marketing. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/bridgeport-court-3051-airey-drive and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3048878-3051-airey-drive-306-richmond-bc-v6x-3x6/brochure
- **The dike is part of the utility bill:** Richmond's 2026 typical single-family metered utility total of $2,251 per year includes a $342 flood protection line — in Bridgeport that charge buys the North Arm dike the River Drive complexes sit behind. https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/finance/utility-fees/utilityrates.htm
- **Richmond's two money dates:** 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% on any outstanding balance after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
