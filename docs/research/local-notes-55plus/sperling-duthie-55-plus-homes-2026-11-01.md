# Sperling-Duthie — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Sperling-Duthie is a City of Burnaby MLS sub-area in central Burnaby, around Sperling Avenue and Duthie Avenue, west and north of Deer Lake and crossed by Canada Way. Buckingham Heights, the prestige pocket on the slope east of Deer Lake, sits within/adjacent to this sub-area. (SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.) ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sperling-duthie.md
- Deer Lake Park borders the sub-area: its trails connect the lake with the Burnaby Art Gallery, the Shadbolt Centre for the Arts, Burnaby Village Museum and Hart House restaurant; the park's main address is 6450 Deer Lake Ave with access from Sperling Avenue. The boardwalk section by the boat launch is closed from September 21, 2026 for a full replacement, with completion scheduled by winter 2027 and a signed detour via Sperling Avenue and Deer Lake Drive. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- Sperling Avenue fronting Deer Lake Park lies within the Deer Lake Park Heritage Precinct, where the City's heritage register lists early-1900s houses such as the Woodward House at 5195 Sperling Ave (built 1912). https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/list?objectType_facet=Landmark%20Description%20&objectType_facet=History%20Links&ps=50&q=&sort=title_sort%20asc&creator_facet=johnreid000000%7CJohn%20Reid&creator_facet=bernardrhill000000%7CBernard%20R.%20Hill&place_facet=sperlingavenue000000%7CSperling%20Avenue&p=1
- Schools are School District 41 (Burnaby): École Sperling Elementary (2200 Sperling Ave) is the in-area elementary school. An SD41 locator run for 2200 Sperling Ave returned Sperling Elementary (K–7) and **Burnaby North Secondary** (8–12) — not Burnaby Central Secondary (6011 Deer Lake Parkway), which is the secondary school located in the Deer Lake area but is not the catchment school for that address. Catchments are address-specific. https://sperling.burnabyschools.ca/ and https://burnabyschools.ca
- Burnaby City Hall is at 4949 Canada Way, just west of the sub-area, and Burnaby Hospital (3935 Kincaid St, Fraser Health) is just south of Canada Way; its redevelopment includes a new six-storey pavilion with 83 beds in Phase 1 (2021–2026) and, in Phase 2 (dates to be determined), the 160-bed Keith and Betty Beedie Acute Care Tower and a new cancer centre. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/capital-projects/projects/burnaby-hospital/
- Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station, an elevated Millennium Line SkyTrain station at Sperling Avenue and Lougheed Highway, serves the sub-area's north end, with bus routes 110, 134 and 144 connecting there (the 144 runs Metrotown Station–SFU); Burnaby Lake Regional Park is walkable directly from the station. Homes at the Deer Lake end rely on those buses to reach the station. https://alz.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/skytrain-accessible-entrance-maps/sperling_burnaby_lake_station_elevator_map.pdf and https://burnabylakepark.ca/directions/transit/
- There is no enclosed mall or town centre inside the sub-area; Burnaby's plan framework designates Brentwood (northwest) and Metrotown (south) as the city's town centres and they are this area's main shopping destinations. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- Where 55+ housing concentrates: **nowhere inside Sperling-Duthie.** The sub-area is almost entirely detached single-family housing with effectively no named condo or townhome complexes; the seniors-specific buildings sit on its western edge along the Canada Way / Norland Avenue corridor (Section 2), and even their exact MLS sub-area assignment is UNCONFIRMED.

### Subsidized seniors rental (non-profit, income-based — not ownership)

- **Dania Gardens — 4277 Norland Ave (western edge).** 155 new affordable rental homes for seniors opened in 2025: 109 subsidized suites for seniors with low incomes and 46 market-rate suites for moderate incomes, in a six-storey building with adaptable/accessible design, amenity spaces, patios, laundry and underground parking. BC Housing provided nearly $17 million and the City of Burnaby $1.6 million; the Dania Society owns and operates it. Rental only. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/affordable-homes-open-for-seniors-in-burnaby
- **Carl Mortensen Manor — Dania campus, Norland Ave.** Together with Dania Gardens, the society states Carl Mortensen Manor and Dania Gardens provide over 200 subsidized and market affordable housing suites for individuals 55 and over, supported by BC Housing. The City's housing agreement for the Dania Gardens lands (4279 Norland Ave, Bylaw 14477, 2022) defines "Senior" as an individual 55 years or older. https://www.dania.bc.ca/about-us/about-dania/ and https://bylaws.burnaby.ca/media/14000/14477.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 — in effect July 1, 2026.** The new Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026, superseding the old 1965 bylaw framework; the City had already consolidated its single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District in June 2024 — R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Burnaby 2050 OCP:** the City adopted the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan on December 9, 2025; it replaces the 1998 OCP Bylaw, and its land use map takes precedence over older neighbourhood community plan maps where they conflict — check it before assuming density or subdivision potential on a large Sperling-Duthie lot. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Burnaby taxes:** 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
- **Heritage Precinct rule:** properties on Sperling Avenue fronting Deer Lake Park sit in the Deer Lake Park Heritage Precinct; heritage-register properties carry heritage review before renovations or a rebuild — check the register before planning work. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/list?objectType_facet=Landmark%20Description%20&objectType_facet=History%20Links&ps=50&q=&sort=title_sort%20asc&creator_facet=johnreid000000%7CJohn%20Reid&creator_facet=bernardrhill000000%7CBernard%20R.%20Hill&place_facet=sperlingavenue000000%7CSperling%20Avenue&p=1
- **How "55+" is secured in non-market housing here:** not by strata bylaw but by housing agreement — the City's Dania housing agreement (Bylaw 14477) secures the Norland Ave homes as non-market rental and defines "Senior" as 55 years or older, which is why Dania Gardens / Carl Mortensen Manor can be described as 55+ rental while no strata in the sub-area can make the same claim. https://bylaws.burnaby.ca/media/14000/14477.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ condo or townhome I can buy in Sperling-Duthie?** No — no verified 55+ ownership complex exists inside the sub-area, and there are effectively no named strata complexes here at all: the research file counts 5 attached listings against 28 detached listings in September 2026. A buyer who needs strata ownership must look to other Burnaby sub-areas or other cities. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sperling-duthie.md
- **Q: What seniors housing is nearby, then?** The Dania Campus of Care on Norland Ave at Canada Way, on the sub-area's western edge: independent affordable rental for 55+ (Dania Gardens, 155 homes opened 2025; Carl Mortensen Manor), assisted living (Dania Manor, 40 publicly funded + 10 private-pay suites) and long-term care (Dania Home) on one 7-acre campus — plus Chartwell Carlton, a private rental independent-living residence at Canada Way and Gilmore. All of it is rental or care; none of it is buyable, and the campuses' exact MLS sub-area assignment is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.dania.bc.ca/about-us/about-dania/ and https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/affordable-homes-open-for-seniors-in-burnaby and https://chartwell.com/bc/burnaby/carlton
- **Q: Why do downsizers still buy houses here?** Lot and location: the stock runs from post-war bungalows on the flatter western streets to estate homes in Buckingham Heights, with Deer Lake Park at the door and Burnaby Hospital just south of Canada Way. A bungalow gives single-level living, but it is a general-market freehold house — no age bylaw, no strata services. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sperling-duthie.md
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Burnaby?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a 55+ unit for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. Note the contrast at Dania: its 55+ eligibility is secured by a housing agreement on rental housing, not by strata law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://bylaws.burnaby.ca/media/14000/14477.pdf
- **Q: Can I add units to a Sperling-Duthie house for family or a caregiver?** The R1 SSMUH district allows 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 on larger lots and 6 on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified — now carried in the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (in effect July 1, 2026). Large Buckingham Heights and Canada Way-edge lots are where this starts to matter for pricing. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St, just south of Canada Way — a Fraser Health hospital mid-redevelopment, with an 83-bed six-storey pavilion in Phase 1 (2021–2026) and a 160-bed acute care tower plus a new cancer centre planned in Phase 2 (dates to be determined). https://www.fraserhealth.ca/capital-projects/projects/burnaby-hospital/
- **Q: How is transit if I give up driving?** Split by end of the sub-area: the north end walks to Sperling–Burnaby Lake SkyTrain Station (Millennium Line; buses 110, 134, 144), while the Deer Lake end depends on those buses to reach the station — the transit claim does not stretch to the lake end. https://alz.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/skytrain-accessible-entrance-maps/sperling_burnaby_lake_station_elevator_map.pdf
- **Q: Is the market here active enough to sell into?** It was quiet in September 2026: 1 detached sale against 28 active listings (4% sales ratio) and no attached sales in the sub-area; Burnaby-wide, the detached median was $1,825,000 and the condos & townhomes median $686,000 that month (SnapStats GVR September 2026 report). Low turnover means pricing to the most recent comparable is what moves a listing. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sperling-duthie.md

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The newest seniors housing near this sub-area opened in 2025 — and it is all rental.** Dania Gardens (4277 Norland Ave, 155 homes: 109 subsidized + 46 market-rate) is the Dania Society's fourth campus building; the society has run non-profit seniors housing in Burnaby since 1938 and its care home opened in 1944. No new age-restricted strata product accompanies it anywhere in the sub-area. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/affordable-homes-open-for-seniors-in-burnaby and https://www.dania.bc.ca/about-us/about-dania/
- **The catchment secondary for Sperling Avenue is not the school beside the lake.** The SD41 locator run for 2200 Sperling Ave returned Burnaby North Secondary, not Burnaby Central — Central is the secondary school physically in the Deer Lake area, which is exactly the assumption a listing is likely to print. Verify by exact address. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sperling-duthie.md
- **The Deer Lake boardwalk is mid-replacement.** The boardwalk section by the boat launch closed September 21, 2026 for a full replacement, completion scheduled by winter 2027, with the signed detour running via Sperling Avenue and Deer Lake Drive — disclose it when marketing a home on the park's trail network. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- **Burnaby rewrote both governing documents within seven months.** The Burnaby 2050 OCP was adopted December 9, 2025 (replacing the 1998 OCP, with its land use map prevailing over older community plan maps), and the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026. Any zoning or OCP citation dated before those adoptions is stale for this sub-area. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **The Sperling Avenue park frontage floods and has no established sidewalks.** The Burnaby Lake Park Association warns the Sperling Ave / Sprott St frontage has no established sidewalks and the road will flood after heavy rainfall; parcel-level floodplain status for individual Sperling-Duthie lots is UNVERIFIED — check lot by lot. https://burnabylakepark.ca/directions/transit/
- **Heritage review is a live constraint on the lake side of Sperling Ave.** The Deer Lake Park Heritage Precinct includes registered early-1900s houses on Sperling Avenue (e.g., the Woodward House, 5195 Sperling Ave, built 1912) — a rebuild plan on a listed property carries heritage review, which sellers near the lake routinely over- or under-estimate. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/list?objectType_facet=Landmark%20Description%20&objectType_facet=History%20Links&ps=50&q=&sort=title_sort%20asc&creator_facet=johnreid000000%7CJohn%20Reid&creator_facet=bernardrhill000000%7CBernard%20R.%20Hill&place_facet=sperlingavenue000000%7CSperling%20Avenue&p=1
