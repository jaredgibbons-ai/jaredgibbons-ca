# S.W. Marine (Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- S.W. Marine is the Greater Vancouver board's sub-area name ("S.W. Marine") for the southwest corner of the City of Vancouver along Southwest Marine Drive and the north arm of the Fraser River, taking in the Southlands area and the streets between roughly Granville Street and the Musqueam lands/Camosun Street side. Nearby areas are Kerrisdale, Marpole, Dunbar-Southlands and Oakridge, with Musqueam Indian Reserve 2 adjoining to the west/southwest. (Area research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sw-marine.md, read 2026-11-01.)
- Southlands is governed by its own zoning district: the RA-1 (Limited Agriculture) District, created in 1955 specifically for Southlands, whose intent is "to maintain and encourage the semi-rural, equestrian and limited agricultural nature of this District," permitting one-family dwellings and, in specific circumstances, infill one-family dwellings; the provincial Agricultural Land Reserve designation was implemented over the area in 1973, and the City's Southlands Plan (1988) remains the area plan, now read within the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-plan-southlands.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-ra-1.pdf
- Elsewhere in the sub-area, standard detached-house lots fall under Vancouver's multiplex framework (By-law No. 13817 replaced the RS districts with the R1-1 district) — but RA-1 Southlands lots are governed by their own district schedule, not R1-1, and must be checked individually. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf
- Schools: Southlands Elementary (5351 Camosun Street), a Vancouver School Board (SD39) International Baccalaureate Primary Years Programme school, is the public elementary school in this corner of the city and is the designated catchment school for students from the Musqueam Nation; for the sample address 5351 Camosun Street the SD39 locator gives Southlands Elementary and Point Grey Secondary. Catchments for other addresses are address-specific and otherwise UNVERIFIED. https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods and https://media.vsb.bc.ca/media/Default/medialib/2026-southlands-slp.817ed592572.pdf
- Flood setting: the City's Flood Plain Standards and Requirements apply flood construction levels and setbacks to lands in the designated floodplain, which includes the Fraser River coastal floodplain, and the Standards contain a dedicated section (Section 6.0) for the RA-1 District; the City publishes the designated floodplain as an open dataset. Designation for an individual lot must be confirmed against that dataset. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/guidelines-flood-plain-standards-requirements.pdf and https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- Transit is bus-based: TransLink's 49 (Metrotown Station–UBC), a Frequent Transit Network route, runs along West 49th Avenue and, west of Dunbar Street, along Southwest Marine Drive itself, with stops on SW Marine Drive before continuing to UBC; the nearest Canada Line station is Marine Drive Station in Marpole, east of the sub-area. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt049.pdf
- Recreation anchors: McCleery Golf Course (7188 Macdonald Street), a City of Vancouver Park Board golf course at the area's southwest edge, and Deering Island Park (3530 Deering Island Place), a semi-natural Park Board park on the Fraser River; the Fraser River trail/dyke paths complete the local trail network. http://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/mccleery-golf-course.aspx and http://covapp.vancouver.ca/parkfinder/parkdetail.aspx?inparkid=38
- Shopping is outside the sub-area: there is no retail strip within S.W. Marine itself; the closest established shopping streets are Kerrisdale Village (West 41st Avenue) to the north and Marpole (Granville Street) to the east — stated as an area characteristic; specific store tenancy is UNVERIFIED.
- Where 55+ housing concentrates: there is no 55+ ownership housing anywhere in the sub-area, and almost no attached housing at all — RA-1's one-dwelling-per-lot framework means there is effectively no strata, rental-apartment or townhouse supply inside Southlands itself. The sub-area's only seniors buildings are a non-profit seniors rental on SW Marine Drive itself and a long-term care home in a heritage estate on SW Marine Drive (Section 2).

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. Moot across most of this sub-area, where the housing is freehold detached rather than strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: the RA-1 District Schedule (Zoning and Development By-law, City of Vancouver)** is the rule that shapes this sub-area: intent to maintain the semi-rural, equestrian and limited agricultural character, permitting single detached houses and, in specific circumstances, infill single detached houses — with outright/conditional uses including nursery/field crop/fruit farm uses outright and stables conditional. There is no strata, townhouse or apartment form in the district schedule. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-ra-1.pdf
- **Plan: the Southlands Plan (1988)** remains the area plan, now read within the Vancouver Official Development Plan (which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026). https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-plan-southlands.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf
- **ALR overlay:** the provincial Agricultural Land Reserve designation was implemented over Southlands in 1973; whether an individual lot sits inside the ALR boundary must be confirmed parcel by parcel, and ALR parcels carry the ALC's residence rules on top of RA-1. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-plan-southlands.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Flood rules are codified, with an RA-1-specific section.** The City's Flood Plain Standards and Requirements (adopted September 16, 2014, effective January 1, 2015, amended March 7, 2023) impose flood construction level and setback requirements under the Vancouver Building By-law on designated-floodplain lands, and Section 6.0 of the Standards deals specifically with the RA-1 District. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/guidelines-flood-plain-standards-requirements.pdf
- **Seniors housing in Vancouver zoning is a defined use, not an age bylaw.** The City's Zoning and Development By-law treats seniors accommodation under uses such as Special Needs Residential Facility — Congregate Housing ("a building containing residential units specifically to accommodate seniors as they age, with separate common areas for dining and socializing, and where support services are provided") and Seniors Supportive or Assisted Housing — which is how a project like Casa Mia's CD-1 rezoning is framed, rather than through any strata age restriction. https://council.vancouver.ca/20040224/ph5.htm and https://council.vancouver.ca/20140218/documents/p3.pdf
- **City taxes and the Empty Homes Tax (2026):** Vancouver's 2026 advance property taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid balances; separately, the Empty Homes Tax charges 3% of assessed value on homes declared or deemed empty, with the annual declaration due February 3, 2026 and payment due April 16, 2026 — a live cost on any estate home bought and left vacant. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in S.W. Marine?** None that any source verifies — there is no age-restricted strata, co-op, life lease or park in the sub-area at all. A 55+ buyer here is buying a regular freehold detached or estate home, mostly in Southlands, with no age bylaw and no strata. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Why is there no condo or townhouse stock to downsize into here?** Zoning, mainly: the RA-1 district (in place since 1955) permits single detached houses and only limited infill single detached houses, so there is effectively no strata, rental-apartment or townhouse supply inside Southlands itself; the sub-area's attached stock is confined to the conventional streets at its Kerrisdale and Marpole edges, none of it age-restricted in any source checked. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-ra-1.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Vancouver?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I keep horses on a Southlands property?** Southlands is the one part of Vancouver planned for it: the RA-1 district's stated intent expressly supports the area's equestrian and limited-agricultural character, and stables are a conditional use in the district schedule. Permitted use is still property-specific, and the ALR designation (applied in 1973) may govern the parcel — confirm zoning and ALR status before relying on equestrian use. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-ra-1.pdf and https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-plan-southlands.pdf
- **Q: Is flooding a concern on the riverfront streets?** Yes, and it is regulated: the City's Flood Plain Standards apply flood construction levels and setbacks to designated-floodplain lands including the Fraser River coastal floodplain, with a dedicated section for the RA-1 district. Check the specific lot against the City's designated-floodplain dataset and look for registered flood covenants on title before subject removal. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/guidelines-flood-plain-standards-requirements.pdf and https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in the area?** Only at the long-term care step, and only at one address: Casa Mia (1920 SW Marine Dr) is a VCH-contracted long-term care home with 64 subsidized beds per the health authority's listing. There is no independent-living or assisted-living retirement residence in the sub-area — that step means leaving S.W. Marine. Subsidized long-term care access runs through Vancouver Coastal Health, not a waitlist you join by buying. http://www.vch.ca/en/location/casa-mia
- **Q: Is there any subsidized seniors rental in the area?** One building: the Hungarian Senior Citizens Home, 1564 SW Marine Dr — 54 units for senior citizens in a 1988 non-profit building run by the Canadian-Hungarian Housing Society (BC Housing tender document, 2024). It is society rental at the sub-area's eastern edge, with board-level placement of that address UNCONFIRMED — nothing there is for sale. https://canadabuys.canada.ca/en/tender-opportunities/tender-notice/bcitt1070-2425-080
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** One frequent bus: the 49 runs along Southwest Marine Drive west of Dunbar Street (stops at Blenheim and Balaclava, among others) to UBC and Metrotown. The nearest Canada Line station is Marine Drive Station in Marpole, east of the sub-area — there is no rail station in S.W. Marine. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt049.pdf
- **Q: Can I use Vancouver's multiplex rules to split a big Southlands lot?** Do not assume so: By-law No. 13817's R1-1 multiplex permissions apply to standard detached lots, but RA-1 Southlands lots are governed by their own district schedule (one dwelling per lot, limited infill in specific circumstances), not R1-1 — a zoning confirmation is required before quoting any multiplex or subdivision value. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-ra-1.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The semi-rural character is locked in by bylaw, not by neighbours.** Southlands' RA-1 district has held since 1955 and its area plan since 1988 — "protected character" is a documented policy fact here, which is also why no downsizer product will be built inside Southlands under the current framework. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-plan-southlands.pdf
- **The one care home in the area is inside a 1932 heritage mansion.** Casa Mia — built for brewer George Conrad Reifel, "A"-listed on the Vancouver Heritage Register — was rezoned CD-1 in the 2014 process specifically to become a Community Care Facility for seniors with heritage designation attached; the care home and the protected mansion are the same property. https://council.vancouver.ca/20140218/documents/p3.pdf
- **Casa Mia's bed count depends on which source you read.** The health authority's current listing says 64 subsidized beds; the operator offers private-pay rooms on top of that; and published total-bed figures run 87, 90 and 95 across the 2016 announcement, a 2017 advisory-committee letter and a Family Practice listing. Anyone quoting a single total is picking one source — the file flags the conflict rather than choosing. http://www.vch.ca/en/location/casa-mia and https://bccare.ca/2016/06/vch-announces-expansion-residential-care-capacity/
- **Three tenures sit within a few blocks of each other.** City of Vancouver freehold (RA-1/R1-1), and Musqueam reserve leasehold at the area's edge, finance, insure and resell differently — and Southlands Elementary, inside the city, is the catchment school for Musqueam students, so the two communities are interleaved even though the land jurisdiction is not. https://media.vsb.bc.ca/media/Default/medialib/2026-southlands-slp.817ed592572.pdf
- **"SW Marine Drive" in a headline does not mean Southlands.** The Province's 102-home New Chelsea Society project at 75 and 95 SW Marine Dr is on the eastern Marine Drive corridor, kilometres from Southlands — Marine Drive addresses run across the whole south side of the city, and the sub-area in this file is only the Granville-to-Musqueam stretch. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2017-2021/2020MAH0005-000089.pdf
- **A withdrawn rezoning shows which way proposals go here.** The 2090 SW Marine Dr application (heritage retention plus two infill buildings with 25 secured market rental units, R1-1 to CD-1, under the Affordable Housing Choices Interim Rezoning Policy) was withdrawn in September 2025 — rental infill at the sub-area's edge is proposed project-by-project, not permitted as of right. https://www.shapeyourcity.ca/2090-sw-marine-dr?tool=qanda
- **The Empty Homes Tax is a real carrying cost on an empty estate home.** Vancouver charges 3% of assessed value on homes declared or deemed empty, with the declaration due February 3, 2026 for the 2026 cycle — on S.W. Marine assessed values, leaving a purchased estate vacant while planning a move is an expensive default. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
