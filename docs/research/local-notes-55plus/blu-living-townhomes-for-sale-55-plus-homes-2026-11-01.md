## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. At Blu Living this matters in the negative: the complex is strata, but no source states it has a 55+ bylaw — or any age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. The live page for this complex states the same frame: long-term rentals cannot be restricted under the Nov 24, 2022 amendments, while short-term rentals remain subject to strata bylaws and municipal licensing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://jaredgibbons.ca/blu-living-townhomes-for-sale.html
- **The in-suite suite at Blu Living is a strata fact, not a separate lot.** Current listings describe a one-bedroom nanny/in-law suite inside the townhome itself, with its own entry; Surrey's secondary-suite definition is an accessory unit within a single family, semi-detached or duplex building, not a separate strata lot, with only one suite permitted per dwelling unit — a buyer relying on suite income or family use should confirm how this complex's in-home suite is treated in the strata bylaws and the Form B. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3114378-21-15988-32-avenue-surrey-british-columbia-v3z2j4-998000/brochure and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Secondary suites in Surrey (city frame):** the City defines a secondary suite as an accessory dwelling unit whose habitable floor area does not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit, within a single family, semi-detached or duplex building, and not a separate strata lot; only one suite is permitted per dwelling unit. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing in Surrey:** the City adopted its SSMUH zoning changes on July 8, 2024 — a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. This affects the detached streets around the complex, not the strata lots inside it. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000** is the zoning bylaw governing Surrey strata lots, cited in City corporate reports as "Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000, as amended." https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r128-proposed-amendments-surrey-zoning-law-1993-no-12000
- **Strata paperwork is where an age bylaw would be proved:** for any strata purchase, the buyer paperwork path is the Form B information certificate, Form F, strata bylaws and rules, fees and special levies — and the live page for this complex directs buyers to the Form B, the bylaws and rules, the last two years of minutes and the depreciation report, and to confirm what the monthly fees cover, before subjects are removed. If a Blu Living listing ever claims 55+, the Form B and the registered bylaws are the proof, not the listing text. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing and https://jaredgibbons.ca/blu-living-townhomes-for-sale.html
