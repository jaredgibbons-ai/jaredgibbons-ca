## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; sub-55 bylaws invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). For an evaluation this is a valuation fact: any older CMA or listing history describing a complex as "19+", "40+" or "45+" is describing a bylaw that no longer exists — the complex is either 55+ or unrestricted today, and the comparable set changes with the answer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** Under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in them — which is why a 55+ unit can still draw investor and buy-for-a-parent demand at evaluation time. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. The May 2023 expansion widened the lawful resident pool for every 55+ complex — an evaluation relying on pre-2023 "restricted pool" reasoning is working from superseded rules. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rental bylaws are still allowed. A CMA that discounts a 55+ unit for a "no rentals" bylaw is discounting for a void bylaw; a CMA that assumes short-term rental income is assuming something a bylaw can still prohibit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **The evaluation itself is a regulated-service moment.** BCFSA requires disclosure at the outset of dealings — whether the professional can represent the consumer as a client, the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and how to file a complaint — and requires that consumers be given information about commissions and fees. The page does not state when that disclosure occurs in this process: UNVERIFIED. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **What a strata CMA must price from documents, not impressions:** the Form B information certificate, Form F, registered bylaws and rules, fees, special levies and depreciation position — the Province's strata buying guidance lists exactly these as the pricing documents, and they are the same documents that establish whether an age bylaw exists and in what words. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/first-time-owners
- **Municipal rules are area-specific underneath the provincial frame:** Surrey — Zoning By-law No. 12000 and Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026); White Rock — Zoning Bylaw 2024, No. 2506 and OCP Bylaw 2017, No. 2220; Langley City — Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300; Township of Langley — Zoning Bylaw No. 2500. Suite potential, SSMUH counts and tax due dates in a CMA must come from the home's own municipality. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 and https://www.whiterockcity.ca/514/Resources-Documents and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **First-home programs (for the buyer side of the eventual sale):** FHSA and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits are UNVERIFIED in this file — no live CRA page was read in this pass, so no figure is published here.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What does the home evaluation on this page actually produce?** A Comparative Market Analysis: Jared evaluates the prices of properties similar to yours that have recently sold in your area, giving you a better idea of your property's market value as a first step in preparing to sell. https://jaredgibbons.ca/home-evaluation.html
- **Q: How do I request one, and what happens next?** Complete and submit the form on the page — the page says it takes a few quick minutes — and Jared contacts you as soon as he has the results; the form's visible selector asks for the home's size in bands from 500 sq. ft. to 4,000+ sq. ft. https://jaredgibbons.ca/home-evaluation.html
- **Q: What does it cost, and how long does it take?** UNVERIFIED — the page states no price (and does not call the evaluation free in the text read) and no turnaround beyond "as soon as I have the results." BCFSA requires a licensee to give consumers information about commissions and fees, so the fee answer belongs in the first conversation. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: Does requesting an evaluation make me Jared's client?** The page does not say. Under BCFSA's framework, a licensee must disclose at the outset of dealings whether they can represent you as a client and what duties are owed to clients and non-clients — ask for that disclosure when the evaluation conversation starts. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: My home is in a 55+ complex. What should the evaluation do differently?** Price it against the same-tenure, same-form roster (Section 2), state the registered age bylaw's exact words from the strata documents, and treat the restriction as a defined buyer-pool fact — 55+ residents, exempt spouses/partners, caregivers, legacy residents, plus under-55 owners who cannot live in the unit — not as a generic discount. An evaluation that reaches for unrestricted townhouse or condo sales without adjusting for the bylaw is answering a different question. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Which sold properties were used as comparables for my 55+ unit?** Ask directly — the page presents the CMA as built from prices of similar properties that recently sold in your area, and for a 55+ home "similar" should mean: same complex first, then the verified same-form 55+ roster in the same municipality, with any unrestricted comparable identified as unrestricted. https://jaredgibbons.ca/home-evaluation.html
- **Q: My building's old listing said 45+. Does that still affect value?** No — sub-55 age bylaws have been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. The complex is either 55+ under a valid bylaw or unrestricted today; an evaluation should establish which, from the current registered bylaws, before pricing anything. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can a younger buyer still be part of my buyer pool?** Yes, in defined ways: as an owner who does not reside, as a buyer for a 55+ parent, as an exempt spouse/partner of a qualifying resident, or as a legacy/caregiver case. Long-term rental to a 55+ tenant is also open, because rental-restriction bylaws are void. The pool is narrower than an unrestricted unit's — it is not family-and-investor-free. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: I hold a unit at Sullivan Mews / in a manufactured-home park. Is the evaluation the same?** No — tenure changes the exercise. A membership unit has no strata title and, per its listings, cannot be mortgaged, so its market is cash buyers; a park home's value rides on pad rent, park rules and assignment consent under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. Expect the evaluation to price the home and the tenure together, not borrow strata comparables. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3058951-15020-66a-avenue-49-surrey-bc-v3s-2a5/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Q: Is the evaluation result a guaranteed sale price?** No — the page says the analysis gives "a better idea of your property's market value"; it does not promise a sale price, and no accuracy figure, testimonial or result statistic appears in the page's body copy as read. https://jaredgibbons.ca/home-evaluation.html

- **A 55+ CMA in this market has a documented trap list.** Four verified/unrestricted near-twin pairs sit inside the service areas (Glenwood Village / Heights; Capistrano / Blackwood Manor; the two Bedford Landing buildings; the two Westwood parks in Surrey) — pull the wrong twin's sales and the evaluation prices the wrong legal product. ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/surrey-condos-under-800k-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md and https://www.zolo.ca/white-rock-real-estate/1442-blackwood-street/119
- **The law moved underneath every older evaluation.** Between Nov 24, 2022 (sub-55 bylaws invalidated, rental bans voided) and May 1, 2023 (spouse/child exemptions expanded), the lawful resident and renter pools for every 55+ unit in BC changed twice. A CMA or appraisal from before those dates that reasons from the old pools should be re-run, not refreshed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **The seven service areas do not share one 55+ market.** Surrey's roster is 15 strata deep with a Newton cluster; White Rock's verified roster is a single 23-unit building; Langley's splits across two municipalities with different suite rules (Township 120 m² cap versus City of Langley's lesser of 100 m² and 40% under Bylaw 2025 No. 3300). An evaluation template carried across the seven areas without swapping the roster and the municipal rules will be wrong in at least six of them. ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/surrey-condos-under-800k-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
