# Downtown VW (Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

**NAMING POINT — read first:** /downtown-vw.html is Downtown **VANCOUVER** (Vancouver West board side). The archive label "Downtown West Vancouver" is wrong — West Vancouver is a different municipality across Burrard Inlet, with its own 55+ stock, and nothing in this file is in West Vancouver. The research file's title is "Downtown VW (Downtown, Vancouver West)"; its verified geography is followed here.

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw — the live issue in VW-core towers, where short-term rental bylaws and the City's licensing rules, not age bylaws, are what a buyer will actually meet in the strata documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **How the City creates new 55+ rental:** Vancouver's Seniors Housing Rezoning Policy (approved July 23, 2024; last amended May 19, 2026) treats "Social Housing (where occupancy is restricted to older adults 55+)" as social housing secured by a housing agreement restricting occupancy to residents aged 55 or older, owned and operated by a non-profit or government, and secures seniors supportive or independent living housing as rental — no stratification, no separate sales covenants — for 60 years or the life of the building, whichever is greater. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-rezoning-seniors-housing.pdf
- **Downtown VW zoning:** the Downtown District (DD) schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 governs much of the core — the only permitted uses are those in the Downtown District Official Development Plan, including commercial, residential, institutional and light industrial uses, parks and open spaces and public uses; CD-1 comprehensive development districts and C-3A districts also apply in parts of the area, and C-3A shows no minimum site area for its listed uses. There is no house-lot minimum to work with in the core; every seniors project here is a site-specific rezoning or a non-profit building inside those districts. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-DD.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-C-3A.pdf
- **Tax calendar and the extra core taxes:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after each due date; the Empty Homes Tax requires an annual property status declaration, with the 2025 payment due April 16, 2026 — a standing check on any VW-core purchase, where investor-held units are common. At core price points the Province's additional school tax can also apply: 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Downtown VW?** None that any source verifies. No age-restricted strata building was found in the VW core — a buyer who wants an age-restricted community buys in another neighbourhood, and a buyer set on the core buys a general-market strata unit in a tower whose bylaws to check are its short-term rental and pet bylaws, not an age bylaw. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Downtown VW the same as West Vancouver?** No — and the confusion is costly. Downtown VW is the Vancouver West board slice of the City of Vancouver's one Downtown local area, on the south shore of Burrard Inlet. West Vancouver is a separate municipality on the north shore, with its own sub-areas and its own 55+ buildings; a "Downtown West Vancouver" search mixes two municipalities. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- **Q: Where does Downtown VW end and Downtown VE begin?** UNCONFIRMED from any board source read for this file — VW and VE are the board/site split of one City local area, this file covers the west-side (VW) CBD core, and the exact dividing street must not be stated until the board source is in hand. Name the street and the station in listing copy, not just "downtown." https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- **Q: What seniors housing does exist in the Downtown local area?** At its south side, not in the VW core: Yaletown House (1099 Cambie Street, 127 subsidized long-term care beds, built 1985), and the BC Housing Zone 6 directory's Downtown South/Yaletown non-profit buildings on Seymour, Davie, Granville, Smithe, Richards Streets and Pacific Boulevard. All rental or care — nothing to buy — and filed in full in the Downtown Vancouver (VE-side) notes. https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-6-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf and https://travelclinic.vch.ca/en/location/yaletown-house
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a BC 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in the defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. Under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a 55+ parent can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a VW-core unit, or run it as a short-term rental?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Short-term rentals are the exception that survives: a strata can still restrict them by bylaw, which is the bylaw that matters most in core towers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Does the core work without a car for an older buyer?** On transit, yes, on the sourced facts: the core is the hub where the Expo, Millennium and Canada Lines meet, the R5 RapidBus terminates at Burrard Station, and the Central Library (350 West Georgia Street) and St. Paul's Hospital (1081 Burrard Street) are inside the area — the practical question is the specific tower's walk to its station, not the area's service level. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/ttR5.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Inside the area: St. Paul's Hospital, 1081 Burrard Street, on the Burrard corridor — a Providence Health Care hospital, so do not cite Vancouver Coastal Health pages for it. Vancouver General Hospital, 899 West 12th Avenue, is across False Creek in Fairview. https://www.providencehealthcare.org/en/clinics/rapid-access-stroke-prevention-clinic
- **Q: Is a waterfront core unit in the floodplain?** The City's designated floodplain includes the False Creek coastal floodplain, and the VW core's Burrard Inlet shoreline is the edge to check against that dataset; land in the floodplain is subject to Vancouver Building By-law setbacks and flood construction levels. Whether a specific parcel is in it: UNCONFIRMED — check the parcel in the City dataset. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The VW/VE split is a board artefact laid over one City neighbourhood:** the City has one Downtown local area; VW and VE are search-set labels. Coal Harbour and Yaletown have their own sub-areas and searches on top of that, and the West End is a separate City area — four different geographies a buyer will treat as one "downtown." https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- **St. Paul's Hospital is the VW core's real seniors-relevant amenity, and it is routinely mis-cited:** it sits at 1081 Burrard Street inside the area, but it is a Providence Health Care hospital, not a Vancouver Coastal Health site — the reason the VE-side Downtown notes, working from VCH pages, could not confirm an in-area hospital. https://www.providencehealthcare.org/en/clinics/rapid-access-stroke-prevention-clinic
- **The core's history explains its housing form:** the City states the first passenger train arrived at the foot of Howe Street in 1887, and within seven years a building boom moved the city centre from Water and Cordova Streets up to Georgia and Granville Streets — the streets at the heart of today's VW core, which is why the core is commercial buildings and later residential towers, with no house streets and no house-lot seniors product. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/downtown.aspx
- **BC Housing's own directory merges Downtown with the West End:** the Zone 6 seniors directory (May 2025) runs one combined "Vancouver - Downtown and West End" table, mixing Downtown South buildings with Burnaby, Nelson and Haro Street (West End) buildings in a single list. Any "downtown seniors" list built straight off that table without address-checking will import West End stock — and none of it is in the VW core. https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-6-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **The Empty Homes Tax declaration is the standing annual chore core owners actually face:** every Vancouver owner must file the yearly property status declaration, and the core's investor-held stock makes the seller's declaration status a standard due-diligence check on any purchase here — the 2025 payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
