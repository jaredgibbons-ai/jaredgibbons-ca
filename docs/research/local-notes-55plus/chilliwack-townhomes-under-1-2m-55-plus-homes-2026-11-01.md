## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. That is why the 40+/45+ parks in the Checked list cannot be marketed as age-restricted products on a 55+ page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw, and a tenant in a 55+ building must still meet the age bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Leasehold is the first tenure check on this page's corridors.** The live page warns that Vedder Road, Bailey Road and Promontory Road carry prepaid First Nations leasehold townhomes alongside freehold strata; on a leasehold unit, check the lease expiration year, whether the lease is prepaid or collects monthly ground rent, and which lenders will finance that specific complex before pricing it against freehold stock. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-townhomes-under-1-2m.html
- **Zoning and suites: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000** governs. Its Section 2 caps a secondary suite at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the habitable floor space of the building, in a building containing only 1 principal dwelling unit; Section 4.06(1) permits only 1 accessory dwelling unit per lot where the zone permits one (secondary suite, coach house or garden suite; garden suites capped at 100 m²); and no secondary suite is legal unless built with a building permit meeting the BC Building and Fire Code and flood protection requirements, with new suites not permitted below the flood construction level. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **SSMUH: Bylaw 5395, adopted June 18, 2024,** is the City's small-scale multi-unit housing amendment to Bylaw No. 5000, allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **New-build tax break inside this band:** effective April 1, 2024, a qualifying newly built home bought as a principal residence is fully exempt from property transfer tax at a fair market value of $1,100,000 or less, with a partial exemption phasing out between $1,100,000 and $1,150,000 — the live page's FAQ thresholds match the Province's page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Manufactured-home parks (Cottonwood Village):** tenancies fall under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act — the resident owns the home and rents the pad, and a park's age rule is a park rule, not a strata bylaw. Cottonwood Village also sits on First Nations leased land, so the park lease sits underneath the tenancy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- **Property taxes:** City of Chilliwack notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1 each year; a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522

- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live at Cottage Grove?** Yes in a strata — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ complex as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: How do I tell a freehold townhome from a leasehold one on Vedder or Promontory Road?** The live page flags both tenures on those corridors and warns leasehold units often list lower. The check is documentary: the title and lease — expiration year, prepaid vs monthly ground rent, and lender acceptance for that complex — before comparing prices. Which specific complexes are leasehold is UNVERIFIED in this file. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-townhomes-under-1-2m.html
- **Q: Does a new townhome under $1.2M get a property transfer tax break?** A qualifying newly built home bought as a principal residence is fully PTT-exempt at $1,100,000 or less, with a partial exemption phasing out to $1,150,000, effective April 1, 2024 — and the buyer does not need to be a first-time buyer. First-home programs beyond that (FHSA / Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits) are UNVERIFIED in this file — no live CRA page stating the limits was read in this pass. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Q: What should I read in the depreciation report for a hillside townhome?** The live page directs Promontory and Eastern Hillsides buyers to roof, siding, window and driveway timelines, pet and parking bylaws (including truck length in garages), and any retaining-wall maintenance obligations in the complex. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-townhomes-under-1-2m.html
