# Mission — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves. This file covers the whole City of Mission (the city-wide slug), urban and rural sub-areas together.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The City of Mission sits on the north side of the Fraser River in the Fraser Valley Regional District, bounded by the Fraser River to the south, the District of Maple Ridge to the west, and FVRD electoral areas to the north and east. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan and https://careers.fraserhealth.ca/pages/mission-community-profile
- The board sub-areas that make up this city page are "Mission" (Paragon F83) and Mission West (Paragon F88) as the urban umbrellas, plus the rural reporting sub-areas Dewdney Deroche, Durieu, Hatzic, Hemlock, Lake Errock, Stave Falls and Steelhead. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/current-city-projects/rural-mission-strategy
- Where the 55+ stock actually sits: almost all of it is in the flat central bench around 7th Avenue and Cedar Street — Cherry Lane and Luther Place (strata), Chartwell Cedarbrooke and Chartwell Carrington House (retirement residences), Cedar Valley Manor (life lease) and St. Andrew's Place (co-op) are all within a few blocks of each other — plus the Hurd Street care cluster (The Cedars, Pleasant View) near Mission Memorial Hospital at 7324 Hurd St, and Welton Towers downtown at 33214 2nd Ave. The only rural 55+ product is manufactured-home parks on Lougheed Highway (Mission Mobile Home Park in Hatzic; the adult side of Oasis Country Estates in Dewdney Deroche). Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below, and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Mission/mission-memorial-hospital
- 7th Avenue is the east–west spine of the central bench: Mission Senior Secondary is at 32939 7th Ave, and the 55+ strata, both Chartwell residences and Heritage Park all front or sit just off it. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/075
- Mission City Station, 33200 North Railway Ave, is the eastern terminus of the West Coast Express, with weekday rush-hour trains to Downtown Vancouver; BC Transit Route 31 links Mission to Abbotsford. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express and https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/
- Mission Leisure Centre is at 7650 Grand Street and Mission Library at 33247 Second Avenue. http://www.mission.ca/leisure and https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations/mission-library
- Schools are SD75 (Mission Public Schools), with a middle tier neighbours do not have: elementary K–6, middle schools Grades 7–9, Mission Senior Secondary Grades 10–12. For the downtown sample address 7466 Welton St, the SD75 locator gives École Mission Central Elementary → Heritage Park Middle School → Mission Senior Secondary; catchments are address-based, so run the exact address. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/075 and https://www.mpsd.ca
- Market context (SnapStats FVR September 2026, community level only): Mission's attached stock is concentrated in the urban "Mission" sub-area — 104 of the city's 122 active Condos & Townhomes listings — while Hatzic, Dewdney Deroche, Durieu, Stave Falls and Steelhead recorded zero active attached listings. Per-sub-area medians are not published: UNVERIFIED at sub-area level.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. Cherry Lane's listing wording "55+ Community with Family Exemptions" points at this same framework — confirm the complex's registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm and https://www.zolo.ca/mission-real-estate/32165-7-avenue/5
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why the "rentals not allowed" lines still showing in directories for Cherry Lane and Cedarbrook Estates cannot be relied on for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home parks run on tenancy law, not strata law.** Pad tenancies at Mission Mobile Home Park and Oasis Country Estates fall under BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act: the resident owns the home and rents the pad, pad rent may be raised only once in 12 months on at least three full months' written notice, and the raise is capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026) plus a proportional share of changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Life lease is its own legal animal.** At Cedar Valley Manor the operator states the resident does not own the unit or the building and instead purchases an interest (the right to occupy), with the original entrance fee returned at termination less a set percentage for operation costs — no strata Form B, no title transfer. Read the life lease agreement and the society's disclosure before treating it like a purchase. https://www.missionseniors.ca/our-communities/cedar-valley-manor/
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020** is Mission's zoning bylaw; rural zones such as RU16 carry a 1.6 ha minimum lot size in City notices — relevant to the rural sub-areas, where there is no strata stock at all. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning
- **Current plan: Official Community Plan Bylaw 6405-2026** replaces and repeals OCP Bylaw 6350-2025, keeping the same vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction with a Regional Context Statement added; the OCP carries Urban Residential, Suburban Residential and Urban Residential Townhouse designations. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026 and https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing:** the City's SSMUH page states zones previously restricted to single family or duplex dwellings are subject to the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation the City has adopted, and City planning pages set out how secondary suites and detached accessory units are handled by zone — relevant to a senior selling a central detached house. https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh
- **Property taxes:** Mission's 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a flat 10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026 (Mission has no staged 5% + 5% scheme); the City's tax rates page also states additional taxes are levied for diking on top of the municipal, school, regional district and BC Assessment levies. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/tax-rates
- **Flood:** the Fraser River floodplain runs along the city's south edge and low areas such as Hatzic Prairie — where Mission Mobile Home Park sits — are protected by the Dewdney Dyke system; flood status is parcel-specific. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually BUY in Mission?** Two verified age-restricted strata complexes: Cherry Lane (32165 7th Ave, 20 townhouses, 1989, NWS2971) and Luther Place (7525 Martin Place, NWS2729, 1988). Beyond strata, the buy-in products are a life lease at Cedar Valley Manor (8358 Cedar St — an occupancy interest, not ownership) and manufactured homes on rented pads at Mission Mobile Home Park (34519 Lougheed Hwy). Sources: the building entries in Section 2.
- **Q: Is Cedar Valley Manor a condo I own?** No. The operator states it directly: as a life lease, "you do not actually own the unit or the building" — you purchase an interest that gives you the right to live there, and at termination the original entrance fee is returned less a set percentage for operation costs. https://www.missionseniors.ca/our-communities/cedar-valley-manor/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live at Cherry Lane or Luther Place?** Yes in a strata — provincial rules exempt a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Cherry Lane's own listing text advertises "Family Exemptions." In a manufactured-home park the park's own rules govern instead — read them before writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.zolo.ca/mission-real-estate/32165-7-avenue/5
- **Q: I'm 52. Can I buy in a 55+ strata now and move in at 55?** You can buy now — the age bylaw restricts residing, not owning. You could not live in the unit until you meet the bylaw or fit an exemption (for example, as the spouse or partner of a resident who qualifies). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Directories say Cherry Lane doesn't allow rentals. Can I rent my unit out?** For a long-term tenancy, the directory line is stale law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void across BC since Nov 24, 2022. Your tenant still has to meet the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and bylaws can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/cherry-lane-32165-seventh-ave
- **Q: Is Oasis Country Estates a 55+ park?** Not as a whole. Listings split it into an "adult side" and a "family section," and no source checked states the adult side's age number — treat "55+" for this park as UNCONFIRMED and get the park rules in writing. Mission Mobile Home Park on the same highway is the one whose listings state 55+ outright. https://www.zolo.ca/mission-real-estate/41168-lougheed-highway/13 and https://www.cotala.com/tours/85367/FullPublic.pdf?i=979581669
- **Q: What pets are allowed?** Building by building: Cherry Lane records 1 cat or 1 small dog (15 in. at the shoulder); Luther Place records pets welcome with restrictions; St. Andrew's Place co-op has a strict no-pets policy; Mission Mobile Home Park listings record cats and dogs OK with a two-pet limit. Confirm against the registered bylaws or park rules for the specific home. Sources: the building entries in Section 2.
- **Q: Where is the hospital, and what care is in town if I need it later?** Mission Memorial Hospital is at 7324 Hurd St, with the Mission Community Health Centre at 7298 Hurd St and the Mission Urgent and Primary Care Centre at 304-32555 London Ave (open 7 days, 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m.). The in-town ladder runs: independent strata (Cherry Lane, Luther Place) → life lease (Cedar Valley Manor) → independent/assisted rental (Chartwell Cedarbrooke, Chartwell Carrington House, The Cedars' assisted and supported units) → long-term care (The Residence in Mission, 200 beds per the 2020 inventory). https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Mission/mission-memorial-hospital and https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/Homelessness/2020%20Mission%20Housing%20Inventory.pdf
- **Q: How is transit for someone giving up driving?** The West Coast Express from Mission City Station (33200 North Railway Ave) is a weekday rush-hour commuter service to Downtown Vancouver — it is not all-day transit. Local movement is BC Transit's Central Fraser Valley system, including Route 31 to Abbotsford; the central-bench 55+ buildings sit on or near 7th Avenue, the corridor the station, hospital and downtown services cluster around. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express and https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, a caregiver, or a legacy case — or buy without living in the unit. Mission's attached market is also thin and volatile (community Condos & Townhomes median days on market hit 61 in September 2026 on 10 sales, SnapStats FVR September 2026), so price off the specific complex, not the monthly median. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Two buildings eight civic numbers apart on 7th Avenue are opposite products.** Chartwell Cedarbrooke (32331 7th Ave) is a rental retirement residence; Cedarbrook Estates (32339 7th Ave) is a general-market family townhouse complex with no age restriction in any source. Search results blur the names — check the civic number, not the name. https://chartwell.com/bc/mission/cedarbrooke and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/cedarbrook-estates-32339-7-ave
- **Even the official-adjacent sources disagree about addresses and counts here.** Chartwell's site puts Carrington House at 32679 6th Ave while the Assisted Living Registry puts it at 32700 7th Ave; the 2020 housing inventory heads Welton Towers "61 units" and describes "a 60-unit" facility in its own text; Cherry Lane's feeds split between Freehold Strata (NWS2971) and "Freehold NonStrata," and between 1989 and 1991 build years. On Mission's small 55+ stock, the Form B, the registered bylaws and the operator — not any single directory — are the settling documents. Sources: the flagged entries in Section 2.
- **One non-profit runs most of Mission's seniors housing ladder.** The Mission Association for Seniors Housing (MASH) operates The Cedars (7380 Hurd St), Welton Towers (33214 2nd Ave) and, through its life lease society, Cedar Valley Manor (8358 Cedar St), and states all of its housing is for people 55 and older — a single application conversation can span independent, supportive and assisted stock. https://www.missionseniors.ca/our-communities/cedar-valley-manor/ and https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/Homelessness/2020%20Mission%20Housing%20Inventory.pdf
- **Mission's tax penalty is a single flat step, not the staged pattern in some neighbouring cities.** Taxes were due July 2, 2026 and a flat 10% penalty landed on outstanding current balances on July 3, 2026 — a one-day miss costs the full 10%, and diking levies ride on top of the municipal rate. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/tax-rates
- **The newest downtown seniors product is rental, and it sits on a heritage street.** The Bellevue (32998 1st Ave) is a brand-new seniors rental building with its own bistro and small grocery at grade and commercial space in the same building — downtown Mission's 55+ growth is arriving as rental and life lease, while the strata 55+ supply is still the two late-1980s complexes, Cherry Lane (1989) and Luther Place (1988). https://thebellevue.ca/ and https://www.estateblock.com/mission/15-32165-seventh-ave-49679
- **The rural 55+ option comes with dyke geography.** Mission Mobile Home Park sits in Hatzic, on the Hatzic Prairie side of the city behind the Dewdney Dyke system, on Lougheed Highway minutes from downtown by car — the trade for the park's price point is floodplain-adjacent land and car dependence, so confirm the parcel's flood status and the pad tenancy terms together. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf and https://www.cotala.com/tours/85367/FullPublic.pdf?i=979581669
