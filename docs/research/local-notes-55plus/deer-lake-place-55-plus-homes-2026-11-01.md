## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Zoning bylaw — note the name change.** Research briefs for Burnaby sometimes still cite the "Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742)". The City states the new **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026** was adopted June 9, 2026, replaced the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965, and took effect July 1, 2026. Use the 2026 bylaw. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/bylaws/zoning-bylaw
- **R1 SSMUH zoning:** Burnaby consolidated its single- and two-family districts into the R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing district in June 2024 — 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; SSMUH lots require a minimum lot width of 10 m and minimum lot area of 281 m², and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. On this pocket's detached streets, SSMUH infill and secondary suites are the only mechanisms adding smaller homes; none of it is age-restricted. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP: the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan** was adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP, and takes precedence over older community-plan maps. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Lakeshore checks:** the pocket sits at the west end of Deer Lake beside its shoreline wetlands, so streamside setbacks and parcel-level flood/hazard status are property-specific checks on west-end lots: UNVERIFIED for individual lots. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
- **Property taxes:** Burnaby's 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date; the City's 2026–2030 Financial Plan approved a 2.9% tax increase (about $61 on the average home). The detached single-family water utility charge is $652.33, with a 5% discount if paid by the due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Deer Lake Place?** None that any source verifies. There is no age-restricted strata building in the pocket — the September 2026 market here was 10 detached listings and no attached listings at all. A 55+ buyer in Deer Lake Place is buying a regular detached house with no age bylaw. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Burnaby?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — a BC age bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I add a suite or a second dwelling for a parent or caregiver?** That is the realistic ageing-in-place route here, and the pocket already does it — current listings include houses with legal suites and in-law suites. Burnaby's R1 SSMUH zoning allows 3–4 units on most house lots (up to 6 near frequent transit), and multi-unit lots may be stratified. What a specific lot can hold depends on its size, width and transit proximity, plus the lakeshore setback checks on west-end lots. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://vancouvernashdom.com/burnaby-south-deer-lake-place-houses/SearchResults.form/
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St (Fraser Health), is immediately north-west of the pocket, across the Burnaby Hospital sub-area. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day?** No SkyTrain station sits inside the pocket; Metrotown Station (Expo Line) is the nearest station named in the research. Bus route numbers for Deer Lake Parkway / Gilpin Street: UNVERIFIED. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in Deer Lake Place?** No care housing of any kind was verified inside the pocket — no assisted living and no long-term care. The assisted-living step means moving to one of Burnaby's registered residences elsewhere in the city (the eight Registry addresses are listed in Section 2). https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ABurnaby&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=asc
- **Q: Is Deer Lake Place the same as Deer Lake?** No. In the SnapStats GVR report, Deer Lake, Deer Lake Place, Upper Deer Lake and Buckingham Heights are four separate Burnaby sub-areas with separate inventory and solds — Deer Lake recorded 1 detached sale on 13 listings in September 2026 while Deer Lake Place recorded 0 on 10. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Keep the four Deer Lake sub-areas separate in every price opinion.** Deer Lake Place recorded 0 solds on 10 detached listings in September 2026 (sales ratio not applicable), while Deer Lake recorded 1 sale on 13 listings (8%) and Buckingham Heights 0 on 12 — three different pockets that happen to share a lake. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.)
- **The pocket's named anchor is a school, not a housing complex.** The only named institution verified inside the pocket is the independent Deer Lake SDA School at 5550 Gilpin St — it is not the public catchment school, and it is not seniors housing. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **The Deer Lake boardwalk is mid-replacement.** The boardwalk section by the Deer Lake boat launch closed September 21, 2026 for a full replacement, with completion scheduled by winter 2027 and a signed detour via Sperling Avenue and Deer Lake Drive (about five extra minutes of walking). https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- **City Hall is the pocket's neighbour.** Burnaby City Hall is at 4949 Canada Way, just north-west of the pocket — the Deer Lake area is the City's governmental and cultural precinct, with the Shadbolt Centre for the Arts and the Festival Lawn performance venue at the park's east side. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- **Burnaby replaced its entire zoning bylaw in 2026.** The Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (adopted June 9, 2026, in force July 1, 2026) repealed and replaced the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 — documents or summaries that still cite the 1965 bylaw are out of date. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Burnaby taxes have two penalty steps.** 2026 property taxes were due July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date, and the detached water charge ($652.33) carries a 5% early-payment discount. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
