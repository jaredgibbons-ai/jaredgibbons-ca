## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Most Eastern Hillsides homes are freehold, where strata age law does not apply at all.** The stock described in the plan is freehold detached housing on individual lots; a freehold detached house carries no strata bylaw, so there is no age-restriction mechanism of the strata kind for the typical hillside house. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557
- **City zoning — confirmed from chilliwack.com: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000.** Its hillside zones set the lot math here: SHR (Suburban Hillside Residential) minimum lot area is set by the bylaw's slope table and ranges from 360 m² to 990 m², with minimum lot width 12 m and minimum lot depth 20 m; HR (Hillside Residential) minimum lot area is 0.2 ha with a community water system and 0.4 ha in all other cases, minimum lot width 25 m; RH (Rural Hillside) minimum lot area is 8 ha, minimum lot width 25 m. The zone applying to any specific parcel: UNVERIFIED — confirm per property. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **City plan — confirmed from chilliwack.com: Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500,** with the Eastern Hillsides Neighbourhood Plan as Schedule B4; the 2050 update replaced the plan's land-use designations with city-wide OCP designations, while the plan's development cells and unit allocations continue to regulate how many units may be developed in the plan area. City documents headed "Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500" confirm the number. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- **Slope and geotech rules — as the City sources state them:** the Neighbourhood Plan was adopted in 2012, amended in 2017 to clarify development-approval requirements protecting future development from rock fall, landslide or earthflow with unstable conditions, and updated in 2025 as part of the OCP update; Council directed an expanded Marble Hill development moratorium area in 2017 after continuing ground movement was reported. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1962
- **Hillside Development Permit Area:** under the 2050 OCP's Hillside Development Permit Area (Schedule D-5), a development permit is required for works including subdivision of land with 20% or greater slopes, and new construction or earthworks on 20% or greater slopes. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- **Marble Hill slide areas:** the City's Greenspace Plan identifies the lower and upper Marble Hill slide areas as undevelopable lands. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2650/2021-05-28%20GSP%20MASTER%202020%20-%20Urban%20Format.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ housing I can buy in Eastern Hillsides itself?** No verified product. No age-restricted strata, townhome complex or 55+ park with an address inside the plan area was found in any source checked; the plan-area stock is freehold detached housing on individual lots. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557
- **Q: Where are Chilliwack's actual 55+ strata buildings, then?** Elsewhere in the city, on the valley floor and in the central areas — e.g. Ivy Green (45775 Spadina Ave, Downtown — 55+, 24 condos, built 1994, LMS1336) and Knight Lodge (45660 Knight Rd, Chilliwack Proper — 55+ per listing data), plus the gated 55+ rancher community Cottage Grove (8500 Young Rd, Proper South). None of those is an Eastern Hillsides address. https://www.admiraal.ca/buildings/view/27410 and https://www.estateblock.com/chilliwack/203-45660-knight-rd-49464 and https://www.zolo.ca/chilliwack-real-estate/8500-young-road/21
- **Q: Can a freehold hillside house be "55+" the way a strata can?** Not by strata bylaw — a freehold detached house has no strata corporation and no strata age bylaw. The BC age-bylaw rules (55+ only, in force since Nov 24, 2022) are strata rules under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1/123.2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What slope and slide checks matter before I buy a hillside lot here?** The plan identifies steep slopes and geotechnical hazard areas; the 2017 amendments exist specifically to protect development from rock fall, landslide and earthflow; the Marble Hill slide areas are identified as undevelopable lands; and subdivision or construction on slopes of 20% or greater needs a development permit under Schedule D-5. Get the property's geotechnical reports and any covenants on title before removing subjects. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1962 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- **Q: Is Marble Hill a separate neighbourhood I should treat differently?** Marble Hill is a named slope area inside the Eastern Hillsides plan area, and it carries the area's sharpest hazard history: an expanded development moratorium area was directed there in 2017 after continuing ground movement was reported, and its lower and upper slide areas are identified as undevelopable. Check whether a specific lot sits in the moratorium area before counting on subdivision or a build. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2650/2021-05-28%20GSP%20MASTER%202020%20-%20Urban%20Format.pdf
- **Q: Is there a school or a bus in the plan area?** Neither. The Neighbourhood Plan states there is no school inside the plan area, and no BC Transit route in the Chilliwack route overview runs into the plan area — every child travels out of the area, and catchments are checked by exact address in the SD33 School Locator. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557 and https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview and https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- **Q: Can I add a suite or rebuild for aging in place on a hillside lot?** The constraint is the permit and zoning regime, not age law: lot potential runs off Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 — the SHR minimum lot area alone swings from 360 m² to 990 m² depending on slope — and works on 20%+ slopes need a Hillside Development Permit. Confirm the specific lot's zone and measured slope first. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- **Q: Where are the hospital, recreation centre and library?** All on the valley floor: Chilliwack General Hospital, 45600 Menholm Road; the Chilliwack Urgent and Primary Care Centre, #104 – 7955 Evans Road; Cheam Leisure Centre, 45501 Market Way, in Sardis; and Chilliwack Library, 45860 First Avenue. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557
- **Q: If I buy here now and need care later, what is in Chilliwack?** The registered assisted-living residences are elsewhere in the city (e.g. Waverly Seniors Village, 8445 Young Rd; The Cascades, 45586 McIntosh Dr; Chartwell Birchwood, 45650 Patten Ave), the subsidized seniors buildings are downtown/central, and the hospital is on the valley floor — the care ladder exists in Chilliwack, just not in Eastern Hillsides. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3AChilliwack&name=&address=&order=alr_residence_type&sort=asc and https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-7-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The 2017 plan amendment is a hazard amendment, not a growth amendment.** The Eastern Hillsides Neighbourhood Plan was amended in 2017 specifically to clarify development-approval requirements protecting future development from rock fall, landslide or earthflow with unstable conditions — a buyer reading only the plan's adoption date (2012) misses why the current version reads the way it does. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1962
- **The Marble Hill moratorium followed reported ground movement.** Council directed an expanded Marble Hill development moratorium area in 2017 after continuing ground movement was reported, and development there requires extensive geotechnical evaluation first. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2756/2017%2011%2021%20%20%2D%20%20OCP31%20RZ1130%20%20Full%20Council%20Report%20Marble%20Hill%20Area%2Epdf
- **The same zone can mean two very different lots.** In the HR zone the minimum lot area is 0.2 ha with a community water system and 0.4 ha in all other cases, and in SHR the minimum runs 360 m² to 990 m² by slope band — two lots with the same zone label can carry very different subdivision and build potential. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **The plan area has parks and trails but no daily services.** City listings give the area Allan Creek Park, Forest Gate Park, Marble Hill Natural Area, Allan Creek Trail, Chilliwack Community Forest and the Elk Mountain Trail — and the research records no shopping centre or grocery store inside the plan area confirmed on an official source, and no recreation centre or library branch inside it. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=203 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557
- **The historic wagon road is a protected heritage asset in the plan.** The Neighbourhood Plan identifies the historic wagon road (Browlee Road) as a heritage asset to protect, and states wagon roads in the area include Nixon, Ruddock, Allan and Hinkley. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557
- **Chilliwack market numbers do not come from the SnapStats reports used elsewhere in this program.** Chilliwack is Chilliwack and District Real Estate Board (CADREB) territory and is not in SnapStats coverage, so no SnapStats figure describes this area — any price evidence has to come from CADREB/Paragon comparables on the specific deal.
