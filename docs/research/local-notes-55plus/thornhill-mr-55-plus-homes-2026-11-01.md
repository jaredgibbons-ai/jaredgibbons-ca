## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://choa.bc.ca/resources/strata-property-act/legislative-updates/
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019.** The zoning schedule lists minimum lot areas of A-1 2.0 ha, A-2 4.0 ha, A-3 8.0 ha, RS-2 0.4 ha and RS-3 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha depending on the zone text — the rural zones that cover most of this hillside. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **City plan: OCP Bylaw No. 7060-2014.** The OCP's Urban Reserve designation identifies lands the City holds for long-term future urban services and housing, and OCP policy states development of the Urban Reserve will not happen until specific triggers and milestones have been attained. The live page's specific claim — about 343 hectares of Thornhill designated Urban Reserve, with subdivision below 2.0 ha restricted until the city population exceeds 100,000 and an area plan is adopted — was not confirmed in the OCP chapter read in this pass: UNVERIFIED as to the hectarage and the trigger wording. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/official-community-plan-chapter-2
- **Suites and added units:** one secondary suite per lot is permitted in listed zones including A-1, A-2 and A-3, contained within the single detached building, to a maximum of 40% of gross floor area, and not in the Floodplain Area. On ALR parcels, the provincial ALR Use Regulation also governs: one secondary suite in the principal residence (s. 31), and an additional residence only if the s. 32 conditions are met. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo105/loo105/30_2019
- **ALR, wells and septic:** ALR status is per-parcel — check the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder. Rural properties may be on wells and septic: wells are recorded in the provincial GWELLS database, and onsite sewerage must comply with the Sewerage System Regulation, including the 30 m setback from a well unless professional advice is obtained. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo104/loo104/22_326_2004
- **Flood and hazards:** the City's floodplain framework addresses flood risks from the Fraser River, the Alouette River system, steep creek watercourses and groundwater-related flooding. A City-documented wildfire interface map for specific Thornhill streets: UNVERIFIED. Parcel-level flood status: UNVERIFIED. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-advances-climate-action-flood-resilience-environmental-protection-and-mckenney-creek

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ complex in Thornhill itself?** No. No age-restricted strata, 55+ park, seniors rental or care residence with a Thornhill address was verified in any source checked. The only strata complexes on the hillside — The Terraces and Terraces II on 248 Street — are family townhomes with no age restriction in any source checked. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: The Terraces at 248 Street look quiet and low-maintenance — are they 55+?** No source says so. Listings describe 4-bedroom family townhomes (built 2015 at 10550 248 St, 2017 at 10480 248 St); quiet and strata-maintained is not the same as age-restricted. Confirm on the Form B if a listing ever claims a 55+ bylaw. https://www.zolo.ca/maple-ridge-real-estate/10550-248-street/3 and https://www.zolo.ca/maple-ridge-real-estate/10480-248-street/2
- **Q: Where is the nearest actual 55+ ownership stock?** West in East Central Maple Ridge — the four Fraserview Village strata buildings around 116 Avenue and 225–227 Streets, plus the Alouette Retirement Mobile Home Park (12868 229 St, own the home, rent the pad) and the Haney Pioneer Village Co-op (11830 230 St, 60+). https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/rosewood-court-22611-116-ave and https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata (in the city complexes)?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I age in place on a Thornhill acreage and add a suite or second dwelling?** One secondary suite per lot is permitted in the A-1, A-2 and A-3 zones if it sits inside the house, is at most 40% of gross floor area and is not in the Floodplain Area; on ALR land the ALR Use Regulation also applies, and an additional separate residence is allowed only under its s. 32 conditions. Well and septic capacity is the practical limit — both are per-lot checks. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo105/loo105/30_2019
- **Q: Is there bus service if I stop driving?** Limited: route 748 loops from Haney Place through Thornhill (100 Avenue, 256 Street, 98 Avenue, 264 Street) with weekday and Saturday trips only at wide intervals, and route 701 runs along Dewdney Trunk Road on the south side. There is no West Coast Express station in Thornhill — a car is effectively required on the hillside. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt748.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/194/tt701.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital and urgent care?** Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, is the community hospital for Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows with 24/7 emergency care, in the Town Centre to the west; the Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 121 – 11900 Haney Place, open 7 days a week, 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital and https://www.healthlinkbc.ca/clinic/ridge-meadows-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
- **Q: If I need care later, is there anything in Thornhill?** No care or assisted-living residence in Thornhill appears in BC's Assisted Living Registry; the registered Maple Ridge residences are all at downtown addresses, so ageing out of an acreage here means moving west to the Town Centre cluster. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=carouselnext&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&page=0&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Treat Urban Reserve land as long-term, not imminent:** OCP policy holds Urban Reserve development until specific triggers and milestones are met, so a Thornhill parcel's designation alone does not support a near-term-development premium — and the live page's specific hectarage and population-trigger wording for Thornhill could not be confirmed in the OCP chapter read for this file and remains UNVERIFIED. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/official-community-plan-chapter-2
- **The North 256 Street Industrial Lands Area Plan touches this area's north side:** it covers both sides of 256 Street north of 128 Avenue (about 500 acres, per the City release); the plan establishes future designations and does not itself approve construction — a designation change to disclose, not a development approval. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-approves-new-500-acre-256-street-industrial-area-plan-support-jobs-and-economic
- **Thornhill's attached shelf exists but did not sell in September 2026:** 8 Condos & Townhomes listings, 0 sold — one of only two Maple Ridge sub-areas (with Silver Valley) where attached listings existed but recorded no sale that month — so a single month's figures cannot carry a valuation for The Terraces pocket — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report.
- **The 248–249 Street hillside is claimed by two areas at once:** Thornhill's feed carries 249 Street parcels whose own listing text calls them Albion, next to the Albion growth area — when comparing a 248 Street townhome or acreage, check which sub-area's evidence the price is being justified from.
- **Blue Mountain Elementary has two addresses in circulation:** the live page's 12661 248 Street versus the BC Ministry K-12 file's 12453 248 Street. Use the Ministry figure, and run the exact address in the SD42 locator before making any catchment claim — the schools named for Thornhill sit in three different pockets (Whonnock, Albion and Webster's Corners sides). https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **The trail network is the amenity, and it is documented by the City:** 17 mountain-biking and hiking trails on 256 Street, moderately difficult, typically about an hour each, usable year-round — the recreation draw that substitutes for the recreation centre and library branch Thornhill does not have. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- **Property taxes:** Maple Ridge property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to any outstanding balance on July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% on September 2, 2026. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
