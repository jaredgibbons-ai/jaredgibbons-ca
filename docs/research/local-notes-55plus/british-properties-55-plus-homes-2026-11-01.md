# British Properties — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The British Properties is the District of West Vancouver's original large-lot hillside subdivision, on the benches above Ambleside and Park Royal, developed from the early 1930s by Guinness-led British investors — the same group behind the Lions Gate Bridge (1937–38), which was built to open up these lands. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)
- Inside the wider area sit the Chartwell and Canterbury enclaves, and the Whitby Estates pocket sits inside Chartwell. Street-by-street boundaries for the British Properties as a whole are UNVERIFIED — no District source read publishes them. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)
- Schools inside the area: Chartwell Elementary, 1300 Chartwell Dr, and Sentinel Secondary, 1250 Chartwell Dr; Westcot Elementary, 760 Westcot Rd, sits at the foot of the hill. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)
- Transit: TransLink route 254 is the "British Properties / Park Royal" bus — the one route that climbs into the subdivision. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)
- The District's OCP (Bylaw 4985, 2018) treats the British Properties as an established neighbourhood and directs growth to the District's centres instead — which is why the housing stock here remains almost entirely detached houses on RS lots. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)
- Where the 55+ housing actually is: not here. West Vancouver's verified 55+ strata buildings are all down the hill in Ambleside (see Section 2). The one new multi-family project inside the wider British Properties, Hawksley in the Uplands, is general-market — checked and NOT 55+ (Section 2).

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- Provincial law (included because buyers cross-shop the Ambleside 55+ strata from here): under the Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, only 55+ age-restriction bylaws are valid in BC; all lower age bylaws (19+/40+/45+) have been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it. There is effectively no strata stock in the British Properties for these rules to attach to. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- Exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01: legacy residents who lived in the unit when the bylaw passed, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse/partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the resident cared for before age 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- All residential rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022; stratas may still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- District zoning (Bylaw 4662, s. 200) RS minimum lot sizes: RS1 8,094 m², RS2 1,625 m², RS3 975 m², RS4 731 m², RS5 488 m², RS6 site of record. Secondary suites and detached secondary suites are permitted; ground-oriented multiple-dwelling rules allow up to 4 dwellings, or 6 within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)
- **British Pacific Properties restrictive covenants — the rule most buyers miss:** BPP plan approval is required before building, altering or fencing a property in the original subdivision. This is a private covenant layer (from the developer's own materials) on top of District permits, and it survives resale. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)
- Newer development area inside the wider Properties: Rodgers Creek (CD3 zoning) is capped at 354 homes in Areas 5 and 620 in Area 6, with apartments to 16 storeys; the Area 5 development permit (354 units, 3900 Cypress Bowl Rd) was approved Jul 8, 2025. This is general-market development, not 55+ housing. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)
- Hazard development permits: an Environmental DP applies within 15 m of a watercourse bank, and a Wildfire Hazard DP applies within 100 m of the undisturbed forest interface — both live issues on bench and ravine lots here. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)
- West Vancouver taxes 2026: due Jul 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after Jul 2 and a further 5% after Sep 2. Water and sewer are metered and billed quarterly. Provincial additional school tax for 2026: 0.2% on the portion of residential value between $3M and $4M and 0.4% above $4M — directly relevant at British Properties price levels — rising to 0.3% / 0.6% on Jan 1, 2027. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

**Q: Is there any 55+ condo or townhome to buy in the British Properties?**
A: No — zero verified. West Vancouver's 55+ buildings list contains only three buildings, all in Ambleside: Westview Terrace (1390 Duchess Ave), Tudor Gardens (843 22nd St) and Lincoln Gardens. https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/on-the-market/55-plus-buildings-west-vancouver/

**Q: Why is there no strata stock here at all?**
A: The original subdivision was laid out as large freehold estate lots, and the District's zoning still enforces it: RS minimum lot sizes under Bylaw 4662 s. 200 run from 488 m² (RS5) up to 8,094 m² (RS1), and the OCP (Bylaw 4985, 2018) treats the area as an established neighbourhood with growth directed to the District's centres. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)

**Q: I heard Hawksley is an "executive" project — is it age-restricted?**
A: No age restriction is stated in any source checked. Hawksley (3271–3281 Uplands Way) is a general-market presale of 57 condominiums and 7 townhomes by British Pacific Properties with Beedie — checked, and NOT 55+. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/hawksley-british-properties-west-vancouver-presale-executive-condos/

**Q: What approval do I need before renovating or fencing — beyond District permits?**
A: British Pacific Properties plan approval. The original subdivision's restrictive covenants require BPP approval before building, altering or fencing, on top of the District's own permit process. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)

**Q: Does the additional school tax hit British Properties homes?**
A: For most of them, yes: the provincial additional school tax for 2026 is 0.2% on residential value between $3M and $4M and 0.4% above $4M, and it rises to 0.3% / 0.6% on Jan 1, 2027. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)

**Q: Can I add a suite for family or a caregiver?**
A: District zoning permits a secondary suite and a detached secondary suite on RS lots, and ground-oriented rules allow up to 4 dwellings (6 within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop) — subject also to the BPP covenant approval layer on original-subdivision lots. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)

**Q: Are there wildfire or creek rules on these lots?**
A: Yes: an Environmental development permit applies within 15 m of a watercourse bank, and a Wildfire Hazard DP within 100 m of the undisturbed forest interface. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)

**Q: Where would I go for retirement living or care, staying in West Vancouver?**
A: Down the hill to Ambleside: Hollyburn House, Amica West Vancouver, Westerleigh PARC, Kiwanis Manor and Inglewood Care Centre are the verified cluster — all outside the British Properties. See the Ambleside 55+ local notes for their sourced details.

**Q: What new housing is actually being built up here?**
A: Rodgers Creek: CD3 caps of 354 homes (Area 5) and 620 homes (Area 6), apartments to 16 storeys, with the Area 5 development permit (354 units, 3900 Cypress Bowl Rd) approved Jul 8, 2025 — general-market, not age-restricted. Hawksley in the Uplands is the other current project. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)

**Q: If I buy an Ambleside 55+ strata as a second property, can a younger family member live there?**
A: Only within the Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exemptions: a younger spouse/partner of the 55+ specified resident, children under 19 in that resident's care, adult children the resident cared for before age 19, a live-in caregiver, or someone who lived in the unit when the bylaw passed. The bylaw itself restricts who may live in the unit, not who may own it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- (Early 1930s / 1937–38) The subdivision and the bridge are one story: Guinness-led British investors developed the British Properties from the early 1930s and built the Lions Gate Bridge in 1937–38 to reach it. The lot pattern a buyer sees today is that original estate plan. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)
- (Standing covenant) A British Properties purchase carries a second, private approval step most buyers have never heard of: BPP plan approval is required before building, altering or fencing — it is a covenant obligation, not a District permit, and it does not expire. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)
- (Jul 8, 2025) The Rodgers Creek Area 5 development permit (354 units at 3900 Cypress Bowl Rd) was approved — the largest new housing node inside the wider Properties, and general-market rather than seniors housing. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)
- (Jan 1, 2027) The provincial additional school tax steps up to 0.3% on the $3M–$4M portion of residential value and 0.6% above $4M — a dated, certain cost increase for most homes in this subdivision. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)
- (Nov 1, 2026) Despite the "Properties" name suggesting planned-community product, no strata complex of any kind in the original subdivision was confirmed on an official source — the only multi-family product inside the wider area is in the newer Uplands and Rodgers Creek pockets, and none of it is age-restricted. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)
