Its neighbourhoods page lists Albion/Kanaka, Port Hammond, Port Haney, Ruskin, Silver Valley, Thornhill, Town Centre, Webster's Corners, Whonnock and Yennadon; "Cottonwood" and "East Central / West Central / Northwest / Southwest" are Paragon (MLS) sub-area names, not City neighbourhood names. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods

Albion, Silver Valley, Thornhill, Webster's Corners, Whonnock and Northwest Maple Ridge have no verified 55+ ownership stock in this series. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below and the sub-area files in this series.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://choa.bc.ca/resources/strata-property-act/legislative-updates/
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed; live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid (this overrides the "rentals not allowed" wording still shown for Rosewood Court). Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/rosewood-court-22611-116-ave
- **Co-op age rules are membership rules, not strata bylaws.** St. George's Village sets a 55-year membership qualification and Haney Pioneer Village sets 60+ in their own documents; the provincial strata exemptions do not automatically apply to a co-op or a life lease — read the occupancy agreement itself. https://www.stgeorgesvillage.ca/_files/ugd/070889_39221c746a9b483980e20a3596cdb580.pdf and https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Manufactured-home parks run on different law.** Pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not the Strata Property Act; a park's age rule is a park rule. For rent increases taking effect in 2026, the maximum for manufactured home park tenancies is 2.3%, plus a proportional amount for the change in local government levies and regulated utility fees, once per 12 months on 3 full months' notice. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2025HMA0067-000786.htm
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019.** Subdivision minimums that set lot scale: RS-1 at 668.0 m² (18.0 m width, 27.0 m depth, maximum coverage 40%), RS-2 at 0.4 ha, RS-3 at 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha depending on the zone text, A-1 at 2.0 ha, A-2 at 4.0 ha, A-3 at 8.0 ha. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **City plan: OCP Bylaw No. 7060-2014** (an OCP update was adopted Nov 25, 2025, and the Albion Area Plan was adopted Oct 28, 2025 — see the Albion file). The research file for this page also records a newer OCP bylaw number (7998-2024, adopted Jun 25, 2024) in its identity section — SOURCE CONFLICT flagged, not resolved here: the East Central / Albion files cite OCP Bylaw No. 7060-2014 while the batch-12 research files cite 7998-2024 as program-verified. Confirm the current OCP citation with the City before publishing either number as the sole citation. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/albion?bidId=
- **ALR land:** subdivision of Agricultural Land Reserve land requires Agricultural Land Commission permission outside the limited regulation exceptions; on ALR parcels the housing frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² (secondary suite only inside it) plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). Which parcels on this page's feed are in the ALR: UNVERIFIED — check parcel by parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Suites and added units on non-ALR lots:** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Floodplain:** the Fraser River flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), no habitable floor space may sit below it on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. Parcel-level status: UNVERIFIED. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Thornhill Urban Reserve:** OCP policy holds Urban Reserve land for long-term future urban services and housing until specific triggers and milestones are attained; the live page's specific hectarage, section number and population-trigger wording are UNVERIFIED in the research file. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/official-community-plan-chapter-2

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a 55+ lot in Maple Ridge and build a 55+ home on it?** A lot itself carries no age restriction, and no 55+ bare-land strata was verified anywhere in the city — buying land and building gives you a general-market house, not a unit in an age-restricted community. The verified 55+ ownership product is built: the Fraserview Village stratas, St. George's Village co-op, and the two manufactured-home parks. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: What is the full list of 55+ homes I can actually buy in Maple Ridge?** Four strata buildings at Fraserview Village in East Central (Rosewood Court, Emerald Manor, The Willows, Castlemount), one 55+ co-op in Cottonwood MR (St. George's Village — share purchase), and homes in two 55+ manufactured-home parks (Alouette in East Central, Val Maria in Southwest — own the home, rent the pad). Haney Pioneer Village is 60+, not 55+, and Legion Gardens is a life lease — both are different products, listed separately in Section 2. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption), with further defined exemptions for children and live-in caregivers. At St. George's Village the co-op's own form allows a dependent aged 19+ to live with a member; whether a younger spouse qualifies as that dependent is UNCONFIRMED in the form — ask the co-op. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.stgeorgesvillage.ca/_files/ugd/070889_39221c746a9b483980e20a3596cdb580.pdf
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted in a strata — the bylaw governs who may reside; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. Co-op membership and park tenancy have their own age rules — at a 55+ park, the park may refuse an assignment where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** In a strata, long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022 — though the tenant must still satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. In a manufactured-home park, renting and assignment run through the park rules and the MHPTA instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: How much can a pad rent go up in the 55+ parks?** For increases taking effect in 2026, the provincial maximum for manufactured home park tenancies is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees, once per 12 months on 3 full months' notice. The current pad rent for a specific Alouette or Val Maria home is listing-stated / tenancy-document business — confirm it in the park documents. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2025HMA0067-000786.htm
- **Q: Can I build a second home or suite on a rural Maple Ridge lot for a parent?** On ALR land: at most one additional residence (90 m² at ≤40 ha, 186 m² above 40 ha) with the principal residence capped at 500 m² and any suite inside it — and ALR status is per-parcel, UNVERIFIED for any feed lot until checked. Off the ALR: one secondary suite per lot inside the house (maximum 40% of gross floor area, not in the floodplain), with SSMUH paths to three, four or six units on eligible lots. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Q: What is the smallest lot I can create?** It depends on the zone under Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019: RS-1 requires 668.0 m², RS-2 requires 0.4 ha, and the agricultural zones run from A-1 at 2.0 ha to A-3 at 8.0 ha — and ALR land also needs Agricultural Land Commission permission to subdivide outside limited exceptions. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, Maple Ridge — 24/7 emergency care for Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows; the Urgent and Primary Care Centre is in Haney Place Mall, open 7 days a week, 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital
- **Q: Are the market numbers on a lots page land prices?** No. SnapStats publishes no vacant-land category; the Maple Ridge residential figures sometimes quoted as context (detached median $1,131,000; Condos & Townhomes $525,000 in September 2026) are housing-market context only, not land statistics — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report.

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The city's 55+ ownership is a downtown story, not a land story.** Every verified 55+ strata in Maple Ridge shares one amenity building at Fraserview Village, and the co-op, park and life-lease options all sit within the East Central / Cottonwood MR / 224 Street ground — the rural sub-areas that dominate the lots-and-land feed (Silver Valley, Thornhill, Webster's Corners, Whonnock) contribute zero verified 55+ ownership complexes in this series. Sources: Section 2 above and the sub-area files in this series.
- **The co-op with the higher age bar is the one buyers misquote.** Haney Pioneer Village is 60+ in every source (CHF BC and the Seniors Resource Directory), while St. George's Village is genuinely 55 — swapping the two figures is the most common error in Maple Ridge seniors copy. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/ and https://www.stgeorgesvillage.ca/_files/ugd/070889_39221c746a9b483980e20a3596cdb580.pdf
- **St. George's Village holds clear title to its land** — stated in its own application form, a tenure point worth confirming in the share purchase because many co-ops sit on leased land; this one states it does not. https://www.stgeorgesvillage.ca/_files/ugd/070889_39221c746a9b483980e20a3596cdb580.pdf
- **SnapStats has no land category at all.** Any "Maple Ridge land median" or "price per acre" figure circulating in copy has no permitted source in this program — the research file states the limitation openly and quotes only residential context figures, labelled as such. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/maple-ridge-lots-land.md
- **Two OCP citations are in circulation for this city in this program's own files** — Bylaw No. 7060-2014 in the East Central / Albion files and Bylaw No. 7998-2024 in the batch-12 research files, with an OCP update adopted Nov 25, 2025 also recorded.
