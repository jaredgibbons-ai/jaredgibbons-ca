# Edgemont Village, District of North Vancouver — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Edgemont Village is a neighbourhood in the District of North Vancouver and one of the District's six Village Centres in its Upper Capilano area; its core is the Edgemont Boulevard shopping strip at Highland Boulevard, where the Capilano Library (3045 Highland Boulevard) sits — described by the library as "located in the heart of Edgemont Village". https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf and https://www.nvdpl.ca/capilano-library
- Edgemont is one of six village centres named in the District's OCP, with Lions Gate, Queensdale, Maplewood, Parkgate and Deep Cove. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- Nearby areas are Canyon Heights (west), Capilano Highlands (north), Delbrook and Upper Lonsdale (east), and Queensdale (southeast) — per the area research file.
- Transit through the village: TransLink Route 232 (Grouse Mountain/Phibbs Exchange) runs via Highland Boulevard, Edgemont Boulevard and Capilano Road, and Route 246 (Downtown/Highland) runs via Capilano Road, Ridgewood Drive, Edgemont Boulevard and Highland Boulevard, with downtown-bound trips crossing the Lions Gate Bridge. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt232.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt246.pdf
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates: in the village core itself, on two streets a short walk apart — Connaught Crescent (Edgemont Villa, the verified 55+ strata) and Highland Boulevard (Amica Edgemont Village, the retirement/care residence at 3225 Highland Blvd). Both sit within the walkable Edgemont Boulevard strip, beside the library, grocery and cafés. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **District zoning: Zoning Bylaw 3210** is the District of North Vancouver's zoning bylaw — the Lynnmour/Edgemont research files cite it as amended by Bylaw 8698 (June 2024) for the District's suite and coach-house rules. Verify the current consolidated text and zone for a specific lot with the District, as the District's zoning rewrite was in progress in 2026 per the area research file. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/new-provincial-housing-legislation-fact-sheet.pdf
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw 7900** — District growth is directed to its town and village centres under the OCP (Bylaw 7900), with Edgemont designated as one of the six village centres, while surrounding established streets remain low-density. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf
- **Secondary suites and coach houses:** since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary; December 2023 changes let a secondary suite be up to 49% of the house with no minimum size and raised the maximum coach house height to two storeys. That is the route for a caregiver or family suite on an Edgemont house lot. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **Hazard permit areas matter on Edgemont's ravine lots:** Schedule B of the District OCP establishes Development Permit Areas for Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard; Mackay Creek and Mosquito Creek run through the neighbourhood's ravines. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-single-family-residential-1-5.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Edgemont?** On current evidence, one building: Edgemont Villa, 3151 Connaught Crescent — a 20-unit (listing-stated), freehold strata built in 2003, described across multiple listings as a boutique 55+ residence in the heart of the village. Confirm the exact age bylaw in the strata documents before an offer. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3102670-3151-connaught-crescent-206-north-vancouver-bc-v7r-4x6/brochure and https://storage.ubertor.com/nshomes.myubertor.com/content/featuresheet_289_document/46715.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in Edgemont Villa?** Under provincial strata rules, a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption) is exempt. Live-in caregivers are also exempt, as are children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children the resident cared for before 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in Edgemont Villa as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022 — but the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is Amica Edgemont Village something I buy into?** No. It is a rental senior-living residence at 3225 Highland Boulevard offering independent living, assisted living, memory care and licensed residential care, and it is registered with BC's Assisted Living Registry (operator Edgemont Senior Living Inc.). https://www.amica.ca/edgemont-village and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B1%5D=city%3AVernon&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=desc
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Edgemont Villa?** UNCONFIRMED — no public source checked states Edgemont Villa's pet bylaw. Read the building's bylaws on the Form B / strata documents for the specific unit; do not rely on a listing summary.
- **Q: What about parking and storage at Edgemont Villa?** Listings state underground parking (1 stall) and storage, with elevator access, in a low-rise village building — confirm the specific unit's stall and locker assignment on the Form B. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3102670-3151-connaught-crescent-206-north-vancouver-bc-v7r-4x6/brochure
- **Q: Which schools serve Edgemont — relevant for resale and visiting family?** Highlands Elementary (3150 Colwood Drive) and Canyon Heights Elementary (4501 Highland Boulevard), both K–7, split by address, feed Handsworth Secondary (Grades 8–12, 1033 Handsworth Road). SD44 locator samples confirm Canyon Heights/Handsworth for 4501 Highland Boulevard and Highlands/Handsworth for 3150 Colwood Drive; confirm any other address with SD44. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/044
- **Q: Where is the hospital and recreation?** Edgemont has no hospital in the village — the in-village recreation anchor is the Capilano Library at 3045 Highland Boulevard, and the nearest full-service NVRC centre named in the research file is the Delbrook Community Recreation Centre at 851 West Queens Road, with a main pool and leisure pool. Hospital name/distance: UNCONFIRMED from a source in this file — verify before page copy states it. https://www.nvdpl.ca/capilano-library and http://www.nvrc.ca/facilities-fields/accessibility
- **Q: Can I add a suite for a caregiver to an Edgemont house instead of buying in a 55+ building?** In many cases yes as a housing form: since June 2024 the District permits a principal dwelling plus a secondary suite plus a coach house (three units total) on single-family zoned lots within the Urban Containment Boundary, subject to the lot's zone and any creek/slope development permit areas. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-single-family-residential-1-5.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Edgemont's 55+ supply is tiny and old-listing-driven.** The entire verified ownership supply is one 20-unit building from 2003 (Edgemont Villa); the village's newer attached supply — Grosvenor's Connaught, 82 condos and townhomes completed in 2019 — is general-market, not age-restricted. A "55+ Edgemont" search will mostly surface resales in that one small building, when any are listed at all. https://www.grosvenor.com/property/property-america/connaught and https://storage.ubertor.com/nshomes.myubertor.com/content/featuresheet_289_document/46715.pdf
- **The care ladder is in the same few blocks.** A buyer can move from an owned suite at Edgemont Villa (Connaught Crescent) to rental independent/assisted/memory and licensed residential care at Amica Edgemont Village (3225 Highland Boulevard) without leaving the village — Amica is registered with the provincial Assisted Living Registry, operator Edgemont Senior Living Inc. https://www.amica.ca/edgemont-village
- **The village grocery site was rebuilt around the seniors buildings.** The Grosvenor Edgemont project redeveloped the village's main grocery site at the Edgemont/Ridgewood gateway with an expanded grocery store, ground-floor shops and 83 apartments and townhomes (NVRC document; Grosvenor states 82 homes for Connaught) — the retail Edgemont Villa and Amica residents walk to is this renewed core. https://www.nvrc.ca/sites/default/files/2022-09/grosvenor_edgemont_public_call_for_art_submissions_v3b.pdf
- **District taxes and utilities for 2026, for budgeting:** District of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value, and single-family utility fees include water at $932.80 and sewer/drainage at $1,255.60 (combined single-family utilities $2,571 in 2026). Strata units carry their own utility arrangements — confirm on the Form B. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- **The elementary catchment split runs through the neighbourhood.** The Highland Boulevard side samples to Canyon Heights Elementary and the Colwood Drive side to Highlands Elementary in the SD44 locator, both feeding Handsworth Secondary — a street-by-street fact that affects resale to families even for a 55+ buyer thinking about exit value. (SD44 School Locator, run 2026-11-01, per the area research file.)
- **Creek ravines are a permit fact, not just scenery.** Mosquito Creek and Mackay Creek run through Edgemont's ravines; District planning material describes Mosquito Creek as a "creekside natural area with well used trails" and MacKay Creek as a "creekside natural area with fish habitat", and the OCP's hazard Development Permit Areas apply to creek and slope lots. https://app.dnv.org/OpenDocument/Default.aspx?docNum=4096768 and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-single-family-residential-1-5.pdf
