## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **ALR housing frame (the rule set that governs most Fraser Valley acreage housing):** land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless it qualifies for an additional residence; the principal residence must be 500 m² or less in total floor area, an additional residence is capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha, and a secondary suite is only inside the principal residence; local governments may regulate further but not be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **ALR stratification rule, stated exactly:** residences / land in the ALR cannot be stratified **without the ALC's permission** — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALCA s. 19 / s. 21(1), as explained in ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban — and it is the structural reason a "55+ strata on ALR acreage" product effectively does not exist without an ALC approval no source in this series records. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **ALR short-term accommodation limits:** renting out the entire principal residence for short-term stays is not permitted outright in the ALR and requires ALC approval; an additional residence may be used for short-term accommodation only as agri-tourism accommodation on a property that is actively farmed and holds farm classification under the Assessment Act. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Manufactured-home parks run on different law.** Pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not the Strata Property Act; a park's age rule is a park rule, not a strata bylaw. For rent increases taking effect in 2026, the maximum for manufactured home park tenancies is 2.3%, plus a proportional amount for the change in local government levies and regulated utility fees, once per 12 months on 3 full months' notice. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2025HMA0067-000786.htm
- **Farm classification (BC Assessment Class 9) income thresholds:** a producing farm must meet minimum gross income at farm gate prices of $2,500 if the operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if larger; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — and a buyer continuing to farm must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by the legislated October 31 deadline for the next tax year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Co-op age rules are membership rules, not strata bylaws** — St. George's Village sets 55 and Haney Pioneer Village sets 60+ in their own documents, and the provincial strata exemptions do not automatically apply. https://www.stgeorgesvillage.ca/_files/ugd/070889_39221c746a9b483980e20a3596cdb580.pdf and https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Servicing on acreages** — wells, septic, water licences and irrigation rights for any specific parcel: UNVERIFIED in this regional file; these are parcel-level facts in municipal / provincial records and on title. https://jaredgibbons.ca/fraser-valley-acreages-for-sale.html
- **FHSA / Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits: UNVERIFIED** — not read off a live CRA page in this pass; do not quote a figure from this file.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ acreage community anywhere in the Fraser Valley?** Not on any source in this series. No verified 55+ complex sits on an acreage parcel, and on ALR land a strata split itself needs Agricultural Land Commission permission — so the product a buyer is imagining (a 55+ rancher subdivision on farmland) has no verified example in the valley in this research. The verified 55+ ownership roster is urban strata, one co-op, and manufactured-home parks (Section 2). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: So where CAN I buy 55+ in the Fraser Valley?** By city, from the verified rosters: South Surrey has the deepest shelf (six strata complexes); Maple Ridge has the four Fraserview Village buildings plus the St. George's Village co-op; Ladner has Delta Green and South Pointe; Langley (Aldergrove), Abbotsford, Mission and Chilliwack have one verified strata complex each; and five 55+ manufactured-home parks (Langley, Surrey, two in Maple Ridge, Abbotsford, Mission) sell the home on a rented pad. Haney Pioneer Village in Maple Ridge is 60+, not 55+. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Can I create my own 55+ set-up on a general-market acreage — a main house plus a home for a parent?** That is the realistic acreage route, and it runs on residence caps, not age bylaws: on ALR land, one principal residence ≤500 m² (suite only inside it) plus at most one additional residence at 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha); off the ALR, the municipality where the parcel sits sets the suite / secondary-dwelling rules. No age restriction attaches to any of it — the arrangement is a family plan, not a 55+ community, and it does not restrict a future buyer. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata (from the roster)?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. Co-op and park rules are set by those operators instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted in a strata — the bylaw governs who may reside; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. In a 55+ manufactured-home park, assignment runs through the park rules and the MHPTA, and a park may refuse an assignment where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata, or rent out an acreage house short-term?** Two different regimes. In a strata: long-term rental bans are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022, the tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. On ALR acreage: renting the entire principal residence short-term is not permitted outright and requires ALC approval; an additional residence can be short-term accommodation only as agri-tourism on an actively farmed, farm-classified property. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: How much can a pad rent go up in the 55+ parks?** For increases taking effect in 2026, the provincial maximum for manufactured home park tenancies is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees, once per 12 months on 3 full months' notice. The current pad rent for a specific home is a tenancy-document figure — several parks' pad figures in this series are UNCONFIRMED park-wide, so confirm in the park documents. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2025HMA0067-000786.htm
- **Q: Is there a published Fraser Valley acreage market statistic I can price from?** No. SnapStats publishes no acreage segment — acreage listings sit inside their own communities' ordinary Detached rows, split only as Detached vs Condos & Townhomes — so any "average acreage price" has no permitted source; price parcel by parcel on its own community's comparables, ALR status, water, buildings and farm income — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: What farm income does an acreage need to keep farm classification?** BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income (farm gate) thresholds are $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha; a buyer continuing to farm must apply by October 31 for the next tax year, and classification is removed if the property stops meeting the requirements. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: If I buy the acreage now, where would I go if I need care later?** On this series' sources, the care ladder is urban in every valley city — retirement residences and assisted living at city addresses (for example Maple Ridge's 224 Street / 119 Avenue cluster, Delta's Ladner and Tsawwassen residences) — so ageing off an acreage normally means a move into the nearest city's stock, not a building on the rural parcel. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=carouselnext&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&page=0&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The absence of 55+ acreage product is structural, not a search failure.** ALR land cannot be stratified without the Agricultural Land Commission's permission (ALCA s. 21(1); ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6), and ALR housing is capped at one principal residence plus at most one small additional residence — the two facts together rule out the standard 55+ strata-on-farmland product across most of the valley's acreage belt. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **The deepest 55+ shelf in the region is not near the acreages at all.** South Surrey's six verified 55+ strata complexes are the valley's largest verified cluster in this series, followed by Maple Ridge's Fraserview Village (four buildings, one shared amenity building) — a buyer weighting "55+ community" over "land" is really choosing between those two urban pockets and the single-complex cities. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **One bare-land strata on the roster proves the exception's shape.** South Pointe in Ladner (Neilsen Grove) is a gated bare land strata of detached ranchers — the closest verified product to "a house on its own lot in a 55+ community" in the region — and it sits on serviced urban lots in Delta, not on acreage or ALR land. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3075824-5371-regatta-way-delta-bc-v4k-4r9
- **SnapStats has no acreage segment and no land category.** This feed's listings are counted inside ordinary community Detached rows; there is no acreage count, share, median or price-per-acre figure in the permitted source, and none is stated in this file — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report.
- **The feed crosses a board line inside one page.** Its visible cards mix Langley sub-areas with Abbotsford sub-areas (two different board communities), so even community-level medians cannot be presented as this page's figures — the research file states this openly rather than picking one community's numbers. https://jaredgibbons.ca/fraser-valley-acreages-for-sale.html
- **Farm classification has a hard calendar edge.** The October 31 application deadline and the size-band income thresholds ($2,500 / $2,500 + 5% / $10,000) decide whether an acreage keeps its Class 9 position into the next tax year — a closing that slips past the deadline without an application can cost the buyer a full year of classification. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **The region's one 60+ co-op is routinely mislabelled 55+.** Haney Pioneer Village (11830 230 St, Maple Ridge) is 60+ in every source; regional copy that folds it into a "55+" count overstates the Maple Ridge roster by one and misstates its age bar. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
