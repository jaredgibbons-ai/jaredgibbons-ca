## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **ALR housing frame — the rule set that governs the farmhouse on a poultry parcel:** on Agricultural Land Reserve land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence is permitted, up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; local government bylaws may be more restrictive and may not be more permissive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm classification is a BC Assessment program with income thresholds, and it does not survive a sale automatically:** the thresholds are $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if larger, and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha; applications must be submitted by October 31 for the next assessment and tax year, and a buyer who plans to keep farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Quota and supply management: UNVERIFIED in every particular** — inclusion or exclusion on any given listing, quota volumes, and the transfer, approval and premises-certification rules of the BC Chicken Marketing Board, BC Egg Marketing Board, BC Broiler Hatching Egg Commission and BC Turkey Marketing Board were not confirmed on any marketing-board source checked for this file. The live page's quota account is site copy only, and the one operating farm in its feed states quota is not included. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-poultry-farms.html
- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now** (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; younger age bylaws invalid since Nov 24, 2022, Bill 44) — the rule that governs the downsizing destination in Section 2, not the farm itself. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The strata bylaw restricts residing, not owning,** and Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted, and a tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home pads are tenancy law:** under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, pad rent can be increased only once every 12 months, on at least three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026) plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **City frame for a farm parcel:** Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 governs zoning (its framework also defines "Congregate Housing," added by Bylaw 3249-2022, for seniors supportive projects in town), and Abbotsforward 2050 (Bylaw 2600-2016, as updated) is the Official Community Plan; a parcel's ALR status, zone and floodplain position are per-parcel checks — UNVERIFIED for any specific farm in this file. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/development-planning
- **Barn, environmental and disease-control rules for a specific operation: UNVERIFIED** — barn construction, manure storage, ventilation, groundwater licensing, biosecurity and any avian-influenza effect on a sale were not confirmed on an official source checked for this file; the live page's due-diligence list is site copy only. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-poultry-farms.html
- **Property transfer tax (general tiers):** 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% on the portion above $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% above $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. How PTT and GST apportion between land, barns, residence and quota on a specific poultry purchase is UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **FHSA and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits: UNVERIFIED** — no live CRA page stating those limits was read for this file.
- **Property taxes:** City of Abbotsford taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is quota included when I buy an Abbotsford poultry farm?** UNVERIFIED as a general rule in this file — no marketing-board source was checked that states it. The live page states quota does not run with the land title and must be expressly specified in the contract, and the one operating farm in its feed states quota is not included. Read the specific contract and verify the transfer position with the applicable board before subjects are removed. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-poultry-farms.html
- **Q: What house can a poultry parcel in the ALR have?** Under the ALC frame: one principal residence of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only inside that principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha), with City rules possibly more restrictive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a farm worker or my adult child live in the second house?** The ALC frame permits at most one additional residence within the 90 m² / 186 m² tiers; who may occupy it, and any farm-worker conditions in City zoning, are parcel- and bylaw-specific — UNCONFIRMED for any specific parcel in this file. The strata exemptions (caregivers, adult children) in Section 3 apply to strata units, not to farm residences. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What income must the operation show to keep farm classification?** BC Assessment's thresholds are $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha; a buyer continuing to farm must apply by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: We're retiring off the farm. Where is the 55+ housing, actually?** In town: 26 verified 55+ strata complexes (15 in Abbotsford West, 9 in Central Abbotsford, 2 in Poplar), 3 manufactured-home parks and 2 life leases — none in the prairie sub-areas. The ground-oriented options closest to farm life are the bare land strata ranchers at Downes Road Estates and the manufactured homes in the three parks. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata after we sell?** Yes, in law: Regulation 7.01 exempts a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident, and several complexes state a one-resident rule themselves — Royal Court ("only one owner must be at least 55") and The Wedgewood ("only one resident needs to be over 55"). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Are market statistics published for Abbotsford poultry farms?** No figure is stated in this file: SnapStats has no farm category, and no official source checked publishes Abbotsford poultry-farm counts, quota prices or per-acre poultry values — each is UNVERIFIED, and a price opinion stands on the specific property, part by part (land, barns, residence, any quota).
- **Q: Is the Sumas Prairie floodplain position settled for a specific farm?** No — the live page's placement of the prairies inside a Floodplain Development Permit Area is site copy here, UNVERIFIED on a City source checked for this file. Confirm Flood Construction Level and permit requirements for the specific parcel with the City before planning a barn or a house. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-poultry-farms.html
- **Q: When are property taxes due on an Abbotsford poultry farm?** For a City of Abbotsford parcel: due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 16, 2026. Farm classification affects the assessed class, not the due dates. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **Q: If we keep the farm and need care later, what's in Abbotsford?** The in-city ladder: independent supportive living and assisted living at the Menno Place campus and Tabor Village, independent living rental at The Mayfair and the Hallmark residences, and long-term care at MSA Manor, Maplewood House, Tabor Home and Menno Home/Hospital — publicly funded beds are coordinated through Fraser Health. Sources: Section 2 above.

- **Farm classification has a hard calendar edge.** The BC Assessment application deadline is October 31 — a poultry purchase that closes after that date, without the buyer's own General Application filed by October 31 after purchase, puts the next tax year's farm class at risk regardless of what the seller's classification was. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **A "second house for the kids" on ALR land is capped small.** The additional-residence tier is 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less — a cottage-scale cap that surprises buyers expecting a full second farmhouse; only properties over 40 ha reach the 186 m² tier. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Downsizing geography runs one way.** Every verified 55+ complex in Abbotsford is in Abbotsford West, Central Abbotsford or Poplar — the prairie sub-areas where the poultry operations sit contribute the sellers, not the stock. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Three "Maplewoods," three tenures.** Maplewood Manor (Tims Ave) is a buyable 55+ strata; Maplewood House (Jackson St) is a Fraser Health-funded care home; Maplewood Estates (Martens St) is a 55+ manufactured-home park. A retiring farm couple searching "Maplewood" will meet all three. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Abbotsford's tax penalty is a two-step.** Taxes were due July 2, 2026, with 5% added July 3 and a second 5% on September 16, 2026 — farm parcels are billed on the same City calendar. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
