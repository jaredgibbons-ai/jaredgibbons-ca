# East Central, Maple Ridge — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "East Central" is a Paragon (MLS) sub-area name only; it is not a City of Maple Ridge plan or neighbourhood name. The downtown part of the sub-area is covered by the City's Town Centre Area Plan, whose boundaries were expanded in the plan update to incorporate River Bend; the wider sub-area takes in the residential streets east and north of downtown toward 232 Street and 124 Avenue. The City publishes no boundary for the MLS sub-area. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) runs along the south side; Dewdney Trunk Road runs east–west through downtown and the residential area to the east; 224 Street and 226 Street are the main north–south streets through the downtown core; the Haney Bypass links Lougheed Highway to Port Haney at the sub-area's southwest corner. https://www.mapleridge.ca/art-parks-rec/recreation-facilities/memorial-peace-park
- Haney Place Exchange is in this sub-area (routes include the R3, 701, 733, 741, 743, 744, 745, 746, 748, 749 and 791); the R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus runs from Coquitlam Central Station to Haney Place. Port Haney Station, 22300 River Road, at the sub-area's southwest corner, runs weekday West Coast Express trains between Waterfront (downtown Vancouver) and Mission City. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Route_Diagrams/194/r748.pdf and https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- The civic cluster sits mid-downtown: Maple Ridge Leisure Centre at 11925 Haney Place, Memorial Peace Park on 224 Street between Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road (City Hall, the ACT Arts Centre, Haney Place Mall at 11900 Haney Place around it), and Maple Ridge Public Library at 130–22470 Dewdney Trunk Road. https://www.mapleridge.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/maple-ridge-leisure-centre and https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations/maple-ridge-public-library
- School District 42 serves the sub-area; schools physically inside it include Golden Ears Elementary (23124 118 Avenue), Harry Hooge Elementary (12280 230 Street) and Thomas Haney Secondary (23000 116 Avenue). Catchments are address-based. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates: this sub-area holds nearly all of Maple Ridge's 55+ stock, in three pockets — (1) the Fraserview Village pocket south of the downtown core around 116 Avenue and 225–227 Streets (four 55+ strata buildings sharing one amenity building, plus Fraserwood beside them); (2) the 224 Street retirement row at the downtown core (Sunwood at 12241, Chartwell Willow at 12275, Legion Gardens at 12127); and (3) the 119 Avenue / 229–230 Street pocket east of downtown (Maple Ridge Seniors Village at 22141 119 Ave, Haney Pioneer Village Co-op at 11830 230 St, Alouette Retirement Mobile Home Park at 12868 229 St, Greystone Manor at 11657 Ritchie Ave just south of Lougheed-side streets). Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Prairie-style caution in reverse: unlike most Maple Ridge sub-areas, the 55+ product here is real and dense — East Central carried 120 active Condos & Townhomes listings in September 2026, the largest attached inventory of any Maple Ridge sub-area — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://choa.bc.ca/resources/strata-property-act/legislative-updates/
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **SOURCE CONFLICT — FLAG, do not smooth over:** building sources for Rosewood Court (Fraserview Village) still record its bylaws as including "rentals not allowed." Under the provincial change above, a residential rental-restriction bylaw has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 regardless of what an older bylaw set or a building-data site still displays. A buyer at Fraserview should have the strata's current bylaws and a Form B reviewed rather than rely on either the building-data summary or a listing. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/rosewood-court-22611-116-ave and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured home parks run on different law.** Pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and its Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003), not the Strata Property Act. The Regulation expressly contemplates parks where every site is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 (or two or more tenants, at least one of whom has reached 55), per the Human Rights Code s. 10(2)(b)(i) permitted age requirements — and a park landlord may refuse an assignment or sublease where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement in such a park. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Co-op and life-lease age rules are contract/membership rules, not strata bylaws.** Haney Pioneer Village's 60+ threshold and the Legion life leases' terms are set by those organizations' own documents; the provincial strata exemptions (spouse, caregiver, legacy) do not automatically apply to them — read the occupancy agreement or life lease itself. Sources: Section 2 entries above. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019.** Houses sit under RS-1 (s. 605 — minimum lot area 668.0 m², width 18.0 m, depth 27.0 m, maximum coverage 40%); Town Centre parcels carry Town Centre commercial, multi-family and comprehensive development zones under the same bylaw. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **Transit-Oriented Area Designation Bylaw No. 8001-2024** covers the Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station TOAs inside the Town Centre plan area — the provincially driven density framework around the two transit anchors nearest the 55+ cluster. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- **Suites and added units:** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-family and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Floodplain (south edge only):** along River Road / Port Haney the flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), a covenant on title identifies construction in the floodplain, no habitable floor space may be located below the flood construction level on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. The Fraserview Village pocket is north of the river flats; floodplain status is per-lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** for this area (Zone 4 directory, May 2025), with some buildings apply-direct to the society — the directory gives the route per building. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What exactly is Fraserview Village, and what do I actually buy?** It is a cluster of separate strata buildings (Rosewood Court, Emerald Manor, The Willows, Castlemount among them) around a shared amenity building with an indoor pool, hot tub, exercise/recreation centre, clubhouse, workshop and a rentable guest suite. You buy a strata lot in one building; the strata fee carries your building's costs plus the village amenity access (listings for The Willows state the fee includes amenity-building use). Each building has its own bylaws — verify the specific building's age bylaw on the Form B. https://vancouvernashdom.com/maple-ridge-east-central-apartments/SearchResults.form/feed/feed/feed/ and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/rosewood-court-22611-116-ave
- **Q: Building sites say Rosewood Court doesn't allow rentals. Can I rent out my Fraserview unit or not?** The building-data summary is stale against provincial law: every residential rental-restriction bylaw in BC became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: a tenant must still satisfy the building's 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and the strata can still restrict short-term rentals. Get the strata's current bylaw set and legal advice rather than relying on the summary. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/rosewood-court-22611-116-ave and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a Fraserview building?** Yes — in a strata, provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. Note this provincial guarantee applies to strata; the Haney Pioneer co-op (60+) and the Legion life leases set their own household rules. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a Fraserview unit as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Fraserview?** Building by building. Rosewood Court records pets allowed with restrictions; a listing for The Willows states the building is pet friendly and allows 2 cats or 2 dogs (or 1 of each). Confirm the specific building's pet bylaw on the Form B. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/rosewood-court-22611-116-ave and https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vcijbJjc6c0
- **Q: Why is Haney Pioneer Village called 55+ when its rule is 60+?** It should not be — its own federation listing and the Seniors Resource Directory both state it is a co-op "for active seniors aged 60+" / "independent seniors age 60+." A 55-to-59-year-old does not qualify on the stated rule. It is carried in this file because it is the area's seniors co-op, with the higher bar flagged rather than rounded down. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/ and https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/MRN/250926/MRNN250926_Z35.pdf
- **Q: What does buying at the Alouette park actually cost each month?** You own the manufactured home and rent the pad under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. A current listing states the pad rental fee at $713.31 plus city taxes of $457.37 and quarterly metered water, with park rules including no dogs, indoor cat only, 2 residents per unit, and no financing permitted in the community (all listing-stated — confirm in the tenancy agreement and park rules). The "no financing permitted" term, if current, means a buyer needs cash or financing structured outside the park's rule — verify before writing an offer. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3067037-12868-229th-street-18-maple-ridge-bc-v2x-6r1/brochure
- **Q: What is a life lease at Legion Gardens — do I own it?** You purchase a life-lease interest from Royal Canadian Legion Branch 88, not a freehold or strata title: the Legion's directory entry lists the cost simply as "Life lease," the 2009 case study records 56 suites of about 1,000 sq ft financed and paid off by selling life leases, and veterans are placed first though the buildings are open to everyone. The buy-back and resale terms live in the Legion's own lease documents, which no public source checked reproduces — UNCONFIRMED, so read the lease itself before comparing it to a strata price. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/MRN/190830/MRNN190830_Z31.pdf and https://dev.ibabchyperarticles.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Seniors-Housing-Development-2010.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital and urgent care?** Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, is the community hospital for Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows with 24/7 emergency care, just west of this sub-area; the Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is inside Haney Place Mall, 121–11900 Haney Place, in the Town Centre part of this sub-area, open 7 days a week, 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital and https://www.healthlinkbc.ca/clinic/ridge-meadows-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
- **Q: Can I get downtown without a car?** Port Haney Station (22300 River Road) runs West Coast Express trains to Waterfront on weekdays, the R3 RapidBus runs from Haney Place Exchange to Coquitlam Central Station (SkyTrain), and the Fraserview Village listings specifically market walking distance to transit, shopping and the West Coast Express. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express and https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects
- **Q: Is flood risk a factor for the 55+ buildings?** The floodplain rules bite at the sub-area's south edge (River Road / Port Haney): flood construction level 6.7 m geodetic, no habitable floor space below it on affected lots, suites prohibited on floodplain properties, and floodplain status registered by covenant on title. The Fraserview and 224 Street buildings sit north of the river flats, but status is per-lot — confirm on title for any specific purchase. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
