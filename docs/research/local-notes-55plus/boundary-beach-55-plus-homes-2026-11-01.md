## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why Monterra's MLS-sheet line "Rentals Not Allowed" cannot be relied on without reading the current Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future")** is Delta's current consolidated OCP — it has no lettered area-plan schedules. Its Hazard Development Permit Area SD3 is named for this exact shore: "Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands." SD3 provides: no building with a habitable floor below 2.9 metres Geodetic Survey of Canada datum, a minimum land surface elevation around construction of 2.3 metres GSC datum, and no building within 7.5 metres of the natural boundary of a tidal area. Whether a specific Boundary Beach parcel sits inside SD3 is a parcel-level check — UNCONFIRMED for any individual lot here. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017** assigns zoning parcel by parcel; the one zoning fact verified for the pocket's named complex is Monterra's CD201 comprehensive development zoning, recorded on its MLS data sheet. https://www.cotala.com/tours/72783/FullPublic.pdf?i=1871186173
- **"Seniors congregate housing" in Delta's zoning:** the Apartment Residential 6 (RA6) zone added to Bylaw No. 7600 lists seniors congregate housing as a principal use alongside apartment buildings, in six-storey development under the OCP's Neighbourhood Centres and Corridors designation — the mechanism by which a future seniors building in this part of Delta would most likely be zoned, since the current pocket has none. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing:** Delta's SSMUH rules allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide, and the City supports accessory dwelling units such as coach houses, garden suites and secondary suites, subject to the bylaw and to any hazard or streamside permit area on the lot — the feed itself shows a coach house included with a 66 Street listing, cited as feed evidence only. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://jaredgibbons.ca/boundary-beach.html
- **Foreshore tenure:** the Province owns nearly all saltwater foreshore; private moorage is held by lease or licence via FrontCounter BC under General Permission or Specific Permission — beach access and a dock are not the same thing, and the tenure is not ownership. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
- **Property taxes and 55+ relief:** Delta's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. The provincial regular property tax deferment loan program covers homeowners 55 and older, and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) covers seniors 65+. No FHSA or Home Buyers' Plan dollar limit is stated in this file — UNVERIFIED, not read off a live CRA page in this research. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
- **Subsidized seniors rental applications** for Delta run through BC Housing's Housing Registry (Zone 3 — Richmond and West Delta directory, May 2025). https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ complex at Boundary Beach itself?** None verified. The pocket's one named strata community, Monterra on 3 Avenue, has no age bylaw stated in any source checked — one MLS sheet calls the site "Adult Oriented" while listing no age restriction among its bylaws. The verified buyable 55+ buildings in Tsawwassen are in Central: The Lexington and Tsawwassen Greene. https://www.cotala.com/tours/72783/FullPublic.pdf?i=1871186173 and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/the-lexington-1150-54a-street
- **Q: Is Monterra 55+? It is gated and the listings sound adult-oriented.** Not verified. Gated is not 55+, and "Adult Oriented" on an MLS data sheet is a site-influence tag, not a bylaw: the same sheet's bylaw line covers only pets and rentals. The only proof would be the strata's registered bylaws, produced on a purchase — ask for them before assuming any age rule, in either direction. https://www.cotala.com/tours/72783/FullPublic.pdf?i=1871186173
- **Q: Are the Southlands homes in this feed age-restricted?** No source says so, and the developer says the opposite: Century Group describes Southlands housing as designed for all life stages, for "families, seniors, and everyone in between." It is mixed-age product that happens to sit inside the Boundary Beach feed. https://centurygroup.ca/stories/multigenerational-living-in-the-21st-century/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55 — does that matter for a Boundary Beach purchase?** Not in the stock checked here, because none of it carries a verified age bylaw. Where a 55+ bylaw does exist (Central's buildings), provincial law exempts a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident, plus defined caregiver and child cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a Monterra or Southlands unit long-term?** In a BC strata, long-term rental bans no longer exist — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, even where an older MLS sheet still says "Rentals Not Allowed." A strata can still restrict short-term rentals, and its other bylaws still apply — read the Form B. A freehold Southlands house is not under a strata rental bylaw at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What flood and foreshore rules apply to a waterfront home here?** Hazard Development Permit Area SD3 (Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands) sets a 2.9 m GSC datum minimum floor level, a 2.3 m minimum land surface elevation and a 7.5 m setback from the natural boundary of a tidal area; the shore sits behind the Boundary Bay dike maintained by the City of Delta; and the foreshore itself is provincial Crown land held by lease or licence. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
- **Q: Where is the nearest care if I age in place at Boundary Beach?** The in-Tsawwassen ladder runs through Central: KinVillage's 68 publicly subsidized assisted-living suites and 100 long-term care beds (West Court), The Waterford's 106 independent suites plus 36 licensed care suites at 1345 56 St, and The Wexford's 65 independent living suites on 56 Street at 18 Avenue. Delta Hospital is in Ladner. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/7/B/assisted-living-at-residence-at-kinvillage and https://briacommunities.ca/communities/waterford/
- **Q: What bus serves Boundary Beach if I stop driving?** TransLink route 601 runs Boundary Bay trips via 12 Avenue, Boundary Bay Road, 3 Avenue and 67 Street to 1A Avenue, continuing via South Delta Exchange and Ladner Exchange to Bridgeport Station (Canada Line) — check a specific address against the current timetable. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- **Q: Does the Point Roberts border proximity change anything for a buyer?** The research file records the pocket as sitting beside the Canada–US border at Point Roberts, but no source checked states a housing, tax or residency rule that follows from that proximity — any claim about border effects on value or use is UNCONFIRMED here. https://jaredgibbons.ca/boundary-beach.html
- **Q: Does a 55+ buyer get any tax break on a Boundary Beach house?** The provincial tools are age-based, not area-based: property tax deferment as a low-interest loan from age 55, and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in this region) from age 65, both for a principal residence. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The hazard permit area is named for this shore.** SD3 in the OCP is literally "Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands" — floor level (2.9 m GSC datum), land elevation (2.3 m) and tidal setback (7.5 m) are written into the plan for this pocket and the Southlands lands in its feed, not generic Delta rules. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **The feed's edge is not the neighbourhood's edge.** This page's feed includes Southlands condos, row homes and townhomes on Market Avenue and Southlands Drive and the 3 Avenue gated community, all labelled Boundary Beach — compare a listing against its own street and peers, not against beachfront cards on the same page. https://jaredgibbons.ca/boundary-beach.html
- **The beach in front of the homes is a protected bird area with dog rules.** Boundary Bay Regional Park is an internationally recognized Important Bird Area on the Pacific Flyway, and most of Centennial Beach is closed to dogs, with signed marker posts — a daily-life detail for a dog-owning downsizer that no listing mentions. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park
- **No published market statistics cover this pocket.** SnapStats publishes no South Delta figures — its Delta coverage is North Delta only — so no Boundary Beach median, inventory or sales figure can be stated from the sources used here, and North Delta figures must never be presented as Boundary Beach figures. (Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, as recorded in the Boundary Beach area research file.)
- **"Adult Oriented" on a data sheet is doing quiet work.** Monterra's MLS sheet uses that tag while stating no age bylaw — in a post-2022 market where the only legal strata age bylaw is 55+, a buyer who needs the legal protection of a 55+ community, or who needs to be sure there is none, has to read the registered bylaws rather than the tag. https://www.cotala.com/tours/72783/FullPublic.pdf?i=1871186173 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta's flood system is city-scale:** over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure maintained by the City — the Boundary Bay dike is one piece of that system, and lot-level SD3 status still has to be confirmed per parcel. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
