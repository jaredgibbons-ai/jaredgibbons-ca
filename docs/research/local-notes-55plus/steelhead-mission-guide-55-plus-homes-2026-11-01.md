## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. There is no strata in Steelhead for this to attach to today — it is the rule a buyer would meet in the city's 55+ strata complexes in Section 2, and the rule that decides Briskham Manor's old "35+!" advertisement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020** divides Mission's rural area into Rural zones RU80, RU36 and RU16, each with a Secondary Dwelling ("s") variant (RU80s, RU36s, RU16s), and Rural Residential zones RR7 / RR7s; a City rezoning notice describes RU16 as carrying a 1.6 ha minimum lot size. Any Steelhead parcel's actual zone is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/notice-rezoning-bylaws-june-1-2026
- **Secondary suites and second dwellings:** a Mission secondary suite is a self-contained living space inside a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity; a suite is not a coach home or garden cottage (those are detached secondary dwellings, which on rural lots generally require the "s" variant of the parcel's zone); suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes; all suites must be registered with the City, new suites go through the building permit process, and an annual secondary suite fee of $63 plus utility fees applies. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program and https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-rsd25-4
- **Current plan: Official Community Plan Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026** on the settled position in this program. CONFLICT FLAGGED: the guide page states a June 1, 2026 adoption date, which conflicts with that position and is recorded in the research file as a live-page error. https://jaredgibbons.ca/steelhead-mission-guide.html and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/steelhead-mission-guide.md
- **SSMUH:** Mission has adopted the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation for zones previously restricted to single-family or duplex dwellings as of December 7, 2023, subject to eligibility and servicing; Steelhead's rural lots sit outside the urban serviced area, so whether SSMUH unit counts apply to a given lot is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh
- **Wells, septic and ALR are the working rules on this ground:** ALR status is per-parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder; wells are recorded in the provincial GWELLS database; onsite sewerage must comply with the Sewerage System Regulation, including the 30 m setback from a well unless professional advice is obtained. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo104/loo104/22_326_2004
- **Property taxes:** City of Mission 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a flat 10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding (including unclaimed home owner grants) on July 3, 2026 — a single flat step inside the city, not a staged 5% + 5%. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes

- **Q: My spouse is under 55. - **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is there bus service or a care home out here if I stop driving?** No bus route into Steelhead was confirmed on a transit source: UNVERIFIED, and no care or assisted-living residence in Steelhead appears in BC's Assisted Living Registry — the registered Mission residences are at urban addresses.
