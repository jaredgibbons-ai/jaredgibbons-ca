The City page does not state a full set of boundary roads in text, and exact boundary roads are UNVERIFIED from a City text source. ## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- **Terraces on 7th (Terraces On Seventh) — 1570 West 7th Avenue (Fairview / South Granville).** The one verified age-restricted strata ownership building in Fairview — and a hybrid product: you buy a strata suite inside a building that also operates as a boutique retirement community. A current listing for a suite in the building (MLS R3117996) states it outright: "Welcome to the Terraces on 7th, a much sought after retirement community with a 55 plus age restriction." The building-data source for the same address records that the strata bylaws include age restrictions (with pets allowed), that the building was built in 2003, and that it has 52 units, with elevator, in-suite laundry, storage and wheelchair access. SOURCE NOTE on the year: the listing text states "Built in 2002" while the building-data source states built in 2003 — treat the exact build year as 2002/2003, UNCONFIRMED to a single year. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3117996-1001-1570-w-7th-avenue-vancouver-british-columbia-v6j5m1-940000/brochure and http://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/terraces-on-7-1570-west-ave
- The services side is the operator's own description: Terraces on Seventh calls itself "a boutique retirement community in Vancouver's South Granville neighborhood, thoughtfully designed for older adults seeking independent living," with private one-bedroom, one-bedroom-plus-den and two-bedroom suites, each with a private bathroom, climate control and generous storage, in a quiet, modern, carefully maintained building. Read the two halves together before buying: the strata documents govern the ownership and the age bylaw, and any retirement-community services are a separate arrangement to confirm with the operator — what services, if any, are mandatory for an owner, and at what charge, is UNCONFIRMED from the sources above. https://terracesonseventh.com/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. Terraces on 7th's 55-plus restriction, as stated in its listing and building data, is the form of bylaw that survives. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **How the City creates new 55+ rental:** Vancouver's Seniors Housing Rezoning Policy (approved July 23, 2024; last amended May 19, 2026) treats "Social Housing (where occupancy is restricted to older adults 55+)" as social housing secured by a housing agreement restricting occupancy to residents aged 55 or older, owned and operated by a non-profit or government, and secures seniors supportive or independent living housing as rental — no stratification, no separate sales — for 60 years or the life of the building. Terraces on 7th predates this framework; it is not a template the City's current policy reproduces. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-rezoning-seniors-housing.pdf
- **Fairview Slopes zoning: FM-1 District Schedule of By-law No. 3575** — its stated intent is to enhance the small-scale residential character of the Fairview Slopes neighbourhood by encouraging retention of existing houses and permitting new low-profile residential development; FM-1 permits single detached houses, duplexes, multiple dwellings and multiple conversion dwellings at a maximum floor space ratio of 0.60, with no minimum site area stated in the schedule overview. Southern Fairview lots also fall under R1-1 (multiplex up to 6 units, or up to 8 rental units; a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house); C-3A covers the commercial mixed-use streets. Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-FM-1.pdf
- **Tenure in False Creek South:** the City describes False Creek South as a waterfront community of housing built on City-held land under ground leases — the lease terms and end dates for a specific building are UNVERIFIED in this pass and must be read off the building's own lease documents; Broadway Lodge's own City-land lease expires in 2040, per the 2022 Council motion. Leasehold does not comp against freehold Fairview product. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/false-creek-south-leases-on-city-land.aspx and https://council.vancouver.ca/20220608ag/documents/cfsc10.pdf
- **The Broadway Plan and the subway:** the Broadway Plan covers Fairview's northern half (Clark Drive to Vine Street, 1st to 16th Avenues) and the Broadway Subway is scheduled to open in fall 2027 — lots inside the plan area, including the West 7th Avenue blocks around Terraces, carry plan expectations the base zoning alone does not show. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/broadway-plan.aspx/ and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0050-000621
- **Tax calendar:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after each due date; the Empty Homes Tax requires an annual property status declaration, with the 2025 payment due April 16, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Fairview?** One building: Terraces on 7th, 1570 West 7th Avenue — a 52-unit strata (built 2002/2003, see the source note in Section 2) whose listing states a 55 plus age restriction and whose building data records age restrictions in the strata bylaws, operating as a boutique retirement community for independent living. Everything else verified in Fairview is subsidized rental or long-term care. Confirm the current bylaw wording and any mandatory services agreement in the strata documents before offering. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3117996-1001-1570-w-7th-avenue-vancouver-british-columbia-v6j5m1-940000/brochure and http://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/terraces-on-7-1570-west-ave
- **Q: Is Terraces on 7th a rental retirement home or a condo I own?** Both halves are documented: suites in the building are listed for sale as strata units (MLS R3117996 is a sale listing), and the operator markets the same address as a retirement community for independent living with private suites. The ownership is strata; what the retirement-community operation adds — services, meals, any mandatory charge on top of strata fees — is UNCONFIRMED from public sources and is the first thing to verify in the disclosure and strata documents. https://terracesonseventh.com/ and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3117996-1001-1570-w-7th-avenue-vancouver-british-columbia-v6j5m1-940000/brochure
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live at Terraces on 7th?** Under provincial law, yes — the Strata Property Regulation requires an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in the defined cases. Confirm the building's own bylaw tracks the regulation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a Terraces suite as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. Under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a 55+ parent can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, and building data for Terraces on 7th records pets allowed alongside its age restriction. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation and http://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/terraces-on-7-1570-west-ave
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. In Fairview that trade-off comes with the area's one in-neighbourhood hospital and the South Granville blocks at the door, which is the specific bargain this building offers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: How do I get into Glynn Manor or the other subsidized buildings?** They are non-profit seniors rentals in BC Housing's Zone 6 directory (May 2025), Fairview section — apply through BC Housing's Housing Registry or directly with the operator, and expect a waitlist. The City points seniors to the Seniors Services Society's Seniors Housing Directory of BC for the full list. https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-6-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/people-programs/find-supportive-housing-for-seniors.aspx
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Inside the area: Vancouver General Hospital, 899 West 12th Avenue, on the West Broadway corridor — a few blocks from Terraces on 7th and the West 7th Avenue seniors pocket. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/vancouver-general-hospital
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in Fairview?** The in-area ladder is: independent strata living at Terraces on 7th (with its retirement-community operation), subsidized independent rental (Glynn Manor and the other directory buildings), then long-term care at Broadway Lodge in False Creek South — which the City records is the only long-term care home in False Creek South or anywhere between Arbutus and Main and north of Broadway. https://council.vancouver.ca/20220608ag/documents/cfsc10.pdf
- **Q: Is a False Creek South home in the floodplain, and is it freehold?** Both checks are mandatory there: False Creek South homes sit on City-held land under ground leases, and the City's False Creek floodplain includes False Creek South along Fairview's south shore, with Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements for parcels in it. A specific building's lease terms and a specific parcel's floodplain status: UNVERIFIED — read the lease and check the parcel in the City dataset. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/false-creek-south-leases-on-city-land.aspx and https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **False Creek South is one of the oldest-skewing pockets in the city, and Council has the numbers:** the June 8, 2022 Council motion records that in False Creek South nearly one in three residents are above the age of 65 — over 2,150 seniors (65 years and over), 35.9% of the area's population, up from 30.4% in the 2016 Census and more than double the 17% for the city as a whole — and that as residents age in place there are very few opportunities to move into appropriate "rightsized" housing. That demographic pressure is the backdrop for every seniors-housing decision in Fairview. https://council.vancouver.ca/20220608ag/documents/cfsc10.pdf
- **False Creek South's care future is being negotiated as a "Campus of Care":** the same 2022 motion records that Broadway Lodge and the Residences for Independent Living Society have both provided care and housing in False Creek South for over 40 years, both lease City-owned land (leases expiring in 2036 and 2040), the Lodge's 114 beds will need substantial upgrading by 2030, and the two operators have joined together as the Broadway Group to propose a Campus of Care vision. https://council.vancouver.ca/20220608ag/documents/cfsc10.pdf
- **Terraces on 7th's build year is stated two ways in its own public record:** the sale listing says "Built in 2002," the building-data source says built in 2003, and a 2003 provincial release records independent living units announced for "The Terraces on 7th Avenue, located at 1570 West 7th" — a one-year discrepancy to settle from the strata plan and depreciation report, not from marketing. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3117996-1001-1570-w-7th-avenue-vancouver-british-columbia-v6j5m1-940000/brochure and http://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/terraces-on-7-1570-west-ave and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/archive/2001-2005/2003MCAWS0129-001127-Attachment1.pdf
- **Heather Place is two different projects with one name:** the existing Heather Place at 774 West 13th Avenue is a directory-listed seniors development, while the Heather Place redevelopment at 725–799 West 14th Avenue is a 230-home mixed project for families, seniors and people with disabilities replacing 86 aging units — citing the redevelopment's unit count as "seniors housing" overstates the seniors stock by design. https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-6-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf and https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/new-affordable-homes-break-ground-in-vancouver
- **The West 7th Avenue pocket predates the Broadway Subway by two decades and will outlast its construction:** Glynn Manor (520 West 7th) and Terraces on 7th (1570 West 7th) both sit on the avenue the Broadway Plan covers and the subway will serve from fall 2027 — the area's seniors stock is concentrated on exactly the corridor now being rebuilt around it, with the South Granville station at the pocket's west end. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0050-000621 and https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-6-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **On the Slopes, FM-1 — not the R1-1 multiplex rules — prices the lot:** FM-1 caps floor space ratio at 0.60 and is written to retain existing houses and keep new development low-profile, so a multiplex pitch copied from a southern Fairview R1-1 street misprices a Slopes lot; the two zoning worlds sit blocks apart inside one small local area. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-FM-1.pdf
