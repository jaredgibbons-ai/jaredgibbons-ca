# Port Moody Centre — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Port Moody Centre" is the board (MLS/GVR) sub-area name in the SnapStats GVR September 2026 report. The City's Official Community Plan covers the same ground as two planning areas — Moody Centre and Inlet Centre — under the Port Moody Official Community Plan, Bylaw 2026, No. 3522 (Chapter 14, Section 14.6 for Moody Centre and Section 14.5 for Inlet Centre and Coronation Park). https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733
- The area sits around St. Johns Street, Clarke Street and Murray Street at the head of the Port Moody Arm of Burrard Inlet, between Moody Centre Station and Rocky Point Park, taking in the Newport and Suter Brook village areas by Inlet Centre Station; exact board sub-area boundary roads are UNVERIFIED. https://www.portmoody.ca/en/city-hall/resources/Documents/OCP-neighbourhood-plan-areas_-_edited_for_EngagePM_website.pdf
- Moody Centre Station combines Millennium Line SkyTrain (Evergreen Extension), West Coast Express commuter rail and a bus loop; Inlet Centre Station is the next SkyTrain station east. https://maps.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/system-maps/fwc26_regional_transit_connections.pdf
- Rocky Point Park, at the head of the inlet, has a pier, boat launch, outdoor pool, skate park and playground and is the start of the Shoreline Trail. https://engage.portmoody.ca/rocky-point-and-old-orchard-parks-master-plans/news_feed/background-rocky-point-today
- The Port Moody Recreation Complex is at 300 Ioco Road, Kyle Centre at 125 Kyle Street, and Port Moody Public Library at 100 Newport Drive. https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/recreation/recreation-facilities-and-locations/
- School District 43 catchments are address-based; central Port Moody schools include Moody Elementary, Moody Middle and Port Moody Secondary at 300 Albert Street, and a locator run for 100 Newport Drive returned Mountain Meadows Elementary, Scott Creek Middle and Gleneagle Secondary — catchments here do not follow the neighbourhood lines. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- Where 55+ and seniors housing actually concentrates: there is no 55+ strata cluster. The seniors buildings are scattered through the centre — Hope Street by Kyle Centre (Legion Kinsmen Manor), Noons Creek Drive at the Inlet Centre building (seniors housing, hospice and The Club seniors centre at one address), St. Johns Street (Moody's Landing, Housing Registry), and Guildford Way at the hospital (Eagle Ridge Manor long-term care). Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Eagle Ridge Hospital, the Tri-Cities hospital, is at 475 Guildford Way in this sub-area. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**This area has ZERO verified 55+ ownership stock. No age-restricted (55+) strata or co-op ownership complex was verified in Port Moody Centre in any source checked. Everything below is rental, care or non-profit seniors housing — none of it is buyable ownership.**

### Retirement residences and care (rental/care — NOT strata ownership)

- **Eagle Ridge Manor — 475 Guildford Way.** A 65-bed long-term care facility operated by Fraser Health at the Eagle Ridge Hospital site, with two residential units (Hillside and Forestview). Care housing — you cannot buy in. SOURCE CONFLICT FLAGGED: a secondary care-directory summary states 75 beds for the same facility; Fraser Health's own location page states 65 beds, and the Fraser Health figure is the one used here. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-manor
- **Crossroads Inlet Centre Hospice — 101 Noons Creek Dr.** A 10-bed hospice operated by Crossroads Hospice Society in partnership with Fraser Health, in the same building as the seniors housing below. - **The Club — 101 Noons Creek Dr.** Not housing: a drop-in gathering space and café for adults 50+ run by the Port Moody Seniors Friendship Society, opened October 2016 in what was the dining room of the Alex Graham Manor seniors residence in the same building, with City and Metro Vancouver support. https://www.portmoody.ca/city-government/awards-and-recognition/freedom-of-the-city-award/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed; live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw 2026, No. 3522** (Port Moody 2050), Chapter 14, ss. 14.5–14.6, covers Inlet Centre, Coronation Park and Moody Centre, including the Moody Centre Station Transit-Oriented Area; its housing policies include encouraging "seniors focused housing opportunities in transit-oriented areas that are adaptable and affordable". https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733 and https://www.portmoody.ca/media/tk1n0rrw/chapter-7-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670726
- **Zoning: Port Moody Zoning Bylaw No. 2937** puts single detached homes in RS zones, multi-residential in RT and RM zones and mixed-use development in TC and CRM zones; the transit-oriented areas are exempt from small-scale multi-unit housing rules. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/
- **Heritage:** Moody Centre is a heritage conservation area (with the Ioco townsite), so a heritage alteration permit can be required for new construction or reconstruction on included properties — relevant to the older detached stock around Clarke and Murray Streets. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** for this area (Zone 4 directory, May 2025), which is how the Inlet Centre and Moody's Landing units are filled. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ condo I can actually buy in central Port Moody?** No verified one. No age-restricted strata ownership building was found in Moody Centre, Inlet Centre, Newport or Suter Brook in any source checked; the centre's seniors stock is non-profit rental (Hope St, Noons Creek Dr, St. Johns St) and care (Guildford Way). The nearest verified 55+ strata ownership is Mayfair Terrace in Port Coquitlam. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Note the local rental version of the same idea: BC Housing's directory marks both Inlet Centre and Moody's Landing "C" — couples under 55 accepted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. A parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a condo here, and does age matter for tenants?** In a general-market Port Moody Centre strata, long-term rental bans are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022. In a 55+ strata (elsewhere), the tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is "50 Electronic" at Klahanie a 55+ building? The name sounds like it.** No. Listings for 55 Klahanie Drive describe a 2023 general-market building with a children's area among its amenities and no age restriction; "Club 50" is the amenity brand. An age bylaw can only be verified in the strata's own bylaws/Form B, and no source states one here. https://www.zolo.ca/port-moody-real-estate/55-klahanie-drive/405
- **Q: Where is the hospital and the long-term care home?** Both are at 475 Guildford Way: Eagle Ridge Hospital (Fraser Health community hospital, 24/7 emergency) and Eagle Ridge Manor, its 65-bed long-term care facility with the Hillside and Forestview units. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-manor
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day?** Two SkyTrain stations serve the sub-area — Moody Centre (also West Coast Express and a bus loop) and Inlet Centre — so most centre buildings are a walk or one bus from the Millennium Line. https://maps.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/system-maps/fwc26_regional_transit_connections.pdf
- **Q: How does subsidized seniors rental work here?** Through BC Housing's Housing Registry: the Zone 4 directory (May 2025) lists Inlet Centre (101 Noons Creek Dr) and Moody's Landing (2330 St. Johns St) as Section 1 developments — apply to the Registry, not the building. Legion Kinsmen Manor (2909 Hope St) is applied to directly through the Port Moody Senior Housing Society and is market rent only. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: If I need more support later, can I stay in Port Moody?** The in-city ladder on the sources checked is short: independent non-profit seniors rental (three buildings above), then long-term care at Eagle Ridge Manor (65 beds) and hospice care at Crossroads Inlet Centre Hospice (10 beds). The City itself states Port Moody has 75 long-term care and assisted living beds through Fraser Health and that all other seniors housing in the community is independent living — there is no registered assisted-living residence in Port Moody in the Registry results checked. https://wWW.ubcm.ca/sites/default/files/2026-01/Port%20Moody%20Questionnaire.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **One address on Noons Creek Drive holds three different seniors services.** 101 Noons Creek Drive is simultaneously the Housing Registry seniors residence (directory name "Inlet Centre"; residence name Alex Graham Manor), the 10-bed Crossroads hospice, and The Club, the 50+ drop-in café run by the Port Moody Seniors Friendship Society out of the residence's former dining room. A buyer reading only one source will mislabel the building. https://www.crossroadshospice.org/hospice-care/covid-19 and https://www.portmoody.ca/city-government/awards-and-recognition/freedom-of-the-city-award/
- **The City has put its seniors-housing shortage in writing.** Port Moody's Canada Community-Building Fund questionnaire states senior households on the BC Housing registry increased from 21 in 2016 to 62 in 2024, and that concerns about insufficient independent, assisted, semi-supportive and supportive housing were raised in the 2020 Housing Needs Report process. https://wWW.ubcm.ca/sites/default/files/2026-01/Port%20Moody%20Questionnaire.pdf
- **The big new supply near Inlet Centre Station is general-market, not seniors.** Wesgroup's Inlet District, on the former Coronation Park lands, is planned as six towers of 26–31 storeys and three 6-storey buildings with 2,486 market condos and 101 market rentals, with first residents expected around 2029 — no 55+ component is stated in the project reporting. (Developer project details as reported: https://canada.constructconnect.com/joc/news/projects/2024/01/wesgroup-applies-for-development-permit-for-master-planned-port-moody-community)
- **Kyle Centre, not the big Recreation Complex, is the older-adult centre.** Kyle Centre at 125 Kyle Street — a short walk from Legion Kinsmen Manor on Hope Street — serves the leisure needs of the older adult, per the Tri-Cities seniors resource listings; the main Recreation Complex is at 300 Ioco Road. https://vcn.bc.ca/srsinfo/seniors-olderadults.html
- **Port Moody bills utilities separately from taxes.** Property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026 (5% penalty after July 2, a further 5% after September 15, 2026); water, sewer and waste are a separate bill due in February (2026: February 27). Downsizers comparing strata fees plus taxes across Tri-Cities cities need both bills. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/property-taxes-and-utility-fees/property-taxes/
- **Heritage rules touch the cheapest detached stock.** The older houses around St. Johns, Clarke and Murray Streets sit in the Moody Centre heritage conservation area, where a heritage alteration permit can be required for construction or reconstruction — a cost and timeline item that does not appear in listing text. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/
