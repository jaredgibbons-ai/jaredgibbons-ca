## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **The rule that actually governs Bradner downsizing is the ALR rule, not strata law:** residences in the Agricultural Land Reserve cannot be stratified (cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately), so a Bradner farm home can never become a strata unit. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **ALR housing caps:** on ALR land, one principal residence per parcel is permitted, with a maximum gross floor area of 500 m²; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; an additional residence for farm use or another permitted use is capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **City zoning: Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014** — Bradner lands are zoned A1 and A2 Agricultural. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/bylaws/zoning-bylaw
- **City plan:** the Abbotsford Official Community Plan (Abbotsforward 2050, Bylaw No. 2600-2016, as updated) applies to Bradner; feedback from Bradner and other rural villages was gathered when the OCP was made, and there is no Bradner neighbourhood plan or seniors-housing designation for the community. https://www.abbotsford.ca/planning-development/development/official-community-plan and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- **No Bradner-specific tax break:** the City offers a Revitalization Tax Exemption for eligible properties within designated revitalization areas; no Bradner-specific exemption was confirmed in the material read. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/tax-incentives-exemptions

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ complex in Bradner itself?** No. No age-restricted strata, 55+ park, seniors rental or care residence with a Bradner address was verified in any source checked, and the sub-area recorded zero attached (condo/townhome) listings in September 2026. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report; https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/bylaws/zoning-bylaw
- **Q: Why not — is it just that nobody has built one yet?** It is structural: Bradner is zoned A1/A2 Agricultural and partly in the ALR, and ALR residences cannot be stratified — the legal form every 55+ ownership complex uses is not available on farm land here. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Where is the nearest actual 55+ ownership stock?** In Abbotsford West / Central Abbotsford — e.g. Evergreen Village (31955 Old Yale Rd, 55+, built 1995, 205 units) and Carriage Lane (32691 Garibaldi Dr, 55+, strata plan NWS2539) — east across the city from Bradner, not in the community. https://www.rodfriesen.com/abbotsford-condos/31955-old-yale-rd-evergreen-village-condos and https://www.estateblock.com/abbotsford/220-32691-garibaldi-dr-34506
- **Q: Can I downsize on my own Bradner farm instead?** Within the ALC caps: keep the principal residence (max 500 m²), put a suite only inside that residence, and add one small additional residence for farm use — 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, 186 m² over 40 ha. None of it can be stratified or sold separately. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata (in the city complexes)?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I get by without a car in Bradner?** No source supports that: no transit route through Bradner was verified on the City or BC Transit sources checked, and there is no grocery store inside the community on any City source read. https://www.abbotsford.ca/search?search_api_fulltext=transit
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Abbotsford Regional Hospital and Cancer Centre, 32900 Marshall Road (24-hour emergency, surgery and maternity); the nearest urgent and primary care centre is at 100-2692 Clearbrook Road, open 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. seven days a week — both Fraser Health services, across the city from Bradner. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Abbotsford/abbotsford-regional-hospital-and-cancer-centre
- **Q: If I need care later, is there anything in Bradner?** No care or assisted-living residence in Bradner appears in BC's Assisted Living Registry; the registered Abbotsford residences (Bevan Village, Hallmark on the Park, Menno, Tabor Court) are all at central/east Abbotsford addresses. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?address=&f%5B0%5D=city%3AAbbotsford&f%5B1%5D=city%3APort%20Alberni&f%5B2%5D=city%3AQuesnel&f%5B3%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&name=&order=alr_title&sort=asc

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Bradner's community infrastructure is a hall and a sports park, not a seniors centre:** Bradner Hall (5305 Bradner Rd) and Grant Park (7399 Bradner Rd) are the named City facilities in the community; the City's 55+ activity programming sits at the Abbotsford Recreation Centre / Matsqui Recreation Centre side of the city (the ARC/MRC 55+ Activity Centre is listed in the Abbotsford seniors service directory at the Clearbrook Road recreation complex). https://www.abbotsford.ca/parks-recreation-culture/parks-trails/where-go/parks and https://archway.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Seniors-Booklet-2026.pdf
- **The 90 m² figure is the one that surprises Bradner sellers:** buyers routinely price a parcel as if a full second house can be added; on ALR land the additional residence is capped at 90 m² on any parcel of 40 ha or less, and it must be for farm use or another permitted use. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **A suite in the barn is not allowed here:** under the ALC housing rules a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence — a detached building is the "additional residence," with its own size cap and use test. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **School catchments are address-based even out here:** the locator run for Bradner Elementary's own address (5396 Bradner Rd) returns Eugene Reimer Middle — the district's boundary-maps page named a different middle school (W.A. Fraser) for the same ground, and the locator governs. Run any specific address before relying on a school. https://eugenereimer.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/ermsfamilyofschools/eugenereimer
- **City taxes have two penalty steps:** City of Abbotsford property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026, each including any unclaimed Home Owner Grant. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
