# Abbotsford East — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Abbotsford East" is the board's sub-area name; it is not a single City of Abbotsford neighbourhood name. The City plans the east side through named areas — McMillan, Old Clayburn, East Townline, Whatcom and McKee — and the mountainside is planned separately through the McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023), which forms Schedule E of the Official Community Plan. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- Rough extent: Highway 1 to the south, the Sumas Mountain slopes and McKee Road area to the east and northeast, Old Yale Road and Old Clayburn Road through the middle, and the McMillan / Clearbrook side to the west; no exact board boundary is published by the City. Central Abbotsford and Abbotsford West lie to the west, Sumas Mountain to the east, Sumas Prairie to the southeast and Mission across the Fraser River to the north. https://www.abbotsford.ca/parks-playgrounds
- This is the city's detached-house side: 165 detached listings versus 69 attached listings in September 2026 — the largest detached selection of any Abbotsford sub-area, and the thinnest attached one among the urban sub-areas (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report). That housing mix is exactly why the 55+ ownership shelf is empty here (Section 2).
- The one 55+ address in the sub-area, Cascade Court, sits on Delair Road in the McMillan-side flats at the sub-area's west edge — the part of Abbotsford East closest to the Clearbrook/Mill Lake corridor where the city's 55+ stock concentrates. Sources: Section 2 below, and the Abbotsford West and Central Abbotsford files.
- Health: the city's hospital, Abbotsford Regional Hospital and Cancer Centre (32900 Marshall Rd), and the Urgent and Primary Care Centre (2692 Clearbrook Rd #100, open 7 days, 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m.) are both west of this sub-area, in or beside Central Abbotsford. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Abbotsford/abbotsford-regional-hospital-and-cancer-centre
- Transit: BC Transit Central Fraser Valley routes on the east side include 2 (Highstreet / McMillan), 7 (Sumas Mountain), 9 (McKee), 15 (Auguston) and 16 (McMillan), plus the 66 Fraser Valley Express on Highway 1; there is no SkyTrain or West Coast Express station in Abbotsford. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=7
- Schools are SD34, with eight schools physically on the east side (Sandy Hill, Mountain, Irene Kelleher Totí:ltawtxw and Auguston Traditional elementaries; Dr. Thomas A. Swift Elementary; Clayburn Middle; Robert Bateman and Yale secondaries); locator runs for two east-side addresses both return Clayburn Middle → Robert Bateman Secondary (SD34 School Locator, run 2026-11-01). https://sandyhill.abbyschools.ca/

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**There is no verified 55+ strata or freehold ownership stock in Abbotsford East.** No public source checked — building directories, MLS listing data, the Abbotsford & Mission Seniors Directory, or the base area guide — records an age-restricted strata or freehold complex in this sub-area. The sub-area's only verified 55+ housing is a life lease residence (below). Abbotsford's verified 55+ strata stock is concentrated in Abbotsford West (15 complexes) and Central Abbotsford (9 complexes), with two more in Poplar — see those files.

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- None. See the statement above.

### Life lease (occupancy right — NOT strata ownership)

- **Cascade Court — 35230 Delair Rd.** A 55+ life lease residence operated as a division of the Salvation Army, per listing data: "CASCADE COURT — 55+, life lease… Cascade Court is a div. of the Salvation Army." A building directory records the building as built in 1998 with age restrictions among its bylaws, and the Abbotsford & Mission Seniors Directory (Spring/Summer 2026) lists it as "Active over 55 living"; a seniors-housing roundup describes 85 private suites on a life lease model. Under a life lease you buy a lifetime right to occupy, not a strata lot — there is no unit to resell on the open market, and the exit terms are whatever the lease document says. Amenities are described on the operator's own site; unit count beyond the roundup figure and current availability: UNCONFIRMED. https://rennie.com/listings/delair-35230-road-cascade-court-118-abbotsford and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/cascade-court-35230-delair-ave and https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/ABB/260226/ABBNZ260226.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Why there is no "45+" anything anymore:** Bill 44 amended the Strata Property Act (ss. 123.1/123.2) effective Nov 24, 2022 so that 55+ is the only age a BC strata bylaw may set; every 19+, 40+ or 45+ bylaw became invalid that day. That is the legal reason the 2015 east-side advertisement in Section 2 proves nothing today — and the rule that governs any 55+ purchase an east-side seller makes across town in Abbotsford West. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **If you do buy 55+ strata elsewhere in Abbotsford:** the bylaw restricts who may live in the unit, not who may own it (an under-55 owner is allowed; residing may not be), and Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — since May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rentals in a 55+ strata:** strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term rental bylaws still allowed), and a tenant must still meet the age bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Life lease is a contract, not a tenure the Strata Property Act governs.** Cascade Court's model is the standard BC one described by the Clearbrook Golden Age Society for its own building: an up-front capital payment buys lifetime occupancy, monthly maintenance charges apply, and on departure the operator redeems the lease under its terms; the Society's plain warning applies to any life lease — the models vary widely, are not well understood, and professional advice should be taken before signing. There is no Form B, no depreciation report and no strata council. https://gardenparktower.ca/life-lease
- **Aging in place in an east-side house — the suite route:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014, s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings; City council reports state the criteria include a lot of at least 400 m², at least 12.0 m frontage, a Collector or Local road frontage, not on a cul-de-sac bulb, and — specific to this part of town — not in a bare land strata unless the road infrastructure meets City bylaw standards. A legal suite is the common east-side arrangement for a caregiver or adult child. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **Mountainside rules:** the McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023) guides the slope neighbourhoods, the OCP applies a Steep Slope Development Permit Area to slope land, and Clayburn Village is protected through a Heritage Conservation Area in the OCP — exterior changes there need heritage review, which matters to a senior planning accessibility renovations. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea and https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- **Floodplain at the south edge:** the low land toward Sumas Prairie is floodplain land by the Sumas River; City flood notices have named properties south of the Sumas River and along Whatcom Road in evacuation order and alert areas. A lot-level check in the City WebMap is part of due diligence for any low east-side lot. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/evacuation-orders-modified-additional-properties-sumas-prairie
- **Manufactured-home parks:** none verified in this sub-area, so the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act has no east-side application in these notes; the Act's pad-rent rules (one increase per 12 months, three full months' notice, 2026 cap 2.3% plus the proportional levy/utility amount) are set out in the Abbotsford West, Central Abbotsford and Poplar files where the parks actually are. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- **Property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there really no 55+ condo or townhouse to buy in Abbotsford East?** Correct, on every source checked: no age-restricted strata or freehold complex is verified in this sub-area. The buyable 55+ stock is across the city — 15 verified strata complexes in Abbotsford West, 9 in Central Abbotsford and 2 in Poplar (see those files). The east side's attached supply is small to begin with (69 attached listings in September 2026) and none of it is verified age-restricted product. Sources: Section 2 and SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: What is Cascade Court, then — can I buy a suite there?** Not in the strata sense. Cascade Court (35230 Delair Rd) is a 55+ life lease residence run as a division of the Salvation Army: you pay for a lifetime right to occupy a suite, and when you leave, the lease's redemption terms — not an open-market sale — decide what comes back. Read the lease document itself before comparing it to a condo price. https://rennie.com/listings/delair-35230-road-cascade-court-118-abbotsford and https://gardenparktower.ca/life-lease
- **Q: I saw an ad years ago for a 45+ building on Trethewey Street. Does that still exist?** UNCONFIRMED as a current 55+ complex. The only trace found is a 2015 advertisement using a 45+ restriction, and 45+ bylaws have been unenforceable in BC since Nov 24, 2022 — a building that only ever had a 45+ bylaw is legally an unrestricted building today unless it amended to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: If I sell my east-side house and buy 55+ in Clearbrook, can my 53-year-old spouse live with me?** Yes — Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption; the same regulation covers live-in caregivers and the defined children cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I just stay in my east-side house and bring in a caregiver?** The bylaw route most owners use is a secondary suite: one is permitted in all single detached dwellings under Zoning Bylaw s.140.3.8, subject to the City-stated criteria (400 m² minimum lot, 12.0 m frontage, Collector or Local road, not a cul-de-sac bulb). Strata age law is irrelevant to a freehold house — there is no age bylaw on a detached lot. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **Q: How far is the hospital from the east side?** Abbotsford Regional Hospital and Cancer Centre is at 32900 Marshall Rd, west of this sub-area in the central area; the Urgent and Primary Care Centre (2692 Clearbrook Rd #100) is open 7 days a week, 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m. No official source publishes drive times — UNVERIFIED. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Abbotsford/abbotsford-regional-hospital-and-cancer-centre
- **Q: Is there any 55+ rental or care home on the east side if I don't want to buy?** None verified. The Assisted Living Registry and the Seniors Directory show the city's care and subsidized stock in Central Abbotsford and Abbotsford West (Menno Place, Tabor Village, the Marshall Road cluster); nothing in the sources checked places a care residence inside Abbotsford East. Sources: this file, Section 2, and the Central/West files.
- **Q: What transit would I use from Auguston or Sandy Hill?** BC Transit routes 15 (Auguston) and 7 (Sumas Mountain) serve the mountainside neighbourhoods, route 2 serves McMillan, and the 66 Fraser Valley Express runs Highway 1 toward Langley/Lougheed Station and Chilliwack. There is no SkyTrain or West Coast Express in Abbotsford. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=15
- **Q: Does the floodplain affect east-side retirement plans?** Only at the low south edge: land toward Sumas Prairie is floodplain by the Sumas River, and City notices have put properties south of the Sumas River and along Whatcom Road under evacuation orders and alerts. The hillside neighbourhoods are a different risk profile — but the check is lot-by-lot in the City WebMap, not by neighbourhood name. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/evacuation-orders-modified-additional-properties-sumas-prairie

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The empty 55+ shelf is a housing-mix fact, not a search failure.** The base guide could not confirm a single named strata condo building or townhome complex in this sub-area on an official source; the east side is detached houses on RS3/RS5/RS6 lots, including compact-lot RS6 subdivisions. Age-restricted developers build where attached stock already clusters — Clearbrook and Mill Lake — which is why the verified 55+ map of Abbotsford is a west-side map. (Base guide, Sections 5–6; SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.)
- **The sub-area's only 55+ address sits at its far west edge.** Cascade Court is on Delair Road by the McMillan side — DeLair Park is at 35570 Old Yale Road in the same pocket — minutes from the Central Abbotsford line rather than from Auguston or Eagle Mountain. A buyer set on both "east side" and "55+" is really buying the McMillan edge. https://www.abbotsford.ca/parks-playgrounds
- **Two life lease buildings bookend the city, run by charities.** Cascade Court here (Salvation Army) and Garden Park Tower in Abbotsford West (Clearbrook Golden Age Society, 111 suites, tower completed 1997) are Abbotsford's two verified life lease seniors residences — neither appears in MLS strata searches, which is why 55+ buyers who search only strata listings never see them. https://gardenparktower.ca/history and https://rennie.com/listings/delair-35230-road-cascade-court-118-abbotsford
- **The mountainside is still being planned, and the plan is new.** The McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023) is Schedule E of the OCP, with an open-space network that includes geotechnical areas and slopes over 40% — a senior buying a newer McKee home for a "forever" purchase should read the plan's ground-oriented housing areas and the Steep Slope Development Permit Area rules before assuming future accessibility additions are simple. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- **Clayburn Village's charm comes with a rulebook.** The village at the west foot of Sumas Mountain is protected through a Heritage Conservation Area in the OCP, so exterior alterations — including the ramps, railings and door widenings seniors often plan — run through heritage review. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- **No library branch on the east side.** No Fraser Valley Regional Library branch inside the sub-area was confirmed on an official source; the Clearbrook Library at 32320 George Ferguson Way is the branch the base guide names for east-side residents — one more daily-life anchor pulling east-side seniors' routines west. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/
- **Recreation, at least, is local.** The Abbotsford Recreation Centre is at 2499 McMillan Road at the sub-area's west edge — the one seniors-serving civic facility actually inside Abbotsford East's orbit — and the park system includes Bateman Park, Eagle Mountain Park and McMillan Park. https://www.abbotsford.ca/parks-recreation-culture/facilities-hours and https://www.abbotsford.ca/parks-playgrounds
