## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. Otter District has no verified strata stock, so this rule matters here only for the neighbouring complexes a buyer widens to. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **A park's 55+ rule is a park rule, not a strata bylaw.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003) recognises a park where every site is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 and allows a park landlord to refuse an assignment where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement. This is the rule behind the Brookswood parks in Section 2 — and Langley Grove Estates in this district has no such rule in its listings. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Pad-rent increases (MHPTA):** a park landlord may increase rent only with 3 full months' written notice, only once every 12 months, and only up to the yearly limit — 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026 (2.2% plus the proportional amount for 2027). The proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park. On assignment (MHPTA s. 28), the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same. This governs any Langley Grove Estates purchase even though that park is not 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Township zoning: Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500** governs the district; the listings page's own cards name RU-2 zoning for acreage parcels, and zone assignments for a specific parcel come from the bylaw Map Schedules through the Township's GeoSource. https://www.tol.ca/at-your-service/bylaw-enforcement/bylaws/
- **The Township's bylaw has its own "seniors' housing" definition:** a Township council report varying Zoning Bylaw 2500 for a seniors' housing project records the bylaw definition's maximum seniors' housing dwelling-unit size as 70 m² (753 ft²) — a zoning term, separate from any 55+ age bylaw or park rule. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=866EA2E9-916E-485E-9075-F59C90961D19&folderId=47498C260508154601958591
- **ALR housing frame:** on ALR land the principal residence maximum is a total floor area of 500 m²; a secondary suite is permitted only when entirely contained within the principal residence; and one additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application at up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, subject to local bylaws. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Added units outside the ALR frame:** the Township's SSMUH page states eligibility requires the property to be within the Urban Containment Boundary, connected to both municipal water and sewer, not larger than 4,050 m² and not in a zone with a minimum lot size larger than 4,050 m²; properties that do not meet those criteria are instead eligible for one residential unit plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit, depending on zoning — the frame most rural Otter acreages fall under. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Coach house definition:** in Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102, a "coach house" is an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/otter-district.md
- **Property taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes and utility fees were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to current-year taxes unpaid at midnight July 2, 2026, with a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026, and utility fees are now billed separately. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Otter District?** None verified inside the district — no 55+ strata, park, co-op, rental or care product with an Otter District address was found in any source checked. The nearest verified 55+ ownership options are Pioneer Park (co-operative manufactured homes, 27111 0 Ave, Aldergrove), the three Brookswood 55+ parks (Cedar Creek Estates, Fernridge Park, Pineridge) and Creekside Villa strata townhouses in Aldergrove (27435 29A Ave). Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Langley Grove Estates a 55+ park? It is in Otter District and has a clubhouse.** No. Listings for the park state "no age restrictions" — it is an all-ages manufactured-home community at 24330 Fraser Hwy / 3665 244 St. The clubhouse and other amenities in its listing text do not make it a seniors park. https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/24330-fraser-highway/52
- **Q: If I buy an Otter District acreage at 55+, is there any age rule on it?** No — a freehold acreage carries no age restriction; age rules in Langley attach to strata bylaws, park rules and co-op rules, none of which apply to a rural freehold parcel. What does govern an acreage is zoning (RU-2 is named in listings) and, where it applies, the ALR housing frame. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I build a second home on an Otter acreage for family or a caregiver?** Within the ALC frame on ALR land: one additional detached residence without an ALC application at up to 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha), provided Township bylaws allow it, and a suite only inside the principal residence. Outside the ALR, Township SSMUH eligibility is narrow (Urban Containment Boundary, municipal water and sewer, lot no larger than 4,050 m²); most rural Otter lots fall to one unit plus a suite or accessory unit depending on zoning. Confirm the parcel's ALR status on the ALC maps first. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in the nearest 55+ options?** It depends on the tenure. At a strata like Creekside Villa, provincial rules exempt a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement. At Pioneer Park, one listing states both occupants must be 55 — a co-op sets its own occupancy rules, and the strata exemptions do not apply. At the Brookswood parks, ask the park for its rule in writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://murphyrealestategroup.ca/recip.html/1272399.search/SearchResults.form?_pg=3
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up at Langley Grove Estates?** Up to the Residential Tenancy Branch limit for manufactured home site tenancies: 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026 (2.2% plus the proportional amount for 2027), on 3 full months' notice, once every 12 months — and on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same. The park's current pad rent itself is UNVERIFIED from any park-operator source. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: What happens if a park is converted to another use?** Under MHPTA s. 42 the landlord must first have all necessary permits and approvals, then give 12 months' notice and pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date, with defined additional-compensation routes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Langley Memorial Hospital, 22051 Fraser Highway — a Fraser Health community hospital with 24/7 emergency. There is no hospital inside Otter District. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Langley/langley-memorial-hospital
- **Q: What well and septic checks matter on an Otter acreage?** Rural properties may use wells — search GWELLS, the provincial database of registered wells and mapped aquifers, for a parcel's record — and Fraser Health requires an authorized person to file a record of sewerage system before construction, alteration or repair of an onsite system, and a letter of certification after construction. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/understanding-aquifers and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/resources-for-onsite-sewerage-systems-property-owners-and-authorized-persons
- **Q: How thin is this market?** On the board's published figures for September 2026, Otter District recorded 2 detached listings with 0 sales and 0 Condos & Townhomes listings with 0 sales — the thinnest shelf in the Langley set, with no sub-area median published (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report). ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/otter-district.md

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The listings page mixes two different markets under one sub-area label.** The Otter District page's own results combine multi-million-dollar freehold acreages with manufactured homes in Langley Grove Estates on pad rent — different tenure, lending and resale markets sharing a label — while the board snapshot for the same sub-area counts only 2 detached and 0 attached listings for September 2026. Neither count describes the other. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/otter-district.md
- **The district's park looks like seniors product and is not.** Langley Grove Estates has the clubhouse-and-amenities presentation buyers associate with 55+ parks, but its listings state no age restrictions — a buyer who assumes an age-restricted community from the presentation alone would be wrong. https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/24330-fraser-highway/52
- **The nearest 55+ co-op has the strictest occupancy wording in the Langley files.** Pioneer Park listings state both occupants must be 55 — stricter than the provincial strata exemptions a buyer may expect from condo experience — and the co-op, not strata law, sets that rule. https://murphyrealestategroup.ca/recip.html/1272399.search/SearchResults.form?_pg=3
- **Otter and Salmon River do not trade alike.** Salmon River, immediately north, recorded 22 detached listings and 5 sales (23%) in September 2026 against Otter District's 2 and 0 — the two rural districts are neighbours, not comparables by default (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report). ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/otter-district.md
- **A "coach house" here has a narrow legal meaning.** Under Township Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102, a coach house sits only within the second storey of a detached garage — a ground-floor detached suite on an Otter acreage is a different accessory unit with different rules, whatever a listing calls it. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/otter-district.md
- **The Township's "seniors' housing" zoning term is not an age promise.** Bylaw 2500 defines seniors' housing with a maximum unit size (70 m² in the definition, per a Township council report) — a property described with that term is a zoning description, not evidence of a 55+ community. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=866EA2E9-916E-485E-9075-F59C90961D19&folderId=47498C260508154601958591
