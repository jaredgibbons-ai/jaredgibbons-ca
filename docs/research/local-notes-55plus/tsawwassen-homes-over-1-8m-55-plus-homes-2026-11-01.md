## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) at this band, as published by the Province:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000; 2% of the portion greater than $200,000 and up to and including $2,000,000; 3% of the portion greater than $2,000,000 — every purchase in this band reaches the 3% tier on its top portion. If a property has residential property worth over $3,000,000, a further 2% tax applies to the residential property value greater than $3,000,000; on a mixed-class property the further 2% applies only to the residential portion. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Neither BC purchase exemption reaches this band:** the first time home buyers' program full exemption caps at a fair market value of $835,000 and the newly built home exemption's full threshold is $1,100,000 with elimination at $1,150,000 — both far below this page's floor, so buyers in this band should budget full PTT. (No FHSA or Home Buyers' Plan dollar limit is stated in this file — UNVERIFIED, not read off a live CRA page in this research.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Home owner grant phase-out at this band:** the Province states the grant threshold for 2026 is $2,075,000 — a full regular grant at or below that assessed (or partitioned) value, reduced by $5 for each $1,000 of assessed value over $2,075,000, and $0 for the regular grant if the value is over $2,189,000 — so many homes in this band will receive a reduced grant or none. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017.** Its residential zones include RS1, RS2 and RS3 at a minimum lot size for subdivision of 4,000 m²; RS4 at 610 m²; RS5 at 550 m² under the Single Family Residential designation or 460 m² otherwise; RS6 at 390 m²; RS7 at 335 m²; RD1 at 836 m²; RD2 and RD3 at 4,000 m²; and RD4 at 504 m²; there is no RD5 zone, and zoning is parcel-by-parcel on the City zoning maps. **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024** governs building in Tsawwassen. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta secondary suites and added units:** Delta's 2023 amendment to Bylaw No. 7600 (Bylaw No. 8242) removed the minimum and maximum floor area requirements for secondary suites and set the suite floor area at less than 50% of the gross floor area of the single detached dwelling (excluding an attached garage); Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing rules allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide, subject to the bylaw and to any hazard or streamside permit area on the lot. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/218413/ and http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Hazard Development Permit Areas in this band's stock:** under OCP Bylaw No. 8400, the hazard DPAs in Tsawwassen are SD2 English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment) and SD3 Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands, where SD3 provides a 2.9 m Geodetic Survey of Canada datum floor level, a 2.3 m Geodetic Survey of Canada datum minimum land surface elevation and a 7.5 m tidal setback. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Flood protection:** the City of Delta is the diking authority and operates and maintains over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure — flood construction and insurance questions are address-specific for any low-lying listing in this band. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **ALR edges:** farm land on the Tsawwassen edges is designated Agriculture (A) in the OCP (Bylaw No. 8400, 2024, s. 1.5.1), and Agricultural Land Reserve rules apply where land is in the ALR; ALR status is parcel-specific and is checked on the Agricultural Land Commission's maps. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Property taxes and 55+ relief:** Delta's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. The provincial regular property tax deferment loan program covers homeowners 55 and older, and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) covers seniors 65+ — separate from the regular-grant phase-out above. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is any of Tsawwassen's 55+ ownership stock in this over-$1.8M band?** No source states that. The verified 55+ ownership stock is two low-rise condo buildings — The Lexington (38 units, built 1988) and Tsawwassen Greene (27 units, built 1991) — and this file makes no claim that either is priced over the band. What the band typically means in home-type terms is UNVERIFIED — no official source documents it. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/the-lexington-1150-54a-street and https://jaredgibbons.ca/tsawwassen-homes-over-1-8m.html
- **Q: How many Tsawwassen homes are for sale over $1.8M right now?** That figure cannot be stated from the sources used here. The market reports held for this program publish no price-band cut and do not cover South Delta at all, and no official source publishes a count or share for the $1.8M band; the live page's own feed is the only place the current band count can be seen, and it changes as listings come and go. https://jaredgibbons.ca/tsawwassen-homes-over-1-8m.html
- **Q: Is there extra property transfer tax on a home over $3 million?** Yes. The Province applies a further 2% tax to the residential property value greater than $3,000,000, on top of the general tiers (1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000); on a mixed-class property the further 2% applies only to the residential portion. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Does the first-time buyer exemption or the new-home exemption help at $1.8M?** No. The first time home buyers' program full exemption caps at a fair market value of $835,000, and the newly built home exemption is fully eliminated at $1,150,000 — both far below this band, so a buyer here budgets full property transfer tax. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: Will I still get the home owner grant on a home in this band?** Possibly a reduced one, or none. For 2026 the grant threshold is $2,075,000: full regular grant at or below that value, reduced by $5 per $1,000 over it, and $0 for the regular grant over $2,189,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a home in this band, or a 55+ unit, long-term?** In a BC strata, long-term rental bans no longer exist — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022; a tenant in a 55+ building still has to satisfy the age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. A freehold detached house is not under a strata rental bylaw at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Q: What should I check on a bluff or waterfront home in this band?** Which hazard Development Permit Area applies: SD2 English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment) or SD3 Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands, where SD3 sets a 2.9 m floor level, a 2.3 m minimum land surface elevation and a 7.5 m tidal setback; and, on farm-edge lots, the parcel's ALR status on the Agricultural Land Commission's maps. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Q: Can a 55+ buyer defer property taxes on a home in this band?** The provincial regular deferment program is open to homeowners 55 and older on their principal residence (a low-interest provincial loan; surviving spouses and persons with disabilities also qualify), and the City's tax page links the program. Confirm eligibility and current terms with the Province before relying on it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Q: Where is the care ladder if I buy in this band and later need help?** In Central: independent/assisted rental at the KinVillage campus (including 68 publicly subsidized assisted-living suites) and The Waterford (106 independent suites, 36 licensed care suites), The Wexford's 65 independent living suites on 56 Street, then KinVillage West Court's 100 publicly subsidized long-term care beds. All of it is rental or care — none of it is in this price band, because none of it is for sale. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/7/B/assisted-living-at-residence-at-kinvillage and https://briacommunities.ca/communities/waterford/
