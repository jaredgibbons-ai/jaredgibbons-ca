## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. This is the rule that makes Fleetwood Gate's unspecified "age restrictions" directory line unusable on its own. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. That is why the "rentals not allowed" directory lines for both Fleetwood Gardens and Fleetwood Gate cannot be read at face value. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000** governs Fleetwood Tynehead; the July 8, 2024 SSMUH changes renamed the former RF / RF-SS zones to R3, RF-12 / RF-12C / RF-13 to R4, and RF-SD to R6 — feed cards here quoting R3 are using a current zone name. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Plans:** the current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — never cite the replaced Bylaw No. 18020 as current. The Fleetwood Plan's Stage 1 was endorsed March 7, 2022 and updated October 16, 2023, with further Transit Oriented Areas alignment changes since and Council consideration of the updated plan in 2026 — a lot's future designation near the two coming stations is a moving target until then. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/fleetwood-land-use-plans/fleetwood-town-centre-plan
- **Secondary suites and SSMUH:** one secondary suite is permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, not inside a coach house or garden suite, capped at 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area; Surrey allows 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 on larger lots, and up to 6 on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Manufactured-home parks carry a City policy layer on top of the provincial Act** (this is Westwood Court's frame, not Fleetwood Gardens'): the City's Manufactured Home Park Policy brochure tells park residents the property owner must give 12 months' notice of a final moving date, can only give that notice after receiving all permits and approvals, and must pay $20,000 on or before the move-out date. Surrey's Tenant Protection Bylaw, 2025, No. 21788 does NOT apply to property governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_reg_21788.pdf
- **ALR edge:** the Fleetwood plan area runs toward the Agricultural Land Reserve in the east; on ALR parcels the ALC frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/fleetwood-land-use-plans/fleetwood-town-centre-plan and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property taxes (city-wide, not area-specific):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Fleetwood Tynehead?** Two verified ownership products, in two different legal forms: Fleetwood Gardens (16068 83 Ave — a 46-home, 2005 strata condo building, BCS1550, "Age Restricted 55+" in MLS building data) and manufactured homes in Westwood Court (8670 156 St — a gated 55+ park where you own the home and rent the pad). Everything else seniors-branded in Fleetwood is rental or care. https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/311-16068-83-ave-1729077 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/8670-156-street/9
- **Q: Is Fleetwood Gardens really the newest 55+ strata in Surrey?** In this research, yes: built 2005, it is the only verified 55+ strata building in the city newer than 2000 — the other fourteen verified complexes are 1986–2000 stock in the listings checked. Sources: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/surrey-condos-under-800k-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md, Section 5.
- **Q: At Westwood Court, do I get a strata fee or a pad rent?** A pad rent. Listings state the pad fee includes water, garbage and management, and there are no strata fees because there is no strata — the tenancy runs under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and the park's own rules, including its 55+ rule and park approval. Confirm the current pad rent for the specific pad with the park before writing. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/8670-156-street/9 and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live at Fleetwood Gardens?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. In the Westwood Court park, a younger spouse is governed by the park's own rules, not the strata exemption — read them before writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in Fleetwood Gardens as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my Fleetwood Gardens unit?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, including any older "rentals not allowed" record for this building. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** It differs by product: Fleetwood Gardens records pets allowed with restrictions (listings specify cats), and Westwood Court listings state pets welcome. Confirm the specific rule in the Form B (strata) or the park rules (park) for the unit or pad you are buying. https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/311-16068-83-ave-1729077 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/8670-156-street/9
- **Q: Is Elim Village something I can buy into, or move a parent into later?** Elim Village is a society retirement/care campus on 159/160 Street with independent living, assisted living and full care on one site — over 500 units and over 600 residents in its own history — so it is the local "later" ladder, not an open-market strata purchase. Its independent-living purchase/lease form is UNCONFIRMED from the sources checked; ask the Elim Housing Society directly. https://ca.indeed.com/cmp/Elim-Village/about and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/C/2/the-harrison-at-elim-village
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** In the strata, mechanically yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. In Westwood Court, the resale pool is set by the park's own age and approval rules. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day, and when does SkyTrain arrive?** Today, buses on Fraser Highway and the Fleetwood streets — route-level detail for this sub-area is UNVERIFIED in this pass. The two named project stations, Fleetwood (160 St) and Bakerview-166 Street, carry an anticipated in-service date of 2029 — Fleetwood Gardens listings already note the future station at Fraser Highway and 160 Street, a short walk west of the building. Price the plan, not operating service. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/stations/ and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/16068-83-avenue/202

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **One short block of 83 Avenue holds three different seniors products with three different tenures.** Fleetwood Gardens at 16068 (strata ownership, 55+), Venvi Fleetwood Villa at 16028 (rental retirement residence, registered assisted living, class SN) and Fleetwood Place at 16011 (24-hour long-term complex care). A buyer who searches the street name alone will mix ownership, rental and care listings that have nothing in common except the block. https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/311-16068-83-ave-1729077 and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=buffer&name=&address=&order=alr_residence_type&sort=asc&page=5 and http://www.fleetwoodplace.ca/
- **Fleetwood's care campus is bigger than its 55+ ownership stock by an order of magnitude.** Elim Village alone reports over 500 units and over 600 residents across independent, assisted and full care on one Fleetwood site, against 46 strata homes at Fleetwood Gardens plus the pads at Westwood Court (park site count UNCONFIRMED). Fleetwood is a place seniors move to for the care ladder; the buyable 55+ shelf is the small part of it. https://ca.indeed.com/cmp/Elim-Village/about
- **The directory line that could mis-sell a townhouse complex.** Fleetwood Gate (16180 86 Ave) carries a generic "Age restrictions" line in a building directory with no age stated anywhere checked — if that bylaw was a sub-55 bylaw, it has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, and if it is 55+, no listing checked says so. The registered bylaws are the only settling source. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/fleetwood-gate-16180-86-ave and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Surrey's tenant-protection bylaw does not cover the sub-area's park.** Bylaw No. 21788 (2025) expressly does not apply to property governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act — Westwood Court residents' redevelopment protection is the provincial framework plus the City's Manufactured Home Park Policy (12 months' notice of a final moving date, only after all permits are received, and $20,000 payable on or before the move-out date). https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_reg_21788.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf
- **Fleetwood Tynehead's attached market was Surrey's third-fastest in September 2026.** The sub-area recorded an 11% Condos & Townhomes sales ratio (15 solds on 136 listings), behind only Bridgeview (25%) and Panorama Ridge (19%) among Surrey's 15 sub-areas, with detached at 10% (14 on 137) — context for a downsizer selling a Fleetwood house to buy the one 55+ condo building in the same sub-area (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file).
- **No new age-restricted strata project appears in any source checked for Fleetwood.** The Fleetwood Plan and the two coming stations are driving general-market density along Fraser Highway; the verified 55+ strata supply remains the single 2005 building on 83 Avenue. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/fleetwood-land-use-plans/fleetwood-town-centre-plan and https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/311-16068-83-ave-1729077
