# Abbotsford West — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Abbotsford West" is the Fraser Valley Real Estate Board sub-area name used in board data; the City's own demographic geography calls the wider west side "West Abbotsford" (profile area 7) and treats Peardonville, Clearbrook, South Clearbrook and Townline as separate profile areas inside it — there is no single City neighbourhood plan named Abbotsford West. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/2020%20McKee%20Profile%20-%20spreads%20final.pdf
- Rough extent: Highway 1 across the north, Mt. Lehman Road and Clearbrook Road as the main north–south routes, South Fraser Way as the east–west arterial, and the Agricultural Land Reserve and Abbotsford International Airport (YXX) lands to the southwest. Precise sub-area edges are not published in text form by the City. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap
- Central Abbotsford sits to the east, Aberdeen and Poplar to the west and northwest inside the city, Matsqui to the north, and Aldergrove (Township of Langley) to the west along Fraser Highway. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/development-planning
- Where the 55+ stock actually is: it clusters in Clearbrook and around Mill Lake — Old Yale Road, Tims Avenue, Gladwin Road, Peardonville Road and Simon Avenue carry almost every verified age-restricted building in this sub-area (see Section 2). The Highstreet / Mt. Lehman end of the sub-area has no verified 55+ complex in any source checked.
- Transit here is BC Transit's Central Fraser Valley system, not TransLink: routes 1 (Highstreet / UFV) and 2 (Highstreet / McMillan) run from Highstreet, route 66 Fraser Valley Express runs from Highstreet toward Lougheed Station, and the Highstreet Transit Exchange on Highstreet Access Road has six bus bays. There is no SkyTrain or West Coast Express station in Abbotsford. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf and https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/projects-go/highstreet-transit-exchange
- Health anchors on the west side: Abbotsford Urgent and Primary Care Centre, 100-2692 Clearbrook Road (urgent care 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., seven days a week); the city's hospital, Abbotsford Regional Hospital and Cancer Centre at 32900 Marshall Road, sits just east in Central Abbotsford. Both are Fraser Health services. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Abbotsford/abbotsford-regional-hospital-and-cancer-centre
- School District 34 serves the area; catchments are set by SD34 Policy 18 and checked address by address in the district School Locator — locator runs show the west side splits (3614 Clearbrook Road returns Clearbrook Elementary → Colleen and Gordie Howe Middle → W.J. Mouat Secondary; 3433 Firhill Drive returns Blue Jay Elementary → Eugene Reimer Middle → Rick Hansen Secondary). https://www.abbyschools.ca/policy-18-school-catchment-areas

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **55+ is the only age bylaw a BC strata may have.** Since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44), the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1/123.2, allows a strata to restrict residents to 55 or older — and any older 19+, 40+ or 45+ bylaw stopped being enforceable that day. That is why the older 45+ description of Trethewey Tower (Section 2) cannot be relied on. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw governs who may live in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states that a person under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Exemptions are built into the law (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** people already living in the unit when the 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents), live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children the resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **No strata in BC can ban long-term rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022, so where an older Abbotsford West listing says "no rentals," that bylaw no longer applies; a strata may still restrict short-term rentals, and a tenant in a 55+ building must still meet the age bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home pads follow tenancy law, not strata law.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act covers most manufactured home tenancies in BC — the buyer owns the home and rents the site (as at Paradise Park, Section 2). Pad rent can be increased only once every 12 months, with at least three full months' written notice, and the increase is capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for increases taking effect in 2026) plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **City zoning already has a seniors-housing category.** Abbotsford's Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 framework defines "Congregate Housing" — an independent living setting with common dining and amenity areas (definition added by Bylaw 3249-2022) — which is the use class the City's draft bylaw work associates with seniors supportive housing, assisted living and long-term care projects. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-abbotsford-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/7217/8514/3396/d9117e5011287c79b090adc71e32c234_Appendix_B_-_Draft_ZB_Amendments.pdf
- **Suites in a house you already own:** Zoning Bylaw s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings; City reports applying the bylaw state the criteria include a minimum 400 m² lot, 12.0 m frontage, not on a cul-de-sac bulb, and frontage on a Collector or Local road. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **The governing plan is new.** Abbotsforward 2050 is the City's Official Community Plan (Bylaw 2600-2016, as updated), and the current OCP document set (Bylaw No. 3690-2025) carries the vision that "people can stay in their neighbourhood as they age" — the policy hook for seniors housing proposals on the west side. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2016b2600schedulee
- **Property tax dates:** City of Abbotsford taxes were due July 2, 2026; unpaid balances took a 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in one of these buildings?** Yes, in law: Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption), and some buildings here state the rule themselves — Royal Court's listing data says only one owner must be at least 55, and The Wedgewood's says only one resident needs to be over 55. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.zolo.ca/abbotsford-real-estate/31930-old-yale-road/215
- **Q: I'm under 55 — can I buy a unit for my parent, or as an investment?** Ownership is not age-restricted; the bylaw restricts residing. The Province states under-55s can own in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in the unit, so a 55+ parent can be the resident of a unit you own. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: A listing says "no rentals allowed." Can I actually rent my unit out?** Yes for long-term tenancies: every strata rental-restriction bylaw in BC has been void since Nov 24, 2022, whatever the listing sheet or an old bylaw printout says. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be banned by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I keep a pet?** It is building-by-building on this side of town, and the 55+ buildings lean strict: Evergreen Village, Elmwood Manor, Heather Ridge, Silverwood Manor, Fernwood Manor, Centennial Court and Fircrest Estates all record no pets in listing/directory sources, while Downes Road Estates allows pets with restrictions, Trethewey Tower's current MLS data allows one cat, and Paradise Park allows one small pet with park approval. Confirm against the strata's registered bylaws or the park rules for the specific unit. Sources: the building entries in Section 2.
- **Q: What's the difference between Garden Park Tower and a condo at Evergreen Village?** Tenure. Evergreen Village is freehold strata — you own the unit and can sell it on the open market. Garden Park Tower is life lease: an up-front capital payment (typically the BC Assessment value, per the Society) buys lifetime occupancy plus monthly maintenance charges, and on departure the Society redeems the lease under its terms — there is no strata lot to list. https://gardenparktower.ca/life-lease and http://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/evergreen-village-31955-old-yale-road
- **Q: In Paradise Park, what do I actually own?** The manufactured home itself. The pad is rented under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act: pad rent can rise only once a year, on at least three full months' notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026) plus the proportional levy/utility amount; resales in parks also run through the park's own approval process — the Paradise Park listing notes one small pet is allowed only with park approval, which shows the approval step is real. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www.zolo.ca/abbotsford-real-estate/31313-livingstone-avenue/9
- **Q: Do any of the older buildings include utilities in the strata fee?** Some do, per listing data: Elmwood Manor's fee is recorded as covering electricity, heat and water; Fernwood Manor's covers heat and hot water; Lakewood Gardens' covers gas, heat and hot water. That varies by building and by year — confirm the current fee inclusions on the Form B. Sources: the building entries in Section 2.
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale market?** Mechanically yes: the next buyer must be able to live there — 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case — or buy without residing. That is the built-in trade-off of every BC 55+ strata since the 2022 changes confined age bylaws to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: How do I get to the hospital and to Vancouver transit from Clearbrook?** Abbotsford Regional Hospital and Cancer Centre is at 32900 Marshall Road, just east of this sub-area, and the Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 100-2692 Clearbrook Road. For regional transit, route 66 Fraser Valley Express runs from the Highstreet Transit Exchange toward Lougheed Station; Abbotsford's own buses are BC Transit, and there is no SkyTrain or West Coast Express station in the city. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Abbotsford/abbotsford-regional-hospital-and-cancer-centre and https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf
- **Q: If I need care later, what's on this side of town?** The Tabor Village campus on Sunrise Crescent stacks the ladder in one place: Tabor Manor's 38 independent rental apartments, Tabor Court's assisted living (88 Fraser Health-subsidized suites plus 16 private), and Tabor Home's 118 complex-care beds; MSA Manor adds 34 publicly subsidized long-term care beds on Gladwin Road, with admissions coordinated through Fraser Health. https://www.findhealthclinics.org/CA/Abbotsford/1588727874710988/Tabor-Village and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/A/F/msa-manor

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Almost all of Abbotsford's 55+ strata stock is in this one corridor.** Fifteen verified age-restricted strata complexes sit in Abbotsford West (Section 2), most of them built 1979–1996 along Old Yale Road, Tims Avenue and Gladwin Road — the comparable counts are nine strata complexes in Central Abbotsford, two in Poplar, and none verified in Abbotsford East or Matsqui (see those files). A buyer who insists on another part of Abbotsford is mostly choosing between detached houses, life lease, or rentals.
- **The two Tims Avenue buildings face each other and are not twins.** Elmwood Manor (32025 Tims Ave, NWS1317, built 1981) and Maplewood Manor (32040 Tims Ave, NWS1482, built 1980) are separate strata corporations with separate bylaws and fees; Elmwood's listing data covers electricity, heat and water in the fee, which Maplewood's sources do not state. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3079459-211-32025-tims-avenue-abbotsford-bc-v2t-2h3 and https://www.rew.ca/buildings/areas/abbotsford-bc
- **Old directories still call Trethewey Tower 45+.** A building directory in circulation describes 32440 Simon Ave as 45+; sub-55 age bylaws have been unenforceable since Nov 24, 2022, and current MLS data for the tower records "Restricted Age: 55+". Always price and qualify against the current registered bylaws, not a directory page. https://geraldlucas.ca/mylistings.html/listing.r3052595-1602-32440-simon-avenue-abbotsford-v2t-5r3.106964680 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Building data for the biggest 55+ building conflicts between sources.** Evergreen Village is recorded by the building directory as 205 units, 10 levels, built 1985 (plan NWS1737); an agent page for the same address says built 1995 with 12 levels. The directory figures are used in Section 2, but a buyer should reconcile against the strata plan and Form B before writing. http://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/evergreen-village-31955-old-yale-road
- **Downes Road Estates is filed "west" only because the MLS says so.** The bare land strata at 32250 Downes Rd carries an Abbotsford West sub-area label in MLS listing headings (postal code V4X 2R1); a buyer searching by map alone would likely look for it in east-side results and miss it. https://www.estateblock.com/abbotsford/16-32250-downes-rd-518712
- **The City runs seniors programming out of the same Clearbrook corridor.** The City's Abbotsford Seniors Connect Directory 2025–2026 lists Garden Park Tower / Clearbrook Golden Age Society among its seniors service profiles — the life lease tower at 2825 Clearbrook Rd doubles as a community centre open to non-residents, with a coffee shop and seniors programs. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-03/SENIORS%20CONNECT%20DIRECTORY%202025-2026.pdf
- **Abbotsford's tax penalty is a two-step.** Taxes were due July 2, 2026, with 5% added July 3 and a second 5% on September 16, 2026 — a buyer closing in late summer inherits whichever step has already landed on an unpaid balance. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
