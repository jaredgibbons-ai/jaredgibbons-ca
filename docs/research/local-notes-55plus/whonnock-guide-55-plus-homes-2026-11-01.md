# Whonnock — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Whonnock is the rural east side of the City of Maple Ridge, centred on Whonnock Lake. It is both a City neighbourhood name and the board (MLS®) sub-area name; the City publishes no boundary for it, so any east/west edge is a working description only — the site's working scope runs from about 264 St east to the Mission border at 288 St. https://jaredgibbons.ca/whonnock-guide.html
- Lougheed Highway (Hwy 7) and Dewdney Trunk Road are the east–west roads; 272 Street and 280 Street run north–south, with 112 Avenue east–west. https://jaredgibbons.ca/whonnock-guide.html
- Whonnock Elementary is at 27471 112 Ave; there is no secondary school inside Whonnock — Garibaldi Secondary is at 24789 Dewdney Trunk Rd, west of the area. Names and addresses confirmed against the BC Ministry K-12 school file. https://jaredgibbons.ca/whonnock-guide.html
- The area's facilities are Whonnock Lake Park (small sandy beach, swimming area, trails, playground; the Ridge Canoe and Kayak Club is based on Whonnock Lake), Selvey Park and Whonnock Lake Centre. There is no City recreation centre or library branch inside Whonnock — the Maple Ridge Leisure Centre (11925 Haney Place) and the Maple Ridge Public Library (22470 Dewdney Trunk Road) are in Town Centre, well west. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods and https://www.mapleridge.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/maple-ridge-leisure-centre
- Transit is thin: TransLink route 701 runs east on Dewdney Trunk Road, but most east trips end at Dewdney Trunk Road at 248 Street — west of Whonnock — and only some weekday trips continue non-stop from Haney Place to Mission City Station. There is no West Coast Express station in this sub-area. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/194/tt701.pdf and https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- Where 55+ housing concentrates: nowhere. No source found places any 55+ strata, 55+ rental building, retirement residence, care home or subsidized seniors building inside Whonnock. The housing stock the guide page's own feed shows is rural detached acreages — homes on roughly 2 to 11.73 acres, bare land, and one highway-exposure commercial property — with zero attached listings in the sub-area in September 2026 (SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, as recorded in the area research file). https://jaredgibbons.ca/whonnock-guide.html

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Why this barely bites in Whonnock:** almost nothing in Whonnock is strata at all. The stock is freehold acreage, so there is usually no strata bylaw book to check — the age-restriction law matters here mainly when a buyer compares a Whonnock acreage against a 55+ strata elsewhere in Maple Ridge. Source: the housing profile in ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/whonnock-guide.md (2 detached listings, 0 attached listings, September 2026, SnapStats GVR September 2026 report).
- **City zoning: Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019.** The zoning schedule lists minimum lot areas including A-1 at 2.0 ha, A-2 at 4.0 ha, A-3 at 8.0 ha, RS-2 at 0.4 ha and RS-3 at 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha depending on the zone text — the large-lot rural zones are why Whonnock has acreages instead of seniors complexes. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **OCP citation — CONTESTED, flagged, not resolved:** program sources conflict on the current Maple Ridge Official Community Plan bylaw number (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024). What is settled in this program: Council adopted updates to the OCP on November 25, 2025, and the Albion Area Plan update on October 28, 2025; no area plan for Whonnock was found. Do not print an OCP bylaw number for Maple Ridge until the conflict is resolved against the City's own bylaw page. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/official-community-plan
- **Secondary suites:** one secondary suite per lot is permitted in listed zones including A-1, A-2 and A-3, contained within the single detached building, to a maximum of 40% of gross floor area, and not in the Floodplain Area — the main legal route to housing a parent or caregiver on a Whonnock acreage. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **ALR rules:** much of rural Maple Ridge is Agricultural Land Reserve. ALR status is per-parcel — check the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder. On ALR land the ALR Use Regulation permits one secondary suite in the principal residence (s.31), and an additional residence only if the s.32 conditions are met. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo105/loo105/30_2019
- **Wells and septic:** Whonnock acreages run on private services. Wells are recorded in the provincial GWELLS database, and onsite sewerage must comply with the Sewerage System Regulation, including the 30 m setback from a well unless professional advice is obtained. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo104/loo104/22_326_2004

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ housing in Whonnock itself?** No — none verified. No source checked places a 55+ strata, 55+ rental building, retirement residence, care home, subsidized seniors building or manufactured-home park inside Whonnock; the sub-area recorded zero attached listings in September 2026. https://jaredgibbons.ca/whonnock-guide.html
- **Q: Where is the closest seniors housing, then?** In central Maple Ridge (Haney / Town Centre) — for example the Haney Pioneer Village Co-op at 11830 230 St, which is a co-op for independent seniors aged **60+** (not 55+), and retirement living such as the Willow residence. None of it is in Whonnock. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/MRN/200924/MRNN200924_Z32.pdf
- **Q: Can I house my parent in a suite on a Whonnock acreage?** Generally yes, if the zoning lines up: one secondary suite per lot is permitted in the A-1, A-2 and A-3 zones, inside the single detached building, up to 40% of gross floor area, and not in the Floodplain Area. On ALR land the suite must sit inside the principal residence under the ALR Use Regulation (s.31). https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo105/loo105/30_2019
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. If we buy into a 55+ strata elsewhere in Maple Ridge, can we both live there?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: A listing says "45+" — is that enforceable?** No. Since Nov 24, 2022, only 55+ age bylaws are valid in BC strata; 45+ (and 40+, 19+) age bylaws are invalid, no matter what old listing text or an old bylaw book says. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What does downsizing to Whonnock actually mean day to day?** An acreage, not an apartment: private well and septic, a drive to Town Centre for the Leisure Centre, library and most shopping, and limited bus service — route 701's east trips mostly end at 248 Street, west of Whonnock. https://www.mapleridge.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/maple-ridge-leisure-centre and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/194/tt701.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, Maple Ridge — the community hospital for Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows, with 24/7 emergency care. The Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 121 – 11900 Haney Place. Both are in central Maple Ridge, not Whonnock. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital
- **Q: Is Whonnock in a floodplain?** Parts of the low south edge sit in the Fraser River floodplain framework, and the City prohibits secondary suites in the Floodplain Area — check City flood mapping for the specific parcel before removing subjects. Parcel-level flood status: UNCONFIRMED in these notes. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The City rewrote its flood rules in 2026.** The City of Maple Ridge Floodplain Management Bylaw No. 8121-2026 designates floodplain land and sets flood-management regulations across the city — directly relevant to low-lying Whonnock parcels on the Fraser side. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/maple-ridge-floodplain-management-bylaw-no-8121-2026pdf
- **Maple Ridge's July 28, 2026 housing package touched manufactured-home parks city-wide.** Council advanced a new Tenant Protection Bylaw and amendments to the Manufactured Home Park Redevelopment Tenant Assistance Policy (Policy No. 6.27, amended effective July 28, 2026) alongside a new Rental Protection Policy — protection that matters to park tenants anywhere in the city if a park is ever redeveloped. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-continues-housing-action-new-zoning-permit-processes-and-rental-policies and https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/manufactured-home-park-redevelopment-tenant-assistance-policy-no-627pdf
- **The OCP was updated in late 2025 — but its bylaw number is contested in this program's sources (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024).** Council adopted OCP updates November 25, 2025 (Albion Area Plan update October 28, 2025); no Whonnock area plan exists. Any page copy naming an OCP number needs the City's bylaw page checked first. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/official-community-plan
- **The "55+ supply" story in Maple Ridge is a Town Centre rental story, not a Whonnock story.** The Royal Crescent site in Town Centre was slated to be redeveloped into roughly 98 units of permanent affordable housing for seniors with New Vista Society under the Building BC: Community Housing Fund, after the 52-unit supportive housing building at 11685 Fraser St opened in 2024 — all central Maple Ridge. https://canada.constructconnect.com/joc/news/projects/2024/08/fraser-valley-complex-care-homes-open
- **Whonnock's market shelf is effectively empty, attached or otherwise.** September 2026: 2 detached listings, 0 detached sales, 0 attached listings, 0 attached sales (SnapStats GVR September 2026 report).
