# Cypress Park Estates (West Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Cypress Park Estates is the listed site/area name for the existing residential area in the District of West Vancouver's Upper Lands above Highway 1, on and around Cypress Bowl Road, Chippendale Road and Uplands Way, taking in the Rodgers Creek streets east of Cypress Bowl Road. **It is not a District neighbourhood name on its own** — District sources call the existing residential area Cypress Park, Rodgers Creek and the Uplands, plus the planned Cypress Village and Cypress West future neighbourhoods in the Official Community Plan. Boundaries are UNVERIFIED as an exact line; District planning sources place the area above Highway 1, below the 1,200-foot contour. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/cypress-park-estates.md)
- Three Cypress names, kept apart: (1) this residential area; (2) Cypress Mountain ski area — BC Parks states Cypress Mountain Resort is entirely within Cypress Park (Cypress Provincial Park), up Cypress Bowl Road, and is not the residential area; (3) "Cypress Bowl," the road/place name. A listing that blurs them has mis-sold the location. https://bcparks.ca/cypress-park/
- Parks inside the area are District parks, distinct from the provincial park: Cypress Falls Park (waterfalls, Cypress Creek, designated off-leash trails), Cypress Trails Park and Woodburn Park. District recreation and the library are in Ambleside, far east and downhill: West Vancouver Community Centre and Aquatic Centre, 2121 Marine Drive; West Vancouver Memorial Library, 1950 Marine Drive. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/cypress-falls-park and https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/facilities/community-centre
- Schools: SD45 catchments are address-based and **the catchment for this sub-area is UNVERIFIED** — the SD45 School Locator was run for the sample address 3900 Cypress Bowl Road on 2026-10-21 and returned NO RESULT (the address falls outside the locator's listed ranges for Cypress Bowl Road: Even 3850–3850, Odd 3755–3757). Cypress Park Primary (IB PYP, K–3), 4355 Marine Drive, is the school physically named for the Cypress Park area, at the foot of the slope on Marine Drive — a K–3 primary school, not a confirmed catchment for an Upper Lands address. SD45 has no middle tier. https://westvancouverschools.ca/admissions/student-admissions-guidelines/ and https://westvancouverschools.ca/schools/cypress-park-ib-pyp/
- Transit: route 256 (Whitby Estates / Park Royal), a community shuttle, runs via Chippendale Road to Westhill at the edge of this area, then via 21st Street, Gordon Avenue, 22nd Street and Marine Drive to Park Royal. No TransLink route up Cypress Bowl Road is documented in the timetables read; BC Parks states the resort runs a free shuttle between Lot 3B and the downhill and nordic areas in ski season. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/210/tt256.pdf and https://bcparks.ca/cypress-park/
- Shopping and health care inside the residential area: UNVERIFIED (none confirmed) — no grocery store, shopping centre, clinic or health unit inside Cypress Park Estates was confirmed on any source. The District plan for the future Cypress Village anticipates neighbourhood shopping, a school and a community centre inside that future village — planned amenities, not existing ones. https://westvancouver.ca/media/4203
- Where the 55+ housing actually sits: nowhere in Cypress Park Estates. There is no seniors product of any kind in the Upper Lands in any source checked; West Vancouver's verified 55+ stratas and its rental/care cluster are all in Ambleside, down at sea level on the far side of the District. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **District zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010** uses RS2, RS3 and RS5 single-family zones (Sections 202, 203, 205) in this area, CD3 for Rodgers Creek (density set by lot-size band, Section 601–603) and the CV (Cypress Village) Zone for Cypress Village (Section 750). Section 200 states minimum site areas of 8,094 m² in RS1, 1,625 m² in RS2, 975 m² in RS3, 731 m² in RS4 and 488 m² in RS5, and lists secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses in the single-family zones; suites must be registered with the District. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf and https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zoning%20Bylaw%204662%20-%20Section%20601%20-%20603%20Comprehensive%20Developmental%20Zone%20%28CD1%20to%20CD3%29.pdf
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018** names Cypress Village and Cypress West as future neighbourhoods in the Upper Lands; the Area Development Plan for Cypress Village and Eagleridge was approved by Council, paired with Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 5206, 2024, with initial works under Development Permit 24-115 to British Pacific Properties Limited and Development Permit 24-106 covering 3900 Cypress Bowl Road (Area 5, Rodgers Creek). https://westvancouver.ca/business-development/development-applications/cypress-village-area
- **Hazard permits — the defining checks here:** a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit is required for work within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface, and the District's Community Wildfire Resiliency Plan describes the Cypress Park area as consistently sloped terrain; an Environmental Development Permit is required within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank (Cypress Creek and Rodgers Creek both run through the area), and a subdivision can trigger a difficult-terrain development permit on steep terrain. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Community%20Wildfire%20Resiliency%20Plan%20-%20Out%202025.pdf and https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Strata-purchase note for the Rodgers Creek pockets:** for Hawksley or Chippendale Mews strata purchases, review the depreciation report, contingency reserve and bylaws under the provincial strata framework early; BC strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **District taxes 2026:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026. Water and sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly. The Province's additional school tax can apply at these price points: 0.2% on the residential portion between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

**Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Cypress Park Estates?**
A: None were verified — there is no age-restricted product of any kind in the Upper Lands in any source checked. West Vancouver's verified 55+ stratas are three Ambleside buildings (Westview Terrace, 1390 Duchess Ave; Tudor Gardens, 843 22nd St; Lincoln Gardens, 2110 Marine Dr). Uphill, the buyable stock is freehold houses plus the general-market Hawksley and Chippendale Mews stratas. https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/on-the-market/55-plus-buildings-west-vancouver/

**Q: Is Hawksley a seniors community? It is quiet and strata-managed.**
A: No. Hawksley is marketed with family amenities — including a covered play area for neighbourhood children in its amenity building — and a Cypress Park Estates listing record states "Senior Community: No" in its MLS fields. No source states any age restriction for Hawksley or Chippendale Mews. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/hawksley-british-properties-west-vancouver-presale-executive-condos/ and https://www.zolo.ca/west-vancouver-real-estate/3317-chippendale-road

**Q: Is there any seniors rental, care or subsidized housing uphill?**
A: None verified. Every West Vancouver seniors residence in the Assisted Living Registry, and the District's below-market Kiwanis Manor rental, sits in the Ambleside/Park Royal flat — a senior who needs care or rental is leaving the Upper Lands, not moving within it. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3AWest%20Vancouver&f%5B1%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=desc

**Q: Can I add a suite to an Upper Lands house for a caregiver or family?**
A: The RS single-family zones list a secondary suite and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses (suites must be registered with the District); in Rodgers Creek the CD3 section, not the RS table, sets a lot's density rules. Wildfire, creek and difficult-terrain permits can still constrain the specific lot. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf

**Q: My spouse is under 55 — how do the exemptions work if we buy a 55+ strata in Ambleside?**
A: Strata Property Regulation 7.01 (expanded May 1, 2023) exempts a younger spouse/partner of the specified 55+ resident, children under 19 in that resident's care, adult children the resident cared for before age 19, live-in caregivers, and legacy residents who lived in the unit when the bylaw passed. The bylaw restricts residency, not ownership. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm

**Q: Which school serves a Cypress Park Estates home?**
A: UNVERIFIED at address level — the SD45 locator returned no result for the sample address 3900 Cypress Bowl Road (outside its listed ranges for that road), and Cypress Park Primary is a K–3 school at 4355 Marine Drive, not a confirmed catchment for an Upper Lands home. Check the exact address directly with SD45 before relying on any school claim. https://westvancouverschools.ca/admissions/student-admissions-guidelines/ and https://westvancouverschools.ca/schools/cypress-park-ib-pyp/

**Q: What is Cypress Village, and will it bring seniors housing?**
A: Cypress Village is the District's planned future neighbourhood west of Cypress Bowl Road (Area Development Plan approved; Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 5206, 2024; initial works under Development Permit 24-115), anticipating neighbourhood shopping, a school and a community centre. No seniors-specific or 55+ housing in Cypress Village was verified in any source — treat any such claim as UNCONFIRMED. https://westvancouver.ca/business-development/development-applications/cypress-village-area and https://westvancouver.ca/media/4203

**Q: How real is the wildfire/insurance conversation on these lots?**
A: It is a permit-level fact, not a talking point: the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area runs within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface, and the District's wildfire plan calls this consistently sloped terrain with Cypress Falls Park to the east. Confirm FireSmart and insurance requirements before subjects come off. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Community%20Wildfire%20Resiliency%20Plan%20-%20Out%202025.pdf

**Q: What transit exists if I stop driving?**
A: Very little uphill: the route 256 community shuttle serves Chippendale Road at Westhill, at the area's edge, running to Park Royal; there is no TransLink route up Cypress Bowl Road. For a senior buyer, this is a car-based area — that is the honest answer, and it is why the District's seniors stock concentrated in flat, bus-served Ambleside. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/210/tt256.pdf

**Q: Where is the hospital?**
A: Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver, serves the North Shore including West Vancouver; the North Vancouver Urgent and Primary Care Centre (Suite 200, 221 West Esplanade) serves North Shore residents including West Vancouver. No clinic or health unit inside Cypress Park Estates is confirmed. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/north-vancouver-urgent-and-primary-care-centre

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The Upper Lands are the District's newest growth area — and still have no seniors housing.** The Rodgers Creek and Cypress Village lands are West Vancouver's current expansion frontier (Area Development Plan, Bylaw No. 5206 in 2024, development permits in 2024), yet no 55+, seniors rental or care component appears for the area in any source checked; the new stock (Hawksley, Chippendale Mews) is family product with children's amenities. https://westvancouver.ca/business-development/development-applications/cypress-village-area and https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/hawksley-british-properties-west-vancouver-presale-executive-condos/
- **One road carries homes, hikers and the ski hill.** Cypress Bowl Road is simultaneously the residential access road, the Cypress Falls/Cypress Trails trailhead route and the only road to Cypress Provincial Park and Cypress Mountain Resort — winter ski traffic and summer trail traffic pass the same driveways. BC Parks states the resort sits entirely within the provincial park, reached by Cypress Bowl Road from Highway 1. https://bcparks.ca/cypress-park/
- **The school named for the area is at the bottom of the hill, and only goes to Grade 3.** Cypress Park Primary (IB PYP, K–3) is at 4355 Marine Drive — a primary school whose students have the opportunity to continue the IB programme at West Bay Elementary — and the SD45 locator could not even return a catchment for a Cypress Bowl Road sample address. School claims for Upper Lands listings need an address-by-address SD45 check, every time. https://westvancouverschools.ca/schools/cypress-park-ib-pyp/ and https://westvancouverschools.ca/admissions/student-admissions-guidelines/
- **Two creeks cross the area, and both trigger permits.** Cypress Creek (through Cypress Falls Park) and Rodgers Creek both run through the residential area; work within 15 metres of a watercourse bank needs an Environmental Development Permit, on top of the wildfire and difficult-terrain layers. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **The Eagleridge lands became park, not housing.** Under the Cypress Village approval package, the Eagleridge lands transferred to the District were dedicated as park — part of the future village's land went to green space rather than to any housing, seniors or otherwise. https://westvancouver.ca/business-development/development-applications/cypress-village-area
- **The name stack is a marketing minefield.** "Cypress Park Estates" (the MLS area name), "Cypress Park" (the District's name for the existing area — and also the provincial park's name), "Cypress Bowl" (the road) and "Cypress Village" (the future neighbourhood) are four different referents sharing one word; the research file flags the residential area itself as not a District neighbourhood name on its own.
