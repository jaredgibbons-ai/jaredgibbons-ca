## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. This is the rule that would govern any future strata built on a Cloverdale lot, and the rule behind both complexes in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. That is why Manor Ridge's directory line "rentals not allowed" cannot be read at face value. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000** governs Cloverdale parcels; Surrey's SSMUH changes of July 8, 2024 replaced the former RF-12 / RF-12C zones with the R4 Small Lot Residential Zone and RF-SD with R6 — older land material using RF-12 / RF-SD names is using pre-2024 zone names, and feed cards describing lots as "R3 zoned" are listing text only; confirm the current zone of any parcel in COSMOS. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **SSMUH unit counts (urban lots):** Surrey allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites:** only one secondary suite is permitted per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite, and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Coach houses and garden suites:** all Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow a coach house or garden suite; the unit must be at least 35 m² and at most 75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR; smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access for a coach house, and one extra parking space is required. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **ALR parcels (the feed's Serpentine-side and acreage cards):** subdivision of Agricultural Land Reserve land requires Agricultural Land Commission permission outside limited regulation exceptions; on ALR parcels the housing frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m², a secondary suite only inside the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). Which feed parcels are in the ALR: UNVERIFIED — check parcel by parcel on ALC mapping. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/
- **OCP: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2026, No. 21900** is the current OCP — adopted June 15, 2026, replacing the 2013 OCP Bylaw No. 18020. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **Short-term rentals in Surrey:** renting a dwelling unit for under 90 consecutive days per patron is short-term; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at $358 per year is required before operating. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Charges and taxes the file cannot quote:** Surrey DCC rates for a specific subdivision or build, and the GST treatment of a specific lot sale, are UNVERIFIED in this pass — the live page's GST position and lender down-payment ranges were not confirmed on a CRA or lender source and are not restated as fact here. Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/cloverdale-lots-land.md

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a 55+ home in Cloverdale today, without building?** Yes — two verified strata complexes: Carriage House (17695 58 Ave, a 1987 condo building in downtown Cloverdale whose listings state "55+, No pets permitted") and The Estates at Manor Ridge (6140 192 St, an 80-unit 1989 townhouse complex whose listings state "1 occupant must be 55+"). No 55+ manufactured-home park exists in Cloverdale in this research. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/17695-58-avenue/102 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8
- **Q: Does buying a lot in this feed get me into a 55+ community?** No. Land carries no age restriction; a 55+ rule could only arise later if the land were developed as a strata that adopted a 55+ bylaw under the Strata Property Act — and sub-55 age bylaws have been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, so 55+ is the only age a new bylaw could set. https://jaredgibbons.ca/cloverdale-lots-land.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live at Carriage House or Manor Ridge?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); Manor Ridge's own listing wording is "1 occupant must be 55+." Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a Cloverdale 55+ complex as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, including any older "rentals not allowed" record for Manor Ridge. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals; Surrey separately allows short-term rentals only for an owner living at the property, with a $358-per-year business licence. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Q: If I build on a Cloverdale lot instead, can I add a suite or coach house for a parent or caregiver?** On an urban lot, Surrey permits one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex unit, capped at 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area, and a coach house or garden suite of 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² only on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR) in the zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes. On an ALR parcel the frame is different: any suite must be inside the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of 90 m² or 186 m² by parcel size. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: How many units can I put on a serviced Cloverdale lot?** Under Surrey's SSMUH rules: 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 on larger lots, and up to 6 on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — confirm the lot's current (post-July 2024) zone name in COSMOS before quoting any of this, because RF-12 / RF-SD names in older material are pre-2024 names for R4 and R6. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: Are pets allowed in Cloverdale's 55+ complexes?** Opposite answers on the same community's two complexes: Carriage House listings state no pets permitted; Manor Ridge listings allow 1 pet with restrictions. There is no Cloverdale-wide pet rule — read the specific complex's bylaws. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/17695-58-avenue/102 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8
- **Q: What extra costs sit on top of a land purchase here?** Budget for development cost charges and servicing works on any subdivision or build, plus GST where it applies — but the DCC rates and the GST answer for a specific parcel are UNVERIFIED in this research; get the DCC figure from the City for the specific build and the GST answer from a CPA or lawyer before removing subjects. Surrey's tax calendar (due July 2; 5% + 5% penalties) belongs in the closing plan. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/cloverdale-lots-land.md
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool at Carriage House or Manor Ridge?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
