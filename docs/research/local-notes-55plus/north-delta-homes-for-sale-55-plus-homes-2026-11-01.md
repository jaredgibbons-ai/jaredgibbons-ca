## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. This is the rule that decides Chancellor Place's old 19+ claim and the unstated "age restrictions" at Delta Cedars and Kennedy Gate. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **A rental building's 55+ rule is a tenancy rule, not a strata bylaw.** Sunstone's requirement — at least one resident over 55, proven by ID on file — is set by the rental operator; a renter's protection runs through the tenancy agreement and the Residential Tenancy Act, not strata documents. https://www.comfortlife.ca/retirement-homes/sunstone-apartments/1370
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017** applies parcel by parcel. Single-detached minimum lots run RS1 at 4,000 m², RS4 at 610 m², RS5 at 550 m² or 460 m², RS6 at 390 m² and RS7 at 335 m²; there is no RD5 zone. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Seniors congregate housing is a named use in Delta's apartment zones.** The Apartment Residential 6 (RA6) zone, added to Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 in 2024, lists "seniors congregate housing" as a permitted principal use alongside apartment buildings. No RA6 seniors site in North Delta was confirmed — the permission exists in the bylaw, not yet as a North Delta building in this research. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future")** is Delta's adopted OCP, adopted July 8, 2024 — a single consolidated plan with NO lettered area-plan schedules. The former North Delta Area Plan was Schedule C.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950; do not cite any lettered schedule of Bylaw No. 8400 for a North Delta property, and farm land in Delta is designated "Agriculture (A)" (OCP s. 1.5.1). Development Permit Areas — ND12 Nordel Social Heart, ND3 Annieville, ND1 Fraser River Escarpment — are in Appendix B. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Secondary suites and small-scale multi-unit housing:** SSMUH is permitted on all single-detached and duplex lots citywide under Delta's SSMUH amendments (Bylaws 8394 + 8395, adopted Jun 24, 2024), allowing combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — in a community with no 55+ ownership stock, this is the practical in-area route to housing a parent or caregiver on the same lot. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/235301/ and http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Property tax deferment at 55+:** BC's Property Tax Deferment Program (regular program) lets a homeowner who is 55 or older during the current year defer annual property taxes on a principal residence as a provincial loan. Delta's 2026 tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility and http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **FHSA / Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits: UNVERIFIED** — not read off a live CRA page in this pass; do not quote a figure from this file.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ condo, townhome or rancher complex I can buy in North Delta?** No verified one, in any of the four sub-areas. Every candidate checked either states no age restriction, carries only a void sub-55 claim (Chancellor Place's 19+), or states an "age restriction" without the age (Delta Cedars, Kennedy Gate). Delta's verified 55+ ownership is in Ladner: Delta Green (4686 51 St) and South Pointe (Neilsen Grove). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What 55+ housing does exist in North Delta, then?** One market rental (Sunstone 55+ Apartments, 70 units, Nordel — at least one resident over 55) and three subsidized / non-market rentals in Scottsdale (Scott Road Lodge, Strawberry Hill Manor, White Birch Manor — 138 units in total). Columbus Lodge in Annieville is a seniors independent-living rental whose exact age threshold is UNCONFIRMED. All are rentals — there is nothing to buy in any of them. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf and https://www.comfortlife.ca/retirement-homes/sunstone-apartments/1370
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Delta?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. At Sunstone's rental the test is different: at least one resident must be over 55. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.comfortlife.ca/retirement-homes/sunstone-apartments/1370
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain where a valid 55+ bylaw exists: the tenant still has to satisfy the age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: How do I apply for the Scottsdale subsidized buildings?** All three apply through BC Housing's Housing Registry. Scott Road Lodge and White Birch Manor are rent-geared-to-income, so income rules apply on top of the 55+ requirement; the City guide lists Strawberry Hill Manor's rent type as "Market rent" despite the Registry route — confirm the current rent basis with the operator before assuming a subsidy. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
- **Q: If there's no 55+ complex to buy, how do downsizers stay in North Delta?** On the sources, the in-area mechanism is the house lot itself: Delta's SSMUH rules allow a secondary suite, coach house or garden suite in combination with a detached or duplex dwelling where the zoning applies, and ground-level general-market product (for example the rancher-style ground units at Scott & Nicholson) is a layout choice, not an age bylaw. Confirm the parcel's zone sheet and any streamside or escarpment permit area before pricing that plan. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day, and is there a seniors bus?** Scottsdale Exchange is the hub (local routes plus the R6 Scott Road RapidBus to Scott Road Station, Expo Line, and Newton Exchange); routes 312 and 391 run through Nordel and Annieville to Scott Road Station, and route 340 runs the Watershed Park edge via Kittson Parkway and 64 Avenue. Separately, the City runs a free Delta Seniors Bus for Delta residents aged 65+ — note the 65+ threshold, not 55+ — with curbside pick-up/drop-off inside Delta and medical appointments given priority. https://www.translink.ca/r6 and http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/seniors-services/seniors-bus
- **Q: Is there a seniors centre in North Delta?** Yes — Kennedy Seniors Recreation Centre at 11760 88 Avenue, in the Kennedy cluster at Annieville's south-east side — the City bills it as recreation for 50+, run with the Kennedy Seniors Society. Note the 50+ threshold differs from both the 55+ housing rule and the 65+ seniors bus. http://www.delta.ca/services/seniors/seniors-recreation-centres/lists/seniors-recreation-centres/kennedy-seniors-recreation-centre
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes here at 55+?** Yes, if the home is your principal residence — BC's regular deferment program is open at 55 or older during the current year (also to surviving spouses and persons with disabilities). Delta's tax page links the program; the City's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility and http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

Annieville, Nordel and Sunshine Hills Woods have none. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf

- **Sunstone's own sources disagree about when it opened and how many floor plans it has.** The operator profile says opened 2022 in its narrative and 2020 in its details field, and seven floor plans where its 2019 launch advertisement said five. - **The "age restriction" wording on the two Kennedy-cluster stratas may legally no longer exist.** Building sources for Delta Cedars and Kennedy Gate say an age restriction is in the bylaws but never give the number; since Nov 24, 2022 the only enforceable strata age bylaw in BC is 55+, so a sub-55 bylaw in either building is void — the strata's filed bylaws are the only proof. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

- **North Delta has no subsidized assisted living at all.** The City guide lists Delta's subsidized assisted living only in Ladner (Augustine House) and Tsawwassen (KinVillage), both through Fraser Health — a North Delta buyer who will need assisted living later is looking at a move to South Delta. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf

Cite Appendix B permit areas (ND1, ND3, ND12) instead. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
