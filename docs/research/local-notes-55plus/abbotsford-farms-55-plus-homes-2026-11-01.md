## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **ALR housing frame — the rule set that governs the farmhouse itself:** on Agricultural Land Reserve land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence is permitted, up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; local government bylaws may be more restrictive and may not be more permissive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **You cannot strata-title your way to a 55+ farm community:** depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR and needs ALC permission — ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6 ("Strata Plans Cause Subdivision"). The legal structure a 55+ strata needs does not exist on reserve land without that permission. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm classification is a BC Assessment program with income thresholds, and it does not survive a sale automatically:** the thresholds are $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if larger, and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha; applications must be submitted by October 31 for the next assessment and tax year, and a buyer who plans to keep farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Quota and supply management: UNVERIFIED in every particular** — whether quota is included or excluded on any given farm listing, quota volumes, and marketing-board transfer and approval rules were not confirmed on any marketing-board source checked for this file. Get any quota position in the contract, in writing, and verify it with the applicable board directly. Source: batch-15 research file for this topic.
- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now** (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; younger age bylaws invalid since Nov 24, 2022, Bill 44) — the rule that governs the downsizing destinations in Section 2, not the farm itself. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The strata bylaw restricts residing, not owning,** and Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted, and a tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home pads are tenancy law:** under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, pad rent can be increased only once every 12 months, on at least three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026) plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **City frame:** Abbotsford's OCP is Bylaw 2600-2016 and zoning is under Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw, 2014, No. 2400; the SSMUH amendment is Bylaw 3560-2024, adopted June 25, 2024. A specific farm parcel's A1/A2 zone specifics, ALR status and floodplain position are per-parcel checks — the A1/A2 zone text was not opened for this file, so zone-by-zone rules are UNVERIFIED here. Source: batch-15 research file for this topic.
- **Property transfer tax (general tiers):** 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% on the portion above $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% above $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. How PTT and GST apportion between land, buildings, residence and quota on a specific farm purchase is UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **FHSA and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits: UNVERIFIED** — no live CRA page stating those limits was read for this file.
- **Property taxes:** City of Abbotsford taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 16, 2026. Note this is the City of Abbotsford schedule — it is not the Mission flat-10% rule and not the rural FVRD regime. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ farm community anywhere in Abbotsford?** No — none was verified on any source. There is zero verified 55+ ownership stock on farm land; Abbotsford's 55+ stock is 26 strata complexes, 3 manufactured-home parks and 2 life leases, all in the urban core (Abbotsford West, Central Abbotsford, Poplar, plus Cascade Court in Abbotsford East). Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: What house can an ALR farm parcel have?** Under the ALC frame: one principal residence of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only inside that principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha), with City rules possibly more restrictive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I strata-title part of the farm later for a seniors rancher project?** Not on assumption: depositing a strata plan is a subdivision in the ALR under ALC Information Bulletin 09 s.6 and needs ALC permission. Treat any pitch of that kind as UNCONFIRMED until the ALC position is in writing. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: Is quota included when I buy an Abbotsford farm?** UNVERIFIED as a general rule in this file — no marketing-board source was checked that states it. Read the specific contract for an inclusion/exclusion statement and verify the transfer position with the applicable board before subjects are removed. Source: batch-15 research file for this topic.
- **Q: What income must the operation show to keep farm classification?** BC Assessment's thresholds are $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha; a buyer continuing to farm must apply by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: We're retiring off the farm. What are the ground-oriented 55+ options in town?** The closest-to-farm-life forms in the verified roster are the bare land strata ranchers at Downes Road Estates (52 homes, gated) and the manufactured homes in the three 55+ parks (Paradise Park, Georgian Park, Maplewood Estates), where you own the home and rent the pad. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata after we sell?** Yes, in law: Regulation 7.01 exempts a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident, and several complexes state a one-resident rule themselves — Royal Court ("only one owner must be at least 55") and The Wedgewood ("only one resident needs to be over 55"). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Are market statistics published for Abbotsford farms?** No figure is stated in this file: SnapStats has no farm category, and no official source checked publishes Abbotsford farm counts, sale prices, quota prices or per-acre values — each is UNVERIFIED, and a price opinion stands on the specific property, part by part (land, barns, residence, any quota). Source: batch-15 research file for this topic.
- **Q: Is the floodplain position settled for a Sumas or Matsqui Prairie farm?** No — parcel-level Flood Construction Level and floodplain status are UNVERIFIED in this file. Confirm both with the City for the specific parcel before planning a barn or a house. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse
- **Q: When are property taxes due on an Abbotsford farm?** For a City of Abbotsford parcel: due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 16, 2026. Farm classification affects the assessed class, not the due dates. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The farms feed is not farms-only.** The feed cards read for the underlying research included a 4,810 sq ft Abbotsford West townhouse-zoned building lot that is not a farm at all, alongside Bradner, Aberdeen, Matsqui and Sumas Prairie farm cards — a buyer scanning the page sees a mixed shelf, and each card's zoning and ALR status has to be checked on its own. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-farms.html
- **The page's sector claims are its own copy.** The statements that roughly 72% of Abbotsford's land base sits inside the ALR, that Abbotsford is the highest-grossing agricultural municipality in Canada per hectare, and that it leads BC in the listed berry, poultry, dairy, egg, vegetable, turf, nursery and greenhouse productions were not confirmed on any City, Province or marketing-board source opened for the underlying research — UNVERIFIED as fact; do not repeat them on a farm valuation. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-farms.html
- **The live farms page carries the wrong contact email in places.** Its FAQ and contact blocks give jared@jaredgibbons.ca, while its listing blocks and the rest of the site use jaredgibbons@royallepage.ca — flagged in the underlying research for correction; the royallepage.ca address is the standing one. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-farms.html
- **Farm classification has a hard calendar edge.** The BC Assessment application deadline is October 31 — a farm purchase that closes without the buyer's own General Application filed by October 31 after purchase puts the next tax year's farm class at risk regardless of what the seller's classification was. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **A "second house for the kids" on ALR land is capped small.** The additional-residence tier is 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less — a cottage-scale cap that surprises buyers expecting a full second farmhouse; only properties over 40 ha reach the 186 m² tier. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Downsizing geography runs one way.** Every verified 55+ complex in Abbotsford is in Abbotsford West, Central Abbotsford or Poplar (plus one life lease in Abbotsford East) — the prairie sub-areas where the farms sit contribute the sellers, not the stock. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Three "Maplewoods," three tenures.** Maplewood Manor (Tims Ave) is a buyable 55+ strata; Maplewood House (Jackson St) is a Fraser Health-funded care home; Maplewood Estates (Martens St) is a 55+ manufactured-home park. A retiring farm couple searching "Maplewood" will meet all three. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Abbotsford's tax penalty is a two-step.** Taxes were due July 2, 2026, with 5% added July 3 and a second 5% on September 16, 2026 — farm parcels inside the city are billed on the same City calendar. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
