# West Cambie (Richmond) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- West Cambie is in the north-central part of Richmond, abutting the east side of the City Centre, and includes the Alexandra neighbourhood. It is an official City planning area — the West Cambie Area Plan, Schedule 2.11A to the Official Community Plan (adopted July 24, 2006). Within it, the Alexandra neighbourhood is bounded by Cambie Road, Garden City Road, Alderbridge Way and No. 4 Road, and the DND lands are bounded by No. 4 Road, Alderbridge Way, Shell Road and Westminster Highway. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf and https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm
- The Area Plan names two established residential neighbourhoods: The Oaks and Odlinwood, plus the older Alexandra lot pattern. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf
- Main roads: Cambie Road, Garden City Road, Alderbridge Way, No. 4 Road and Westminster Highway; the Area Plan notes access to major transportation connections in all directions and walking distance to the City Centre. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf
- Schools: Tomsett Elementary, 9671 Odlin Road, is physically inside West Cambie, and A.R. MacNeill Secondary, 6611 No. 4 Road, is on the area's east side. For the sample address 9671 Odlin Road, the SD38 locator result is Tomsett Elementary (K–7) and MacNeill Secondary (8–12); SD38 has no middle-school tier. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
- Parks: Alexandra Park, 9600 Odlin Road, linked by the Alexandra Greenway; Garden City Community Park, 6620 Garden City Road, sits beside the area. Cambie Community Centre is east of the area at 12800 Cambie Road. https://Richmond.Ca/city-hall/news/city2022/alexandra08jun2022.htm and https://richmond.ca/parks-recreation/about/sports/fitness/fitness.htm?PageMode=HTML
- Library: Richmond Public Library Brighouse (Main) Branch, 100-7700 Minoru Gate, in the City Centre; the Cambie Branch, 150-11590 Cambie Road, is east of the area. https://rpl.yourlibrary.ca/about/hours-locations
- Health: the nearest hospital is Richmond Hospital, 7000 Westminster Highway; the Richmond City Centre Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 110, 4671 No. 3 Road, west of the area, walk-in only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/health/about-bc-s-health-care-system/heath-care-partners/hospitallist.pdf and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/richmond-city-centre-urgent-and-primary-care-centre/plan-your-visit
- Transit: Route 410 runs on Cambie Road between Richmond-Brighouse Station and 22nd Street Station, connecting the area to the Canada Line at Richmond-Brighouse Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt410.pdf
- Where 55+ housing actually sits: the area's one verified seniors-specific component is inside a mixed rental building on Cambie Road — The Remy, 9388 Cambie Rd, with 33 affordable seniors' rental apartments (Section 2). No 55+ ownership building was verified anywhere in West Cambie.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** People under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed; live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** Since Nov 24, 2022 no strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw; stratas may still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500.** Zones documented in the area include Single Detached (RS1/F) in the older Alexandra pattern, site-specific Town Housing zones (ZT67, ZT71) on Alexandra Road, and Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (RSM) zoning on a Cambie Road site at 9720 Cambie Road. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf
- **Plan: the West Cambie Area Plan is Schedule 2.11A to the Official Community Plan** (adopted July 24, 2006), under the city-wide 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm
- **Suites / SSMUH:** the City allows at least one secondary suite and/or an accessory dwelling unit per lot on covered lots, with provincial minimums of three units on lots 280 m² or less and four units on larger lots. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Servicing check that matters here:** the Area Plan describes the older Alexandra pattern as remaining on septic sewage disposal — confirm sewer connection status and cost on older Alexandra lots before subject removal. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf
- **Aircraft noise:** the Area Plan states the area is affected by aircraft noise from Vancouver International Airport and that no new single-family development is permitted in Alexandra under the OCP Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development Policy. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf
- **District energy:** apartment buildings in the Alexandra pocket are served by the Alexandra District Energy Utility — City documents list buildings on Alexandra Road, Odlin Road and Tomicki Avenue, so a district-energy utility charge forms part of carrying costs for strata buyers there; pull it from the strata documents. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/_2_-_2021_Annual_General_Meeting_Resolution_of_the_Lulu_Island_Energy_Company_Sp58691.pdf
- **Flood — Richmond is a dike-protected island city:** Richmond is made up of islands within the Fraser River floodplain at an average of 1 metre above sea level; the flood protection system includes 49 km of dikes, is designed for a 1:500 event (0.2% chance in any given year), and dike raising from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres is under way. All lands in Richmond are designated flood plain under Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw No. 8204, which sets flood construction levels (FCLs) habitable floors must meet unless exempt. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in West Cambie?** None that any source verifies. No age-restricted strata ownership building with a West Cambie address was found in building-data sites, BC Housing directories or the Assisted Living Registry. The Alexandra buildings named in City documents (Alexandra Court, Mayfair Place, Omega, Trafalgar) carry no age restriction in any source. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/_2_-_2021_Annual_General_Meeting_Resolution_of_the_Lulu_Island_Energy_Company_Sp58691.pdf
- **Q: What seniors housing does exist in West Cambie?** One verified component: 33 affordable Seniors' Rental Housing apartments inside The Remy, 9388 Cambie Rd, managed by S.U.C.C.E.S.S. — subsidized rental for low-income seniors, not a purchase, and only part of a mixed building that also holds family affordable units and 178 market rentals. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2014MNGD0009-000204
- **Q: Is the Aberdeen Residence a seniors building?** No source says so. It is a 120-unit, 9-storey building at 8080 Cambie Road connected to Aberdeen Centre, run as general furnished rentals / strata (plan BCS2690) — the "Residence" name and the SkyTrain-adjacent location are not an age restriction. https://rentitfurnished.com/vancouver/building/aberdeen
- **Q: Is the new Camden Square project on Cambie Road a 55+ building?** No age restriction is stated in any developer-facing source for Camden Square (9300 Cambie Rd, Westmark, 5 storeys) — it is a general-market presale. The exact unit count also conflicts between sources (126 vs 128) — UNCONFIRMED. https://condotrend.com/projects/camden-square/ and https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/camden-square-west-cambie-presale-richmond-condos-by-westmark-development-group/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we live in a BC 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in the defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. Under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a 55+ parent can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What extra carrying cost do Alexandra strata units have?** A district-energy utility charge: the City's Alexandra District Energy Utility serves the Alexandra Road / Odlin Road / Tomicki Avenue buildings, so the utility cost sits on top of strata fees and standard utilities. Confirm the current charge in the strata documents. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/_2_-_2021_Annual_General_Meeting_Resolution_of_the_Lulu_Island_Energy_Company_Sp58691.pdf
- **Q: Is West Cambie in a floodplain?** All of Richmond is designated flood plain under Bylaw No. 8204, behind a 49 km dike system designed for a 1:500 event, with dike raising from 3.5 m to 4.7 m under way; habitable floors must meet the lot's flood construction level unless exempt. Check the FCL lot by lot. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in West Cambie?** No assisted-living residence or care home with a West Cambie address was verified; Richmond's registered residences are in the City Centre (e.g. Courtyard Gardens, 7051 Moffatt Rd; Minoru Residence long-term care, 7333 Gollner Ave), and the in-area seniors option is The Remy's subsidized independent rental apartments. https://verveseniorliving.com/courtyard-gardens/ and https://www.vch.ca/en/location-service/long-term-care-minoru-residence

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **West Cambie is really two areas priced on different logic:** the older Alexandra lots (a 0.4 ha pattern, septic history in the Area Plan, and an aircraft-noise policy barring new single-family development) versus The Oaks and Odlinwood streets and the newer Alexandra Road townhouse/apartment pocket. A "West Cambie average" mixes all three. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf
- **The seniors' housing here is 33 apartments inside a building most people know as market rentals:** The Remy (9388 Cambie Rd) holds 178 market units, 48 affordable family/single apartments and 33 affordable seniors' apartments, plus a 60-space daycare — and it was the first six-storey wood-frame residential building built in BC, per the Province's 2014 release. Nothing on the building's exterior tells you a third of its affordable stock is seniors'. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2014MNGD0009-000204
- **Cambie Road crosses two planning areas:** The Remy at 9388 Cambie Rd is West Cambie; Knightsbridge II at 12571 Cambie Rd, the subsidized seniors building in BC Housing's directory, is East Cambie. Same street, different plan, different file. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **The Alexandra Greenway is the pocket's spine:** Alexandra Park (9600 Odlin Road) is linked by sections of the Alexandra Greenway between the Garden City Lands and Cambie Road — the walkable route that ties the Odlin Road buildings to the Garden City Lands parkland beside the area. https://Richmond.Ca/city-hall/news/city2022/alexandra08jun2022.htm
- **A rezoning application, not a project:** the City documents an application (RZ 23-015980) at 9720 Cambie Road for an apartment on a site zoned Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (RSM/XL) in the Alexandra neighbourhood — an application record, not an approved building, and no seniors or 55+ component is stated. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf
- **Richmond's two money dates:** 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% on any outstanding balance after Tuesday, September 1, 2026; flat-rate utilities were due March 31, 2026 with a 10% discount only if paid by the due date, and the City's discounted typical single-family metered utility total for 2026 is $2,251 per year (water $631, sewer $835, garbage/recycling/environmental $443, flood protection $342). https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm and https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/finance/utility-fees/utilityrates.htm
