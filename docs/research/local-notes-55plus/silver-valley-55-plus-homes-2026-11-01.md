## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. Silver Valley has no verified 55+ strata, so this matters here mainly when comparing against East Central's Fraserview Village buildings. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://choa.bc.ca/resources/strata-property-act/legislative-updates/
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Planning frame:** Silver Valley sits under the City of Maple Ridge Official Community Plan and the Silver Valley Area Plan, whose update Council adopted November 25, 2025. SOURCE NOTE on the OCP citation: the batch-12 research file cites OCP Bylaw 7998-2024 (adopted Jun 25, 2024, program-verified) while the East Central / Albion files in this series cite OCP Bylaw No. 7060-2014 with an OCP update adopted Nov 25, 2025 — the conflict is flagged here rather than resolved silently; confirm the current citation with the City before publication. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/official-community-plan
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019.** RS-1 subdivision minimums are 668.0 m² lot area, 18.0 m width and 27.0 m depth, maximum coverage 40%. Which zones apply to specific Silver Valley parcels: UNVERIFIED — check parcel by parcel. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **Suites and added units:** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws — the practical age-in-place route in a sub-area with no 55+ complex. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Development Permit Areas:** the live page claims eco-hamlet planning with mandatory wildfire interface, geotechnical slope and watercourse protection DPAs for Silver Valley — that is site copy in the research pass: the DPA specifics are UNVERIFIED on a City page opened for this file. Treat slope, watercourse and floodplain exposure as per-lot checks regardless. https://jaredgibbons.ca/silver-valley.html
- **Floodplain and ALR:** the City publishes North and South Alouette River and Fraser River floodplain maps; which Silver Valley lots are affected is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. ALR status is likewise per-parcel — one feed card states its acreage property is "not in ALR" in listing text only; check the Agricultural Land Commission's mapping for any specific lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ complex in Silver Valley itself?** No. No age-restricted strata, 55+ park, seniors rental or care residence with a Silver Valley address was verified in any source checked, and the sub-area's attached shelf recorded no sale at all in September 2026 (15 listings, ratio NA) — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: Where is the nearest actual 55+ ownership stock?** South-west, in East Central and Cottonwood MR: the four Fraserview Village strata buildings around 116 Avenue and 225–227 Streets, St. George's Village Co-op on Dewdney Trunk Road (55+, share purchase), plus the Alouette 55+ manufactured-home park (12868 229 St) and the Haney Pioneer Village Co-op (11830 230 St, 60+). https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/rosewood-court-22611-116-ave and https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata (in the city complexes)?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I age in place in a Silver Valley house and add a suite?** The standard route is one secondary suite per lot inside the house (maximum 40% of gross floor area, not in the floodplain area), with SSMUH paths to three, four or six units on eligible lots — verify the specific lot's eligibility, slope/watercourse permit exposure and floodplain status before valuing suite income, because the research file leaves the DPA specifics UNVERIFIED. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Q: Is there bus service if I stop driving?** Not verified for this sub-area: the one route statement in the research record rests on the wrong TransLink diagram, so no route is asserted — check the exact address on TransLink before relying on a stop. The rail anchors (Maple Meadows and Port Haney West Coast Express stations) are south-west of the sub-area in any case. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Route_Diagrams/194/r748.pdf and https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- **Q: Where is the hospital and urgent care?** Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, with 24/7 emergency care, toward the Town Centre; the Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 121 – 11900 Haney Place, open 7 days a week, 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital
- **Q: If I need care later, is there anything in Silver Valley?** No care or assisted-living residence in Silver Valley appears in BC's Assisted Living Registry; the registered Maple Ridge residences are all at downtown addresses, and the in-city ladder (Fraserview Village strata, 224 Street retirement residences, Zone 4 subsidized rentals) is an East Central / Town Centre ladder. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=carouselnext&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&page=0&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc
- **Q: Did the Silver Valley Area Plan update change anything for a buyer?** Council adopted the Silver Valley Area Plan update on November 25, 2025, and a feed card already markets an "Eco-Cluster Residential" designation in listing text — confirm the parcel's current designation and zone with the City before valuing subdivision or assembly potential; listing text is not the plan. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/official-community-plan and https://jaredgibbons.ca/silver-valley.html
