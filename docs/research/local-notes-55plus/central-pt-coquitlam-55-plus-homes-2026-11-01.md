# Central Port Coquitlam — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Central Port Coquitlam is the City of Port Coquitlam's downtown and central area, on the south side of the city between the Coquitlam River and Mary Hill; the City's Official Community Plan identifies Downtown as a mixed-use neighbourhood within this part of the city. Exact board sub-area boundary roads are UNVERIFIED. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- Shaughnessy Street is the main north–south street through the core and Wilson Avenue is its east–west civic street; the Port Coquitlam Community Centre and Terry Fox Library are at 2150 Wilson Ave. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/new-community-centre-brings-amenities-excitement-port-coquitlam
- Port Coquitlam Station (West Coast Express) is at 2125 Kingsway Avenue, just south of the downtown core. https://www.translink.ca/parkandride?utm_source=direct&utm_medium=shorturl&utm_campaign=parkandride
- Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) and the Mary Hill Bypass (Highway 7B) cross the city, and Coast Meridian Road carries traffic from downtown up to the north side. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- School District 43 (Coquitlam) serves the area; a School Locator run for 2150 Wilson Avenue returned Central Community Elementary, École Pitt River Middle and École Riverside Secondary. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- Where 55+ housing concentrates: this sub-area holds essentially all of Port Coquitlam's verified 55+ stock, within a few downtown blocks — Atkins Avenue (Mayfair Terrace), Kelly Avenue (Aspira Astoria and the Aspira Mayfair services frontage) and Mary Hill Road (Kew Lifestyles at Montrose), plus the Shaughnessy Seniors Community care home on Wilson Avenue. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw, 2008, No. 3630** is Port Coquitlam's zoning bylaw; Mayfair Terrace sits in a site-specific CD-19 comprehensive development zone created for that project. Downtown apartment zones are RA1 and RA2 under the same bylaw. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/3630-zoning-bylaw-2008pdf
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2013, No. 3838** identifies Downtown as a mixed-use neighbourhood and carries a "Non-Market and Special Needs Housing" policy section in §7.2. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- **Secondary suites:** the City permits secondary suites only in the RS-1, RS-2, RS-3 and RS-4 zones, and a building permit is required. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/secondary-suites
- **Suite utility-charge exemptions that matter to seniors:** the City waives 50% of the extra suite utility levy when the suite is occupied by a parent or grandparent of the owner, and 100% when a paid caregiver (minimum $150/month) lives in the suite to care for a permanently disabled owner or family member; applications are annual, due by Dec 31. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/utility-bills/secondary-suite-exemptions
- **Manufactured-home parks:** no source found for any manufactured-home park inside Port Coquitlam's city limits, so there are no park rules to apply in this sub-area. The parks that surface in searches (Hideaway Park, 4200 Dewdney Trunk Rd; Wildwood Park, 201 Cayer St) are both in the City of Coquitlam.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What can I actually buy that is 55+ in downtown PoCo?** One building: Mayfair Terrace, 2266 Atkins Ave — 46 strata units, built 2000, recorded as "Restricted Age: 55+". Everything else verified downtown is rental or care. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/mayfair-terrace-2266-atkins-ave
- **Q: Besides strata fees, is there a second mandatory monthly charge at Mayfair Terrace?** Yes. Listing information states a required base service plan on top of the strata fee — $715/mo for the first resident and $400 for a second (MLS R3179064, Oct 2026) — covering daily breakfasts plus 10 meals, weekly housekeeping, a 24-hour emergency response system, activities and transportation. Treat the figures as listing-stated and confirm the current amounts in the strata documents and service agreement. https://www.zolo.ca/port-coquitlam-real-estate/2266-atkins-avenue/311
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in Mayfair Terrace for a parent, or as an investment?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, and listings for Mayfair Terrace state rentals are allowed. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Mayfair Terrace?** Sources record pets allowed with restrictions, and current listings specify lap pets. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/mayfair-terrace-2266-atkins-ave and https://www.zolo.ca/port-coquitlam-real-estate/2266-atkins-avenue/311
- **Q: Is there a 55+ rental option if I don't want to buy?** Yes — Kew Lifestyles at Montrose, 2430 Mary Hill Rd: a five-storey, 56-unit building described by its architect as providing 55+ housing in downtown Port Coquitlam, completed 2023, beside the Community Centre. https://www.soarchitects.com/portfolio/kew-lifestyles-at-montrose-condominiums/
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Port Coquitlam has no hospital of its own. The Tri-Cities hospital is Eagle Ridge Hospital at 475 Guildford Way in Port Moody — a Fraser Health community hospital with a 24/7 emergency department. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital#.XSqzNyUTGEc
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** The West Coast Express stops at Port Coquitlam Station (2125 Kingsway Ave), but it is commuter-only: five trains in each direction on weekdays (mornings toward Vancouver, evenings toward Mission), with no weekend or holiday service, per TransLink's timetable effective Jan 5, 2026. SkyTrain does not reach Port Coquitlam, so local buses are the everyday option. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf and https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- **Q: Is downtown PoCo in a floodplain?** A significant portion of Port Coquitlam, including low-lying central land, is within the Fraser and Pitt River floodplain and protected by dyke infrastructure, per the City — flood status has to be checked lot by lot. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/port-coquitlam-reminds-residents-prepare-possible-flooding

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **There is no SkyTrain coming on any funded timeline.** TransLink's Transport 2050: 10-Year Priorities (approved June 2022) commits only to an "exploratory business case" for extending the Millennium Line to Port Coquitlam. As of the Mayors' Council's Feb 19, 2026 work plan, that exploratory business case is still simply "underway" — no route, no stations, no construction funding and no dates have been approved. https://roar-assets-auto.rbl.ms/files/53822/attachment-2-transport-2050-10-year-priorities-for-translink.pdf and https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/about-translink/governance-and-board/council-minutes-and-reports/2026/february/report--2026-mc-budget--feb-2026.pdf
- **The newest 55+ stock here is rental, not strata.** Kew Lifestyles at Montrose (56 units, 2430 Mary Hill Rd) completed in 2023 beside the Community Centre; no new age-restricted strata project appears in any source checked — the strata 55+ supply is still the single 46-unit Mayfair Terrace, built in 2000. https://www.soarchitects.com/portfolio/kew-lifestyles-at-montrose-condominiums/ and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/mayfair-terrace-2266-atkins-ave
- **The downtown already had its big rebuild.** The $132-million Port Coquitlam Community Centre (205,000 sq ft, 2150 Wilson Ave) fully opened Oct 2, 2021 — three arenas, leisure pool, fitness centre, Terry Fox Library and 425 underground parking stalls. It sits within walking distance of every downtown 55+ building in Section 2. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/new-community-centre-brings-amenities-excitement-port-coquitlam
- **Flood infrastructure is mid-upgrade right now.** The City's Cedar Drainage Pump Station project, in the dike system at the northeast end of the city, is in tendering with construction set for summer 2026 to summer 2027 and completion expected late summer 2027, funded partly by the Government of Canada. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/capital-projects/cedar-drainage-pump-station-0
- **Provincial small-scale multi-unit rules were folded into PoCo's bylaws in 2024.** The City's May 28, 2024 council report recommended amending Zoning Bylaw No. 3630 and OCP Bylaw No. 3838 to comply with Bill 44, allowing four to six units on most single-residential lots — which is why older RS-1 to RS-4 streets around the downtown are slowly adding suites and multiplexes near the 55+ cluster. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/ssmuh-staff-report-may-28-2024
