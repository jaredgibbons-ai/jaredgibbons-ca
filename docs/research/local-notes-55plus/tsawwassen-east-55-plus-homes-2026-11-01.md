# Tsawwassen East, Delta — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Tsawwassen East is a Paragon (FVREB MLS®) sub-area name for the Boundary Bay side of Tsawwassen, east of 56 Street, taking in Beach Grove and the Boundary Bay shore; it is not an official City of Delta neighbourhood name and the City publishes no boundary for it. The planning frame is the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), which consolidated the former area plans into a single Land Use Plan (the old Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Main local roads: Boundary Bay Road, 12 Avenue and 56 Street; Highway 17 is reached via 56 Street or 52 Street, then Highway 17A and Highway 99 via the George Massey Tunnel toward Richmond and Vancouver. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- Boundary Bay Regional Park and Centennial Beach, 570 Boundary Bay Rd (Metro Vancouver), with the Dyke Trail, Savannah and Raptor trails and Cammidge House, anchor the shore. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park
- School District 37 (Delta) catchments are address-based; for the sample address 570 Boundary Bay Rd the SD37 School Locator result is South Park Elementary (K–7), 735 Gilchrist Dr, and South Delta Secondary (8–12), 750 53 St, with no middle-school category. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- Tsawwassen Library (Fraser Valley Regional Library), 1321A 56 St, and South Delta Recreation Centre, 1720 56 St, serve the east side from the 56 Street corridor. http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/sport-recreation/indoor-facilities/south-delta-recreation-centre
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates: not on the east side's waterfront. The east side's one verified seniors residence is The Wexford, a rental retirement residence on 56 Street at 18 Avenue, across from South Delta Recreation Centre. Tsawwassen's verified 55+ ownership buildings are in Tsawwassen Central — see Section 2. Sources: the building entries below.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to 55 or older; age bylaws below 55 have been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). None of the east side's checked buildings carries even a verified 55+ bylaw, so a buyer here is buying general-market strata or freehold, with no age protection and no age restriction either way. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it** — under-55s can own in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside there; exemptions attach to a specified resident. (Why this matters on the east side: it is the rule a buyer would be relying on if they instead buy in Central's Lexington or Greene.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents in place when a 55+ bylaw passed; live-in caregivers; and from May 1, 2023, a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022 — every residential strata rental-restriction bylaw is invalid, with no amendment needed; short-term rental bylaws are still allowed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017.** The Beach Grove waterfront pocket is zoned RD2 (Duplex/Single Detached Residential 2), a zone the bylaw states is for waterfront lots in Beach Grove, with a 4,000 m² minimum lot size for subdivision (sample: 1728 Beach Grove Road). Section 6.2.3 of the bylaw sets a waterfront setback for lots fronting Boundary Bay along Beach Grove Road south of 16 Avenue, under which a seawall may encroach into the minimum front setback, and the bylaw states no Beach Grove flood construction level. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD2.pdf and https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024** applies, with Hazard Development Permit Area SD3 (Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands): no building with a habitable floor below 2.9 metres Geodetic Survey of Canada datum, a minimum land surface elevation around construction of 2.3 metres GSC datum, and no building within 7.5 metres of the natural boundary of a tidal area. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **"Seniors congregate housing" in Delta's zoning:** the Apartment Residential 6 (RA6) zone added to Bylaw No. 7600 lists seniors congregate housing as a principal use alongside apartment buildings, in six-storey development under the OCP's Neighbourhood Centres and Corridors designation — the mechanism by which a future east-side seniors building would most likely be zoned, since the current east-side stock has none. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **Foreshore tenure:** the Province owns nearly all saltwater foreshore; private moorage is held by lease or licence via FrontCounter BC under General Permission or Specific Permission — a Boundary Bay dock is tenure, not ownership. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
- **Property taxes and 55+ relief:** Delta's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026, with 5% penalties after that date and after September 2, 2026. The provincial regular property tax deferment loan program covers homeowners 55 and older, and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) covers seniors 65+. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
- **Subsidized seniors rental applications** for Delta run through BC Housing's Housing Registry (Zone 3 — Richmond and West Delta directory, May 2025). https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ condo I can buy on the east side or in Beach Grove?** None verified. Royal Oaks (1100 56 St), the Pillars (1700 56 St — building source expressly states no age restrictions) and Heron Cove (1706 56 St) are all general-market buildings. The verified buyable 55+ buildings are in Tsawwassen Central: The Lexington and Tsawwassen Greene. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/pillars-1700-56-street and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/the-lexington-1150-54a-street
- **Q: What is The Wexford, then — can I buy a suite there?** No. The Wexford, 1737 56 St, is a Bria Communities rental retirement residence with 65 all-inclusive independent living suites; you rent the suite and the services, you do not buy strata title. https://briacommunities.ca/communities/wexford/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55 — does that matter for an east-side purchase?** Not in the buildings checked here, because none carries an age bylaw. Where a 55+ bylaw does exist (Central's buildings), provincial law exempts a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident, plus defined caregiver and child cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out an east-side condo long-term?** Strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022, so a long-term rental ban cannot be enforced; a strata can still restrict short-term rentals, and the individual building's other bylaws (pets, occupancy) still apply — read the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What flood rules apply to a Beach Grove waterfront home?** Development Permit Area SD3 sets a 2.9 m GSC datum minimum habitable floor level, a 2.3 m minimum land elevation around construction, and a 7.5 m setback from the natural boundary of a tidal area; the shore also sits behind the Boundary Bay dike, which the City of Delta maintains as diking authority. Whether a specific lot is inside SD3 needs a lot-level check. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Q: Is the dock or moorage in front of a waterfront home part of the purchase?** The foreshore is provincial Crown land; private moorage is held under General Permission or Specific Permission (or a lease/licence) via FrontCounter BC, not owned with the upland lot. Verify the tenure paperwork, not just the physical dock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
- **Q: Where is the nearest care if I age in place on the east side?** The in-Tsawwassen ladder runs through Central: KinVillage's 68 publicly subsidized assisted-living suites and 100 long-term care beds (West Court), and The Waterford's 106 independent suites plus 36 licensed care suites at 1345 56 St. The Wexford on the east side is independent living only, per Bria's description. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/7/B/assisted-living-at-residence-at-kinvillage and https://briacommunities.ca/communities/waterford/
- **Q: Where is the hospital and urgent care?** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd in Ladner (Fraser Health), is the nearest hospital; the South Delta After-Hours Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 1826–4949 Canoe Pass Way in Tsawwassen. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- **Q: What bus serves Boundary Bay?** TransLink route 601 runs Boundary Bay trips via 12 Avenue, Boundary Bay Road, 3 Avenue and 67 Street to 1A Avenue, continuing via South Delta Exchange and Ladner Exchange to Bridgeport Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- **Q: Does a 55+ buyer get any tax break on an east-side house?** The provincial tools are age-based, not area-based: property tax deferment as a low-interest loan from age 55, and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in this region) from age 65, both for a principal residence. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The two "Heron" buildings are different buildings in different sub-areas, with opposite age answers.** Heron Cove (1706 56 St, Beach Grove) has no verified age bylaw; Heron Place (1153 54A St, Central) has an express "no age restrictions" statement in its building source — and it is Heron Place, not Heron Cove, that sits across from the 55+ Lexington. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/heron-place-1153-54-a-street
- **Beach Grove's zoning is a named waterfront zone, not standard urban zoning.** RD2 exists, in the bylaw's own words, for waterfront lots in Beach Grove, with a 4,000 m² subdivision minimum — the reason the pocket stayed detached houses while 56 Street got the condo buildings. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD2.pdf
- **There is a specific Beach Grove setback rule in the zoning bylaw.** Section 6.2.3 of Bylaw No. 7600 addresses lots fronting Boundary Bay along Beach Grove Road south of 16 Avenue and lets a seawall encroach into the minimum front setback; the bylaw states no Beach Grove flood construction level, which is why the SD3 permit-area numbers (2.9 m / 2.3 m GSC datum) are the ones to check instead. https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **The Wexford is part of a planned gateway node, not a converted building.** Bria describes The Wexford as part of Northgate, a mixed-use community at 18th Avenue and 56th Street with townhome blocks and a six-storey commercial building — and its event parking spills across 56 Street to South Delta Recreation Centre, which tells you how tight its own site is. https://briacommunities.ca/blogs/the-wexford-sales-centre-open-soon/
- **The east side's growth lands are Southlands, and they are not seniors product.** The City records the Southlands properties (Century Industries Ltd., File LU006390, Market Avenue) as a development area inside this sub-area; no 55+ or seniors component was verified in the sources checked. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/109911/
- **Delta's flood system is city-scale:** over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure maintained by the City — the dike in front of Beach Grove is one piece of that system, and lot-level SD3 status still has to be confirmed per parcel. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
