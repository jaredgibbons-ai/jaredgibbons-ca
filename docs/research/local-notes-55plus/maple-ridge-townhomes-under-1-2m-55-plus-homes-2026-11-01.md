## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; sub-55 bylaws invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). Note what this means for Maple Ridge's co-ops: the Strata Property Act does not govern a housing co-operative at all, so Haney Pioneer Village's 60+ bar is not a strata bylaw and is not touched by the 55+-only rule — it stands or falls on the co-op's own rules and the Human Rights Code, not on ss. 123.1/123.2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it** — for strata product (the Fraserview Village condos). People under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. These exemptions are strata-law exemptions; a co-op's or life lease's treatment of a younger spouse is set by its own documents: UNCONFIRMED for either Maple Ridge co-op from public sources. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022 (strata only); short-term rental bylaws are still allowed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Parks run on tenancy law, not strata law.** Alouette and Val Maria pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act; a sale is an assignment requiring the park owner's consent, and strata exemptions do not apply inside a park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **City bylaws: Zoning Bylaw 7600-2019; OCP Bylaw 7060-2014.** No Maple Ridge zoning or SSMUH rule is stated beyond the bylaw numbers — the research pass opened no City page (fetch 429, stopped): UNVERIFIED. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/maple-ridge-townhomes-under-1-2m.md
