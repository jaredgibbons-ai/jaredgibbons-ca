# Brighouse, Richmond — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Brighouse is Richmond's downtown core, around No. 3 Road, Minoru Boulevard and Westminster Highway; the City's planning name for the same core is "Brighouse Village", one of the villages in the City Centre Area Plan, described as the civic and mixed-use focus of the City Centre. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/10_overview23835.pdf and https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=51899
- Richmond–Brighouse Station, the Canada Line terminus with its bus exchange, sits in the middle of the village on No. 3 Road; CF Richmond Centre occupies the middle of Brighouse Village, its south part at 6551 No. 3 Road. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=51899
- Richmond City Hall is at 6911 No. 3 Road; Richmond Hospital is at 7000 Westminster Highway, at the north side of the city centre. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/health/about-bc-s-health-care-system/heath-care-partners/hospitallist.pdf
- Minoru Park, at the west side of the village, holds the City's civic recreation and culture complex; Richmond Public Library's Brighouse (Main) Branch is at 100-7700 Minoru Gate. https://rpl.yourlibrary.ca/about/hours-locations
- The Brighouse Village Specific Land Use Map (2031), amended by Bylaw 10190, sets transects of Urban Core T6 (45 m), Urban Centre T5 (25 m) and General Urban T4 (15 m), with the village centre at No. 3 Road and Cook Road. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=23858
- Where seniors housing actually concentrates: not in the No. 3 Road tower core. The verified seniors cluster is one block west of it, on Gollner Avenue / Minoru Boulevard south of Westminster Highway — Kiwanis Towers (7378 Gollner Ave) and Minoru Residence (7333 Gollner Ave) — beside Minoru Park and the Minoru Centre for Active Living at 7191 Granville Ave. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below, and https://richmond.ca/parks-recreation/centres/mcal.htm?PageMode=HTML
- No source found places any 55+ age-restricted strata (ownership) building anywhere in Brighouse — see the plain statement at the top of Section 2.

### Subsidized seniors rental (non-profit, income-based — not ownership)

- **Kiwanis Towers (Trafalgar Square) — 7378 Gollner Avenue (also addressed 7388 Gollner Avenue; formerly 6251 Minoru Boulevard).** 296 subsidized seniors rental units in two towers, owned by the Richmond Kiwanis Senior Citizens Housing Society. The City of Richmond describes it as 296 units of affordable seniors' rental housing at 7378 and 7388 Gollner Avenue, with shared parking, laundry, a fitness area, shared amenity space, patios, landscaped outdoor spaces, walking paths and community gardens; the City contributed approximately $24.1 million and BC Housing provided $16.3 million in long-term financing. https://RICHMOND.CA/services/affordable-housing/HousingProjects.htm and https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Kiwanis_CNCL_07101747840.pdf

Note the Society's stated age is 60+, not 55+. The Society's page stated it was not accepting applications at the time it was read. Year built: UNCONFIRMED in the sources above (the City disbursement reports run 2012–2017). Pet restrictions: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.richmondkiwanis.ca/trafalgar-square

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500** is the City of Richmond's zoning bylaw; the area research file notes Single Detached zones RS1/RS2 in Section 8.1 apply outside the City Centre plan designations. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **OCP: Richmond Official Community Plan Bylaw 9000** — the City states its 2041 OCP Bylaw 9000 comprises Schedule 1 (city-wide) and Schedule 2 bylaws containing the six Area Plans (including City Centre) and thirteen Sub-Area Plans; the City Centre Area Plan designates Brighouse Village for high-density, high-rise, mixed-use development. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm and https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=51899
- **Secondary suites (Richmond):** the City states it has adopted Zoning Bylaw amendments allowing a single secondary suite within a single-family detached home, and that for a suite to be legal it must be inspected and upgraded to BC Building Code and City bylaw requirements; suite rules sit in Zoning Bylaw 8500 (Section 5.4 per the area research file). https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/bylaws/property/residentalregs/suites.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **Flood rules that affect ground-oriented homes at the village edges:** new construction in Richmond is subject to flood construction levels under the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204, within a city-wide dike system designed for a 1:500 flood event. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ condo I can actually buy in downtown Richmond?** No verified one in Brighouse. No public source checked states a 55+ age-restriction bylaw for any Brighouse strata building, so a Brighouse "55+ homes" search is, on current evidence, a search with no ownership product in the core itself — the verified Richmond 55+ ownership buildings are outside Brighouse. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Kiwanis Towers looks like a condo tower — can I buy a unit there?** No. It is 296 units of subsidized seniors rental housing owned by the Richmond Kiwanis Senior Citizens Housing Society, at 7378 and 7388 Gollner Avenue — rental only, with income and asset eligibility. https://RICHMOND.CA/services/affordable-housing/HousingProjects.htm
- **Q: What age do I need to be for Kiwanis Towers?** The Society states 60 years or older, capable of living independently, with a co-applicant aged 50 or older permitted alongside a primary applicant who is 60 or older — it is a 60+ eligibility rule, not the strata-law 55+ rule. https://www.richmondkiwanis.ca/trafalgar-square
- **Q: My spouse is under the age limit. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Under provincial strata rules, yes in the defined exemption cases — a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption) is exempt, as are live-in caregivers, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children the resident cared for before 19. That is the strata-law answer; Kiwanis Towers has its own separate co-applicant rule above. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I buy in a 55+ building if I am under 55, as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted in BC strata law — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022 — but the tenant still has to satisfy any 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Where is the seniors centre?** The Seniors Centre at the Minoru Centre for Active Living, 7191 Granville Ave, in Minoru Park — the City offers programs for those 55+ at eight community facilities including this centre, and seniors facility passes are available for individuals 55+ (and spouses under 55). https://richmond.ca/parks-recreation/centres/mcal.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/about/seniors.htm
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Richmond Hospital, 7000 Westminster Highway, at the north side of the city centre; the Richmond City Centre Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at #110, 4671 No. 3 Road. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/richmond-city-centre-urgent-and-primary-care-centre/plan-your-visit
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day?** Richmond–Brighouse Station is the Canada Line terminus with the bus exchange in the village; TransLink's published timetable (effective September 7, 2026) gives 26 minutes from Richmond–Brighouse to Waterfront Station and 15 minutes to YVR–Airport Station (the latter requiring a transfer at Bridgeport). https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt991.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve Brighouse — does it matter for visiting grandchildren or resale?** Richmond Secondary School (7171 Minoru Boulevard) is in the village and Samuel Brighouse Elementary is the namesake elementary school; SD38 catchments are address-based, and on January 21, 2026 the Richmond Board approved the catchment for the new Odlin Crescent Elementary School, which redefines where city-centre students attend from September 2026 — check any specific address in the SD38 locator. https://boyd.sd38.bc.ca/news/2026/01/board-education-approves-catchment-new-city-centre-school

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The seniors cluster and the condo core are two different projects on one story.** Kiwanis Towers exists because the Kiwanis Society replaced an aging seniors complex on its nearly five-acre site with two concrete towers, financed in part by selling a portion of the site to Polygon, which also built three market condominium towers on its parcel — so the subsidized seniors towers and the market towers beside them were one combined development, with different tenure. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=42205
- **Kiwanis Towers was finished in two 148-unit towers.** City disbursement reports condition payments on completion of 148 seniors housing units in the first tower, then 148 in the second — 296 in total. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=42205
- **The mall in the middle of the village is planned for redevelopment.** The south part of CF Richmond Centre at 6551 No. 3 Road — roughly 11 ha (28 ac) that the City describes as a low-rise, automobile-oriented shopping centre — was redesignated in part under Official Community Plan Amendment Bylaw 9892, amending the Brighouse Village specific land use map around the redevelopment. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=51899
- **Airport rules cap some Brighouse heights.** The City's own Brighouse Village map notes maximum building height in parts of the village may be subject to established Airport Zoning Regulations. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=23858
- **Richmond property taxes run on a single July due date with two penalty steps** — due the first business day of July (Thursday, July 2, 2026), with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026 (city-wide Richmond rule). https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- **The 55+ programming hub is unusually large.** The Seniors Centre at Minoru Centre for Active Living opened to the public on March 11, 2019, as a 33,000 sq ft seniors centre for adults 55 and older inside a 110,000 sq ft facility with aquatic and fitness centres, in Minoru Park. https://www.seniorlivingmag.com/richmond-seniors-centre/
