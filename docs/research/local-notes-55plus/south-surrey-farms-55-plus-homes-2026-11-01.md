## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Farmland carries no age bylaw.** The ALR frame governs residences, not residents' ages: one principal residence at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; local bylaws may be more restrictive, never more permissive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **No strata subdivision in the ALR without ALC permission.** Depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR — ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6 — so a buyer cannot assume a farm parcel can be split into strata lots, 55+ or otherwise. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **For the townsite strata in Section 2 — only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The strata bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it,** with Regulation 7.01 exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** in strata since Nov 24, 2022; stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Farm classification is a separate, income-tested status.** BC Assessment's thresholds are $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha; a buyer who plans to keep farming must file the General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. Farm class says nothing about age — it is a tax classification, not a seniors designation. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Surrey zoning and OCP:** Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000 (the live page states farm land falls mainly under A-1 General Agriculture and A-2 Intensive Agriculture — site copy only; the A-1/A-2 footprint, minimum-lot and additional-farm-house specifics were NOT confirmed on an official City source in the research pass and are UNVERIFIED as verified rules) and Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026). https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-farms.md
- **Surrey suite cap (settled position in this program):** a secondary suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit — a "90 m²" cap is NOT the current Surrey suite rule; the 90 m² figure in the ALR frame above is the ALC's additional-residence limit, a different rule. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-farms.md
- **Property transfer tax (general rates):** 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% on the portion above $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% on the portion above $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. How PTT and GST split between land, buildings, residence and farm assets on a specific farm purchase: UNVERIFIED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a 55+ home on a South Surrey farm?** No verified product exists: no source states an age restriction for any farm parcel or acreage complex in this page's scope, and strata subdivision in the ALR needs ALC permission. The verified 55+ homes are the townsite strata in Section 2. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: Does farm classification make a property a seniors property?** No. Farm classification is a BC Assessment tax status tested against farm-income thresholds ($2,500 / $2,500 plus 5% / $10,000 by operation size). It has no age element at all. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: What homes can I have on an ALR farm parcel?** One principal residence up to 500 m², a suite only inside that residence, and at most one additional residence up to 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha), subject to stricter local bylaws. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: If I sell the farm and downsize, where is the nearest verified 55+ ownership stock?** The 152 Street corridor and Sunnyside Park set in Section 2 — Peninsula Village (2500 152 St), Semiahmoo Estates (2239 152 St), Bishop's Green (2533 152 St), St. Martin's Lane (15020 27A Ave) and 13965 16 Ave — plus The Crescent (3500 144 St) and The Greens at Douglas (350 174 St). Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a townsite 55+ strata?** Yes, under the strata framework — a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption) is exempt, as are children under 19 in the resident's care and certain adult children. That exemption system belongs to strata law; it does not apply to a farm residence, which has no age rule to be exempt from. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I build a second home on the farm for my parents?** Only inside the ALC frame: at most one additional residence, capped at 90 m² or 186 m² by parcel size, and only where the local bylaw allows it. Whether a specific parcel qualifies is a parcel-by-parcel check — UNVERIFIED for any specific South Surrey farm in this file. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Are there 55+ manufactured-home parks near the farm country?** Not in South Surrey: zero verified 55+ pad-rent parks in South Surrey, and the one verified 55+ manufactured community, Sea Ridge Bays (15875 20 Ave), is a bare-land strata in the townsite. Surrey's verified 55+ parks are in Fleetwood and Newton. ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/south-surrey-mobile-homes-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: What are South Surrey farms selling for?** No farm figure is stated in this file: SnapStats has no farm category, and the S Surrey White Rock residential figures in the research file are board context only, not farm, ALR-land or per-acre values. Farm counts, prices and per-acre values: UNVERIFIED. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-farms.md
- **Q: Is a specific farm parcel in the ALR, and what is its flood position?** Check parcel by parcel: the live page directs readers to the ALC's ALR Property and Map Finder by PID or civic address, and to City mapping for flood construction levels, which are set lot by lot. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The farms page's feed is broader than farms.** The research file records estate, rezoning-holding and land-banking cards from Elgin Chantrell, Morgan Creek and Grandview Surrey inside a feed titled "Farms" — a buyer filtering by the page title alone will price working-farm land against estate product. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-farms.md
- **Two different "90 m²" traps sit on this page's ground.** The ALC's 90 m² is an additional-residence cap on ALR land; Surrey's suite rule is 40% of the principal dwelling, not 90 m². Mixing them up misstates both rules. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-farms.md
- **The October 31 date runs a farm purchase.** A buyer who plans to keep farming a farm-classified property must file the BC Assessment farm-classification application by October 31 after purchase, or the classification — and its tax treatment — is at risk for the next year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Quota is never in this file, deliberately.** Whether any quota is included on a specific South Surrey farm, and any marketing-board transfer rule, is UNVERIFIED — no marketing-board source was opened in the research pass. Get any quota position in the contract, in writing, and verify it with the board directly. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-farms.md
- **Serpentine's filing crosses a municipal-research boundary.** The board files Serpentine under Cloverdale while the lowland itself is South Surrey's southern edge — farm research that follows only the slug or only the map will mis-file parcels on that seam. Standing correction for this series.
