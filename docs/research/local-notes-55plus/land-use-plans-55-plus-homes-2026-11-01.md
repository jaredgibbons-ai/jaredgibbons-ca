## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **The age rule is provincial and identical in all four cities.** Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; age bylaws below 55 invalid since Nov 24, 2022, under Bill 44); the bylaw restricts residency, not ownership; Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19; and residential rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, while short-term restrictions remain allowed. What differs by city is only where the plans let seniors buildings be built. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Surrey: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) replaced OCP Bylaw 18020.** The South Newton plan was rescinded by that update and is no longer in effect, and the East Newton NCP designations were superseded by it — read the current OCP and Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, not the old NCP documents. SSMUH in Surrey: a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Delta: OCP Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future"), adopted July 8, 2024** — a single consolidated plan with NO Schedule D.1 and no Tsawwassen or Ladner Area Plan (those were schedules of the superseded Bylaw No. 3950). Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 (2017) delivers seniors housing mainly through site-specific CD zones (The Waterford's CD276; South Pointe's CD98A), and its RA6 apartment zone lists seniors congregate housing as a principal use. SSMUH: up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide, subject to hazard/streamside permit areas. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/71542/ and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/ and http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Langley is two municipalities with two plan sets.** The Township of Langley is governed by Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 (Township material names Rural Zones RU-1 and RU-2 and Suburban Residential Zones SR-1 to SR-3); the City of Langley's zoning bylaw is Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300. A Langley 55+ search crosses both jurisdictions. The Township states 235 square kilometres of the Township is within the Agricultural Land Reserve, and ALR residences cannot be stratified — Langley's 55+ stock is urban product only. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Abbotsford: Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 defines "Congregate Housing"** — an independent living setting with common dining and amenity areas (definition added by Bylaw 3249-2022) — the use class the City's bylaw work associates with seniors supportive housing, assisted living and long-term care projects. Suites: Zoning Bylaw s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings, with criteria in City reports including a minimum 400 m² lot and 12.0 m frontage. Abbotsford's OCP bylaw number: not re-verified in this pass — UNCONFIRMED here. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-abbotsford-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/7217/8514/3396/d9117e5011287c79b090adc71e32c234_Appendix_B_-_Draft_ZB_Amendments.pdf and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140 and ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/abbotsford-west-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **ALR housing caps apply in all four cities' farm belts.** A principal residence built without an ALC application is capped at 500 m² total floor area with a secondary suite only inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence is permitted without an application at 90 m² or less (parcels of 40 ha or smaller) or 186 m² or less (larger parcels), where local bylaws also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property tax deferment is provincial and identical across the four cities:** the regular deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. Due dates and penalties are municipal — Delta's 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026 (5% penalty after, a further 5% after September 2, 2026); Surrey's are due July 2 (5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2, a further 5% after September 2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there one land use plan that covers Surrey, Langley, Abbotsford and Delta together?** No. Each municipality plans through its own Official Community Plan and zoning bylaw — there is no single shared "land use plan" document covering the four cities. (Verified for Delta and Abbotsford from primary sources in the research file; the framework statement is the research file's own finding.) ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/land-use-plans.md
- **Q: Which plan documents are already out of date?** Two big ones: Surrey's OCP Bylaw 18020 (replaced by the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026 — the South Newton plan and the East Newton NCP designations went with it) and Delta's OCP Bylaw No. 3950 (superseded by Bylaw No. 8400, 2024, which has no area-plan schedules). Any page or listing still citing them is citing a repealed plan. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton and https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: Does an OCP "seniors housing" designation make a building 55+?** No. The plan decides where seniors-oriented buildings may be built; the age restriction itself is a strata bylaw under the Strata Property Act (or, in a park, a park rule under tenancy law). Verify the age rule in the building's own bylaws and Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata in any of these cities?** Yes — the provincial exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident applies province-wide, with further defined exemptions for children and live-in caregivers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, in all four cities. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I add a suite or extra units to my house for a caregiver or family member?** City by city under SSMUH: Delta allows up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide (subject to hazard/streamside permit areas); Surrey allows 3–4 units by lot size and up to 6 near frequent bus stops; Abbotsford's zoning permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings (s.140.3.8, with lot criteria). On ALR land the provincial caps in Section 3 govern instead. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **Q: Where is each city's hospital?** Abbotsford Regional Hospital and Cancer Centre, 32900 Marshall Road; Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd (Ladner); Langley Memorial Hospital, 22051 Fraser Highway; Surrey Memorial Hospital is Surrey's Fraser Health hospital — its street address is UNCONFIRMED in this pass. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Abbotsford/abbotsford-regional-hospital-and-cancer-centre and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Langley/langley-memorial-hospital
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes at 55+ in any of these cities?** Yes — the Province's regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and it is provincial, so it works the same way in all four municipalities. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
- **Q: I'm downsizing from a farm in one of these cities. What limits the house or second dwelling on the land I'm leaving — or buying?** ALR rules: a principal residence built without an ALC application is capped at 500 m² with a suite only inside it, and one additional detached residence is allowed without an application at 90 m² or less (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² or less (larger parcels), where local bylaws also permit it. Farm classification also has continuing BC Assessment income thresholds. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Two of the four cities replaced their OCPs within two years.** Delta's Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) and Surrey's Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) both supersede the plans most existing web copy still describes — including Delta's old area-plan schedules and Surrey's neighbourhood plan designations for South Newton and East Newton. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- **The live land-use-plans page itself showed no editorial body in this program's fetch** — only the MLS® reciprocity boilerplate and the standard footer. What the rendered page contains (an editorial section, a feed, or both) is UNVERIFIED, unlike the Delta-only sibling page, which carries a full editorial guide and FAQ. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/land-use-plans.md
- **"Langley" in a plan search is always two municipalities.** The Township (Zoning Bylaw No. 2500) holds the farm belt and most suburbs; the City of Langley (Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300) is the compact urban core — and the verified 55+ stock splits across both. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx
- **Abbotsford's zoning already names the seniors use.** "Congregate Housing" — an independent living setting with common dining and amenity areas — was added to Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 by Bylaw 3249-2022, which is why Abbotsford's deep 55+ stock (26 verified strata complexes in this program) has a defined use class to be approved under. ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/abbotsford-west-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **Delta's RA6 zone is the newest seniors-housing route in the four cities.** Seniors congregate housing is a named principal use in Delta's Apartment Residential 6 zone, for six-storey development along arterial and collector streets — a shift from the site-specific CD rezonings that produced The Waterford (CD276) and South Pointe (CD98A). https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
