## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. There is no verified 55+ strata in Mission West for this to attach to today — it is the rule a buyer would meet in the city's two 55+ strata complexes in Section 2, and the only age bylaw a Mission West strata (for example, if Archer Green's bylaws were ever in question) could lawfully carry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** People under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; exemptions attach to a "specified resident." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. These are strata exemptions — they do not automatically apply inside a manufactured-home park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022 in stratas; SHORT-TERM rental bylaws are still allowed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home parks run on tenancy law, not strata law.** Pad tenancies in the Wilson Street parks fall under BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act: the resident owns the home and rents the pad; pad rent may be raised only once in 12 months on at least three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026, and 2.2% for 2027 on the Province's manufactured-homes rent-increase page) plus a proportional share of changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. A park's age rule is a park rule — neither Wilson Street park carries a verified one. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **City frame throughout:** Mission West is inside the City of Mission, so City planning, zoning and taxes govern. **Current plan: Official Community Plan Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026,** replacing and repealing OCP Bylaw 6350-2025 with the same vision, framework and mapping plus a Regional Context Statement. Live pages stating a different adoption date for this bylaw are not followed here. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026 and https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020** is Mission's zoning bylaw; rural zones such as RU16 carry a 1.6 ha minimum lot size in City notices. A given Mission West parcel's zone: per-parcel, UNVERIFIED in the research file. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning and https://www.mission.ca/media/file/notice-rezoning-bylaws-june-1-2026
- **Secondary suites and second dwellings (City rules):** a Mission secondary suite is a self-contained space inside a single family home; suites are not coach homes or garden cottages (detached secondary dwellings, which on rural lots generally require the secondary-dwelling "s" variant of the zone); suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes; all suites must be registered with the City, new suites go through building permit, and an annual secondary suite fee of $63 plus utility fees applies. The City's SSMUH framework — Bylaw 6299-2024-5949(163), adopted July 17, 2024 — is the small-scale multi-unit frame for former single-family/duplex zones. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program and https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh and https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-rsd25-4
- **Silverdale development claims are plan-level only.** The SCPA is designated for urban growth with a Central Neighbourhood Plan and Master Infrastructure Strategy underway, but development timing, densities and population claims for individual Silverdale listings in feed text (for example, "set to nearly double the population of Mission" or "2 to 10 units per acre") are listing copy only and are UNVERIFIED as facts for any specific parcel — check the in-force OCP designation and zoning for the PID. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies and https://jaredgibbons.ca/mission-west.html
- **Wells, septic, ALR and lake shoreline are the working rules on this ground:** wells are recorded in the provincial GWELLS database; onsite sewerage must comply with the Sewerage System Regulation, including the 30 m setback from a well unless professional advice is obtained; ALR status is per-parcel (ALR parcels follow the ALC frame of one principal residence to a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha)); and Silvermere Lake shoreline claims need title confirmation, because no official source in the research file confirms dock, moorage or water-access rights for any Mission West parcel. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo104/loo104/22_326_2004 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Property taxes:** Mission West properties are inside the City of Mission and pay the City. City of Mission 2026 taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a flat 10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding (including unclaimed home owner grants) on July 3, 2026, under Community Charter ss.233–234 and Municipal Tax Regulation, B.C. Reg. 426/2003, s.3 — Mission has NO alternative-scheme bylaw, and the rural 5% + 5% regime under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11 does NOT apply on this ground. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/tax-rates
