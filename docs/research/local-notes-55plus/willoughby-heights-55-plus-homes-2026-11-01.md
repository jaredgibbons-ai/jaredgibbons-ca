# Willoughby Heights, Township of Langley — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Willoughby Heights is the board's sub-area name; the Township's official planning name for the area is "Willoughby." The Willoughby Community Plan Area in Bylaw No. 6201 is bordered by the City of Surrey municipal boundary to the southwest, Highway 1 to the north, the Trans Mountain Pipeline ridgeline / Lower Mainland Regional Park and 68 Avenue to the south, and 216 Street and the Willowbrook Connector to the east. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/connect/resources/get-involved/engage-tol/2026/willoughby-community-plan-update/6201-Schedule-1.pdf
- This is Langley's newest large community and its attached-housing centre: in September 2026 the sub-area carried 369 attached listings against 100 detached listings, the deepest attached selection in Langley (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, cited in the Willoughby Heights research file, ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/willoughby-heights.md).
- Where the older, age-restricted stock actually sits: not in the new Transit Corridor or Town Centre product, but at the area's southwest edge, on 197 Street and 64 Avenue near the Willowbrook / Langley City side — Willow Park Estates at 6467 197 St and Highgate Terrace at 19645 64 Ave. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Main streets: 200 Street, 208 Street, 80 Avenue (Willoughby Way), 72 Avenue, 76 Avenue, 84 Avenue and 86 Avenue; Highway 1 runs along the north boundary with interchanges at 200, 208 and 216 Streets. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/connect/resources/get-involved/engage-tol/2026/willoughby-community-plan-update/6201-Schedule-1.pdf
- Hubs: Langley Events Centre, 7888 200 Street, and the Carvolth Exchange area at 200 Street and Highway 1; Muriel Arnason Library is at 130 - 20338 65 Avenue. https://www.tol.ca/en/arts-culture-recreation/langley-events-centre.aspx and https://fvrl.bibliocommons.com/locations/WB
- Schools physically in Willoughby include Donna Gabriel Robins Elementary, 7844 204B Street, and Lynn Fripps Elementary, 21020 83 Avenue; Township statements also name Willoughby Elementary, Yorkson Creek Middle School and R.E. Mountain Secondary as Willoughby schools. Catchments are address-based through the SD35 School Locator. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
- Transit named in the research file: route 501 (Surrey Central to Langley Centre via Fraser Highway and 200 Street), route 372 (Clayton Heights to Langley Centre via 72 Avenue) and route 595 (Langley Centre to Maple Ridge via the Golden Ears Bridge). ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. This is the test for Highgate Terrace above: its building source says "age restrictions" without a number — if the registered bylaw sets any age other than 55, it cannot be valid in that form today. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. Read Willow Park Estates' listing wording ("at least one owner must be 55," 2 occupants maximum) against these exemptions: a listing summary is not the bylaw, and the bylaw cannot remove the regulation's exemptions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why the "rentals not allowed" lines recorded for both Willow Park Estates and Highgate Terrace cannot be taken at face value for a long-term rental. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Township zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 2500** governs Willoughby. Its Residential Compact Lot Zone R-CL (s. 403) is the area's compact-lot pattern: under s. 403.7, R-CL lots run from a minimum lot area of 220 m² to a maximum of 464.5 m², with minimum frontage of 11 m for front-loaded lots and 8.2 m for rear-loaded lots. Neither Willow Park Estates nor Highgate Terrace is an R-CL product — both predate the compact-lot build-out (1987 and 1990). https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **Plans:** Bylaws No. 6201, 6202 and 6203 replaced the Willoughby Community Plan and created the Willoughby Transit Corridor Plan and the Gordon Neighbourhood Plan; the Transit Corridor is the area's high-density spine, and none of that new plan product is age-restricted in any source checked. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/connect/resources/get-involved/engage-tol/2026/willoughby-community-plan-update/6201-Schedule-1.pdf
- **Assisted living is a regulated care product, not a tenancy product a buyer shops for like a condo:** Langley Seniors Village is registered with BC's Assisted Living Registry (class SN), and Fraser Health states its 64 units are publicly subsidized — access runs through Fraser Health, not through a listing. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/D/8/assisted-living-at-langley-seniors-village
- **Property taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes and utility fees were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to current-year taxes unpaid at midnight July 2, 2026, with a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026, and utility fees are now billed separately. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Willoughby?** One verified complex: Willow Park Estates, 6467 197 St — a 64-unit townhouse complex built in 1987, described in current listings as a 55+ community. Highgate Terrace at 19645 64 Ave is age-restricted on its building record, but no source checked states the age as 55, so treat it as UNCONFIRMED 55+ until the registered bylaws are read. Everything else attached in Willoughby is general-market. https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/6467-197-street/46 and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/highgate-terrace-19645-64-avenue
- **Q: Why is there so little 55+ stock in Langley's biggest attached market?** Because the stock is new: Willoughby carried 369 attached listings in September 2026, almost all of it recent general-market condo and townhome product built for first-time buyers, families and investors, while its two older low-rise complexes at the southwest edge (1987 and 1990) are the only ones carrying age restrictions in any source. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/connect/resources/get-involved/engage-tol/2026/willoughby-community-plan-update/6201-Schedule-1.pdf
- **Q: Do both of us have to be 55 at Willow Park Estates?** A listing states the rule as "at least one owner must be 55" with a maximum of 2 occupants, and provincial strata rules in any case exempt a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement. Get the registered bylaw wording before writing — the listing form and the bylaw form are not the same document. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3092302-46-6467-197-street-langley-british-columbia-v2y1k7-435000/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy at Willow Park Estates for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a Willow Park Estates unit?** The building source records "rentals not allowed," but long-term strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. Confirm the current position from the bylaws and Form B. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/willow-park-estates-6467-197-street and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Willow Park Estates?** Current listing data records cats OK and dogs OK, number limit two, with restrictions; an earlier listing recorded one cat or one dog to a maximum weight — SOURCE CONFLICT between listings on the pet rule, flagged here. The registered pet bylaw governs; check it in the strata documents. https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/6467-197-street/46 and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3092302-46-6467-197-street-langley-british-columbia-v2y1k7-435000/brochure
- **Q: Is Langley Seniors Village something I can buy or rent on the open market?** No. It is a registered assisted-living residence at 20363 65 Ave with 64 publicly subsidized assisted-living units, operated by Retirement Concepts; subsidized assisted living is accessed through Fraser Health assessment. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/D/8/assisted-living-at-langley-seniors-village
- **Q: Is Chartwell Langley Gardens in Willoughby?** No — it is at 8888 202 St in Walnut Grove, north of Highway 1, and Willoughby's plan area is bounded by Highway 1 to the north. It is a continuum-of-care rental residence (independent living, assisted living, memory living and long-term care), not a Willoughby purchase option. https://chartwell.com/en/bc/langley/langley-gardens
- **Q: What transit serves the 197 St / 64 Ave corner?** The research file names routes 501, 372 and 595 as the area's routes, with the Carvolth Exchange at 200 Street and Highway 1 as the hub; a route-by-route stop for the 6467 197 St address itself is UNCONFIRMED from the sources checked — verify the exact stop on TransLink before promising a walk-to-bus time. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt501.pdf
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. In a sub-area with only one verified 55+ complex, that also means very little direct competition when a unit does list. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Willoughby's 55+ stock predates Willoughby.** Willow Park Estates (1987) and Highgate Terrace (1990) were built decades before the Transit Corridor and Town Centre build-out that defines the area today — a buyer expecting "new Willoughby" form in a 55+ complex will not find it; the age-restricted product here is 1980s–1990 low-rise at the area's southwest edge, near Willowbrook. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/willow-park-estates-6467-197-street and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/highgate-terrace-19645-64-avenue
- **The name is spelled two ways in the records.** Listings and the building source alternate between "Willow Park Estates" and "Willowpark Estates" for the same 6467 197 St complex — a name-only search under one spelling will miss listings filed under the other. https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/6467-197-street/46
- **Highgate Terrace's public record contradicts itself.** One building source gives both 22 units and, through the strata-plan listings at the address, two plans totalling a 58-unit figure — and it records an age restriction with no age attached. Until the strata plan and registered bylaws are pulled, neither a unit count nor a "55+" label for this building should be published as fact. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/highgate-terrace-19645-64-avenue
- **The richest 55+ townhouse supply near Willoughby is not in Willoughby.** Walnut Grove, directly across Highway 1, holds Churchill Park (147 units), Spencer Green (97 units), Chartwell Green and Chelsea Green, all marketed as 55+ — search engines and listing portals routinely blur the two sub-areas because they share the 208 Street / 88 Avenue corridor. https://www.tmbrealestate.com/walnut-grove-townhomes/churchill-park and https://www.tmbrealestate.com/walnut-grove-townhomes/spencer-green
- **Willoughby's one in-area seniors institution is care, not housing product.** Langley Seniors Village at 20363 65 Ave sits on the 65 Avenue corridor inside the plan area, but its 64 units are publicly subsidized assisted living — it will appear in every "Willoughby seniors housing" search while offering nothing a buyer can purchase. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/D/8/assisted-living-at-langley-seniors-village
- **The plan update changed what will be built next door, not the age rules.** Bylaws 6201–6203 replaced the Willoughby Community Plan and created the Transit Corridor Plan and the Gordon Neighbourhood Plan — a buyer at the older 197 St / 64 Ave complexes is buying beside a corridor planned for high-density general-market growth, with no age-restricted project named in the plan material checked. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/connect/resources/get-involved/engage-tol/2026/willoughby-community-plan-update/6201-Schedule-1.pdf
