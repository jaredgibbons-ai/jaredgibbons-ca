# Upper Lonsdale — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**⚠️ TWO MUNICIPALITIES — READ THIS FIRST:** Upper Lonsdale straddles the City of North Vancouver and the District of North Vancouver. Lonsdale Avenue from about 25th to 29th Street is in the **City**; 29th Street north to Rockland Avenue (the top of Lonsdale) is in the **District**. The 29th Street line is the working boundary — and it runs straight through this area's 55+ cluster: Somerset Green sits south of West 29th Street in the City, while Vista 29 and Chartwell Churchill House sit on the District side of the same block. Taxes, zoning, OCP and permit desk follow the municipality, not the street. Confirm any specific address on municipal mapping. - The sub-area runs up Lonsdale Avenue from about 24th/25th Street to Rockland Avenue, where Lonsdale ends. TransLink's North Shore Area Transit Plan jurisdiction table corroborates the split on the avenue itself: it assigns the Lonsdale Avenue at 29th Street layover to City jurisdiction and the Upper Lonsdale layovers at Osborne Avenue and at Prospect/Rockland to District jurisdiction. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/plans-and-projects/area-planning/north-shore/phase1/nsatp_phase1_app7.pdf

- The commercial node is Queensdale, at Lonsdale Avenue and Queens Road — one of the six Village Centres designated in the District's OCP, described as providing a range of shops and services for daily needs. Full-service shopping is down the hill in Central Lonsdale. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- Schools serving the sub-area are in School District 44 for both municipalities: Westview Elementary and Larson Elementary (K–7; Larson, at 2605 Larson Road, is dual-track English and French Immersion), École André-Piolat (francophone K–12, 380 West Kings Road), and Carson Graham Secondary (2145 Jones Avenue) for most addresses. Catchments are address-based — easternmost addresses should be checked against the Argyle boundary with SD44. https://www.sd44.ca/school/larson/About/ and https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/school/09344004
- Transit: TransLink Route 230 runs the full length of Lonsdale Avenue from Lonsdale Quay to the top of Upper Lonsdale and down Rockland; the SeaBus crosses from the Quay to Waterfront Station in 12 minutes, every 15 minutes during the day and every 10 minutes at peak. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt230.pdf and https://www.translink.ca/translink/schedules-and-maps/seabus
- Highway 1 (Upper Levels) interchanges at Lonsdale and Westview give east–west highway access. No official drive-time figure is published — drive times UNCONFIRMED. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/upper-lonsdale.md)
- Where the 55+ housing actually concentrates: in one block only — the 100 block of West Queens Road / West 29th Street at Chesterfield Avenue, at the Queensdale node. All of the area's verified 55+ and seniors stock (Somerset Green, Vista 29, Chartwell Churchill House) sits in that block, which the District's own planning record designated for seniors' housing (see Section 5). The rest of Upper Lonsdale is predominantly single-detached houses climbing the slope, with no other 55+ complex verified. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 25+, 40+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. The Province's page states: "As of November 24, 2022, strata corporations can no longer have bylaws which restrict residents to other ages." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states: "People younger than age 55 can own strata lots in an age 55+ restricted strata corporation but they may not be able to live in the strata lot. Age-restriction bylaws apply to the residents of the strata lot, not the ownership." Listings for both in-area buildings phrase it the same way — Somerset Green: "one resident must be 55+"; Vista 29: "One owner to be 55+" (listing wording varies; the registered bylaws and, at Vista 29, the 55+ occupancy covenant govern). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/council_reports/512271.pdf
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents who were lawfully living in the unit when the 55+ bylaw passed; live-in caregivers for a resident dependent due to illness, disability or frailty; and family exemptions for a specified resident — a younger spouse or partner, a child under 19 in the specified resident's care, and a person 19 or older (e.g. an adult child) whom the specified resident cared for before that person turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw. This is why older building-data pages that still show "rentals not allowed" for area buildings (Kings Court included) are out of date. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Extra layer at Vista 29 — a covenant, not just a bylaw.** The District's 2004 approval for the Vista 29 site included, as item 9 of the application, "a restrictive covenant to limit occupancy to households where one member is 55 years of age or older," alongside the CD 52 comprehensive development zoning. A buyer should have the covenant on title reviewed as well as the strata bylaws — the two are separate instruments. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/council_reports/512271.pdf
- **CITY rules (south of 29th Street — Somerset Green block):** the City of North Vancouver's Official Community Plan is the Official Community Plan, 2014, No. 8400, amended December 8, 2025 (Amendment Bylaw 2025, No. 9114) for provincial housing requirements. On Dec 8, 2025 the City also adopted the Ground Oriented (GO) Zone, increasing permitted units from 3 to 4 on nearly 4,300 residential properties and allowing up to 6 units per lot on approximately 600 properties. Secondary suites have been legal in all City single-family dwellings since 1993 — one suite per property, owner must reside at the property — and a suite may be combined with an accessory coach house, which requires a front lot line of at least 10 m (32.81 ft) plus a Development Permit and a Building Permit. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Official-Community-Plan-2014-No-8400-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9114-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-t.pdf and https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/HTO-Year-2-Progress-Report-July-1,-2025-June-30,-2026.pdf and https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Permits-Inspections/building-permits/secondary-suites and https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Accessory-Coach-Houses/Coach-House-Information-Handout.ashx
- **DISTRICT rules (29th Street north — Vista 29 / Churchill House block and the slope above):** the District's Official Community Plan is Bylaw 7900; it designates Queensdale as a Village Centre and directs growth to its centres while preserving established low-density neighbourhoods, which covers the District portion of Upper Lonsdale. District zoning has permitted a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary since June 2024. Slope is the defining hazard condition: the District designates Natural Hazard Development Permit Areas and publishes property-level GeoWeb hazard reports, so steep upper-Lonsdale lots should be checked before additions or rebuilds. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-single-family-residential-1-5.pdf
- **Taxes differ by side of the line.** Both municipalities set 2026 property taxes due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026. City 2026 residential total rate: 3.34028 per $1,000 of assessed value, plus a separate residential storm drainage rate of 0.10697 per $1,000. District 2026 residential total rate: 3.28195 per $1,000 (municipal portion 1.83419). Same street, different bills — confirm which municipality the specific unit is taxed in. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/City-Finances/2026/2026_TAX_RATE_SUMMARY_CITY_OF_NORTH_VANCOUVER-2.PDF and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://www.cnv.org/home-property/property-taxes
- **School district is the same on both sides:** School District 44 (North Vancouver) serves both the City and District blocks — the municipal split does not change the school district, only the catchment, which is address-based. https://www.sd44.ca/school/larson/About/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

**Q: Which buildings in Upper Lonsdale can I actually buy into as 55+?**
A: Two verified: Somerset Green, 121 West 29th St (143 units per a District council report; City side of 29th Street), and Vista 29, 188 West 29th St (50 units, plan BCS2494, built 2007; District side). Both appear on the North Shore list of buildings carrying a 55+ bylaw, and current listings for both state a 55+ occupancy rule. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/council_reports/512271.pdf and https://dyhx7is8pu014.cloudfront.net/www.thereferraladvantage.com/homes/409381/feature.pdf?mod=1611854321 and https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/on-the-market/55-buildings-north-vancouver/

**Q: The two buildings face each other — are they in the same municipality?**
A: No. Somerset Green is south of West 29th Street and is described in the District's own report as a seniors' condominium development "in the City of North Vancouver"; Vista 29 was rezoned by the District (CD 52) on the block between West Queens Road and West 29th Street. Different OCP, zoning bylaw, permit desk and tax rate apply to each — City total residential rate 3.34028 vs District 3.28195 per $1,000 for 2026. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/council_reports/512271.pdf and https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/City-Finances/2026/2026_TAX_RATE_SUMMARY_CITY_OF_NORTH_VANCOUVER-2.PDF and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf

**Q: Do I have to be 55 to buy in a 55+ building, or only to live there?**
A: Only to live there. The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the bylaw applies to residents, not ownership. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

**Q: Can my younger spouse or partner live with me in a 55+ strata?**
A: Yes — Strata Property Regulation 7.01 requires exemptions for a younger spouse/partner of a specified resident, a child under 19 in that resident's care, an adult child the resident cared for before age 19, live-in caregivers, and legacy residents who lived in the unit when the bylaw passed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

**Q: Is Vista 29's 55+ rule only a strata bylaw?**
A: No — it has a second layer. The District's approval for the site included a restrictive covenant limiting occupancy to households where one member is 55 or older, in addition to the strata's own bylaws. Have both reviewed on a purchase. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/council_reports/512271.pdf

**Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?**
A: Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw (or an exemption) — and at Vista 29, the occupancy covenant as well. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation

**Q: Is Chartwell Churchill House something I can buy?**
A: No. It is a private-pay rental retirement residence at 150 West 29th St, registered with the Assisted Living Registry (operator Chartwell Master Care LP, class SN), offering independent living and assisted living in rental suites — not strata ownership. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B1%5D=city%3APort%20Coquitlam&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_residence_type&sort=asc and https://chartwell.com/bc/north-vancouver/churchill-house

**Q: Is there any subsidized or non-profit seniors rental in Upper Lonsdale?**
A: None verified. No Upper Lonsdale address appears as a subsidized seniors building in BC Housing's Zone 1 seniors directory (May 2025) in the sources checked; the Kiwanis subsidized buildings are in Central Lonsdale, Lower Lonsdale and Lynn Valley. https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-1-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

**Q: What amenities do the two ownership buildings have?**
A: Somerset Green listings record an indoor pool, hot tub, workshop, gym, library, games room, lounge, courtyard gardens and 2 guest suites. Vista 29 listings record a club/amenity room, guest suite, workshop, secure underground parking, 13 visitor stalls and a storage locker. Confirm the current amenity list and any guest-suite charge against the strata documents — one Vista 29 listing states a $35-per-night guest suite, which is listing-stated only. https://www.zolo.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate/121-west-29th-street/403 and https://www.zolo.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate/188-west-29-street/208

**Q: How do I get downtown or to the hospital without driving up and down the hill?**
A: Route 230 buses run the full length of Lonsdale Avenue past the 29th Street block, down to Lonsdale Quay, where the SeaBus crosses to Waterfront Station in 12 minutes. For hospital access, Chartwell — in the same block as both strata buildings — states its residence is five minutes from Lions Gate Hospital. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt230.pdf and https://www.translink.ca/translink/schedules-and-maps/seabus and https://chartwell.com/bc/north-vancouver/churchill-house

**Q: Are pets allowed in the 55+ buildings?**
A: Listings for both buildings describe them as pet friendly (Somerset Green listings also record cats OK in the listing data). The operative limit is each strata's registered bylaws — confirm the number/size rules on the Form B for the specific unit. https://www.zolo.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate/121-west-29th-street/403 and https://www.zolo.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate/188-west-29-street/208

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The whole 55+ cluster exists because one block was designated for it.** The District's North Lonsdale–Delbrook Community Plan designated all of the 100 block of West Queens Road / West 29th Street for seniors' or empty-nester multi-family development at a maximum density of 36.3 units per acre — which is why the area's only three seniors buildings (Somerset Green just across the City line, Vista 29, and the assisted-living site that became the Churchill House block) all sit within a few hundred feet of each other at Queensdale, and why no other block in Upper Lonsdale has any. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/council_reports/512271.pdf
- **Vista 29 was purpose-built as 55+, not converted.** The District's Dec 22, 2004 report shows the project was designed from the start for ageing in place: units built to "Level 2" adaptable design guidelines (one suite to Level 3), wheelchair movement through doorways, kitchens and bathrooms, intruder alarms with panic buttons in the suites, corridor surveillance cameras, and facilities for charging scooters — with the 55+ occupancy covenant written into the rezoning. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/council_reports/512271.pdf
- **Somerset Green has two addresses.** The complex is listed as 121 West 29th St, but units are also addressed as 2800 Chesterfield Ave — a listing for 201–2800 Chesterfield Ave describes the same Somerset Green building. Searching only one address misses listings under the other. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3120637-201-2800-chesterfield-avenue-north-vancouver-british-columbia-v7n4m1-735000/brochure
- **Somerset Green's build year is not consistent across sources.** Most current listings state 1987; at least one current listing (MLS R3117942) states 1989. Its unit count in the District's 2004 report is 143 — a figure most listing copy never mentions. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/council_reports/512271.pdf and https://www.robbiejohal.com/listing/r3117942-121-w-29-street-322-north-vancouver-bc-v7n-4l6/brochure
- **The seniors' services anchor for the City-side blocks is brand new — and in Central Lonsdale, not here.** The new Silver Harbour Seniors' Activity Centre at 2325 St Georges Ave, beside the new Harry Jerome Community Recreation Centre (main entrance 130 East 23rd St), opened July 25, 2026; the City records it as offering programs and services for adults 55+. It is a short trip down the hill from the 29th Street buildings, but it is not in Upper Lonsdale. https://www.cnv.org/City-Hall/News-Room/News-Releases/2026/6/3/New-HJCRC-and-Silver-Harbour-Seniors%E2%80%99-Activity-Centre-to-Open-July-25,-2026
- **The District-side recreation anchor is the other direction.** The Delbrook Community Recreation Centre at 851 West Queens Road is the North Vancouver Recreation & Culture centre serving the District blocks, with a main pool and a leisure pool plus indoor racquet facilities — west along Queens Road from the 29th Street cluster. http://www.nvrc.ca/facilities-fields/accessibility
- **Slope rules, not just slope views, come with the upper blocks.** The District designates Natural Hazard Development Permit Areas and publishes property-level GeoWeb hazard reports; on the steep streets above Queensdale, a geotechnical check can add cost to additions and rebuilds in a way the flat Central Lonsdale blocks never face. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-single-family-residential-1-5.pdf
- **The 29th Street tax difference is small but real, and it is permanent.** For 2026 the City total residential rate is 3.34028 per $1,000 (plus 0.10697 storm drainage) against the District's 3.28195 — two otherwise comparable 55+ units across West 29th Street from each other are taxed by different municipalities at different rates, on the same July 2 due date. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/City-Finances/2026/2026_TAX_RATE_SUMMARY_CITY_OF_NORTH_VANCOUVER-2.PDF and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
