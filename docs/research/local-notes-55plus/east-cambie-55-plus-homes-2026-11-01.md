## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** People under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed; live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** Since Nov 24, 2022 no strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw; stratas may still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500.** The documented base zoning on Cambie Road is Single Detached (RS1/E) under Section 8.1 — minimum lot area 550.0 m², minimum width 18.0 m, minimum depth 24.0 m, one principal dwelling per lot, secondary suite as a permitted secondary use; multiple-family sites in East Cambie are Development Permit Areas under the Area Plan. https://Richmond.Ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf and https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=558
- **Plan: the East Cambie Area Plan is Schedule 2.11B to the Official Community Plan** (adopted October 21, 2002; Land Use Map updated by Bylaw 9874), under the city-wide 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000. The plan anticipates townhouse or apartment densities along some major roads — a City application already documents an RS1/E-to-RTL4 townhouse rezoning at 11671/11691 Cambie Road. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm and https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/_1_App_11671_11691_CambieRd46833.pdf
- **Suites / SSMUH:** the City allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot on covered lots, with Bill 44 minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Flood — Richmond is a dike-protected island city:** Richmond is made up of islands within the Fraser River floodplain at an average of 1 metre above sea level; the flood protection system includes 49 km of dikes, is designed for a 1:500 event (0.2% chance in any year), and dike raising from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres is under way. All lands in Richmond are designated flood plain under Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw No. 8204, which sets flood construction levels (FCLs) habitable floors must meet unless exempt; the Area Plan requires new East Cambie development to provide adequate flood protection. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **ALR edge:** the planning area abuts the Agricultural Land Reserve along No. 6 Road and Westminster Highway, and Richmond Nature Park is within the ALR; a specific lot's ALR status is a per-address check on Agricultural Land Commission maps. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=558 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** (Zone 3: Richmond and West Delta directory, May 2025) — that is how Knightsbridge II fills units. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in East Cambie?** None that any source verifies. No age-restricted strata ownership building with an East Cambie address was found in building-data sites, BC Housing directories or the Assisted Living Registry. The area's housing base is freehold detached houses under RS1 zoning, with strata townhouse/apartment forms only anticipated by the Area Plan along major roads. https://Richmond.Ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf and https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=558
- **Q: A listing says a Cambie Road townhouse is "55+." Should I believe it?** Not without the bylaws. The Cambie Road complexes that can be checked — Cambie Gardens, 11331 Cambie Road — are described in listings as family townhouse product with no age restriction stated. Since Nov 24, 2022 the only enforceable strata age bylaw in BC is 55+, and it has to be in the registered bylaws; ask for the bylaws and Form B. https://www.zolo.ca/richmond-real-estate/11331-cambie-road/1 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What seniors housing does exist in East Cambie?** One verified building: Knightsbridge II, 12571 Cambie Rd — a non-profit subsidized seniors rental in BC Housing's Zone 3 directory, applied for through the Housing Registry. It is rental, not a purchase. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we live in a BC 55+ strata if one existed here?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in the defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. Under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a 55+ parent can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is East Cambie in a floodplain?** All of Richmond is designated flood plain under Bylaw No. 8204, behind a 49 km dike system designed for a 1:500 event, with dike raising from 3.5 m to 4.7 m under way; habitable floors must meet the lot's flood construction level unless exempt, and the Area Plan requires new development here to provide adequate flood protection. Check the FCL lot by lot. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital and urgent care?** Richmond Hospital, 7000 Westminster Highway, is the city's hospital; the Richmond East Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 95-10551 Shellbridge Way, on the area's east side. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/health/about-bc-s-health-care-system/heath-care-partners/hospitallist.pdf and https://news.gov.bc.ca/30577
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day?** The confirmed timetable service is the 430 Express at Garden City Road and Cambie Road, at the area's west edge, between Richmond-Brighouse Station and Metrotown Station. The full route list on Cambie Road and No. 5 Road is UNVERIFIED — do not promise a specific bus the timetable does not support. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt430.pdf
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in East Cambie?** No assisted-living residence or care home with an East Cambie address was verified; Richmond's registered residences are in the City Centre (e.g. Courtyard Gardens, 7051 Moffatt Rd; Minoru Residence long-term care, 7333 Gollner Ave), and subsidized independent living in-area is Knightsbridge II through the Housing Registry. https://verveseniorliving.com/courtyard-gardens/ and https://www.vch.ca/en/location-service/long-term-care-minoru-residence

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The "55+" supply story here is a zoning story:** the Area Plan's residential base is single-detached RS1/E (550 m² minimum lots) with townhouse/apartment density only anticipated along some major roads, and multiple-family sites are Development Permit Areas — which is why no purpose-built 55+ strata has ever been the product on offer in this planning area. https://Richmond.Ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf and https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=558
- **The one seniors building hides in a BC Housing PDF, not on real-estate sites:** Knightsbridge II (12571 Cambie Rd) appears in BC Housing's Zone 3 seniors directory under "Richmond - North and East" alongside Alderwood Place (8700 General Currie Rd), Cedarwood Place (7260 Granville Ave) and Fiorella (3699/3779 Sexsmith Rd) — a buyer searching condo sites for "55+ East Cambie" will never see it, because it is subsidized rental applied for through the Housing Registry. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Cambie Road crosses two different planning areas:** the same street runs through West Cambie (Garden City Road to No. 4 Road side) and East Cambie (No. 5 Road toward the ALR). The Remy at 9388 Cambie Rd, with its 33 seniors' rental apartments, is in West Cambie — not this area — and Camden Square at 9300 Cambie Rd is also West Cambie. Address alone does not settle which Cambie you are in. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2014MNGD0009-000204
- **The ALR is the east wall of the neighbourhood:** the plan states the area abuts the ALR along No. 6 Road and Westminster Highway and sets a goal of a distinct boundary between agricultural and non-agricultural lands — farm activity, not more housing, is what sits past those two roads, and Richmond Nature Park itself is within the ALR. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=558
- **Mitchell Elementary is a heritage asset, not just a catchment:** the Area Plan asks the School Board to conserve the Mitchell School buildings as heritage structures, and Mitchell is the longest continuously operating school in the Richmond public system. https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/archives/exhibits/schools/early/mitchell.htm
- **Richmond's two money dates:** 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% on any outstanding balance after Tuesday, September 1, 2026; flat-rate utilities were due March 31, 2026 with a 10% discount only if paid by the due date, and the City's discounted typical single-family metered utility total for 2026 is $2,251 per year (water $631, sewer $835, garbage/recycling/environmental $443, flood protection $342). https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm and https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/finance/utility-fees/utilityrates.htm
