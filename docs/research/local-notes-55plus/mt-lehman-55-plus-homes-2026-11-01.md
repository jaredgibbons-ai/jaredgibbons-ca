# Mt Lehman — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Mt Lehman (also written Mount Lehman / Mt. Lehman) is a small rural community on raised ground in northwest Abbotsford, between Matsqui Prairie to the east and Glen Valley (Langley) to the west; it was part of the former District of Matsqui until Matsqui amalgamated into the City of Abbotsford in 1995. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/community-planning/abbotsforward
- It is a local/official-community name, not a board sub-area: the Fraser Valley board's Abbotsford sub-areas in SnapStats reporting are Abbotsford East, Abbotsford West, Aberdeen, Bradner, Central Abbotsford, Matsqui, Poplar, Sumas Mountain and Sumas Prairie — there is no "Mt Lehman" row, and Lehman-area listings are absorbed into the neighbouring rural sub-areas (Bradner/Matsqui side). (Area research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/mt-lehman.md, citing stats/subarea-stats-sep2026.csv, read 2026-11-01.)
- Mt Lehman Road is the spine; the community's institutions sit on or near it — Mt Lehman Elementary (K–5), 6381 Mt Lehman Rd, School District 34, and Mount Lehman Hall, 6418 Mount Lehman Road, built in 1904 and operated by the Mount Lehman Community Association. https://mtlehman.abbyschools.ca/ and https://heritagebc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/City-of-Abbotsford-2005-Heritage-Strategic-Plan.pdf
- The feeder pattern is Mt Lehman Elementary → Eugene Reimer Middle (from Grade 6) → Rick Hansen Secondary, per SD34's Eugene Reimer "Family of Schools" page; confirm any individual address in SD34's School Locator. https://eugenereimer.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/ermsfamilyofschools/eugenereimer
- Land use is rural residential and agricultural: the workhorse zone is Agricultural One Zone (A1) under Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 — intent "to accommodate Agricultural and Agri-Tourism uses on lots that are 8.0 ha and larger in size," with principal uses including Agricultural Use, Agri-Tourism, Farm Retail Sales, a Single Detached Dwelling and a Winery, plus accessory family/employee residential uses and a Secondary Suite (Bylaw No. 2400-2014, s. 210). https://www.kpu.ca/sites/default/files/City of Abbottsford_ Agricultural Zoning Bylaw_A1_consolidated 2014.pdf
- The community sits on an upland plateau above Matsqui Prairie's diked floodplain; the lowland at its foot is protected by the Matsqui Dike, listed in the Province's dike inventory (City-administered) at 11.3 km, 4 pump stations and 2 flood boxes, serving 4,370 ha. https://www.env.gov.bc.ca/wsd/public_safety/flood/pdfs_word/dikes_listed_by_authority-2013.pdf
- Transit is minimal and car-based: BC Transit's Central Fraser Valley Route 21 Aldergrove / Abbotsford runs via Highstreet Exchange and Mt Lehman Road's south end, roughly hourly on weekdays with reduced weekends; no route runs to the village core. A new five-bay Highstreet transit exchange opened September 6, 2026 to serve "growing neighbourhoods in the Mt. Lehman area." https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=21&schedule=2026-04-05 and https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-08/2026-08-21%20-%20New%20transit%20exchange%20opening%20near%20Highstreet%20Shopping%20Centre%20.pdf
- Fire protection is Abbotsford Fire Rescue Service Fire Hall 5 (Mt. Lehman), 30373 Merryfield Avenue. https://www.abbotsford.ca/public-safety/fire-rescue-service/fire-safety-events
- Where 55+ housing concentrates: nowhere in Mt Lehman. No 55+ strata, 55+ rental, retirement residence, assisted-living residence or subsidized seniors building with a Mt Lehman address was verified in any source checked — see Section 2. Abbotsford's seniors housing is in the city's urban areas, a drive away.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014** governs Mt Lehman; the A1 Agricultural One Zone (lots 8.0 ha and larger) permits a Single Detached Dwelling plus accessory family/employee residential uses and a Secondary Suite — the suite is the main legal way a second household (for example, a caregiver or adult family member) lives on a Mt Lehman farm parcel. https://www.kpu.ca/sites/default/files/City of Abbottsford_ Agricultural Zoning Bylaw_A1_consolidated 2014.pdf
- **ALR residence limits sit on top of zoning.** On ALR parcels the Agricultural Land Commission states the as-of-right package: generally one residence per parcel unless it qualifies for an additional one; a principal residence of 500 m² or less; a secondary suite only inside the principal residence; anything beyond needs ALC approval, and the City may further restrict residences. ALC Policy L-26 (amended May 2025) allows an additional residence beyond the regulation only if necessary for a farm use. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_policy_l-26_-_non-adhering_residential_use_applications.pdf
- **Plan: Abbotsforward 2050**, Abbotsford's Official Community Plan, adopted by Council at the end of 2025, directs growth to Abbotsford's urban centres and protects the agricultural land base — it does not plan a seniors-housing node in Mt Lehman. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/city-begins-implementing-updated-official-community-plan
- **Secondary suites city-wide:** Abbotsford's 2025 Zoning Bylaw updates changed the secondary-suite maximum size in most zones from 100 m² to less than 50% of the size of the principal unit, and the City's Secondary Suite Incentive Program (adopted July 2024) supported up to 150 new or registered suites — 105 completed and 45 pending at the City's last report. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-01/Zoning%20Bylaw%20Updates%20Overview%202025.pdf and https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **Farm class changes the tax bill.** BC Assessment Class 9, Farm requires land to produce a prescribed amount of qualifying primary agricultural products for sale; farm buildings are Class 1, and 50% of Class 9 land's assessed value is exempt from school taxes. Farm class is earned by production, not automatic on ALR land. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/understanding-property-classes-and-exemptions?TermStoreId=2e29bc59-558d-448c-9c19-d11b487dbf89&TermSetId=0a8def97-2239-4f44-b0fc-f2f10a0ea2d4&TermId=582130f6-038f-4842-ada7-0166ce03a246
- **City taxes (2026):** Abbotsford property taxes were due July 2, 2026; a first 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty applied September 16, 2026, under Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3727-2026; penalties are legislated and cannot be waived. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Mt Lehman?** None that any source verifies. There is no 55+ strata, co-op, life lease or manufactured-home park in this rural community — the housing is freehold acreage homes and farms. A 55+ buyer here is buying a regular acreage home with no age bylaw. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: But Abbotsford has lots of 55+ buildings — aren't some of them in Mt Lehman?** No. Abbotsford's 55+ stock (26 strata, 3 parks and 2 life leases city-wide from earlier batches) sits in the urban sub-areas — Abbotsford West, Central Abbotsford and Abbotsford East. Listings verify examples such as Elmwood Manor (32025 Tims Ave) and Wedgewood (2626 Countess St) in Abbotsford West; none carries a Mt Lehman address. https://vancouvernashdom.com/abbotsford-abbotsford-west-apartments/SearchResults.form/?_pg=7
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Abbotsford?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I add a suite or a second home on a Mt Lehman acreage for a caregiver or family?** That is the realistic ageing-in-place route here. A1 zoning permits a Secondary Suite as an accessory use, and on ALR land the ALC allows a secondary suite only inside the principal residence, with anything beyond the permitted number/size needing ALC approval — check the specific parcel's zone and ALR status before pricing in a second dwelling. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is Mt Lehman in the floodplain?** The village itself is upland — that elevation is the origin of the "Mount" — but it is ringed by ALR farmland above Matsqui Prairie's diked floodplain, protected by the 11.3 km Matsqui Dike. Plateau-edge and prairie-floor parcels need their own flood check; do not generalize from the village core. https://www.env.gov.bc.ca/wsd/public_safety/flood/pdfs_word/dikes_listed_by_authority-2013.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital and day-to-day shopping?** Not in Mt Lehman: there is no civic recreation facility or retail centre in the community, and residents drive to Abbotsford's centres and to Clearbrook/Highstreet-area shopping in urban Abbotsford. No source checked states a Mt Lehman-specific hospital travel time — UNCONFIRMED. https://www.abbotsford.ca/parks-recreation-culture/facilities-hours
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** Very little. Route 21 runs along Mt Lehman Road's south end roughly hourly on weekdays with reduced weekend service, and no route reaches the village core — a car is a practical requirement for a Mt Lehman home. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=21&schedule=2026-04-05
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in Mt Lehman?** Not in a facility — there is no retirement residence, assisted-living residence or long-term care home with a Mt Lehman address in the Assisted Living Registry; the registry's Abbotsford entries are all at urban addresses such as Tabor Court (31954 Sunrise Crescent). Staying in the village past independent living means home support in a house, or a move into urban Abbotsford. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3AAbbotsford&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc
- **Q: Does farm classification lower my taxes on a Mt Lehman property?** It can, materially — but Class 9 farm status requires actual production of qualifying agricultural products for sale, and 50% of Class 9 land value is exempt from school taxes. Verify the parcel's current BC Assessment classification rather than assuming ALR land is farm-classed. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/understanding-property-classes-and-exemptions?TermStoreId=2e29bc59-558d-448c-9c19-d11b487dbf89&TermSetId=0a8def97-2239-4f44-b0fc-f2f10a0ea2d4&TermId=582130f6-038f-4842-ada7-0166ce03a246

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **There is no board sub-area to price against.** SnapStats/FVREB reporting has no Mt Lehman row at all — pricing has to be built from Bradner, Matsqui and Aberdeen rural comparables plus acreage sales, not from a sub-area stats sheet. (Area research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/mt-lehman.md, citing stats/subarea-stats-sep2026.csv, read 2026-11-01.)
- **The village buildings are historic but not heritage-protected.** Mount Lehman Hall dates to 1904, yet no Mount Lehman building — church, hall, Taylor House or cemetery — appears on the City's published Community Heritage Register list, and only Trethewey House, Clayburn Church and Clayburn School carry Local Heritage Designation city-wide. Do not market a Mt Lehman home as "heritage-registered" on the strength of the village's age. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/Heritage%20Alteration%20Permits.pdf
- **The school has been on its site since the 1880s and is the village's anchor.** Mt Lehman Elementary's first school opened in 1884 and schooling has been continuous on the site since; a still-operating K–5 school in a rural village is the main family draw — and the main reason younger families compete with downsizers for the same acreages. https://mtlehman.abbyschools.ca/
- **Winery addresses are the search trap.** Mt Lehman Road searches surface three estate wineries (Mt. Lehman Winery; Singletree at 5782 Mt. Lehman Road; Seaside Pearl at 5290 Olund Road) — farm-gate retail is effectively the community's only "shopping," and none of it is housing. https://singletreewinery.com/contact/ and https://seasidepearlwinery.ca/visit-us/
- **The new transit exchange is not in the village.** The five-bay Highstreet exchange that opened September 6, 2026 is described by the City as serving "growing neighbourhoods in the Mt. Lehman area," but it sits at the Highstreet shopping area in urban Abbotsford — a drive, not a walk, from any Mt Lehman home. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-08/2026-08-21%20-%20New%20transit%20exchange%20opening%20near%20Highstreet%20Shopping%20Centre%20.pdf
- **Abbotsford's tax calendar has two penalty steps, not one.** Taxes were due July 2, 2026, with 5% added July 3 and a further 5% on September 16, 2026 — and area-specific charges such as prairie dyking-district levies can appear on parcels at the plateau's foot, so check a parcel's local charges rather than assuming the village rate. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **Growth is deliberately not coming here.** Abbotsforward 2050, adopted at the end of 2025, protects the agricultural land base and directs growth to urban centres — a buyer assuming future subdivision or a future seniors project in Mt Lehman is assuming against the adopted plan. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/city-begins-implementing-updated-official-community-plan
