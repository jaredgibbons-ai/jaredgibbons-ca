# Montecito (Burnaby) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Montecito is in northeast Burnaby; the City also knows the area as "Sperling-Broadway." It runs between Kensington Ave on the west and the Greystone/Arden side on the east, with Halifax St on the north and Lougheed Hwy on the south. The exact MLS sub-area boundary lines are UNVERIFIED. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
- Schools: Montecito Elementary, 2176 Duthie Ave — the SD41 locator for that address returns Montecito Elementary and Burnaby North Secondary. École Sperling Elementary, 2200 Sperling Ave, sits at the southwest side (it appeared in research only as an Early French Immersion site). Burnaby is School District 41 — 41 elementary and 8 secondary schools, no middle-school tier (K–7 / 8–12), with address-based catchments. https://burnabyschools.ca/about-sd41-overview/ and https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- Transit: Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station (Millennium Line) is at the sub-area's south edge on Lougheed Hwy, and bus routes 110, 134 and 144 serve the area. https://translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/regional-maps/2026/september-2026/burnaby-and-new-westminster-map.pdf
- Recreation: Burnaby Lake Regional Park borders the south side, and the Burnaby Lake Sports Complex / Bill Copeland Sports Centre, 3676 Kensington Ave, is at the west/south edge. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/burnaby-lake-regional-park and https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/bill-copeland-sports-centre
- Where the seniors housing actually is: exactly one verified seniors residence — the Swedish Assisted Living Residence at 7230 Halifax St, on the sub-area's north boundary street. It is assisted living (mostly publicly subsidized), not a building anyone can buy into.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Assisted living is not an age-bylaw product:** the Swedish residence's suites are registered assisted living under BC's Assisted Living Registry — admission runs through the operator for private-pay suites and through Fraser Health for the 46 subsidized suites, under health-authority eligibility rules, not under the Strata Property Act. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/0/A/assisted-living-at-swedish-assisted-living-residence
- **Zoning bylaw — note the name change.** Research briefs for Burnaby sometimes still cite the "Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742)". The City states the new **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026** was adopted June 9, 2026, replaced the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965, and took effect July 1, 2026. Use the 2026 bylaw. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/bylaws/zoning-bylaw
- **R1 SSMUH zoning:** Burnaby consolidated its single- and two-family districts into the R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing district in June 2024 — 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; multi-unit lots may be stratified. On Montecito's detached streets, SSMUH infill is the only mechanism adding smaller ground-oriented homes; none of it is age-restricted. The Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station area also carries transit-oriented-area density rules. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP: the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan** was adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP, and takes precedence over older community-plan maps. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Property taxes:** Burnaby's 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date; the City's 2026–2030 Financial Plan approved a 2.9% tax increase (about $61 on the average home). The detached single-family water utility charge is $652.33, with a 5% discount if paid by the due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Montecito?** None that any source verifies. There is no age-restricted strata building in the sub-area; the one seniors residence, the Swedish Assisted Living Residence on Halifax St, is assisted living. A 55+ buyer in Montecito is buying a regular house, townhome or condo with no age bylaw. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Can I buy into the Swedish Canadian Village on Halifax St?** No. It is an assisted-living residence — 64 one-bedroom suites, 46 of them Fraser Health-subsidized and 18 private-pay — operated by the Swedish Canadian Rest Home Association. You apply (or are referred through Fraser Health for a subsidized suite); there is nothing to purchase. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/0/A/assisted-living-at-swedish-assisted-living-residence and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2009-2013/2013emng0044-000426.htm
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Burnaby?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — a BC age bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I add a suite or a second dwelling for a parent or caregiver?** That is the realistic ageing-in-place route here: Burnaby's R1 SSMUH zoning allows 3–4 units on most house lots (up to 6 near frequent transit), suites are part of the permitted forms, and multi-unit lots may be stratified. What a specific lot can hold depends on its size and transit proximity — check the lot against the City's R1 rules. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day?** Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station (Millennium Line) is at the south edge on Lougheed Hwy, with bus routes 110, 134 and 144 in the area — the south side of Montecito is genuinely SkyTrain-served even though the Halifax St side is not. https://translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/regional-maps/2026/september-2026/burnaby-and-new-westminster-map.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** There is no hospital in Montecito; the nearest is Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St, southwest across the city side of the lake area. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in Montecito?** Only at the assisted-living step: the Swedish residence covers assisted living (with subsidized suites allocated through Fraser Health), but there is no long-term care home verified inside the sub-area — the care step beyond assisted living means leaving Montecito. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/0/A/assisted-living-at-swedish-assisted-living-residence
- **Q: Is Montecito the same as Sperling-Broadway?** Substantially, yes — the City uses "Sperling-Broadway" for the same northeast Burnaby area the MLS calls Montecito. When checking City plans, permits or zoning history for a Montecito property, search both names. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The sub-area has two names and you need both.** City documents file this area under "Sperling-Broadway"; the MLS and market data call it Montecito. A City-side search under only "Montecito" will miss plan and permit material. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
- **The one seniors building sits on the boundary street.** The Swedish Assisted Living Residence is at 7230 Halifax St — Halifax St being the sub-area's north boundary — and most of its 64 suites (46) are publicly subsidized, so even the private-pay route in is only 18 suites. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2009-2013/2013emng0044-000426.htm
- **The operating society predates the building by generations:** the Swedish Canadian Rest Home Association was established in 1947; the current Halifax St assisted-living residence itself opened in 2013. Do not let the association's 1947 date be quoted as the building's age. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2009-2013/2013emng0044-000426.htm
- **Halifax St changes character along its length:** residential at the Montecito end, commercial/industrial further east (e.g. 4290 Halifax St). A "Halifax St, Burnaby" search hit is as likely to be a warehouse as a home. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
- **Burnaby replaced its entire zoning bylaw in 2026.** The Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (adopted June 9, 2026, in force July 1, 2026) repealed and replaced the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 — documents or summaries that still cite the 1965 bylaw are out of date. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Burnaby taxes have two penalty steps.** 2026 property taxes were due July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date, and the detached water charge ($652.33) carries a 5% early-payment discount. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
