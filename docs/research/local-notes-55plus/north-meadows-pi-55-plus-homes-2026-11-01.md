# North Meadows (Pitt Meadows) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**GEOGRAPHY FLAG — read first:** The area research file for this slug (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/north-meadows-pi.md) describes North Meadows as the Paragon sub-area north of Lougheed Highway and names Highland Park Elementary, 18961 Advent Road, as physically in the sub-area. ## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "North Meadows" is not an official City neighbourhood name and the City publishes no boundary for it; it is the Paragon sub-area name for the part of Pitt Meadows north of Lougheed Highway, covering the Highland Park and Wildwood residential areas, Harris Road north, and the rural Pitt Polder lands beyond. The closest official planning name for its southern edge is the North Lougheed Area. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/homes-development/zoning-land-use/north-lougheed-area-plan
- Main streets: Harris Road (the north–south spine), Advent Road, Ford Road, McNeil Road and Old Dewdney Trunk Road, with agricultural Pitt Polder land to the north and east. The North Lougheed Area itself is approximately 50 ha on the north side of Lougheed Highway between Meadows Gardens Golf Course and Harris Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/homes-development/zoning-land-use/north-lougheed-area-plan
- Schools: Highland Park Elementary, 18961 Advent Road, Kindergarten to Grade 7, is the elementary school the research file places in the sub-area; School District 42 has no middle tier in Pitt Meadows, and Pitt Meadows Secondary, 19438 116B Avenue, Grade 8 to 12, sits south of the sub-area. For the sample address 18961 Advent Road, the SD42 School Locator returns Highland Park Elementary and Pitt Meadows Secondary. https://elementary.sd42.ca/highlandpark/ and https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- Parks: Harris Road Park, 12474 Harris Road (concession, spray park, skate park, playground, walking trail, ball diamonds, outdoor pool, basketball court) and Grabenhorst Garden, 13895 Harris Road; the Codd Island Wetlands, undiked floodplain wetlands on the Alouette River in the rural north, are on the City heritage register. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/harris-road-park and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/arts-culture-heritage/heritage
- Shopping: no shopping centre or grocery store inside this sub-area is confirmed on an official source; Meadowtown Shopping Centre, 19800 Lougheed Highway, south of the sub-area, is the nearest centre confirmed on City sources, with Real Canadian Superstore listed as a grocery tenant in the City's Commercial & Industrial Lands Analysis. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/dmlive-155673-v1-gpra_report_to_pitt_meadows_20190405_v2_pdf.pdf
- Transit: the R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus stops at Harris Road on the south edge; route 701 runs via Harris Road and Hammond Road along the south edge to Pitt Meadows Station; route 719 serves Wildwood Crescent and Hammond Road. There is no West Coast Express station in this sub-area (Pitt Meadows Station is south of Lougheed Highway), and no bus route serving the rural north itself was confirmed. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt719.pdf
- Flood/ALR setting: the city is almost entirely protected by 60 km of dikes and its land base lies in the Fraser Valley floodplain; the northern drainage areas (Alouette Pump Station catchment, Fenton, and the Pitt Polder catchment) are drained by floodboxes and pump stations, and most of the city's land falls within the Agricultural Land Reserve. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163 and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/media/8909
- Where 55+ housing concentrates: not in North Meadows on board classification. The one strata complex in the sub-area, The Fairways on Meadow Gardens Way, is a general-market condo (Section 2). The city's 55+ pocket is the Advent Road pair (Meadowland Village strata + Meadowlands co-op) that MLS data classes as Central Meadows — see the flag at the top of this file and the Checked list in Section 2.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011** governs North Meadows. The rural zones set the lot pattern: A-1 General Agricultural — minimum lot area for subdivision 8 ha, one farm house per lot to a maximum gross floor area of 500 sq m; A-2 Large Lot Agricultural — minimum 16 ha; RR-2 — minimum 4 ha; RR-3 — minimum 2 ha; RR-4 — minimum 1 ha (RR zones are for land outside the ALR); RS Large Lot Residential — minimum 0.2 ha. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 - Consolidated Version_0.pdf
- **Second dwellings:** a garden suite is permitted in the A-1, A-2, RR-1 to RR-5 and RS zones, one per lot, 33 sq m to 90 sq m, and a property may have a garden suite or a secondary suite, not both. On ALR land, the ALC also allows one additional detached residence without an application where conditions are met, to a maximum of 90 sq m on properties 40 ha or less. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Plan: the Official Community Plan "I See Pitt Meadows 2040"** covers the whole city including this sub-area; the North Lougheed Area Plan, adopted by OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3060, 2026, covers approximately 50 ha north of Lougheed Highway between Meadows Gardens Golf Course and Harris Road, with development to proceed gradually and further approvals required. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/homes-development/zoning-land-use/official-community-plan-0 and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/homes-development/zoning-land-use/north-lougheed-area-plan
- **Flood:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of Pitt Meadows is in the floodplain, protected by roughly 60 km of dikes — and the North Meadows / Pitt Polder side is the low, pump-drained part of that system (Alouette Pump Station, Fenton and Pitt Polder catchments drain by floodboxes and pump stations). Floodplain, dike and drainage checks are a standing item on every North Meadows parcel. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- **Siting near water:** zoning requires residential and agricultural buildings to be sited 15 m from watercourses, key ditches and constructed ditches in the A-1 and A-2 zones. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 - Consolidated Version_0.pdf
- **City taxes:** City of Pitt Meadows taxes apply under the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026; the rural north also sits inside dike/drainage service areas, and the specific levy set for a given lot is UNVERIFIED from an official source in this pass. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in North Meadows?** None that any source verifies under the board's North Meadows classification. The sub-area's one strata complex, The Fairways, has no age restriction, and the rest of the stock is freehold houses and farm properties. A 55+ buyer here is buying a regular home with no age bylaw. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is The Fairways a 55+ building? It sits on a golf course and gets marketed to downsizers.** No. The developer describes a 268-home resort-style condominium complex, and current listings describe it as suiting "downsizers, families & professionals alike," pet-friendly, with a kids room among the amenities. No source checked states any age restriction. https://magusta.com/our-portfolio/the-fairways/ and https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3104451-19677-meadow-gardens-way-120-pitt-meadows-bc-v3y-0a2/brochure
- **Q: Where is the nearest buyable 55+ home, then?** Across the board line in Central Meadows: Meadowland Village, 18960 Advent Road — ground-level rancher townhomes in a freehold strata (plan NWS2727) whose MLS data records "Age Restricted 55+." It is a short drive south of Lougheed Highway, but MLS data classes it Central Meadows, so it is counted in that file. https://www.zolo.ca/pitt-meadows-real-estate/18960-advent-road/7
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I add a garden suite on a North Meadows acreage for a caregiver or family member?** In the A-1, A-2, RR and RS zones the City permits one garden suite per lot, 33 sq m to 90 sq m (a lot may have a garden suite or a secondary suite, not both), and ALR parcels have their own additional-residence path under ALC rules. That suite route — not a 55+ complex — is how multi-generation living is usually structured on the rural north side. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is North Meadows in a floodplain?** Effectively yes, at the city scale: the City's 2020 Business Plan puts over 95% of Pitt Meadows in the floodplain behind roughly 60 km of dikes, and this sub-area is the low Pitt Polder land drained by floodboxes and pump stations. Check the specific lot's dike, drainage and insurance position rather than relying on the city-wide figure. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Pitt Meadows has no hospital. Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, Maple Ridge, is a Fraser Health community hospital with 24/7 emergency care; the Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 121-11900 Haney Place, Maple Ridge, open 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., 7 days a week. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/F/C/urgent-primary-care-centre---ridge-meadows
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** Only at the south edge: the R3 RapidBus at Harris Road, route 701 along Harris/Hammond, and route 719 through Wildwood Crescent. No route serving the rural north itself was confirmed, and there is no West Coast Express station in the sub-area. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt719.pdf
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in Pitt Meadows?** At one address, outside this sub-area: The Wesbrooke, 12000 190A Street, offers independent living plus a registered assisted-living component as a rental. There is no long-term care home in Pitt Meadows. https://thewesbrooke.com/ and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=pique%20newsmagazine&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=41&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=desc&page=1

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The board line on Advent Road cuts across the street.** MLS data classes 18960 Advent Road (Meadowland Village) as Central Meadows while the research file for this area places Highland Park Elementary at 18961 Advent Road — the school facing it — in North Meadows. Anyone searching "55+ Pitt Meadows" by street will land on Advent Road; anyone searching by board sub-area will not find it under North Meadows. https://www.zolo.ca/pitt-meadows-real-estate/18960-advent-road/7
- **Two near-identical names, two tenures, one street.** Meadowland Village (18960 Advent Rd) is the buyable 55+ strata; Meadowlands Housing Co-operative (19032 Advent Rd) is a 55+ co-op where members hold shares and nothing is ever listed as a strata sale. Both are board-classed with Central Meadows and covered there — but a North Meadows buyer will meet both names first. https://www.meadowlandscoop.ca/
- **The golf-course condos are the sub-area's only strata — and they are family product.** The Fairways' 268 homes (strata plan BCS2427) come with a kids room and listings aimed at families and professionals as much as downsizers; "resort-style" in the marketing does not mean age-restricted. https://magusta.com/our-portfolio/the-fairways/
- **The rural north has no bus.** Confirmed transit is all on the Lougheed/Harris south edge; the Pitt Polder farm roads have no confirmed route at all, which matters for a senior planning to age in place without driving. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt719.pdf
- **The North Lougheed Area Plan is adopted but not built.** OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3060, 2026 covers roughly 50 ha north of Lougheed Highway, and the City states development will proceed gradually with further planning, studies and Council approvals — no residential presale in the sub-area was verified. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/homes-development/zoning-land-use/north-lougheed-area-plan
- **Heritage here is farm and wetland, not houses.** The City register entries in the sub-area include the Japanese Canadian Hall, 19089 Advent Road, the Pitt Meadows School House, 13414 Harris Road, farm properties on Ford Road, and the undiked Codd Island Wetlands — a reminder that the north side's lots come with agricultural and drainage histories that a title and ALR check should surface. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/arts-culture-heritage/heritage
- **Well and septic are per-lot questions.** Whether a given North Meadows lot is on municipal water/sewer or on a well and septic system is not confirmed per lot on an official source — check the provincial GWELLS well records and Fraser Health's onsite-sewerage rules (only an Authorized Person can design, install, repair, maintain or inspect a system) before subject removal on a rural lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
