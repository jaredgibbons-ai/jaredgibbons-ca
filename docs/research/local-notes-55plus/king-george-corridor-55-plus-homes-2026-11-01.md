## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Bare-land strata is still strata law.** Sea Ridge Bays is a bare-land strata, so the Strata Property Act — not the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act — is the frame: the buyer owns the lot, pays a strata fee instead of pad rent, and the age rule sits in the bylaws restrictions recorded in MLS property data. A pad-rent park is the opposite tenure — get the park rules in writing there. https://realtylink.org/en/print/property/263120958 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000** governs every corridor property, shown parcel by parcel in the City's COSMOS map. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Plans: work from the current plan, not the 1995 concept plan.** The corridor's designations now come from the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026), following Council's December 2, 2024 endorsement of Corporate Report R246 updating the corridor designations. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH):** Surrey allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, applying to eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including certain hazard lands and properties not connected to community water and sewer. Boulevard-adjacent house lots may fall inside a Frequent Bus Stop Area — check the parcel in COSMOS. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites:** Surrey permits only one suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, and never inside a coach house or garden suite. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Planned transit is a plan, not a service:** TransLink is advancing King George Boulevard BRT — approximately 19 km with 12 stations connecting Surrey City Centre and Semiahmoo Town Centre, dedicated lanes along most of the route, peak service every five minutes and peak end-to-end times of about 40 minutes — and the City states Council voted to move the project into the full design stage. Only its southern portion runs through this page's plan area, and station locations and construction timing for a specific corridor address are UNVERIFIED. https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/news/council-approves-next-stage-of-king-george-boulevard-brt
- **Property taxes (city-wide, not area-specific):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in the King George Corridor?** Two verified ownership complexes in the south segment: Peninsula Village (2500 152 St — gated strata townhouses, LMS 133, 95 townhomes) and Sea Ridge Bays (15875 20 Ave — 55+ bare-land strata manufactured homes, where you own the lot). No 55+ rental building or subsidized complex was verified inside the segment. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3098715-2500-152-street-67-surrey-bc-v4p-1m8/brochure and https://realtylink.org/en/print/property/263120958
- **Q: Is the corridor the whole of King George Boulevard?** No — not on this page, in the City plan or in the board data. The plan area and sub-area run from 8 Avenue to the Nicomekl River in South Surrey, bordered by Highway 99 to the east; the boulevard continues north through Newton to City Centre, but those stretches sit in other sub-areas with other stock (the King George Boulevard parks at 7790 and 8220, for example, are East Newton and all-ages). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south and https://www.mhbo.com/browse/mobile_home_parks/bc/surrey
- **Q: Do both of us have to be 55 at Peninsula Village?** Peninsula Village listings state the rule as "One person 55+." Provincially, a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption) is exempt in any case — confirm the complex's own bylaw wording in the Form B. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3098715-2500-152-street-67-surrey-bc-v4p-1m8/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: At Sea Ridge Bays, do I rent a pad like in a mobile-home park?** No — listings state "NO PAD FEES" and "YOU OWN THE LOT": it is a bare-land strata, so you pay a strata fee ($210/mo including water in the listings checked — listing-stated, confirm current in the Form B) and strata law governs, not the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. https://realtylink.org/en/print/property/263120958 and https://www.jagsidhu.com/listing/r3099331-15875-20-avenue-50-surrey-bc-v4a-2b1/brochure
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ complex as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is Breakaway Bays on 160 Street a 55+ park?** UNCONFIRMED — the sources conflict directly: a park directory calls it age-restricted (55+), while MLS listings for homes in it state "NO AGE RESTRICTION" and describe a family park. Do not rely on either claim; ask the park for its current rules in writing before writing an offer. https://www.mhbo.com/mobile-home-park/45813-breakaway-bays-1840-160th-street-surrey-bc-v4a-4x4 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/1840-160-street/337
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay on the corridor?** The corridor's own care address is Chartwell Crescent Gardens at 1222 King George Blvd — independent living, assisted living and long-term care with 24-hour nursing under one roof, per the operator, with subsidized long-term care suites accessed through a Fraser Health assessment. It is rental/care, not ownership. https://chartwell.com/bc/surrey/crescent-gardens/care-and-support
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What should I check at the corridor's north end?** The floodplain: parcels near the Nicomekl River sit in Surrey's coastal floodplain system (the Serpentine–Nicomekl lowlands), and flood-construction requirements are parcel-specific — run the City check per parcel before subjects come off. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **South Surrey's best-known 55+ complex and its nearest 55+ neighbour are in different MLS sub-areas.** Peninsula Village (2500 152 St, 95 townhomes) is filed under King George Corridor while Bishop's Green (2533 152 St) — a few blocks north on the same street — is filed under Sunnyside Park. A buyer searching only one sub-area's 55+ page misses half the 152 Street corridor. https://storage.ubertor.com/cl2464/listing/Listing_780_extra/128267.pdf and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/2533-152-street/98
- **The corridor's two ownership forms are opposites in everything except the age rule.** Peninsula Village is a conventional gated townhouse strata (clubhouse, private gate to the Peninsula Village Shopping Centre); Sea Ridge Bays is manufactured homes on owned bare-land strata lots with no pad rent. Financing, insurance and the documents you review differ by form — the Form B and bylaws decide, not the "55+" label they share. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3098715-2500-152-street-67-surrey-bc-v4p-1m8/brochure and https://realtylink.org/en/print/property/263120958
- **One listing also calls Peninsula Village "The Peninsula."** Same address (2500 152 St), same strata plan (LMS 133), two marketing names — an alert set on one name does not cover the other. https://www.jagsidhu.com/listing/r3097116-2500-152-street-48-surrey-bc-v4p-1m8/brochure
- **The care residence is the one 55+ address actually ON King George Boulevard.** Chartwell Crescent Gardens, 1222 King George Blvd, sits on the boulevard at the Highway 99 side of South Surrey, per its operator — the ownership complexes are both on side streets (152 St, 20 Ave). https://chartwell.com/bc/surrey/crescent-gardens
- **The BRT's corridor is not this page's corridor.** The planned King George Boulevard BRT runs the whole 19 km from Surrey City Centre to Semiahmoo Town Centre with 12 stations — only its southern portion runs through this plan area, so a BRT station claim for a specific south-segment address needs its own confirmation; none is stated here. https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/news/council-approves-next-stage-of-king-george-boulevard-brt
- **The governing plan changed under this corridor.** The 1995 concept plan's designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update (Corporate Report R246, endorsed December 2, 2024; OCP Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026) — a seller quoting 1995 concept-plan densities is quoting a superseded document. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **Gated does not mean 55+ on this corridor.** Woodgrove (2588 152 St) is a "quiet gated community" with no age restriction in any source checked, and the building actually named Sunnyside (15290 18 Ave) carries no age restriction in its directory record — both sit inside this sub-area and neither belongs on a 55+ list. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/2588-152-street/61 and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/sunnyside-15290-18-ave-stratford-by-the-park
