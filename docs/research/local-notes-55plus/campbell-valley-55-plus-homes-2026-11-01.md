## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **There is no age bylaw on a freehold acreage house.** Strata age law only bites inside a strata corporation, and this sub-area has no verified strata stock (Section 2). A senior buying a detached acreage home here faces zoning and, where it applies, Agricultural Land Reserve rules — not a 55+ residency bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **ALR housing frame — the first check on this sub-area's acreage stock:** on ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence is permitted, up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; local government bylaws may be more restrictive and may not be more permissive. Whether any specific Campbell Valley parcel is in the ALR is UNVERIFIED at parcel level — check the ALC maps. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Strata conversion on ALR land needs ALC permission:** depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR — ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6 ("Strata Plans Cause Subdivision"). Feed cards for this sub-area raise strata-conversion ideas in their own text; on an ALR parcel that idea is under this rule. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **The second-dwelling rule is the ageing-in-place rule on an acreage.** The ALC's additional-residence tier (90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size, above) is the legal route to a caregiver's or family member's dwelling on an ALR Campbell Valley parcel, and Township bylaws must also permit it. There is no strata-style caregiver exemption here because there is no strata. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Township frame:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 applies across the Township, and s.102 is the cited source for the Township's coach-house definition (an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage). What a specific Campbell Valley parcel may build is UNVERIFIED at parcel level in this file; feed cards use RU1 and RU-3 labels in their own listing text only. Source: batch-15 research file for this area.
- **Do not apply SSMUH math to these acreages.** The Township adopted Bylaw 6020 on November 18, 2024 — 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², lots over 4,050 m² ineligible, and no 6-unit tier. Most Campbell Valley acreage parcels exceed the 4,050 m² ceiling by a wide margin, so the SSMUH framing generally does not apply to them; parcel-level eligibility is UNVERIFIED. Source: batch-15 research file for this area.
- **Farm classification (for farmed parcels) is a continuing obligation.** BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income thresholds are $2,500 if the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha, and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha; applications must be submitted by October 31 for the next assessment and tax year, and a buyer who plans to continue farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **The strata age-bylaw rules, for the record — with no local strata to apply them to.** Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; age bylaws below 55 invalid since Nov 24, 2022, under Bill 44); the bylaw restricts residency, not ownership; Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19; and residential rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, while short-term restrictions remain allowed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home parks, if a park purchase is involved elsewhere:** tenancies fall under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act — the resident owns the home and rents the pad, and pad rent may be raised only once in 12 months on at least three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026) plus a proportional share of changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. A park's age rule is a park rule, not a strata bylaw. No park is verified in this sub-area (Section 2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Property transfer tax (general tiers):** 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% on the portion above $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% above $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. How PTT and GST apportion on a specific Campbell Valley purchase is UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **FHSA and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits: UNVERIFIED** — no live CRA page stating those limits was read for this file.
- **Property taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a penalty of 5% is applied to current-year taxes, including the unclaimed home owner grant, at midnight July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there really no 55+ condo, townhouse or park to buy in Campbell Valley?** Correct, on every source checked: the sub-area recorded 0 Condos & Townhomes listings and 0 attached sales in September 2026, its feed is individual acreage parcels, and no age-restricted complex or park of any kind is verified here. Langley's 15 verified 55+ ownership complexes are in the urban communities (Section 2). (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, as recorded in the batch-15 research file.)
- **Q: So how does a senior actually live in Campbell Valley?** In a regular freehold acreage house, on the sources checked. There is no strata age bylaw on a detached parcel, so there is also nothing to qualify for — and nothing restricting who can buy the home from you later. The seniors-specific questions here are zoning and ALR questions: a suite for a caregiver or family member, and a second dwelling where the rules allow one. Sources: Sections 2 and 3 above.
- **Q: Can an acreage here have a second home or a suite for family or a caregiver?** Parcel by parcel. On ALR land, the ALC frame allows one principal residence up to 500 m² plus at most one additional detached residence of 90 m² (parcels 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha), with a suite only inside the principal residence; Township bylaws must also permit it. Confirm the parcel's ALR status and permits before valuing any second dwelling. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: A listing mentions a mobile home pad on the property. Does that make it a mobile home park purchase?** No. A pad on a private acreage parcel, described in listing text, is not a manufactured-home park: there is no park tenancy, no park rules and no 55+ status, and the pad's own legality is a Township document check. The verified 55+ parks in Langley are the three Brookswood parks in Section 2, under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. https://jaredgibbons.ca/campbell-valley.html
- **Q: Is this the same page as campbell-valley-acreages-farms-for-sale?** No — they are two distinct pages. This file covers campbell-valley, the area/homes page; the acreages/farms slug is a separate topic feed page with its own batch-15 research file and its own 55+ notes in this set. https://jaredgibbons.ca/campbell-valley.html and https://jaredgibbons.ca/campbell-valley-acreages-farms-for-sale.html
- **Q: How active is this market, for timing a purchase?** Very thin in the published data: Campbell Valley recorded 16 detached listings and 0 detached sales in September 2026, so no sub-area median exists for that month and none is stated. Price from comparables over a longer look-back and wider geography, not from the Langley community median, which is board context and expressly not Campbell Valley's. (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, as recorded in the batch-15 research file.)
- **Q: Does provincial small-scale multi-unit housing law let me add units to a Campbell Valley acreage?** Generally no, on size alone: Township SSMUH eligibility under Bylaw 6020 ends at 4,050 m². The parcels on this page are outside that shape, and parcel-level eligibility is UNVERIFIED in this file. Source: batch-15 research file for this area.

Source: BC Ministry K-12 file, as recorded in the batch-15 research file.

Transit serving a specific Campbell Valley parcel is UNVERIFIED on an official source for this file; the sub-area is rural acreage country in the feed's own framing. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Langley/langley-memorial-hospital and https://jaredgibbons.ca/campbell-valley.html
- **Q: If I buy 55+ strata elsewhere in Langley instead, can my younger spouse live with me?** Yes — Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption; the same regulation covers legacy residents, live-in caregivers and the defined children cases. That is strata law in action in the urban complexes — a Campbell Valley acreage house has no age bylaw to exempt anyone from. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
