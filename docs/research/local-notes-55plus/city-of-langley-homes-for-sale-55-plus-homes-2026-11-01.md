# City of Langley — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Scope note: this file covers the CITY of Langley only — a separate municipality from the Township of Langley, with its own council, Official Community Plan and Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300. It serves the city-of-langley-homes-for-sale slug, whose feed at reading was detached-led (65 results, first page all detached houses), while the City's verified 55+ ownership stock is attached: 4 strata complexes — three condo buildings in one 205/206 Street cluster and one gated rancher townhouse community on 53 Avenue. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; exemptions attach to a "specified resident." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, all strata residential rental-restriction bylaws are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals. This overrides the "rentals not allowed" wording still displayed for Parkway Estates and Parkway II. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw, 2025, No. 3300** is the City of Langley's current zoning bylaw; it repeals Zoning Bylaw 1996 No. 2100. Its exact adoption date is UNVERIFIED in this program — a rules-gaps date does not match the City's November 17, 2025 public-hearing notice for Bylaw No. 3300, so no adoption date is stated here. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/public-hearing-noticepdf
- **SSMUH / plex homes:** Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024, permits 4 units on RS1 and RS2 lots, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — the rule behind feed cards marketing a City lot as having "fourplex potential" (listing text only; check the specific lot). https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/bylaw-3284
- **Secondary suites (Bylaw No. 3300):** a suite must not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area; the registered owner must reside on the lot (s. 2.4); at least one extra off-street parking space is required; and the suite must be inspected, approved through a building permit and recorded in the City's secondary suites registry; a carriage/garden ground-floor unit is capped at 100 m². https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw-2025-no-3300pdf-2
- **Short-term rentals in Langley City: UNVERIFIED** — no City STR bylaw, licence requirement or principal-residence rule was read on an official City page in this program. Confirm with the City before any Airbnb/Vrbo claim.
- **Property taxes:** payments received after the July 2, 2026 due date incur a penalty; the penalty percentage is UNVERIFIED — the City page states a penalty applies but does not state the rate. The same page lists City Hall at 20399 Douglas Crescent and a 2.2% processing fee on credit-card payments. https://www.langleycity.ca/PayPropertyTaxes
- **Tax relief at 55+:** the Province's regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in the City of Langley?** Four strata complexes: Huntsfield Green (gated rancher townhouses, 19649 53 Ave), Parkway Estates (5360 205 St), Parkway II (5363 206 St) and Glenmont Park (5375 205 St). That is the whole verified ownership list for the City — no verified 55+ park, co-op or life lease inside the City boundary. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: This page shows detached houses — where are the 55+ homes?** Not on its first page. The feed at reading was 65 results, first page all detached, while the City's board shelf is attached-led (195 attached listings vs 64 detached in September 2026). The 55+ product is the attached stock in Section 2 — search by complex name, not by this feed alone. https://jaredgibbons.ca/city-of-langley-homes-for-sale.html (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report)
- **Q: Is Morningside Estates a fifth option?** UNCONFIRMED. No source states both that name and a 55+ restriction for 5365 205 St; its verified neighbour is Glenmont Park at 5375 205 St. Do not rely on the name until a strata document or listing for that address states the age rule. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a Parkway or Glenmont unit?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children and live-in caregivers are exempt in defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: The records say "rentals not allowed" at Parkway Estates. Can I rent my unit out?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid Nov 24, 2022, whatever a building-data site still displays. The tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals; the City's own STR rules are UNVERIFIED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Building by building, and the 205/206 cluster is restrictive: building sources record pets not allowed at Parkway Estates and Parkway II, and a Glenmont Park listing describes no pets. Huntsfield Green's current listing records pets "Yes With Restrictions" — read the specific listing and the bylaws together. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/parkway-estates-5360-205-street and https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/19649-53-avenue/5
- **Q: What do strata fees cover in these buildings?** More than in most condos, per current listings: Parkway II listings state heat, hot water and electricity are covered, and a Glenmont Park listing states electricity/gas/hot water are included. Confirm the current fee and inclusions on the Form B for the specific unit. https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/5363-206-street/210
- **Q: Is Harrison Landing a condo I can buy?** No. Harrison Landing (20899 Douglas Crescent) is a rental retirement residence — 67 suites marketed to independent seniors aged 55+, registered as an assisted-living residence. The condo-style suites are rented, not sold. https://harrisoncommunities.com/senior-housing-langley/
- **Q: If I buy a detached City house instead, can it hold a suite or extra units for family or a caregiver?** Possibly: RS1/RS2 lots permit 4 units (6 within 400 m of a frequent bus stop) under Bylaw No. 3284, and a secondary suite under Bylaw No. 3300 is capped at the lesser of 100 m² and 40%, with owner-occupancy and registry recording required. Verify the specific lot with the City — a card's "fourplex potential" is listing text. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/bylaw-3284 and https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw-2025-no-3300pdf-2
- **Q: Where is the hospital, and is it in the City?** Langley Memorial Hospital (22051 Fraser Highway) is the Langleys' hospital with 24/7 emergency — and Fraser Health places it in the Township, immediately outside the City boundary. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/langley/langley-memorial-hospital
