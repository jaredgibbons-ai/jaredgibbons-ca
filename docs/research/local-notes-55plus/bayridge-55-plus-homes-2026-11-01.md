## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **District zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010, Section 200** — the RS single-family zones state minimum site areas of 8,094 m² in RS1, 1,625 m² in RS2, 975 m² in RS3 and RS10, 731 m² in RS4 and 488 m² in RS5, and list secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses in the single-family zones; secondary suites must be registered with the District. The bylaw's ground-oriented dwelling rules state 4 dwellings maximum per lot, and 6 maximum if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf and https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018** covers the area District-wide (Council adopted OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 5406, 2025, aligning the OCP with the Interim Housing Needs Report); a local area plan naming Bayridge is UNVERIFIED (none confirmed). https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/housing/official-community-plan-housing-policy-update
- **Hazard permits:** a Foreshore Development Permit is required for work within the Foreshore Development Permit Area (waterfront property or other properties at risk of coastal flooding) — the live check on Marine Drive waterfront lots; an Environmental Development Permit for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank; and a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit for work within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **District taxes 2026:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026; approximately 50% of the tax bill is collected by the District and remitted to other taxing authorities. Water and sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes and https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees
- **Additional school tax:** at Bayridge price points the Province's additional school tax can apply — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

**Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Bayridge?**
A: None were verified. West Vancouver's whole verified 55+ strata stock is three Ambleside buildings — Westview Terrace (1390 Duchess Ave), Tudor Gardens (843 22nd St) and Lincoln Gardens (2110 Marine Dr) — all east of Dundarave. In Bayridge itself the buyable stock is freehold detached houses only. https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/on-the-market/55-plus-buildings-west-vancouver/

**Q: Is Bayridge an official neighbourhood?**
A: No official District neighbourhood or planning-area name "Bayridge" was confirmed in District sources — it is a local area/site name for the West Bay–Westmount slope, covered District-wide by OCP Bylaw No. 4985, 2018. Market the verifiable anchors (West Bay Elementary, Benbow Park, West Bay Park, the Marine Drive shore), not an official-neighbourhood claim. https://westvancouver.ca/government-administration/major-projects/official-community-plan-review

**Q: Is there any seniors rental or care in Bayridge or Dundarave?**
A: None verified in either. The registry's West Vancouver residences and the District's seniors rental cluster are all in Ambleside/Park Royal: Hollyburn House, Amica West Vancouver, Amica Lions Gate, Kiwanis Manor, Westerleigh PARC (rental) and Inglewood Care Centre (long-term care). A Bayridge owner who needs rental or care is moving east to Ambleside. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3AWest%20Vancouver&f%5B1%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=desc

**Q: Can I put a suite in a Bayridge house for family or a caregiver?**
A: The RS single-family zones list a secondary suite and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses, and suites must be registered with the District; ground-oriented rules cap a lot at 4 dwellings (6 within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop). That suite route — not an age-restricted purchase — is the in-area way to house a caregiver or adult family member. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf and https://westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/development-regulations/single-family-lots/secondary-suites

**Q: My spouse is under 55 — how do the exemptions work if we buy a 55+ strata in Ambleside?**
A: Strata Property Regulation 7.01 (expanded May 1, 2023) exempts a younger spouse/partner of the specified 55+ resident, children under 19 in that resident's care, adult children the resident cared for before age 19, live-in caregivers, and legacy residents. The bylaw restricts who may live in the unit, not who may own it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

**Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building? Building data for the Ambleside buildings still says "rentals not allowed."**
A: That line is stale. Every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, so long-term renting is permitted as a matter of provincial law; the tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

**Q: What hazard permits can apply on a Bayridge waterfront or creek-side lot?**
A: A Foreshore Development Permit in the Foreshore Development Permit Area (with Flood Construction Level requirements where the risk applies), an Environmental Development Permit within 15 metres of a watercourse bank, and a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits

**Q: Where is the hospital and health care?**
A: Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver, serves the North Shore including West Vancouver; the West Vancouver Community Health Centre at 2121 Marine Drive provides primary health care to residents of West Vancouver, Lions Bay and Bowen Island, and the North Vancouver Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at Suite 200, 221 West Esplanade, North Vancouver. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/west-vancouver-community-health-centre

**Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?**
A: One corridor: route 250 on Marine Drive, east through Dundarave, Ambleside and Park Royal over the Lions Gate Bridge to downtown Vancouver, and west toward Horseshoe Bay. Off Marine Drive, the slope is car-oriented. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt250.pdf

**Q: What extra taxes apply at Bayridge price points?**
A: District taxes were due July 2, 2026 (5% penalties July 2 and September 2), utilities are metered and billed quarterly, and the Province's additional school tax can apply: 0.2% on the residential assessed portion between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate

- **The school result on file is one address deep.** The SD45 locator pairing of West Bay Elementary + Rockridge Secondary was recorded for the sample address 3175 Thompson Place only; the slope's other streets have no verified locator result in the research — verify each listing address rather than extending the pairing. https://westvancouverschools.ca/admissions/student-admissions-guidelines/
