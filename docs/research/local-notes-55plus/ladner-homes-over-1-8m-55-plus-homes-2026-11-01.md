## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) at this band, as published by the Province:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000; 2% of the portion greater than $200,000 and up to and including $2,000,000; 3% of the portion greater than $2,000,000 — every purchase in this band reaches the 3% tier on its top portion. If a property has residential property worth over $3,000,000, a further 2% tax applies to the residential property value greater than $3,000,000; on a mixed-class property the further 2% applies only to the residential portion. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Neither BC purchase exemption reaches this band:** the first time home buyers' program full exemption caps at a fair market value of $835,000 and the newly built home exemption's full threshold is $1,100,000 with elimination at $1,150,000 — both far below this page's floor, so buyers in this band should budget full PTT. (No FHSA or Home Buyers' Plan dollar limit is stated in this file — UNVERIFIED, not read off a live CRA page in this research.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Home owner grant phase-out at this band:** the Province states the grant threshold for 2026 is $2,075,000 — a full regular grant at or below that assessed (or partitioned) value, reduced by $5 for each $1,000 of assessed value over $2,075,000, and $0 for the regular grant if the value is over $2,189,000 — so many homes in this band will receive a reduced grant or none. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017.** Its residential zones include RS1, RS2 and RS3 at a minimum lot size for subdivision of 4,000 m²; RS4 at 610 m²; RS5 at 550 m² or 460 m² depending on designation; RS6 at 390 m²; RS7 at 335 m²; RD1 at 836 m²; RD2 and RD3 at 4,000 m²; and RD4 at 504 m²; there is no RD5 zone, and zoning is parcel-by-parcel on the City zoning maps. The RS1 zone lists a secondary suite as an accessory use to a single detached dwelling. **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024** governs building in Ladner — a single consolidated plan with no area-plan schedules. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Suites and SSMUH:** Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing rules allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide, subject to the bylaw and to any hazard or streamside permit area on the lot — relevant to a buyer in this band adding a suite or coach house for a caregiver or family member. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Streamside rules:** all properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area — a live diligence item on Ladner's slough-edge and riverfront lots. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **ALR edges:** farm land on the Ladner edges is designated Agriculture (A) in the OCP (Bylaw No. 8400, 2024, s. 1.5.1), and Agricultural Land Reserve rules apply where land is in the ALR; ALR status is parcel-specific and is checked on the Agricultural Land Commission's maps. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Foreshore tenure, for the riverfront stock in this band:** the Province owns nearly all saltwater foreshore; private moorage is held by lease or licence via FrontCounter BC under General Permission or Specific Permission — upland title, a dock, and foreshore tenure are three different things. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
- **How Delta Green's 55+ status was secured at approval:** the City's 2018 public hearing material for the 51 Street site discusses the project's "55+ age category" and a restrictive covenant on "adaptable-ready" design under the BC Building Code. A strata age bylaw and a covenant are different instruments — confirm which one(s) are registered on title and in the bylaws via the Form B and strata documents. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/179807/
- **Property taxes and 55+ relief:** Delta's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. The provincial regular property tax deferment loan program covers homeowners 55 and older, and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) covers seniors 65+ — separate from the regular-grant phase-out above. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
- **Subsidized seniors rental applications** for Delta run through BC Housing's Housing Registry (Zone 3 — Richmond and West Delta directory, May 2025). https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

- **Q: Is any of Ladner's 55+ ownership stock in this over-$1.8M band?** No source states that. - **Q: How many Ladner homes are for sale over $1.8M right now?** That figure cannot be stated from the sources used here. SnapStats publishes no price-band cut and does not cover South Delta at all, and no official source publishes a count or share for the $1.8M band; the live page's own feed is the only place the current band count can be seen, and it changes as listings come and go. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-homes-over-1-8m.html
- **Q: If I want a legal 55+ bylaw in Ladner, what are my actual options?** Three complexes: Delta Green (condo form, village core), South Pointe (gated bare-land strata ranchers, Neilsen Grove) and Laburnum Park Place (bare-land strata ranchers, Delta Manor). In a bare-land strata you own the lot and the house and the strata carries the common roads, gates and landscaping — a different purchase from a condo unit, and the Form B and bylaws still govern. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3048423-5246-laburnum-park-place-delta-bc-v4k-4s6/brochure
- **Q: Is there extra property transfer tax on a home over $3 million?** Yes. The Province applies a further 2% tax to the residential property value greater than $3,000,000, on top of the general tiers (1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000); on a mixed-class property the further 2% applies only to the residential portion. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Does the first-time buyer exemption or the new-home exemption help at $1.8M?** No. The first time home buyers' program full exemption caps at a fair market value of $835,000, and the newly built home exemption is fully eliminated at $1,150,000 — both far below this band, so a buyer here budgets full property transfer tax. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: Will I still get the home owner grant on a home in this band?** Possibly a reduced one, or none. For 2026 the grant threshold is $2,075,000: full regular grant at or below that value, reduced by $5 per $1,000 over it, and $0 for the regular grant over $2,189,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a home in this band, or a 55+ unit, long-term?** In a BC strata, long-term rental bans no longer exist — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022; a tenant in a 55+ building still has to satisfy the age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. A freehold detached house is not under a strata rental bylaw at all. Delta Green's own rental position must be confirmed in its bylaws — no building source in this research states it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What should I check on a riverfront or acreage home in this band?** Three parcel-level checks: the flood and streamside position (the lot sits behind the City's dike system, and land within 30 m of a slough or ditch is in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area), the foreshore tenure for any dock or moorage (provincial Crown land, by lease or licence), and the parcel's ALR status on the Agricultural Land Commission's maps. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Where is the care ladder if I buy in this band and later need help?** In Ladner itself: Augustine House on Arthur Drive (independent and assisted living plus care, including a 20-unit Fraser Health assisted living program at 55+ accessed through a case manager), and Delta Hospital in the village. All of the care stock is rental or care — none of it is in this price band, because none of it is for sale. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
