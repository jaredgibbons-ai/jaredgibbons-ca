## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **How the age rule is actually verified in a purchase:** the bylaw that governs is the strata's registered bylaw set, read with the Form B Information Certificate and the strata documents — not the listing write-up. This program's files repeatedly found listing and directory text that was stale (rental bans still displayed years after voiding) or conflicting (build years, tenure, addresses) — each conflict is flagged in the relevant sibling file rather than smoothed over. Sources: the sibling files in ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/ (e.g. the Rosewood Court rental conflict in ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/east-central-maple-ridge-guide-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md, Section 3)
- **Non-strata 55+ forms run on different law.** Manufactured-home parks: the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and its Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003), with park age rules operating through park rules and assignment consent (Human Rights Code s. 10(2)(b)(i)). Co-operatives and life leases: the organisation's own membership / lease documents — the provincial strata exemptions do not automatically apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Representation rules for the first conversation:** BCFSA states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party; dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Tax relief that matters at 55+:** the Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What's the fastest way to reach Jared about a 55+ property?** Phone or text — the contact page states most messages get a response the same day, often within a couple of hours during business hours, and that email works too but slightly slower. The page asks a first message to cover what you're looking at (buying, selling, just starting), any specific property, and a timeline. Office and cell are the same number on the page: 604-928-1361; email jaredgibbons@royallepage.ca. https://jaredgibbons.ca/contact.html
- **Q: A listing says a building is 55+. How do I know that's real?** Check the strata's registered bylaws and the Form B, not the listing copy. Since Nov 24, 2022 only a 55+ bylaw can be valid at all — a building advertised as 40+ or 45+ strata is advertising a bylaw that no longer has effect — and this program found buildings marketed as 55+ whose current sources state no age restriction at all (they are listed, with sources, in the sibling area files). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases, and live-in caregivers are exempt. In parks, co-ops and life leases the organisation's own documents govern — ask for them at the same time. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can the agent showing me a 55+ unit also act for the seller?** No. BCFSA states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party; dual agency has been prohibited in BC since 2018. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are there mandatory charges beyond strata fees anywhere?** In one verified case, yes: Mayfair Terrace (Port Coquitlam) carries a listing-stated mandatory base service plan on top of strata fees — $715/mo for the first resident and $400 for a second (MLS R3179064, Oct 2026) — covering meals, housekeeping, emergency response, activities and transportation. Treat the figures as listing-stated and confirm the current amounts in the strata documents and service agreement. https://www.zolo.ca/port-coquitlam-real-estate/2266-atkins-avenue/311
- **Q: What's the difference between the 55+ forms — strata, park, co-op, life lease?** Strata: you own a lot under the Strata Property Act, with the provincial exemptions. Park: you own the home and rent the pad under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act; the age rule runs through park rules and assignment consent. Co-op: you buy shares and join (Haney Pioneer Village in Maple Ridge is 60+, not 55+). Life lease: you purchase a right to occupy, with the entrance fee returned at termination less a set percentage (Cedar Valley Manor, Mission). The legal form changes the financing, resale and exemption rules — it is the first thing to pin down on any "55+" enquiry. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/ and https://www.missionseniors.ca/our-communities/cedar-valley-manor/
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or a buyer who purchases without living in the unit. That trade-off is built into every BC 55+ strata since the 2022 law change. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes at 55+?** The Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
