## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01) — the catchment-relevant ones:** residents lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, **children under 19 in the specified resident's care**, and **adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19**. It is those child exemptions that make a 55+ home's catchment a live question for some households: an exempt child under 19 living in the home attends the catchment school for that address. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Catchments are the district's lines, not the market's.** SD36 catchments are set by street address through the School Locator and boundary map; they can change on enrolment review; and SnapStats publishes NO school-catchment segment — there is no catchment-based count, share or median for any school, and any "in-catchment median" claim is UNVERIFIED by construction. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=BC36 and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/south-surrey-school-catchments.md
- **School existence vs school assignment are different claims.** The BC Ministry K-12 file is the existence authority (name + street address, 2025/2026); a district URL slug is not evidence of a school or a catchment — the phantom "East Newton Elementary" was created from exactly such a slug in an earlier batch. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/south-surrey-school-catchments.md
- **Surrey-side building rules (Surrey addresses only):** Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000 SSMUH bands — up to 3 units on lots under 280 sq m, up to 4 units on lots over 280 sq m, up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex unit capped at 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area; short-term rentals owner-only with a $358-per-year business licence; property taxes due July 2 (5% + 5% penalties); current OCP is Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — never cite the replaced Bylaw No. 18020 as current. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **White Rock-side building rules (White Rock addresses only — a different city, same district):** White Rock's Zoning Bylaw 2024, No. 2506, adopted June 27, 2024, caps small-scale multi-unit housing at 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots over 280 m², and 6 units within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop; its OCP is Bylaw 2017, No. 2220. Both are listed on the City of White Rock's own Resources & Documents page. https://www.whiterockcity.ca/514/Resources-Documents and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How do I find the catchment school for a South Surrey home?** Enter the exact address in the Surrey Schools School Locator. Catchments are set by street address, not by neighbourhood or MLS sub-area name, so the listing's area name cannot answer the question — and the utility page's own practice is to check before booking a showing and again before subjects are removed, because lines can change on enrolment review. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=BC36 and https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-school-catchments.html
- **Q: Is there a recorded catchment result for a South Surrey address in this research?** Yes — one: 1785-148 St (Sunnyside Park) → H.T. Thrift Elementary / Semiahmoo Secondary (SD36 School Locator, run 2026-11-01). Both schools are confirmed in the Ministry K-12 file at 1739 148 St and 1785 148 St respectively. It is evidence for that address, not for the whole Sunnyside Park sub-area — the live page's own Sunnyside summary splits the area at 152 Street, which is exactly why area-level school claims fail. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/south-surrey-school-catchments.md
- **Q: What is the catchment for Semiahmoo Estates / Bishop's Green / Peninsula Village?** UNVERIFIED for each — no locator run for any 55+ complex's own address is on record in this program. The complexes sit on the same 152 Street corridor as the recorded sample, but corridor proximity is not a catchment result; run the specific address in the locator before making any school claim for a unit. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=BC36
- **Q: Why would a 55+ buyer care about catchments at all?** Two sourced reasons. First, the provincial exemptions let defined younger people live in a 55+ home — a child under 19 in the specified resident's care, an adult child cared for before 19, a younger spouse or partner, a live-in caregiver — and a school-age exempt resident attends the address's catchment school. Second, resale: the 55+ bylaw narrows the resident pool, but a tenant or exempt household with children still values the catchment, and sellers market on it — which is why the claim has to be locator-verified, not assumed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Does all of White Rock go to one secondary school?** The live page states it does not: it presents White Rock Elementary feeding Semiahmoo Secondary and Peace Arch Elementary feeding Earl Marriott Secondary. Those feeder statements are the page's own copy and were not confirmed against a district document opened in this pass — treat them as to-be-verified and run the exact address in the locator. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-school-catchments.html
- **Q: Is there an "East Newton Elementary," and what is East Newton's catchment?** No such school exists — it appears in neither the Ministry K-12 file (2025/2026) nor SD36's directory, and no school stands at 7235 144 St. There is also no East Newton locator result on record: the recorded address ranges jump 7069–7195 → 7263–7263, so no range contains 7235. Never take a school name from a district URL slug. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/south-surrey-school-catchments.md
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a South Surrey 55+ complex?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); Peninsula Village listings state the local form expressly — "One person 55+." Confirm each complex's bylaw wording in its Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3098715-2500-152-street-67-surrey-bc-v4p-1m8/brochure
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ complex as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit to a family that wants the catchment?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant household still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption (a 55+ tenant with children under 19 in their care fits the exemption frame), and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Is there a published catchment premium for Semiahmoo or Earl Marriott homes?** No published figure exists to quote: SnapStats has no catchment segment, so there is no "in-catchment median" for Semiahmoo, Elgin Park, Earl Marriott or Grandview Heights — any catchment premium claim for a listing must come from that listing's own comparables, not from a segment statistic. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/south-surrey-school-catchments.md
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
