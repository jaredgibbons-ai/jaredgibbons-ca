# Sunshine Hills Woods — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Sunshine Hills Woods is the southern tier of North Delta, in the City of Delta, around Watershed Park and Cougar Canyon. The Paragon (FVREB MLS) sub-area name combines two City of Delta neighbourhood names from the former North Delta Area Plan (Schedule C.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950) — Sunshine Hills, neighbourhood 12 of 14, and Sunshine Woods, neighbourhood 13 of 14. The City publishes no street-by-street written boundary for either neighbourhood; the working grid used in research is 72 Avenue north, 64 Avenue south, Watershed Park and Lyon Road west, Scott Road (120 Street) east — a working grid, not an official boundary. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf
- Watershed Park, 11600 Kittson Pkwy, is described by the City as Delta's largest park, with eleven kilometres of gravel trails. Cougar Canyon Environmental Reserve is at 11300 72 Ave and Westview Park at 6500 Westview Dr. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/watershed-park
- Kittson Parkway cuts through the Watershed Park area; 72 Avenue and 64 Avenue run east–west; Scott Road (120 Street) is the east side, with Highway 91 access via 64 Avenue. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt340.pdf
- School District 37 catchments are address-based. For the sample address 11285 Bond Blvd, the SD37 School Locator result is Sunshine Hills Elementary (K–7), 11285 Bond Blvd., and Seaquam Secondary (8–12), 11584 Lyon Rd. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- The Nicholson Road corridor at the east side is the sub-area's strata pocket: Scott & Nicholson (6950 and 6960 Nicholson Road), Nicholson Green (6866 Nicholson Road) and Chateau Wynd Estates (6940 Nicholson Road), beside the Scott Road shopping node at 72 Avenue. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Transit: route 340 runs Scottsdale Exchange to 22nd Street Station via Scott Road, 64 Avenue, Kittson Parkway, Highway 91 and the Alex Fraser Bridge, through the Watershed Park edge; route 364 runs along the south edge on 64 Avenue; route 319 runs along the north edge on 72 Avenue; the R6 RapidBus runs on Scott Road at the east side. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt340.pdf and https://www.translink.ca/r6
- George Mackie Library (Fraser Valley Regional Library), 8440 112th Street, is north of the area in Nordel; no recreation centre inside the working grid was confirmed — the North Delta Recreation Centre at 11415 84 Avenue serves North Delta. The nearest hospital is Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, in Ladner; the North Delta Public Health Unit is at 11245 84 Avenue. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- Cougar Creek is a salmon-bearing watercourse running through Surrey and Delta, with City work on creek bank stabilization in Cougar Canyon; streamside setback detail is lot-specific. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates in Sunshine Hills Woods: nowhere. There is no verified 55+ housing of any tenure in this sub-area (see Section 2).

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017** applies parcel by parcel; no City source names a predominant zone for Sunshine Hills Woods. Single-detached minimum lots run RS1 at 4,000 m², RS4 at 610 m², RS5 at 550 m² or 460 m², RS6 at 390 m² and RS7 at 335 m²; duplex minimums run RD1 at 836 m², RD2 and RD3 at 4,000 m², and RD4 at 504 m²; there is no RD5 zone. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Seniors congregate housing is a named use in Delta's apartment zones.** The City's Apartment Residential 6 (RA6) zone, added to Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 in 2024 for six-storey apartment development under the OCP's Neighbourhood Centres and Corridors designation, lists "seniors congregate housing" as a permitted principal use alongside apartment buildings. No RA6 site in this sub-area was confirmed — the permission exists in the bylaw, not yet as a building here. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future")** is Delta's adopted OCP, adopted July 8, 2024 — a single consolidated plan with no area-plan schedules; Map 2 shows a Small Scale Residential designation for this part of North Delta. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Secondary suites and small-scale multi-unit housing:** small-scale multi-unit housing is permitted on all single-detached and duplex lots citywide, and Delta's SSMUH amendments allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — in this sub-area of detached houses, this is the practical route to housing a parent or caregiver on the same lot. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/235301/ and http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Streamside rules bite on canyon-side lots.** Any property within 30 m of a stream falls in Delta's Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area, which can limit additions, coach houses and rebuilds — directly relevant on Cougar Creek lots when a buyer is pricing suite or garden-suite potential. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Property tax deferment at 55+:** BC's Property Tax Deferment Program (regular program) lets a homeowner who is 55 or older during the current year defer annual property taxes on a principal residence as a provincial loan; the City of Delta's tax page links the program and the Home Owner Grant. Delta's 2026 tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility and http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ condo or townhome I can buy in Sunshine Hills Woods?** No verified one. Scott & Nicholson, the area's only condo development, states pets and rentals allowed with restrictions and no age restriction in its listing sources; the townhome complexes state none either. A buyer who needs a verified 55+ purchase has no option in this sub-area on the sources checked. https://www.real-estate.ca/listing/r3103586-501-6950-nicholson-road-delta-british-columbia-v4e0a8/brochure
- **Q: What 55+ housing is nearby, then?** The verified North Delta options sit in the two sub-areas north of here: Sunstone 55+ Apartments, a 70-unit market rental in Nordel, and in Scottsdale the three subsidized rentals — Scott Road Lodge, Strawberry Hill Manor and White Birch Manor. There is no verified 55+ ownership strata anywhere in North Delta in this research; Ladner's verified 55+ ownership buildings are in South Delta (see the Ladner files). http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf and https://www.comfortlife.ca/retirement-homes/sunstone-apartments/1370
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Delta?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain where a valid 55+ bylaw exists: the tenant still has to satisfy the age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. Scott & Nicholson's own listings already state rentals allowed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: If there's no 55+ complex, how do downsizers here usually stay in the area?** On the sources, the in-area mechanism is the house lot itself: Delta's SSMUH rules allow a secondary suite, coach house or garden suite in combination with a detached or duplex dwelling where the zoning applies, so a downsizer can add a ground-level suite on a family lot instead of moving to a complex. Confirm the parcel's zone sheet and any Cougar Creek streamside setback before pricing that plan. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the Nicholson Road buildings?** It is building-by-building. Scott & Nicholson listings record pets allowed with restrictions (cats and dogs, number limit two, per listing data). No age or pet rule was found stated for Nicholson Green or Chateau Wynd Estates — UNCONFIRMED for those two; check the Form B and bylaws. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/6950-nicholson-road/416
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day, and is there a seniors bus?** The working routes are at the edges: route 340 on Kittson Parkway / 64 Avenue to 22nd Street Station, route 364 on 64 Avenue, route 319 on 72 Avenue, and the R6 RapidBus on Scott Road. The interior streets have no route of their own in the timetables checked. Separately, the City runs a free Delta Seniors Bus for Delta residents aged 65+ — note the 65+ threshold, not 55+ — with curbside pick-up/drop-off inside Delta and medical appointments given priority. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt340.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/seniors-services/seniors-bus
- **Q: Is there a seniors centre or recreation centre in the sub-area?** No — none inside the working grid was confirmed. The North Delta seniors centre is Kennedy Seniors Recreation Centre at 11760 88 Avenue (recreation for 50+), and the North Delta Recreation Centre is at 11415 84 Avenue, both in Nordel. http://www.delta.ca/services/seniors/seniors-recreation-centres/lists/seniors-recreation-centres/kennedy-seniors-recreation-centre
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes here at 55+?** Yes, if the home is your principal residence — BC's regular deferment program is open at 55 or older during the current year (also to surviving spouses and persons with disabilities). Delta's tax page links the program; the City's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility and http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The sub-area name itself hides the split.** "Sunshine Hills Woods" is a Paragon combination of two separate City neighbourhoods — Sunshine Hills and Sunshine Woods — and the City publishes no boundary for the combined name, so two listings under the same sub-area label can sit in different catchments and different park pockets. Verify by address, not by the label. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf
- **Listing aggregators file this area's flagship building under two different sub-areas.** Scott & Nicholson listings variously describe the building as in "Sunshine Hills," "Sunshine Hills Woods" and "North Delta's heart" — the building is one physical complex at 6950/6960 Nicholson Road regardless of the label a portal assigns. Its age position does not vary between those listings: none states an age restriction. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/6950-nicholson-road/416
- **The co-op in the canyon is family housing, despite the quiet setting.** Cougar Canyon Co-op (6800–6846 Nicholson Rd, 18 units) lists "No age requirement" in the City guide and is described by the co-op federation as family-oriented — the canyon setting reads as retirement product and is not. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf and https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **A big lot near the canyon may not build like a big lot elsewhere.** Cougar Creek is a salmon-bearing watercourse with City bank-stabilization work in Cougar Canyon, and the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area applies within 30 m of a stream — check the setback before valuing suite, coach-house or rebuild potential. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **The market signal here was the softest in North Delta in the last verified snapshot.** In the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, this sub-area's detached sales ratio was 6% — the softest of North Delta's four sub-areas that month — on 50 detached listings and 3 sales. A downsizer selling a detached house here is selling into the slowest of the four pockets; price to street-level comparables, not to the North Delta community median. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.)
- **Watershed Park is the amenity, and it is a working forest park, not a facility.** The City describes it as Delta's largest park with eleven kilometres of gravel trails for walking, cycling, jogging and horseback riding — there is no recreation centre, pool or seniors programming inside the sub-area to go with it. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/watershed-park
