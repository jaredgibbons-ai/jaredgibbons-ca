# Langley City — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Scope note: this file covers the CITY of Langley only — a separate municipality from the Township of Langley, with its own council, Official Community Plan and zoning bylaw. The site slug behind it (langleycity) is a land-use / OCP information page, not a listing feed. The city's verified 55+ ownership stock is 4 strata complexes, verified by name in fresh checks for this file: three condo buildings in one 205/206 Street cluster and one gated rancher townhouse community on 53 Avenue. One widely circulated fifth name, Morningside Estates, stays UNCONFIRMED.**

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The City of Langley is a small, separate municipality inside the larger Township of Langley; the two have different councils, bylaws, tax notices and planning frames, and a Township fact applied to a City property is an error. The City's Official Community Plan was adopted on November 22, 2021 and is built on the "Nexus of Community" vision adopted July 23, 2018. https://www.langleycity.ca/city-hall/reports-plans-strategies/strategic-vision
- The board geography trap, stated plainly: SnapStats treats "Langley" as ONE board community combining the City and the Township (11 sub-areas, including a "Langley City" sub-area row). There is NO published median price for the City of Langley alone — the combined Langley medians blend in Township product and must never be quoted as City prices (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report). ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/langleycity.md

- The 55+ cluster is walkable downtown product: Parkway Estates (5360 205 St), Parkway II (5363 206 St) and Glenmont Park (5375 205 St) sit within a block of each other at the city's south downtown edge, and listings for Parkway II state the buildings are steps from the Langley Seniors Centre, with shopping, recreation and walking trails nearby (listing-stated). https://vancouvernashdom.com/langley-langley-city-apartments/SearchResults.form/?_pg=9

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This overrides the "rentals not allowed" wording still displayed for Parkway Estates and Parkway II in building-data sources. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw, 2025, No. 3300** is the City of Langley's current zoning bylaw; it repeals Zoning Bylaw 1996 No. 2100 (cited at s. 1.1 of the new bylaw). Its exact adoption date is UNVERIFIED in this program — the rules-gaps file gives a date that does not match the City's November 17, 2025 public-hearing notice for Bylaw No. 3300, so no adoption date is stated here. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/public-hearing-noticepdf and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **SSMUH / plex homes:** the City amended its Zoning Bylaw to permit small-scale multi-unit housing on RS1 and RS2 lots by Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024 — RS1/RS2 permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **Secondary suites (Bylaw No. 3300):** a suite must not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the gross floor area of the building; the registered owner must reside on the lot; at least one off-street parking space is required in addition to the two normally required for a single detached use; and the suite must be inspected, approved through a building permit and recorded in the City's secondary suites registry. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **Garden suites and carriage homes (Bylaw No. 3300, s. 2.4(d)):** permitted in the R1 zone; maximum gross ground floor area 100 m² for a carriage home or garden suite in R1, maximum total floor area of accessory buildings on an R1 lot 60 m², and the registered owner must reside on the lot. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **Short-term rentals in Langley City: UNVERIFIED** — no City STR bylaw, licence requirement or principal-residence rule could be read on an official City page in this program. Confirm with the City before any Airbnb/Vrbo claim is written. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **Property taxes:** City of Langley payments received after the July 2 due date incur a penalty; the penalty percentage for 2026 is UNVERIFIED — the City payment page read states that a penalty applies but does not state the rate. The same page lists City Hall at 20399 Douglas Crescent and a 2.2% processing fee on credit-card payments. https://www.langleycity.ca/PayPropertyTaxes
- **Tax relief at 55+:** the Province's regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Langley City?** Four strata complexes: Huntsfield Green (gated rancher townhouses, 19649 53 Ave), Parkway Estates (5360 205 St), Parkway II (5363 206 St) and Glenmont Park (5375 205 St). That is the whole verified ownership list for the City — there is no verified 55+ park, co-operative or life lease inside the City boundary in this program. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Morningside Estates a fifth option?** UNCONFIRMED. The name and the 5365 205 St address circulate as a 55+ complex and units at that address exist, but no source found — in the earlier Langley files or in a fresh search for this file — states both the name and a 55+ restriction for 5365 205 St. Its verified neighbour is Glenmont Park at 5375 205 St. Do not rely on the Morningside name until a strata document or listing for that address states the age rule. https://kw.com/property/20307-53-AVENUE-28-Langley-BC-V3A6S8/2035969692624078
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a Parkway or Glenmont unit?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases, and live-in caregivers are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: The building records say "rentals not allowed" at Parkway Estates. Can I rent my unit out?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, whatever an older bylaw set or a building-data site still displays. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. Langley City's own STR rules are UNVERIFIED in this program. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** It is building-by-building, and the 205/206 cluster is restrictive: building sources record pets not allowed at Parkway Estates and Parkway II, and a current Glenmont Park listing describes it as a no-pet complex. Huntsfield Green's current listing records pets "Yes With Restrictions" (no cats, no dogs in that listing's pet fields — read the specific listing and the bylaws together). https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/parkway-estates-5360-205-street and https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/19649-53-avenue/5
- **Q: What do strata fees cover in these buildings?** More than in most condos, per current listings: Parkway II listings state heat, hot water and electricity are all covered by the strata fee, and a Glenmont Park listing states the fee includes electricity/gas/hot water. Confirm the current fee and inclusions on the Form B for the specific unit. https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/5363-206-street/210 and https://vancouvernashdom.com/langley-langley-city-apartments/SearchResults.form/?_pg=4
- **Q: Is Harrison Landing a condo I can buy? It has full kitchens and balconies.** No. Harrison Landing (20899 Douglas Crescent) is a rental retirement residence — 67 suites, marketed to independent seniors aged 55+ — and it is registered as an assisted-living residence. The condo-style suites are rented, not sold. https://harrisoncommunities.com/senior-housing-langley/
- **Q: Where is the hospital, and is it in the City?** Langley Memorial Hospital (22051 Fraser Highway) is the Langleys' hospital, with 24/7 emergency — and its own Fraser Health listing places it in the Township, immediately outside the City boundary. A City buyer is minutes from it, but it is not a City building. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/langley/langley-memorial-hospital
- **Q: How many units can a Langley City house lot hold, if I want family or a caregiver nearby instead?** Under Bylaw No. 3284 (adopted June 17, 2024), RS1 and RS2 lots permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; a secondary suite under Bylaw No. 3300 is capped at the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's floor area, with owner-occupancy and registry recording required. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes at 55+?** The Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
