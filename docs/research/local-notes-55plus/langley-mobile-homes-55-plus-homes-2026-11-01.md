## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **The governing law for a rented pad is provincial, not municipal.** Manufactured home site tenancies are governed by BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA) wherever the park sits; in that tenure the tenant owns the manufactured home and rents the site under a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement, and landlords and tenants must have a written tenancy agreement for the site. Where both the home and the site are rented, a Residential Tenancy Agreement applies instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- **The 2026 pad-rent increase limit: 2.3% plus a proportional amount.** For manufactured home site tenancies the 2026 limit is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees; the 2027 limit is 2.2% plus the proportional amount. The proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **How an increase may be given:** only with 3 full months' written notice, only once every 12 months, and only up to the yearly limit; the landlord may not round up and may not carry forward an unused portion of a prior year's limit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Buying a park home does not reset the pad rent (MHPTA s. 28 assignment).** Assignment permanently transfers the site tenancy to the buyer; a tenant may assign only with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the regulations), an order of the director, or where the tenancy agreement authorizes it — s.28(1)(b) wording is "an order of the director" (NOT arbitrator); the landlord cannot charge a fee for consent; and on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, as does the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **A park's 55+ rule is a park rule, not a strata bylaw.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003) expressly recognises a park where every site is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 (per Human Rights Code s. 10(2)(b)(i)) and allows a park landlord to refuse an assignment where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement. The Strata Property Act exemptions do not answer a park occupancy question — get the park's rules in writing. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park closure / conversion (MHPTA s. 42):** where a landlord intends to convert all or a significant part of a park to another use, the landlord must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date, with defined additional-compensation routes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Registration and the CSA label gate every park purchase.** In BC the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered with the Manufactured Home Registry, through a lawyer or notary filing the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership; a home may not be sold without its original legible CSA decal (Z-240 for mobile homes, A-277 for modular), and a lost label requires Technical Safety BC re-certification. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Tax on a park home excludes the land.** The Province states a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, but the assessed value will not include the value of the land if the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **Township zoning frame:** Township parks sit under Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500; a Township neighbourhood-plan table names the corresponding zone for the "Manufactured Home Park" designation as "MH-1 Residential Manufactured Home Park Zone," and an MLS® data sheet for a Brookswood park home records zoning "MH-1." City of Langley property sits under the separate City of Langley Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300. The park-zone rules in either bylaw beyond the zone name: UNVERIFIED — neither bylaw's park-zone section was opened in this program. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Neighbourhood-Plan-Bylaws/Booth-Neighbourhood-Plan-(No.-6008).pdf and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-mobile-homes.md
- **No manufactured-home market statistics exist to quote.** SnapStats publishes no manufactured-home category; manufactured-home-specific sale counts, medians and days-on-market for Langley are UNVERIFIED, and the all-residential Langley community figures do not describe this shelf. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-mobile-homes.md

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which parks in this feed are actually 55+?** Three verified pad-rental parks, all in Brookswood in the Township: Cedar Creek Estates (3031 200 St), Fernridge Park (20071 24 Ave) and Pineridge (2270 196 St). Pioneer Park in Aldergrove is 55+ but is a co-operative, not a pad-rental park. Langley Grove Estates in the same feed states no age restrictions, and Forest Green Estates' age rule is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.estateblock.com/langley/5-3031-200-st-1968212 and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3109616-33-20071-24-avenue-langley-british-columbia-v2z2a1-99000/brochure and https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/24330-fraser-highway/52
- **Q: How much is pad rent on a Langley mobile home?** No official source publishes a Langley pad-rent figure, average or range, so no figure is stated here. Sibling pages on this site carry two conflicting unverified ranges ($600–$900 and $650–$950 per month); neither is adopted, and the "$510" / "$729/mo" figures in individual feed cards are individual listings' copy (the first is Pioneer Park's co-op fee, not pad rent at all). The figure that matters is the specific park's current rent in its written Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-mobile-homes.html
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up each year?** Up to the Residential Tenancy Branch limit for manufactured home site tenancies: 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026, and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027. The proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of sites in the park. Increases require 3 full months' notice and can occur only once every 12 months. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does the park raise my pad rent when I buy the home?** No. On assignment under s. 28 of the MHPTA, the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same and the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase does not change; assignment requires the landlord's prior written consent (or an order of the director) and the landlord cannot charge a fee for consent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Q: Can a park refuse me because of age?** In a 55+ park, yes on assignment: the MHPTA Regulation recognises a park where every site is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 and allows the landlord to refuse an assignment where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement. That is a park rule under tenancy law — the strata exemptions for younger spouses, caregivers and legacy residents do not automatically apply, so ask the park for its rule in writing. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a park home?** Not automatically, and the answer is park-specific. A park's occupancy rule sits in tenancy regulation and the park's own rules; Pioneer Park listings state the rule in its strictest form ("both occupants must be 55"), while other parks' spousal rules are UNCONFIRMED in the sources checked. Ask the specific park in writing before writing an offer. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www.estateblock.com/langley/134-27111-ave-1991100
- **Q: What is the monthly cost stack besides the purchase price?** In structure, not figures: pad rent under the regulated formula, plus home insurance, plus property tax on the home's assessed value excluding the land in a park, plus utilities as the specific park's agreement allocates them. No dollar figure for any element is stated here, because no official source publishes a Langley figure for any of them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **Q: What happens if the park is converted to another use?** Under MHPTA s. 42: the landlord must first have all necessary permits and approvals, then give 12 months' notice and pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date, with defined additional-compensation routes where the home cannot be moved or no site is available within a reasonable distance. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How is Pioneer Park different from the Brookswood parks?** It is a co-operative, not a pad-rental park: listings state the buyer must be accepted by the co-op, must purchase co-op shares ($9,200 in one listing, refunded upon sale) and pays a monthly co-op fee ($510 in the same listing) rather than pad rent to a landlord. Confirm the current figures and rules with the co-op — they are listing-stated, not a published tariff. https://murphyrealestategroup.ca/recip.html/1272399.search/SearchResults.form?_pg=3
- **Q: What should I check before anything else on a park home?** The CSA label: without the original legible CSA decal (Z-240 for mobile homes, A-277 for modular) the home cannot be sold until Technical Safety BC re-certification — and the sale itself is only effective if registered with the Manufactured Home Registry. Label and registration status are price and timeline facts on every park purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry

- **All of Langley's verified 55+ pad-rental parks are in one Township pocket.** Cedar Creek Estates, Fernridge Park and Pineridge are all in Brookswood; there is no verified 55+ pad-rental park in Langley City, Walnut Grove, Willoughby or Fort Langley in the roster. A buyer fixed on a 55+ park in Langley is, in practice, shopping Brookswood — or the Aldergrove co-op. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **The site contradicts itself on pad rent — twice.** One sibling page states pad fees "typically range from $600–$900 per month"; the pad-fees page states "$650–$950/month." No official source publishes either range. A buyer who budgets from either number is budgeting from unsourced copy; budget from the specific park's written tenancy agreement. https://jaredgibbons.ca/cheap-mobile-homes-langley-under-500k.html and https://jaredgibbons.ca/cost-of-mobile-home-pad-rental-fees-in-langley.html
- **A transfer does not trigger a market re-pricing of the pad.** Unlike a strata purchase, where fees are whatever the current budget says, an MHPTA assignment carries the existing pad rent and the existing next-increase date to the new tenant — the seller's rent history is part of what a buyer is acquiring, and it is documented in the tenancy agreement the seller must share before consent is sought. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **The proportional amount makes two parks' increases differ in the same year.** It is the park's own levy plus regulated-utility change divided by that park's own site count — so the same 2.3% headline limit for 2026 produces a different dollar increase at Cedar Creek Estates than at Fernridge Park or Pineridge. Ask the specific park for its last proportional-amount calculation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **"55+" in a Langley park and "55+" in a Langley strata are different legal objects.** The park rule sits in tenancy regulation and park rules and can support refusing an assignment; the strata rule sits in a registered bylaw under ss. 123.1/123.2 with Regulation 7.01 exemptions. A couple who qualify to live in a Parkway Estates strata unit on a spousal exemption have no automatic equivalent right in a Brookswood park. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **One feed card carries a placeholder postal code and one carries a lease-to-2072 claim.** The card for 98 2270 196 Street shows "V0V 0V0" — a feed data defect — and an Aldergrove card states "Lease runs to 2072," a tenure signal needing title verification. Treat any single card's details as listing copy until the tenancy agreement confirms them. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-mobile-homes.html and https://jaredgibbons.ca/cheap-mobile-homes-langley-under-500k.html
