## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. This applies in both municipalities in this feed — it is provincial strata law, not a city bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Surrey-side rules (Surrey addresses only):** Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000, with SSMUH bands of up to 3 units on lots under 280 sq m, up to 4 units on lots over 280 sq m, and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (July 8, 2024 changes); one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex unit, capped at 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area; short-term rentals permitted, owner-only, with a $358-per-year business licence; property taxes due July 2 with 5% + 5% penalties; current OCP is Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — never cite the replaced Bylaw No. 18020 as current. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals and https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **White Rock-side rules (White Rock addresses only — a different city):** White Rock's Zoning Bylaw 2024, No. 2506 (adopted June 27, 2024) caps small-scale multi-unit housing at 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots over 280 m², and 6 units within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop; its Official Community Plan is Bylaw 2017, No. 2220. Both are listed on the City of White Rock's own Resources & Documents page. White Rock tax dates and zone-by-zone rules for a specific luxury listing: UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www.whiterockcity.ca/514/Resources-Documents and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md

- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a South Surrey 55+ complex?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); Peninsula Village listings state the common local form expressly — "One person 55+." Children can also be exempt in defined cases. Confirm each complex's bylaw wording in its Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3098715-2500-152-street-67-surrey-bc-v4p-1m8/brochure
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ complex as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals; on the Surrey side, short-term rental also needs owner-occupancy and a $358-per-year City business licence. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Q: The White Rock houses on this page — do Surrey's rules apply to them?** No. White Rock is a separate municipality: its addresses follow White Rock Zoning Bylaw 2024, No. 2506 (SSMUH caps 3 ≤280 m² / 4 >280 m² / 6 within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop) and OCP Bylaw 2017, No. 2220 — not Surrey's Zoning Bylaw 12000, Surrey's tax dates, or the Surrey 2050 OCP.
