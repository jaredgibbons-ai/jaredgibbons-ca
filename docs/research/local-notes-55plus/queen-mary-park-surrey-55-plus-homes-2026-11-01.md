## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. There is no strata in Queen Mary Park for this to attach to today — it is the rule a buyer meets in the neighbouring sub-areas' complexes in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. Note the boundary of this rule: these are STRATA exemptions. Westwood Estates is a manufactured-home park, and its age rule runs through park rules under tenancy law (below), not through Regulation 7.01. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home parks run on a different statute.** Pad tenancies at Westwood Estates are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA) — the law that applies where a tenant rents a pad site but owns the manufactured home. Age rules in a park are park rules/policies, not strata bylaws — get them in writing from the park, with the tenancy agreement. Surrey's own Manufactured Home Park Policy brochure states the property owner must give 12 months' notice of a final moving date using a Notice to End Tenancy, which can only be given after the owner receives all their permits, and the City policy requires an affordable housing program for residents when a park is redeveloped. Surrey's Tenant Protection Bylaw, 2025, No. 21788 does NOT apply to property governed by the MHPTA. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_reg_21788.pdf
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000** governs every property in the sub-area; zoning is confirmed parcel by parcel in the City's COSMOS map. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **Plans: the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900** — adopted June 15, 2026 — is the current OCP, replacing the 2013 OCP Bylaw No. 18020 (never cite 18020 as current). Older neighbourhood plan designations in this part of Surrey have been folded into or superseded by that update in areas across Newton — read the current OCP and Zoning Bylaw 12000, not just old plan documents. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH):** Surrey allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, applying to eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including certain hazard lands and properties not connected to community water and sewer. With RapidBus corridors on both Scott Road and King George Boulevard, edge lots in this sub-area are the ones most likely to carry the up-to-6 permission — check the parcel in COSMOS. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites:** Surrey permits only one suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, and never inside a coach house or garden suite. Suite legality on any specific Queen Mary Park property: UNVERIFIED — the feed includes homes described with unauthorized suites, which are listing admissions, not City records. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Property taxes (city-wide, not area-specific):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Tax relief that matters at 55+:** the Province's property tax deferment regular program is for people 55 or older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), applied for through the Province — the City of Surrey does not accept paper deferment applications. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility and https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/deferring-your-property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ strata complex in Queen Mary Park itself?** No. No age-restricted strata condo or townhouse complex with a Queen Mary Park address was verified in any source checked. The sub-area's one verified 55+ product is Westwood Estates, a manufactured-home park on 134 Street where you own the home and rent the pad. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: So what can I actually buy as a 55+ home in Queen Mary Park?** A manufactured home in Westwood Estates — listings describe a gated, "55+ age restricted" park with a clubhouse, pad rent including land taxes, garbage, sewer and water in the listings checked, and large dogs allowed with park approval (no vicious breeds). You are buying the home and taking a pad tenancy under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not a strata lot — financing, resale and the age rule all run through the park, so get the park rules and the tenancy agreement in writing before writing. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/8254-134-street/48 and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf
- **Q: Where is the nearest actual 55+ strata stock?** West and east, in the neighbouring Newton sub-areas: Glenwood Village (7156 121 St) and Lakeview Estates (6090 W Boundary Dr) in West Newton; Chelsea Gardens (13870 / 13888 70 Ave) and Charleston Green (6537 138 St) in East Newton; Lakewood Heights (6211 W Boundary Dr) and Lakewood Place (6061 W Boundary Dr) in Panorama Ridge. Sullivan Mews (15020 66A Ave) is also nearby, but it is a membership purchase, not a strata. Sources: the closing list in Section 2.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ home here?** It depends on the form. In a strata (the neighbouring complexes), provincial rules exempt a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. In Westwood Estates, the park's own rules govern who may reside — the strata exemptions do not automatically apply to a pad tenancy, so ask the park for its rule in writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** In a strata, ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside; the Province states under-55s can own but may not be able to live in the unit, and a 55+ parent can be the resident. In a manufactured-home park, the equivalent question is answered by the park rules and the assignment/tenancy documents, not the Strata Property Act. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a home I buy in a 55+ complex?** In a strata, long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022; the tenant must still satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. In Westwood Estates, renting out a home on a pad is a park-rules and tenancy-agreement question — confirm with the park before pricing any rental income. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What is the pad rent at Westwood Estates?** There is no single published park rate in the sources checked: listings for different homes state $822/mo, $915/mo and $935/mo, and one states $912.52/mo as a January 1, 2027 figure including land taxes, garbage, sewer and water. Treat every one of those as listing-stated for that home and date — confirm the current rent for the specific pad in the tenancy documents. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/8254-134-street/48 and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3121759-103-8224-134-street-surrey-british-columbia-v3w4v1-249800/brochure
- **Q: Is there a care home or seniors rental in the sub-area if I need support later?** None was verified with a Queen Mary Park address in any source checked, including the Assisted Living Registry listings for Surrey. The sub-area's hospital is Surrey Memorial Hospital at 13750 96 Ave, at its north edge; the verified Newton care and subsidized options (Arbourside Court in East Newton; PICS Guru Nanak Niwas and PICS Assisted Living in West Newton) sit across King George Boulevard or south of the sub-area and are documented in those files. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ASurrey&address=&name=&page=2&order=alr_residence_type&sort=desc and ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/east-newton-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md and ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/west-newton-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day if I stop driving?** Two RapidBus corridors frame the sub-area: the R1 on King George Boulevard (east side) and the R6 on Scott Road / 120 Street (west side), both connecting to Expo Line stations. There is no SkyTrain station inside Queen Mary Park. Westwood Estates' listings note Bear Creek Park is a short walk from the park — local bus stops and exact walking distances for a specific pad are UNVERIFIED here. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/R6/direction/0/round-trip
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** In a strata, mechanically yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or a buyer who purchases without living in the unit. In Westwood Estates the resale pool is set by the park's own age and approval rules and by the pad tenancy's transfer terms — a different, park-specific version of the same narrowing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

- **The park's own listings cannot agree on its address or its pad rent.** Westwood Estates appears as both 8224 and 8254 134 Street, with listing-stated pad rents from $822 to $935 a month. - **The name "Queen Mary Park" promises a City neighbourhood that the City has not drawn.** The City page for the park itself lists trails, a playground and the Healy Creek ecosystem at 8972 Queen Mary Blvd — and no City plan area or neighbourhood boundary by this name was confirmed on any official source. The sub-area is a board filing centred on a park, so price comparisons "in Queen Mary Park" inherit a listing-feed geography, not a municipal one. https://www.surrey.ca/activities-parks-recreation/parks/queen-mary-park
- **A "Lakewood" name inside this sub-area is not the 55+ Lakewood across the line.** Lakewood Grove (8257 121A St, Queen Mary Park) shows no age restriction in any listing checked, while Lakewood Place and Lakewood Heights on W Boundary Drive (Panorama Ridge) are verified 55+ townhouse complexes.
