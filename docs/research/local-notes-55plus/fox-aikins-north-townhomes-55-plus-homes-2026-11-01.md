## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. At Fox & Aikins North this matters in the negative: the complex is strata, but no source states it has a 55+ bylaw — or any age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **New-complex paperwork is the live issue here, not age.** For a recently completed strata, the live page itself directs buyers to the bylaws, budget and any warranty information, and to confirm what the monthly strata fees cover for the specific unit; the standard buyer set is the Form B, bylaws and rules, the last two years of minutes and the depreciation report before subjects are removed. https://jaredgibbons.ca/fox-aikins-north-townhomes.html
- **Secondary suites in Surrey:** the City defines a secondary suite as an accessory dwelling unit whose habitable floor area does not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit, within a single family, semi-detached or duplex building, and not a separate strata lot; only one suite is permitted per dwelling unit. A townhome's lower-level bedroom is part of the strata lot, not a suite under this definition — confirm any separate-occupancy plan against the strata bylaws. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing in Surrey:** the City adopted its SSMUH zoning changes on July 8, 2024 — a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. This affects the detached streets around the complex, not the strata lots inside it. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **City plan and zoning:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026), per this program's standing correction — the former OCP Bylaw No. 18020 is superseded and must not be cited as current; the Grandview Heights General Land Use Plan remains the area-plan frame. Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000 is the zoning bylaw governing these strata lots. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/grandview-heights-general-land-use-plan and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r128-proposed-amendments-surrey-zoning-law-1993-no-12000
- **Strata paperwork is where an age bylaw would be proved:** for any strata purchase, the Province's buyer paperwork path is the Form B information certificate, Form F, strata bylaws and rules, fees and special levies. If a Fox & Aikins listing ever claims 55+, the Form B and the registered bylaws are the proof, not the listing text. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing

There is simply no such building to buy in Grandview. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
