## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. In Hazelmere this rule is mostly academic today: the sub-area's stock is freehold acreage with no strata to hold a bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **ALR housing frame — the rule set that actually governs Hazelmere parcels in the Agricultural Land Reserve:** land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel; a principal residence may be up to 500 m² total floor area without ALC application; one additional detached residence may be permitted of up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence and only where local bylaws allow it; local governments may further restrict residences through zoning. Whether any specific Hazelmere parcel — or what share of the sub-area — is inside the ALR is UNVERIFIED in this file: ALR status is parcel-specific on ALC mapping. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Zoning:** the live page states A-1 General Agriculture zoning for Hazelmere Valley acreages, Part 10 (Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000) minimum lot areas of 4 hectares inside the ALR and 2 hectares outside it for new A-1 lots, one single-family dwelling which may contain one secondary suite under A-1, and RA Acreage Residential zoning on some nearby land with a 4,050 m² minimum lot size. The RA figure is consistent with the City's SSMUH zone table, which carries RA lots at over 4,050 m²; the A-1 / Part 10 figures themselves were not re-read in Bylaw 12000 in the research pass — UNVERIFIED on the bylaw text; confirm in the current consolidated bylaw before quoting them for a listing. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere.html and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Plans:** the current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — never cite the replaced Bylaw No. 18020 as current. The neighbouring plan frame is the South Campbell Heights Local Area Plan, immediately north. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/SouthCampbellHeightsLocalAreaPlan.pdf
- **SSMUH caution for this ground:** Surrey's small-scale multi-unit allowances apply to eligible residential-zoned properties under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including lands not connected to community water and sewer — most Hazelmere acreage ground is outside that shape, so the standard 3–4 unit framing must not be applied to Hazelmere lots without a parcel check. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **A separate coach house on an A-1 or ALR acreage:** the live page states this is not confirmed on a City source, and the ALC frame permits an additional residence only within its size limits (90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size) — a Hazelmere coach-house claim is UNVERIFIED parcel-by-parcel, even though feed listings here include coach-house product. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere.html and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property taxes (city-wide, not area-specific):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. Whether a specific Hazelmere property carries farm-class taxation is a BC Assessment / parcel question: UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Hazelmere?** None. No verified 55+ complex, park or membership product exists in this sub-area in any source checked. A buyer who wants both Hazelmere's rural ground and an age-restricted community cannot have both in one purchase — the verified 55+ supply is in the South Surrey corridor sub-areas listed at the end of Section 2. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Redwood Estates a 55+ community?** No source says so. Redwood Estates appears in listing-card text only, as the area name for a freehold acreage home; no developer or strata source confirms it as a complex, and no age restriction is stated anywhere checked. Treat it as a rural estate area, not an age-restricted community. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere.html
- **Q: Could a 55+ complex be built in Hazelmere later?** UNCONFIRMED — no proposal, plan designation or project for age-restricted housing in Hazelmere appears in any source checked. What the sources do show is the constraint set any such project would face on ALR parcels: one principal residence to 500 m² and at most one additional residence within the ALC size limits, with local zoning able to be more restrictive. Do not price a Hazelmere acreage on a future 55+ project. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: I want to age in place on a Hazelmere acreage — what should I check instead of an age bylaw?** The parcel facts that decide rural aging in place here: servicing (well and septic versus city water/sewer — parcel-specific; the Province's GWELLS system is the well-record route the live page points to), streamside setbacks on the Little Campbell River and Jacobsen Creek, whether a legal additional residence or in-principal-residence suite fits the ALC frame if the parcel is in the ALR, and distance to care — there is no seniors residence, recreation centre or library inside the sub-area in any source checked. Well/septic and setback detail is UNVERIFIED on a Province source in this file; confirm parcel-by-parcel. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere.html and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Does that matter if we buy a Hazelmere acreage?** Not under strata law — a freehold acreage has no strata age bylaw, so the provincial 55+ exemptions are not engaged at all. The spouse/partner exemption (and the child and caregiver exemptions in Regulation 7.01) matter only if you instead buy into a 55+ strata in a neighbouring sub-area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I add a suite or a coach house for a caregiver on a Hazelmere lot?** Parcel-by-parcel, and the answer differs by regime: on ALR land, a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence where local bylaws allow it, and one additional detached residence is capped at 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha); a separate coach house on A-1 land is UNCONFIRMED on a City source even in the live page's own account. Get the parcel's ALR status, zone and servicing confirmed before designing around a caregiver unit. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere.html
- **Q: Can I rent out a Hazelmere home, or run a short-term rental?** There is no strata here, so the province-wide voiding of strata rental-restriction bylaws is not the governing question; tenancy law, City rules and — on ALR parcels — the ALC residence frame are. A specific property's short-term rental position: UNVERIFIED in this file — no City STR page was opened for Hazelmere in this pass. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Hazelmere is the only South Surrey sub-area in this series with literally nothing on the seniors ladder.** No ownership complex, no park, no rental residence, no care campus, no subsidized building — every other South Surrey sub-area checked in this series has at least one verified seniors product. A "55+ in Hazelmere" search that returns listings is returning neighbouring sub-areas' stock or general-market acreages with downsizer marketing language. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **The sub-area's name changed once already, and the old name still searches.** Hall's Prairie was renamed Hazelmere after the rail station named for Henry Thrift's homestead, per the City's South Campbell Heights plan document — acreage listings and local references still use both names for the same ground. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/SouthCampbellHeightsLocalAreaPlan.pdf
- **A century of rural character is the plan document's own finding, not a slogan.** The City's plan document records that Hazelmere largely maintained its rural, agricultural character over the past century — which is the sourced reason there is no strata stock here for a 55+ bylaw to attach to. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/SouthCampbellHeightsLocalAreaPlan.pdf
- **The urban Surrey rules most downsizers read about do not reach this ground.** Surrey's SSMUH 3–4 unit allowances apply to eligible lots under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary on community services, with exemptions including lands not connected to community water and sewer — Hazelmere acreages generally sit outside that shape, so a Hazelmere lot's "development potential" claim needs a parcel-level City check, not the standard framing. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **The highest sales ratio in South Surrey in September 2026 was two sales.** Hazelmere's 29% detached ratio — the set's highest — is 2 solds on 7 listings, the set's smallest pool. Quoting that ratio without its base makes the thinnest market in the set look like its hottest. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.)
- **Even the local school's catchment is not settled on paper in this program.** Xw'epiteng Elementary (18035 8 Ave) is confirmed by name and address inside Hazelmere, but no SD36 locator run for a Hazelmere address is on record and the live page itself flags a catchment mismatch for the 17–20 Avenue strip — a grandchild-care plan built on a Hazelmere school claim needs the exact address run first. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere.html (area research file, Section 4)
