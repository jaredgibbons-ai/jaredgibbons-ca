# Big Bend (Burnaby) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Big Bend is the City of Burnaby's main agricultural area in south Burnaby, on the Fraser River foreshore flats below the city's steep south slope — it combines ALR farmland, the Big Bend industrial lands along the river, Burnaby Fraser Foreshore Park, and a small rural-residential component (the Riverside area). It is **not a conventional residential neighbourhood**, and this file is written on that honest basis. The City publishes no street-by-street written boundary for the MLS sub-area — exact boundary streets are UNVERIFIED. http://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/water-and-sewers/flood-prevention and https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/agriculture-big-bend
- The MLS name: "Big Bend" is the GVR board sub-area name as it appears in the SnapStats GVR September 2026 report under community "Burnaby," and it matches the name the City itself uses for its south Burnaby agricultural area. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report)
- The land base: the City describes the Big Bend lowland as nearly 800 hectares between the steep south slope and the river, along nearly 7 km of the North Arm of the Fraser River — and states that lowland is a floodplain, protected by river and shoreline dykes, pump stations and flood boxes. http://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/water-and-sewers/flood-prevention
- The market footprint is correspondingly tiny: September 2026 recorded 2 detached listings and 3 condos & townhomes listings, with **zero solds of either type** — no sales ratio is applicable and no price conclusion can be drawn from the month. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report)
- Roads: Marine Way / Marine Drive runs along the top of the foreshore flats, and Byrne Road and Sussex Avenue descend to the river lands; Highway 1 and the Edmonds / Metrotown town centres are north up the slope. There is no SkyTrain station in Big Bend — the district is car- and truck-served, and specific TransLink bus routes serving the foreshore streets were not confirmed: UNVERIFIED. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- Schools: no public school inside Big Bend was confirmed on the Province's School District 41 list; the specific elementary and secondary for a Big Bend / Riverside address is UNVERIFIED — check the exact address in the SD41 locator. Burnaby is SD41: 41 elementary and 8 secondary schools city-wide, no middle-school tier (K–7 / 8–12). https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041 and https://burnabyschools.ca/about-sd41-overview/
- Library and health care, both up the slope: the nearest library branch is the Tommy Douglas Branch, 7311 Kingsway, in Edmonds, and the city's hospital is Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St (Fraser Health), in central Burnaby. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/city-departments/burnaby-public-library and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- Shopping: farm-gate and nursery sales in the Big Bend farming district are promoted on the City's Burnaby Farm Tour Map; no main shopping centre in the area was confirmed on an official source — everyday shopping is up the slope in Edmonds and South Slope. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/agriculture-big-bend
- Where the seniors housing actually is: nowhere in Big Bend. There is no seniors building of any type — no 55+ strata, no seniors rental, no assisted-living residence, no care home, no subsidized seniors building — verified with an address in this district. The nearest seniors housing is up the south slope in Edmonds (see Section 4).

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. In Big Bend this law has nothing local to attach to — there is no 55+ strata here — but it governs any 55+ purchase a Big Bend or Riverside owner makes elsewhere in Burnaby. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **The rule that actually governs Big Bend land is the ALR, not strata law:** land in Big Bend is in the provincial Agricultural Land Reserve, administered by the Agricultural Land Commission, and the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan supports protecting and expanding agriculture in Big Bend. ALR status and permitted farm / non-farm uses for a specific property are checked in the ALC's ALR Property and Map Finder before any value is priced on a non-farm use. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Zoning bylaw — note the name change, and check the district in writing.** A1 is the City's agricultural zoning district in Big Bend under the previous framework, and the City is updating zoning regulations for agricultural districts through its rewrite: the new **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026** was adopted June 9, 2026, replaced the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965, and took effect July 1, 2026. The current district and minimum lot size for a specific Big Bend farm parcel are UNVERIFIED — confirm in writing with the City and the ALC; an A1 assumption from the old bylaw is not enough for a purchase. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/bylaws/zoning-bylaw
- **SSMUH does not turn farmland into housing:** Burnaby's R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing district (June 2024: 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 on larger lots, 6 on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop) applies to residential house lots. Big Bend's farm parcels are agricultural land — do not apply R1 unit counts to an ALR parcel. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Flood rules are a purchase condition here:** the City protects the lowland with river and shoreline dykes, pump stations and flood boxes, maintains a Flood Response Plan, and receives regular updates from the BC River Forecast Centre during flooding events and freshet. A specific parcel's flood construction requirements, dyke position and insurability are per-property confirmations before subjects come off. http://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/water-and-sewers/flood-prevention
- **Property taxes:** Burnaby's 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date, and a 2.9% increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan (about $61 for the average home). Farm-class assessment and tax treatment for a specific Big Bend parcel is a BC Assessment / tax-notice check per property: UNVERIFIED here. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Big Bend?** None. There is no 55+ strata, no seniors rental and no care housing verified anywhere in this district — it is ALR farmland, industrial land, foreshore park and a small rural-residential pocket. A senior buyer "in Big Bend" is buying an ordinary farm, industrial parcel or Riverside house, none of it age-restricted. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Where is the nearest seniors housing, then?** Up the south slope in Edmonds, the neighbouring town centre: Mulberry PARC (7230 Acorn Ave) is an independent-living rental retirement residence operated by PARC Retirement Living, and the New Vista Society's Edmonds campus pairs subsidized independent seniors rental (New Vista Place and Ernest Winch Tower, 7210 and 7216 Mary St — 348 independent seniors, the majority in rent-geared-to-income homes) with assisted living and long-term care nearby. All of it is in the Edmonds sub-area, all of it is rental or care, and none of it is in Big Bend. https://parcliving.ca/mulberry-parc/ and https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/more-than-150-affordable-homes-coming-to-burnaby
- **Q: Can I buy a Big Bend farm and build a retirement home or suites on it?** Not on the strength of anything in these notes: the land is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, and permitted farm and non-farm uses, the current zoning district and any homesite rules are per-parcel confirmations with the Agricultural Land Commission and the City under the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026. Run the ALR check in the ALC's Property and Map Finder first, before pricing any non-farm value into a parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: Is Big Bend in a floodplain?** Yes — the City states the nearly 800 hectares of low-lying land between the south slope and the Fraser River is a floodplain, with a substantial flood hazard from snowmelt in late spring and early summer and from combined heavy rain and winter storm surge events, protected by dykes, pump stations and flood boxes along nearly 7 km of the North Arm. For a senior buyer that means flood construction requirements and insurance are front-page diligence, not fine print. http://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/water-and-sewers/flood-prevention
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Does any age rule affect a Riverside house purchase?** No — a freehold house carries no strata age bylaw. The 55+ bylaw rules, including the exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident, only matter inside a strata corporation, and there is no 55+ strata in this district. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out part of a Big Bend property?** There is no strata to restrict rental in the freehold farm and rural-residential stock, and province-wide, strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022 in any case; what an ALR parcel may be used for is the binding question, and that is an ALC confirmation, not a rental-bylaw question. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: What transit and daily services serve the district?** Very little inside it: no SkyTrain station, no confirmed bus route for the foreshore streets in the sources read, no school and no shopping centre. Daily services — the Tommy Douglas library branch at 7311 Kingsway, the Edmonds town centre, and Burnaby Hospital at 3935 Kincaid St — are up the slope or across the city, and the district is car- and truck-served in practice. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/city-departments/burnaby-public-library
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in Big Bend?** No — there is no assisted-living residence or long-term care home in the district. The nearest care ladder is the Edmonds cluster up the slope (assisted living at Nikkei Home, Rosewood by Nikoniko and Liberty Place; long-term care at George Derby Centre, Normanna and New Vista Care Home), accessed through Fraser Health and the operators, not by purchase. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ABurnaby&f%5B1%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASupportive%20Recovery&name=&address=&order=alr_residence_type&sort=asc

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The farming heritage here is specific, not generic.** The City states Big Bend has a culturally diverse farming heritage, with historic Chinese Canadian small-scale farms that still exist today, and it promotes the district's farm-gate and nursery sales on a Burnaby Farm Tour Map. The "neighbourhood" a buyer is purchasing into is a working farm district. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/agriculture-big-bend
- **Zero is the honest market statistic.** In September 2026 Big Bend listed 2 detached and 3 attached properties and sold none of them — any price opinion built on "Big Bend sales" is built on other areas' farm and industrial comparables and the property's own assessment file, not on this sub-area's month. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report)
- **A registry search for this district returns a recovery residence, not seniors housing.** Inspire Change Wellness at 6293 Marine Drive sits in the Assisted Living Registry's supportive-recovery class (SU). Only the SN class is seniors — checking the class code is the difference between a correct page and a wrong one. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ABurnaby&f%5B1%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASupportive%20Recovery&name=&address=&order=alr_residence_type&sort=asc
- **The 2050 OCP points the other way from development.** Where most Burnaby plans add housing, the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan supports protecting and expanding agriculture in Big Bend — a buyer pricing in a future residential rezoning of ALR land is pricing against the City's stated direction. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **The agricultural zoning itself is mid-rewrite.** The Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 took effect July 1, 2026 and agricultural-district regulations were being updated through that rewrite — the current district for a specific farm parcel must be confirmed in writing, because the old A1 framework is no longer the current bylaw. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Burnaby taxes have two penalty steps.** 2026 property taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date — and for a farm parcel, the farm-class assessment check matters more than the city-wide average figures. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
