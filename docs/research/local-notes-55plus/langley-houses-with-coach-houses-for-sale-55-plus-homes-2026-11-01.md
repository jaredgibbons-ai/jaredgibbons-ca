## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**There is ZERO verified 55+ ownership stock as coach-house product in Langley — no 55+ complex of houses with coach houses, no age-restricted carriage-home community, in either municipality.** A coach house or carriage home sits on a freehold house lot: there is no strata corporation to hold a 55+ bylaw, and the second unit's occupants are governed by the zoning and permit rules in Section 3, not by an age rule. A senior who buys a house with a coach house gets the arrangement — a self-contained second home for family or a caregiver on the same lot — without an age gate on themselves, on the second unit, or on resale. Langley's verified 55+ ownership roster (11 strata complexes, 3 manufactured-home parks and 1 co-operative) is listed at the end of this section for contrast — it is **not** coach-house stock.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Township coach house definition (settled program position):** in Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s.102, a coach house is defined as a dwelling unit on the second storey of a detached garage only. Section 100's own text, searched in full in the rules-gaps pass, contains no size cap for a coach house — but whether a size cap exists in the bylaw's individual zone sections is UNVERIFIED, because those sections were not opened in that pass. A ground-level cottage or a barn loft marketed as a "coach house" is therefore a different legal thing until its permit proves otherwise. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **City carriage homes (Bylaw 2025 No. 3300):** the City of Langley's current zoning bylaw uses the term "carriage home," and the program's settled position from the rules-gaps pass is that garden suites and carriage homes are permitted in the R1 zone with a maximum gross ground floor area of 100 m² for a carriage home or garden suite in R1, and the registered owner must reside on the lot. The live page's further City detail (two levels / 6.8 m maximum, and a stated March 9, 2026 adoption date for Bylaw No. 3300) was not opened in the bylaw documents themselves in the research pass: UNVERIFIED as stated. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-houses-with-coach-houses-for-sale.html and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **Township secondary suites, and a documented conflict reported not resolved:** Zoning Bylaw s.102, as amended, caps a Township secondary suite at 120 m² with no percentage cap. The Township's January 2025 secondary-suites guide states suites are NOT permitted in townhouses/rowhouses, while the current s.102 definition (July 2025) states a suite may be located in a townhouse or rowhouse — both documents are reported here with their dates; a buyer relying on either point should confirm with the Township. The Township also states a building permit is required for all secondary suites, and that in 2025 properties with secondary suites are subject to an Annual Secondary Suite Infrastructure Fee collected with property taxes. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md and https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/secondary-suites.aspx?_mid_=41367
- **City secondary suites (Bylaw 2025 No. 3300):** a suite must not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the gross floor area of the building; the registered owner must reside on the lot; at least one off-street parking space is required in addition to the two normally required for a single detached use; and the suite must be inspected, approved through a building permit and recorded in the City's secondary suites registry. Source: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **Do not stack permissions — Township SSMUH vs the suite/ADU route:** Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — eligible lots (Urban Containment Boundary, municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area) may have 3 units at ≤280 m² or 4 units at 280–4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible and there is no 6-unit tier in the Township. Properties that do not qualify are instead eligible for one unit plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit, depending on zoning. The live page's R-CL(CH) detail (either one SSMUH development or one house and one coach house, not both; 50% lot coverage; 390 m² / 465 m² corner minimum rear-loaded lots) was not opened in the bylaw documents themselves in the research pass: UNVERIFIED as stated, carried as site copy. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx and https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-houses-with-coach-houses-for-sale.html
- **ALR carriage-house exception (relevant to the rural cards in this feed):** for an ALR additional residence, an attached garage is excluded from the total floor area calculation if the additional residence sits on the second storey above a one-storey garage, the garage is for the principal residence's vehicles, its floor area does not exceed the additional residence's maximum (90 m² / 186 m²), there is no internal connection, and the residence is clustered close to the principal residence. On ALR ground the baseline frame is one principal residence of 500 m² or less, a suite only inside it, and one additional residence at 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Permit status is the whole diligence question on this page type.** The live page itself warns that a listing may call a building a coach house when it was approved as a garage, a studio or a workshop. The permit status of the second unit on any specific card in this feed is UNVERIFIED — no permit record was opened for any card. Ask for the building permit and the final inspection for the coach house itself, and confirm the zone with the right municipality. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-houses-with-coach-houses-for-sale.html
- **The strata age-bylaw rules, for contrast.** Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; age bylaws below 55 invalid since Nov 24, 2022); the bylaw restricts residency, not ownership; Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19; and residential rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, while short-term restrictions remain allowed. None of this applies to a freehold house with a coach house — there is no strata, so there is no caregiver exemption to need: a caregiver or family member lives in the second unit as an occupant or tenant under the land rules above. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Property taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026; the City of Langley also sets a July 2, 2026 due date, with payments received after it incurring a penalty — the City's penalty percentage is UNVERIFIED, as the City page read does not state the rate. Establish which municipality — and which calendar — applies from the address. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx and https://www.langleycity.ca/PayPropertyTaxes
- **Tax relief at 55+:** the Province's regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ community of houses with coach houses in Langley?** No — none was verified in either municipality. A coach house sits on a freehold lot with no strata to hold an age bylaw. What exists is (a) individual houses with a second unit, governed by the zoning and permit rules in Section 3, and (b) Langley's verified 55+ complexes — 11 strata, 3 parks and 1 co-operative — none of which is coach-house product. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: What is a coach house in the Township of Langley, exactly?** Under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s.102, a dwelling unit on the second storey of a detached garage only. That narrow definition is why a ground-level cottage, a garden suite or a barn loft marketed as a "coach house" needs its permit checked — it may be a different legal thing entirely, with different rules. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: Is a carriage home the same as a coach house?** They describe similar buildings under different bylaws: the Township's Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 uses "coach house," while the City of Langley's Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 uses "carriage home," with the program's settled R1 position being a 100 m² maximum gross ground floor area and the registered owner required to reside on the lot. The live page's further City detail is UNVERIFIED in this file because the City bylaw text was not opened in the research pass. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-houses-with-coach-houses-for-sale.html and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: Can my parent, adult child or a caregiver live in the coach house?** That is the arrangement this page type exists for, and no age bylaw stands in the way — there is no strata. The governing questions are land questions: is the second unit permitted as what it is (coach house, carriage home, suite, garden suite or additional ALR residence), does an owner-occupancy rule apply (the City's carriage-home and suite rules require the registered owner to reside on the lot), and, on ALR ground, does the unit fit the 90 m² / 186 m² additional-residence frame. Confirm each for the specific lot with the right municipality. Sources: Section 3 above.
- **Q: How do I know the "coach house" was actually approved as one?** Follow the page's own check: ask for the building permit and the final inspection for the coach house itself, and confirm the zone with the right municipality, because a building marketed as a coach house may have been approved as a garage, studio or workshop. The permit status of every card in this feed is UNVERIFIED in this file. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-houses-with-coach-houses-for-sale.html
- **Q: How big can a secondary suite be, if the second unit is a suite instead?** It depends on the municipality: Township s.102 caps a suite at 120 m² with no percentage cap; City Bylaw No. 3300 caps a suite at the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area, with owner-occupancy and registry recording required. Note the documented Township conflict on suites in townhouses/rowhouses (January 2025 guide vs July 2025 s.102 text) — get the Township's current answer in writing if a townhouse suite matters. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: Can an ALR property's additional residence sit over a garage?** Yes, on the ALC's carriage-house exception: the garage below is excluded from the additional residence's floor area if it is one storey and for the principal residence's vehicles, does not exceed the additional residence's maximum floor area (90 m² or 186 m²), has no internal connection to the residence, and the residence is clustered close to the principal residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I have both an SSMUH multiplex and a coach house on one Township lot?** Do not assume permissions stack: an eligible lot's SSMUH route (3–4 units under Bylaw No. 6020) and its suite/ADU route are alternatives governed by eligibility and zoning, and the live page's R-CL(CH) statement puts it as either/or — that page detail is UNVERIFIED as bylaw text in this file. Confirm which route the lot is actually on with Township staff before pricing the extra unit. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx and https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-houses-with-coach-houses-for-sale.html
- **Q: Coach house or a 55+ strata rancher — which suits a downsizer better?** They solve different problems, on the verified record: a 55+ strata rancher (Huntsfield Green, Creekside Villa, the Walnut Grove duplex-style complexes) gives a single-level home, an age-gated community and strata maintenance, with residency governed by the bylaw and Regulation 7.01 exemptions; a house with a coach house gives a full house plus a second home for family or a caregiver, with no age gate, no strata, and the owner carrying the land, permit and maintenance obligations. Neither is a version of the other. Sources: Sections 2 and 3 above.
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes at 55+ on a house with a coach house?** The Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities). Which municipality's tax calendar applies depends on the address. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits and https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The word changes at the municipal boundary, and so do the numbers.** Cross from the Township into the City and "coach house" becomes "carriage home," the suite cap changes from 120 m² flat (Township) to the lesser of 100 m² and 40% (City), and an owner-occupancy and suite-registry regime appears. Two near-identical second units a few streets apart can sit under materially different rules — the address's municipality is the first fact to settle on any card in this feed. Sources: Section 3 above.
- **Only two of this feed's first six cards are its own subject.** The research pass found a garden suite, a second home with a cabin, a barn loft and a legal cottage filed under a coach-house heading — so a buyer using this page must classify each second unit from its own permit, not from the page it was found on. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-houses-with-coach-houses-for-sale.html
- **The Township's own documents currently disagree about suites in townhouses.** The January 2025 secondary-suites guide says suites are not permitted in townhouses/rowhouses; the July 2025 s.102 definition says a suite may be located in one. The conflict is documented in this program and reported, not resolved — a live example of why a second unit's status is confirmed in writing, not read off a guide. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **The coach house is Langley's un-gated answer to the 55+ strata's caregiver exemption.** In a 55+ strata, a younger caregiver or family member lives in the unit only through a Regulation 7.01 exemption; on a coach-house lot, the same person lives next door in a separate dwelling with no exemption to need — but also no strata, no age gate for the neighbourhood, and no restriction on who buys the property next. Buyers choosing between the two are choosing a legal structure, not just a floor plan. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **This feed is rural at reading, whatever its suburban name suggests.** The research file records the visible cards as dominated by large rural properties on 2.03–13.21 acres whose second units are cottages, second homes and a barn loft, with one suburban Aldergrove coach-house house and one Brookswood garden-suite house — so ALR and rural permit questions, not compact-lot questions, decide most of what this page currently shows. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-houses-with-coach-houses-for-sale.html
- **A size cap for a Township coach house may exist where nobody in this program has looked yet.** Section 100 of Bylaw No. 2500 was searched in full and states no cap — but a cap, if there is one, would sit in the individual zone sections, which were not opened. A buyer told "there is no size limit on a coach house" is being told more than the verified record supports: UNVERIFIED beyond Section 100. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
