# Notary & Lawyer Conveyancing Fees — Langley, Surrey, South Surrey / White Rock, BC

**Researched:** November 2, 2026. Every figure below was read on the firm's **own website** (page opened, not a search snippet). All amounts CAD. Directory/third-party figures (TrustAnalytica, Yelp, Google listings, realtor blogs attach price ranges to many of these firms) were **excluded** — only a firm's own published price counts.

## The headline

**Published conveyancing prices are rare in all three areas — and non-existent in South Surrey / White Rock.**

- **Langley:** 3 of 21 conveyancing firms checked publish any price; only 2 publish a usable fee schedule.
- **Surrey (proper):** 4 of 36 firms checked publish any price; only 3 publish a fee schedule (the 4th publishes rough budget estimates).
- **South Surrey / White Rock:** **0 of 15 firms publish their own price.** The only dollar figures on any local firm's site are a *generic BC market range* on one firm's FAQ page — not that firm's fee.

Where firms do publish, the typical **legal fee** (before taxes and disbursements) is roughly **$995–$1,475 to buy with a mortgage, $795–$1,200 to sell with a mortgage discharge, and $845–$1,350 to refinance**. All-in totals (fee + taxes + disbursements) run roughly **$1,500–$1,900** on the standard transactions where an all-in figure or its components are published.

## 4. Plain-language summary — what a buyer and a seller typically pay

**First, the honest caveat:** most notaries and lawyers in these three areas do not publish prices at all, so "typical" here means "typical of the firms that publish." The published sample is small (6 firms with any numbers across three areas), and several are multi-office firms headquartered elsewhere (Chilliwack, Coquitlam, Pitt Meadows) with satellite/local offices here. Almost every purely local firm — and **every** local notary — quotes case by case.

- **Buying a home:** expect a **legal fee of roughly $1,000–$1,500** if you have a mortgage (published range $995–$1,475; three-area average ≈ **$1,154**), or roughly **$800–$1,300** for a cash purchase (average ≈ $1,030). On top of the fee, budget taxes (GST + PST on the fee) and disbursements — the one firm publishing all-in totals (Peak Law, Langley) puts a purchase with a mortgage at ≈ **$1,772 all-in**; LeBeau's Surrey components sum to ≈ $1,922. Property Transfer Tax is extra everywhere and is not part of any figure in this report.
- **Selling a home:** expect a **legal fee of roughly $800–$1,200** if your mortgage has to be discharged (published range $795–$1,200; average ≈ **$985**), or roughly **$650–$1,050** on a clear title (average ≈ $863). All-in, Peak Law's published figure for a sale with a discharge is ≈ **$1,485**.
- **Refinancing:** legal fee roughly **$845–$1,350** (average ≈ **$1,043**); all-in ≈ $1,633 at the one firm that publishes it.
- **By area:** Langley and Surrey published fees are in the same band — Surrey's averages sit slightly higher only because LeBeau's (possibly stale, July 2024) higher "from" fees pull them up; the cheapest published schedule in both areas is the same firm (Alpine Legal: $995 purchase with mortgage / $795 sale with discharge / $845 refinance, fee only). **South Surrey / White Rock has no published benchmark at all** — a buyer or seller there has to phone for a quote; the one local firm that prints any numbers (Pacific Point Law) describes the general BC market as $1,300–$2,100 to buy with a mortgage and $1,000–$1,600 to sell, plus disbursements.
- **Notary vs lawyer:** cannot be compared from published prices — notaries in these areas simply do not publish conveyancing prices.

## Caveats

- Figures are the firms' published "from"/flat fees for standard transactions; complications (extra payouts, second mortgages, new construction, rush, transactions over $1M at LeBeau) cost more.
- Freshness: Raj Dhillon's page is © 2026; Alpine's price sheet is dated January 2026 (fees unchanged since September 2022); Peak Law's page carries no date (read Nov 2, 2026); **LeBeau's price list is dated July 2024 and may be stale**; Spagnuolo's figures are explicitly approximate budget estimates, and its Surrey office could not be re-confirmed from its own site's readable text.
- Two multi-office firms (Alpine, Peak) publish the most complete schedules; both are headquartered outside the three areas with offices inside them.
- Spot-checks performed Nov 2, 2026: Raj Dhillon's published fees ($1,050 / $950 / $950, fee-only wording) and Peak Law's pricing table ($1,095 fee / $1,772 all-in for a purchase with one mortgage) were each re-opened and verified against this report.
