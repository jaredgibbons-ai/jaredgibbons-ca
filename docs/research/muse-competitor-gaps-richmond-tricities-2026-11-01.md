# Competitor scan — Richmond (read 2026-11-01)

Scope note: offerings and page structure only. Guide depths are rough estimates from the opened page text. One Richmond-wide pattern runs through almost every area below: the same 3–4 operators (an Oakwyn Realty templated network under two brands, Hani & Les | BC Condos And Homes, Michael Lepore, and Faith Wilson) publish a page for nearly every Richmond sub-area, so the "competition" in most of these neighbourhoods is templated coverage, not neighbourhood specialists.
