# Candidate Areas — Official Board List Check

Prepared for Jared Gibbons · All sources read 2026-11-01

**Question settled:** for each of the 9 candidate names on the missing-areas list, is it (a) a named sub-area on a real estate board's official list, (b) a community/municipality a board reports on without being a sub-area, or (c) on no board list found?

**Evidence standard:** verdicts rest on the boards' own publications — FVREB's published Statistics Package, GVR's own site (gvrealtors.ca), CADREB's own site (cadreb.com) and CADREB's own registered description of its territory — corroborated by the SnapStats September 2026 extraction (`~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/stats/subarea-stats-sep2026.csv`, 340 rows; SnapStats states its data is sourced from the boards' MLS®). Where the only public evidence is the boards' MLS® taxonomy as republished through the MLS® Reciprocity program / Paragon-coded listing records, that is stated plainly. No realtor or blog source carries any verdict.

**One structural fact that shapes everything below:** neither FVREB nor CADREB publishes a public sub-area list. FVREB's public Statistics Package reports at community level only; CADREB's public statistics are board-wide only. The sub-area tier exists in the boards' MLS® (Paragon) taxonomy and is visible publicly through SnapStats' board-sourced tables and Paragon-coded listing records.

## Per-candidate findings

### 1. Port Kells (Surrey) — BOARD SUB-AREA, FVREB, community Surrey
- Evidence: SnapStats FVR September 2026 (data sourced from the FVREB MLS®) lists "Port Kells" as a Surrey sub-area in both its Detached and its Condos & Townhomes tables (Detached: 4 active / 0 sold, Sep 2026). Paragon-coded MLS® records show the coding directly: Area "North Surrey", Sub Area "28 Port Kells" (e.g., 18963 84 Ave, MLS® R3065841, board-portal listing display).
- Sources: `~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/stats/subarea-stats-sep2026.csv` (rows: FVR, Surrey, Detached/Condos & Townhomes, Port Kells); https://rebgvportal.xposureapp.com/listing/33548?u=676170 (MLS® record display, read 2026-11-01).

### 2. Surrey City Centre — NOT a board sub-area (board community: Surrey; sub-area: Whalley)
- Evidence: no "Surrey City Centre" (or "City Centre") row exists anywhere in the SnapStats FVR September 2026 tables; the sub-area covering downtown Surrey is **Whalley** (Sep 2026: 86 detached active; 635 condos & townhomes active / 40 sold — the City Centre tower stock reports under Whalley). FVREB's own package groups this area as "Surrey – North" / "North Surrey" inside the community of Surrey. "Surrey City Centre" is the City of Surrey's official name for its downtown (City Centre Plan) — a municipal name, not a board name.
- Sources: `~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/stats/subarea-stats-sep2026.csv` (Surrey sub-area list, 15 names, no City Centre); FVREB Monthly Statistics Package, October 2025, MLS® Summary p.3 ("City of Surrey – Combined: North Surrey, Central Surrey, Cloverdale and South Surrey, excl. White Rock") — https://storage.googleapis.com/bchouse-listing-images/1762317928893_Package202510.pdf (FVREB's published package, read 2026-11-01).

### 3. Barnston Island (Surrey) — NOT on any board list found under its own name
- Evidence: "Barnston Island" appears in no board sub-area table (absent from all 15 Surrey sub-areas in the SnapStats FVR extraction). MLS® records for island properties code the board fields as Area "North Surrey" / Sub-Area **Port Kells** — e.g., 52 Dyke Road, MLS® R2470345, displayed as "North Surrey | Port Kells" with "Barnston Island" carried only as the subdivision/neighbourhood name; 148 Barnston Island likewise shows Community "Port Kells". So for board purposes the island is FVREB, community Surrey, sub-area Port Kells. (Governance is a separate matter — Metro Vancouver Electoral Area A plus Katzie IR No. 3, per the candidate research file — the board coding is Surrey regardless.)
- Sources: https://www.zealty.ca/property/bc/surrey/52-dyke-road-mls_r2470345 and https://bode.ca/sold-property/2106733-surrey-port-kells-vacant-land-429937-acres (MLS® record displays, read 2026-11-01); SnapStats extraction as above.

### 4. Mt Lehman (Abbotsford) — BOARD COMMUNITY ONLY, FVREB (Abbotsford)

No "Mt Lehman" row exists, and FVREB's own package reports Abbotsford at community level only. ### 5. Lindell Beach (Chilliwack) — BOARD SUB-AREA, CADREB (MLS area: Cultus Lake)

Cultus Lake sits inside CADREB's territory (south of Chilliwack, FVRD Electoral Area H — it is NOT inside the City of Chilliwack). Caveat, stated plainly: CADREB publishes no public area list of its own — its September 2026 Detail Sheet and CREA HPI report are board-wide only — so the sub-area status rests on the MLS® taxonomy evidence, and a cadreb.com-published list confirming it is NOT CONFIRMED (none exists publicly).
- Sources: https://www.cadreb.com/market/ and https://www.cadreb.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/09-2026-Statistics.pdf (CADREB's own September 2026 statistics — board-wide, no area breakdown, read 2026-11-01); MLS® Reciprocity area taxonomy display: https://suttongroupwestcoastabbotsford.com/blog.html/categories/aberdeen%2C%20abbotsford%20real%20estate (carries the three boards' MLS® data disclaimer, read 2026-11-01).

### 6. Hope — BOARD COMMUNITY, CADREB

### 8. Agassiz — BOARD COMMUNITY, CADREB
- Evidence: named in CADREB's own territory description (same filing as §6) and in the board's boilerplate list of communities served. ### 9. Kent — NOT on any board list found
- Evidence: "Kent" appears in no board source checked: not in CADREB's own territory description (which names Agassiz, the community inside Kent), not as an area or sub-area in the MLS® taxonomy (the District of Kent's land is carried under the Agassiz area, incl. Harrison Mills), and not in any FVREB or GVR list. "Kent" is the municipal name (District of Kent) only.
- Sources: CADREB territory filing and MLS® taxonomy display as in §§5–6 (absence across both), read 2026-11-01.

## The eastern board situation, plainly

Hope, Harrison Hot Springs, Agassiz, Kent and Lindell Beach are **not FVREB and not GVR** territory. They belong to the **Chilliwack and District Real Estate Board (CADREB)**, whose territory runs from Yarrow to Lytton and includes Chilliwack, Agassiz, Harrison Hot Springs and Hope (board's own description). CADREB is a separate, still-operating board (343 REALTORS® in its September 2026 Detail Sheet) publishing statistics for "Chilliwack and District" as a single board-wide market — it does not publish sub-area statistics at all. Practical consequence for the rebuild: no SnapStats or board-package stats exist for these five areas at sub-area level from any board; any numbers for them would have to come from a Paragon pull filtered by the MLS® area/sub-area codes (Hope, Harrison Hot Springs, Agassiz, Cultus Lake/Lindell Beach).
