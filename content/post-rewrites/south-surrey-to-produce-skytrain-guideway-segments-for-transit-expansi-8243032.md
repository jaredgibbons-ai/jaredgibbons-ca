In March 2024 I wrote that the concrete guideway for the Surrey-Langley SkyTrain might be made in South Surrey. It is. Here's how the precast yard in Campbell Heights works, and where the line stands.

## The precast yard

- **Where:** 195 Street and 24 Avenue in Campbell Heights, a South Surrey business area with no station of its own ([Surrey-Langley SkyTrain project](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/precast-concrete-facility/)).
- **What it does:** casts the concrete guideway segments, the box-shaped pieces that are lifted onto the columns along Fraser Highway and joined to carry the trains. The 2024 City report put the number at about 5,000 segments.
- **How:** the land is zoned for agriculture, and council gave a temporary use permit for the yard.
- **Hours:** from February 9, 2026, overnight work ran for six months to keep segments moving to the route ([construction notice](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/construction-update-extended-hours-at-precast-concrete-facility/)).

## Where the line stands

- **Route:** 16 km along Fraser Highway, from King George Station to 203 Street in Langley City, with eight stations.
- **Progress:** in September 2026 the Province reported the guideway about 65% complete.
- **Opening:** expected in late 2029. I cover the stations, the budget and what it means for nearby homes in [Surrey-Langley SkyTrain: 2026 progress](/blog.html/bcs-surrey-langley-skytrain-extension-building-progress-8243028).

## What it means for South Surrey

Campbell Heights won't get a station, but the yard brings jobs and truck traffic to the area until the guideway is done. The bigger change for South Surrey buyers is the line itself: Clayton and Langley City stations are a short drive from Grandview Heights and Campbell Heights.
