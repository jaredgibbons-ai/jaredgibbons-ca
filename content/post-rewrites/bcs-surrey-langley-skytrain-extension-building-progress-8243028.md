When I first wrote about the Surrey-Langley SkyTrain in March 2024, the station contract had just been awarded and the line was expected to open in late 2028. Here's where the project stands in November 2026, and what it means if you own or are buying near the line.

## The project at a glance

- **Route:** 16 km of elevated SkyTrain along Fraser Highway, from King George Station in Surrey to Langley City Centre.
- **Stations (west to east):** Green Timbers, 152 Street, Fleetwood, Bakerview-166 Street, Hillcrest-184 Street, Clayton, Willowbrook and Langley City Centre.
- **Opening:** service is now expected in **late 2029** (it was late 2028 in 2024).
- **Budget:** $5.996 billion, up from the $4 billion estimated when the business case was approved ([project status report, May 2026](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/app/uploads/sites/938/2026/06/SLS_Monthly-Status-Report_May-2026.pdf)).

## Where construction is now

In September 2026 the Province reported the guideway about 65% complete, with foundation piles and columns more than 95% complete. A second guideway-launching gantry, the "Langley Launcher", arrived in Langley and started at Hillcrest-184 Street, working toward Willowbrook ([B.C. government, September 21, 2026](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0102-001115)). Expect lane closures along Fraser Highway to continue through 2027.

## What it means for homes near the line

- **More density near stations:** B.C.'s 2023 Transit-Oriented Area rules require cities to allow more height and density within 800 metres of designated rapid transit stations, and Surrey and Langley City are planning for far more housing around the new stations. Older homes on larger lots near the line are increasingly valued as land.
- **Langley City:** the downtown station at 203 Street has triggered a wave of condo and mixed-use proposals. Langley City's provincial housing target is 1,844 new homes over five years.
- **Fleetwood and Clayton:** expect more townhomes and mid-rise condos along Fraser Highway, and more choice for buyers as they finish.
- **Buying near the line:** being close to a station is a plus for most buyers, but construction noise, traffic and parking changes are real until 2029. Ask what's planned on neighbouring lots before you buy.
