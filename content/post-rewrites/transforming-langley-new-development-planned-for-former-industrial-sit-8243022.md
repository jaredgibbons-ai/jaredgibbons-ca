In March 2024 I wrote about a plan to replace the former Gabby's Country Cabaret in downtown Langley City with a six-storey condo building. That plan didn't go ahead. Here's what happened, and why the corner is still one to watch.

## The original plan

In April 2024 Kerr Properties applied to Langley City for a development permit at **20297 Fraser Highway**, the northwest corner of 203 Street and Fraser Highway. The proposal was a six-storey mixed-use building with **78 apartments** (one, two and three bedrooms) over **678 m² of ground-floor commercial space**, with bike parking for residents.

Gabby's had hosted live country music for years and appeared in TV shoots such as *Riverdale*. It closed during the pandemic in 2020 and the building was demolished in 2021.

## What happened

The application went to Langley City's Advisory Design Panel on April 16, 2025. Langley City's development activity report then lists it as **"Application Cancelled" on May 2, 2025** ([Langley City, June 2025](https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/development-activity-june-2025)). In late 2025 the 0.68-acre corner came up for sale as a development site, zoned C1 and inside the city's Transit-Oriented Core in the Official Community Plan.

Projects get cancelled for many reasons, from construction costs to financing and a slower condo presale market. It doesn't mean the site won't be built on.

## Why the corner still matters

- **SkyTrain:** Langley City Centre Station, the end of the Surrey-Langley SkyTrain line at 203 Street, is right across the street. Service is expected in late 2029. I cover the project in my [SkyTrain update](/blog.html/bcs-surrey-langley-skytrain-extension-building-progress-8243028).
- **Housing target:** Langley City has a provincial target of 1,844 new homes over five years, and the downtown core is where most of them are planned.
- **For nearby owners:** more density planned around the station is generally good for land values, but it also means more construction nearby in the next few years.
