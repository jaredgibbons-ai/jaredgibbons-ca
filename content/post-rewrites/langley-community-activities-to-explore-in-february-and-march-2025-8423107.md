In early 2025 I put together a list of library programs running in Langley that February and March. Those dates have passed, so here's a year-round version: the places and programs that keep Langley families busy in every season.

## Langley's libraries

Langley is served by the Fraser Valley Regional Library, with branches in Aldergrove, Brookswood, Fort Langley, Langley City, Murrayville, Walnut Grove and Willoughby (Muriel Arnason Library). The programs I listed in 2025 are typical of what runs all year:

- **Book clubs** for adults, such as Between the Pages at Brookswood and the Afternoon Book Club at Aldergrove.
- **Games:** Scrabble and Dungeons & Dragons nights for adults at Murrayville.
- **Little ones:** sensory playtime for babies up to two, storytimes and creative play.

Programs are free with a library card; check the FVRL event calendar for the current season.

## Parks and outdoors

- **Campbell Valley Regional Park** in South Langley: trails, the Little River Loop and horse trails, run by Metro Vancouver.
- **Derby Reach Regional Park** on the Fraser River: camping, fishing and the Edgewater Bar.
- **Fort Langley:** the Fort Langley National Historic Site, the village shops on Glover Road, and the Fort-to-Fort Trail along the river.
- **Aldergrove Regional Park** and the Otter Co-op Outdoor Experience water park in Aldergrove.

## Seasonal favourites

Summer farmers' markets and berry farms, fall harvest and pumpkin patches, and holiday light displays in winter. The Township of Langley and Langley City both post community events on their websites.
