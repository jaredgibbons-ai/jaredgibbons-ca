Under $420,000 is the entry level for a condo in South Surrey and White Rock. There aren't many listings at that price, but they do come up, and with prices lower than last year buyers have room to negotiate. Here's what that budget buys in late 2026.

## What condos cost in South Surrey right now

The benchmark South Surrey and White Rock apartment was **$558,600** in October 2026, down 3.5% from a year earlier. The median sale was $562,250 ([FVREB](/south-surrey-white-rock-real-estate-market-stats.html), October 2026). So $420K is well below the typical sale. At that price you're usually looking at one of three things:

- a one-bedroom or studio in an older building
- a 55+ building, where age restrictions keep prices lower
- a unit with a leasehold or other unusual title, which needs extra checking

## What to check on a lower-priced condo

- **Why it's priced lower.** Age, size, a special levy or a building issue? The answer decides whether it's a deal.
- **Depreciation report and reserve fund.** Older buildings may need envelope, elevator or plumbing work in the next few years.
- **Strata fees.** Older buildings often have higher fees, which reduce what you can borrow.
- **Rental and age bylaws.** These matter if you might rent it out later or sell to a younger buyer.
- **Insurance deductibles.** Ask for the building's current deductibles. A high water deductible is common and you'll want matching coverage.
