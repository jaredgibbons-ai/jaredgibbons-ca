Every summer the Canada Cup International Softball Championship brings the best women's fastpitch teams in the world to Softball City in South Surrey. I first wrote about it when the 2024 tournament opened on Canada Day. Here's what happened in 2026, and how to make the most of it next year.

## Canada Cup 2026

- **Dates:** July 3 to 12, 2026, at Softball City, with the gold medal final on Sunday, July 12 ([Canada Cup](https://canadacup.com/?p=8457)).
- **Divisions:** Women's International, plus the Futures (U19) and Showcase (17U) divisions, which bring more than 1,500 young athletes to Surrey.
- **Teams:** the Women's International field included Canada, China, Chinese Taipei, New Zealand, Israel, Czechia, Brazil, Peru, Venezuela, Singapore and American Samoa.
- **Result:** Team Canada beat China 7-3 in the final to finish 9-0, with Kelsey Harshman hitting a go-ahead three-run home run in the seventh inning.

## Tips for going

- **Tickets:** tournament and day passes are sold through [CanadaCup.com](https://canadacup.com/womens/).
- **Getting there:** Softball City is in South Surrey near 24 Avenue and 148 Street. Parking fills up for Team Canada's evening games, so arrive early.
- **Bring:** a hat, sunscreen, and water; July games can be hot.
