In July 2024 I wrote that the former Hall's Prairie Elementary in South Surrey would reopen with a new name gifted by the Semiahmoo First Nation. Xw'epiteng Elementary has now run its outdoor, land-based program since the 2024-25 school year. Here's what families should know.

## The school at a glance

- **Address:** 18035 8 Avenue, South Surrey, in the rural area near Campbell Valley Park
- **Grades:** Kindergarten to Grade 7
- **Program:** the Xw'epiteng Outdoor Land-Based Learning Program. Students learn outdoors every day and cover the full B.C. K-7 curriculum, with Coast Salish culture, language and history built in. Teaching is inquiry-based, play-based and place-based, and students learn to manage risk outdoors ([Surrey Schools](https://surreyschools.ca/page/179729/xwepiteng-outdoor-land-based-learning-program-k-7)).
- **Size:** a small school setting
- **Contact:** 604-538-1083, Xwepiteng@surreyschools.ca

## The name

Xw'epiteng (pronounced roughly *woh-pee-ten*) means "place of many rabbits". The name was gifted by Semiahmoo First Nation Chief Harley Chappell, and the Surrey school board approved it in June 2024. The school had worked with the Semiahmoo for years before that, including changing its mascot to the hawk in 2009 after consulting the Nation.

## How enrolment works

Xw'epiteng isn't a typical neighbourhood school. Kindergarten spots go through the Surrey Schools online kindergarten lottery each winter, and families with children in Grades 1 to 7 contact the school directly. Because kindergarten places go by lottery, living nearby doesn't guarantee a spot. Ask the school about transportation before you count on it.
