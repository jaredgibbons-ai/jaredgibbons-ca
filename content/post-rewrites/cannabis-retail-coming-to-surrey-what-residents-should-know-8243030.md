For years Surrey was the largest city in Canada without a legal cannabis store. That changed in 2024, and by spring 2025 council had backed the first locations. Here's where things stand, and what it means if you own or are buying a home nearby.

## How Surrey's cannabis rules came together

- **April 2024:** council approved a framework for up to **12 stores**, two in each of six communities: Whalley/City Centre, Guildford, Fleetwood, Newton, South Surrey and Cloverdale. A city survey that January drew more than 4,100 responses, and about 70% supported 12 or more stores citywide.
- **Applications:** the City took applications in fall 2024 and then chose which went forward.
- **April 14, 2025:** council recommended nine store applications to the Province, each by a 5-4 vote ([StratCann](https://stratcann.com/?p=41646)).
- **April 28, 2025:** council recommended two more, in Cloverdale (5828 176 Street) and North Surrey (104-12499 88 Avenue), again 5-4.

A City recommendation isn't a licence. Each store still needs a licence from the Province's Liquor and Cannabis Regulation Branch before it can open, so check the Branch's list for stores open today.

## What it means near your home

- **Only a few stores, in commercial areas:** with a cap of about two per community, there won't be one on every corner, and stores go in commercial zones, not residential streets.
- **Buyers ask:** I get asked about stores near schools and parks. The City set location rules for each application, so if a proposed store matters to you, look at the application on the City's site before you write an offer.
- **Values:** one store in a strip mall rarely moves home prices. Traffic, parking and the mix of other tenants in the plaza tend to matter more.
