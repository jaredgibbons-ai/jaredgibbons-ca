## The location

The building is in central Penticton, close to shopping, groceries, transit, schools and downtown. Central Penticton suits renters who want to walk to services, which tends to help with vacancy.

## Why a newer fourplex

- **Lower maintenance:** a 2018 building shouldn't need roofs, windows or major systems for years, unlike most older rental buildings.
- **Separate metering:** tenants pay their own utilities, so rising utility costs don't come out of your income.
- **Unit mix:** two 2-bedroom and two 1-bedroom units appeal to different tenants, which spreads the risk.
- **Price per door:** about $325,000 a unit, less than a new one-bedroom condo in much of the Lower Mainland.

## What I'd check as a buyer

- the current rent roll, lease terms and deposits
- the last two years of operating costs: insurance, property tax and maintenance
- remaining new home warranty coverage on a 2018 build
- financing: a fourplex is still residential (four units or fewer), so you can usually finance it with a residential mortgage, but get your lender's numbers early
