## The neighbourhoods

- **Crescent Beach and Ocean Park:** older character homes and newer builds on bigger lots, a short walk or bike ride to the beach. Expect to pay a premium for anything near the water. [Crescent Beach and Ocean Park guide](/crescent-beach-ocean-park-guide.html).
- **Elgin Chantrell:** large lots, estate homes and some of South Surrey's most sought-after school catchments. [Elgin Chantrell guide](/elgin-chantrell-guide.html).
- **Grandview Heights:** the newest part of South Surrey, with townhomes, condos and new detached homes, Ta'talu Elementary and the Grandview Corners shops. [Grandview Heights guide](/grandview-heights-guide.html).
- **Hazelmere and Campbell Valley:** acreages, hobby farms and ALR land along the Little Campbell River. Zoning matters here; my [zoning guide](/zonings.html) covers the farm and rural zones. [Hazelmere guide](/hazelmere-guide.html).
- **King George Corridor:** central, with older townhomes and condos close to Semiahmoo Town Centre, often South Surrey's best value. [King George Corridor guide](/king-george-corridor-guide.html).
- **Morgan Creek and Rosemary Heights:** golf course living, larger family homes and several 55+ townhome complexes. [Morgan Creek guide](/morgan-creek-guide.html).
- **Pacific Douglas:** family homes near the Peace Arch border crossing, with quick access to Highway 99. [Pacific Douglas guide](/pacific-douglas-guide.html).
- **Sunnyside Park:** established neighbourhoods, mature trees and parks, close to Semiahmoo Secondary. [Sunnyside Park guide](/sunnyside-park-surrey-guide.html).
- **White Rock:** the promenade, the pier and hillside homes with ocean views, plus most of the area's condos. [White Rock guide](/white-rock-guide.html).
