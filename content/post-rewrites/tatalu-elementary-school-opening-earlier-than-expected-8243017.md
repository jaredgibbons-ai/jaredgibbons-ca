When I wrote about Ta'talu Elementary in May 2024, the school was running ahead of schedule. It opened on October 1, 2024, three months before its planned January 2025 start. Here's what families buying in Grandview Heights should know about it now.

## The school at a glance

- **Address:** 1970 165A Street, Grandview Heights, South Surrey
- **Grades:** Kindergarten to Grade 7
- **Capacity:** up to 655 students. It opened with more than 220 students and 11 divisions ([Surrey Schools](https://www.surreyschools.ca/_ci/p/177872)).
- **Building:** a three-storey, 27-classroom hybrid mass timber school with a large gym, library learning commons, maker spaces and two rooms for child care.
- **Grounds:** an old-growth western red cedar beside the playground, a gravel field, an outdoor basketball court and preserved rocks and stumps for outdoor learning.
- **Name:** Ta'talu is SENĆOŦEN for "little arms", gifted by Semiahmoo First Nation Chief Harley Chappell. It refers to the Campbell River and its tributaries.

## Why it matters for buyers

Grandview Heights has grown faster than its schools for years. Ta'talu added hundreds of elementary seats close to the area's newer townhomes and detached homes, and it changed catchment boundaries for nearby schools when it opened.

That's why I always tell buyers: **check the catchment by address, not by neighbourhood name.** Catchment lines follow streets, and two homes a block apart can be in different schools. Use the Surrey Schools school locator, and read my [South Surrey school catchment guide](/south-surrey-school-catchments.html) for how to check before you write an offer.

Being in a new school's catchment is a selling point. Homes within walking distance of Ta'talu tend to get more attention from families, especially townhomes with three bedrooms.

## Homes near Ta'talu

The streets around 165A Street and 20 Avenue are mostly newer detached homes, coach-house homes and townhome complexes. Nearby is the 20 Avenue overpass of Highway 99, under construction since early 2026 and scheduled to finish in December 2027, which will link Grandview Heights to the new Costco site on 164 Street.
