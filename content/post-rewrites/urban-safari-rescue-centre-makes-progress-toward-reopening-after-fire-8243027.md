h1: Urban Safari Rescue Society in South Surrey: Back After the Fire
---
In March 2024 I wrote that the Urban Safari Rescue Society was getting close to reopening after a fire. It did: by November 2024 the rescue was welcoming visitors back ([Global News](https://globalnews.ca/video/10862663/exotic-animal-rescue-in-surrey-b-c-re-opens-to-visitors-after-devastating-fire/amp)). Here's the story and how to visit.

## What happened

- **The fire:** in late January 2023, a fire at the centre at **1395 176 Street** in South Surrey killed about 60 exotic animals and badly damaged the building.
- **The rebuild:** through 2023 and 2024 the society got its building permits, passed its electrical inspection, and restored the smoke-damaged rooms with insulation, drywall and new fixtures, while it kept running outreach programs and caring for the surviving animals.
- **Back open:** the reopening was paid for in large part by community donations.

## What the rescue does

Urban Safari takes in exotic animals that people can no longer keep, such as reptiles, birds and small mammals, and uses them in education programs for schools, camps and community groups. Check the society's website for visiting hours, tours and summer camps before you go.

## How to help

Donations, volunteering and booking a tour or a birthday party all support the animals. It's a good family outing in the rural part of South Surrey, close to Campbell Heights and the Hazelmere Valley.
