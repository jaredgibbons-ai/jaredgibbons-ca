In March 2024 I wrote that Chilliwack and Mission were about to get provincial housing targets. Both now have them. Here's what the targets are, how the Fraser Valley is tracking, and what it means if you're buying or selling.

## How the targets work

Under B.C.'s Housing Supply Act, the Province gives fast-growing municipalities a five-year target for net new homes, with yearly milestones. Cities report progress twice a year, and the Province can step in if a city falls well short and isn't trying. The targets come with other changes: small-scale multi-unit housing on most single-family lots, Transit-Oriented Areas near SkyTrain stations, and faster approvals.

## Fraser Valley targets

Five-year targets ([Storeys tracker](https://storeys.com/bc-housing-supply-targets-progress-tracker/), August 2025):

- **Surrey:** 27,256
- **Abbotsford:** 7,240
- **Township of Langley:** 6,596
- **Chilliwack:** 4,594
- **Maple Ridge:** 3,954
- **Langley City:** 1,844
- **Mission:** 1,798, from 211 in year one to 1,798 cumulative by year five ([City of Mission](https://www.mission.ca/council-government/news/city-mission-receives-housing-target-order-province))
- **White Rock:** 1,067

## How they're tracking

After their first full year, Chilliwack had completed 1,069 net new homes against a year-one target of 659, or 162%. Surrey (153%) and Maple Ridge (123%) were also ahead, while Abbotsford (59%) and White Rock (70%) were behind. Mission and the Township of Langley hadn't reported year-one results when that data was published.

## What it means for buyers

- **More choice in townhomes and condos.** Targets mean more approvals. Combined with a slower market, buyers in Chilliwack and Mission have more new and resale homes to choose from than a few years ago.
- **Prices:** in October 2026 the benchmark Mission home was $787,800, down 11.9% in a year ([FVREB](/mission-real-estate-market-stats.html)).
- **Neighbourhood change:** single-family streets can now see duplexes, triplexes and small multiplexes. Check the zoning of nearby lots, not just the one you're buying.
