When I first wrote this post in August 2025, three-bedroom townhomes in South Surrey were the most affordable they'd been in years. Fourteen months later, they're cheaper again. Here's where the market sits now, where the under-$800K townhomes actually are, and what I'd check before writing an offer.

## What South Surrey townhomes cost right now

In October 2026 the benchmark South Surrey and White Rock townhome was **$800,900**, down 9.2% from a year earlier ([Fraser Valley Real Estate Board](/south-surrey-white-rock-real-estate-market-stats.html), October 2026). The median sale was $832,000 and the average $876,620, so roughly half of all townhome sales still close above $800K. The ones under it are usually older complexes, end-of-row units that need updating, or smaller three-bedroom plans.

Buyers have more choice than they've had in a long time. There were 261 active townhome listings at the end of October against 35 sales that month. That's about seven months of supply, which is a buyer's market by any measure.

## Where to look under $800K

- **King George Corridor and Sunnyside:** older 1980s and 1990s complexes with larger floor plans and lower prices per square foot. See the [King George Corridor guide](/king-george-corridor-guide.html).
- **Grandview Heights:** newer townhomes start higher, but older resales and smaller three-bed plans come up close to the line. Browse [Grandview Surrey townhomes](/grandview-surrey-townhomes-for-sale.html).
- **Morgan Creek and Rosemary Heights:** mostly above $800K, but 55+ and older complexes occasionally dip under. See the [Morgan Creek guide](/morgan-creek-guide.html).
- **Ocean Park and Crescent Heights:** few townhomes and rarely under $800K, but worth watching if you want to be near the beach.

## What I'd check before you buy

- **Strata documents:** at least two years of minutes, the depreciation report and the contingency reserve fund balance. Older complexes may be heading for roofs, windows or rainscreen work, and that's often why they're cheaper.
- **Bylaws:** rental, pet and age restrictions vary by complex and can affect resale.
- **Schools:** catchments follow the address, not the neighbourhood name. My [South Surrey school catchment guide](/south-surrey-school-catchments.html) explains how to check.
- **Monthly cost:** strata fees on older townhome complexes can run several hundred dollars a month. Add them to your mortgage payment before you decide what you can afford. My [payment calculator](/first-time-buyer-calculator.html) does the maths.

## Should you buy now?

With prices down and listings up, buyers can negotiate on price, subjects and dates again. That doesn't mean prices can't fall further, but well-kept three-bedroom townhomes in good complexes are still selling. If you've been waiting to move up from a condo, this is a reasonable time to start looking seriously.
