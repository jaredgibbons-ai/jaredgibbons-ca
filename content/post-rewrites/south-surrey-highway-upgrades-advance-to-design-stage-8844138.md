In October 2025 I wrote that Surrey was moving ahead on two road projects around Highway 99 in South Surrey. One of them is now under construction. Here's the update, and what it means for the neighbourhoods around it.

## The 20 Avenue overpass of Highway 99

- **What it is:** a new 20 Avenue bridge over Highway 99 with four lanes (two each way) and a multi-use path for walking and cycling. It's an overpass, not an interchange: there are no ramps on or off Highway 99.
- **Road widening:** 20 Avenue is being widened to four lanes between 160 Street and just before 164 Street.
- **Contract:** $28.75 million, awarded to Tybo Contracting.
- **Timing:** construction started in early 2026 and is scheduled to finish in **December 2027** ([Daily Hive, January 2026](https://dailyhive.com/vancouver/20-avenue-overpass-highway-99-south-surrey-costco-construction)). Details are on the [City of Surrey project page](https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/capital-projects/20-avenue-improvements-and-highway-99-overpass).

## The Costco next door

The overpass is tied to a new Costco on a 33-acre site at 1891-1947 164 Street: a 165,000 sq. ft. store, a gas station and 975 parking stalls, with more than 400 jobs. Costco is targeting a late 2027 opening. Together, the store and road work are a project of more than $100 million.

## The 24 Avenue interchange

The second project, new north-facing ramps at the 24 Avenue interchange on Highway 99, is at a much earlier stage. In early 2026 the city described it as exploring additional ramps at 24 Avenue. Don't count on it when buying; treat it as a possible long-term improvement.

## What it means for homes nearby

- **Grandview Heights and Morgan Creek:** a second east-west crossing of Highway 99 at 20 Avenue should take pressure off 24 Avenue and 16 Avenue. That makes it easier to get between Grandview Heights, Morgan Creek and the shops along 152 Street.
- **Living close to the Costco site:** convenience for most, more traffic on 164 Street and 20 Avenue for those right next to it. If you're buying on one of those streets, look at the site plan and visit at a busy time.
- **During construction (to the end of 2027):** expect lane closures and detours on 20 Avenue and around 164 Street.
- **Selling:** new infrastructure and a major retailer are usually good for nearby values over time, but buyers will still discount homes right next to construction until it's finished. Timing your sale around that can matter.
