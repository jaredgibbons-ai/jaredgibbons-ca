In 2024 I listed an assignment at Holden Residences in Grandview Heights: a 2 bedroom and den, 954 sq ft, sold before the building completed. Assignments are common in South Surrey and Langley, where so many condos and townhomes are sold as presales. Here's how they work, and the rules that changed since.

## What an assignment is

When you buy a presale, you sign a contract with the developer for a home that isn't built yet. An **assignment** is selling that contract to someone else before completion. The new buyer (the assignee) takes over your contract and completes the purchase with the developer when the building is finished.

## How an assignment sale works

- **Developer consent:** most presale contracts need the developer's written consent to assign, and many charge an assignment fee. Some don't allow marketing an assignment on the MLS® until a set share of the project is sold. Read your contract first.
- **The price:** the assignee usually pays you back your deposit plus any profit (the "lift"), and pays the rest of the original price to the developer at completion.
- **The deposit:** your deposit stays with the developer's trust account and counts toward the purchase at completion.
- **GST:** on a new home, GST can apply both to the profit on the assignment and to the price at completion. Get tax advice before you list.

## The BC home flipping tax

Since January 1, 2025, BC has taxed profits on homes, and presale contracts, sold within two years ([Province of BC](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/income-taxes/bc-home-flipping-tax/pre-sale-contracts)):

- **Held under 366 days:** 20% of the net profit.
- **Held 366 to 729 days:** the rate falls on a sliding scale.
- **Held 730 days or more:** no tax.

For an assignment, the clock generally starts when you paid the deposit and stops when the assignee pays you. Presale contracts don't qualify for the builder or renovator exemptions, and a return is due within 90 days of the sale. The federal residential property flipping rule can also treat a quick profit as business income, so talk to an accountant.

## Buying an assignment

You get a nearly new home, often at a price set a year or two ago, with time before completion. Check the remaining deposit, the completion date, the strata disclosure statement and the developer's consent terms before you write an offer.
