In May 2024 I wrote about a public hearing for a new hotel and restaurant on King George Boulevard in South Surrey. Here's what was proposed and what council decided.

## What's proposed

- **Where:** 2160 King George Boulevard, on a site that already had an older hotel and restaurant.
- **What:** a four-storey, 80-room hotel with a restaurant and underground parking, about 14 metres tall.
- **Approvals needed:** an Official Community Plan change from Urban to Commercial, a rezoning from a tourist accommodation zone to a Comprehensive Development zone (CD 208), and a development permit. The application is No. 7921-0290-00 ([City of Surrey planning report](https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7921-0290-00.pdf)).

## What council decided

At the public hearing on May 27, 2024, council gave **third reading** to both bylaws: OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 21262 and CD 208 Bylaw No. 21263 ([council minutes](https://surrey.ca/sites/default/files/minutes/MIN_RCPH_2024_05_27.pdf)). Final adoption comes once the applicant meets the City's conditions, and building permits follow. Check the application number on the City's development map for the latest step.

## Why it matters for the area

The site sits in the King George Boulevard corridor, where the local area plan already designates it Commercial, and it's on frequent bus routes. A new hotel adds jobs and visitors close to Semiahmoo and the border, and more evening activity along the boulevard. If you live nearby, expect construction traffic when work starts.
